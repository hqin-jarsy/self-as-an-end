Abstract

This study establishes the term accountability and draws its boundary. Through Paper 0 the rights spectrum has finished with the category of duty: the horizontal duty deleted, the one inward duty kept, the correlativity axiom left without an object. Yet at the end of that chain of handovers one position was left standing with a place and a force and no name—the position a recipient holds when the demand of the power that a recognition-based right corresponds to converges upon it. This study gives that position its name, and cuts it apart from duty, from liability, and from act.

§I establishes the term and draws the divisions. F36 establishes the term: accountability is the answering load a recipient holds in a position once the demand of the power-face—the face a recognition-based right running through the three-fold interlock shows when it actually converges that recipient—has landed by registration in the recipient's action-space. F37 fixes the grain of a position at a single registration event. F38 separates duty, accountability, and the deleted horizontal duty on two columns, source and what is bound, reads the efficacy of exit off the second column instead of erecting a third, and sets three limits on source-tracing. F39 divides the holders: only a subject can hold a duty, while construct and subject alike can hold accountability.

§II establishes three stations and two chains. F40: one demand runs three stations, the last two both on the converged side. F41: the readability of an unmet demand shows that the demand is distinct from the act that would discharge it. F42: the two chains run in opposite directions with three stations each, each chain's issuing end requiring a running recognition-based right on which to rely, and where a construct issues, its power-face is borrowed and the construct serves only as an address of attribution. F43: there is no universal quid pro quo.

§III separates accountability from liability. F44: liability is the bearing of that position itself. F45: liability can stand empty. F46: the position is filled with accountability only when a demand with content lands in it.

§IV separates generation, completion, termination, and failure. F47 checks the conditions under which an accountability stands by the type of its bearer: four layers in conjunction for a subject bearer, the T5 layer not counted for a construct bearer. F48 through F51 handle the completion of a demand, exit's terminating the running answering load that registration supported, the withdrawal of recognition, and termination's not generating a moral residue of itself. F52: accountability erects no failure typology of its own; it is the reading, on the converged side, of a right's failure.

§V draws the boundary. F53: T5 is a gate, and the gate governs only the minimal relational position, and only subject bearers. F54: no separate class of violation of accountability itself is erected. F55: tension inside the boundary is an interface, not a quantitative theorem. F56: the name accountability does not distinguish nurturing from suppression from colonization.

§VI handles coexistence and engagement. F57: accountability and duty can coexist. F58 sets this study against the unconditional horizontal-duty model it excludes: one point stands established, the difference in the efficacy of exit is remitted to F38 and F57, and the difference over a residual moral debt stands only as a conditional.

§VII handles the accountability of a subject-to-be. F59: where a registration belongs during the phase of network-exercise is judged item by item. F60: the termination of a delegated accountability allots no unfinished matter to a successor. F61: no hard binary threshold is set.

§VIII closes, and lists what is left standing. One employee's single term of employment at a company runs through the whole as the working example, setting out on a single line a registration, several demands carried at once, a change of carrier, two chains running in opposite directions, and a demand gone unmet. Twenty-six propositions in all, F36 through F61.

0. Introduction · The Remainder Left on a Chain of Handovers

Through Paper 0 the rights spectrum has finished with the category of duty (义务). A subject has no duty toward others; it has one inward duty only, its answerability to its own law, which the termination lemma of A16 gives: one cannot-not legislate only for oneself (per Paper 0 M1). What is called a duty toward others is the pushing-outward of a subject's inner law into a demand upon other people; that is ethics, structurally an overreach, and it is deleted. The thing the correlativity axiom asks for has, in this series, no object.

At the end of that chain of handovers one position was left without a name.

P6, at its close, left correlativity outside the trunk and handed it to Paper 0, writing there that this series does not found rights on a rights-duty correlativity: a basic right has no correlate, a recognition-based right corresponds to power, and a duty-form demand is the demand of that power and not named a duty. Paper 0 took up the stroke and carried it through. It handled the correlativity axiom, established five propositions, and in the net of four paired legal-relation positions claimed the pair of power and liability while refusing the pair of right and duty. M4 read what the tradition calls a government's duty as the demand of the institutional power that this recognition-based right corresponds to: the government is the bearer of that power and not a duty-bearer, and the demand may be very real and very enforced.

Its place and its force both stand fixed; its name does not. The position has been identified as not a duty, and identified as the demand of a power; what it is called on the recipient's side Paper 0 did not give. P6's phrase—"not named a duty"—is an exact negation, and what it negates is the naming of this position as a duty.

The stroke of the correlativity axiom Paper 0 has taken up and carried through, and this study does not touch it. What this study takes up is the place that stroke left still to be named: a position with a place, with a force, and with no name. The name given it here is accountability (职责).

Three old words each already have an owner, and the position in the middle stands empty. Duty, in this series, is down to the one inward member, its source in the bearer's own law; to call this position by that word would be to move the source out of the power that converges upon the position and into the interior of the bearer—which is precisely the step this series deleted. Liability (责任) is the position Paper 0 has already claimed; it governs the bare fact that a position can be altered, and not the content of the alteration. An act (施为) is the discharge, the motion that issues when this position is discharged, and not the position itself. The middle position is the one this study fills.

On the English side neither responsibility nor obligation will serve. Both carry, in ordinary use, an ethical charge; both are read easily as a debt the bearer owes to someone. The source of this position lies neither inside the bearer nor in another's right, but in the power that converges upon the position. Accountability is here a stipulated technical term, its weight falling on answering and on being open to being called to answer. But changing the English word proves nothing by itself: the escape from the duty-model is carried by the division of the four positions, not by a choice of vocabulary.

One working example runs through the whole: one employee's single term of employment at a company. Every cut this study needs can be laid out on that one line—a registration; several demands carried within one and the same registration; a change of carrier when he moves to another company; two chains running in opposite directions between company and employee; and a demand that came due and was not delivered. It is chosen to adjudicate the rightness or wrongness of no employment arrangement whatever, but only to bring these cuts into one place where they can be seen together. §VII, on the subject-to-be, takes a second example—a child whose negation-gate is passing from network-exercise to self-exercise—because the employment line carries no layer of gate-state at all.

This is not a meta-study. It does not step outside the trunk to engage traditions beyond it; it is a companion study standing outside the trunk to make good one remainder, and it keeps the discipline of the main series—naming no theorists, adducing no historical events, citing no particular cases. Wherever a tradition's position is touched, the reference points back to the places Paper 0 has already fixed, and is not reopened here.

Twenty-six propositions in all, in eight sections; what each section establishes stands in the abstract.

I. Establishing the Term and Drawing the Divisions

F36 Accountability Established as a Term. A recognition-based right running through the three-fold interlock shows a power-face when it actually converges a recipient (per P4 F14, F15); once the demand of that power-face has landed by registration in the recipient's action-space, the answering load the recipient holds in that position is named accountability. The term takes up the root laid down in How Is Institution Possible, and this study carries that root forward while widening its boundary. The demand must already have identifiable content; that condition is at F46.

The root of accountability was laid down already at the institutional layer: when a construct applies a shared rule expressly to a determinate member position and holds the corresponding machinery of enforcement and disposition, the rule forms at that position an accountability whose performance can be required; and enforcement, while it keeps the demand from stopping at a form of words, neither proves the content of the demand right by itself nor makes that content the member's own law. A company's confidentiality clause lands on a post because the company has the power to dispose of that post; this does not make the clause's content right, nor does it mean the employee has thereafter taken confidentiality for his own law.

This study carries the root forward and widens its boundary. In the root sentence the converging party is a construct and the means of convergence is the machinery of enforcement and disposition; neither is required here. What is required is convergence by power in the general case—the power-face a recognition-based right shows when it actually converges a recipient. An institution is the most auditable form such convergence takes; it is not the only form.

At the widening there is one limit. The power taken here is the power-face shown by a recognition-based right running through the three-fold interlock; it is not sheer force. The upstream has already judged this (per P4 F16): if a network only takes the wielder's command as a threat to be obeyed and processes no claimable position, that is the entrenchment of sheer force, not the sedimentation of a right. A kidnapper's demand upon a hostage may have perfectly identifiable content; the hostage's action-vector is genuinely converged; the hostage can still answer and still refuse—and that position is not accountability. With no running recognition-based right there is no power-face of one, and what the hostage bears is sheer force. This limit adds no moral sieve to the study; it takes the word power, inside the definition, in the sense the definition needs. The full conditions of generation are at F47.

Nor does the landing of a registration do the work by itself. Registration makes a demand actually stand at this position; it supplies neither the demand's content nor, of itself, any warrant for it—the source of content is at F46, the question of warrant at F56. A demand whose content is already identifiable, landing by such a registration upon some position of the recipient's, leaves the recipient holding, in that position, an answering load.

An answering load is not an asset: holding it gains the bearer nothing claimable. Nor is it a credential: holding it gains the bearer no standing of any rank. It is the demand that stands in this position, awaiting an answer.

F37 A Position Is a Single Registration Event. Position, as this study uses the word, means one registration of a given bearer at a given relational slot; it does not mean the organizational office itself, nor the person occupying that office taken alone. An accountability is individuated jointly by three things—bearer, relational slot, registration event. Where one position carries several demands, accountability is individuated further, by the content of each demand.

Accountability binds a position, and the word position can name three things that lie far apart: the office of finance director; the person sitting in that office at this moment; and this person's single term in that office. Leave the three undivided and the sentence accountability binds a position says nothing at all. This study takes the third.

This is a convention of individuation, not a theorem derived from elsewhere; so what it yields and what it does not are set down together. It yields one thing only: distinct instances of registration are not one and the same accountability. A man who leaves, and years later returns to the same company and sits back down in the same office, has a second registration; what he bears is a second accountability, and not the old one running on.

It does not yield three further things. That every change of carrier must bring a new instance of registration does not follow from it—whether the registration has in fact changed must still be looked at: a company is acquired while the post and the contract stand untouched, and the carrier has changed while the registration may not have. That another's past fault in performance does not become one's own does not follow from it either; that has its own premises of belonging and attribution: the item the predecessor failed to report within his term does not become the successor's fault merely because the successor has sat down in this position. And that no later recourse can stand follows from it least of all; such a recourse must be examined separately for its source, its addressee, its registration, and its warrant. The three fall at F49, F51, and F60.

Below this grain lies one further layer, because a single registration can load several demands at once. One and the same term of employment loads a one-off demand of handover and a standing demand of confidentiality; the two share a triple of bearer, relational slot, and registration event exactly, and it must still be sayable that the handover is done while the confidentiality runs on. So beneath the triple, accountability divides again by the content of the demand. This builds no second system of identifiers; it only grants that one registration may hold several demands, each living out a life of its own.

The convention is not derived from any necessity that content change. A man moves to another company and the job description is not altered by a word: the content of the demand is what it was, and the instance of registration has changed all the same. Continuity of demand and discontinuity of the instance of bearing can stand together—the first on the side of the rule, the second on the side of the bearing. To think that wherever the content is unchanged the bearing must be one and the same is to have run the two sides together.

F38 Source and What Is Bound. A duty has its source in the bearer's own law, binds the subject itself, and is not released by exit. Accountability has its source in the power that converges upon the position, binds the position, and is released by exit: to exit that registration is to hold it no longer. The deleted horizontal duty has its source in the pushing-outward of one subject's inner law toward another; defined by that source alone, it settles nothing about the efficacy of exit. This section takes up in addition a stipulated model—one conditioned on the persistence of no particular registered relation, binding the demanded-of subject directly—and under that added stipulation exiting a registration does not release it; this is the model's stipulation, and not anything A16 proves about the other upon whom a demand falls. The three are separated by two columns, source and what is bound, and not by direction. The efficacy of exit is no third column but a reading off the second: what binds a position is held no longer once that registration is exited, and what binds the subject itself is not released by the act of exiting.

The axis of division is source and what is bound, not horizontal and inward, because horizontal already carries a technical sense in this series. Paper 0 defines a horizontal duty as the pushing-outward of a subject's inner law into a demand upon others—a definition about source. Take horizontal and inward for the axis, and one must say that accountability is horizontal; whereas accountability's source lies neither inside the bearer nor in anyone's inner law pushed outward, but in the power that converges upon the position. To use horizontal that way is to borrow its ordinary sense and drop the technical sense it holds here, and the division would fail at just the point where it matters most.

Paper 0 has a further passage, setting the correlativity duty in contrast: such a duty is a universal mapping, one claim answered by everyone's one duty, spread unconditionally toward all. That passage and the definition of the horizontal duty are not one place—the first draws the shape of what is contrasted, the second gives the source. The two are not run together; the shape of what is contrasted is left to F58.

The employee carries two such things side by side. The company requires the handover by its date: that demand issues from the power converging upon his post, and it fastens on the post. The line he has laid down for himself, that he will not go through the motions, issues from his own law, and it fastens on him. He resigns; the first no longer falls on him thereafter, and the second resignation cannot release. The deleted horizontal duty is a third thing: it issues from the pushing-outward of some subject's inner law. That source says only whose law it has been pushed out of; it says nothing about whether it ends with some relation, and the two questions are not one. The stipulated model this section takes up carries a further stipulation, that it is conditioned on the persistence of no particular registered relation; under that stipulation exiting the registration likewise cannot release it. Set the two columns out, and each of the three occupies its own cell, with no need to borrow direction.

The efficacy of exit and the availability of exit are two things, and what does the dividing is efficacy. That exit does not release a duty means that the act of exiting cannot loose it, because by the termination lemma one cannot-not legislate only for oneself, and there is no exiting into not being the subject of that law. That exit releases accountability means that if the registration is exited it is held no longer. Both sentences say only what follows if one exits; neither asserts that there is always a channel of exit to walk.

Availability has its own door. The upstream has written it down: an exit right in the strict sense presupposes a registered or recognized relational position, and without such a position there may be flight, refusal, or slipping free, but no exit right. Whether a channel of exit has been sealed off is judged for violation against the uncrossable line of P5 F21. Availability belongs to those two upstream places; efficacy belongs to this column.

Three apparent counter-examples dissolve here. A government converged by a constitutional order cannot exit unilaterally; a minor cannot exit an arrangement of guardianship; one held in custody has no channel of exit on the physical side. In all three, availability is absent. In all three, efficacy is present: let the position actually terminate, and the corresponding cell is indeed held no longer—so all three are accountability still. The counter-examples look sound only because exitable has been read as there must be a channel of exit, which is to read efficacy as availability.

On the duty side, exit's inefficacy read too widely goes wrong in the same way. What it names is the structure of bearing oneself under one's own law; it is not the permanent unrevisability of every particular purpose or judgment that has ever appeared within that law. The employee who comes to think a demand he once laid on himself too harsh, and alters it, has not exited self-bearing; altering it is precisely a thing done inside self-bearing.

The efficacy of exit is not erected as a third column, because it is not independent. Erect it, and every cell in it could be read off the column of what is bound: within the three technical senses this section has fixed, what binds a position is held no longer once the registration is exited, and what binds the subject itself is not released by the act of exiting. A column that takes the same value as another in every cell divides no two things; to erect it is to write one thing twice. The accountability half has a further upstream landing: Paper 0, in treating the unborn beneficiary of a trust, wrote it plainly—the recipient holds a liability, subject to the conditioned power's demand, bearing it if it does not exit and no longer holding the right if it does. What is spoken of there is liability, and this study's concept of accountability is not yet in use; what is drawn on here is the structure of exiting one and the same relation, and the loss of that right is not on its own re-read as a release from accountability. Accountability in this study points by F46 to an answering load with content and is fastened to a particular registration; where that registration has in fact been exited, the running load that stood upon it terminates by F49. The duty half needs the termination lemma to carry it; both places are cited together at F58.

Source-tracing is set up as a step of its own. To ask whether a demand can land on someone is first to ask whether the power that converges upon that person's present position contains it. Source-tracing has three limits, and drop one and it is no longer source-tracing but another name for a verdict reached in advance.

First: that a demand's content points back to an old matter does not by itself settle its present source. The item the predecessor failed to report speaks of an old matter, and it does not follow that the demand can issue only from the old relation; the company, holding the power now to dispose of this post, may perfectly well lay a fresh demand to clear old accounts upon the successor's position. That an old load is the content proves neither, by itself, that the present demand has a source, nor that it lacks one.

Second: the ending of a position does not prove that the power which set it up has gone. After the employee resigns, the company and its oversight arrangement stand as before; whether there is some further relation for handling prior matters is judged at F49 and F51. One may therefore say only if the source too has in fact ceased; one may not say that because the position terminated the source has ceased.

Third: the bare fact of taking over does not establish a source. A position, per F37, is a single registration event, and not a vessel on which loads may be hung; but whether taking over is itself the registration-trigger some standing arrangement provides for must be judged by that arrangement. If the contract of appointment says in terms that a successor takes up every unsettled matter, that is a trigger the arrangement provides for, and it is not what this limit excludes.

Source-tracing is not the pronouncing, from a demand's content, that it has no source, and then the calling of that advance verdict source-tracing. It can catch a substitution; it does not guarantee in advance that it will return the same negative answer for every content. To skip the checking of source on the strength of a past in which another acted in one's stead, of the fact of having benefited, or of the occupancy of a position taken over, is not enough to settle that an accountability stands.

F39 The Division of Holders. Only a subject can hold a duty; construct and subject alike can hold accountability. A duty's source must lie inside the bearer, and a construct has no inward source. Accountability's source lies in the power that converges upon the position, and a construct can be converged.

A duty's source must lie inside the bearer, and a construct has no inward source, so a construct cannot hold a duty; accountability's source lies in the power that converges upon the position, and a construct can be converged, so a construct can hold accountability. A company converged by a regulatory body holds accountability at that position; and a company cannot legislate a law of its own for itself—it has no such inward source.

That a construct holds accountability means that a demand may take an organizational or institutional position as its address of attribution, while the answering, the refusing, the carrying out, and the revising must all be realized through the corresponding nodes and procedures. This grants the construct no independent first-person inward source, and it does not of itself spread that accountability into one and the same duty for every member. The address of attribution falls on the company, while the hands that move are a few people in the compliance department; the two are not one thing.

On the issuing side too, a construct serves only as an address of attribution. A construct does not hold a recognition-based right in its own name, and a demand issuing from a construct can nonetheless generate accountability, because the power-face on that side is a borrowed one, and what the construct does on that chain is again to serve as an address, not as a holding end. §II's F42 is the other face of this move. On neither side is a construct the self-holding end of a recognition-based right; and on the converged side a construct may still be the registered bearer of accountability, where holds accountability takes the sense of organizational attribution this proposition has fixed, and gains it no first-person inward source thereby. On the converged side, bearing accountability, a construct may serve as an address of attribution; on the issuing side, a construct serves only as an address of attribution too.

Three things are separate, and to run them together yields two opposite errors. Which nodes actually carry out the organization's work is one. Which nodes handle the inquiry, the account, and the remedy after the company has failed in its accountability is a second. Which member, on what independent ground, bears a personal accountability, a liability, or an adverse consequence is a third. The first two are realized through persons and procedures, and they do not of themselves establish the third.

Each of the two opposite errors is barred once. The name of a construct may not screen a personal liability that already has an independent ground; a construct does not serve as a shell that blocks personal bearing. Nor, when a construct has failed in its accountability, may that load be transferred outright onto the books of some one member; a construct's internal charter is not the sole ground. The third thing must have a source, a competence, a registration, and facts of conduct of its own.

If a demand penetrates through to a natural person inside the construct and zeroes out that natural person's subject position, the T5 gate is read as broken through at that natural person, and not at the construct. The gate's jurisdiction is drawn separately in §V.

Any predicate that requires an inward source can be attached only to members inside a construct, and not to the construct. In this study that sentence is a discipline of attribution: it governs the handling of attribution, keeping a predicate that requires an inward source from being misattached to the construct. It is not offered as a proven theorem ranging over the whole of language.

II. Three Stations and Two Chains

§I gave the definition and drew the divisions. This section measures the road a demand travels from its issuing to its discharge, and asks whether two demands running in opposite directions between the same two nodes are counterparts of each other.

F40 One Demand Runs Three Stations. It issues as the demand of some recognition-based right's power-face; it lands by registration as accountability; it is discharged by the corresponding act. The last two stations stand on one and the same side, the converged side, and are not divided between two parties.

The three stations are three states of one and the same demand, and not three demands unrelated to one another.

The first station is on the side of power: a recognition-based right shows its power-face when it actually converges a recipient, and the demand issues from there. Power is taken here still in the sense §I limited it to, and does not mean any social force whatever; if at the first station there is only a bare force and no such right, what lands is sheer force, and the three stations have no point of departure. The second station is on the converged side: the demand lands by registration in the recipient's action-space, and the answering load he holds in that cell is his accountability. The third station is on the converged side too: the accountability is discharged by the corresponding act.

The company requires the employee to complete the handover before the end of the month, and on that one demand the three stations are run through at a stroke: the right by which the company disposes of this post shows a power-face and issues the demand; the demand lands in his action-space and becomes his accountability; he makes the handover, and the accountability is discharged.

The second station and the third stand on the same side. Set the demand with the one party and the discharge with the other, and accountability becomes a voucher in the first party's hand while the act becomes a delivery the second party owes the first—which is the shape of credit and debt. Accountability and act are both on the recipient: the one a load standing upright, the other that load's discharge. What is divided between two parties is only the first station against the last two, and that division is the division of the power-face from the face that is converged.

The demand a power issues and the cell a recipient holds are one and the same demand, and not one and the same thing. One and the same demand, because from first to last it is this one demand that runs the three stations; not one and the same thing, because the two stations fall on different sides—at the first what is read off is a right's power-face making its demand, at the second an answering load. Call them one and the same thing, and accountability is put into the company's hands, where it is not; call them two unrelated demands, and one goes looking for a second source for the second demand, which has no other source.

The three stations do not exhaust act. The issuing of a demand can itself be an act, and a discharge may be a required forbearance—confidentiality is discharged in just that way. The three stations divide the states of one demand; they do not define act.

F41 The Readability of an Unmet Demand Proves the Demand Independent of Act. Given that the demand already stands on its own, non-discharge does not cancel it. If the demand just were the act, then the act's not occurring would be merely that thing's not occurring, and there would be nothing left standing that could go unmet.

Let an accountability stand and the bearer not discharge it: the demand is then unmet. For that reading to hold at all, something must still be standing; if the demand just were the act itself, the act's not occurring would be merely that thing's not occurring, and nothing would be left over that could go unmet. The end of the month comes and he has not made the handover: the motion of handing over did not occur, and the demand did not vanish with it. That unmetness can be read at all is itself the proof that demand and act are two.

The inference runs one way only. Disappointment, or an expectation come to nothing, is not enough to establish that an accountability was ever there. A colleague hoped he would put a certain file in order along with the rest, and waited in vain; that proves the colleague's expectation came to nothing, and not that there was ever a demand with a source, with content, and registered. Unmetness yields the independence of the demand; whether the demand stands is still checked against what §I laid down.

Unmetness and a second reading that looks much like it differ by a hair, and they come out opposite. Unmetness is a demand standing while the act has not occurred, and it is evidence that an accountability stands. The second runs thus: the statement of accountabilities for that post hangs on untouched, while the company long ago ceased to dispose of it—no one asks after it and there is nowhere to ask—and the bearer is in position with no accountability to speak of. This study calls the latter nominal convergence, and its place is §IV. Where a demand goes unmet there is an accountability; where convergence is nominal there is none.

F42 The Two Chains Run in Opposite Directions, with Three Stations Each. If the two chains stand separately, then the position-holder's recognition-based right converges the bearer, and the bearer holds accountability and issues an act; and the power-face of the recognition-based right the bearing side relies on converges the position-holder, and the position-holder holds accountability and issues an act. Each chain's issuing end must rely on a recognition-based right running through the three-fold interlock; only then does that right's power-face generate the chain's accountability. Where the issuing end is a subject, it holds that right in its own name. Where the issuing end is a construct, the construct does not hold a recognition-based right in its own name, and its power-face is borrowed from the right held by the subject who invokes that apparatus (per Power Theory P2 M2); the construct is that chain's address of attribution, the phrase in its own name holds only of a subject issuing end, and the borrowed right must still run through the three-fold interlock, a requirement that borrowing does not relax. Each chain has its own conditions of constitution, and neither is the price of the other.

Between the same two nodes there can be two chains running in opposite directions. The first sets out from the position-holder: the power-face of his recognition-based right converges the bearing party, the bearing party holds accountability in that cell, and discharges it by an act. The second sets out from the bearing party: the power-face of the recognition-based right the bearing side relies on converges the position-holder, the position-holder holds accountability in that cell, and discharges it by an act. Each chain has the structure of three stations; they run in opposite directions and are the same in form. Between company and employee there are just such two: the one discharged by pay at its term, the other by the handover and by confidentiality.

On the second chain there is one thing that cannot be left out: the bearing side too must rely on a recognition-based right running through the three-fold interlock, and only then does its power-face generate that chain's accountability. If what this side puts forward is merely a social force, and the network processes no claimable position, then what lands on that chain is sheer force and not accountability, and it is not a chain in the sense of this section.

Here relying on a right and holding one in one's own name must be kept apart. A recognition-based right is a thing a subject holds, and it takes standing to hold one (per On Rights and Entitlements F27, P1 F4), whereas a construct has no negation-gate and therefore has no standing to hold with. A company does not hold a recognition-based right in its own name. That it can nonetheless issue a demand which generates accountability has a reading given upstream: an apparatus does not wield power; individuals invoke an apparatus to wield power, and the topological legitimacy of an apparatus is borrowed, not native (per Power Theory P2 M2). A company does not lay people off; key-node managers lay people off through the apparatus the company is, which is the very example that paper adduces; and the company's requiring the handover of that employee likewise issues from some position holding the corresponding right, by way of the apparatus the company is. What a construct does on the chain is to serve as an address of attribution, and not as a holding end.

Borrowing relaxes no requirement. The right borrowed must still run through the three-fold interlock, or there is nothing there to borrow: a bare demand with no subject's corresponding right underneath it finds no position to borrow from, and what lands is sheer force still. The requirement stands in the same cell on both chains, and is not relaxed because the bearing side happens to be a construct; how the borrowed right itself stands is checked by its own source and its own registration, and not here. How a construct serves as an address of attribution on the holding side is at §I.

That each chain must have a running right underneath it does not make one chain's standing conditional on the other's. What each chain requires is the right its own issuing end relies on; each asks its conditions of its own end, and neither asks anything of the other. So one chain may stand while the other does not, and both may stand without being counterparts of each other.

F43 No Universal Quid Pro Quo. A recognition-based right is not got in exchange for recognizing a power. The generation, the continuance, and the termination of a chain's accountability are judged by that chain's own content and conditions; an accountability is not generated automatically because the other chain has been performed, nor released automatically because the other chain has not. Whether a particular arrangement makes some fact a condition of application is judged separately, by that arrangement.

The transactional reading has a complete shape: the recipient has recognized the other's power and gets in exchange the right on his own side, so that the right is the counterpart of recognizing a power and accountability is the price of holding the right. The shape follows naturally enough, and it does draw the look of many a particular arrangement; as a universal rule it does not stand. The conditions under which a recognition-based right stands are given upstream: it takes its content-identity through constitutive recognition, and then enters operation through the three-fold interlock of institutional embedding, effective community recognition, and real power backing. Not one step along that road is handed over as the counterpart of recognizing another's power; and power stands by registration in the first place, and needs no recognitional gloss laid over it before it can take effect. There is therefore no universal point of exchange.

What this proposition blocks is two automatics. That the company has paid in full and at its term the accountability on its own chain does not of itself generate a fresh accountability on his; for one to be generated, that chain must itself have a source, content, and a registration. That the company owes wages and does not pay does not of itself release the confidentiality accountability already standing on his chain; to release it, a reason must be found within that chain's own conditions.

To block the two automatics is not to declare that no relation of condition exists. A contract may say in terms that pay having been made is a condition of application for some demand of delivery, and then there is indeed a relation of condition between the two; but that relation comes from the contract, and is judged by the contract's content and conditions, and not from any rule that sets rights and accountabilities universally against each other.

III. Accountability and Liability

§I cut accountability apart from duty; this section cuts it apart from liability. The two stand on the same side, the converged side, and are the likeliest pair to be taken for two names for one thing.

F44 Liability Is the Bearing of That Position Itself. The liability held by one converged by a power is subject to that power's demand. Liability says that a position can be altered; it does not say what the alteration contains. It is conditioned neither on fault nor on whether a channel of exit is available.

Liability is the cell Paper 0 has already claimed. This study takes it as it stands there, and only says exactly how far it reaches.

What it governs is the bare fact that a position can be altered. That one converged by a power holds a liability means that his relational position stands within that power's reach: let the power move, and his position alters with it. What it alters into is another question.

Two conditions do not enter this cell.

First, liability is not conditioned on fault, and the one who holds it may have done nothing wrong. Paper 0 has already set up the odious-debt example, and the reference here only points back to it: after a regime falls, the new regime and people may declare the odious debts of the former regime void, and the creditor's legal state is thereby altered; what is at work there is not a duty laid on the creditor but a power to alter a systemic legal state. The creditor has been derelict in nothing. What the creditor holds is a liability.

Second, liability is not conditioned on whether a channel of exit is available. Paper 0's sentence on liability came with the clause "bearing it if it does not exit, and no longer holding the right if it does"; the general definition given here carries no such clause. The reason is not that the sentence is wrong, but the layer it is so easily read as carrying: that bearing presupposes the availability of a channel of exit. The odious-debt example rules that reading out exactly: what the creditor stands across from is a power to alter a legal state, and stipulate that no channel of exit is available to him—the liability stands all the same. The clause no channel of exit is an added condition this study stipulates expressly for the sake of the display, and not a fact the upstream has found: the upstream takes from that example only its structural position, and says nothing whatever about whether the creditor has a channel of exit; nor does this study thereby assert that no registered position exists there, still less pass judgment on any actual relation of debt. One expressly stipulated case is enough to carry the display. The availability of exit belongs, by §I's division, to the two upstream places, and this cell of liability is not moved by it.

F45 Liability Can Stand Empty. In the odious-debt example the creditor holds a liability with nothing required of him: his legal state is altered, and that is all. So liability is not conditioned on a demand with content.

A liability can be empty. The creditor holds a liability and there is nothing whatever for him to do: nothing to deliver, nothing to answer, nothing to declare—his legal state has been altered by another's power, and that is the whole of it.

An empty liability is a liability still, because what that cell says all along is that a position can be altered, and not that there is something at the position to be done. This is the footing the next proposition stands on: if liability had to carry content, liability and accountability could not be told apart.

F46 The Position Is Filled with Accountability Only When a Demand with Content Lands in It. Accountability points to a demand whose content, whose position of application, and whose conditions of discharge are already identifiable. Registration gives it its actual standing at that position; it does not supply the content by itself, nor does it prove by itself that the demand is warranted. Liability governs bearing and accountability governs answering; the two stand on the same side, the converged side, and are not two ways of saying one cell.

When that cell is filled by a demand with content, it becomes accountability.

The content comes from the side of the rule. The employee's confidentiality clause has already set out what counts as a thing to be kept, within what compass it is to be kept, and by what procedure a breach is handled; none of that is given by registration. Registration does another thing: it makes this already-set-out demand actually stand at the position of this one term of employment. What the rule sets out, and whom registration makes it land on, are two things and belong in two places. Nor does registration thereby prove the content of that clause warranted; warrant is at §V.

Liability governs bearing, accountability governs answering. The first is that a position can be altered; the second is that a demand awaiting an answer stands at the position. The two are on one side, the converged side, and are not two ways of saying one cell: were they two ways of saying one thing, an empty liability could not exist—and it does.

Nor does accountability come free of liability. There is no such thing as a demand landing on a man whose position is wholly unmoved by that power; a demand can be made of him precisely because the position lies within reach. So every accountability presupposes a liability, while not every liability is filled out into an accountability.

IV. Generation, Completion, Termination, and Failure

Four things each have a door of their own, and packed into one question they collide with one another: a particular demand is completed or brought to an end by its content; a bearing position terminates upon exit; the relation that supports its standing fails at the upstream's break point; and before these three there stands the question of how an accountability is generated at all. This section takes them in that order.

F47 The Conditions Under Which an Accountability Stands Are Checked by the Type of Bearer. The criterion is whether the action-vector is objectively converged by a field of power; it lies not in emotion and not in identification, and a registration may be a willing one or a coerced one (per P3 F13). Given F46's condition that the demand's content, its position of application, and its conditions of discharge are already identifiable, what must be checked is the constitutive recognition and the three-fold-interlock operation of the corresponding recognition-based right, together with that demand's actual registration in the recipient's action-space. Where the recipient is a subject bearer in F53's sense, the minimal relational position T5 preserves must be checked in addition, and the conditions of standing are then a conjunction of four layers; where the recipient is a construct, a construct's not having a subject position is not counted as a failure to pass T5, and its bearing and its boundary are judged by F39 and F53. The layers are not all read on one and the same side: registration in the action-space and the T5 slot are read on the recipient's side, while content-identity and operative efficacy are properties of the corresponding recognition-based right itself and are read on the side that holds that right. Registration alone does not supply content. As regards first generation, where the required conditions have never been complete no accountability has stood; a case that was once complete and later fell below threshold does not belong here, and is read by F52 and the propositions with it.

The four layers are not pieced together by this study. The upstream, in writing the T5 seam, already set them side by side: T5 gives the minimal relational slot, constitutive recognition gives content-identity, the three-fold interlock gives operative efficacy; and registration in the action-space and T5's preservation of the subject position the upstream had long since cut into two things. What this study adds is only the marking of which side each layer is read on.

Marking the side is not a flourish. The first two layers are facts about the recipient: his action-vector is indeed converged by that field of power, and he is still preserved as a subject position able to answer, to refuse, and to revoke the registration. The latter two are not about him at all; they are properties of that recognition-based right itself—it takes its content-identity through constitutive recognition and its operative efficacy through the three-fold interlock. Where the bearer is a subject, the check runs two layers on each of the two sides, the evidence gathered in different places, and any one of them missing generates no accountability; where the bearer is a construct, the T5 layer does not apply and the remaining three are checked as before. Leave the sides unmarked and the layers get read as a single checklist on the converged side, so that completeness on the recipient's side is taken for completeness overall.

If the employee's post manual has printed in it a clause that no one has ever actually applied to this position of his, what is missing is the layer of registration. If the domain in which that demand sits has never acquired the operative backing the three-fold interlock requires, what is missing is the layer of operative efficacy. Neither way of being missing generates an accountability, and neither is the same thing as failure: failure is having once stood and then fallen below threshold, whereas conditions of generation unsatisfied is never having stood at all. To say that some department has long gone unattended to and that the company brings no disposition to bear on it any longer is not enough: that no disposition is actually brought to bear does not of itself prove that the backing of disposition never existed, nor does it rule out that it once existed and later fell below threshold—and the latter is exactly the failure F52 speaks of. For an example actually to lack this layer, it must be expressly stipulated or ascertained that real backing was never acquired.

What falls out when this layer is missing depends further on which support the break falls on, and the two are separated at P3 F10. Where what is missing is the operative efficacy the three-fold interlock gives, one thing only can be concluded: no accountability stands. If force is still being applied at the same time and the network processes no claimable position, the case reads as sheer force in the way §I set out (per P4 F16); if even actual convergence is gone, one may not infer back from there being no accountability that sheer force is there still. The failure of a necessary condition for accountability rules out that accountability; it does not by itself establish sheer force.

F48 A Demand Is Completed or Brought to an End by Its Content. A registered one-off demand of delivery is discharged once the delivery is complete, while the role, the right, and the power backing may all continue to exist. The expiry of a term set within the content, the disappearance of a condition, or a revision of the demand are likewise not necessarily any kind of failure.

He completes the handover, and that demand is completed. After the completion his post is still there, the company's right to dispose of that post is still there, and that right's power backing is still there. The closing of a single demand does not disturb the whole set it hangs on.

Expiry of a term, disappearance of a condition, and revision of the demand all go the same way. The demand agreed to run only to the year's end comes to an end when the year's end arrives; the demand that applies only because some condition holds no longer applies once the condition disappears; the demand that has been rewritten runs thereafter by the new content. All of this is written in the demand's own content, and has nothing to do with failure. Failure is a break in the relation that supports this demand, and here the relation takes no damage at all.

F49 Exit Terminates the Running Answering Load That Registration Supported. By F38, what binds a position is held no longer once that registration is exited; so what exit terminates is the running answering load that registration supported, including demands that would fall thereafter and demands still running on. Exit does not retroactively erase the fact that an accountability did once stand, nor the performance or non-performance that has already occurred; nor does this exit by itself cancel demands generated on other relational positions that still stand, which are checked separately, each by its own source and its own conditions.

After he resigns, the demands that fall on that position thereafter no longer fall on him; and the load still running on by that registration terminates with them—the confidentiality accountability does not run on merely because it is a standing one. The two terminate together, and that is what exit terminates.

It does not terminate two things. The first is what has already happened: what he handed over within his term and what he left out, resignation does not rewrite that history. The second is a source elsewhere: if he has besides a non-compete agreement still in force, or if some other relational position between him and the company still stands, then the demands at those positions are settled by their own conditions, and do not lapse along with this one resignation. Exit is a knife that falls on this one registration; it is not an eraser that can wipe out the whole record.

F50 After Recognition Is Withdrawn, the Conditions of Constitution and of Operation Must Be Rechecked by Domain. Only where all sources exit in that domain does it go as outright withdrawal; only if some subdomain falls below threshold does it go as local failure; where the remaining sources still suffice to support the original compass, the withdrawal of one support does not of itself yield a verdict of failure.

When recognition is withdrawn, ask first which domain has been withdrawn from, and then ask whether what is left after the withdrawal is still enough.

The three cases come apart. All constitutive sources exit in that domain: that is outright withdrawal, and the accountability on that domain goes out with them. Only some subdomain falls below threshold: that is local failure, and the accountability on the domain that fell below threshold disappears while the rest runs on. And if what has been withdrawn is only one support among several, the remaining sources still sufficing to support the original compass, then the withdrawal of one support does not of itself yield a verdict of failure. One support's exiting and the whole position's giving way are two things, and a threshold stands between them.

F51 Termination Does Not of Itself Generate a Moral Residue. This study does not, from the termination of the original accountability, erect of itself a sourceless residual moral debt; nor does it declare on that ground that every subsequent matter has been settled and closed. Positions of subsequent inquiry and of subsequent handling each require a ground of their own, and are not struck off together by the disappearance of the original registration.

After termination there are two opposite misreadings, and this proposition blocks one on each side.

On the one side is the reading of termination as leaving a residual moral debt behind—as though, the position being gone, he owed something morally still. This study erects no such sourceless debt. Accountability has its source in the power that converges upon that position; the source being gone, it does not go on existing under another face.

On the other side is the reading of termination as everything having been settled and closed: the position has terminated, so nothing need be asked further. This does not stand either. Positions of subsequent inquiry and of subsequent handling may each have a ground of their own, and they are not struck off together by the disappearance of the original registration.

Two kinds of old account must be written separately. The first is the bearer's own historical fact of performance or non-performance, which exit does not erase. The second is the load another left behind during a phase of exercise in his stead, which runs the other way and is handled separately in §VII.

F52 Accountability Erects No Failure Typology of Its Own; It Is the Reading, on the Converged Side, of a Right's Failure. Where a change in an accountability arises from a corresponding break at the same seam, it is read using the upstream failure signature; no synonymous set of names is introduced.

The upstream has already divided the failure of a recognition-based right into five types by where the break falls. Accountability starts no second set; it is only the reading of that same break on the converged side.

The hollowing-out cell has three further points. The first: the fact that the accountability did once stand before the hollowing-out, and the fact of the non-discharge that had already occurred, are preserved by F49 and F51, and what this table reads is the state of the accountability after the hollowing-out. What §II called nominal convergence is exactly this cell.

The carrier migration cell leaves one place without an independent check. At the moment of carrier migration a gap may open in which the old registration has stopped and the new has not yet formed; this case depends at once on two conditions, this study's convention of individuation and real backing having in fact fallen below threshold, and it cannot be confirmed by analogy with actuality alone, so this study does not for the present take it as an independent check.

V. The Boundary

Accountability has a boundary, and in the drawing of it there are two places where one easily goes astray: one is drawing the boundary somewhere else, the other is taking the drawing of a boundary for a protection. This section sets up the gate first, and then says how far the gate's jurisdiction runs and what it does not govern.

F53 T5 Is a Gate, and the Gate Governs Only the Minimal Relational Position. This gate governs only subject bearers. Where the recipient is a subject, he must be preserved as a subject position able to answer, to refuse, and to revoke the registration; let that position be zeroed out in the recipient's action-space, and the relation passes out of social power, and no accountability stands. On the construct side this gate does not apply: whether its accountability stands and continues is checked against F47's conditions of application, and completion, termination, and failure are handled separately by §IV. It is only where that position is zeroed out that a power recedes, in this study's terms, into non-social power; other boundary-crossings may still occur while the relation has not left the field. A demand's exceeding the compass of a role, violating a basic right, or forming a lock-in does not take the zeroing out of the subject position as a necessary condition, and each is judged separately by its own criterion. That T5 has not given way is not a sufficient condition for a demand's being warranted or for its being free of violation.

The gate is set where the upstream has already set it. A relation remains social power only so long as the recipient is still preserved as a subject position able to answer, to refuse, and to revoke the registration; let the position be zeroed out, and social power leaves the field. Accountability has its source in that power, and with the source gone from the field no accountability stands. No second gate is set up here; the gate is only applied to the accountability side.

The gate governs only subject bearers. §I established that a construct can hold accountability, and the two items can refuse and can revoke the registration make no sense said of a construct: a company's refusing regulation is not a refusal in T5's sense, a company's revoking its registration just is dissolution or exit, and terminating a construct can be a routine move inside an institution, while the same shape done to a subject is the gate broken through. Leave the jurisdiction undrawn, and no accountability a construct holds could pass this gate, so that none would stand at all—which pulls against what §I established.

A construct is not judged to hold no accountability for want of a subject position, nor is it thereby spared the continuing check of the conditions of standing. A demand completed or brought to an end by its content goes by F48; an exit from that registration goes by F49; a break on the same seam in the corresponding right's conditions of constitution or of operation is read by F50 and F52. What is borrowed is the corresponding right's failure signature, and what is reported is the state of the construct's accountability on the converged side; this neither reclassifies the construct as a self-holding end of a recognition-based right, nor do the five types exhaust every combination.

There is a further case that falls at a third place. If a demand penetrates through to a natural person inside the construct and zeroes out that natural person's subject position, the gate is read as broken through at that natural person, and not at the construct. §I wrote that a construct does not serve as a shell that blocks personal bearing; this is the other face of that: a construct does not serve as a shell that absorbs personal protection either.

Boundary-crossings outside the gate are not the gate's to govern. A demand exceeding the compass of a role, or violating a basic right, or forming a lock-in—all of these can occur while the relation is far from having left the field. The employee's post is still there and he can still answer, still refuse, still revoke the registration, while some demand of the company's has already reached into a place it should not reach. Such matters are each judged by their own criterion, and do not take the zeroing out of the subject position as a necessary condition. That T5 has not given way is no proof that a demand is warranted, and no proof that it is free of violation.

From this the office of this study must be nailed down: it classifies; it issues no warrant. A load classified as accountability gains no endorsement thereby; and conversely, a demand that has crossed the definitional boundary and is no longer called accountability in this study's terms has not thereby had its coercion stop in actuality. The reclassifying of a concept is no mechanism of protection in actuality. An exit clause written on paper does not thereby prove that the position T5 requires is really preserved in actual operation; that must be checked separately.

F54 No Separate Class of Violation of Accountability Itself Is Erected. The performance of an accountability can be blocked, and no separate class of violation of accountability itself is erected on that account. Whether the blocking violates, and what right of the position-holder, of the accountability's bearer, or of some other subject it violates, is judged by placing it on the upstream's scale (P5 F22): the basic component is read by limit and violation, the recognition component is read for failure by the five types, and the two sets are not mixed. Performance blocked, performance impossible, whether the accountability continues to stand, and how the attendant consequences are handled do not follow automatically one from another. As regards the converged party, this proposition registers only the facts of external blocking and of non-delivery; it does not on those facts alone find a breach or a dereliction, nor does it find on them that he is of course free of bearing any attendant consequence. The corresponding judgments are still handled separately by the content of the demand, its conditions of application, and the relevant facts, and no defensive status with respect to accountability is generated from the blocking itself.

Someone has blocked his performance; this adds no new class of violation.

The reason lies in what a violation takes as its object. What a violation is directed at is the subject position and its degrees of freedom, whereas an accountability is a converged answering load: it is not an asset, and it is not a credential protected by a right. When a load is blocked, what takes the damage is something else—it may be the position-holder's recognition-based right, it may be some basic right of the bearer's own, it may be something of a third party's. Which it is, is judged by placing it on the upstream's scale: the basic component read by limit and violation, the recognition component read for failure by the five types, and the two sets not mixed.

Nor may the inference be run backwards. That accountability is not an asset does not yield the conclusion that the channels, the credentials, and the persons involved in performance can never have a rights protection of their own. If those rights are there, they must have sources of their own.

Four things do not follow automatically one from another: performance blocked is one, performance impossible is the second, whether the accountability continues to stand is the third, how the attendant consequences are handled is the fourth. The company shuts off the system he needs, and he therefore cannot make the handover: the blocking is a fact, the non-delivery is a fact, and these two facts by themselves neither prove him in breach or derelict nor prove that he is of course free of bearing any attendant consequence. These are still handled separately by the content of that demand, its conditions of application, and the relevant facts.

Only the facts are registered, and no settlement is made in either direction. No defensive status is generated from the blocking itself, and no fault is found from the blocking itself.

F55 Tension Inside the Boundary Is an Interface, Not a Quantitative Theorem. Within T5 there may still be tensions of conflicting demands, of the cost of performance, and of practical feasibility. Whether the compass of answering widens correspondingly as the compass of power exercised grows depends on constraining arrangements that stand on their own footing, and does not follow directly from the definition of accountability.

The gate holds, and the tensions inside the boundary do not disappear on that account. Demands can conflict with one another, performance can be expensive, actuality can make a thing impossible. All of this lies within T5, and the gate does not reach it.

One of these is especially easy to take for a theorem: the greater the power exercised, the heavier the answering. This does not follow from the definition of accountability. Accountability has its source in the power that converges upon this position, and not in the power this position exercises outward. That a position exercises great power outward does not show that inquiry into it and constraint upon it have correspondingly been established; the two can come apart, and it is exactly this coming apart that institutional design has to handle. Whether the gradient of power exercised against answering stands depends on what that particular construct has actually set up, and that is a matter of experience, not an inference from a definition.

Tension inside the boundary is marked here only as an interface; it is given no quantitative law.

F56 The Name Accountability Does Not Distinguish Nurturing from Suppression from Colonization. That a demand is found to be an accountability says only that it has a source, has content, and has landed by registration at some position; it does not say whether it makes the bearer grow or collapse. That judgment has a door of its own: whether the power is nurturing or colonization is judged by the structural criterion the upstream has already set, and whether the demand crosses the boundary is judged by limit and violation. A bearer's holding an accountability gains it no endorsement of warrant.

One and the same person recognizes one and the same power, and the accountability he takes up thereby can be the kind that holds him up, or the kind that presses him down, or even the kind that gathers him in as material for another's ends. And on this study's naming the three are not distinguished at all; all three are accountability.

The company requires him to write a quarterly review, and this may force him to think one layer further about what he has done, or it may only leave his superiors a file by which he can be called to account; the company requires him to be on call at all hours, and this may be a genuine lifting-up, or it may be the gathering of the whole of his time into someone else's arrangements. In all three cases that cell stands: there is a source, there is content, and it has landed by registration on his post. The name accountability reports that the conditions of standing are satisfied; it does not report that the content of the demand has an endorsement of warrant.

Warrant, and the judgment of nurturing, suppression, and colonization, have doors of their own. Whether a power is nurturing or colonization the upstream has already cut apart by a structural criterion (per P6 F26), writing expressly that it is not settled by motive, by feeling, or by good and evil: a well-meant inquiry can be, structurally, colonization, and an ill-meant inquiry can be, structurally, nurturing. Whether the demand crosses the boundary is judged by limit and violation. Neither door is in this study.

This proposition is a negative one. It rules out one inference; it asks after no motive, asks after no identification, and passes no judgment on effect. The upstream settles limit and violation by structure and not by motive and feeling; the upstream's criterion for registration lies neither in emotion nor in identification; and this proposition collides with neither of those.

From this there falls a further conclusion, one less easy on the eye, and it has a boundary of its own: two sentences must stand together. First, so long as the conditions of standing that F47 applies by the type of bearer and the corresponding boundaries of F53 are still satisfied, a demand can violate a basic right, can form a suppression or a colonization, and have the identity of an accountability all the same—the name accountability does not thereby endorse its content; cut such a case out, and whatever is called an accountability would carry the implication that no boundary has been crossed, which is exactly to let the classification issue warrant, the contrary of this proposition. Second, where the conditions of standing are no longer satisfied, the identity of accountability may not be retained merely because the demand still has coercive effect; if the same demand at once zeroes out the minimal relational position of a subject bearer, or the corresponding conditions of source, of registration, and of operation no longer stand, then its identity and its destination are judged by F53 or by §IV. Whether a boundary has been crossed is typed by the upstream, and the typing does not itself answer for the conditions of standing. If a suppressed bearer refuses a demand that violates a right, F54 does not on that fact alone classify the non-discharge as dereliction. This refusal is non-delivery, not external blocking; here too only the facts are registered, with no settlement in either direction.

Last, how far this proposition actually governs must be made plain. §IV set the conditions of standing. Among suppressive demands, any that fails to satisfy the corresponding conditions on the right's operation and is imposed by sheer force alone fails to be an accountability there already, because where there is no recognition-based right running through the three-fold interlock there is no power-face of one. So this proposition actually governs only those cases in which the conditions of standing are still satisfied and there is suppression all the same, and its jurisdiction is narrower than its letter. This is no general accommodation of suppression; the only thing it refuses is one step of inference, the step from a load's being an accountability to its not suppressing the bearer.

VI. Coexistence and Engagement

Accountability and the one inward duty that has been kept do not exclude each other, while between it and the deleted model of horizontal duty there is a genuine difference. Both matters are read off the two columns, source and what is bound: the first asks whether what stands on the two columns can land on one and the same motion, the second asks where each of them lands.

F57 Accountability and Duty Can Coexist. The two can land at once upon one and the same act. Their sources differ, what they bind differs, the efficacy of exit differs. To exit that registration does not of itself cancel the bearer's self-bearing under his own law; but whether a self-legislated demand bearing on some particular act continues to apply is judged by the content and the conditions within his own law.

Upon one and the same act an accountability and a duty can land at once, and the two do not exclude each other, because they stand apart on the two columns.

The employee's confidentiality is just such an overlap. The company's confidentiality clause converges upon his post; this one has its source in that convergence, what it fastens on is the post, and it is accountability. He may himself have laid down a line about not selling what has been entrusted to him; this one has its source in his own law, what it fastens on is the man, and it is duty. The two land at once upon one and the same motion, and that motion is a required forbearance: he does not speak. Where §I set the two columns out it took two different motions; what is asked here is another thing—whether what stands on the two columns can land on one and the same motion. It can.

The division shows itself only after he resigns. The accountability half no longer falls on him thereafter, along with that registration (per F49). The duty half is not held up by this registration and does not go out with it: he is still the subject that answers to its own law, and self-bearing at that layer is conditioned on the persistence of no external registration whatever.

That self-bearing at the meta-layer is not conditioned on the persistence of that registration does not amount to saying that every particular demand inside his own law runs on of itself. Whether the line about not speaking still applies to him after he leaves is judged inside his own law: whether, when he laid that line down, he confined it to the term of employment; whether that matter is still a thing entrusted to him once he has left. The answers to these two questions are not given by the termination of the accountability, and they are not given here. The meta-layer and the particular item are two layers, and neither can stand in for the other.

One counter-question must be caught first: if the inward duty can already require the bearer not to take others for instruments, why establish accountability at all.

The answer lies in the difference of source and of the efficacy of exit. The inward duty has its source in the bearer's own law and is not released by exit; there is no exiting into not being the subject of that law. Accountability has its source in the power that converges upon his position and is released by exit; to exit that registration is to hold it no longer. The two can overlap upon one and the same act, while to the questions why this thing falls on him at all, and whether it still falls on him once he has left this position, the two give different answers. On the day he resigns the company's item lapses with the post, while his structure of self-bearing does not lapse with it; whether his own particular demand still applies is judged by his own law. On the one motion of not speaking one support is gone, and not necessarily the whole of it.

There is a weightier place still. Whether an accountability stands does not turn on whether there is a corresponding line inside the bearer's own law. The company requires him to make the handover before the end of the month, and that demand falls on him not because he holds that he ought to make it; §IV has judged the point already—the criterion is whether the action-vector has been objectively converged by the field of power, and not feeling, and not assent. Were the inward duty the only category to hand, those demands which have in fact landed and which the bearer does not own would have nowhere to be put: either they must be called his own law, which is to ratify an incoming demand as self-legislation; or they must be called nonexistent, while they are at work. The cell the Introduction spoke of—with a place, with a force, and with no name—is here.

F58 The Contrast with the Excluded Model of Unconditional Horizontal Duty. One contrast stands established: that model is a universal mapping, one claim answered by everyone's one duty, spread unconditionally toward all; accountability is a conditional convergence, and lands only on specific recipients. This one is structural, not verbal. The difference in the efficacy of exit has its own place elsewhere: that is the contrast between the inward duty and accountability, at F38 and F57. What the termination lemma of A16 proves is that inward self-bearing is not released by exit; it does not thereby prove that the model is likewise not released by exit on the side of the party demanded of, and for the latter to stand it must be got from the model's own stipulations. Whether withdrawal leaves a residual moral debt is not listed in this proposition as an established difference: that on the accountability side, however it ends—by exit, by completion, or by the withdrawal of recognition—termination leaves no residue of itself is laid down at F51; while the claim that the model leaves a residual debt has no source upstream, and can stand only as a conditional contrast under an added stipulation.

What this proposition sets itself against is the one model this series excludes, and not the whole company of traditional accounts of duty. Those accounts have each their own construction, and this proposition lays nothing down that ranges over them all. One contrast only stands established; the other two are each remitted to their own place, on the following grounds.

The first is directly on view in one upstream sentence. Paper 0, in answering a counter-question, has already written this difference out: power is a conditional convergence, only where it actually converges, and converging only specific recipients; whereas duty is a universal mapping, one claim answered by everyone's one duty—the one conditioned and landing upon a specific relation, the other unconditioned and spread toward all. Accountability is generated by that power-face, and is therefore, with it, a conditional convergence. The company's confidentiality demand lands only upon this cell of this one term of his employment, and lands upon no one else; whereas the duty that model speaks of is that one man has a claim, and thereupon every man gets a line of his own. This one needs no deriving; it stands within one sentence of the upstream text.

The difference in the efficacy of exit does not belong to this proposition; it belongs to F38 and F57. The two ends of that contrast are the inward duty and accountability, and each end has its own warrant. The warrant at the inward end is the termination lemma of A16: "one cannot-not legislate only for oneself", "the subject's answerability to its own law"; hence there is no exiting into not being the subject of that law—this last sentence is a restatement made here from the termination lemma, and is not the upstream's own wording. The warrant at the accountability end is the upstream passage on the unborn beneficiary of a trust: the recipient holds a liability, subject to the conditioned power's demand, "bearing it if it does not exit, and no longer holding the right if it does." That passage states both that the recipient holds a liability and that on exiting the trust relation the right is held no longer; it does not yet use this study's concept of accountability. What is drawn on here is the structure of exiting one and the same relation, and the loss of that right is not on its own re-read as a release from accountability. Accountability in this study is fastened by F38 to a particular registration and points by F46 to an answering load with content; where that registration has in fact been exited, the running load that stood upon it terminates by F49. Brought together upon that employee it runs thus: he resigns, and the registration of that confidentiality accountability terminates, while he cannot resign his standing as the subject of his own law.

This contrast cannot be moved onto the face of the present proposition. The face of this proposition is that excluded horizontal model, whereas what the termination lemma proves is inward self-bearing; and the sources of the two already stand apart within F38—the inward source in the bearer's own law, the model's source in the pushing-outward of some subject's inner law. That one subject cannot exit into not being the subject of his own law does not yield that another cannot exit the demand the first has pushed outward onto him. Should the model likewise not be released by exit, it must be got from the model's own stipulations—its being stipulated, say, as conditioned on the persistence of no registered relation whatever; and that is a reading of a stipulation, not a property the upstream has checked. This study does not borrow the termination lemma to make the case for it.

Whether withdrawal leaves a residual moral debt is likewise not listed as an established difference. That the termination of the original accountability generates no sourceless moral debt of itself—that half is laid down at §IV's F51, and F51 never needed some other model's necessarily leaving a residual debt in order to stand. The other half stands otherwise: it has no source in the published papers of the rights spectrum, and the word residue does not occur once in those texts. One may of course stipulate a model of one's own and set it beside this one; but two standings must be kept apart. To posit a model that still retains a residual moral debt after withdrawal is a conditional contrast under an added stipulation; to say that the excluded model is so of itself still requires that model's own warrant. To draw the thing contrasted into a shape convenient for the contrast, and then to declare on that ground that a difference stands, is a contrast that proves itself. So the writing here is conditional only: if some other model does so stipulate, then this study differs from it upon this point.

What F51 actually reaches is a class of question like this: he resigns with one item of the handover unfinished, and upon whom that matter falls thereafter is judged, at F51 and F60, by their several sources and registrations; the sentence the original accountability has terminated will not catch it, and a residual moral debt conjured out of nothing will not catch it either.

That one has changed an English word does not of itself prove an escape from every duty-model, and the Introduction has said as much. It can be put more exactly here: what this proposition establishes is that one limited contrast and no more, the rest are each remitted to their own place, and whether the escape holds is carried by the division of the four positions, and not by the number of items in this section.

VII. The Accountability of a Subject-to-Be

On the side of the subject-to-be no separate scheme of accountability is erected. The criteria of the first six sections are applied as they stand, and the difficulty lies at one point only: gate-state and where a registration belongs look like one and the same thing, and they are two. These two are divided first, and then that batch of unhandled matters left once network-exercise has terminated.

Only a subject-to-be whose standing already stands falls here. One whose very qualification as a subject-to-be is not yet settled lies outside the jurisdiction.

F59 Where a Registration Belongs During the Phase of Network-Exercise Is Judged Item by Item. What the gate's coming to self-exercise alters is the manner in which the corresponding answering function is carried; it does not of itself settle to whom an accountability belongs. Each demand must have its content, the answering-space actually converged, and the registered bearer checked separately, and is not settled alone by who is able to answer for himself or by who actually exercises in another's stead. If the exercising network is in fact converged on account of that demand and the registration is completed upon it, the corresponding accountability lies with the network. If the subject-to-be itself, within the compass in which the gate is self-exercised, acquires the corresponding source, content, and registration, the corresponding accountability lies with the subject-to-be. Several demands about one and the same subject-to-be may belong to different bearers, and no migration is made in a batch. Whether an old accountability terminates and whether a new one stands are judged separately, by source, registration, content, and compass of application.

Gate-state says how answering is realized; where a registration belongs says who, within this relation, bears that demand. The former may be a relevant condition of the latter; it does not by itself stand in for the latter's checking.

The structure of the three gate-states is settled upstream (On Rights and Entitlements F33 and F34), and this study only points back to it and does not reopen it. The negation-gate divides into three states—no gate, network-exercise, and self-exercise—and what migrates with the gate is the operating host of a recognition-based right, while the standing-host of a basic right remains with the subject-to-be itself throughout, the gate's first-person belonging never transferring, and what the network exercises in its stead being the gate's function only. The upstream has lit both ends of this line and its middle stretch alike; here one thing only is asked further, a thing it did not ask: along that stretch of network-exercise, of those demands that land, with whom does the accountability register.

Answering-space admits two readings, and only the second is taken here. If it means what capacity to answer someone has at this moment, or who is doing the answering in fact, it cannot settle belonging by itself, because §I has already allowed a construct to hold accountability while the answering is realized through particular nodes: the one who actually carries out and the registered bearer need not be the same bearer. If it means the action-space that this demand actually converges and in which it is registered, then it can be applied item by item under F36 and F46; and to whom that space belongs must still be checked out from the demand's addressee, its source, and the fact of registration, and is not allotted in advance by whose gate has come to self-exercise.

A child's gate is passing from network-exercise to self-exercise, and along this stretch several demands commonly hang at once. The arrangement the guardianship network has brought off between the child and a school makes a demand that converges the position the network occupies, and the registration falls on the network too; the accountability lies with the network. An engagement the child has separately taken up within the compass in which its gate is self-exercised: if source, content, and registration are all three present, that item's accountability lies with the child. The two stand at once, do not conflict, and do not carry each other along. To ask to whom an accountability belongs is to look at that item's own three things, and not at how far along the gate-line this child has come as a whole.

Several demands about one and the same subject-to-be may belong to different bearers, and no migration is made in a batch. The gate's coming to self-exercise over some compass is not a removal that carries that batch of demands over from the network to the person at one stroke. Whether an old accountability terminates and whether a new one stands are each judged by their own source, registration, content, and compass of application.

Along that stretch of network-exercise three things each settle their own fate: the agency accountability that forms within a relational network because the subject-to-be exists and is actually carried by the network's nodes; the network's own accountability, carried because it has itself taken up positions of care, of representation, of protection; and the capacity to answer for its own position that the subject-to-be newly forms as the gate comes by degrees to self-exercise. These three are divided so that each may be seen to settle its own fate, and not in order to give three rules of disposal. Disposal goes in every case by source-tracing under F38, and is not divided by class. No opening may be made by class: allow an old arrear to be sorted into some class and thereby skirt the checking of source, and that opening will eat the whole of source-tracing.

Registration, too, is not conditioned on the subject-to-be's own assent. If the subject-to-be, once the gate has come to self-exercise, acquires an accountability upon a new registration, that accountability does not take its subjective assent to the power for a condition of its standing; so long as the corresponding conditions of constitution and of application remain in force, the demand is borne accordingly. The warrant is the upstream ruling on registration (P3 F13): a registration may run with the grain or under compulsion, and is not consent; the criterion is whether the action-vector has been objectively converged by the field of power, and not feeling, and not assent. In one sentence together: not assenting does not by itself make an accountability already standing disappear; not having exited does not by itself make an accountability come into being, or run on forever.

What is ruled out here is only the subject-to-be's own assent as a necessary condition; the source-structure of a recognition-based right is not thereby cancelled. Psychological assent and constitutive recognition must not be swallowed up by one general word recognition. Exit is here still efficacy and not availability: if exit occurs it is held no longer, while whether a channel of exit is available at that position is judged by P4 F18 and P5, and does not belong to the division drawn here.

The judgment of belonging is this study's own charge. The upstream supports the separate accounting of standing-host, operating host, and the gate's function; it does not add a new classification for accountability, nor does it complete a determination of belonging automatically out of gate-state. What is taken from it is the form of checking item by item, and no substantive rule that allots belonging by gate-state.

F60 The Termination of a Delegated Accountability Allots No Unfinished Matter to a Successor. This proposition handles only those accountabilities actually registered and carried by the exercising network. Once network-exercise terminates, whether the item can fall upon a successor goes by source-tracing under F38. Before source-tracing is complete, no past of having been exercised for, no fact of having benefited, and no occupancy of a position taken over may stand in for the checking of source. Where source-tracing returns that neither a present source nor a present registration stands, and where there is no other independently standing arrangement of succession, it is not registered as the successor's accountability. Successor includes the subject-to-be itself, once the gate is self-exercised and it has acquired the answering-space upon that same item. That the demand did once stand, that the non-performance did once occur, and the matters still unhandled are each preserved separately, and are not erased by the termination of the present accountability. For those particular matters among them for which no standing relation of succession yet exists, this study lists them as reserved items. Such a reservation does not certify them as accountabilities still running, nor does it prejudge that a new source can never be formed otherwise.

When network-exercise terminates, the matters left unfinished neither fall automatically upon the successor nor thereby disappear. One error is to use the termination to wipe the old accounts away, the other is to push the old accounts onto whoever takes over, and the two sentences block one each.

Three objects must be told apart. The first is the historical fact that something once stood and once went unperformed; the absence of a present bearer is not enough to cancel it, and exit does not alter history. The second is a still identifiable unmet matter, effect, or content of demand; it is not cancelled of itself, while the party asserting that it exists must say what in particular is left. The third is a demand that still holds the status of an accountability at this moment; it must have the present source, content, position, and registration this study has fixed, and cannot be derived automatically from the first two. To lose the present status of an accountability is not never to have occurred; and to have occurred in the past is not for there still to be, at present, an object of that same status.

Some matter the guardianship network ought to have done for that child during the phase of network-exercise and did not bring off falls just among these three. That it did stand and did go unperformed is the first. That the matter is still not done is the second. And whether, once the gate has come to self-exercise, it becomes an accountability of the child's own is a question for the third: is there now a power converging the child's present position that issues this matter as a demand, is there content, is there a registration. Three present, and it is a new accountability; one lacking, and it is not registered. That the child has taken over its own position is not that it has taken over that item.

Source-tracing is here applied as it stands. What matters is that limit: before source-tracing is complete, no past of having been exercised for, no fact of having benefited, and no occupancy of a position taken over may stand in for the checking of source. That a demand speaks of an old matter from the phase of network-exercise does not yield that it can issue only from the old relation, nor that it has a source as a matter of course; to have benefited from that arrangement does not prove that a demand now falls upon the beneficiary; and taking over a position is less than enough to prove that a source stands, because a position, per F37, is a single registration event, and not a vessel on which loads may be hung.

If source-tracing checks out that neither a present source nor a present registration stands, and there is no other independently standing arrangement of succession, then it is not registered as the successor's accountability. Successor here includes the child itself: the subject-to-be that has acquired the answering-space upon that same item once the gate is self-exercised must pass this same test, and the matter does not fall to it as a matter of course because it was done for it in the first place.

Not registered is not that the matter is thereby settled up. It is listed as a reserved item, which means acknowledging that it exists without pretending that it already has a bearer. Its actual landing point is an unredeemed record in the name of the original exercising network and of the construct that network sits in, and not a demand awaiting settlement left hanging over a successor's head.

This handling has a reason at the level of what there is: an accountability must be held by a recipient. When network-exercise terminates and the source in power no longer persists, then as regards the successor subject the thing has simply not become an accountability at all, rather than having become an accountability that no one bears; and at the same time the historical fact of its non-performance and the identifiable unmet matter exist objectively, and do not disappear for want of a bearer. The two sentences stand side by side and do not cancel each other.

Two mis-hits are to be avoided. The first: if that matter has an independent source of its own, it is judged by that source's own conditions and does not fall here; suppose a burden upon some thing itself constitutes an independent demand upon whoever now holds the thing, whether it stands is judged by that source, and what this proposition cancels is only the step of skipping the checking of source on the ground of a position taken over. The second: to have confirmed that there is no present bearer, and not to have ascertained as yet who or what kind of organization bears it, are two different things; the latter is an unsettled state of materials or of identification, and may not be registered straight off as the former. And §I has already allowed a construct to hold accountability: not to have landed upon some individual is not to have landed upon no bearer at all.

One opponent is blocked on each side. If the original construct declares that the exercising party has withdrawn and that therefore the facts which have occurred and the plainly unhandled matters vanish where they stand, that is a false closure; the original accountability may indeed terminate, and what is opposed is the erasing of the other objects by that termination, and not the genuine ending of an accountability. If it declares that the exercising party has withdrawn and that therefore the successor must take the matters up, that is a shifting of the burden. An accountability may genuinely end, and its ending clears no one's accounts for him.

F61 No Hard Binary Threshold Is Set. The compass in which the gate is self-exercised settles which answering functions may be carried by the subject-to-be itself; it does not directly move an accountability already standing. Where the generation, the continuance, and the termination of an accountability are concerned, the judgment is still made item by item under F59. This process of demarcation cannot be replaced by a dead threshold externally fixed, non-rebuttable, and cross-situational.

The compass in which the gate is self-exercised and the moving of an accountability are not one thing. The first says which answering functions may be carried by the subject-to-be itself; the second must ask after source, content, and registration; and between the two there runs no automatic channel.

The upstream has given a warning upon that line (On Rights and Entitlements F34a): to lay upon a subject-to-be standing on the continuous stretch between network-exercise and self-exercise a binary threshold externally fixed, non-rebuttable, and used uniformly across situations, so as to draw once and for all which of its things count as entitlements and which as recognition-based rights, produces a systematic risk of misclassification; and the elaboration of that same entry puts the risk harder: a dead threshold laid across a gradual and moving line must, at some point and for some child of some rate of maturation, land on the wrong side of the line. That both sentences come out of one entry establishes their provenance; it does not establish that the two are of equal strength. The first is a warning that such a threshold cannot be exempted from misclassification; the second, under the relation it treats between continuous change and a fixed threshold, asserts in addition that at least one misclassification occurs. This study takes that boundary and does not take that step of strengthening, and does not re-read the stronger sentence as wrong in every case. What must be read as it stands is the boundary the warning draws: the upstream writes expressly in the same place that a threshold which can be overturned by the facts of a particular case, which judges matter by matter, and which preserves appeal and review does not fall here—it does not necessarily misclassify. What is restricted is a dead threshold, and not every clear judgment.

That step of strengthening is not made a footing of this study either. By F59, gate-state is in any case not the complete criterion for where an accountability belongs; and even were some threshold, within some compass, to judge gate-state rightly, it would not thereby give the source and the registration of each accountability. Hold the checking item by item, and the hard threshold needs no separate disproof.

The corresponding disposal is therefore negative: no hard binary threshold is set, rather than a finer threshold set in its place. How far along the gate-line that child has come, and which accountabilities lie on it at this moment and with whom each of them belongs, are two strings of things, each to be checked on its own. To cut once across by age, by some test, by the judgment of some single node, is to replace precisely the checking item by item that F59 requires. This process of demarcation keeps to the form of item by item, and hands itself over to no dead threshold whatever.

VIII. Closing, and the Reserved Items

The cells on the converged side are by this point all in place. Liability governs bearing, accountability governs answering, an act is the discharge of answering; and with the one inward duty that remains, four words each hold a cell of their own, and none stands in for another.

All four are present at once upon that employee. He lies within the compass the company may dispose of: that is liability. The company requires him to make the handover by its date and requires him to keep confidence throughout: those are two accountabilities. He makes the handover, he does not speak: those are acts. The line he laid down for himself, that he will not go through the motions: that is duty. Four things land upon one and the same man without running together, and more than one ruler divides them. Duty and accountability are divided by the two columns, source and what is bound. Liability and accountability have the same source and stand at the same position; what divides them is the difference between the bearing of a position and the content of a particular answering. Accountability and act are divided upon the difference between a demand and its discharge. Three rulers, each governing one join; and only together do they keep this cell from collapsing into a single word.

There is one further thing this study has not done, and it must be said in the open at the close: it classifies, it does not issue legitimacy. A load's being sorted as accountability gains it no endorsement whatever; and a demand that has crossed the boundary of the definition, and is under this study's terms no longer called accountability, does not thereby stop being enforced in the world. §V laid this down, and it is said once more here, because it is the sentence most easily dropped in citation.

The three reserved items are these.

Reserved item one · The quantity of accountability. F55 gave an interface and gave no measure. Whether a gradient stands between the compass over which power is exercised and the compass of answering, to what thickness it stands, and who is to settle it—these are empirical matters, the institutional layer has propositions of its own that handle them, and this study does not take them up.

Reserved item two · The registration of a dependency at the layer of proof. This study cites How Is Institution Possible; that paper's own text does not cite this study. If some two-sided condition at some place in that paper can in fact be raised only once the paper on the constitution and the withdrawal of recognition is in hand, that dependency must be registered as it stands; and before the independent derivation has been written out, it is not signed off in advance as already independent. Register by the actual relation of proof, and not by the order of publication.

Reserved item three · The design of protective arrangements. The criterion half the upstream has already given: a constraint counts as a limit only so long as it does not seal the subject's structural channel for exiting, refusing, or revoking a standing bond, and "Whatever crosses these is violation" (per P5 F21). So an exit written down in form while sealed off in substance the upstream has already judged a violation; this study need only cite it, and waits neither on a separate establishing here nor on some further paper downstream. The half that remains is how a protective arrangement is to be designed, such that a channel of exit written on paper is really available in actual operation. That belongs to the work of the layer of institutional design; this study does not take it up, and leaves it to the later papers that handle that layer expressly.

第一节立词与分野。F36 立词，职责是某项经三支咬合运行的承认性权利在实际收敛受效者时所显之权力面，其要求在受效者行动空间登记落地后，受效者在该格中所持的应答负荷。F37 定位置的粒度为一次登记事件。F38 以源与所束缚者两栏分开义务、职责与已删的横向义务，退出效力作所束缚者一栏的读数，并立追源三限度。F39 分持有者：只有主体能持义务，构与主体都能持职责。

第二节立三站与两链。F40 一条要求走三站，后两站同在被收敛的一侧。F41 落空之可读证要求独立于施为。F42 两链方向相反各有三站，每链发出方各须凭一项运行中的承认性权利，构发出者其权力面借位而构只作归责地址。F43 无普遍对价。

第三节分职责与责任。F44 责任是承受那一格本身，F45 责任可以空，F46 有内容的要求落进来那一格才被职责填满。

第四节分生成、完成、终止与失效。F47 职责的成立条件按承担者类型核，主体承担者四层合取，构承担者不计 T5 那一层。F48 至 F51 分处要求的完成、退出终止该登记所支撑的现行应答负荷、承认的撤回，以及终止不自动生成道德余留。F52 职责不另立失效分型，它是权利失效在受收敛一侧的读数。

第五节划界。F53 T5 为闸，而闸只管最低关系位置，且只管主体承担者。F54 不另立一类职责本身之侵。F55 界内张力是接口，不是量的定理。F56 职责的名分不区分涵育、压制与殖民。

第六节处理并存与对接。F57 职责与义务可并存。F58 与所排除的那种无条件横向义务模型的对照，已证成者一项，退出效力之别归 F38 与 F57，道德余债之别只作条件句。

第七节处理准主体的职责。F59 代行阶段的登记归属逐项判，F60 代行职责的终止不替未处理事项配给后继者，F61 不设二元硬阈。

第八节收束并列留置事项。受雇者在一家公司里的这一次任职作贯通的工作例，一条线上摆出登记、多项要求同载、载体更替、方向相反的两条链与要求落空。全篇二十六条命题，F36 至 F61。

0 导言 · 一条交接链上的残余

权利谱到 Paper 0 为止，已经把义务（duty）这个范畴处理完毕。一个主体没有对他者的义务，它只有一条朝内的义务，即对自己的法负责，那是 A16 的终止引理所给：不得不只自己给自己立法（承 Paper 0 M1）。所谓对他者的义务，是把一个主体内在的律外推成对他人的要求，那是伦理，结构上是越界，删。相关性公理所要的那样东西，在 SAE 权利论里没有宾语。

这条交接链的末端留下一格无名之位。

P6 收束时把相关性留在主干之外，交给 Paper 0，并在那里写明：本系列不以权利义务相关性立权利，基础权利无对应项，承认性权利对应的是权力，义务形的要求即那权力的要求而不名义务。Paper 0 接了这一笔并且接完了，它处理相关性公理，立五命题，在那张四对法律关系位的网里认领权能与责任那一对，不认领权利与义务那一对。M4 把传统所说的政府义务读成这项承认性权利对应的制度性权力的要求，政府是那权力的承载而不是一个义务人，而这项要求可以非常真、非常强制。

位置与强度都已立定，名字没有。那一格被指认为不是义务，被指认为权力的要求，而它在受效者那一侧叫什么，Paper 0 未给。P6 那句「而不名义务」是一次准确的否定，它所否定的是把这一格命名为义务。

相关性公理那一笔已由 Paper 0 接完，这里不动。本篇接的是它留下的那处仍待命名的位置：一格有位置、有强度而无名。给它的名是职责（accountability）。

三个旧词各已有主，中间那一格空着。义务在本谱只剩朝内一条，其源在承担者自己的法，以它称这一格，等于把源从收敛这个位置的权力搬进承担者内部，而那正是本谱删去的那一步。责任（liability）是 Paper 0 已认领的那一格，它管位置可被变更这件事本身，不管变更的内容。施为（act）是兑现，是这一格被兑现时所出的动作，不是这一格。中间那一格由本篇来填。

英文一侧不取 responsibility，亦不取 obligation。二者在通行用法里都带伦理意味，都易被读成承担者欠了谁一笔债，而这一格的源既不在承担者内部，也不在他者的权利，它在收敛这个位置的权力。accountability 是本篇明示的技术规定，其重心在应答与可被追问。但换一个英文词本身并不证明已摆脱义务模型，摆脱由四格分野承担。

全篇取一条贯通的工作例：一个受雇者在一家公司里的这一次任职。这条线上摆得出本篇所需的全部切面：一次登记、同一次登记里的几项要求、换一家公司之后的载体更替、公司与受雇者之间方向相反的两条链，以及一项到期未交付的要求。选它不为裁断任何一种雇佣安排的是非，只为把这些切面摆到同一个位置上看。第七节讲准主体那一段另取一例，一个门正在从代行迁向自行的孩子，因为雇佣这条线上没有门态这一层。

本篇不是元篇，不走出主干去对接主干之外的传统，它是主干之外补一处残余的一篇专论，守主系列的规矩：不点名理论家，不举历史事件，不引具体判例。所涉传统的位置一律回指 Paper 0 已立好的那几处，不在这里重开。

全篇二十六条命题，分八节，各节所立见摘要。

I 立词与分野

F36 职责立词。某项经三支咬合运行的承认性权利，在实际收敛受效者时所显之权力面（承 P4 F14 F15），其要求在受效者行动空间登记落地后，受效者在该格中持有的应答负荷，名职责。承《制度如何可能》立词，本篇承其根而扩其界。要求须已能识别内容，该要件见 F46。

职责的根在制度那一层已经立过：构把一项共同规则明确施用于特定成员位置，并具有相应的执行与处置机制时，这项规则在该位置上形成可要求履行的职责；而执行使要求不止于口头规定，却不独自证明其内容正当，也不使它成为成员自己的法。一家公司的保密条款落到某个岗位上，靠的是公司有权处分这个岗位；这不表示那条款的内容因此正当，也不表示受雇者从此把保密当成了自己的法。

本篇承此根而扩其界。根句里收敛的一方是构，收敛的手段是执行与处置机制，这里两者都不要求；所要求的是一般的权力收敛，即某项承认性权利在实际收敛受效者时所显之权力面。制度是这种收敛最可审计的一种形态，却不是唯一的形态。

扩界处有一道限：此处的权力取某项经三支咬合运行的承认性权利所显之权力面，不取赤裸的强力。上游已判（P4 F16），一个网络若只把施力者的命令当作必须服从的威胁，而不处理任何可主张的位置，那是强力固化，不是权利沉淀。一个绑匪对人质提出的要求，内容可以清楚可辨，人质的行动矢量确被收敛，人质也仍能回应与回绝，而那一格不是职责：没有一项运行中的承认性权利，就没有它的权力面，人质所承受的是强力。这一限不为本篇添一道道德筛子，而是把定义里的权力取在它该取的义上，完整的生成条件见 F47。

登记落地这一步也不独自成事。登记使一项要求在这个位置上实际成立，它既不提供要求的内容，也不凭自身证明要求正当；内容的来源见 F46，正当与否见 F56。一项已能识别内容的要求经这样一次登记而落在受效者的某一格上，受效者在那一格中所持者，是一份应答负荷。

应答负荷不是资产，承担者不因持有它而多出一份可主张的东西；它也不是资格，持有它不使人取得任何位格。它是这一格里立着的那个待应答的要求。

F37 位置即一次登记事件。本篇所称位置，指某一承担者在某一关系位上的一次登记，不指组织职能本身，也不单指占位的那个人。一项职责按承担者、关系位与登记事件三者共同个体化。同一位置承接多项要求时，职责再按要求内容分别个体化。

职责束缚位置，而位置一词可指三样差得很远的东西：财务主管这个职能，此刻坐在那个职能上的那个人，以及这个人在那个职能上的这一次任职。三者不分，束缚位置这句就是空的。本篇取第三种。

这是一个个体化的约定，不是从别处推出的定理，故它给得出什么、给不出什么，一并交待。它给出的只有一件：不同的登记实例不是同一项职责。一个人离职数年后再回同一家公司坐回同一个职能，那是另一次登记，他所担的是另一项职责，而不是旧职责的续行。

它不给出的有三件。每一次载体变化必有一个新的登记实例，不由它给出，那还要看该登记是否确已改变：公司被并购而岗位与合同原封不动，载体换了而登记未必换。他人的旧履行过错不自动变成本人的过错，不由它给出，那另有归属与归责的前提：前任在任内漏报的那一笔，不因后任坐上这个位置就成了后任的过错。至于任何后来的追索都不成立，更不由它给出，那要另审其来源、对象、登记与正当性。三者分别落在 F49、F51 与 F60。

粒度之下还有一层，因为一次登记可以同时载入多项要求。同一次任职里既载入一项一次性的交接要求，又载入一项持续的保密要求，二者的承担者、关系位与登记事件三元组完全相同，而交接已经办完、保密仍在续行这件事仍要说得出来。故在三元组之下，职责按要求内容再分。这不是另造一套标识系统，只是承认同一次登记里可以有几项各自走完自己一生的要求。

这个约定并不由内容必变推出。换一家公司而岗位说明书一字不改，要求的内容照旧，登记实例却已换；要求的连续性与承担实例的不连续可以并存，前一件在规则那一侧，后一件在承担那一侧。以为凡内容不变则承担亦是同一份，正是把这两侧混作了一件。

F38 源与所束缚者。义务源在承担者自己的法，束缚主体本身，退出无效。职责源在收敛这个位置的权力，束缚位置，退出有效，即退出该登记即不再持有。已删的横向义务，其来源是一个主体内在律向他者的外推；仅由这一来源定义，不判定退出效力。本节另取一种不以任何特定登记关系的存续为条件、直接约束受要求主体的限定模型，在这一附加规定下，退出某次登记不解除它；此为该模型的规定，不是 A16 对受要求他者所作的证明。三者由源与所束缚者两栏分开，不由方向分开。退出效力不是第三栏，而是所束缚者一栏的读数：束缚位置故退出该登记即不再持有，束缚主体本身故退出的动作解除不了它。

分野轴取源与所束缚者，不取横向与朝内，因为横向在本谱里已有技术义。横向义务在 Paper 0 的定义是把一个主体内在的律外推成对他人的要求，这是一个关于来源的定义。若以横向与朝内作分野轴，就要说职责是横向的；而职责的源既不在承担者内部，也不由谁的内在律外推，它在收敛这个位置的权力。那样用横向，是借它的日常义而丢它在本谱里的技术义，分野会在最要紧的地方失效。

Paper 0 另有一段讲相关性义务的对照，说它是普遍的映射，一项主张对应所有人的一条要求，无条件铺向所有人。那一段与横向义务的定义不是一处：前者刻画对照对象的形状，后者给来源。两处不并作一处，对照对象的形状留 F58。

那位受雇者身上正好并着两样东西。公司要他按期交接，这项要求从收敛他这个岗位的权力发出，扣住的是岗位；他自己立下不以敷衍了事的那一条，从他自己的法发出，扣住的是他本人。他辞职，前一项此后不再落到他身上；而后一项，辞职解除不了。已删的横向义务则是第三种：它从某个主体内在律的外推发出。这一来源只说它由谁的法推出来，不说它随不随某个关系终止，两问不是一件事。本节所取的那个限定模型另有一条附加规定，即不以任何特定登记关系的存续为条件，在这一规定之下，退出该登记同样不能解除它。两栏一摆，三者各占一格，不必借方向。

退出效力与退出可用性是两件事，分野项是效力。义务退出无效，指退出这个动作解除不了它，因为按终止引理，一个主体不得不只自己给自己立法，无从退出为不是那条法的主体。职责退出有效，指若退出该登记即不再持有。两句都只说若退出则如何，都不断言总有一条退出通道可走。

可用性另有其门。上游已写明，严格意义的退出权预设一个被登记或被承认的关系位置，无这样的位置，可能有逃离、拒绝或脱身，而无退出权；一条退出通道是否被封死，则按 P5 F21 的不可越界线判侵。可用性归上游那两处，效力归本栏。

三个看似的反例由此化解。受宪制收敛的政府不能单方退出，未成年人不能退出监护安排，羁押者在物理信道上没有退出通道，三者皆无可用性；而三者的效力俱在，若那个位置真的终止，相应的那一格确实不再持有，故三者仍是职责。反例之所以看似成立，是把可退出读成了必有退出通道，即把效力读成了可用性。

义务一侧的退出无效，读得太宽同样会错。它所指的是以自己的法自我承担这个结构，不是这条法里曾出现的每一项具体目的或判断永久不可修订。那位受雇者后来认为当年立下的某条自我要求过苛而改掉它，这不是退出了自我承担，那正是在自我承担里做的事。

退出效力不另立第三栏，因为它不独立。设若立它，那一栏的每一格都能由所束缚者一栏读出：在本节已固定的三种技术义之内，束缚位置者退出该登记即不再持有，束缚主体本身者退出的动作解除不了它。一栏若在每一格上与另一栏同值，它分不出任何两者，立它只是把同一件事写两遍。职责那一半另有上游落点：Paper 0 讲信托未出世受益人时明写，受效者持一个责任，受制于附条件的权力要求，不退出则承担，退出则不再持有。那里讲的是责任，尚未用本篇的职责概念；此处取用的是退出同一关系这一结构，不把失去那项权利单独换读成免除职责。本篇的职责按 F46 指向有内容的应答负荷并系于特定登记，该登记确已退出时，依它成立的现行负荷按 F49 终止。义务那一半则须终止引理支撑，两处出处一并见 F58。

追源另立一步。凡问一项要求能否落在某人身上，先问收敛该人当前位置的那项权力里有没有它。追源有三条限度，缺一条它就不再是追源，而是一个预判的别名。

其一，内容回指旧事，不独自决定当前来源。前任漏报的那一笔谈的是旧事，这不推出它只能由旧关系发出；公司当前有权处分这个岗位，完全可以在后任的位置上另立一项清理旧账的要求。以旧负荷为内容，既不单独证明当前要求有源，也不单独证明它无源。

其二，一个位置的结束，不证明设立它的权力也已消失。那位受雇者离职之后，公司与它的监察安排照旧存在，是否另有处理先前事项的关系，按 F49 与 F51 判。故只能说若来源也确已不存续，不能说位置终止所以来源不存续。

其三，接管事实本身不足以证明来源成立。位置按 F37 是一次登记事件，不是可挂负荷的容器；但接管是否正是某项既有安排所规定的登记触发条件，须依那项安排判。若聘任合同本就写明后任承接一切未结事项，那就是安排规定的触发，不在此条排除之列。

追源不是先按要求内容断定它没有来源，再把这个预判称作追源。追源能识别偷换，却不预先保证对任何内容都给出同一个否定答案。仅以代行经历、受益事实或接管位置省去核源这一步，不足以判一项职责成立。

F39 持有者之别。只有主体能持义务，构与主体都能持职责。义务的源必在承担者内部，构无内部之源。职责的源在收敛这个位置的权力，构可被收敛。

义务的源必在承担者内部，而构无内部之源，故构不能持义务；职责的源在收敛这个位置的权力，而构可被收敛，故构能持职责。公司受监管机构收敛，它在那一格上持职责；而公司立不出一条自己给自己的法，它没有那个内部之源。

构持职责，指要求可以组织或制度位置为归责地址，其回应、拒绝、执行与修订须由相应的节点和程序实现。这不授予构一个独立的第一人称内部源，也不把这份职责自动分摊为全体成员的同一义务。归责地址落在公司，而实际动手的是合规部门的某几个人，两者不是一回事。

构在发出那一侧同样只作归责地址。构不自持承认性权利，而一项由构发出的要求仍可生职责，因为那一侧的权力面是借位的，构在那条链上作的也是地址，不是持有端。第二节 F42 是这一招的另一面。构在两侧均不是承认性权利的自持端；而在受收敛的一侧，构仍可作职责的登记承担者，持职责一语取本条已规定的组织归责之义，不因此取得第一人称的内部源。受收敛、承担职责的一侧，构可作归责地址；发出的一侧，构也只作归责地址。

三件是分开的，混则出两种相反的错：哪些节点实际执行组织的工作是一件，公司失责以后由哪些节点处理调查、说明与补救是第二件，哪一个成员因何种独立根据承担个人职责、责任或不利后果是第三件。前两件依赖人员与程序实现，不自动证明第三件。

两种相反的错各挡一次。构的名义不得遮蔽已有独立根据的个人责任，构不作为阻隔个人承担的外壳；构失责亦不得径把那份负荷转记到某一个成员，构内章程不是唯一根据。第三件须有自己的来源、权限、登记与行为事实。

一项要求若穿透到构内的自然人，并使那个自然人的主体位置清零，则在那个自然人处读 T5 闸击穿，不在构处读。闸的辖域第五节另划。

凡需要内部源的谓词只能加在构内的成员身上，不能加在构上。此句在本篇是一条归责纪律，管的是处理归责时不把需要内部源的谓词错加到构上，不作已证的无限语言定理。

II 三站与两链

第一节给出定义与分野。本节量一条要求从发出到兑现所经的路，并问两个节点之间方向相反的两条要求是否互为对价。

F40 一条要求走三站。发出为某项承认性权利权力面的要求，登记落地为职责，以相应施为兑现。后两站同在被收敛的一侧，不分属两方。

三站是同一条要求的三个状态，不是三条彼此不相干的要求。

第一站在权力一侧：一项承认性权利在实际收敛受效者时显出它的权力面，要求由此发出。此处的权力仍取第一节所限之义，不指任何一种社会力量；若第一站上只有一股赤裸的力而无这样一项权利，落下的是强力，三站没有起点。第二站在被收敛一侧：要求在受效者的行动空间里登记落地，他在该格中所持的那份应答负荷，即职责。第三站仍在被收敛一侧：职责以相应的施为兑现。

公司要那位受雇者在月底前交接完毕，三站在这条要求上一次走完：公司据以处分这个岗位的那项权利显出权力面而发出要求，要求在他的行动空间里落地而成为他的职责，他交接而职责兑现。

第二站与第三站同在一侧。把要求摆在一方而把兑现摆在另一方，职责就成了甲方手里的一张凭据，而施为成了乙方欠甲方的一次交付，那是债权债务的形状。职责与施为都在受效者身上，一个是立着的负荷，一个是这份负荷的兑现；分属两方的只有第一站与后两站，而那一分是权力面与受收敛面之分。

权力所发的那个要求与受效者所持的那一格，是同一条要求而不是同一样东西。同一条要求，因为走三站的自始至终只有这一条；不是同一样东西，因为两站落在不同的侧，第一站读出的是一项权利的权力面所发之求，第二站读出的是一份应答负荷。说成同一样东西，会把职责放进公司手里，而它在受雇者手里；说成两条不相干的要求，则会去找第二条的另一个来源，而它没有另一个来源。

三站不穷尽施为。发出要求本身可以是一项施为，而兑现还可能是一次应有的不作为，保密就是这样兑现的。三站分的是一条要求的状态，不是给施为下定义。

F41 落空之可读证要求独立于施为。在要求已经独立成立的前提下，未兑现不取消要求。若要求即是施为，施为未发生便只是没有那件事，不会有一个东西可以落空。

设一项职责已经成立而承担者未兑现，此时这项要求落空。落空这个读数要能成立，须有一个东西还立着；若要求即是施为本身，施为未发生便只是那件事没有发生，不会剩下一个可以落空的东西。月底到了而他没有交接，交接这个动作没有发生，而那项要求并未随之消失。落空之可读，本身即证要求与施为是两样。

此推论单向。仅凭失望或期待落空，不足以断定本来有一项职责。一位同事盼着他把某份资料一并整理出来而没有等到，这只证明那位同事的期待落了空，不证明那里曾有一项有源、有内容、已登记的要求。落空可读出要求的独立，而要求是否成立，仍按第一节那一套核。

落空与另一种形近的读数一字之差，而结论正相反。落空是要求立着而施为未发生，它是职责立着的证据。另一种是这样：那个岗位的职责说明原封不动地挂着，而公司早已不再处分它，无人过问也无从追问，承担者在位而无职责可谈。本篇称后者为无实收敛，其位置在第四节。落空处有职责，无实收敛处没有。

F42 两链方向相反，各有三站。两链若分别成立，则持位者的承认性权利收敛承载，承载持职责出施为；承载一侧所凭承认性权利的权力面收敛持位者，持位者持职责出施为。每链发出方各须凭一项经三支咬合运行的承认性权利，其权力面才生该链之职责。发出方为主体时自持该权利。发出方为构时，构不自持承认性权利，其权力面借位于调用该装置的主体所持之权利（承权力论 P2 M2）；构为该链的归责地址，自持一语只对主体发出方成立，而所借之权利仍须经三支咬合运行，此要件不因借位而放宽。两链各有各的构成条件，谁也不是谁的价钱。

同样两个节点之间可以有两条方向相反的链。第一条从持位者出发：他的承认性权利其权力面收敛承载方，承载方在那一格上持职责，并以施为兑现。第二条从承载方出发：承载方一侧所凭承认性权利的权力面收敛持位者，持位者在那一格上持职责，并以施为兑现。两链各自具有三站结构，方向相反而结构同形。公司与受雇者之间正是这样两条：一条以按期计酬兑现，另一条以交接与保密兑现。

第二条链上有一处不可省：承载一侧亦须凭一项经三支咬合运行的承认性权利，其权力面才生这一链的职责。这一侧若拿出的只是一股社会力量，而网络并不处理任何可主张的位置，该链上落下的是强力而非职责，它不成其为本节所说的链。

这里要分清凭与自持。承认性权利是主体所持之物，要份位才持得（承《论权利与权益》F27 段与 P1 F4），而构没有那道否定门，也就没有份位可持。一家公司不自持一项承认性权利。它之所以仍能发出一项生职责的要求，另有上游给的读法：装置不施权，是个体调用装置施权，装置的拓扑学合法性是借位的，不是自有的（承权力论 P2 M2）。公司不裁员，是关键节点上的管理者通过公司这个装置裁员，那一篇举的正是这个例；公司要那位受雇者交接，也是某个持有相应权利的位置凭公司这个装置发出的。构在链上作的是归责地址，不是持有端。

借位不放宽任何要件。所借的那一项仍须经三支咬合运行，否则借无可借：一股没有任何主体持有相应权利作底的赤裸要求，借不到位，落下的仍是强力。此要件在两链同格，不因承载一侧是一个构而放宽；至于被借的那项权利如何成立，按它自己的来源与登记核，不在本条。构在持有一侧如何作归责地址，见第一节。

每链各须有一项运行中的权利作底，这一要件并不使一链的成立以另一链为条件。每链所要求的是它自己那一侧发出方所凭的权利，两链各向自己一头要条件，谁也没向对方要。故两链可以一条成立而另一条不成立，亦可两条俱成立而彼此不为对价。

F43 无普遍对价。承认性权利不由承认权力换来。一链职责的发生、继续与终止，依该链自身的内容和条件判断，不因另一链已经履行就自动生成，也不因另一链未履行就自动免除。具体安排是否把某项事实作为适用条件，另按该安排判断。

交易式的读法有一个完整的形状：受效者承认了对方的权力，以此换来自己那一侧的权利，于是权利是承认权力的对价，而职责是持有权利的代价。这个形状顺理成章，也确实描出了许多具体安排的样子，而作为一条普遍规则不成立。承认性权利的成立条件上游已给定，它经构成式承认取得内容身份，再经制度载入、共同体有效承认与真实权力支持三支咬合而进入运行，这一路没有一步是以承认对方的权力为对价交出；而权力本就靠登记成立，不需一层承认性的修饰才生效。普遍的兑换口因此并不存在。

本条挡住的是两个自动。公司按期付清了它那一链的职责，这件事本身不自动在他那一链上生出一项新的职责，要生出来，那一链自己得有源、有内容、有登记。公司欠着薪不付，这件事本身也不自动免掉他那一链上已成立的保密职责，要免掉，也得在那一链自己的条件里找到理由。

挡住两个自动，不等于宣布一切条件关系不存在。合同可以明写计酬到位是某项交付要求的适用条件，那时两者之间确有条件关系；而那个条件关系来自合同，由合同的内容和条件判，不来自一条把权利与职责普遍对举的规则。

III 职责与责任

第一节把职责与义务分开，本节把它与责任分开。二者同在受收敛的一侧，最易被当成一件事的两个名字。

F44 责任是承受那一格本身。受权力收敛者持有的责任，受制于那权力的要求。责任说的是位置可被变更，不说变更的内容。它不以过错为条件，也不以有无退出通道为条件。

责任是 Paper 0 已认领的那一格，本篇照它取用，只把射程说准。

它管的是位置可被变更这件事本身。一个受权力收敛者持有责任，意思是他的关系位置处在那项权力的射程里，那项权力一动，他的位置随之变。至于变成什么样，那是另一问。

两个条件不进这一格。

其一，责任不以过错为条件，持有它的人可以什么都没做错。恶债那一例 Paper 0 已经立起，此处只回指：一个政权倒台之后，新的政权与人民可以宣布前政权所借的债无效，债权人的法律状态因此被变更；而这里起作用的不是要债权人负什么义务，是一种变更系统法律状态的权力。债权人无所失职，他所持的是责任。

其二，责任不以有无退出通道为条件。Paper 0 写责任那一句时带着「不退出则承担」，本篇的一般定义不带这一语。理由不在那句话错，而在它极易被读成的那一层：承担以退出通道的可用为前提。恶债那一例正好排除这一读：债权人相对的是一项变更法律状态的权力，而设其无退出通道可用，责任仍成立。无退出通道一语是本篇为展示而明设的附加条件，不是上游所查明的事实，上游只取该例的结构位置，未曾论及债权人有无退出通道；本篇也不因此断言那里不存在任何登记位置，更不对任何现实债务关系作判断。一个明示的设例足以承担这一展示。退出的可用性按第一节所分归上游两处，责任这一格不受它牵动。

F45 责任可以空。恶债例中债权人持一个责任，而无所须为，其法律状态被变更而已。故责任不以有内容的要求为条件。

责任可以是空的。债权人持一个责任，而没有任何事情要他去做：不必交付，不必回应，不必申报，他的法律状态被别人的权力变更了，如此而已。

空的责任仍是责任，因为那一格说的本就是位置可被变更，不是位置上有什么要做。这一点是下一条的地基。若责任必带内容，责任与职责就分不开。

F46 有内容的要求落进来，那一格才被职责填满。职责指向一项已能识别要求内容、适用位置与兑现条件的要求。登记给它在该位置上的实际成立，不独自提供内容，也不凭自身证明要求正当。责任管承受，职责管应答，二者同在受收敛一侧，不是一格的两个说法。

那一格被有内容的要求填满时，它成为职责。

内容从规则那一侧来。那位受雇者的保密条款已经说清了什么算作应守之事、在什么范围内守、违反时按什么程序处理，这些不是登记给的。登记做的是另一件事：使这项已经说清的要求，在他这一次任职的位置上实际成立。规则说清什么，登记使它落在谁身上，两件事分属两处。登记也不因此证明那条款的内容正当，正当与否见第五节。

责任管承受，职责管应答。前者是位置可被变更，后者是位置上立着一项待应答的要求。二者同在受收敛的一侧，而不是一格的两个说法：若是两个说法，空的责任就无从存在，而它存在。

职责也不脱离责任。一项要求落在某人身上，而他的位置全然不受那项权力牵动，这种事没有；能被要求，正因为位置在射程之内。所以每一项职责都预设一个责任，而不是每一个责任都被填成职责。

IV 生成、完成、终止与失效

四件事各有各的门，装进同一个问题就会互相顶撞：一项具体要求依其内容完成或终结，一个承担位置依退出而终止，支持其成立的关系按上游的断点失效，而在这三件之先，还有职责如何生成。本节依次处理。

F47 职责的成立条件按承担者类型核。判据是行动矢量是否被权力场客观收敛，不在情绪，不在认同，登记可顺向可受迫（承 P3 F13）。在 F46 所定的要求内容、适用位置与兑现条件已可识别的前提下，须核相应承认性权利的构成式承认与三支咬合运行，以及该要求在受效者行动空间中的实际登记。受效者属 F53 所说的主体承担者时，另须核 T5 所保留的最低关系位置，此时成立条件为四层合取；受效者为构时，不把构不具有主体位置计为 T5 未通过，其承担与界限依 F39 与 F53 判断。各层不读在同一侧：行动空间登记与 T5 槽位读在受效者一侧，内容身份与运行效力是所对之承认性权利自身的属性，读在持该权利一侧。登记独自不给内容。就首次生成而言，所须条件未曾齐备则职责未成立；曾齐备而后来跌破者，不在此列，按 F52 等条读取。

四层不是本篇拼出来的。上游写 T5 接缝时已把它们并列：T5 给最低关系槽位，构成式承认给内容身份，三支咬合给运行效力；而行动空间的登记与 T5 的主体位置保留，上游也早已切成两件事。本篇所加的只是标出每一层读在哪一侧。

标侧不是修辞。前两层是受效者身上的事实：他的行动矢量确被那个权力场收敛，而他仍被保留为可回应、可拒绝、可撤回登记的主体位置。后两层不在他身上，是那项承认性权利自己的属性：它由构成式承认取得内容身份，由三支咬合取得运行效力。承担者为主体时，核验两侧各核两层，取证的地方不同，缺一皆不生职责；承担者为构时，T5 那一层不适用，其余三层照核。不标侧，各层会被读成受收敛一侧的一张检查表，于是受效者这边齐备就被当成全部齐备。

那位受雇者的岗位手册里若印着一条谁也没有真正施用于他这个位置的条款，缺的是登记那一层。若那条要求所在的作用域从未取得三支咬合所需的运行支持，缺的是运行效力那一层。两种缺法都不生职责，而它们与失效不是一回事：失效是曾经成立而后跌破，生成条件未满足是从未成立。说某个部门早已无人过问、公司也不再对它动用任何处分，这还不够：没有实际动用处分，不单凭自身证明处分支持本不存在，也不排除它曾经存在而后来跌破；后者正是 F52 所说的失效。要让一个例子确实缺这一层，须明设或查明真实支持从未取得。

缺了这一层，落下的是什么，还要看断在哪一支，两支由 P3 F10 分开。缺三支咬合所给的运行效力，所能断定的只有一件：不成立职责。若同时仍有施力，而网络不处理任何可主张的位置，则按第一节所述那一情形读作强力（承 P4 F16）；若连实际收敛也已不在，则不因无职责反推仍有强力。一项职责成立的必要条件未满足，足以排除该职责，却不因此证成强力。

F48 要求依其内容完成或终结。一项已登记的一次性交付要求，交付完成即已兑现，而角色、权利与权力支持皆可继续存在。内容上预定的期限届满、条件消失或要求修订，同样不必是任何一种失效。

他交接完毕，那一项要求就完成了。完成之后，他的岗位还在，公司处分这个岗位的权利还在，那项权利的权力支持也还在。一项要求的了结不牵动它所依附的那一整套。

期限届满、条件消失与要求修订，三者同理。约定只到年底的那一项，年底一到便终结；因某个条件成立才适用的那一项，条件消失便不再适用；被改写过的那一项，此后按新内容走。这些都在要求自己的内容里写着，与失效无关。失效是支撑这项要求的关系出了断点，而这里的关系毫无损伤。

F49 退出终止该登记所支撑的现行应答负荷。依 F38，束缚位置者退出该登记即不再持有，故退出终止的是该登记所支撑的现行应答负荷，含此后落下的要求与仍在续行的要求。退出不追溯抹去此前确曾成立的事实与已发生的履行或未履行，也不单凭这一退出取消基于其他仍成立的关系位置所产生的要求，那些各按其自身来源与条件另核。

他辞职之后，那个位置此后落下的要求不再落到他身上；而依该登记仍在续行的负荷，也随之终止，保密那一项职责不因它是持续的就续行下去。两样一并终止，这就是退出所终止的。

它不终止两样东西。其一是已经发生的事实：他在任内交接过什么、漏掉过什么，辞职不改写这段历史。其二是别处的来源：他若另有一份仍在生效的竞业约定，或他与公司之间另有一个仍成立的关系位置，那些位置上的要求由它们自己的条件决定，不因这一次辞职而一并落空。退出是一把落在这一次登记上的刀，不是一块能抹掉全部记录的橡皮。

F50 承认撤回后须按作用域重新核其构成与运行条件。全部来源在该域退出，才按明撤；仅若某子域跌破，才按局部失效；剩余来源仍足以支撑原范围，则不因撤去一支自动判失效。

承认被撤回时，先问撤的是哪一域，再问撤去之后剩下的还够不够。

三种情形分得开。全部构成来源在该域退出，那是明撤，该域上的职责随之消。只有某个子域跌破，那是局部失效，跌破的那一域上职责消失，其余续行。而若撤去的只是若干支之一，剩余来源仍足以支撑原来的范围，那就不因撤去一支自动判失效。一支退出与整个位置失守是两件事，中间隔着阈值。

F51 终止不自动生成道德余留。本篇不由原职责的终止自动增设一种无源的道德余债，也不由此宣布一切后续事项已经清结。后续的追问与处理位置各自须有根据，不被原登记的消失统一注销。

终止之后有两种相反的误读，本条两边各挡一个。

一边是把终止读成留下一笔道德余债，以为那个位置虽没了，他在道德上仍然欠着。本篇不增设这样一种无源的债。职责的源在收敛那个位置的权力，源不在了，它不会换一副面孔继续存在。

另一边是把终止读成一切已经清结：位置终止了，所以什么都不必再问。这同样不成立。后续的追问与处理位置各自可以有自己的根据，它们不因原登记的消失而被统一注销。

两种旧账须分开写。其一是承担者自己的历史履行或未履行事实，退出不抹去它。其二是他人在代行阶段留下的负荷，那一种方向相反，第七节另处理。

F52 职责不另立失效分型，它是权利失效在受收敛一侧的读数。凡职责的变化来自同一接缝上的相应断点，借上游的签名读取，不另造同义名称。

上游已按断在何处把承认性权利的失效分作五型。职责不另起一套，它只是同一断点在受收敛那一侧的读数。

上游五型（受位一侧） 受收敛一侧的读数
明撤：全部构成来源退出该登记上的职责随之消
掏空：权力支持跌破而外壳仍在退为宣告无实收敛。要求名义上仍落着而无实收敛落下，承担者在位而无职责
脱权：网络不再按权利位置处理而强制仍在，退化为强力承担者所承受的不再是职责，是强力所加

上游明说五型是典型的失效签名，组合不止这些，本表同样不主张穷尽。

脱权那一格有一条审计要求随上游过来：须以网络是否仍把持位者作为可发起程序、调用程序、要求响应的权利持有人来审计，不得把不受欢迎误作不再是权利。

掏空那一格另有三点。其一，掏空之前该职责确曾成立的事实与已发生的未兑现事实，按 F49 与 F51 保留，本表读取的是掏空之后的职责状态。其二，若另问造成这一状态的具体行为是否侵犯何人的何项权利，按 F54 已写明的分层分别核验，不由职责失效直接判侵，也不以职责终止本身证明该行为正当。其三，承担者在位而无职责一语，只描述掏空之后的现行状态，既不追认此前无职责，也不预判此后不能另有来源重新支撑。掏空是运行效力那一层曾有而跌破，与 F47 所处理的从未取得不同：二者同指三支咬合所给的运行效力那一层，而时态不同。第二节所称的无实收敛，指的正是这一格。

转载那一格留一处不作独立验算。载体迁移之际可能出现一段间隙，其间旧登记已止而新登记未成；这一情形同时依赖本篇的个体化约定与真实支持确已跌破两个条件，不能仅凭现实类比确证，本篇暂不把它作为独立的验算。

V 界

职责有界，而划界有两处容易走岔：一处是把界划在别的地方，一处是把划界当成了保护。本节先立闸，再说闸管到哪里、不管什么。

F53 T5 为闸，而闸只管最低关系位置。本闸只管主体承担者。受效者若为主体，须保留为可回应、可拒绝、可撤回登记的主体位置；该位置在受效者的行动空间中被清零，关系退出社会权力，职责随之不成立。构那一侧不适用本闸，其职责的成立与继续按 F47 的适用条件核验，完成、终止与失效按第四节分别处理。该位置被清零的那一处，才在本篇术语下退为非社会权力；其他越界仍可能发生在关系未退场时。要求超出角色范围、侵害基础权利或形成锁定，并不以主体位置已经清零为必要条件，依各自判据分别判断。T5 未失守不是要求正当或免于侵害的充分条件。

闸设在上游已立的地方。关系只在受效者仍被保留为可回应、可拒绝、可撤回登记的主体位置时，才仍是社会权力；位置被清零，社会权力退场。职责的源在那项权力，源退场则职责不成立。此处不另设闸，只把它用到职责这一侧。

闸只管主体承担者。第一节已立构可持职责，而可拒绝与可撤回登记两项对构讲不通：一家公司拒绝监管并不是 T5 意义上的回绝，一家公司撤回登记就是解散或退出，而终止一个构在制度里可以是常规动作，同一形状对主体却是闸击穿。辖域若不划，凡构所持的职责都过不了这道闸，职责随之一概不成立，那与第一节相拆。

构不因不具有主体位置而被判无职责，也不因此免于继续成立条件的核验。要求依内容完成或终结，按 F48；该登记退出，按 F49；相应权利的构成或运行条件出现同一接缝上的断点，按 F50 与 F52 读取。所借的是相应权利的失效签名，所报的是构在受收敛侧的职责状态；这既不把构改认作承认性权利的自持者，五型也不穷尽一切组合。

还有一种情形要落在第三处。一项要求若穿透到构内的自然人，并使那个自然人的主体位置清零，则在那个自然人处读闸击穿，不在构处读。第一节写构不作阻隔个人承担的外壳，此处是它的另一面：构同样不作吸收个人保护的外壳。

闸之外的越界不归闸管。一项要求超出角色范围，或侵害基础权利，或形成锁定，这些都可能发生在关系远未退场的时候。那位受雇者的岗位仍在，他仍能回应、能拒绝、能撤回登记，而公司的某项要求已经伸进了不该伸的地方。这类事各按各的判据判，不以主体位置已经清零为必要条件。T5 未失守，不是要求正当的证明，也不是免于侵害的证明。

由此要把这一篇的职能说死：它分类，不发正当性。一项负荷被归为职责，不因此获得任何背书；反过来，一句要求越过定义边界之后在本篇术语下不再称职责，也不意味着它的强制在现实里停止了。概念重分类不是现实的保护机制。写在纸面上的退出条款不因此证明 T5 所需的位置在实际操作中真被保留；那要另核。

F54 不另立一类职责本身之侵。职责履行可以受阻，不因此另立一类职责本身之侵。阻断是否侵犯持位者、职责承担者或其他主体的何项权利，按上游的刻度归位判（P5 F22）：基础分量按限侵读，承认分量按五型读失效，两套不混。履行受阻、履行不能、职责是否继续成立，以及相关后果如何处理，互不自动推出。对受收敛者而言，此条只登记外部阻断与未给付的事实，不单凭这些事实认定违约失职，也不据此认定其当然无须承担任何相关后果。相应判断仍依要求内容、适用条件及有关事实分别处理，不从受阻本身生成关于职责的防御性位格。

有人阻断了他履行，这不新增一类侵。

理由在侵的标的物。侵所针对的是主体位置与自由度，而职责是一份受收敛的应答负荷：它不是一项资产，也不是一项受权利保护的资格。一份负荷被挡住，受损的是别的东西：可能是持位者那项承认性权利，可能是承担者自己的某项基础权利，也可能是第三方的什么。那是什么，按上游的刻度归位判，基础分量按限侵读，承认分量按五型读失效，两套不混。

也不可反着推。职责不是资产，这句话不推出与履行有关的通道、资格和人员绝不可能另有权利保护。那些权利若有，须有它们自己的来源。

四件事互不自动推出：履行受阻是一件，履行不能是第二件，职责是否继续成立是第三件，相关后果如何处理是第四件。公司把他要用的系统关了，他因此没能交接：受阻是事实，未给付是事实，而这两个事实本身既不证明他违约失职，也不证明他当然无须承担任何相关后果。这些仍按那项要求的内容、适用条件与有关事实分别处理。

只登记事实，两向都不结算。不从受阻本身生成一个防御性的位格，也不从受阻本身认定过失。

F55 界内张力是一个接口，不是一条量的定理。在 T5 之内，仍可能存在要求冲突、履行成本与现实可行性的张力。施权范围越大，应答范围是否相应扩大，取决于另行成立的约束安排，不由职责定义直接推出。

闸守住了，界内的张力并不因此消失。要求之间可以冲突，履行可以昂贵，现实可以做不到。这些都在 T5 之内，闸拦不到它们。

其中有一条特别容易被当成定理：施权越大则应答越重。这不由职责的定义推出。职责的源在收敛这个位置的权力，而不在这个位置对外所施之权。一个位置对外施权很大，并不说明针对它的追问与制约已相应成立；二者可能脱节，而制度设计要处理的正是这种脱节。施权与应答的梯度是否成立，取决于那一个构实际设置了什么，那是经验的事，不是定义的推论。

界内张力在此只标为一个接口，不给它一条量的规律。

F56 职责的名分不区分涵育、压制与殖民。一项要求被认定为职责，只说明它有源、有内容、已登记落在某位置，不说明它使承担者长还是塌。那一判断另有其门：那项权力是涵育还是殖民，按上游已立的结构判据判；该要求是否越界，按限与侵判。承担者持有一项职责，不因此取得任何正当性背书。

同一个人承认同一项权力，由此担下的职责可以是把他撑起来的，也可以是把他压下去的，甚至可以是把他收作他人目的之材料的。而这三者在本篇的名分上没有分别，都是职责。

公司要他每季写一份复盘，这可能逼着他把自己做过的事想明白一层，也可能只是给上级留一份可供追究的底档；公司要他随时待命，这可能是一段真实的托举，也可能是把他的全部时间收进别人的安排里。三种情形里那一格都成立：有源，有内容，已登记落在他的岗位上。职责的名分报告成立条件得到满足，不报告要求内容得到正当性背书。

正当性，以及涵育、压制与殖民的判断，另有其门。那项权力是涵育还是殖民，上游已按结构判据切开（P6 F26），且明写不由动机、感受或善恶定：善意的追问可以在结构上是殖民，恶意的追问可以在结构上是涵育。该要求是否越界，按限与侵判。两道门都不在本篇。

这一条是否定性的。它排除一条推论，不问动机，不问认同，不判效果；上游判限与侵由结构定而不由动机与感受定，上游判登记的判据不在情绪也不在认同，与这两条都不相犯。

由此还落下一个不那么顺眼的结论，而它有它的边界，两句须同时成立。其一，在 F47 按承担者类型适用的成立条件与 F53 的相应边界仍满足时，一项要求可以侵害基础权利、形成压制或殖民，而仍具有职责身份，职责的名分不因此为其内容背书；若切出去，凡称职责者便暗含未越界，那恰恰是让分类发了正当性，与本条相反。其二，成立条件不再满足时，不能只因它仍有强制作用就保留职责身份；若同一要求同时使主体承担者的最低关系位置被清零，或相应来源、登记与运行条件不再成立，则按 F53 或第四节判断其身份与去向。越界与否按上游归型，而归型本身不替成立条件作答。若被压制者拒绝一项侵犯性的要求，按 F54，其未兑现也不自动读作失职。这个拒绝属未给付而不属外部阻断，同样只登记事实而不作两向结算。

最后要说清这一条实际管到哪里。第四节立了成立条件。压制性要求中，凡不满足相应运行权利条件而仅由强力施加者，先在那里就不成其为职责，因为没有一项经三支咬合运行的承认性权利，就没有它的权力面。所以本条实际只管那些成立条件仍满足而仍然压制的情形，它的管辖面比字面窄。这不是对压制的一般容纳，它所拒绝的只有一步推论，即由一项负荷是职责推出它未压制承担者。

VI 并存与对接

职责与保留下来的那条朝内义务不相排斥，而它与被删去的那种横向义务模型确有分别。两件事都在源与所束缚者两栏上读，前者问两栏上的东西能否落到同一个动作上，后者问分别落在何处。

F57 职责与义务可并存。二者可同时落在同一施为上。源不同，所束缚者不同，退出效力不同。退出该登记不凭自身取消承担者对自己法的自我承担，但与某项具体施为有关的自律要求是否继续适用，按其自己的法中的内容与条件判断。

同一个施为上可以同时落着一项职责与一项义务，二者不相排斥，因为它们分在两栏上。

那位受雇者的保密正是这样一处重合。公司的保密条款收敛到他的岗位上，这一项的源在那项收敛，扣住的是岗位，是职责。他自己也可能立有一条关于不出卖托付给自己之事的自律，这一项的源在他自己的法，扣住的是他本人，是义务。两项同时落在同一个动作上，而那个动作是一次应有的不作为：他不说出去。第一节摆开两栏时取的是两个不同的动作，此处问的是另一件事，即两栏上的东西能否落到同一个动作上。能。

分野要到他辞职之后才显出来。职责那一份随该登记此后不再落下，按 F49。义务那一份不由这次登记支撑，也不随它消失：他仍是对自己的法负责的那个主体，那一层的自我承担不以任何一次外部登记的存续为条件。

元层的自我承担不以该登记的存续为条件，这句话不等于他自己的法里每一条具体要求都自动续行。离职之后仍不说出去这一条是否仍然适用于他，要在他自己的法里判：他当初立那一条时是否限定了任职期间，那件事在他离职之后是否仍属受托之事。这两问的答案不由职责的终止给出，也不由本篇给出。元层与具体条目是两层，不可互相顶替。

有一个反问要先接住：既然朝内的那条义务已经能要求承担者不把他者当作工具，何必再立职责。

答在源与退出效力之别。朝内的义务其源在承担者自己的法，退出无效，一个主体无从退出为不是那条法的主体。职责的源在收敛他那个位置的权力，退出有效，退出该登记即不再持有。二者在同一施为上可以重合，而问这件事为什么落在他身上、他离开这个位置之后还落不落，两者给的答案不同。他辞职那一天，公司那一项随岗位落下，而他的自我承担结构不随之消失；他自己那一条具体要求是否继续适用，按他自己的法判断。同一个不说出去的动作上少了一重支撑，而未必少了全部。

还有更要紧的一处。一项职责是否成立，不取决于承担者自己的法里有没有相应的一条。公司要他在月底前交接，这项要求之所以落在他身上，不因为他认为自己应当交接；第四节已判，判据在行动矢量是否被权力场客观收敛，不在情绪，也不在认同。若只有朝内的义务这一个范畴可用，那些确已落下而承担者并不自认的要求就无处安放，只能要么被说成他自己的法，那是把外来的要求追认为自律；要么被说成不存在，而它正在起作用。导言所说那一格有位置、有强度而无名者，即在此处。

F58 与所排除的那种无条件横向义务模型的对照。已证成的对照一项：该模型是普遍的映射，一项主张对应所有人的一条要求，无条件铺向所有人；职责是条件性的收敛，只落特定受效者。这一项是结构的，不是措辞的。退出效力之别另有其位：那是朝内的义务与职责之间的对照，见 F38 与 F57；A16 的终止引理所证者为朝内自我承担的退出无效，不因此证成该模型对受要求一方亦退出无效，后者若要成立，须由该模型自身的规定取得。撤回是否留下道德余债，本条不列为已证差异：职责这一侧，无论经退出、完成还是承认撤回而止，终止都不自动留余留，已由 F51 立定；而该模型留余债一说在上游无出处，只能作附加设定之下的条件对照。

本条所对的是本谱所排除的那一种模型，不是全部传统 duty 诸说。那些学说各有其构造，本条不对它们一概立论。已证成的对照只有一项，另两项各归其处，理由如下。

其一直接见于上游一句。Paper 0 回应一个反问时已把这一分别写出：权力是条件性的收敛，只在实际收敛时，只收敛特定的受效者；而义务是「普遍的映射」，「一项 claim 对应所有人的一项 duty」，一个有条件而落在特定关系上，一个无条件而铺向所有人。职责由那个权力面生，故随它而为条件性的收敛。公司的保密要求只落在他这一次任职的这一格上，不落到任何别人身上；而那种模型所说的义务，是一个人有一项主张，所有人便各得一条。这一项不必推，上游原文一句里就有。

退出效力之别不在本条，在 F38 与 F57。那一对照的两端是朝内的义务与职责，各有其据。朝内那一端的据在 A16 的终止引理：主体「不得不只自己给自己立法」，「主体对自己的法负责」，故一个主体无从退出为不是那条法的主体；此句是本篇由终止引理所作的重述，不是上游原文。职责那一端的据在上游讲信托未出世受益人的那一处：受效者持一个责任，受制于附条件的权力要求，「不退出则承担」，「退出则不再持有」。上游该段同时说明受效者持责任，以及退出该信托关系后不再享有相应权利；它尚未使用本篇的职责概念。此处取用的是退出同一关系这一结构，不把失去那项权利单独换读成免除职责。本篇的职责按 F38 系于特定登记，并按 F46 指向有内容的应答负荷；该登记确已退出时，依它成立的现行负荷按 F49 终止。合到那位受雇者身上是这样：他辞职，保密那一项职责的登记终止，而他无从辞去自己那条法的主体身份。

这一对照移不到本条的题面上。本条的题面是那个被排除的横向模型，而终止引理所证的是朝内的自我承担，两者的源在 F38 里本就分在两处：朝内的源在承担者自己的法，那个模型的源在某个主体内在律的外推。甲无从退出为不是自己那条法的主体，这一句推不出乙无从退出甲外推给乙的那项要求。该模型若亦退出无效，须由它自身的规定取得，例如它被规定为不以任何登记关系的存续为条件；那是对一个设定的读，不是上游已核的属性。本篇不借终止引理替它作证。

撤回留不留道德余债，同样不列为已证差异。原职责终止不自动生成无源的道德余债，这一半是第四节 F51 已立的，F51 本来就不靠另一种模型必留余债才能成立。另一半不同：它在已发布的权利论各篇中没有出处，余留这个说法在那些正文里一次也没有出现过。自己规定一个假定模型，当然可以拿来比，而两种身份要分开：设某个模型在撤回之后仍保留一份道德余债，那是附加设定之下的条件对照；说那个已被排除的模型本来如此，则仍须那个模型自身的依据。把对照对象刻画成便于对照的样子，再据此宣布分别成立，那样的对照是自证。故此处只写作条件句：若另有模型如此规定，则本篇与它在这一点上相异。

F51 实际管到的是这样一类问题：他辞职时有一笔没交接完，那件事此后落在谁身上，按 F51 与 F60 各自的来源与登记判；一句原职责已经终止接不住它，一份凭空的道德余债同样接不住它。

换用一个英文词本身不证明已经摆脱了所有 duty 模型，这一点导言已经说过。此处可以说得更准：本条已证成者只有那一项限定的对照，其余各归其处，而摆脱与否由四格分野承担，不由这一节的条目数承担。

VII 准主体的职责

准主体这一侧不另立一套职责。前六节的判据照用，难处只在一处：门态与登记归属看上去像同一件事，而它们是两件。先把这两件分开，再处理代行终止之后的那一批未处理事项。

份位已立的准主体才在此列。准主体资格本身尚未确定者，不在辖域之内。

F59 代行阶段的登记归属逐项判。门自行改变的是相应应答功能的承担方式，不凭自身决定职责归谁。每项要求须分别核其内容、实际收敛的应答空间与登记承担者，不由谁能自行应答或谁实际代行单独决定。代行网络若因该要求实际受到收敛并完成登记，相应职责在网络。本人在门自行范围内若取得相应来源、内容与登记，相应职责在本人。围绕同一准主体的多项要求可以分属不同承担者，不作整批迁移。旧职责是否终止、新职责是否成立，分别按来源、登记、内容及适用范围判断。

门态说明应答怎样实现，登记归属说明谁在这项关系里承受该要求。前者可以是后者的相关条件，不单独替代后者的核验。

三门态的结构上游已经立定（《论权利与权益》F33 与 F34），本篇只回指，不重开。那道否定门分无门、代行与自行三态，随门迁移的是承认性权利的运行宿主，而基础权利的份位宿主始终在准主体本人，门的第一人称归属从未转移，网络所代行的只是门的功能。上游已把这条线的两端与中段照全，此处只接着问一件它没有问的事：在代行的那一程上，落下来的那些要求，其职责登记在谁身上。

应答空间有两种读法，此处只取后一种。若指某人此刻有什么应答能力、或者谁正在实际应答，它不能单独决定归属，因为第一节已允许构持职责而由具体节点实现应答，实际执行者与登记承担者不必是同一承担者。若指这项要求实际收敛并登记的行动空间，它可以按 F36 与 F46 逐项应用，而这个空间归谁，仍须从要求的对象、来源与登记事实核出，不由谁的门已能自行先行分配。

一个孩子的门正在从代行迁向自行，这一程上通常同时挂着好几项要求。监护网络为它与一所学校之间办成的那项安排，其要求收敛的是网络所占的那个位置，登记也落在网络，职责在网络。它自己在门自行范围内另行接下的一项约定，若来源、内容与登记三者俱足，那一项的职责在它本人。两项同时存在，不相冲突，也不互相带动。问一项职责归谁，要看那一项自己的三件，不看这个孩子整体走到了门线的哪一段。

围绕同一准主体的多项要求可以分属不同承担者，不作整批迁移。门自行到某个范围，不是一次把那一批要求从网络搬到本人的搬家。旧职责是否终止、新职责是否成立，各按各的来源、登记、内容与适用范围判断。

代行那一程上有三件事各自定命运：因准主体之存在而在关系网络中形成、并由网络节点实际承担的代理职责；网络因自己承接照护、代表、保护等位置而承担的它自己的职责；以及准主体在门渐次自行后新形成的对自身位置的应答能力。分出这三件，是为了看见它们各自定命运，不是给出三条处置规则。处置一律按 F38 追源，不因类别而分。不得按类别开口子：若允许把一笔旧欠归入某一类便可绕过核源，那道口子会把追源整条吃掉。

登记也不以本人认同为条件。本人在门自行之后若取得新登记上的职责，该职责不以其主观认同该权力为成立条件，只要相应成立与适用条件仍有效，就按该要求承担。据在上游对登记的判定（P3 F13）：登记可顺向可受迫，不是同意，判据是行动矢量是否被权力场客观收敛，不在情绪也不在认同。合起来一句：不赞同，不独自使已成立的职责消失；未退出，也不独自使一项职责生成或永远继续。

此处排除的只是本人赞同作为必要条件，不取消承认性权利的来源结构。心理上的认同与构成式的承认，不可被同一个承认总词吞掉。退出在此仍是效力不是可用性：若退出发生则不再持有，至于那个位置上有无退出通道可用，按 P4 F18 与 P5 判，不属此处的分野。

归属判断由本篇自负。上游支持份位宿主、运行宿主与门功能三者分账，而它不替职责新增一套分类，也不由门态自动完成归属裁定。所取的只是逐项核验这一形式，不含由门态分配归属的实质规则。

F60 代行职责的终止不替未处理事项配给后继者。本条只处理由代行网络实际登记承担的那些职责。代行终止后，该项能否落在后继者处，按 F38 追源。追源完成之前，不得以代行经历、受益事实或接管位置代替核源。追源结果为现行来源与登记均不成立，且无其他独立成立的承接安排者，不登记为后继者的职责。后继者包括门自行后在同一项上取得应答空间的准主体本人。此前要求确曾成立、未履行确曾发生，以及仍未处理的事项，分别保留，不由现行职责终止而抹去。对其中尚无已成立承接关系的具体事项，本篇列作留置事项。此一留置不把它认证为仍在运行的职责，也不预判新来源绝不可能另行形成。

代行终止时，那些没办完的事既不自动落到后继者头上，也不因此消失。一个错是借终止把旧账抹掉，另一个错是把旧账推给接手的人，两句各挡一个。

三种对象要分清。其一是曾经成立、曾经未履行的历史事实，无现行承担者不足以取消它，退出不改历史。其二是仍可指认的未满足事项、影响或要求内容，它不自动取消，而主张它存在的一方须说明具体剩下什么。其三是此刻仍具职责身份的要求，它须有本篇所定的现行来源、内容、位置与登记，不能由前两项自动推出。失去当前职责身份，不等于从未发生；过去发生过，也不等于当前仍有一项同身份的对象。

监护网络在代行期间该为那个孩子办而未办成的某件事，就落在这三者之间。它确曾成立并确曾未履行，这是第一样。那件事至今仍未办成，这是第二样。而门自行之后它是否成为那个孩子自己的一项职责，那要问第三样：现在有没有一项收敛它当前位置的权力把这件事作为要求发出，有没有内容，有没有登记。三件都有，是新的一项职责；缺其一，不登记。它接过了自己的位置，不等于它接过了那一笔。

追源在此处只是照用。要紧的是那条限度：追源完成之前，不得以代行经历、受益事实或接管位置代替核源。一项要求谈论的是代行期间的旧事，不推出它只能由旧关系发出，也不推出它当然有源；曾经受益于那套安排，不证明当前有一项要求落在受益者身上；接过位置更不足以证明来源成立，因为位置按 F37 是一次登记事件，不是可挂负荷的容器。

若追源核下来现行来源与登记均不成立，且没有别的独立成立的承接安排，那就不登记为后继者的职责。这里的后继者也包括那个孩子自己：门自行后在同一项上取得应答空间的准主体本人，同样要过这一关，不因为那件事当初是为它办的就当然归它。

不登记，不等于那件事就此结清。它列作留置事项，意思是承认它存在而不假装已经有了承担者。它的现实落点是原代行网络及其所在之构名下的未兑现记录，不是悬在后继者头上的一项待结算要求。

这样处理有一个本体上的理由：职责须有受效者持有。当代行终止而权力源不再存续，对后继主体而言它根本没有成为一项职责，而不是成了一项无人承担的职责；与此同时，它未履行的历史事实与可指认的未满足事项客观存在，不因无人承担而消失。两句并立，不相抵。

两处误伤要避开。其一，若那件事另有独立来源，依其自身条件判断，不在此列；设某物上的负担本身构成一项对现持有人的独立要求，其成立与否按该来源判，本条取消的只是以接管位置为由省掉核源那一步。其二，已确认没有当前承担者，与目前没有查明谁或何种组织承担，是两回事；后一种是材料或识别未定，不能直接登记成前一种。而且第一节已允许构持职责，没有落到某个个人，不等于没有落到任何承担者。

两侧各挡一个对手。若原来的构宣称代行者已退、所以那些已发生的事实与明确未处理的事项就地消失，那是虚伪闭合；原职责确实可以终止，所反对的是由这个终止抹去其他对象，不是反对职责真正结束。若它宣称代行者已退、所以后继者必须承接，那是转嫁。一项职责可以真正结束，而结束不会替任何人把账清空。

F61 不设二元硬阈。门自行的范围决定哪些应答功能可以由本人承担，不直接搬动既有职责。涉及职责的生成、继续与终止，仍依 F59 逐项判断。这一分域过程不能由一条外部固定、不可反驳、跨情境的死阈替代。

门自行的范围与职责的搬动不是一回事。前者说的是哪些应答功能可以由本人承担，后者要问来源、内容与登记，二者之间没有一条自动的通道。

上游已就那条线给过一个警告（《论权利与权益》F34a）：对一个正处在代行与自行之间连续段上的准主体，施加一道外部固定、不可反驳、跨情境统一使用的二元阈，去一律画定它哪些算权益、哪些算承认性权利，会系统性地产生错分的风险；而同一条的展开里把这个风险说得更死：一道死阈落在一条渐进而移动的线上，必在某一点、对某一个成熟速率的孩子落到线的错边。两句同出一条，这证明的是出处，不证明二者同强：前一句是一道不能免除错分的警告，后一句在其所论连续变化与固定阈的关系之下另断言至少出现一处错分。本篇取那道界限而不取那一步加强，也不把较强的一句改读成每案必错。要照实读的是它划的那道界：上游同处明写，可被个案事实推翻、按事项分别判断、保留申诉与复核的阈不在此列，它不必然错分。受限制的是死阈，不是一切清楚的判断。

那一步加强也不作本篇的地基。按 F59，门态本就不是职责归属的完整判准；纵使某道阈在某个范围内恰好把门态判对，它也不因此给出每一项职责的来源与登记。逐项核验守住了，硬阈就不必另证。

对应的处置因此是否定性的：不设二元硬阈，而不是另设一条更细的阈。那个孩子在门线上走到哪里，与它身上此刻有哪几项职责、各归谁，是两串各自要核的事。按年龄、按某个测验、按某个单一节点的判断一刀切下，替代的正是 F59 所要求的逐项核验。这一分域过程守在逐项这一形式上，不交给任何一条死阈。

VIII 收束与留置事项

受收敛一侧的位格至此配齐。责任管承受，职责管应答，施为是应答的兑现；加上仅存的那条朝内的义务，四个词各占一格，谁也不顶替谁。

那位受雇者身上四样同时都在。他在公司可处分的范围之内，这是责任。公司要他按期交接、要他持续保密，这是两项职责。他交接、他不说出去，这是施为。他自己立下的那条不敷衍了事，这是义务。四样落在同一个人身上而不相混，而分开它们的不止一把尺。义务与职责由源与所束缚者两栏分开。责任与职责同源同位，分它们的是承受位置与具体应答内容之别。职责与施为分在要求与兑现之别上。三把尺各管一处，合起来这一格才不塌成一个词。

还有一件这一篇没有做的事，要在收束处说在明处：它分类，不发正当性。一项负荷被归为职责，不因此取得任何背书；一项要求越过定义边界之后在本篇术语下不再称职责，也不因此在现实里停止强制。这一条第五节立过，此处再说一次，因为它是引用时最容易掉的一句。

三项留置如下。

留置一 · 职责的量。F55 给了一个接口，没有给一条度量。施权范围与应答范围之间的梯度是否成立、成立到什么厚度、由谁来定，这些是经验的事，制度那一层另有命题处理，本篇不接。

留置二 · 证明层依赖的登记。本篇引制度篇，制度篇正文不引本篇。若制度篇某一处的双侧条件实际上要到承认的构成与撤回那一篇才提得出，那个依赖须照实登记；在独立的推导写出来之前，不先签它已经独立。按实际的证明关系登记，不按发表的时间排序。

留置三 · 保护安排的设计。判据那一半上游已经给了：一项约束要算作限，须不封死主体退出、拒绝或撤回持续绑定的结构通道，「越出此者是侵」（P5 F21）。所以形式上写着可以退出而实质上封死退出，上游已判为侵，本篇引它即可，不待另立，也不待下游另篇。余下的那一半是保护安排如何设计，才能使一条写在纸面上的退出通道在实际操作中真的可用。那属于制度设计那一层的工作，本篇不接，留给专门处理这一层的后续篇目。
