Abstract

This paper is Paper 0 of Cooperation Theory: written last, placed first. It lays down no propositions; it only does a few things for those who read this series: it states this series' place in SAE, sets out how far it reads and what its main line is, gives the method and the shared words the papers have in common, interfaces as a whole with several traditions, says once, all together, the directions of colonization read in the papers, sets side by side the two faces that the moment of two-way 15DD shows in Love Theory and in this series, lists together the thirty-eight propositions of the whole series, and registers the remainders that are left over and not closed out.

Cooperation Theory is the third branch downstream of SAE Relation Theory, following Love Theory and Marriage Theory, and parallel to Marriage Theory: at the normative layer the two do not conflict; at the rule layer they can differ, because the kinds of relation differ. This series does not define all cooperation; the cooperation it reads has as its premise that at least one party is headed in the 15DD direction, that is, that at least one party recognizes the other as an end too in this undertaking; the direction is a premise, not a component, nor a sufficient condition for entering scope. Under this premise, two people, each for his own purpose, do together one thing that both avow and in which each has a share; neither party holds the purpose and adjudication of this undertaking alone, nor does it automatically produce, above the two, a "we" that legislates for both. The main line, put plainly: You for yours, I for mine, and we do this one thing together. For direction one party is enough; for participation both are needed; in judgment each uses his own. The seven papers read, in order: Doing Together; Each for Their Own Ends; Division of Labor; "We"; One Side; Each Bears a Share; The Process.

This paper interfaces as a whole with Hobbes, Kropotkin and Mozi; Rawls and Tomasello are placed only as neighbors, and Kant's perpetual peace is pointed back to the Theory of Inter-State Cooperation. The directions of colonization read in the papers borrow things that are, or may come to be, in a cooperation: avowal; names; division of labor; procedure; giving, reciprocal exchange and contribution; friendship; the name of cultivation, and dependency; exit, and what is said out loud on exiting; and the premise, regarding a third person, of an arrangement the two jointly maintain. These things carry no direction in themselves; the direction is read on the one effect in which they are taken up to do something; each has its own antecedents to be checked first. This paper proves no proposition at the object layer.

Introduction

The seven papers that follow each read one thing. Paper 1, Doing Together, reads what the cooperation this series speaks of looks like: the premise; the structure; the three things, direction, participation and judgment; the object layer and the operation layer; the three kinds commonly met outside scope; last, it looks once at the prisoner's dilemma, at how the step that rules can derive and the step where judgment falls are kept apart. Paper 2, Each for Their Own Ends, reads each of the two people's purposes: each is each one's own, and neither examines the other's; several shapes of giving and getting; the two readings of "exchange"; the same purpose still being two shares. Paper 3, Division of Labor, reads how division of labor is set, how it is changed, and how it slides into a fixed requirement aimed at the other; how organizations, contracts and positions are registered as conditions; and what is handed over to a procedure the two have recognized beforehand. Paper 4, "We", reads which branch "we" is when it is said in a cooperation; judging one party's law for him in the name of the team, the project or the company; guarding coming back to the individual; mutual recognition borrowed as adjudication; several people, and little circles. Paper 5, One Side, reads cooperation in which one end is headed in the 15DD direction and one leans toward 14DD: stance; the setting of cultivation; the measure of outcomes; what can be seen and what cannot be certified; not being used, and not calculating in return; and the case in which, in this end's judgment, the other is reduced to calculation. Paper 6, Each Bears a Share, reads the judgment layer: each judges his own, each answers for his own; what has been done is not acknowledged by someone else in one's place; contribution is not used to possess; breach of faith is entered apart and entered on the books; one wrong result is not on its own entered as non-fulfillment of what was promised. Paper 7, The Process, reads what the thing done together sediments into over time: the process is read, not the settlement; completion and looking back are entered apart; withdrawing; after it is done; and that moment.

Written this way, several things are left that no single paper takes care of: why this series is here, and how far it reads; how the main line strings the seven papers together; the method the papers share, and what the same word means in which paper; several traditions whose questions concern the whole series; what the directions of colonization read in the papers look like when set side by side; and which face of the moment of two-way 15DD this series and Love Theory each take up. This paper takes care of these.

Method as in the papers: the conclusions of upstream papers are said in plain words, and sources and extensions go in the notes. This paper lays down no propositions, and alters no sentence of the papers that follow. How the pieces upstream handed to this series are placed is for readers who want to check against upstream, and goes in the appendix at the end.

§1 Place: The Third Branch Downstream of the Relational Side

SAE has three sides: the subject side, the relational side and the institutional side. The relational side is unfolded by Relation Theory; once Relation Theory is written, there are several branches downstream: Love Theory, Marriage Theory, Parent-Child Theory and Cooperation Theory; friendship is not given a branch of its own, and serves as a running example through all the branches.1 Love Theory and Marriage Theory come first; this series is the third branch, written before Parent-Child Theory.

The division of labor between the two layers Relation Theory has written out: Relation Theory in the abstract establishes only what all relations have; what holds only in one kind of relation is handed to the series on that kind of relation to argue.2 Whatever has already been laid down at the layer of relation, this series uses directly and does not rewrite: the minimal condition of a relation, the two directions of cultivation and colonization, expectation, commitment, the sediment of dealings and ending are all read by Relation Theory. Of what Relation Theory handed to this series, three things it gave first itself: no third bearer is generated; a shared goal is not a shared ultimate value; what doing things together sediments into over time (Appendix).

With Marriage Theory: parallel. Marriage Theory, in its scope, hands over this boundary: doing things together in general belongs to Cooperation Theory, and Marriage Theory reads only doing things together within marriage.3 This series is not downstream of Marriage Theory; the two series are parallel. Where the two read differently, it is first seen whether the difference can be traced to the kind of relation: where it can, it falls at the rule layer, and the two can differ; where it cannot, it falls at the normative layer, and the two do not conflict. Where a real conflict is found, it is judged by Relation Theory, and the two series do not judge each other; what Relation Theory has not decided is registered as a remainder of Relation Theory. The words "this is the rule layer" are not, in themselves, a reason: it has to be said which difference in the kind of relation makes the readings differ.

The two differences read so far both trace to one and the same distinction: marriage has the love between the two as one thing it cannot do without; cooperation does not require love. One is scope: Marriage Theory takes both parties headed in the 15DD direction, this series at least one; the case outside Marriage Theory's scope, one end headed in this direction and one leaning toward 14DD, is here a proper object (Paper 5). The other is exchange: in marriage a moment of reciprocal exchange is read as falling back; in cooperation an arrangement of reciprocal exchange both parties avow does not make this undertaking exit scope, nor is it read as falling back (Paper 2). Tracing this point to love takes one step in between. The ground Marriage Theory gives for falling back is that at that moment there was no operation in the direction of recognizing the other as an end too; in marriage that direction is the direction in which the love between the two lies, and Love Theory has written that loving each other is not exchange. In cooperation, recognizing the other as an end too does not need love, nor does it rule out the two agreeing within C on how to exchange. This reading is this series' own undertaking (Paper 2).4 Some things do not change with the kind of relation: writing one's own giving as an account the other must repay is read in cooperation, at the layer between person and person, just as it is read in marriage (Paper 2); division of labor is a present arrangement the two have avowed together, whose binding force passes separately through each one's own law, and which, when it slides into a fixed requirement aimed at the other, has a tendency toward colonization; the two series have the same form at this point, and each reads by this form (Paper 3).

With Action Theory: one person, and two together. Relation Theory has written out its division of labor with upstream: Action Theory reads a single drawing of the line; Relation Theory reads what the effects between the parties sediment into in dealings and over time.5 How one person does things together with another, Action Theory Paper 8 has established: one person is for purpose A, another for purpose B, and the two cannot not do C; C is something both of them avow and both are able to do, whose purpose and adjudication neither side holds alone.6 The two series overlap here: Action Theory reads the individual's side, this series reads the two together; at the overlap this series calls on that proposition, gives its number, and does not establish it again. Applying this structure of arrangement to an object whose premise is that at least one end is headed in the 15DD direction is a step this series takes on as its own undertaking (Paper 1).

With Love Theory: mutual recognition, and the thing done together. Relation Theory divides "we" into two branches, doing things together and mutual recognition; which branch occurs is checked case by case.7 In Love Theory, the mutual recognition of that moment belongs to the branch of mutual recognition; this series reads chiefly the branch of doing things together. Mutual recognition happens in cooperation too, and can be real; only it is not borrowed to pass final judgment on a thing done together (Paper 4). The two series take up the moment of two-way 15DD from two faces; §7 sets them side by side.

With Parent-Child Theory. Of a thing parents and their adult children do together, the face of doing it together is here; what lies between parents and child belongs to Parent-Child Theory.

With the institutional side. The part made up of organizations, contracts, employment and positions belongs to the institutional side; this series reads only the part of what passes between persons that the institution does not provide for, or that goes beyond its provisions. The provisions themselves are acknowledged but not judged, and are registered as conditions of the effect.8 What lies between state and state, between construct and construct, belongs to the Theory of Inter-State Cooperation; in Chinese the two series use different words, that series 协作 and this series 合作, and this series does not use the former as a term.9 Institutional relations between company and company, and between states and companies, this series does not take up either (§8).

With Political Economy: five points. The range of Political Economy is: "how the capital, fame, status and qualifications that people accumulate in the Emergence Layer become, in others' decisions, power acting on others".10 The two series meet at five points, each on its own side.

First, exchange. Exchange in two people's doing things together is one shape of cooperation, and is read here (Paper 2); how exchange in the Emergence Layer accumulates, and how it is converted into power acting on others, belongs to Political Economy. That series says that outside the gates exchange is left open as usual, and also says that not running into those two gates does not certify a transaction as warranted in every respect; when it is cited, the two sentences are brought along together.11

Second, reputation and fame. They are used apart, as in the Political Economy Prequel: fame concerns how many people know of someone and how he is talked about; reputation is the state that recognition relations naturally show within a community.12 Breaking a commitment is entered on the books between person and person, and is read here; reputation, or a score taken as standing for it, being used by a position as a condition of a chokepoint decision, together with records and scores, belongs to Political Economy (Paper 6).

Third, non-compete agreements. An agreement made within a cooperation, backed by liquidated damages, injunction or compulsory enforcement, that forbids one party, after leaving, to go in a certain direction is read by Political Economy; a conditional payment is read differently, and the criteria are brought along with their full antecedents. The other party to the cooperation is himself the party that sets the agreement: if what the agreement protects is his own share, it is not among those "set to protect others" (Paper 3).13

Fourth, registered partners. Within a construct a registered partner is in a position, and accountability is said to him; this belongs to the institutional side; the side between the two that the institution does not provide for, or that goes beyond its provisions, belongs here.14 The construct the two have registered is the address of attribution, not a third bearer (Paper 3, Paper 4).

Fifth, the judgment layer. Political Economy's judgment layer is the particular case, handed to judgment in positions, and the account falls on positions and persons;15 this series' judgment layer falls on each person himself (§3). Political Economy takes "judge the operation, not the person" as a convention of the whole series; it runs the same way as this series' saying that stances are not labels, and each stands on its own side.

§2 Scope and Main Line

Scope. There are six items.

First, it does not define all cooperation. Some call a balance that two parties reach by calculating against each other, each getting what he is after, cooperation as well; this series does not object to the usage, it only does not read it. To say that a case is not within the cooperation this series reads is not to say that it is not cooperation (Paper 1, CO1, CO5).

Second, at least one party headed in the 15DD direction. In SAE's levels, the layer at which the other is recognized as an end too is written 15DD. 15DD is non dubito: "it is not only I who am an end; the other, too, is an end in itself."16 The cooperation this series reads has as its premise that at least one party is headed in the 15DD direction, and this is said of this one undertaking. The direction is a premise, not a component of cooperation, nor a sufficient condition for entering scope; entering scope is checked on three further things: both avow this undertaking and actually take part, each having a share of his own; the other is not, in this undertaking, reduced to calculation; the arrangement the two jointly avow and actually maintain does not carry the direction of colonization toward a third person, and does not cross his basic rights (Paper 1). Leaning toward 14DD and being reduced to calculation are not one thing: in this undertaking, the end that leans toward 14DD and is within scope at least structurally acknowledges that the other has purposes of his own; to be reduced to calculation is to treat the other only as a strategy set.17 Here and throughout, this series writes "acknowledge" for this structural grade, so that it is not taken for recognizing the other as an end too; a quoted page keeps its own words, which may differ.

Third, stances, not labels. 15DD and 14DD here speak of the stance of one operation; they are read by the current relational condition, not by persons;18 a layer records one operation; it does not record a person, nor a stretch of cooperation; people are all mixed, and falling back is read as it occurs, and does not overturn the direction. The object layer and the operation layer are kept apart: the object layer reads the cooperation within scope; at the operation layer each party can confirm only his own end, and cannot on that basis declare on the other end's behalf that it avows; the other end's taking part and arrangements are described by words and deeds and checkable conditions, and their inner grounds are not certified (Paper 1, CO4). If it is later confirmed that the object did not obtain, this series exits this case, and does not turn and judge the other end; this is entered by period, and the shared process that has already happened is not erased backward.

Fourth, what is not read, in three groups. The three kinds commonly met outside scope are each read differently: that in which neither party is headed in the 15DD direction, whose ideal type is the case in which both parties treat each other only as a strategy set and reach a balance by mutual calculation; that of being passively helped, which is an act of help, read as one-way dealings per Relation Theory; and the stretch in which the arrangement the two jointly avow and actually maintain carries the direction of colonization toward a third person or crosses his basic rights. In the last kind, in collusion premised on "a third person is merely a means," the arrangement itself decides who counts as an end by whether someone is one of our own. What 15DD recognizes is that the other, too, is an end in itself; it does not recognize him on the strength of who he is or what he has. The third person is a person too, yet in this arrangement he is treated merely as a means; then the recognition between the two does not rest on the other's being a person, or else the third person would be included too; it rests on some other item; which item is not decided; what can be decided is only that, on the strength of this item, people are ranked high and low; in this undertaking, neither end is headed in the 15DD direction. Recognition of this kind carries with it the item it rests on; this speaks of structure; it does not predict how long such an arrangement lasts, nor does it say that once that item changes the recognition is bound to be withdrawn. This reads only the premise of that arrangement, not persons, and a single falling back is not among these; it goes one step further than what Relation Theory reads from the same dependency condition, and this step is this series' own undertaking (Paper 1, CO5).19 Beyond the three kinds, where one end is headed in the 15DD direction and the other, in this undertaking, is reduced to calculation, another of the conditions of scope is not met, and Paper 5 reads this: where, in this end's own fallible judgment, this condition of scope is no longer met, he from then on no longer does this undertaking as the cooperation this series reads; what this series reads is this judgment of his and his side from then on, and it does not on this basis certify that the other is reduced to calculation (Paper 5, CO27). Within cooperation inside scope there are several aspects that are not read: the institutional side of contracts, organizations and employment, acknowledged but not judged, and only registered; settlement, that is, how credit, authorship and proceeds are divided (Paper 7). Boundaries this series does not handle: between humans and animals, and between humans and AI; this is a choice of range, not a determination of whether they hold the position of a bearer.

Fifth, the upper bound. The C this series reads is, as in Action Theory Paper 8, doing something together, and does not require both ends to be headed in the 15DD direction. When two one-way 15DDs meet at a moment of doing this undertaking together, that is the moment of two-way 15DD, and this series takes up only that moment; it does not touch 16DD itself; mutual non dubito that can hold at any time and in any place it does not take up, and only registers, without proving that it does not exist; nor does it take up the change in the individual at that moment (Paper 7, CO38; §7).20

Sixth, the boundaries with neighboring series: see §1.

Main line. In plain prose it is one sentence: You for yours, I for mine, and we do this one thing together.

Put technically, as the main text and the abstracts of the papers use it: two people, each for his own purpose, do together one thing that both avow and in which each has a share; neither party holds the purpose and adjudication of this undertaking alone, nor does it automatically produce, above the two, a "we" that legislates for both. The first half is what Paper 2 reads as each for their own ends: the two people's purposes are each one's own, neither examines the other's, and where what they want is in one place it is still two shares. The second half is Paper 1's structure and Paper 4's "we": each has a share, and neither holds it alone; the ordinary plural holds as usual, only no position is set up that legislates for both.

At the core of the passage on scope in Paper 1 is the one sentence that states scope: at least one person, when doing things together, does not treat the other merely as a means. This sentence sums up the entrance; it is not an equivalent substitute for all the conditions of scope. It points to the direction premise, and is wider than the direction premise: where both ends lean toward 14DD and neither is reduced to calculation, this sentence takes the case in, and scope does not (Paper 1, CO5).

What also strings the seven papers together is the division into three things. Direction: one party is enough, and it is the direction premise; participation: both are needed, and it is cooperation itself; judgment: each party uses his own judgment and answers for his own share, and it is where the remainder that neither norms nor rules cover falls. Judgment is not the 15DD direction, so "for direction one party is enough" and "in judgment both are needed" do not conflict (Paper 1, CO3). The kind in which one end is headed in the 15DD direction and one leans toward 14DD (Paper 5), each bearing a share (Paper 6), and the process (Paper 7) are all read under these three things.

§3 Method

The whole series is written by the following rules.

Three layers. What this series reads is spoken of in three layers. The normative layer is the lines of structure and direction that Relation Theory and the series further upstream have already laid down; the rule layer is readings and arrangements that hold only in one kind of relation, and concrete rules serve only as examples; the judgment layer is the remainder that neither norms nor rules cover, which falls on each person's own judgment, and it is on the account of the one who judges. The Theory of Inter-State Cooperation and Political Economy also speak in three layers; there the ought is said to constructs, and the judgment layer is handed to those in positions: "The judgment layer is how a particular act is judged under the rules; this layer is left to the judgment of those in positions, and the account falls on positions and persons."21 That is the institutional side. This series issues no ought; between two people there is no one in a position to judge for them, and the judgment layer falls on each person himself. To speak of bearing responsibility is to speak of the account falling on the one who judges, facing inward: the subject's answerability to its own law.22

A layer records one operation; stances, not labels. Neither party is said to be lower; the stances of the two ends are not inferred backward from the direction of one effect. Where an arrangement the two jointly avow and maintain is premised on "a third person is merely a means," it is read by the arrangement's premise, and this does not count as inferring backward from an effect.

Speak of structure, not of good and bad; issue no ought. Cooperation is not ranked by length, by gain and loss, or by whether it got done; whether to go on or to withdraw, whether to cooperate or not, is the judgment of those concerned.

Do not read minds; do not predict. Inference goes by structure, or by the original sentences of upstream; how a person thinks is, from outside, only clues. Where an example lacks conditions, it is left undetermined, and not filled in with an inner narrative added afterward.

Only the layer between person and person. The authority that contracts, organizations, employment and positions bring is acknowledged but not judged, and registered as a condition of the effect. Acknowledging but not judging draws the range this series reads: registering an authority is not endorsing its legitimacy on its behalf, nor is it saying that the effects within the institution have all become unproblematic; how that side is to be evaluated belongs to the institutional side (Paper 4). The obligations a contract gives belong to the institutional side; at the layer between person and person, commitment does not turn into a duty to the other party, and the other's response is material, not a settling of the account.23 Where workplace examples are used, a factual record of the relevant provisions is given first, and what is not given is recorded as not checked.

Read only the process, not the settlement. Endings are not classified; how credit, authorship and proceeds are divided is not read; how causation, fault and remedy are allocated is not unfolded in this series (Paper 6, Paper 7).

Cultivation and colonization in the broad sense. They name the direction of one effect, not labels stuck on persons or on a stretch of cooperation: cultivation goes toward making space; colonization goes toward pouring in and occupying; the two are not the two extremes of one scale; the same act can carry both directions, and can also carry no direction at all; the direction is read on this one effect, and whether it took hold is seen in the other party's later understanding and action. Violation is judged separately, and only at the layer of basic rights.24 Saying out loud a characterization that fixes for the other what something means to him slides in the direction of colonization; making something unchangeable for the other in running, or making his path to saying no hard to travel, carries the direction of colonization. "Tendency" and "slides" are not read as having already taken hold.

Exchange is not calculation; division of labor is consensus. Calculation names only one stance: treating the other only as a strategy set. Looking out for one's own concerns is not calculation, and neither is keeping clear accounts. "Exchange" is read in two ways; see §4.

Examples. Only adult characters are used; made-up examples are used only to see structure, and each paper has its own set of people; two people's words and deeds are only clues, and do not certify who is at which layer. One sentence can take on different effects in different arrangements, and the examples are often written as forking examples. Friendship serves as a running example: Yu Ping and He Shan mending a fence in Paper 1, Ke Yuan and Liang Dong running the repair corner in Paper 2, Wei An and Jing He running a secondhand bookshop together in Paper 3, and Xia Chi and Kuang Ming running a woodworking class together in Paper 4 are all cooperation between friends; the face of mutual recognition between friends is read by Relation Theory, and this series reads only the thing they do together.25 Historical examples are read for structure and direction only by the words and deeds in the historical sources; only the deceased, whose records are public, are used, and at which layer those two people were is not judged; Paper 2 reads Guan Zhong and Bao Shuya, and Engels and Marx.

Interfacing with tradition. The papers each interface with figures by question, and this paper interfaces as a whole. The papers keep to one rule: no one is cast in advance as the opposing side; first it is written faithfully what that figure asks, why he looks, and what he sees; where a passage of the original quoted really runs against this series, it is written at the original's strength, and then where they meet and where they part; where there is no real opposition, only how they shed light on each other and how the questions divide the work are written; a counter-proposition that is not in the original is not invented for it. The steps the papers take are not all the same: Paper 1 writes the common question, where they shed light on each other, and where they part; Papers 2, 3 and 4 first write why that figure looks and what he sees, write out the opposition where there really is one, and then where they meet and where they part, and where there is none, write only how they shed light on each other, or at which scale the question lies; Paper 5 writes the common question and where they differ; Paper 6 goes in four steps: the common question, whether it is the same thing, where they shed light on each other and where they part, how far agreement goes; Paper 7 writes what was seen, where they shed light on each other, and how the questions divide the work. The interfacing as a whole in this paper writes the common question, where they shed light on each other, and where they part. What has already been interfaced at the layer of relation and in neighboring series is pointed back to; when it is pointed back to, the restrictions of that place come along.

Sources and extensions. The conclusions of upstream papers are said in plain words in the main text; sources, original sentences and the proposition numbers of other papers go in the notes; what is carried beyond its original place is stated to be an extension, this series' own undertaking.

§4 Shared Words

Several words are shared across the papers, and their senses have to be kept apart.

Cooperation: 合作 and 协作. The "cooperation" this series speaks of refers only to the kind it reads, and does not define all cooperation. The Theory of Inter-State Cooperation reads cooperation between state and state, between construct and construct; in Chinese the two series use different words, that series 协作 and this series 合作, and the former is not used here as a term. The "cooperative game" of the Games of Judgment series is one kind of game: the partner's next step is his, and is also part of what the two want together;26 it can be borrowed to look at one aspect of doing things together, and is not the same as the cooperation this series reads. The prisoner's dilemma example Paper 1 adopts labels the one action of not betraying the other "cooperation"; that label and the cooperation this series reads are not one word, nor does this series define the word for game theory as a whole (Paper 1).

The normative layer, the rule layer, the judgment layer; rules. This series' three layers: see §3; the three layers of the Theory of Inter-State Cooperation and of Political Economy speak to constructs, and hand the judgment layer to those in positions. The "rules" of the rule layer are readings and arrangements that hold only in one kind of relation; when Relation Theory and Marriage Theory say that "the provisions themselves are acknowledged but not judged," what they speak of are the rules of institutions. The two are not mixed.

Calculation, exchange, giving. Calculation is a stance: treating the other only as a strategy set (Paper 1). "Exchange" is of two kinds: an arrangement of reciprocal exchange the two have avowed, you cover for me this week and I cover for you next week, does not make this undertaking exit scope, and in cooperation is not read as falling back either; writing one's own giving as an account the other must repay is another matter, and it does not change with the kind of relation (Paper 2). Exchange in the Emergence Layer, as Political Economy speaks of it, is a third usage, on the institutional side (§1). Giving and getting speak of the time, effort, money and things each puts into this undertaking, and of what each gets from the other; to say that neither got anything is to say plainly that nothing was got, and no invisible gain is added afterward (Paper 2).

Leaning toward 14DD, and being reduced to calculation. In this undertaking, the end that leans toward 14DD and is within scope at least structurally acknowledges that the other has purposes of his own; to be reduced to calculation is to treat the other only as a strategy set. Learning IV places what it calls recognizing the other's independent 14DD on the bridge from 14DD to 15DD; the end that leans toward 14DD and is within scope is, in this undertaking, at least already at this step. The 14DD world Economics P2 speaks of, in which the person across from you is a strategy set, falls in this series on the side of being reduced to calculation. Reading the two places against each other in this way is this series' own undertaking (Paper 5; §8, the thirteenth remainder).27

Purpose and end. In the Chinese of this series these are one word. In the shape of doing things together, A and B are each one's purposes, and C is the thing done together (Paper 2). The end in "recognizing the other as an end too" is at 15DD: the other has his own cannot-not.

Doing things together, a shared goal, a shared ultimate value. Doing things together is two people doing one thing together, not a shared law. Where what the two want is in one place, that is a shared goal; each one's value belongings stay as they were, and it is not a shared ultimate value (Paper 2).

Law and arrangement; division of labor. Law is the layer a person bears in the first person and by which he goes on organizing his action; arrangement is how time, resources, roles, procedures and the conditions of action are laid out. Division of labor lies at the layer of arrangement; it is a present arrangement the two have avowed together, and it is consensus, not calculation: division of labor is an arrangement, calculation is a stance, and the two are not in one place. Two people who treat each other only as a strategy set can also each avow a very detailed division of labor; this kind is not within scope on the ground of scope's premise, not on the ground that they have not avowed it (Paper 3).

Procedure and process. "Procedure" refers only to the method the two have recognized beforehand, to which a certain class of matters is handed to decide, and to external mechanisms that have authority by law, or legitimate authority for protecting basic rights; what is called a "workflow" in everyday speech is part of the division of labor, and is not in this sense (Paper 3). "Process" speaks of what the thing done together looks like over time (Paper 7). The two words are not mixed.

"We." Everyday speech has three uses of it. One is speaking of things together, putting what several people have each done into one sentence; the second is one of the branches Relation Theory divides, doing things together; the third is the other branch, mutual recognition. Neither of the latter two branches is a "we" that stands above the several people and makes decisions for them (Paper 4).

Construct; bearer and the address of attribution. "Construct" has two uses. When Relation Theory says "A relation is a construct," it takes the broad sense: the construct left when the effect of one side enters the conditions of occurrence of the other side, which does not legislate and bears nothing.28 The construct Political Economy speaks of is an arrangement with institutional status: a company, a foundation and a level of government are all constructs; it "is the address of attribution, not the subject of the act".29 A bearer, as in Relation Theory, is the one at one end of a relation: "The other side of a relation must be a bearer".30 The address of attribution is the construct taken as the place where the account falls first. Where two friends register their small shop as a company, the company is the address of attribution, not a third bearer (Paper 3, Paper 4).

Going in together. In everyday speech, two friends going in together to open a shop means doing this thing together; a "registered partner" or "shareholder" after registration is a position the institution gives (Paper 3).

Answering for; accountability; liability and responsibility; duty. Answering for, as this series speaks of it, faces inward: judgment and what has been done cannot be acknowledged by someone else in one's place, the account falls on the one who judges, and the subject answers to its own law; this stands together with commitment's not turning into a duty to the other party, and is not owing the other party a duty.31 "Accountability" and "liability" take the sense of On Accountability: accountability is a demand standing at a position and awaiting an answer, and what it binds is the position; liability is the bearing of that position itself, and is not conditioned on fault; both cells are on the institutional side (Paper 6). When the Conflict Treatise says that "first-person responsibility cannot be borne by proxy," it uses the word in its everyday sense, and what it says is exactly that what has been done is not acknowledged by someone else in one's place.32

Reputation and fame. They are used apart, as in the Political Economy Prequel (§1). The word "reputation," in the Moral Law series, stands on the scope of the pure 15DD community.33 When this series writes of breach of faith being entered on the books, it does not borrow "reputation": the layer between the two, and between pairs, is entered as it is; how many people know of it once it has spread, and how it is talked about, is fame; the side on which reputation, or a score taken as standing for it, is used by a position as a condition of a chokepoint decision belongs to Political Economy (Paper 6).

Judge the operation, not the person; stances, not labels. The former is a convention of the whole Political Economy series; the latter is the way this series reads 15DD and 14DD; the two run the same way, each on its own side.34

Non-compete agreements and conditional payments. As used in Political Economy Paper 2. The former is backed by liquidated damages, injunction or compulsory enforcement, and forbids a person, after leaving, to go in a certain direction; the latter forbids nothing, but only ties an additional payment to a choice. Which side a particular agreement falls on is checked by its three criteria taken together, and is not decided by any one alone (Paper 3).35

Completion and settlement. "Completion" has two uses: the standard of completion the two have avowed beforehand, and the completion of cultivation, which is not in the enactor's hands; when Paper 7 speaks of completion, it means the former. "Settlement" means how credit, authorship and proceeds are divided, who owes whom how much, worked out to a total; this series does not read it (Paper 7).

Withdrawing, exit and walking away. Withdrawing one's own side, no longer putting in effort, is exit; it faces inward, and does not carry the direction of colonization, even if the other party's cost rises as a result.36 "One's own side" means the share that is still one's own once the items have been separated: deliveries already avowed and other matters still standing are checked separately, item by item, as they were; whether an act is on one's own side is not decided by the person's calling it a boundary (Paper 5). Where one first monopolizes, or first induces dependency, and then withdraws, violation is checked separately at the layer of basic rights per Relation Theory; actual dependency alone does not on its own yield the conclusion of violation.37 Walking away is only an external act, and takes no technical identity by itself (Paper 5, Paper 7).

Cultivation; discernment. Cultivation is taken in the broad sense: it is the direction of one effect, not a project, nor anyone's identity. Discernment speaks of what the end headed in the 15DD direction reads from the situation in his own judgment; it is a fallible judgment, not a certification (Paper 5).

Collusion. Two people jointly maintain an arrangement premised on "a third person is merely a means"; what is read is the premise of that arrangement, not the persons (§2, the fourth item of scope). This series follows the usage of the Theory of Inter-State Cooperation and of Power Theory, and uses "collusion" (Paper 1).38

The 16DD cooperative imperative, and D47. Action Theory says that the form of its D47, one person is for purpose A, another for purpose B, and the two cannot not do C, has been set down elsewhere in this system; this refers to the 16DD cooperative imperative listed in Reason Cannot Not, I.3: I for purpose A, the other for purpose B, we cannot not do C; that place states further that the complete definition of the cooperative imperative is given in another paper. Action Theory borrows it to read doing things together.39 In III.4 of Reason Cannot Not, the C of the cooperative imperative carries a further condition: C, as the emergence after the meeting of two purposes, requires both subjects to expose themselves to the other's interrogation. The C this series reads follows Action Theory: it is doing something together, does not carry this condition, and does not require both ends to be headed in the 15DD direction; getting one thing done together is not automatically the moment of two-way 15DD (Paper 1, Paper 7; §7).

§5 Interfacing as a Whole

The papers each interfaced with figures by question. Paper 1 interfaced with the prisoner's dilemma example, Axelrod, Hume on two men rowing a boat, Rousseau on the stag hunt, and Hobbes; Paper 2 with Smith on our dinner and self-love, Aristotle on friendship for usefulness, Kropotkin on mutual aid, and the Analects on harmony without sameness (和而不同); Paper 3 with Plato on one man, one thing, Durkheim on organic solidarity, and Smith's pin manufactory; Paper 4 with Rousseau's general will, Searle's collective intentionality, and the Analects on being sociable but not partisan (群而不党); Paper 5 with the Analects on repaying resentment with uprightness (以直报怨) and Kant's unsocial sociability; Paper 6 with Weber's ethic of responsibility, Arendt on guilt and collective responsibility, and the Analects on being heavy on oneself (躬自厚); Paper 7 with Aristotle on how a friendship of usefulness is dissolved, Arendt on the irreversibility and unpredictability of action, and the hexagrams Jiji and Weiji (既济, 未济), already across and not yet across, in the Book of Changes. Gilbert's joint commitment and plural subject Relation Theory has already interfaced with, and the papers point back to it. Several figures ask about the whole series, and are placed here.

Competition and mutual aid (Hobbes, Kropotkin). Each of the two has an overall reading of people's doing things together. In Chapter 17 of Leviathan, Hobbes writes that covenants without the sword are only words, and have no power at all to keep anyone safe; his answer lies in a common power.40 In the Introduction to Mutual Aid, Kropotkin takes up an idea of the zoologist Kessler: that in Nature there is, besides a law of mutual struggle, a law of mutual aid; he says that his book treats mutual aid as one of the main factors of evolution, without covering all the factors and how much each counts; where the instinct of mutual aid comes from in Nature he leaves to later research.41 The common question: what keeps people's doing things together going, and what part it plays among people. One of the two lands on force, the other on mutual aid; this series casts neither in advance as the opposing side, nor does it decide between the two.

They shed light on each other at two points. First, Hobbes asks what makes a covenant bind, and answers with a common power at the level of the commonwealth; that level lies between persons and constructs, belongs to the institutional side, and this series does not judge it (Paper 1). At the level between two people, the binding this series reads does not rest on the sword: commitment binds a person, on my side by where I stand, and on the other party's side by the effects already left; breaking it is entered on the books (Paper 1, Paper 6). The two are not reading the same level. Second, Kropotkin saw that giving to one another and helping one another happen in fact, just as competition does; this series too reads giving that is not conditioned on getting a return from the other (Paper 2). What he reads is the part mutual aid plays in species and in evolution; the part mutual aid plays for a group or a species is not the same as the return the one who gives gets on this occasion, nor the same as what he gave for; the two can stand together, and need not refute each other. This series does not add an invisible gain to the kind in which no one benefits (Paper 2).

They part over what is asked. Each of the two gives an overall reading: Hobbes reads how covenants come to have force, Kropotkin reads what part mutual aid plays in evolution; this series does not read where cooperation comes from, nor what part it plays in evolution, and reads only what structure the thing done together has. Its scope is a premise, not a theory of human nature: that at least one party recognizes the other as an end too in this undertaking does not yield that everyone does so, nor that no one does.

Inclusive love for one another, exchange of benefit with one another (Mozi). Relation Theory has already interfaced with Mozi, reading him on inclusive love for one another and exchange of benefit with one another (兼相爱，交相利), and on its feasibility; the restriction of that place is that the several figures all bring into view persisting practices beyond one-sided acts, and cannot, on these sentences alone, be taken as one and the same theory of a duty to return.42 Here only one sentence points back, and brings that restriction along: exchange of benefit with one another is close, among the shapes this series reads, to reciprocal exchange, and this series does not derive from it a duty to return; inclusive love for one another is not this series' premise; this series requires only that at least one party recognize the other as an end too in this undertaking, and does not require love.

A fair system of cooperation (Rawls). Rawls reads society as a fair system of cooperation over time, from one generation to the next, and asks by what principles the basic structure of such a system is to be ordered.43 That is a question of the institutional side, and this series places him only as a neighbor: it reads the thing two people do together, not the basic structure of society, nor does it argue for principles of distribution.

Shared intentionality (Tomasello). Tomasello reads shared intentionality in human cooperation from developmental and comparative research, and asks where the capacity to cooperate comes from; this series does not read where the capacity comes from, and places him only as a neighbor (Paper 1).

Perpetual peace (Kant). In Perpetual Peace Kant says that a league between states need not be a state of nations; the Theory of Inter-State Cooperation has already interfaced with him.44 Cooperation between states belongs to that series; it is pointed back to here, and not rewritten.

§6 The Directions of Colonization Read in the Papers, Said Once Together

How the directions of colonization the papers have found are read lies in each paper; here only one layer is said together: what they borrow are things that are, or may come to be, in a cooperation. These things carry no direction in themselves; whether they carry one is seen from the antecedents of that one effect, not from the name of the act:

  • Avowal: declaring an avowal on the other's behalf slides in the direction of colonization; a faithful report is not among these (Paper 1).
  • Names ("we," the team, the project, the company, or the undertaking itself): when taken up to take in and replace one party's direction, they carry the direction of colonization; making a request, or proposing an arrangement within authority that has been checked, is not of this kind merely because a name was used, and registering the authority does not endorse its legitimacy either (Paper 2, Paper 4).
  • Division of labor: when it is turned against the other, treated as something he may not change, hung on who he is, or swapped out within habit, it has a tendency toward colonization; not avowing a new division of labor is not, on that alone, sealing off a path; only where a change already avowed is held down by way of a link in its execution is this checked separately (Paper 3).
  • Procedure: where it is called a third party, yet in running the party questioned still controls the review, the adjudication and the delivery, it is the same thing going on under another name; unilateral nomination, and its having once been recognized, are both only clues (Paper 3).
  • Giving, reciprocal exchange and contribution: when the other is treated merely as the condition for getting something, when settlement is extended to the person, when giving is written as an account to be repaid, when giving or contribution is used to possess the other, to take his share of judgment in exchange, to complete his step for him, or to record his share as one's own (Paper 2, Paper 6).
  • Friendship: when it is borrowed to pass final judgment on a thing done together (Paper 4).
  • The name of cultivation, and dependency: when the other's direction is decided for him in the name of cultivation; when dependency has been made and has locked the path to saying no and to changing over, which cannot be read from exclusive use alone, nor ruled out by an initial nod alone; incomplete cultivation is not colonization (Paper 2, Paper 5).
  • Exit: withdrawing the side that is still one's own once the items have been separated faces inward, and does not carry it; saying for the other what this exit amounts to, or fixing his path from then on, slides toward it; acting in a way directed at him that raises his cost, after withdrawing, carries it (Paper 5, Paper 7).
  • An arrangement's premise regarding a third person: the stretch that carries the direction of colonization toward a third person or crosses his basic rights is not within scope, and where it is premised on a third person's being merely a means, it is collusion; mere coincidence in time, competition that makes someone lose out, and one person's addition kept from his partner are not among these (Paper 1, Paper 4).

One and the same sentence, "For this project, everyone has to come in this weekend," is a request in one arrangement, the authority of a position in another, and only in a third the borrowing of a name to take in and replace; one and the same money for parts can be only a piece of giving, and can also be spoken of as an account; one and the same withdrawal is exit, not acting in a way directed at the other. The direction is read on the one effect in which they are taken up to do something; whether it took hold is seen in how the other afterward understands and acts.

Not heading toward colonization in doing things together is not easy: the things done together are many, and each is a place that can be taken up to do something else. So relations are all mixed: mixing is the normal state, and a pure 15DD community is only an ideal type.45 What this series reads is direction; a single sliding is read on that one occasion, and it is not on that basis judged that a stretch of cooperation has exited scope. Exit from scope is read by Paper 1's three kinds commonly met outside scope; where, in one end's own fallible judgment, the other is already reduced to calculation in this undertaking, this end from then on no longer does this undertaking as the cooperation this series reads, and this is read by Paper 5; this series does not on that basis certify that the other is reduced to calculation. This series does not on this account say that cooperation is dangerous, nor does it on this account urge people to cooperate or not to cooperate; it only reads.

§7 The Moment of Two-Way 15DD: Two Faces

In SAE's levels, 16DD is mutual non dubito: two subjects "mutually acknowledge each other as ends in themselves, mutually chisel, mutually do not doubt."46 This series does not touch 16DD itself: what it reads is only two one-way 15DDs meeting at a moment, recorded as the moment of two-way 15DD; what that moment has to do with 16DD, this series does not judge. Relation Theory reads this moment as something that happens on an occasion, not a grade that a person or a relation is in; it takes up only that moment; it does not touch 16DD itself, nor does it take up mutual non dubito that can hold at any time and in any place, nor the change in the individual at that moment.47 Love Theory and this series both take up only that moment, and they take up two faces of it.

Love Theory takes it up from the face of mutual recognition. The moment it reads is two one-way loves meeting, each recognizing the other as an end too, without waiting for the other to recognize first; loving each other is not exchange, and "I love you, so you ought to love me" writes love as exchange.48 The mutual recognition of that moment belongs to the branch of mutual recognition.

This series takes it up from the face of the thing done together. The moment it reads is two one-way 15DDs meeting at a moment of doing this undertaking together (Paper 7, CO38). In Paper 7's example, two researchers argued all night over the tone of one character, each asking after the other's grounds, and in the end decided on a notation neither of them had thought of before; these are what actually happened that night, not the criterion of that moment, and that moment is not conditioned on having argued all night, or on having arrived at a new way of doing things. The C this series reads follows Action Theory: it is doing something together; the further condition Reason Cannot Not, III.4, attaches to the C of the cooperative imperative, that it "requires both subjects to expose themselves to the other's interrogation", this series does not carry (§4).49 Reason Cannot Not has one more sentence, which is only registered here, and not touched: "The cooperative imperative ('we cannot not do C') is the produce of 16DD's operation — C is the third term emerging from two autonomous subjects' mutual interrogation."50

The two faces are the same at four points. First, both take up only the moment: it is something that happens on an occasion, not a grade that a love or a stretch of cooperation is in. Second, it does not make later dealings mutual automatically, nor does it let one party afterward demand mutuality of the other.51 Third, from outside only clues can be seen: a pair of lovers looking at each other, a night's argument and a new notation, are none of them criteria. Fourth, mutual non dubito that can hold at any time and in any place neither series takes up; each only registers it, and does not prove that it does not exist.52

The two faces differ in where the moment lies: on the face of mutual recognition, that moment is two people recognizing each other; on the face of the thing done together, that moment falls on this thing the two do together, two one-way 15DDs meeting while doing this undertaking; whether a new way of doing things came out of that moment is not its criterion. Two further points are written out. First, this series' scope requires only that at least one party be headed in the 15DD direction, but that moment requires both ends to be; in this series, the upper bound and the premise of scope are not one and the same condition. Getting one thing done together is not automatically that moment; on the great majority of days, the cooperation this series reads is not that moment (Paper 7). Second, the two faces can share one occasion: when two people who love each other do one thing together, and that moment both is their recognizing each other and falls on this thing they do together, which branch occurs is checked case by case, and is not assigned in advance by the kind of relation.

§8 Remainders

Several things this series registers, and does not close out.

First, carrying "each has a share" in the thing done together over to several people, and carrying Relation Theory's little circle of two over to a little circle of several people: these two steps are this series' own undertaking, and are written only where they are used (Paper 4).

Second, whether a team or a project can separately be established as a bearer. Relation Theory establishes only that a little circle of two does not form a third bearer; Relation Theory does not touch the question of when a community has become a collective subject, and this series does not touch it either (Paper 4).

Third, 16DD itself is not touched; mutual non dubito that can hold at any time and in any place is not taken up, nor is the change in the individual at that moment (§7).

Fourth, between humans and animals, and between humans and AI: a choice of range, not a determination about ontology; the quasi-subjecthood of AI is taken up separately in SAE's AI series.

Fifth, whether a relation in which both know is more stable. Relation Theory registered this sentence as handed downstream to be argued, which each branch, if it wants to use it, argues for itself;53 the papers of this series have not used it, and it is only registered, not argued.

Sixth, the stretch in which, when one cannot get out, institutions take over. Economics P2 reads as far as this; it belongs to the institutional side, and this series only registers it.54

Seventh, how causation, fault and remedy are allocated within cooperation; what Action Theory handed over, what others each bear and who institutionally is in charge of what: checked separately by On Accountability, and not unfolded in this series (Paper 6).

Eighth, institutional relations between company and company, and between states and companies. The Theory of Inter-State Cooperation states that it does not take them up, and Political Economy registers them as objects adjacent to its jurisdiction; this series does not take them up either.55

Ninth, how it is read when several undertakings between two people are tangled together. This series reads by the undertaking, entering it item by item and stretch by stretch, and gives no overall reading for a person; with the same two people, the end that operates in the 15DD direction in one undertaking may lean toward 14DD in another. Of the kind in which several undertakings are tangled together, Paper 5 confines its analysis here to a single undertaking.

Tenth, whether an undertaking has been done, where the two go on reading it differently to the end. This series stops at entering the readings apart, and gives it no position of adjudication (Paper 7).

Eleventh, Gilbert's joint commitment. Paper 1 points back to where Relation Theory interfaces with her: at the layer of qualification, when a party withdraws his own side, it is withdrawn, only it does not clear the books on the other party's side. After interfacing with her, Relation Theory wrote: "this shows what it keeps, and does not amount to having already explained all of Gilbert's normative features".56 This restriction comes along to the layer of doing things together, and this series has not made it good.

Twelfth, doing things together in which both ends lean toward 14DD and neither is reduced to calculation, yet neither recognizes the other as an end too in this undertaking. It lacks the direction premise, is not within scope, and this series does not read it; from outside it cannot be told apart from the ideal type of the first kind outside scope, and since what both lack is the direction premise, there is no need to tell them apart. Where one end later comes to be headed in this direction, this is entered by period, and from then on it is checked against scope anew (Paper 1).

Thirteenth, how leaning toward 14DD and the 14DD world Economics P2 speaks of are read against each other. This series places that world on the side of being reduced to calculation, and places the end that leans toward 14DD and is within scope on this step of the bridge Learning IV speaks of; this reading against each other is this series' own undertaking; the scope of that sentence of Economics P2 itself this series does not decide for it, and only registers here (§4; Paper 5).

§9 The Propositions of the Whole Series

The propositions of the papers are listed together in the very words of each paper's propositions, with only a full stop added at the end; argument and qualification are in each paper.

Paper 1, Doing Together

CO1　This series does not define all cooperation. The cooperation it reads has as its premise that at least one party is headed in the 15DD direction; the 15DD direction is a premise, not a component of cooperation, nor a sufficient condition for entering scope. 15DD and 14DD here are stances, not labels for persons; they are read by the current relational condition, not by persons; falling back is read as it occurs, and does not overturn the direction.

CO2　Under this premise, the cooperation this series reads is this: two people, each for his own purpose, do together one thing that both avow and in which each has a share; neither party holds the purpose and adjudication of this undertaking alone. Each one's share can consist in taking up his own part according to the other's arrangement; it can consist in saying what he wants and deciding for himself how it is to be done. Merely having the qualification to refuse or to call a halt is not on that account having done a share; whether a choice, an indication, a correction or a pause actually made is a share depends on how it enters the shared way of doing it: pausing the whole operation in order to correct the shared way of doing it can still be one's own share; only where calling a halt is withdrawing one's own side, that of going on taking part, is it read as exit; the two are not told apart by how much is halted. Merely ceasing to go on being helped does not on that account become cooperation.

CO3　Direction, participation and judgment govern different things: for direction, one party is enough, and it is the direction premise; for participation, both are needed, and it is cooperation itself; in judgment, each party uses his own judgment and answers for his own share. Judgment is not the 15DD direction.

CO4　The object layer and the operation layer are kept apart: what the object layer reads is cooperation that has as its direction premise that at least one end is headed in the 15DD direction, and that is checked further for both parties' avowing and actually taking part, for not being reduced to calculation, and for not colonizing a third person. At the operation layer each party confirms only his own end, and cannot on that basis declare on the other end's behalf that it avows; the other end's taking part and arrangements are described by words and deeds and checkable conditions, and their inner grounds are not certified. If it is later confirmed that the object did not obtain, this series exits this case, and does not turn and judge the other end.

CO5　Three kinds are commonly met outside scope; each is read differently, and all are written as "not within the cooperation this series reads": that in which neither party is headed in the 15DD direction, whose ideal type is the case in which both parties treat each other only as a strategy set and reach a balance by mutual calculation; that of being passively helped, which is an act of help, read as one-way dealings per Relation Theory; and the stretch in which the arrangement the two jointly avow and actually maintain carries the direction of colonization toward a third person or crosses his basic rights. One person's later addition, or a use kept from the partner, is no ground, merely because it bears the same project's name, for inferring backward that the other avowed it too; that competition makes a third person lose out is not the same as colonizing him. If the arrangement the two jointly maintain is premised on "a third person is merely a means," the arrangement itself decides who counts as an end by whether someone is one of our own, and the recognition between the two does not rest on the other's being a person either; in this undertaking neither end is headed in the 15DD direction. One who was at first passively helped, once he avows this undertaking and takes up his own share, and the other conditions are met, is within scope from then on. Where one end is headed in the 15DD direction and the other, in this undertaking, is reduced to calculation, another of the conditions of scope is not met; Paper 5 reads this (CO27).

CO6　Between the balance of mutual calculation and the cooperation this series reads, the markers of the boundary look at three structural clues: whether not holding alone is having recognized that the other's share is the other's, or only that neither can hold it alone; whether the other's share is still borne by him himself; whether the existing arrangement makes room for his refusal and revision. The clues say only where the boundary lies; they do not certify inner recognition, nor prove that that kind is not cooperation. What the three clues tell apart is whether or not one is reduced to calculation; whether at least one party is headed in the 15DD direction only that end itself can confirm (CO4), and the clues do not certify it on its behalf.

Paper 2, Each for Their Own Ends

CO7　The two people's purposes are each one's own, and neither examines the other's; what a given person's purpose is, only his own end can confirm. A person's being willing to support the other's purpose can become a share he himself avows, but the word "willing" alone does not prove that it has reached his own cannot-not; a purpose is not avowed by the other on one's behalf, and not examining purposes does not mean that the action a purpose calls for, its grounds and its effects on others are from then on beyond questioning.

CO8　Cooperation can rest on exchange, but need not. There can be an arrangement of reciprocal exchange; it can be that one end mainly gives conditions, not on condition that the other gives anything back; it can be that both parties give, not on condition of obtaining a return from the other, and neither gets anything. The three are shapes this paper has picked out; they can interweave, and are not an exhaustive classification of purposes and dealings; the kind in which in fact no one benefits is not made over into one that must carry a hidden gain. Cooperation looks at the thing done together, not at what each gets from the other.

CO9　"Exchange" is read in two ways. An arrangement of reciprocal exchange that both parties avow does not make this undertaking exit scope; what exits scope is treating the other merely as a means. That Marriage Theory reads a moment of reciprocal exchange as falling back comes from marriage's having love as one thing it cannot do without, and belongs to the rule layer: this difference can be traced to the kind of relation. Writing one's own giving as an account the other must repay is another matter: it lies at the normative layer and does not change with the kind of relation; in marriage it is read by Marriage Theory and Relation Theory, and in cooperation it is read the same way at the layer between persons, and is not exempted because the relation is called cooperation; settlement in a contract belongs, separately, to the side of the institution. The moment of reciprocal exchange marked as falling back in marriage and the direction of colonization are not one question. Nor is exchange calculation: calculation is the stance of treating the other only as a strategy set.

CO10　Where what the two want lies in one place, there are still two shares: each one's value belongings stay as they were, while the arrangement of action falls in one place; a shared goal is not a shared ultimate value.

CO11　The directions of colonization commonly met on these shapes: in reciprocal exchange, treating the other merely as the condition for getting something, or extending settlement from the side of the contract onto the person; writing one's own giving as an account the other must repay; where one end gives conditions, using one's giving to possess the other, completing the other's step for him, or making him dependent to the point where he cannot say no; where both give, the undertaking itself being raised into a legislator, and one party's law being judged for him under "for the sake of this undertaking" or "for everyone's sake".

Paper 3, Division of Labor

CO12　Division of labor in cooperation is an arrangement the two have avowed together; it is consensus, not calculation; its binding force passes separately through each party's own law, or comes from a procedure both parties have already recognized.

CO13　Division of labor is a present arrangement; it can be avowed anew, and it can be changed. A change into a new shared division of labor must be avowed by both parties separately, or be made by a procedure both have recognized and that applies; this does not make either party the approver of the other's own revision and exit. Unilateral withdrawal does not impose a new arrangement on the other party, nor does it erase the effects already left.

CO14　Where division of labor slides into a fixed requirement aimed at the other, by being treated as something the other may not change, by being hung on who the other is, or by being swapped out within habit, there is a tendency toward colonization: saying out loud a characterization of the division of labor slides in the direction of colonization; making it unchangeable for the other in running carries the direction of colonization. One party's own treating the division of labor as unchangeable is his self-relation; structurally it has no outward direction.

CO15　Organizations, contracts, employment, superiors and subordinates: the provisions, and the authority that comes with positions, are acknowledged but not judged, and are registered as conditions of effects; Cooperation Theory reads only the side the institution does not provide for, or that goes beyond its provisions: what passes between persons. Within a construct, a registered partner is in a position, and belongs to the institutional side; an agreement backed by liquidated damages, injunction or compulsory enforcement that forbids one party, after leaving, to go in a certain direction, that is, a non-compete agreement, is read by Political Economy Paper 2, E12a, and a conditional payment is read differently, also by E12a; the other party to the cooperation is himself the party that sets the agreement, and if what the agreement protects is his own share, it is not among those "set to protect others." The obligations a contract gives belong to the institutional side; at the layer between persons, commitment does not turn into a duty to the other party, and the other party's response is material, not a settling of the account.

CO16　A procedure both have recognized beforehand is handing this one matter over, not a position that legislates for the two; a judgment made by a procedure both have recognized and that applies governs how this one matter is ordered, is valid for the action arrangement, and does not turn every outcome into something that needs consent obtained anew. Whether a procedure has already been recognized by both parties, or has some other fit ground, is checked apart from whether, in running, it is still controlled by the party questioned. A third party selected unilaterally by the stronger side may be only an extension of that side's power: unilateral nomination is a clue to be checked, and does not by itself prove unfitness; nor does having once been recognized, or having been set up by law, by itself prove that this dispute has been removed from the original control. If the party questioned can still, on its own, seal off review of itself, or controls the related adjudication and its delivery, it cannot be held, on the strength of "handed to a third party" alone, that the dispute has been removed; that is the same thing continuing under another name. Ordinary dissatisfaction does not directly prove that the procedure has been captured.

Paper 4, "We"

CO17　Doing things together does not automatically produce a position that legislates each side's own law for them. When "we" is said in cooperation, it is checked first which branch it is: doing things together, or mutual recognition; which branch occurs is checked case by case, and is not assigned in advance by the kind of relation. The ordinary plural "we" holds as usual.

CO18　Judging one party's law for him in the name of "for the team," "for the project" or "for the company," that is, using the name of "we" or of a construct to take in and replace one party's direction, has the same form as judging one party's law for her in the name of "this household" or "for our sake," which Marriage Theory has read, and carries the direction of colonization. That the one who takes the lead puts forward a request concerning an arrangement is not, in itself, of this kind; whether it is, is checked first against the existing authority and commitments.

CO19　Guarding comes back to the individual: not handing over one's judgment under "we decided together," not possessing others under "I have contributed," not cancelling recognition of concrete people for the sake of a shared ideal.

CO20　Mutual recognition occurs in cooperation too; not borrowing mutual recognition as adjudication: "We're friends, so you have to agree" has the same form as "We love each other, so you have to agree," and borrows the branch of mutual recognition to pass final judgment on something done together.

CO21　When several people do one thing together, it is read by pairwise accounting; passing through a third person does not release the instigator from his part; a little circle of several people does not form a third bearer; where a little circle maintains an arrangement premised on "outsiders are merely means," it is read by Paper 1's reading of collusion. Formal organizations and their staffing structures belong to the institutional side. "Each has a share," in something done together, is carried to several people and read in the same way.

Paper 5, One Side

CO22　15DD and 14DD here are stances, not labels for persons: they are read by the current relational condition, not by persons. What is spoken of is the end that operates in the 15DD direction in this undertaking, and the leaning toward 14DD; neither party is said to be lower.

CO23　Cooperation in which one end is headed in the 15DD direction and one leans toward 14DD is within scope; such cooperation can be the setting in which cultivation happens, not the source of cultivation. If the person himself chooses to make a cultivative intervention, and the relevant boundaries hold, the first step is helping the other stand more firmly on the things he himself cannot set down; the intervention cannot compress the other's authority, channels or practical possibility of refusing, interrupting, revising, exiting and restating himself. Cultivation is not instruction, not remaking, and not the general opening move of such cooperation; the completion of cultivation is not in the enactor's hands.

CO24　The direction is read on that one effect, and whether it took hold is looked at separately, in the other party's later understanding and action; whether the other's capacity has increased is a measure of outcomes, and is not used to judge direction.

CO25　The end headed in the 15DD direction can see more clues, and can discern whether the other is making use of him; clues are still only clues, and long-run relational fact can shift the weight of judgment but cannot complete a certification. Whether to go on or to withdraw is his own judgment, on his own account. There is also a bias in the opposite direction: over-cultivation, reading the other's strategic performance as awakening.

CO26　Not being used, and not calculating in return: withdrawing one's own side is inward, is exit, and does not carry the direction of colonization, even if the other party's cost rises as a result; acting in a way directed at him that raises his cost is outward, and carries the direction of colonization.

CO27　If, in this end's own fallible judgment, the existing relation no longer meets the condition of scope "the other is not, in this undertaking, reduced to calculation," this end from then on no longer does this undertaking as the cooperation this series reads; what this series reads is his judgment and his own side from then on, and it does not on that basis certify that the other is reduced to calculation; exit at the object layer still goes by CO4. The other may still be doing the existing division of labor; the participation, facts and effects already left are entered as they stand, and are not erased wholesale by this judgment. The direction premise is not thereby lost automatically, nor does this judgment turn and judge the other's inner grounds. Nor is it that one qualifies to withdraw only after first certifying that the other is purely calculating: this end can stop cooperating on the strength of the current conditions. From then on, things are divided by the matters that actually remain: merely no longer doing this one thing together does not on that account become conflict as the Conflict Treatise reads it; where it already is conflict as the Conflict Treatise reads it and the reciprocal condition is currently unavailable, the unilateral path is read by its safety check and stop gates, and the unilateral path includes arrangements on one's own side, protection of boundaries, ceasing to apply, suspension and removal, and is not the same as going on cultivating the other; walking away is only an external act, and takes no technical identity by itself; the shared matters that leaving does not close out, and the institutional dispositions, are each registered by their source.

Paper 6, Each Bears a Share

CO28　The judgment layer in cooperation has two shares: each judges his own share and answers for his own share; neither party holds alone the adjudication of how this undertaking is done, and neither can declare agreement in the other's place.

CO29　Answering for, as this paper means it, is this: each one's judgments and what he has done cannot be acknowledged by someone else in his place, and the account falls on the one who judges; it faces inward: the subject answers to its own law. This stands alongside commitment's not turning into a duty to the other party, and is not owing the other party a duty. Existing institutional accountability is registered as background; how causation, fault and remedy are allocated is not given by this, and is not unfolded in this series.

CO30　What a person has done himself cannot be acknowledged by someone else in his place; a thing done was done by the one who did it: how it is told can change; the one who did it cannot. Doing things together does not let what one has done oneself turn into something "we" did, something no one did. This sentence says only who did it; it does not on its own judge who is at fault, or which consequences are borne by whom; what others each bear, and who is in charge of what within institutions, are checked separately per On Accountability, and are not closed out in this paper.

CO31　Others are not possessed under "I have contributed"; recording the other's share as one's own carries the direction of colonization; writing one's own giving as an account the other must repay is read as in Paper 2. What these read is one effect within the process; how credit and authorship are reckoned is a separate account, and this series does not settle it.

CO32　Breaking a commitment is entered on the books at the layer between person and person: breach of faith is entered apart; it enters the other party's later conditions of occurrence, and expectation changes accordingly; the stretch that spreads through third parties is entered by pairwise accounting. Being entered on the books is not the same as having caused some visible loss; the face in which reputation, or a score taken as standing for it, is used by a position as a condition of a chokepoint decision belongs to Political Economy Paper 1, and records and scores belong to its Paper 4.

Paper 7, The Process

CO33　Cooperation is a process; this series reads the process, not the settlement. Ending is read only within the process, and endings are not classified: how a thing continues, pauses or stops; the agreed thing being done, the conditions disappearing, the thing not getting done: these are all possibilities of the process; where unilateral withdrawal occurs among them, it is entered apart.

CO34　What is done together sediments between two people, in time: how division of labor is set and how it changes, how expectation grows out of dealings, how an agreed arrangement continues to be borne, and is asked about again and changed when needed; avowing anew each time is not required. Stability is read party by party, and carries no evaluation: the question is, for each end, whether his staying in this undertaking has grounds in his own law.

CO35　The standard of completion, the facts that have already happened, the judgments made by a procedure both parties have already recognized, and each one's looking back on this process are entered separately. The two may disagree about completion, and one party cannot declare an avowal on the other's behalf; existing facts or a valid procedure do not lapse automatically because evaluations still differ. Each one's attribution of meaning remains each one's own, and this series does not borrow completion to clear the books on other accounts for them.

CO36　One party's withdrawing his own side is his exit; shared matters that remain after parting are not settled by unilateral exit; leaving shared matters room for natural development when one exits points in the direction of cultivation; saying, on exiting, something like "Nothing is lost by walking away like this" says for the other party what this exit amounts to, and slides in the direction of colonization.

CO37　After it is done, no "we" is left behind to legislate for the two: when it is done, neither has become the other. This sentence is limited to their not forming one bearer; the two may each change through the cooperation, and the relation, effects and commitments thereafter do not vanish automatically because it is done.

CO38　When two one-way 15DDs meet at a moment of doing this undertaking together, that is the moment of two-way 15DD; this series takes up only that moment: it is something that happens on an occasion, not a grade that a stretch of cooperation is in, and it does not make later dealings mutual automatically; this series does not touch 16DD itself, nor does it take up mutual non dubito that can hold at any time and in any place, nor the change in the individual at that moment.

§10 Drawing Together

This paper lays down no propositions, and proves no proposition at the object layer. What it says is this: Cooperation Theory lies downstream on the relational side, following Love Theory and Marriage Theory and parallel to Marriage Theory, and reads, in two people's doing things together, the part that holds only in cooperation; doing things together in general belongs here; it does not define all cooperation, and reads cooperation whose premise is that at least one party is headed in the 15DD direction; the direction is a premise, not a component, and is not enough to enter scope; under this premise, two people, each for his own purpose, do together one thing that both avow and in which each has a share, whose purpose and adjudication neither party holds alone, and which does not automatically produce a "we" that legislates for both; the main line is You for yours, I for mine, and we do this one thing together; for direction one party is enough, for participation both are needed, in judgment each uses his own. The directions of colonization read in the papers borrow things that are, or may come to be, in a cooperation; the direction is read on the one effect in which they are taken up to do something, and each thing's antecedents are checked first; people are all mixed, and a single sliding is read on that one occasion. Of the moment of two-way 15DD, Love Theory takes up the face of mutual recognition, and this series the face of the thing done together; both take up only that moment.

摘要

这一篇是合作论的 Paper 0，最后写，最先放。它不立命题，只为读这个系列的人做几件事：说明这个系列在 SAE 里的位置，写明它读到哪里、主线是什么，交代各篇共用的写法与同字的词，与几家传统作总对接，把各篇读到的殖民方向合说一次，对照双向 15DD 的瞬间在爱情论与这个系列里的两面，合列全系列的三十八条命题，登记留下来不结的余项。

合作论是 SAE 关系论下游的第三支，接在爱情论、婚姻论之后，与婚姻论平行：两边的规范层不相违，规则层可以因关系的种类不同而不同。这个系列不给一切合作下定义；它读的合作，以至少一方在 15DD 方向上为前提，即至少一方在这件事上认对方也是目的；方向是前提，不是组分，也不是入射程的充分条件。在这个前提下，两个人各为各的目的，一起做一件两人都认下、各有一份的事；这件事不由任何一方独占其目的与裁断，也不自动在两人之上生出一个替两人立法的「我们」。主线的平实说法是：你为你的，我为我的，我们一起做这一件。方向一方就够，参与两方都要，判断两方各用自己的。七篇依次读：一起做；各为其事；分工；「我们」；一端；各担；过程。

这一篇与霍布斯、克鲁泡特金、墨子作总对接，罗尔斯与托马塞洛只作邻接，康德的永久和平回指国家间协作论。各篇读到的殖民方向，借的是合作里已经在或可能形成的件：认下，名义，分工，程序，付出、互换与贡献，交情，涵育之名与依赖，退出与退出时说出口的话，以及两人共同维持的安排对第三人的前提。这几件本身不带方向，方向在它们被拿来做什么的那一次作用上读；每一件各有它要先核的前件。这一篇不证明任何对象层的命题。

引言

后面七篇各读一件。第一篇《一起做》读这个系列所说的合作是什么样子：前提，结构，方向、参与与判断三样，对象层与操作层，射程外常读到的三种；最后在囚徒困境上看一次，规则推得出的那一步与判断落下的那一步怎样分开。第二篇《各为其事》读两人各自的目的：各是各的，不互相审；付出与得到的几种样子；「交换」的两种读法；目的相同也是两份。第三篇《分工》读分工怎样定下、怎样改、怎样滑成指向对方的固定要求，组织、合同与位置怎样作为条件登记，两人事先认下的程序交出去的是什么。第四篇《「我们」》读合作里一说「我们」是哪一支，以团队、项目、公司之名替一方判他的法，守卫落回个人，相认借作裁断，多人与小圈子。第五篇《一端》读一端在 15DD 方向、一端偏 14DD 的合作：姿态，涵育的场合，结果的尺，看得见的与认证不了的，不被利用也不反过来算计，以及在这一端的判断里对方只剩算计的时候。第六篇《各担》读判断层：各判各的，各负各的，做下的事不换人认，贡献不拿来占有，失信分开记、入账，一次结果有错，不独自记作答应过的没做到。第七篇《过程》读一起做的事在时间里沉积成什么：读过程，不读结算；完成与回看分记；收回；做完以后；以及那一刻。

第一节 位置：关系侧下游的第三支

SAE 有三侧：主体侧，关系侧，制度侧。关系侧由关系论展开；关系论写完以后，下游有几支：爱情论、婚姻论、亲子论、合作论；友谊不单列一支，作贯穿各支的例子。1 爱情论、婚姻论在前，这个系列是第三支，写在亲子论之前。

两层的分工，关系论写明了：抽象的关系论只立所有关系都具备的；只在某一种关系里成立的，交那一种关系的系列论证。2 关系一层已经立下的，这个系列直接用，不重写：关系的起码条件，涵育与殖民两个方向，期待，承诺，来往的沉积，结束，都照关系论读。关系论交给这个系列的，有三件是它先给了的：不生成第三个承担者；共同目标不等于共同的终极价值；共同做事在时间里沉积成什么（附录）。

与婚姻论：平行。 婚姻论在它的射程里交出这一界：一般的共同做事，归合作论，婚姻论只读婚姻里的共同做事。3 这个系列不是婚姻论的下游，两个系列平行。两边读法不同时，先看差别能不能追到关系的种类上：追得到的，落在规则层，可以不同；追不到的，落在规范层，两边不相违。真见了冲突，拿关系论判，不拿两个系列互判；关系论没有定的，登作关系论的余项。「这是规则层」这几个字本身，不是理由：要说出是哪一项关系种类的区别使读数不同。

已经读出来的两处不同，都追到同一项区别上：婚姻以两人之间的爱为必要的一件，合作不要求爱。一处是射程：婚姻论取两方在 15DD 方向上，这个系列取至少一方；婚姻论射程外、一端在 15DD 方向而一端偏 14DD 的那一种，在这里是正对象（第五篇）。另一处是交换：婚姻里一次互换的片刻读作回落，合作里双方认下的互换安排不使这件事出射程，也不读作回落（第二篇）。这一处追到爱上，中间有一步。婚姻论给回落写的根据，是那一刻没有在认对方也是目的的那个方向上运作；在婚姻里，那个方向就是两人之间的爱所在的方向，爱情论写明，相爱不是交换。合作里认对方也是目的，不以爱为必要，也不排斥两人在 C 上说定怎样换。这一读由这个系列自担（第二篇）。4 也有不随关系的种类变的：把自己的付出写成对方须偿还的账，婚姻里怎样读，合作里人与人之间那一层也怎样读（第二篇）；分工是两人一起认下的现在安排，约束力分别经过两人各自的法，滑成指向对方的固定要求时有殖民的倾向，两个系列在这一处同形，各自照这一形状读（第三篇）。

与行动论：一个人，与两个人一起。 关系论写明了与上游的分工：行动论读一次划线；关系论读互相之间的作用在来往与时间里沉积成什么。5 一个人怎样与另一个人一起做事，行动论第八篇已经立过：一个人为目的 A，另一个人为目的 B，两人不得不做 C；C 是两人都认下、也都做得成的一件事，不由任何一方独占其目的与裁断。6 两个系列在这一处重叠：行动论读个人侧，这个系列读两人一起；重叠处调用，附号，不重立。这个系列把这一安排结构用到以至少一端在 15DD 方向上为前提的对象上，这一步由这个系列自担（第一篇）。

与爱情论：相认，与一起做的事。 关系论把「我们」分两支：一起做的事，与相认；哪一支发生，逐件另核。7 爱情论里，那一刻的相认属相认那一支；这个系列主读一起做的事那一支。相认在合作里也会发生，可以是真的；只是不借去终审一件一起做的事（第四篇）。双向 15DD 的瞬间，两个系列从两面接，第七节对照。

与亲子论。 父母与成年子女一起做的那件事，一起做的那一面在这里；父母与孩子之间那一面，归亲子论。

与制度侧。 组织、合同、雇佣与位置的那一部分，归制度侧；这个系列只读制度没有规定或超出规定的那一面，人与人之间。规定本身认而不判，作为作用的条件登记。8 国家与国家、构与构之间，归国家间协作论；「协作」是那个系列的词，这个系列不用它作术语。9 公司与公司之间、国家与公司之间的制度关系，这个系列也不接（第八节）。

与政治经济学：五处。 政治经济学的射程是：「人在涌现层积累起来的资本、名声、地位与资质，怎样在别人的决定里成为作用于他人的权力」。10 两个系列在五处相接，各在各的一侧。

一，交换。两个人一起做事里的交换，是合作的一种样子，在这里读（第二篇）；涌现层的交换怎样积累、怎样兑换成作用于他人的权力，归政治经济学。它说闸以外的交换照常放开，也说没有撞上那两道闸，不认证一次交易在一切方面正当；引它时，两句一起带上。11

二，声誉与名声。照政治经济学前置篇分用：名声说的是被多少人知道、被怎样谈论；声誉是承认关系在共同体里自然显出来的状态。12 违背承诺在人与人之间入账，在这里读；声誉或被当作它的评分被一个位置拿去作关口决定的条件，以及记录与评分，归政治经济学（第六篇）。

三，竞业禁止。合作里约定、以违约金、禁令或强制执行为后盾、禁止一方离开以后往某个方向去的，照政治经济学读，附条件的给付另读，判据照它的完整前件带上。合作的另一方就是设约的一方：约定护的若是他自己那一份，不在「为保护他人而设」之列（第三篇）。13

四，合伙人。在一个构里，合伙人在位置上，对他说职责，归制度侧；两人之间制度没有规定或超出规定的那一面，归这里。14 两人登记成的构，是归责的地址，不是第三个承担者（第三篇、第四篇）。

五，判断层。政治经济学的判断层是个案，交给位置上的判断，账落在位置与人；15 这个系列的判断层落在各人自己（第三节）。政治经济学以「判操作，不判人」作全系列的体例，与这个系列说的姿态不是标签同向，各在各的一侧。

第二节 射程与主线

射程。 有六条。

一，不给一切合作下定义。有人把两方互相算计、各得其所而达到的平衡也叫合作，这个系列不反对这个叫法，只是不读它。说一种情形不在这个系列读的合作里，不是说它不是合作（第一篇 CO1、CO5）。

二，至少一方在 15DD 方向上。在 SAE 的层级里，认对方也是目的的那一层，记作 15DD。15DD 是不疑：「不只有我是目的，他者也是目的本身」。16 这个系列读的合作，以至少一方在 15DD 方向上为前提，说的是在这一件事上。方向是前提，不是合作的组分，也不是入射程的充分条件；入射程另核三件：两人都认下这件事、实际参与，各有自己的一份；对方在这件事上不是只剩算计；两人共同认下并实际维持的那项安排，不朝第三人带殖民方向，不越过他的基础权利（第一篇）。偏 14DD 与只剩算计不是一件：偏 14DD 而在射程里的那一端，在这件事上至少结构地认下对方有他自己的目的；只剩算计，是只把对方当作一组策略。17

三，姿态，不是标签。15DD、14DD 在这里说的是一次运作的姿态，按当前的关系条件读，不按人读；18 层按一次运作记，不记一个人，也不记一段合作；人都是混合的，回落照读，不推翻方向。对象层与操作层分开：对象层读射程里的合作；操作层每一方只确认得了自己这一端，不能因此替另一端宣布认下，另一端的参与与安排，按言行和可核条件描述，对其内在根据不作认证（第一篇 CO4）。若后来确认对象并未成立，这个系列退出这一件，不倒判对方那一端；按时段记，不倒抹已经发生的共同过程。

四，不读的，分三组。射程外常读到的三种，读法各不相同：没有一方在 15DD 方向上的，其理想型是两方都只把对方当作一组策略、互相算计而达到平衡的；被动受助的，那是一次帮助，照关系论读单向来往；两人共同认下并实际维持的那项安排，朝第三人带殖民方向或越过他的基础权利的那一段。末一种里，以「第三人只是手段」为前提的合谋，安排本身按是不是自己人定谁算目的。15DD 认的是他者也是目的本身，不凭他是谁、有什么，才认他；第三人也是一个人，在这项安排里却只被当作手段，那么两人之间的认，凭的就不是对方是一个人，否则第三人也该在内；凭的是别的某一项，是哪一项不定，定得下的只是，凭这一项人被排出了高低；在这件事上，两端都不在 15DD 方向上。这样的认带着它所凭的那一项；这说的是结构，不预测这项安排维持多久，也不说那一项变了认就一定撤走。这一读只读那项安排的前提，不读人，一次回落不在此列；它比关系论由同一道依赖条件读出的多走一步，这一步由这个系列自担（第一篇 CO5）。19 三种之外，一端在 15DD 方向上、另一端在这件事上只剩算计的，不满足射程的另一件，第五篇读：在这一端自己的可错判断里，射程的这一件已不满足，他此后就不再按这个系列读的合作来做这件事；这个系列读的是他这一判与他此后这一侧，不据此认证对方只剩算计（第五篇 CO27）。射程内的合作里不读的几面：合同、组织、雇佣的制度那一面，认而不判，只登记；结算，即功劳、署名、所得怎样分（第七篇）。这个系列不处理的边界：人与动物、人与 AI 之间；这是范围的选择，不作它们有没有承担位置的裁决。

五，上界。这个系列读的 C，照行动论第八篇，是共同做事，不要求两端都在 15DD 方向上。两个单向的 15DD 在一起做这件事的一刻碰上，是双向 15DD 的瞬间，只接这一瞬间；16DD 本身不碰；何时何地都能成立的双向不疑不接，只登记，不证明它没有；那一刻时个人身上的变化也不接（第七篇 CO38；第七节）。20

六，与相邻系列的界，见第一节。

主线。 平实的一句是：你为你的，我为我的，我们一起做这一件。

技术式的说法，各篇的正文与摘要照用：两个人各为各的目的，一起做一件两人都认下、各有一份的事；这件事不由任何一方独占其目的与裁断，也不自动在两人之上生出一个替两人立法的「我们」。前一半是第二篇的各为其事：两人的目的各是各的，不互相审，要的在一处也是两份。后一半是第一篇的结构与第四篇的「我们」：各有一份，不独占；普通的复数称呼照常成立，只是不立一个替两人立法的位置。

第一篇讲射程那一段的核心，是射程式的一句：至少有一个人，在一起做事的时候，不把对方只当作手段。这一句是入口的概括，不是射程全部条件的等价替换。它指向方向前提，比方向前提宽：两端都偏 14DD、都不只剩算计的，这一句读得进，射程不收（第一篇 CO5）。

把七篇串起来的，还有三样的分法。方向，一方就够，是方向前提；参与，两方都要，是合作本身；判断，两方各用自己的判断力，各负责自己那一份，是规范与规则都盖不住的余项落下的地方。判断力不等于 15DD 方向，所以方向一方就够与判断两方都要，不相违（第一篇 CO3）。一端在 15DD 方向、一端偏 14DD 的那一种（第五篇），各担（第六篇），过程（第七篇），都在这三样底下读。

第三节 写法

全系列照下面几条写。

三层。 这个系列读的东西分三层说。规范层，是关系论及更上游的系列已经立下的结构与方向的线；规则层，是只在某一种关系里成立的读法与安排，具体的规则只作例子；判断层，是规范与规则都盖不住的余项，落在各人自己的判断力上，账在作判断的人自己。国家间协作论与政治经济学也分三层说话，那里对构说应当，判断层交给位置上的人：「判断层是具体一项施为在规则下怎样判；这一层留给位置上的人的判断力，账落在位置与人」。21 那是制度一侧。这个系列不发应当；两个人之间没有一个位置上的人替他们判，判断层落在各人自己。说承担责任，说的是账落在作判断的人自己身上，朝内：主体对自己的法负责。22

层按一次运作记；姿态，不是标签。 不说哪一方较低；不由一次作用的方向倒推两端的姿态。两人共同认下并维持的安排以「第三人只是手段」为前提的，按安排的前提读，不算由作用倒推。

谈结构，不谈好坏；不发应当。 不以长短、得失、做没做成给合作分等级；继续还是收回，合作不合作，是当事人的判断。

不读心，不预测。 照结构推，或照上游原句；一个人怎样想，从外面只是线索。例子里条件不足的，保留未定，不用后加的内心叙述填上。

只讲人与人之间那一层。 合同、组织、雇佣、位置带来的权限，认而不判，作为作用的条件登记。认而不判划的是这个系列读的范围：登记一项权限，不等于替它签了正当，也不是说制度里的作用都已经没有问题，那一面怎样评价，归制度侧（第四篇）。合同给的义务属制度那一面；人与人之间那一层，承诺不落成对对方的义务，对方的回应是材料，不是结算。23 用职场的例子，先给相关规定的事实底稿，没有给出的记作未核。

只读过程，不读结算。 不给结束分类；功劳、署名、所得怎样分，不读；因果、过失与补救怎样分配，不在这个系列展开（第六篇、第七篇）。

涵育与殖民取宽义。 说的是一次作用的方向，不是贴给人或一段合作的标签：涵育朝腾出空间去，殖民朝灌入并占住去；两者不在一把尺的两端，同一个动作可以两向都有，也可以什么方向都不带；方向在这一次作用上读，成没成，看对方此后的理解与行动。侵另判，只在基础权利一层。24 说出口替对方定一件事对他意味着什么，是滑向殖民的方向；在运行上使对方改不了、把他说不的路弄得难走，是带殖民这个方向。「倾向」「滑向」，不读作已经成了。

交换不等于算计；分工是共识。 算计只指一种姿态：只把对方当作一组策略。为自己打算不是算计，把账算清楚也不是。「交换」分两种读，见第四节。

例子。 只用成年人物；自设的例子只用来看结构，每篇各用各的一组人；两个人的言行只是线索，不认证谁在哪一层。一句话可以在不同安排里取得不同作用，例子多写成分叉例。友谊作贯穿例：第一篇修篱笆的俞平与贺山，第二篇开修理角的柯远与梁冬，第三篇合开旧书店的魏安与景和，第四篇合开木工课的夏迟与邝明，都是朋友之间的合作；朋友之间彼此认出的那一面，照关系论读，这个系列只读他们一起做的那件事。25 历史例只照史料里的言行读结构与方向，只用已故、史料公开的人物，不判那两个人在哪一层；第二篇读管仲与鲍叔牙、恩格斯与马克思。

传统对接。 各篇按问题分接各家，这一篇作总对接。各篇守同一条：不预定哪一家是反面；先照实写那一家问的是什么、为了什么看、认出了什么；所引原文里确与这个系列相抵的一处，照原文的强度写，再写在哪里相遇、在哪里分开；没有真实相抵的，只写相照与问题的分工；原文里没有的反命题，不替它造。各篇所走的几步不全一样：第一篇写共同的问题、相照与分开；第二、三、四篇先写那一家为了什么看、认出了什么，确有相抵的写出相抵，再写相遇与分开，没有的只写相照或问题在哪一个尺度上；第五篇写共同的问题与不同处；第六篇分四步，共同的问题，是不是同一件事，相照与分开，同意到哪里；第七篇写认出了什么、在哪里相照、问题怎样分工。这一篇的总对接，写共同的问题、相照与分开。关系一层与相邻系列已经对过的，回指；回指时，那一处的限制一并带来。

出处与推及。 上游的结论在正文用平实话说；出处、原句与别篇的命题号放在注释；推到原处之外的，写明是推及，由这个系列自担。

第四节 同字的词

几个词在各篇里同字，意思要分开。

合作与协作。 这个系列说的「合作」，只指它读的那一种，不给一切合作下定义。国家间协作论读国家与国家、构与构之间的协作；「协作」是那个系列的词，这里不用作术语。判断力游戏系列的「合作之局」，是游戏的一种局：伙伴的下一步是他的，又是两人一起要的东西的一部分；26 可以借来看一起做事的一面，不等于这个系列读的合作。第一篇采用的囚徒困境设例，把不背叛对方的那一个行动标作「合作」；那个标签与这个系列读的合作不是一个词，这个系列也不替整个博弈论定义这个词（第一篇）。

规范层、规则层、判断层；规则。 这个系列的三层，见第三节；国家间协作论与政治经济学的三层对构说话，判断层交给位置上的人。规则层的「规则」，是只在某一种关系里成立的读法与安排；关系论、婚姻论说「规定本身，认而不判」，说的是制度的规则。两样不混。

算计、交换、付出。 算计是姿态，只把对方当作一组策略（第一篇）。「交换」分两种：两人认下的互换安排，你替我看这一周，我替你看下一周，不使这件事出射程，合作里也不读作回落；把自己的付出写成对方须偿还的账，是另一件，它不随关系的种类变（第二篇）。政治经济学说的涌现层的交换，是第三种用法，在制度那一侧（第一节）。付出与得到，说各人放进这件事里的时间、力气、钱物，与各人从对方那里得到的东西；说都没有获得什么，是照实说没有得到，不在后面补一份看不见的收益（第二篇）。

偏 14DD 与只剩算计。 偏 14DD 而在射程里的那一端，在这件事上至少结构地认下对方有他自己的目的；只剩算计，是只把对方当作一组策略。学习系列第四篇把承认他者有独立的 14DD，放在从 14DD 通往 15DD 的那座桥上；偏 14DD 而在射程里的那一端，在这件事上至少已在这一步上。经济学第二篇说的 14DD 的世界，对面的人是策略集合，在这个系列里落在只剩算计的那一面。这样对读两处，由这个系列自担（第五篇；第八节余项十三）。27

目的。 一起做事的形状里，A、B 是各人的目的，C 是一起做的那件事（第二篇）。「认对方也是目的」的目的，在 15DD：对方有他自己的不得不。

共同做事、共同目标、共同的终极价值。 共同做事是两人一起做一件事，不是共同的法。两人要的在一处，是共同目标；各自的价值归属不变，它不等于共同的终极价值（第二篇）。

法与安排；分工。 法，是一个人第一人称承担、持续组织他行动的那一层；安排，是时间、资源、角色、程序与行动条件怎样排。分工在安排这一层，是两人一起认下的现在安排，是共识，不是算计：分工是安排，算计是姿态，两件不在一处。只把对方当作一组策略的两个人，也可以各自认下一份很细的分工；这一种不在射程里，凭的是射程的前提，不凭他们没有认下（第三篇）。

程序与过程。 「程序」专指两人事先认下、把某一类事交给它定的那个办法，以及依法有权、或为保护基础权利而有正当权限的外部机制；日常说的「流程」，是分工的一部分，不在这一义里（第三篇）。「过程」说的是一起做的事在时间里的样子（第七篇）。两个词不混。

「我们」。 日常话里有三用。一是并说，把几个人各自做下的事放在一句里；二是关系论所分的一支，一起做的事；三是另一支，相认。后两支都不是一个站在几个人上面、替他们做决定的「我们」（第四篇）。

构；承担者与归责的地址。 「构」有两用。关系论说「关系是一种构」，取宽义：一端的作用进入另一端的发生条件而留下的那个构，它不立法，不承担。28 政治经济学说的构，是有制度身份的安排，一家公司、一个基金会、一级政府都是；它「是归责的地址，不是施为的主体」。29 承担者，照关系论，是关系一端的那一位：「关系的另一端须是承担者」。30 归责的地址，是一个构作为账先落的那一处。两个朋友把小店登记成公司，公司是归责的地址，不是第三个承担者（第三篇、第四篇）。

合伙。 日常说两个朋友合伙开店，说的是一起做这件事；登记以后的「合伙人」「股东」，是制度给的位置（第三篇）。

负责、职责、责任、义务。 这个系列说的负责朝内：判断与已做之事不可替认，账落在作判断的人自己身上，主体对自己的法负责；它与承诺不落成对对方的义务并存，不是欠对方一项义务。31 「职责」「责任」取论职责那一篇的义：职责是位置上立着的一项待应答的要求，束缚的是位置；责任是承受那一格本身，不以过错为条件；两格都在制度那一面（第六篇）。冲突论说「第一人称责任不能换人承担」，用的是日常义，说的正是做下的事不换人认。32

声誉与名声。 照政治经济学前置篇分用（第一节）。「声誉」一词，在道德律里立在纯 15DD 共同体的射程上。33 这个系列写失信入账，不借「声誉」：两人之间、两两之间那一层照记；传开以后被多少人知道、怎样谈论，是名声；声誉或被当作它的评分被一个位置拿去作关口决定的条件的那一面，归政治经济学（第六篇）。

判操作，不判人；姿态，不是标签。 前一句是政治经济学全系列的体例，后一句是这个系列读 15DD、14DD 的方式；两句同向，各在各的一侧。34

竞业禁止与附条件的给付。 照政治经济学第二篇的用法。前者以违约金、禁令或强制执行为后盾，禁止人离开以后往某个方向去；后者不禁止，只把一笔额外的给付系在一个选择上。一条具体约定落在哪一边，照它那三件判据合起来核，不凭一件单独定（第三篇）。35

完成与结算。 「完成」两用：两人事先认下的完成标准，与涵育的完成，后者不在施为者手里；第七篇说完成，指前一用。「结算」指功劳、署名、所得怎样分，谁欠谁多少，算出一个总数；这个系列不读它（第七篇）。

收回、退出与走开。 收回自己这一侧，不再出力，是退出；它向内，不带殖民这个方向，对方的成本因此上升也一样。36 「自己这一侧」，说的是把各笔分开以后仍归自己的那一份：已经认下的交付与其余仍在的事项，照原来各笔另核；一个动作是不是自己这一侧，不由本人把它叫作边界就定下（第五篇）。先独占或先致其依赖、再撤出的，在基础权利一层照关系论另核侵；仅有实际依赖，不独自取得侵这一结论。37 走开只是一项外部动作，本身不取得技术身份（第五篇、第七篇）。

涵育；识别。 涵育取宽义，是一次作用的方向，不是一项工程，也不是谁的身份。识别，说的是在 15DD 方向上的那一端在自己的判断里读出了什么，是可错的判断，不是认证（第五篇）。

合谋。 两人共同维持一项以「第三人只是手段」为前提的安排；读的是那项安排的前提，不读人（第二节射程四）。这个系列照国家间协作论与权力论的用字，用「合谋」（第一篇）。38

16DD 协同律令与 D47。 行动论 D47 的句式，一个人为目的 A，另一个人为目的 B，两人不得不做 C，行动论说在这个体系别处立过，指《理性不得不》一·三列出的协同律令（16DD）：我为目的 A，他者为目的 B，我们不得不做 C；那一处又写明，协同律令的完整定义另在一篇。行动论借它读共同做事。39 同篇三·四写，协同律令的 C 另带一个条件：C 作为两个目的相遇后的涌现，要求两个主体都把自己暴露给对方的追问。这个系列读的 C 照行动论，是共同做事，不带这一条，也不要求两端都在 15DD 方向上；共同做成一件事，不自动就是双向 15DD 的瞬间（第一篇、第七篇；第七节）。

第五节 总对接

各篇按问题接了各家。第一篇接囚徒困境的设例、阿克塞尔罗德、休谟论两人划船、卢梭论猎鹿与霍布斯；第二篇接斯密论饭食与自爱、亚里士多德论有用的友爱、克鲁泡特金论互助与《论语》「和而不同」；第三篇接柏拉图论一人一事、涂尔干论有机团结与斯密的别针工场；第四篇接卢梭的公意、塞尔的集体意向与《论语》「群而不党」；第五篇接《论语》「以直报怨」与康德的非社会的社会性；第六篇接韦伯的责任伦理、阿伦特论罪责与集体责任与《论语》「躬自厚」；第七篇接亚里士多德论有用的友爱怎样解开、阿伦特论行动的不可逆与不可预测与《周易》的既济、未济。吉尔伯特的共同承诺与复数主体，关系论已经对过，各篇回指。有几家，问的是全系列，放在这里。

竞争与互助（霍布斯、克鲁泡特金）。 两家对人与人之间一起做事，各有一种总读法。霍布斯在《利维坦》第十七章说，没有剑的约定只是空话，全无保护人的力量；他的回答在一个共同的权力。40 克鲁泡特金在《互助论》的导言里，接过动物学家凯斯勒的一个想法：自然里除了互相斗争的法则，还有互助的法则；他说自己写的，是互助作为演化的主要因素之一，不是演化的全部因素与它们各自的分量；互助的本能在自然里从哪里来，他留给以后的研究。41 共同的问题：人与人之间一起做事，靠什么维持，在人群里起什么作用。两家一个落在强力，一个落在互助；这个系列不预定哪一家是反面，也不在两家之间裁。

相照在两处。一，霍布斯问约定靠什么约束人，答在国家那一层的一个共同的权力；那一层是人与构之间，归制度侧，这个系列不判（第一篇）。在两个人之间这一层，这个系列读的约束不靠剑：承诺约束人，在我这一侧靠站，在对方那一侧靠已经留下的作用，违背入账（第一篇、第六篇）。两家读的不是同一层。二，克鲁泡特金看见了：互相付出、互相援手，与竞争一样实际发生；这个系列也读不以从对方取得回报为条件的付出（第二篇）。他读的是互助在物种与演化里起的作用；互助对一个群体、一个物种起的作用，不等于付出的那个人这一次取得的回报，也不等于他付出时为的是什么，两样可以并存，不必互相驳倒。这个系列不替无人获益的那一种补一份看不见的收益（第二篇）。

分开在问的东西上。两家各给一种总读法：霍布斯读约定怎样才有力量，克鲁泡特金读互助在演化里起什么作用；这个系列不读合作从哪里来，也不读它在演化里起什么作用，只读一起做的那件事是什么结构。它的射程是一个前提，不是一种人性论：至少一方在这件事上认对方也是目的，推不出人人如此，也推不出人人不如此。

兼相爱，交相利（墨子）。 关系论已经与墨子对过，读他论兼相爱、交相利与它的可行；那一处的限制是，几家都把单方行为之外的持续实践放进视野，不能只凭这几句视为同一种回礼的义务论。42 这里只回指一句，并带上那一处的限制：交相利，在这个系列读的几种样子里，近于互换的那一种，这个系列不由它推出回报的义务；兼相爱不是这个系列的前提，这个系列只要至少一方在这件事上认对方也是目的，不要求爱。

公平的合作体系（罗尔斯）。 罗尔斯把社会读作世代相续的一个公平的合作体系，问的是这样一个体系的基本结构依什么原则安排。43 那是制度一侧的问题，这个系列只作邻接：它读两个人之间一起做的事，不读社会的基本结构，也不论证分配的原则。

共享意向（托马塞洛）。 托马塞洛从发展与比较研究读人类合作里的共享意向，问合作的能力从哪里来；这个系列不读能力从哪里来，只作邻接（第一篇）。

永久和平（康德）。 康德在《永久和平论》里说，国家之间的联盟不必是一个国际国家；国家间协作论已经与他对过。44 国家之间的协作归那个系列，这里回指，不重写。

第六节 各篇读到的殖民方向，合说一次

各篇读出的殖民方向，读法在各篇，这里只合说一层：它们借的，是合作里已经在或可能形成的件。这几件本身不带方向；带不带，看那一次作用的前件，不看动作的名称：

  • 认下：替对方宣布他认下了什么时，滑向殖民的方向；照实转述，不在此列（第一篇）。
  • 名义（「我们」、团队、项目、公司，或那件事本身）：被拿来收下并替下一方的方向时，带殖民方向；提出要求、在已核的权限之内提出安排，不凭用了名字就是这一种，登记权限也不替它签正当（第二篇、第四篇）。
  • 分工：被拿来对着对方，当作他改不得的、挂到他是谁上、在习惯里偷换时，有殖民的倾向；不认一项新分工，单凭这一样不是封路，已认下的变更被借执行的一环压住，才另核（第三篇）。
  • 程序：名为第三方，运行中仍由被问一方控制审查、裁断与兑现时，是换了个名字继续；单方提名、曾经认下，都只是线索（第三篇）。
  • 付出、互换与贡献：把对方只当作得到东西的条件，把结算延伸到人身上，把付出写成须偿还的账，拿付出或贡献占有对方、换他那一份判断、替他完成他那一步、把他那一份记成自己的时（第二篇、第六篇）。
  • 交情：被借去终审一件一起做的事时（第四篇）。
  • 涵育之名与依赖：以涵育之名替对方定方向时；依赖被造出来、又锁住说不与改换的路时，只凭专用读不出，只凭当初点头也排除不了；未完成的涵育不是殖民（第二篇、第五篇）。
  • 退出：收回分开各笔以后仍归自己的那一侧，向内，不带；替对方说这次退出算什么、把他此后的路定死，滑向；收回以后冲着对方去抬高他的成本，带（第五篇、第七篇）。
  • 安排对第三人的前提：朝第三人带殖民方向或越过他基础权利的那一段，不在射程里，其中以第三人只是手段为前提的是合谋；只是时间上重合、竞争使人落选、一人瞒着伙伴的追加，不在此列（第一篇、第四篇）。

同一句「为了这个项目，周末大家都得来」，在一种安排里是一项要求，在另一种里是位置上的权限，在第三种里才是借名收下并替下；同一笔零件钱，可以只是一笔付出，也可以被说成一笔账；同一次收回，是退出，不是冲着对方去。方向在它们被拿来做什么的那一次作用上读，成没成，看对方此后怎样理解、怎样做。

一起做事里不朝殖民去，并不容易：一起做的件多，每一件都是一处可以被拿来做别的事的地方。所以关系都是混合的：混合是常态，纯 15DD 的共同体只是理想型。45 这个系列读的是方向，一次滑向照那一次读，不据此判一段合作出了射程。出射程，照第一篇读射程外常读到的三种；在一端自己的可错判断里，对方在这件事上已只剩算计的，这一端此后不再按这个系列读的合作来做这件事，照第五篇读，这个系列不据此认证对方只剩算计。这个系列不因此说合作危险，也不因此劝人合作或不合作；它只读。

第七节 双向 15DD 的瞬间：两面

在 SAE 的层级里，16DD 是双向不疑：两个主体「互相承认对方是目的本身，互相凿，互相不疑」。46 这个系列不碰 16DD 本身：它读到的只是两个单向的 15DD 在一刻碰上，记作双向 15DD 的瞬间；那一刻与 16DD 是什么关系，这个系列不判。关系论把这一瞬间读成一次发生的事，不是一个人或一段关系所处的等级；只接这一瞬间，16DD 本身不碰，何时何地都能成立的双向不疑不接，那一刻时个人身上的变化也不接。47 爱情论与这个系列都只接这一瞬间，接的是两面。

爱情论从相认那一面接。它读的那一刻，是两份单向的爱相遇，各自认对方也是目的，不等对方先认；相爱不是交换，「我爱你，所以你该爱我」，是把爱写成了交换。48 那一刻的相认，属相认那一支。

这个系列从一起做的事那一面接。它读的那一刻，是两个单向的 15DD 在一起做这件事的一刻碰上（第七篇 CO38）。第七篇的例子里，两位研究者为一个字的声调争了一夜，各自去问对方的根据，最后定下一种两人原先都没有想到的记法；这些是那一夜实际发生的情形，不是那一刻的判准，那一刻不以争过一夜、有了一种新的做法为条件。这个系列读的 C 照行动论，是共同做事；《理性不得不》三·四给协同律令的 C 另带的条件，「要求两个主体都把自己暴露给对方的追问」，这个系列不带（第四节）。49 《理性不得不》另有一句，这里只登记，不碰：「协同律令（我们不得不做C）是16DD运作的produce——C是两个自律主体在互相追问下涌现的第三项」。50

两面相同的有四处。一，都只接瞬间：它是一次发生的事，不是一段爱情或一段合作所处的等级。二，它不使此后的来往自动成为双向，也不使一方此后可以向另一方要求双向。51 三，从外面只见线索：一对恋人的对望，一夜的争论与一种新的记法，都不是判准。四，何时何地都能成立的双向不疑，两个系列都不接，只登记，不证明它没有。52

两面不同在所在：相认那一面，那一刻是两个人彼此认出；一起做的事那一面，那一刻落在两人一起做的这件事上，两个单向的 15DD 在做这件事的时候碰上；那一刻出没出一种新的做法，不是它的判准。另有两处写明。一，这个系列的射程只要至少一方在 15DD 方向上，那一刻却要两端都在；在这个系列里，上界与射程的前提不是同一个条件。共同做成一件事，不自动是那一刻；这个系列读的合作，绝大部分日子不是那一刻（第七篇）。二，两面可以同场：一对相爱的人一起做一件事，那一刻若既是彼此认出，又落在一起做的这件事上，哪一支发生，逐件另核，不按关系的种类预先分派。

第八节 余项

几件事，这个系列登记，不结。

一，一起做的事里「各有一份」推到多人，关系论说的两个人的小圈子推到几个人：这两步由这个系列自担，只在用到处写（第四篇）。

二，团队、项目能否另行成立为一个承担者。关系论只立两个人的小圈子不成第三个承担者；共同体什么时候成了集体的主体，关系论不碰，这个系列也不碰（第四篇）。

三，16DD 本身不碰；何时何地都能成立的双向不疑不接，那一刻时个人身上的变化也不接（第七节）。

四，人与动物、人与 AI 之间：范围的选择，不作本体论的裁决；AI 的准主体性，另有 SAE 的 AI 系列。

五，知情的关系是否更稳定。关系论把这一句登作交给下游论证的，各支若要用，各自论证；53 这个系列各篇没有用到，只登记，不论证。

六，走不掉的时候由制度接管的那一段。经济学第二篇读到这里；它属制度侧，这个系列只登记。54

七，因果、过失与补救在合作里怎样分配；行动论交出的别人各担什么、制度上谁管什么：照论职责那一篇另核，不在这个系列展开（第六篇）。

八，公司与公司之间、国家与公司之间的制度关系。国家间协作论写明不接，政治经济学登作辖域相邻的对象；这个系列也不接。55

九，两个人之间几件事缠在一起时怎样读。这个系列按那件事读，逐件、逐段记，不给一个人总的读数；同样两个人，在这一件里以 15DD 方向运作的那一端，在另一件里可能偏 14DD。几件事缠在一起的那一种，第五篇只读到一件。

十，一件事做没做完，两人始终读得不一样。这个系列停在分别记那里，不给它一个裁断的位置（第七篇）。

十一，吉尔伯特的共同承诺。第一篇回指关系论那一场：在资格这一层，一方收回自己这一侧，就收回了，只是不替对方那一侧消账。关系论与她对过以后写明：「这说明它保留了什么，不等于已经解释了吉尔伯特的全部规范特征」。56 这一处限制带到一起做事这一层，这个系列没有补。

十二，两端都偏 14DD、都不只剩算计，却没有一方在这件事上认对方也是目的的一起做事。它缺方向前提，不在射程里，这个系列不读；从外面，它与射程外第一种的理想型分不出，两种缺的都是方向前提，也不必分。其中一端此后进到这个方向上，按时段记，从那时起照射程另核（第一篇）。

十三，偏 14DD 与经济学第二篇说的 14DD 的世界怎样对读。这个系列把那个世界收在只剩算计的那一面，把偏 14DD 而在射程里的那一端，放在学习系列第四篇说的那座桥的这一步上；这一对读由这个系列自担，经济学第二篇那一句本身的射程，这个系列不替它定，只在这里登记（第四节；第五篇）。

第九节 全系列命题

各篇的命题，照各篇正文的命题原句合列，只在句末补句号；论证与限定在各篇正文。

第一篇 一起做

CO1　这个系列不给一切合作下定义。它读的合作，以至少一方在 15DD 方向上为前提；15DD 方向是前提，不是合作的组分，也不是入射程的充分条件。15DD、14DD 在这里是姿态，不是人的标签，按当前的关系条件读，不按人读；回落照读，不推翻方向。

CO2　在这个前提下，这个系列读的合作是这样：两个人各为各的目的，一起做一件两人都认下、各有一份的事；这件事不由任何一方独占其目的与裁断。各人那一份，可以是照对方的安排做起自己那一部分，可以是说出自己要什么、在怎么做上自己拿主意。仅有拒绝或叫停的资格，不因此已做了一份；实际作出的选择、指认、校正或暂停，是不是一份，看它怎样进入共同的做法：为修正共同的做法而暂停整个操作，可以仍是自己那一份；叫停若是收回自己继续参与的那一侧，才按退出读；两者不按停了多少区分。仅仅停止继续受助，不因此变成合作。

CO3　方向、参与、判断三样，管的不同：方向，一方就够，是方向前提；参与，两方都要，是合作本身；判断，两方各用自己的判断力，各负责自己那一份。判断力不等于 15DD 方向。

CO4　对象层与操作层分开：对象层读以至少一端在 15DD 方向上为方向前提、另核双方认下并实际参与、不是只剩算计、不朝第三人殖民的合作。操作层每一方只确认自己这一端，不能因此替另一端宣布认下；另一端的参与与安排，按言行和可核条件描述，对其内在根据不作认证。若后来确认对象并未成立，这个系列退出这一件，不倒判对方那一端。

CO5　射程外常读到的有三种，读法各不相同，都写作「不在这个系列读的合作里」：没有一方在 15DD 方向上的，其理想型是两方都只把对方当作一组策略、互相算计而达到平衡的；被动受助的，那是一次帮助，照关系论读单向来往；两人共同认下并实际维持的那项安排，朝第三人带殖民方向或越过他的基础权利的那一段。一人事后的追加、瞒着伙伴的用途，不因带着同一个项目名就倒推另一人也认下了；竞争使第三人落选，不等于朝他殖民。两人共同维持的那项安排若以「第三人只是手段」为前提，安排本身就按是不是自己人定谁算目的，两人之间的认凭的也不是对方是一个人，在这件事上两端都不在 15DD 方向上。起初被动受助的人，认下这件事、做起自己那一份，又满足其余条件，从那时起在射程里。一端在 15DD 方向上、另一端在这件事上只剩算计的，不满足射程的另一件，第五篇读（CO27）。

CO6　互相算计的平衡与这个系列读的合作，界的标记看三处结构线索：不独占，是认了对方那一份是对方的，还是只是谁也独占不了；对方那一份是否仍由他本人承担；现有安排是否容纳他的拒绝与修订。线索只说界在哪里，不认证内在的承认，也不证明那一种不是合作。三处线索分的是只剩算计与不只剩算计；至少一方在不在 15DD 方向上，只有那一端自己确认得了（CO4），线索不替它认证。

第二篇 各为其事

CO7　两人的目的各是各的，不互相审；某一个人的目的是什么，只有他那一端确认得了。一个人愿意支持对方的目的，可以成为他自己认下的一份，但不由「愿意」二字单独证明已到他自己的不得不；目的不由对方代认；不审目的，也不等于目的所要求的行动、依据与对旁人的作用从此不受询问。

CO8　合作可以基于交换，但不必须。可以有互换的安排；可以是一端主要给条件，不以对方回给为条件；可以是双方都付出，不以从对方取得回报为条件，都没有获得什么。三种是本篇选取的样子，可以交错，不作目的与来往的穷尽分类；实际无人获益的那一种，不补成必有隐性的收益。合作看一起做的那件事，不看各人从对方得到什么。

CO9　「交换」分两种读。双方认下的互换安排，不使这件事出射程；出射程的，是把对方只当作手段。婚姻论把互换的片刻读作回落，是婚姻以爱为必要的一件所致，属规则层：这一处不同追得到关系的种类上。把自己的付出写成对方须偿还的账，是另一件：它在规范层，不随关系的种类变，婚姻里照婚姻论与关系论读，合作里人与人之间那一层同样读，不因关系叫合作就免；合同里的结算另属制度那一面。婚姻里标作回落的互换片刻，与殖民方向不是一问。交换也不等于算计：算计是只把对方当作一组策略的姿态。

CO10　两人要的在一处，也是两份：各自的价值归属不变，行动的安排落到同一处；共同目标不等于共同的终极价值。

CO11　几种样子上常见的殖民方向：互换里，把对方只当作得到东西的条件，或把结算从合同那一面延伸到人身上；把自己的付出写成对方须偿还的账；一端给条件时，拿付出占有对方，替对方完成他那一步，或致其依赖到说不了不；都付出时，那件事本身被抬成立法者，以「为了这件事」「为了大家」替一方判他的法。

第三篇 分工

CO12　合作里的分工是两人一起认下的安排，是共识，不是算计；它的约束力分别经过两人各自的法，或者来自双方已经承认的程序。

CO13　分工是现在的安排，可以重新认领，可以改。改成一项新的共同分工，须双方分别认下，或按已认且适用的程序作出；这不使任何一方取得批准另一方自身修订与退出的地位。单方收回，不把新安排强加给另一方，也不抹去已留下的作用。

CO14　分工滑成指向对方的固定要求，被当作对方改不得的、被挂到对方是谁上、或在习惯里被偷换，有殖民的倾向：说出口给分工定性，滑向殖民的方向；在运行上使对方改不了，带殖民这个方向。一方自己把分工当作改不得的，是他的自我关系，从结构上看没有向外的方向。

CO15　组织、合同、雇佣、上司与下属：规定与位置带来的权限认而不判，作为作用的条件登记；合作论只读制度没有规定或超出规定的那一面，人与人之间。在一个构里，合伙人在位置上，归制度侧；以违约金、禁令或强制执行为后盾、禁止一方离开以后往某个方向去的约定，即竞业禁止，照政治经济学第二篇 E12a 读，附条件的给付照 E12a 另读；合作的另一方就是设约的一方，约定护的若是他自己那一份，不在「为保护他人而设」之列。合同给的义务属制度那一面；人与人之间那一层，承诺不落成对对方的义务，对方的回应是材料，不是结算。

CO16　两人事先都认过的程序，是把这一件交出去，不是一个替两人立法的位置；已认且适用的程序作出的判断，管的是这一件怎么排，对行动安排有效，不把每一次结果都改成须重新征得同意。程序是否已经双方承认、或有其他适格的根据，与它在运行中是否仍由被问一方控制，分开核。由强势一方单独选定的第三方，可能只是那一方权力的延伸：单方提名是须核的线索，不独自证明不适格；曾经认下、依法设置，也不独自证明这一次的争议已经移出原来的控制。被问的一方若仍能独自封住对自己的审查，或控制相关的裁断与兑现，便不能只凭「交给了第三方」认定已经移出，那是同一件事换了个名字继续。普通的不满意，不直接证明程序被捕获。

第四篇 「我们」

CO17　共同做事不自动产生一个替双方立自己的法的位置。合作里一说「我们」，先核是哪一支：一起做的事，还是相认；哪一支发生，逐件另核，不按关系的种类预先分派。普通的复数称呼「我们」照常成立。

CO18　以「为了团队」「为了项目」「为了公司」之名替一方判他的法，即拿「我们」或一个构的名义收下并替下一方的方向，与婚姻论读过的以「这个家」「为了我们」之名替一方判她的法同形，带殖民方向。带头的人提出一项安排要求，本身不是这一种；是不是，先核现有的权限与承诺。

CO19　守卫落回个人：不以「我们共同决定」交出判断，不以「我作出过贡献」占有他者，不以共同理想取消对具体人的承认。

CO20　相认在合作里也会发生；不把相认借作裁断：「我们是朋友，所以你得同意」，与「我们相爱，所以你得同意」同形，是把相认那一支借去终审一件一起做的事。

CO21　多人一起做一件事，照两两记账；经过第三人，不免除始作俑者那一段；几个人的小圈子不成第三个承担者；小圈子以「外人只是手段」为前提维持一项安排的，照第一篇对合谋的读法读。正式的组织与编制归制度侧。一起做的事里「各有一份」，推到多人照读。

第五篇 一端

CO22　15DD、14DD 在这里是姿态，不是人的标签：按当前的关系条件读，不按人读。说的是在这件事上以 15DD 方向运作的那一端、偏 14DD 的那一面，不说哪一方较低。

CO23　一端在 15DD 方向、一端偏 14DD 的合作在射程里；这样的合作可以是涵育发生的场合，不是涵育的来源。若本人选择作涵育性的介入，且相关边界守得住，第一步是帮对方更稳地站在他自己那些放不下的事上；干预不能压缩对方拒绝、中断、修订、退出与重新表述自己的权限、通道及现实可行性。涵育不是教化，不是改造，不是一端合作的普遍起手；涵育的完成不在施为者手里。

CO24　方向在那一次作用上读，成没成另看对方此后；对方的能力增加没有，是一把结果的尺，不拿它判方向。

CO25　在 15DD 方向上的那一端，看得见的线索更多，能分辨对方是否在利用自己；线索仍只是线索，长期的关系事实可以改变判断的权重，不能完成认证。继续还是收回，是他自己的判断，账在他自己。也有反向的偏误：过度涵育，把对方策略性的表演读成觉醒。

CO26　不被利用，也不反过来算计：收回自己这一侧是向内，是退出，不带殖民方向，对方的成本因此上升也一样；冲着对方去抬高他的成本，是向外，带殖民方向。

CO27　在这一端自己的可错判断里，若现有的关系已不满足「对方在这件事上不只剩算计」这一射程条件，这一端此后就不再按这个系列读的合作来做这件事；这个系列读的是他这一判与他此后这一侧，不据此认证对方只剩算计；对象层上的退出，仍照 CO4。对方仍可能做着原有的分工；已有的参与、事实与留下的作用照记，不由这一判断一并抹去。方向前提不因此自动失掉，也不倒判对方的内在根据。这也不是先认证对方纯属算计，才有资格收回：这一端可以据当前的条件不再合作。此后按实际仍在的事项分开：只是不再共同做这一件，不因此成了冲突论所读的冲突；已是冲突论所读的冲突、互向条件当前不可用的，照它的安全检查与停止门读单向路，单向路含自侧安排、边界保护、不再施加、暂停与移出，不等于继续涵育对方；走开只是一项外部动作，本身不取得技术身份；离开不结清的共同事项与制度上的处置，各按来源登记。

第六篇 各担

CO28　合作里的判断层有两份：各人判自己那一份，负自己那一份；那件事怎么做，不由任何一方独占裁断，也不能替另一方宣布同意。

CO29　这一篇说的负责，指各自的判断与已做之事不可替认，账落在作判断的人自己身上，朝内：主体对自己的法负责。这与承诺不落成对对方的义务并存，不是对对方欠一项义务。既有的制度职责照背景登记；因果、过失与补救怎样分配，不由此给出，不在这个系列展开。

CO30　自己做下的事，不能换一个人来认；做下的事，是做下它的人做的，说法换了，做下它的人换不了。一起做事，不让自己做下的事变成「我们」做下的、没有谁做下的。这一句只说谁做下，不单独判谁有过失、哪些后果由谁承担；别人各担什么、制度上谁管什么，照论职责那一篇另核，不在这一篇结。

CO31　不以「我作出过贡献」占有他者；把对方那一份记成自己的，带殖民的方向；把自己的付出写成对方须偿还的账，照第二篇读。这几条读的是过程里的一次作用；功劳、署名怎样算，另有它的账，这个系列不结。

CO32　违背承诺，在人与人之间这一层入账：失信分开记；它进入对方此后的发生条件，期待随之改；经第三方传开的那一段，两两记账。入账不等于已造成某个看得见的损失；声誉或被当作它的评分被一个位置拿去作关口决定的条件的那一面，归政治经济学第一篇，记录与评分归它的第四篇。

第七篇 过程

CO33　合作是一个过程；这个系列读过程，不读结算。结束只在过程里读，不给结束分类：一件事怎样继续、暂停或停止；约定做完、条件消失、没有做成，都是过程的可能；其中发生单方收回的，另记。

CO34　一起做的事在两个人之间、在时间里沉积：分工怎样定下又怎样变，期待怎样从来往里长出，说定的安排怎样被持续承担，必要时重问与改动；不要求每一次都重新认领。稳定逐方读，不带评价：问的是每一端留在这件事里，在他自己的法里有没有根据。

CO35　完成的标准、已经发生的事实、已认程序作出的判断，与各人对这一过程的回看，分别记。两人可能对完成有分歧，不能由一方替另一方宣布认下；已有的事实或有效的程序，不因尚有不同评价而自动失效。各人意义的归属仍各自，这个系列不借完成替他们结清别的账。

CO36　一方收回自己这一侧，是他的退出；分开以后仍在的共同的事，不以单方退出结清；退出时给共同的事留出自然发展的空间，朝涵育这个方向；退出时说出「这样走开什么也没损失」一类的话，替对方说了这次退出算什么，滑向殖民的方向。

CO37　做完以后，没有一个「我们」留下来替两人立法：做完了，没有谁变成了谁。这一句限于不合成一个承担者；两人可能经合作而各自改变，此后的关系、作用与承诺也不因做完自动消失。

CO38　两个单向的 15DD 在一起做这件事的一刻碰上，是双向 15DD 的瞬间；这个系列只接这一瞬间：它是一次发生的事，不是一段合作所处的等级，不使此后的来往自动成为双向；16DD 本身不碰，何时何地都能成立的双向不疑不接，那一刻时个人身上的变化也不接。

第十节 收束

这一篇不立命题，不证明任何对象层的命题。它说的是：合作论在关系侧的下游，接在爱情论、婚姻论之后，与婚姻论平行，读两个人一起做事里只在合作里成立的那一部分，一般的共同做事归这里；它不给一切合作下定义，读的是以至少一方在 15DD 方向上为前提的合作，方向是前提，不是组分，也不够入射程；在这个前提下，两个人各为各的目的，一起做一件两人都认下、各有一份的事，不由任何一方独占其目的与裁断，也不自动生出一个替两人立法的「我们」；主线是你为你的，我为我的，我们一起做这一件；方向一方就够，参与两方都要，判断两方各用自己的。各篇读到的殖民方向，借的是合作里已经在或可能形成的件；方向在被拿来做什么的那一次作用上读，每一件先核它的前件；人都是混合的，一次滑向照那一次读。双向 15DD 的瞬间，爱情论从相认那一面接，这个系列从一起做的事那一面接，都只接这一瞬间。
