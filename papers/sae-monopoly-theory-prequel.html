What a Monopoly Is Not

Han Qin (秦汉) · Independent Researcher · 2026
DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.24377536 · Full PDF on Zenodo · CC BY 4.0
Abstract

This series diagnoses and criticizes one kind of structure: large-scale markets in which control is concentrated in the hands of one firm or a few. Its focus falls on three areas: the digital, technology, and AI as it is now unfolding. Before setting to work, this paper clears away what a monopoly is not. It is not a charge: where one firm or a few control a large-scale market, in the Base Layer or the Emergence Layer alike, it falls within this series' diagnosis and criticism; the broad sense fixes a scope, not a charge. It is not "bigness," nor market share: shares, thresholds and rankings are signals; the criterion is structural influence, read by three tests: whether there is a way out, whether there is a way forward, and whether questioning has effect. It is not infrastructure: within the scope, the name follows the direction of the structure; where it serves the interest of those who control it rather than everyone's, it is monopoly; where it serves everyone's, it is infrastructure; the direction is examined by effect, without reading minds. It is not cancelled by being open to questioning: when too many people depend on it, those who question it cannot move it, and questioning has no actual efficacy; too big to fail is structural influence, not an exemption. It is not a verdict given once: the same power can be innovation early on and ossification later; the diagnosis goes by what it is doing now, and in both directions it is recognized as it is now. It is not despotism, but it has the same form as despotism: one or a few control the common life of many people; people cannot leave, and having left they have no way forward; questioning does not move it; the same form is taken, not identity. The ought is addressed to four kinds of constructs: those that make and enforce rules and review; those in a monopoly position; those that invest and form alliances; and those that adopt it as a gate. Last, the paper sets out how the whole series is written: three things diagnosed, namely formation, exemption from questioning and names; three areas, the digital, technology and AI; three remedies, checks, supervision and questioning, together with letting it be able to fail. The criticism lies at the normative layer and is directed at the direction of a structure, not at control as such. The introduction uses a constructed example: the same Crossways, and what it was doing at different points across thirty years.

Introduction

The Kingdom State is a state set up in the Political Economy series and carried on in the Theory of the Company and the Theory of the Market. Its towns, companies, bodies and clauses are all constructed examples; they serve only as facts, and this series does not claim that any of them is the best design. Apple City is the largest city in the state; Crossways is a company that began with four stalls in the city's East Market.

The earlier series have already read a good deal about Crossways. Nothing new is added here; what has already been recorded is simply laid out year by year.

In Year 2, the first generation of the Qiao family rented four stalls in the East Market and sold sundries on commission for small workshops from out of town. From Year 6 it did catalogue mail order under the name "Crossways"; in Year 11 it opened an online market; from Year 14 it built its own logistics.

From Year 15, Crossways ran the Small Shop Starter Program: new sellers paid no commission in their first year.

By Year 20, about sixty percent of online retail in the Kingdom State went through Crossways Market; there were about forty thousand sellers, more than ten thousand of them small workshops of five people or fewer.

In Year 22, a statistician took a sample: where comparable goods had similar prices and ratings, Crossways' own brand, Crossways Home, appeared on the front page three times as often as other sellers' goods. That year's seller terms contained a most-favored-nation clause; the "Express" section took only sellers who shipped through Crossways Logistics; the commission rose from twelve percent to fifteen. In the summer of the same year Crossways bought Willow Basket Market, and in the spring of Year 23 Willow Basket stopped running.

In Year 23, Crossways set up the Seller Appeals Committee; five of its seven members were appointed by management. In Year 25 it stopped the Small Shop Starter Program, and the two committee members elected by and from among the Gold Sellers were changed to appointment by management as well.

In Year 26, settlement at Crossways Pay was delayed for three days, and the state gave it an emergency loan.

In Year 29, Crossways bid to buy Ferry Crossing, a company that did cold-chain and small-batch delivery for workshops in the townships.

Put these years side by side and the questions appear. Was Crossways in Year 20 a monopoly? And in Year 29? If the readings for the two years differ, in which year did they change, and on what is that recognized? It is big, it holds sixty percent: what does that fact by itself show, and what does it not show? The roads of questioning were there all along: the state court, the Appeals Committee, the Competition Review Division; does their being there change the reading?

This paper does not answer whether Crossways is a monopoly. What it sets out is how to recognize one, by what, what is criticized once it is recognized, and to whom the ought is addressed.

The scope of this series is:

This series diagnoses large-scale markets in which control is concentrated in the hands of one firm or a few: how such a market became something that cannot be got around, how it closes the roads by which it is questioned, or, while the roads remain, leaves questioning without effect on it, and how it answers for itself in the name of development or of safety. The diagnosis goes by what it is doing now and looks at structural influence; it does not judge persons, and gives no company a verdict on its character. The remedies are checks, supervision and questioning: checks before the fact, supervision during, questioning after the fact, and letting it be able to fail.

Its main line is one sentence:

Early development can become later ossification; monopoly is diagnosed by what it is doing now, and the remedy is checks, supervision and questioning.

§1 Not a Charge: The Broad Sense Fixes a Scope

The Chinese word for monopoly, longduan, comes from the Mencius (Gongsun Chou, Part II): a man who "must seek out a longduan and climb it." A longduan was originally a raised mound; standing on it, one could see to the left and the right and net in all the profit of the market. Where the word came from and how it changed later are taken up in Paper 0. Here only the image it leaves is taken: a position so high that others must pass under its eye.

This series takes the broad sense: where one firm or a few control a large-scale market, in the Base Layer or the Emergence Layer alike, it falls within this series' diagnosis and criticism.

A few words are made clear one by one.

One firm or a few. Oligopoly is included. Whether the few coordinate, and whether they collude, are examined separately under the Theory of the Market's way of examining: coordination and collusion are examined by where they press, and closing others' roads through an association or a standard is examined as sealing off roads; where it is neither, price alone does not complete a normative judgment (Theory of the Market Paper 4, SC8). Whether there is a way forward when several firms coexist is examined separately in Paper 4.

Control. Being able to set the terms of this market: prices, rules, access, and who stays or goes; the people in the market cannot get around it. Political Economy has fixed how "cannot get around" is reckoned: "Whether it is a chokepoint depends on whether it can actually be got around, not on whether there is nominally another way" (Political Economy Paper 2, E7). Where one can nominally switch to another provider but the default position has in fact already chosen for people, it still cannot be got around.

Large-scale. This is what that paper calls breadth: how many people must pass through it. How shares and thresholds are measured lies at the rule layer; Political Economy says that measures, forms and thresholds all lie at the rule layer, and this series gives only examples (Political Economy Paper 3, E19).

Both layers count. Supplies necessary to life, and the markets through which people unfold their own paths, are both included. A water source or a granary in the Base Layer, a market or an entry point from which everyone takes information in the Emergence Layer: as long as one firm or a few control it, and its scale is such that many people must pass through it, it enters the diagnosis.

The broad sense fixes a scope, not a charge. Once inside the scope, a case belongs to this series' diagnosis and criticism; what structural influence is diagnosed, and where a line is crossed, must be examined item by item. Political Economy said, when identifying chokepoints: "To identify a chokepoint is only to identify where questions are to be asked, not to judge that it crosses the line" (Political Economy Paper 2, E7). This has the same form: entering the diagnosis is not judging that it crosses the line (the broad sense; own undertaking).

Crossways in Year 20 is already within this scope: about sixty percent of the Kingdom State's online retail went through it, and forty thousand sellers had to pass through it. Being within the scope is not having been judged. What it did that year, and which of its acts left people unable to leave, without a way forward, or without effect when they questioned it, must be examined item by item.

Nor does the broad sense look at names. Companies, platforms and operators of infrastructure can all be within it, state-run ones included. The Theory of the Market has fixed that "The line does not change with ownership," that positions are recognized by power-face, not by public or private, and that "State ownership is not automatically in the direction of the Kingdom of Ends, nor does it automatically cross the line" (Theory of the Market Paper 1, SC3). A company owned by the state that controls a large-scale market enters the diagnosis just the same; structures are judged, not names.

§2 Not "Bigness," Nor Market Share: Structural Influence

This series is not against bigness, not against innovation, and not against profit. Success is not a charge. A company does well, many people want to go to it, and so it becomes big; bigness by itself does not show whom it presses on.

Shares, thresholds and rankings are signals. Institution Theory says: "Forms such as appraisal metrics, rankings and grades are signals, not criteria" (Institution Theory Paper 2, Z13). The original speaks of forms of appraisal within an institution; here the form is the same: a market's shares and rankings indicate where to look, and do not judge in anyone's place.

The criterion is structural influence. It is read by three tests.

Whether there is a way out. Whether the structural channel for exiting, refusing, revoking a standing bond, or passing into a reviewable procedure is still in the person's own hands.

Whether there is a way forward. Whether, after leaving, there is somewhere to go; whether the direction of choosing has been sealed off from outside.

Whether questioning has effect. The channel by which it is questioned is there; once it is questioned, does the questioning have actual efficacy on its reasons, actions, rules or the holding of office? When Political Economy extended control within the line to economic positions, it used the three questions of the Theory of Government: "who can activate it; what actual efficacy it has on reasons, actions, rules, or the holding of office; how the party questioned cannot cancel it on its own." (Political Economy Paper 3, E16)

Two things must be kept apart here. "Cannot get around," as said under control, concerns a position: the people in the market must pass through it. "Whether there is a way out" concerns a person's standing bond with it: a set of terms, an account, a tie; can the person exit, refuse, revoke it, or pass into a reviewable procedure? A position can be impossible to get around while the bond can still be left: no one in a city can get around its water mains, yet a household is not, by being connected to the water, tied to other services as well; and if it disputes its water bill, there is an independent procedure where it can ask. The infrastructure side often looks just like this: the position cannot be got around, and the bond can be left.

The first two tests are the two things of the Political Economy specialist paper: a way out, a way forward, and both are needed (A Way Out, a Way Forward, E40). Monopoly in both layers is asked about both. Monopoly in the Base Layer adds one more layer: there lies the life-substrate, and the supply itself must be secured; the institution does not only check the construct that does the work of the Base Layer, but must also secure this supply: "The object of securing is the supply, not that construct" (Political Economy Paper 3, E14a); the closer to the life-substrate, the stronger the checks and supervision (Theory of the Market Paper 1, SC2). The drinking water of a city that Paper 7 reads is in this layer: it is a far-future scenario, an imagined one, in which the water is dispatched by a single operator; at the renewal of the contract, a council member who voted in favor said: "Without renewal, there is no water."

Why does the criterion fall on these three tests? The ground is that a human being is an end. The specialist paper says that the ground of a way out and a way forward is that a human being is an end: an institution has no standing to seal off a person's leaving; an institution that does not secure his Base Layer, or does not give him room in the Emergence Layer, has no reason to require him to stay (A Way Out, a Way Forward, E40). The Economics series has a ready sentence: "the ontological motivation of antitrust is: preventing one party from demoting others to means." (Economics P1 §5.1, as quoted in Political Economy Paper 3, E18) Where control leaves people unable to leave, without a way forward and without effect when they question, those it controls are in danger of being treated as means; this danger is not stated by a share, but by these three tests.

So the conclusion of a diagnosis falls on the three tests, not on share or scale as such. That Crossways held about sixty percent in Year 20 does not, as a number, show anything about a way out, a way forward or the effect of questioning. The several clauses from Year 22 on, the rate at which its own brand reached the front page, an "Express" section open only to sellers using Crossways Logistics, the raised commission, the purchase of Willow Basket: these are the places to be examined. What effect each had is examined in Paper 1 and Paper 4.

§3 Not Infrastructure: The Name Follows the Direction

Within the scope there is one more thing to tell apart: where one firm or a few likewise control a large-scale market, what it is to be called.

This series' answer is that the name follows the direction of the structure. Where the direction of the structure serves the interest of those who control it rather than everyone's, it is monopoly; where it serves everyone's, it is infrastructure.

"Whose interest it serves" is examined by effect, without reading minds. It does not ask what the controller has in mind, nor does it look at how it describes itself; it looks at which side the three tests, a way out, a way forward and the effect of questioning, lean toward when the interests of the controller and of everyone else diverge. Where interests do not diverge, many arrangements benefit both sides and no direction can be seen; the direction shows where interests diverge.

This is aligned with the Theory of the Market. Reading infrastructure that cannot be got around, the Theory of the Market said that it is a position with two faces: it is first a place that opens roads for people, and it can also be a chokepoint; where it is a chokepoint, the scope of the rules it makes is drawn by the institution, and those rules can be questioned (Theory of the Market Paper 3, SC6). This series calls the face that serves everyone infrastructure, and the face that serves the controller monopoly. The two names do not speak of two kinds of company, but of two directions of the same kind of position.

This division has two safeguards.

First, infrastructure is questioned just the same. Being called infrastructure does not obtain exemption from questioning; the scope of the rules made by a position that cannot be got around is still drawn by the institution, and the channel of questioning is still functionally independent of it (Theory of the Market Paper 3, SC6).

Second, "we are infrastructure" can itself be a name. When a company says it is infrastructure, that it serves everyone, the sentence is examined by its effect, not taken on its own word; a name does not buy exemption from questioning, which is the subject of Paper 3.

Direction changes. What served everyone early on can later turn to serving the controller; the reverse too is recognized as it is now (§5).

Back to Crossways. The four stalls of Year 2 sold sundries on commission for small out-of-town workshops; the Small Shop Starter Program of Year 15 waived a new seller's commission for the first year: this is the face that opened roads for small workshops. From Year 22, its own offerings were given preferential placement, "Express" was tied to its own logistics, and Willow Basket was bought: where the interests of Crossways and of the sellers diverged, which way the direction leaned is the face to be examined. Both faces are recorded as they are and do not offset each other: the face that opened roads cannot be the name for the later clauses, and the later clauses do not erase the roads first opened.

The Theory of the Market Prequel read how this position grew: from an operator in the market, Crossways grew into the ledger by which the buyers and sellers trading there settle. This paper asks only one new thing: once it has grown into such a position, which way does its direction go?

From this it is also clear what this series criticizes. The criticism is a criticism at the normative layer, directed at the direction of a structure: a direction that serves the controller rather than everyone. It does not criticize control as such, nor bigness as such; where a place of control serves everyone, it is infrastructure, and this series still questions it but does not call it monopoly. Criticism at the rule layer, such as where the threshold of an antitrust law should be set, this series offers only as suggestion; criticism at the judgment layer, such as whether this company crossed the line in that year, is made by the positions and persons present, and a paper cannot take their place.

§4 Not Cancelled by Being Open to Questioning

There is a view that as long as it can be questioned, it is not a monopoly. The courts are there, the regulators are there, the roads of appeal are there; anyone who objects can go and ask; and since it can be questioned, there is no talk of control.

This series does not take that view. Being open to questioning does not cancel monopoly. When too many people depend on it, those who question it cannot move it, and questioning has no actual efficacy.

Three places upstream fit this.

One is the "actual efficacy" of the three questions. When Political Economy asks about control within the line, it asks not only who can activate it, but also what actual efficacy it has on reasons, actions, rules or the holding of office (Political Economy Paper 3, E16). That the channel is there is a matter for the first question; whether it has efficacy is a matter for the second.

One is the exit of the strong. Reading a construct that tied its relocation to a piece of legislation, Political Economy said: "What can leave is the construct; what cannot are those who depend on it" (Political Economy Paper 3, E20). Under structural dependence, its line "we can leave" presses on those who question it.

One is public support. When many people depend on it, its failure obtains public support; that paper says that obtaining support does not issue legitimacy to its existing acts and its continued existence, and that "public support obtained by scale itself needs a ground of its own" (Political Economy Paper 4, E25).

So too big to fail is a structural influence that concentration itself brings, not an exemption from it. That it cannot be allowed to fail is precisely one of the readings of its control.

This has to stand together with another sentence. Political Economy says: "Checks fall on how it is used, not aimed at lowering capacity" (Political Economy Paper 3, E17). The capacity of a company that cannot be allowed to fail is often real: many people cannot afford its failure precisely because it does well. The way for the two to stand together is the remedy of "letting it be able to fail": reduce dependence, not capacity. Paper 7 is devoted to it.

An overreaching reading must also be guarded against. That questioning did not reverse a decision does not mean that questioning has no effect. Actual efficacy can fall on reasons, where the party questioned must give its reasons; on actions, pausing or correcting; on rules; or on the holding of office. Not every questioning must reverse a decision, still less must there be a breakup before questioning counts as having effect. Paper 2 examines this item by item.

Crossways' Seller Appeals Committee is a road of questioning: sellers who object to terms and ranking can go there. In Year 26 settlement was delayed, the state gave an emergency loan, and the state government's spokesperson said: "If it falls, what falls is forty percent of the small merchants." The road of questioning was there, and the money was given. Whether this road and this loan change the reading is taken up in Paper 2. Political Economy has already read the two matters, from the composition of the committee and from the conditions of the loan; this paper only sets out the place of one sentence: being open to questioning does not cancel it.

§5 Not a Verdict Given Once: Examined as It Is Now

Power has never been the problem; what power is doing is the problem. The same power can be innovation and development early on, and ossification and the restriction of development later.

So the diagnosis goes by what it is doing now. Once the effect of a position has changed, earlier conclusions cannot be carried over directly: which of the original grounds still hold must be examined again by the present relation.

The Theory of Disciplines has said something of the same form elsewhere. Reading the independence of a reviewing position, it said: "After the premises or duties change, the previous round's conclusion on independence cannot be carried over directly," and the existing evidence must be examined against the new relation to settle which of it still holds and which is no longer enough (Theory of Disciplines Paper 8, S41). The original speaks of conclusions on independence; here it is extended to conclusions of diagnosis (own undertaking).

There are two more places upstream. When Political Economy lists the uses that must be subject to checks, it includes uses that "entrench positions" (Political Economy Paper 3, E17). The Theory of Government says that a decision can be final, yet "after a case is closed, it can be questioned again under new evidence or new problems" (Theory of Government Paper 6, G36). A position recognized in its early years as opening roads is not thereby exempt from examination for life.

What is examined again is the present structure; conduct that has already occurred, and its accounts, are not rewritten. The acquisition of Year 22 was made that year, and its account lies in that year; examining it as it is now does not rewrite the old account, but asks what the position is doing today.

This holds in both directions.

In one direction, early road-opening does not serve as the name for later conduct. The road opened in Year 2 by selling on commission for small workshops was real; it does not answer for the clauses of Year 22. Using early truth as a later name is the subject of Paper 3.

In the other direction, what once controlled and no longer does is also recognized as it is now. Constraints and remedies laid on it early are likewise examined again as it is now.

This direction is looked at through a supposition. In Year 24, the Competition Review Division's report recommended "separating the marketplace from its own retail": Crossways Market and Crossways Home would belong to two companies and be run separately. Suppose that from Year 30 two other online markets together came to exceed Crossways Market, and that by Year 33 the share of the Kingdom State's online retail going through Crossways Market had fallen to about twenty percent, while the sellers in the state who sold only on Crossways had fallen from about thirty percent to under ten. Is the recommendation of Year 24 then still needed as it is now, and to what extent?

Examining it as it is now asks about the structure of Year 33: whether sellers have a way out, whether they have a way forward, whether questioning has effect. If the readings have changed, the recommendation of that year must be examined again as it is now; it can be narrowed, and it can also be withdrawn. This supposition does not show that Crossways no longer needs to be questioned: examining it as it is now is not exempting it from examination; nor does it show that the recommendation of Year 24 should not have been made in its own year: it was made on the structure of Year 24. When Paper 7 reads how early constraints are examined again as they are now, it returns here.

What was the same Crossways doing in Year 15 and in Year 29? In Year 15, it used its own accumulation to waive a year's commission for new small workshops; in Year 29, it bid to buy a logistics company that had just grown up. In both years it was the same power, with different effects. In which years the readings changed must be examined by the structure of each year.

§6 Not Despotism, but of the Same Form as Despotism

This series shares a common shape with another thing: despotism.

The upstream has already taken this step. The Theory of Government says that positions cannot be final: "no position may monopolize the power to shut the door" (Theory of Government Paper 6, G36); Political Economy extended this sentence to economic positions (Political Economy Paper 3, E18). A political position and an economic position are, on the point of "not monopolizing the power to shut the door," under one and the same constraint.

For the same form, this series takes three features. They are not a definition of despotism; what despotism is, is handed to the Theory of Despotism.

One or a few control the common life of many people.

People cannot leave; having left, they have no way forward.

Permitting petitions and memorials does not cancel despotism either: one asks, and it does not move.

The third feature and §4 are two faces of one sentence: being open to questioning does not cancel monopoly; being open to petition does not cancel despotism.

The same form is taken, not identity. It is not said that a monopolist is a despot, and no person and no company is given a verdict on its character. A company may control a large-scale market, yet today's companies usually have no army and no courts, and cannot lock people up; to call it a despot is to read a structural sameness of form as an identity of name. To say that the two "work to the same end by different means" is a judgment; this series writes it as a sameness of form in structure, not as a historical conclusion. History serves as example, not as proof.

The two have one more relation, which is a different matter from sameness of form: conversion into each other.

In one direction, monopoly converts an economic position into political protection and buys up the road by which it is questioned. Political Economy has fixed the two directions of political–economic separation: "Money does not buy that share of political power which reviews and questions it," and decisions made in a position are not cashed in, either, as wealth later obtained from the party the position questioned (Political Economy Paper 4, E23). Paper 2 uses this when reading questioning without effect.

In the other direction, despotism converts a political position into economic monopoly: granted monopolies, state monopolies of sale. The English Statute of Monopolies of 1624 restricted precisely the monopolies granted by the crown; the Han dynasty's state monopoly of salt and iron led, under Emperor Zhao, to a conference on salt and iron, and Huan Kuan recorded the debate between the two sides as the Discourses on Salt and Iron; what was disputed was exactly whether the state monopoly competed with the people for profit. Political Economy has fixed that public and private are alike, "recognized by power-face, not by public or private"; a granted position is subject to checks according to its source, an accumulated position according to its accumulation, and a position that has both sources is examined under each separately (Political Economy Paper 3, E15). These two pieces of history are, in this series, entry points to "conversion into each other," not examples of the same form; their original texts and the questions they leave are taken up in Paper 0.

The upstream has one more place that speaks of "despotism," which is only pointed to here. Political Economy Paper 6 quoted Kant: under a government that takes the happiness of its subjects as its end and exercises benevolence toward them as a father toward his children, how the subjects ought to be happy can only wait on the judgment of the head of state; Kant calls such a government "the greatest conceivable despotism" (Kant, AA 8:291, as quoted in Political Economy Paper 6). Judging in another's place how he ought to be: this shape connects with acting in the name of safety, and Paper 3 returns to it.

What the same form looks like in the far future is seen through one household's day. This is an imagined scenario, set in Year 55 of the Kingdom State, after machines have taken over Base Layer work such as farming and drinking water. A household in Apple City's East Market: the rice and vegetables in the morning come from the fields of an agricultural operating company, and are ordered in the market through the market's own buying assistant, which is driven by a model company's model; the water bill is paid in the assistant the family uses at home, which is also that model company's assistant; the Basic Payment of the two elderly parents arrives through the same assistant. In the evening, the daughter wants to switch the family's assistant to another firm's. She works it out: the parents' payment would have to be verified again at a service window, which takes about six weeks, and the nearest service window is two hours away by road; the family's three years of records cannot be taken along; with another firm's assistant, paying the water bill would be put further back in the queue; and in the market, the only assistant that can place orders for her would, even after switching, still be the market's own. She does not switch. She writes a complaint in the assistant: "I want to switch, and I can't." The assistant replies: "Your request has been recorded; the relevant department will handle it." By the end of the year there is no response.

Each item, taken on its own, has its own effects and costs, and some are very good: the payment has never been late, leakage in the water mains has fallen, and the villages in the north of the city have been connected to water. This paper asks only one thing: taken together, is there a way out? This household is imagined, not predicted; it does not show that any firm is already a despot, nor that every item crosses the line, still less that every road has been sealed off. What it shows is what the same form could grow into: several dependences stacked in one place, the costs of leaving adding up, a complaint with no response. Whether there is a way out and whether questioning has effect must be examined place by place; this household only puts the places to be asked about side by side. Paper 6 brings this household back to AI's monopoly over the Base Layer; Paper 7 asks to which year's checks one must go back to fill in the places where questioning has no effect.

Despotism itself, and why states fail, are handed to the Theory of Despotism. This series only sets out the same form, and leaves an interface.

§7 To Whom the Ought Is Addressed

This series carries on Institution Theory's three tiers: "The construct takes the ought, the position takes accountability, the person takes his own law" (Institution Theory Paper 1, Z4). The ought is addressed to constructs; a person in a position has the accountability of the position he is in; a person himself follows his own law.

In this series, the constructs that can receive an ought are of four kinds, divided by function.

1. Constructs that make and enforce rules and review. Competition and regulatory bodies, on the side of enforcement; trade associations and the constructs that write standards and certify; positions of independent testing and audit; positions that give public support. The Theory of the Market has already written that regulators too are questioned; this series writes what they guard against monopoly, and how they themselves remain subject to questioning that has effect. Findings made by courts are cited by this series by tier; no ought is written for the courts.

2. Constructs in a monopoly position. Companies, platforms and operators of infrastructure, state-run ones included. Structures are judged, not names; a position that reviews a publicly run supply is, under the way of examining the Theory of the Market has set out, functionally independent of the supplier and of its owner.

3. Constructs that invest and form alliances. Investors, cloud and computing partners, buyers in mergers. They are examined by actual control: Political Economy says that nominal holding and mobilization come apart, and how much is held is not how much can be mobilized (Political Economy Paper 1, E2).

4. Constructs that adopt it as a gate. Governments or bodies that make procurement or adoption decisions and write one firm's product in as required; delegation and outsourcing likewise. The Theory of Disciplines says that when a standard is adopted as a gate, "the construct that adopts it answers for its adoption" (Theory of Disciplines Paper 6, S34); the Theory of Government says that when judgment is handed to a machine, the bearing is not handed over with it (Theory of Government Paper 1, G8). Where a government writes one firm's assistant in as the sole entry point for public business, it must answer for this adoption itself.

The same construct can belong to several kinds at once, and is addressed according to its function in the matter at hand. A company can be in a monopoly position in one market and be the investing party in another; a government can be the construct that makes rules, and also the construct that adopts one firm's product as a gate.

A person follows his own law; this series writes no ought for him.

§8 How This Series Is Written

Three things diagnosed. Formation: how a position became something that cannot be got around, in Paper 1. Exemption from questioning: the road by which it is questioned is closed; or the road remains, and questioning has no effect on it, in Paper 2. Names: answering for itself in the name of development or of safety, in Paper 3.

Three areas. The digital, technology and AI, in Papers 4 to 6. The titles are written "The Digital and Monopoly," "Technology and Monopoly" and "AI and Monopoly": the "and" does not presuppose that the area is already monopolized. AI is written as concentration in formation and its structural risks. The far future is written only for the Base Layer: the forms monopoly could take after machines have taken over work such as farming and drinking water; it is written as imagination, not prediction.

Three remedies. Checks before the fact, supervision during, questioning after the fact; the three divide their work by their main effect and are not three mutually exclusive stages. The remedies must also let it be able to fail: reduce dependence, not capacity. That the rules have not caught up does not mean it is not questioned. Paper 7.

Three layers of criticism. The normative layer criticizes the direction of a structure, and this series does that. Design at the rule layer this series offers only as suggestion and example; there are always remainders and exceptions, and a remainder yields no permission. The judgment layer is made by the positions and persons present.

Three kinds of example; naming in two ways. The three kinds divide examples by where the material comes from: concluded historical cases; present-day platforms; AI as it is now unfolding. Naming is of two kinds. To judge a company's conduct as crossing the line, unlawful or abusive requires a finding already made by a court judgment or a regulatory decision; it is written by the body and the date concerned, as "the court found" or "the Commission found," with the level and status of the proceedings marked; where there are only allegations, investigations or suits and no finding, it is not judged on that basis. To read the structure of a transaction or arrangement that has already ended and whose facts are public, where the reason a regulator stated is only the weakening of competition, with no other reasonable cause, counts as structural antitrust: it may be named and its possibility read (the possibility of monopoly, not monopoly already formed), without judging conduct and without giving the company a verdict on its character; where national security or another reason was stated, that reason is not read as monopoly. Matters still in process are not used; for such matters of the AI kind, constructed examples are used instead. Every company is held to the same set of tests; where like cases have been decided, they are used together if used at all. Published research can be cited, written as "such-and-such a study notes," and not treated as a final verdict. Conduct judged is written by the finding; a structure read after it has ended is written by the public facts and the reasons relied on, and only its possibility is read; neither stands in for a judgment of conduct, and neither is this series' verdict on the character of a company.

Constructed examples. The Kingdom State is carried on, written forward to Year 36; the far future is written to Year 55. Constructed examples record only facts, close with questions, and record both faces as they are: at least one place where roads are opened for people, and one where others' roads are held. They may imagine forms boldly, but draw no conclusions; they do not allude to real cases still in process, and make no predictions about real industries.

What is not done. Breakup across the board is not advocated: breakup is the last resort, and "Breakup is used only when lighter means are not enough, and when used it states its effect, cost and alternatives" (Theory of the Market Paper 4, SC9). No threshold is set for antitrust law, and no company is judged in the place of courts or regulators. It relies neither on "inevitable collapse" nor on "inevitable progress": Political Economy says "This series takes up only the inevitability of emergence, not the inevitability of dominance" (Political Economy Paper 6, E32); this sentence reaches as far as not relying on "inevitable progress"; extending it to not relying on "inevitable collapse," and not saying that concentration will disperse of itself, is this series' own undertaking. No ought is written for persons. Political events are not touched, and no person is made the subject.

With the tradition. How this series interfaces with existing accounts is gathered in Paper 0. The origin of the Chinese word longduan is also taken up there.

This paper sets out no propositions; it only clears away what a monopoly is not, and gives the broad sense, the tests of structural influence, the name following the direction, and the same form. 摘要

这个系列诊断与批判一类结构：控制集中在一家或几家手里的大规模市场。重点在三处：数字、技术与正在进行的 AI。动笔之前，这一篇先清出垄断不是什么。它不是罪名：一家或几家控制了一个大规模的市场，基础层、涌现层都算，就在本系列的诊断与批判之内；宽义定的是范围，不是罪名。它不是"大"，也不是份额：份额、门槛与排名是信号，判准是结构性影响，判据三样：离不离得开，有没有的选，问不问得动。它不是基础设施：在范围之内，名字跟着结构的方向，为了控制者的利益、而非每个人的，是垄断；为了每个人的，是基础设施；方向照作用核，不读心。它不是能被问就算了：依赖它的人太多，问它的人动不了它，问就没有实际效力；大到不能倒是结构性影响，不是豁免。它不是一次定案：同一份权力，早期可以是创新，后期可以是僵化；诊断照它现在在做什么，两头都照现在认。它不是专制，但与专制同形：一个或几个控制了许多人的共同生活，人离不开，离开了也没的选，问了也动不了它；取同形，不取等同。应当说给四类构：立规、执规与审的构；处在垄断位置的构；出资与结伙的构；把它采作门的构。最后交代全系列怎样写：诊断三件，形成、免问、名义；三个领域，数字、技术、AI；药方三件，制衡、监督、追问，并让它倒得起。批判在规范层，批的是结构性的方向，不批控制本身。引言用一个设例：同一个通衢，三十年里各在做什么。

第一节　不是罪名：宽义定范围

"垄断"这个词，出自《孟子·公孙丑下》：有一个人"必求龙断而登之"。龙断，本是一处高起的土冈；站上去，左右都望得见，市上的利便都网罗了去。词的来处与后来的演变，在 Paper 0 展开。这里只取它留下的一个形象：一个位置，高到别人都要从它眼皮底下过。

这个系列取宽义：一家或几家控制了一个大规模的市场，基础层、涌现层都算，就在本系列的诊断与批判之内。

几个词，逐一说清。

一家或几家。 寡头在内。几家之间有没有协调、是不是合谋，照市场论的核法分开核：协调与合谋照它压到哪里核，借协会、标准关了别人的路的，照封路一类核；都不是的，单凭价格不完成规范判断（市场论第四篇 SC8）。几家并存时有没有的选，第四篇另核。

控制。 能替这个市场定条件：价格、规则、准入与去留；市场里的人绕不开它。怎样算绕不开，政治经济学定过：「是不是关口，看实际绕不绕得过去，不看名义上有没有别的路」（政治经济学第二篇 E7）。名义上可以换一家，实际上默认的位置已经替人选了，这仍是绕不开。

大规模。 即那一篇说的广度：多少人要经过它。份额与门槛怎样量，在规则层；政治经济学说量法、形态与门槛都在规则层，本系列只举例（政治经济学第三篇 E19）。

两层都算。 生活所必需的供给，与人展开自己的路所经的市场，都在内。基础层的一处水源、一处粮仓，涌现层的一个市集、一个人人取信息的入口，只要一家或几家控制了、规模大到许多人要经过，都进诊断。

宽义定的是范围，不是罪名。进了范围，就归本系列诊断与批判；诊断出什么结构性影响、哪一处越了线，要逐项另核。政治经济学认关口时说过：「认出一处关口，只是认出要问的地方，不是判它越线」（政治经济学第二篇 E7）。这里同形：进入诊断，不等于判它越线（宽义，自担）。

第 20 年的通衢，已经在这个范围之内：王国州的线上零售约六成经它，四万家卖家要从它那里过。在范围之内，不等于已判。它在这一年做了什么，哪一样使人离不开、没的选、问不动，是要逐项核的。

宽义也不看名称。公司、平台、基础设施的运营者，都可以在里面，国营的也在内。市场论定过：「线不随所有制变」，按权力面认，不按公私认；「国有不自动在目的王国方向上，也不自动越线」（市场论第一篇 SC3）。一家州属的公司若控制了一个大规模的市场，同样进诊断；判结构，不判名称。

第二节　不是"大"，也不是份额：结构性影响

这个系列不反对大，不反对创新，也不反对盈利。成功不是罪名。一家公司做得好，许多人愿意去，它就大了；大本身不说明它压到了谁。

份额、门槛、排名，是信号。制度论说：「考核指标、排名、等级这些形态是信号，不是判准」（制度论第二篇 Z13）。原说的是制度里的考核形态；这里同形：一个市场的份额与排名，提示要往哪里看，不替人下判断。

判准是结构性影响。判据三样。

离不离得开。 退出、拒绝、撤回一项持续的结合，或转入可审查的程序，这条结构通道还在不在人自己手里。

有没有的选。 离开以后有没有去处；选的方向，有没有被外部封死。

问不问得动。 问它的通道在；问了，对它的理由、行动、规则或任职位置有没有实际效力。政治经济学把线内的控制推到经济位置时，用了政府论的三问：「谁能启动；对理由、行动、规则或任职位置有什么实际效力；被问的一方怎样不能独自取消它。」（政治经济学第三篇 E16）

这里要分清两件事。控制里说的"绕不开"，说的是位置：市场里的人要经过它。"离不离得开"说的是人与它的一项持续的结合：一份条款、一个账户、一种绑定，人能不能退出、拒绝、撤回，或转入可审查的程序。一个位置可以绕不开，结合却离得开：城里的管网谁也绕不过去，可一户人家不必因为接了水，就被绑上别的服务；对水费有异议，有一条独立的程序可以去问。基础设施的一面，常常就是这个样子：位置绕不开，结合离得开。

前两样，就是政治经济学专论的两件：离得开，有的选，两件都要（政治经济学专论 E40）。两层的垄断都问这两件。基础层的垄断多一层：那里是生命基底，供给本身要有保障，制度不只制衡做基础层之事的构，还要保障这一供给，「保障的对象是供给，不是那个构」（政治经济学第三篇 E14a）；越接近生命基底，制衡与监督越强（市场论第一篇 SC2）。第七篇读到的一座城的饮用水，就是这一层：它是设想中的远景，水由一家运维方调度；续约时，一位赞成的议员说："不续，就没有水。"

为什么判准落在这三样上？根据在人是目的。专论说，离得开、有的选，根据在人是目的：一个制度对人没有封死他离开的资格；不保障他的基础层、或不给他涌现层空间的制度，没有理由要他留下（政治经济学专论 E40）。经济学系列早有一句现成的话：「反垄断的本体论动机是：防止一方把其他主体降格为means。」（经济学第一篇 §5.1，经政治经济学第三篇 E18 所引）一处控制使人离不开、没的选、问不动，被它控制的人，就有被当作手段的危险；这一危险，不由份额说出来，由这三样说出来。

所以，诊断的结论落在三样上，不落在份额或规模本身。第 20 年通衢占约六成，这个数字本身，不说明离得开、有的选、问得动这三样里的任何一样。第 22 年起的几项条款，自营品牌上首页的比例，"快送"栏只收用通衢物流的卖家，佣金的上调，收购柳筐，这些才是要核的地方。它们各自起了什么作用，第一篇与第四篇核。

第三节　不是基础设施：名字跟着方向

在范围之内，还有一件要分清：同样是一家或几家控制了一个大规模的市场，叫它什么。

这个系列的回答是：名字跟着结构的方向。结构的方向为了控制者的利益、而非每个人的，是垄断；为了每个人的，是基础设施。

"为了谁的利益"，照作用核，不读心。不问控制者心里想什么，也不看它怎样说自己；看离不离得开、有没有的选、问不问得动这三样，在控制者与其余人的利益相左的时候，偏向哪一边。利益不相左的时候，许多安排两边都得益，看不出方向；方向在利益相左处显出来。

这与市场论对齐。市场论读绕不过去的基础设施时说过，它是一个位置，有两面：先是给人开路的地方，也可以是关口；是关口时，所立的规由制度划范围，可以被问（市场论第三篇 SC6）。本系列把为了每个人的那一面叫基础设施，为了控制者的那一面叫垄断。两个名字说的不是两类公司，是同一类位置的两个方向。

这一分有两条防线。

第一，是基础设施，也照样受问。名字叫基础设施，不取得免问；绕不过去的位置所立的规，范围仍由制度划，问的通道仍在功能上独立于它（市场论第三篇 SC6）。

第二，"我们是基础设施"本身可以是一种名义。一家公司说自己是基础设施、为了每个人，这一句要照它的作用核，不照它的说法认；名义换不来免问，第三篇专写。

方向会变。早期为了每个人的，后期可以转为了控制者；反过来，也照现在认（第五节）。

回到通衢。第 2 年的四个摊位，替外地小作坊代卖杂货；第 15 年的小店扶持，新卖家头一年免佣金：这是给小作坊开路的一面。第 22 年起，自营排前，"快送"绑定自家物流，收购柳筐：在通衢与卖家的利益相左处，方向偏到了哪一边，是要核的一面。两面都照记，不互相抵销：开路的一面不能作后来那些条款的名，后来的条款也不抹去当初开的路。

市场论前置篇读过这个位置的长成：通衢从市场里的一个经营者，长成了在这里交易的买卖双方据以结算的那本账。这一篇只问一件新的：长成了那样的位置以后，它的方向往哪边走。

由此也就清楚，本系列批的是什么。批判是规范层的批判，批结构性的方向：为了控制者、而非每个人的方向。不批控制本身，也不批大本身；一处控制若方向为了每个人，它就是基础设施，本系列照样问它，但不叫它垄断。规则层的批判，比如一条反垄断法的门槛该定在哪里，本系列只作建议；判断层的批判，比如这一家公司这一年越没越线，由在场的位置与人作出，论文不能代替。

第四节　不是能被问就算了

有一种说法：只要能问，就不算垄断。法院在，监管在，申诉的路在，谁不服都可以去问；既然问得了，就谈不上控制。

这个系列不取这一说。能被追问，不取消垄断。依赖它的人太多，问它的人动不了它，问就没有实际效力。

上游有三处对得上。

一处是三问里的"实际效力"。政治经济学问线内的控制时，问的不只是谁能启动，还问对理由、行动、规则或任职位置有什么实际效力（政治经济学第三篇 E16）。通道在，是第一问的事；有没有效力，是第二问的事。

一处是强者的退出。政治经济学读一个构把迁出系在一项立法上时说：「走得了的是它，走不了的是依赖它的人」（政治经济学第三篇 E20）。在结构依赖下，它的一句"我可以走"，就压在问它的人身上。

一处是公共支持。依赖它的人多了，它的失败会取得公共支持；那一篇说，取得支持，不为它既有的施为与存续发放正当性，「规模本身换来的公共支持，要另立根据」（政治经济学第四篇 E25）。

所以，大到不能倒是集中本身带来的结构性影响，不是对它的豁免。它倒不起，正是它控制的读数之一。

这与另一句话要并存。政治经济学说：「制衡落在怎样使用上，不以压低能力为目的」（政治经济学第三篇 E17）。一家倒不起的公司，它的能力常常是真的：许多人倒不起它，正因为它做得好。两者并存的办法，是药方取"让它倒得起"：减依赖，不减能力。第七篇专写。

也要防一个过头的读法。问了没有改判，不等于问不动。实际效力可以落在理由上，被问的一方要说出理由；可以落在行动上，暂停、修正；可以落在规则上；也可以落在任职位置上。不是每一次追问都必须改判，更不是必须拆分才算问得动。第二篇逐项核。

通衢的申诉委员会是问的路：卖家对条款与排序不服，可以去那里。第 26 年结算延迟，州给了紧急贷款，州政府的发言人说："它倒了，倒的是四成的小商户。"问的路在，钱也给了。这一路与这一笔，改不改读数，第二篇展开。政治经济学已经从委员会的构成与贷款的条件读过两件事，这一篇只立一句的位置：能被问，不取消。

第五节　不是一次定案：照现在核

权力从来不是问题，权力在干什么才是问题。同一份权力，早期可以是创新、是发展，后期可以是僵化、是限制发展。

所以诊断照它现在在做什么。位置的作用变了以后，早期的结论不能直接沿用：原有的根据哪些仍有效，要照现在的关系重核。

学科论在另一处说过同形的话。它读一个审查位置的独立性时说：「前提或职责变化后，上一轮的独立性结论不能直接沿用」，已有的证据要按新关系核定哪些仍有效、哪些已不足（学科论第八篇 S41）。原说的是独立性的结论；这里推到诊断的结论（自担）。

上游另有两处。政治经济学列须制衡的用法时，把"位置固化"列在里面（政治经济学第三篇 E17）。政府论说，一项决定可以终局，「结案以后，新证据或新问题之下仍可再受问」（政府论第六篇 G36）。一个位置早年被认作开路的，不因此终身免核。

重核的是当下的结构，不重写已经发生的行为及其账。第 22 年那一笔收购，当年作了，账就在那一年；照现在核，不是把旧账改写，是问那个位置今天在做什么。

这一条两头都成立。

一头是：早期的开路，不作后期的名。第 2 年替小作坊代卖的那一份开路，是真的；它不替第 22 年的条款作答。拿早期的真作后期的名，第三篇专写。

另一头是：从前控制、现在不再控制的，也照现在认。早期加给它的约束与救济，同样照现在重核。

这一头，用一个"设若"来看。第 24 年，竞争审查处的报告建议"场与自营分开"：通衢市集与通衢家用分属两家公司，各自经营。设若第 30 年起，另两家线上市集合计超过了通衢市集；到第 33 年，王国州的线上零售经通衢市集的降到约两成，州内只在通衢卖的卖家，由约三成降到不足一成。这时，第 24 年那份建议，照现在还要不要，要到哪一步？

照现在核，要问的是第 33 年的结构：卖家离不离得开，有没有的选，问不问得动。读数若变了，当年的建议就要照现在重核，可以收窄，也可以退出。这一个"设若"不说明通衢已经不须受问：照现在核，不是免核；也不说明第 24 年的建议当年就不该提：那是照第 24 年的结构提的。第七篇读早期的约束怎样照现在重核时，回到这里。

同一个通衢，第 15 年与第 29 年各在做什么？第 15 年，它拿自己的积累给新来的小作坊免一年佣金；第 29 年，它出价收购一家刚长出来的物流公司。两年都是同一份权力，作用不同。读数在哪几年变了，要照每一年的结构核。

第六节　不是专制，与专制同形

这个系列与另一样东西有一个共同的形状：专制。

上游已经走过这一步。政府论说，位置不能终局：「没有一个位置可以独占关门之权」（政府论第六篇 G36）；政治经济学把这一句推到了经济位置（政治经济学第三篇 E18）。一个政治的位置与一个经济的位置，在"不能独占关门之权"这一点上，受的是同一条约束。

本系列为同形取三条。它们不是专制的定义；专制是什么，交给专制论。

一个或几个，控制了许多人的共同生活。

人离不开；离开了，也没的选。

允许请愿、上书，也不取消专制：问了，动不了它。

第三条与第四节是同一句话的两面：能被问，不取消垄断；能请愿，不取消专制。

取同形，不取等同。不说垄断者就是专制者，不给任何人、任何公司定性。一家公司控制了一个大规模的市场，可今天的公司通常没有军队、法庭，也不能把人关起来；把它叫作专制者，是把结构上的同形读成了名称上的等同。说两者"异曲同工"，是一种判断；本系列把它写成结构上的同形，不写成史学结论。历史作例子，不作证明。

两者还有一种关系，与同形是两件事：互相兑换。

一个方向是垄断把经济位置兑换成政治的保护，买走问它的路。政治经济学定过政经分离的两个方向：「钱不买成审它、问它的那份政治权力」，位置上作出的决定，也不变现为日后从被它问过的一方拿到的财富（政治经济学第四篇 E23）。第二篇读问不动时会用到。

另一个方向是专制把政治位置兑换成经济的独占：授予的专营，官营的专卖。英格兰 1624 年的《垄断法》，限制的就是王室授予的专营；汉代的盐铁官营，到昭帝时有一场盐铁会议，桓宽把双方的辩论记成《盐铁论》，争的正是官营与民争利。政治经济学定过，公私同一，「按权力面认，不按公私认」；授予的位置照它的来源受制衡，积累的位置照积累受制衡，一个位置两种来源都有的，分别核（政治经济学第三篇 E15）。这两处历史，在本系列里是"互相兑换"的入口，不是同形的例子；原文与它们留下的问题，在 Paper 0 对接。

上游另有一处"专制"，这里只作指引。政治经济学第六篇引过康德：一种以臣民的幸福为目标、像父亲对孩子那样施行善意的政府，臣民应当怎样幸福，只能等元首的判断；康德说这样的政府是"可设想的最大专制"（康德，AA 8:291，经政治经济学第六篇所引）。替人判断他应当怎样，这一形状与以安全为名相接，第三篇回到它。

同形在远景里是什么样子，用一户人家的一天来看。这是一个设想，写在王国州的第 55 年：机器做了农业、饮水等基础层的活以后。苹果城东市的一户人家，早上的米与菜出自一家农业运营公司的田，在市集里经市集自己的代买助手下单，那个助手由一家模型公司的模型驱动；水费在家里用的助手里缴，那也是这家模型公司的助手；两位老人的基础给付，经同一个助手到账。晚上，女儿想把家里的助手换成别家的。她算了一下：老人的给付要到窗口重新核实，约六周，最近的窗口要坐车两小时；家里三年的记录带不走；换了别家，缴水费要排在后面；在市集里能替她下单的助手，换了也仍只有市集自己的那一个。她没有换。她在助手里写了一条申诉："我想换，换不了。"助手答："已为您记录，相关部门会处理。"到年底，没有回音。

每一件，单看都各有作用与代价，有的还很好：给付从没有延误，管网的漏损降了，城北的村子接上了水。本篇只问一件：合起来，离不离得开？这户人家是设想，不是预测；它不说明哪一家已是专制者，也不说明每一件都越了线，更不说明所有的路都已封死。它显出的是同形可能长成的样子：几处依赖叠在一处，离开的代价相互叠加，申诉没有回音。离不离得开、问不问得动，要逐处核；这一户人家只把要问的地方摆在了一起。第六篇把这一户人家收回到 AI 对基础层的垄断；第七篇问，问不动的地方，要回到哪一年的制衡去补。

专制本身，国家为什么失败，交给专制论。本系列只立同形，留一个接口。

第七节　应当说给谁

这个系列沿用制度论的三层：「构用应当，位置用职责，人用自己的法」（制度论第一篇 Z4）。应当说给构；位置上的人，有他所在位置的职责；人自己，照他自己的法。

在本系列里，能接应当的构有四类，按功能分。

一、立规、执规与审的构。 竞争与监管机构，执法的一侧；行业协会、写标准与认证的构；独立检验与审计的位置；作出公共支持的位置。市场论已经写过监管者也受问；本系列写它们对垄断守什么，怎样不被问不动。法院作出的认定，本系列照档次引用，不对法院写应当。

二、处在垄断位置的构。 公司、平台、基础设施的运营者，国营的在内。判结构，不判名称；审公营供给的位置，照市场论已立的核法，在功能上独立于供给者与它的主人。

三、出资与结伙的构。 投资方，云与算力的伙伴，并购的买方。按实际控制核：政治经济学说，名义持有与调动分开，持有多少不等于能调动多少（政治经济学第一篇 E2）。

四、把它采作门的构。 作采购或采用决定、把一家的产品写成必用的政府或机构；委托与外包同此。学科论说，一条标准被采作门时，「采用它的构就它的采用作答」（学科论第六篇 S34）；政府论说，判断交给机器，承担不随之交出（政府论第一篇 G8）。一个政府把某一家的助手写成办事的唯一入口，这一采用，它自己要答。

同一个构可以兼处几类，按它在这一件事里的功能落址。一家公司可以在一个市场处在垄断的位置，在另一个市场是出资的一方；一个政府可以是立规的构，也可以是把一家产品采作门的构。

人照他自己的法，本系列不对他写应当。

第八节　这个系列怎样写

诊断三件。 形成：一个位置怎样成了绕不过去的，第一篇。免问：问它的路被关掉；或者路还在，问了动不了它，第二篇。名义：以发展、以安全为名，替自己作答，第三篇。

三个领域。 数字、技术、AI，第四至第六篇。篇名写作"数字与垄断""技术与垄断""AI 与垄断"："与"不预设这一领域已经垄断。AI 写成在形成中的集中与它的结构性风险。远景只写基础层：机器做了农业、饮水这些活以后，垄断可能的形态；写成设想，不是预测。

药方三件。 制衡在事前，监督在事中，追问在事后；三件按主要作用分工，不是互斥的三段。药方还要让它倒得起：减依赖，不减能力。规则没跟上，不等于不受问。第七篇。

批判三层。 规范层批结构性的方向，本系列做。规则层的设计，本系列只作建议与例子；永远有余项与例外，余项推不出许可。判断层由在场的位置与人作出。

例子三档，点名分两种。 三档分的是材料的来处：已结的历史案；当下的平台；正在进行的 AI。点名分两种。判一家公司的行为越线、违法、滥用，要法院判决或监管决定已作出认定，照相应机构、相应日期写，写成"法院认定""委员会认定"，标出审级与状态；只有指控、调查或起诉而没有认定的，不据以判它。读一个已经结束、事实公开的交易或安排的结构，监管机构明说的理由只是削弱竞争、没有别的合理原因的，算结构性的反垄断，可以点名读它的可能（有垄断的可能，不是已成垄断），不判行为，不给公司定性；明说了国家安全等别的理由的，那一条理由不读作垄断。还在过程中的，不用；AI 一档的这类事，改用设例。对每一家公司用同一套判据；同类的定案，用就一起用。已发表的研究可以引，写成"某某研究指出"，不作定案。判行为的，照认定写；读已经结束的结构的，照公开的事实与所据的理由写，只读它的可能；两种都不代作行为的判定，不作本系列对一家公司的定性。

设例。 沿用王国州，续写到第 36 年；远景写到第 55 年。设例只记事实，以问作结，两面照记：至少一处在给人开路，一处握着别人的路。设例可以大胆设想形态，不下结论；它不影射在过程中的真实案子，也不对真实的行业作预测。

不做。 不主张一概拆分：拆分是最后的手段，「较轻的办法不够时才拆分，用时说明作用、代价与替代」（市场论第四篇 SC9）。不给反垄断法定门槛，不替法院、监管者判一家公司。不倚靠"必然崩溃"，也不倚靠"必然进步"：政治经济学说「本系列只承涌现的必然，不承主导的必然」（政治经济学第六篇 E32）；这一句够得上不倚靠"必然进步"，推到不倚靠"必然崩溃"、不说集中会自己散去，是本系列自担。不对人写应当。不碰政治事件，不以人物为题。

与传统。 这个系列与已有的论述怎样对接，集中在 Paper 0。"垄断"一词的来处，也在那里展开。

篇序照诊断在先、药方在后：先认它怎样形成、怎样免问、以什么为名，再看三个领域，最后开药方。

这一篇停在哪里

这一篇不立命题，只把垄断不是什么清出来，并给出宽义、结构性影响的判据、名字跟着方向，以及同形。它清出的几条，各在后面论证：控制按实际认、照现在核，在第一篇；能被问不取消、问了要动得了，在第二篇；名义换不来免问，在第三篇；三个领域各在第四至第六篇；药方在第七篇。

它没有证明的：同形即等同；宽义即罪名；结构性影响的三样判据，在每一处都已核到。通衢是不是垄断，这一篇没有回答；它在各篇里逐项核。

留下的余项：结构性影响的三样判据，是本系列的判准，它们各在后面各篇的事例里受检验；"为了谁的利益照作用核"，要在利益相左处才读得出，利益不相左时看不出方向，这一点各篇照实记。
