Abstract

Paper 3 said that whether misrouting can last depends on where the function of questioning the ought lies, and how large it is. This paper looks at seats. A seat means a function, not an institution: within one institution, several committees each have their own powers and touch the same kind of decision; which of them is questioning which line on the normative ledger is registered by powers and by the actual record of handling, not sorted by name. Comparison must align levels and also functions: at the same level, compare at the same stage; the several functions of one institution are registered separately (S21). In the rule-making cases this paper examined, one can see: the evidence function is standing, routine, and has decision-making power; the function of questioning normative premises, in the public participation arrangements examined, is advisory only, and the committee that bore it was set up and then stopped; the jurisdiction of appeals is narrowed to procedure. Three further types, absent, set up ad hoc for one topic, and set up only after something goes wrong, are registered only as types; this paper has no rule-making instance of them. This is existential and typological, not a claim about numbers (S22). Ethics review at the project level has real power; as to the long-range effects of applying knowledge, the text excludes them from this assessment of risk (S23). Comparison is registered column by column by condition; "advisory power only" is not by itself judged to be toothless (S24). An arrangement in which the four conditions hold jointly and which is standing cannot be pointed to as an instance of their not being met; it may still take an advisory or other organizational form; existential claims can also be refuted (S25). Five propositions in all (S21 to S25), stated at the end of each section and listed together at the end.

Introduction

When the UK National Institute for Health and Care Excellence (NICE) appraises a new drug or technology, a technology appraisal committee looks at the evidence: clinical effectiveness, cost, cost-utility; it also makes trade-offs according to the thresholds and methods the Institute uses. The committee's recommendations have consequences: they decide whether the technology can be used in the National Health Service.

The same Institute also had a Citizens Council, made up of ordinary citizens, which gave views on questions of social value in the Institute's work. In its principles document Social Value Judgements, the Institute drew on the Citizens Council's reports (NICE 2008). The Citizens Council could only advise. It did not meet after 2015. The Institute later set up NICE Listens, a deliberative public engagement programme, whose first project was in 2021; the Institute's annual report states: "NICE Listens replaces our Citizens Council." (NICE 2022, print edition p.24). Its recommendations, too, are only for the consideration of the Institute's executive team and board. Whether this kind of questioning had any other bearer in the interval, this paper has not checked; what is registered here are the powers and continuance of the two arrangements, and it is not concluded from them that this function of the Institute ever stopped as a whole.

The two committees touch the same kind of decision. The technology appraisal committee assesses evidence and also makes trade-offs according to the thresholds and methods the Institute uses, and its recommendations have consequences; the Citizens Council gave views on the social values behind these trade-offs, could only advise, later stopped, and was succeeded by NICE Listens. Which of them is questioning which line on the normative ledger is not sorted by name; it is registered item by item by the columns of §4. This paper starts from here. It does not judge whether NICE does well or badly; what it asks is how arrangements like this should be compared.

The reading follows the Prequel. This paper concerns the second domain, standards that act on persons (Prequel §5). §1 says that comparison must align levels and functions; §2 registers several forms at the rule-making level; §3 speaks of narrowing at the project level; §4 gives the columns for comparison; §5 gives conditions for counterexamples; §6 lists several contrasts on the government side.

§1 Aligning Levels and Functions

The reviewer of a single paper can reject a manuscript that used the wrong statistical method, but cannot change the threshold of "significance." What can change a discipline's premises is at the rule-making level: the working groups of a diagnostic manual, the working groups of evidence grading, standards committees, the revision groups of a guideline's methods manual.

So comparison must first align levels. Compare a reviewer with a standards committee, and the difference that comes out is only a difference of level.

Once levels are aligned, stages must be aligned too. From its formation to its taking effect, a standard passes through several stages: development, recommendation, adoption, implementation, review. Institutions at the same level are responsible for different stages. Comparison must be made at the same stage: compare a committee that develops standards with a body that reviews their implementation, and the difference that comes out is again only a difference of stage.

One institution often carries several functions. In NICE, the technology appraisal committee and the Citizens Council have different powers, yet both touch lines on the normative ledger: appraising cost-utility against a threshold is itself a trade-off (Paper 6, §3). So when this paper says "the evidence seat" and "the ought seat," it means functions, not institutions: an institution can carry both functions, or only one; in registering, the several functions of one institution are recorded separately. The main comparison is placed within one construct, because there levels and stages are easiest to align.

S21 Aligning levels and functions. The reviewer of a single paper cannot change a discipline's premises; what can is at the rule-making level: the working groups of a diagnostic manual, the working groups of evidence grading, standards committees. Comparison is made at the same level and the same stage (development, recommendation, adoption, implementation, review); the several functions of one institution are registered separately. "The evidence seat" and "the ought seat" mean functions, not institutions.

§2 Forms at the Rule-Making Level

In the rule-making cases this paper examined, the evidence function is standing, routine, and has decision-making power: the committees that assess evidence meet regularly, work by their manuals, and their conclusions enter decisions. For the function of questioning normative premises, two forms can be seen in the cases examined:

  • Advisory only. There is a position that can voice views, but the decision lies elsewhere. NICE's Citizens Council, and NICE Listens which succeeded it, are both of this type.
  • Set up and then stopped. It was set up, it ran, and later it stopped. NICE's Citizens Council, as an arrangement, is of this type; its work was later taken up by NICE Listens, and what is recorded here is the continuance of this arrangement, not the continuance of this function of the Institute.

Three further types are registered only as types; this paper has no rule-making instance of them:

  • Absent. No stage in the procedure deals specifically with that line on the normative ledger.
  • Set up ad hoc for one topic. A question arises, a committee is set up, the question is answered, the committee is dissolved. There is one instance on the government side; see §6.
  • Set up only after something goes wrong. Only once a dispute has blown up is a position set up to deal with it.

There is also an instance of "set up and then stopped" on the government side, the US Congress's Office of Technology Assessment; see §6.

There is one more form, which falls on appeals. Standard development has a seat for appeals, but its jurisdiction can be narrowed to procedure. The Board of Standards Review of the American National Standards Institute "does not evaluate the content of standards and does not hear appeals with regard to technical issues"; the IEEE Standards Association provides that it "shall not provide an appeals process to resolve a disagreement about a technical decision" (ANSI; IEEE SA). There are appeals, and the appeals ask only about procedure. The contrast is the International Organization for Standardization and the International Electrotechnical Commission: their Directives allow appeals on technical matters (ISO/IEC Directives, Part 1).

The Red Book cited in Paper 3 is a close forerunner at this level: it called for the assessment and management of risk to be separated in concept, but did not recommend placing the two in different organizations (National Research Council 1983). The functions were separated in concept; the seats were not.

These are existential and typological: the two types that have instances are written as they are; the other three are only types. It is not said that "most rule-making bodies have no function of questioning normative premises." To use a quantifier such as "most," one must first fix a sampling frame, for example all the bodies that write standards in a field, and then check them one by one; this paper has not taken that step.

The type "set up only after something goes wrong" joins Paper 5: a remainder covered by a default value is still on the ledger; when someone takes it up, newly established questioning is one shape that taking up can have.

S22 Forms at the rule-making level. In the rule-making cases this paper examined, one can see: the evidence function is standing, routine, and has decision-making power; the function of questioning normative premises, in the public participation arrangements examined, is advisory only, and the committee that bore it was set up and then stopped (NICE's Citizens Council, and NICE Listens which succeeded it); the jurisdiction of appeals is narrowed to procedure (ANSI, IEEE SA). Three further types are registered only as types, and this paper has no rule-making instance of them: absent; set up ad hoc for one topic (one instance on the government side); set up only after something goes wrong. This is existential and typological, not a claim about numbers.

§3 Narrowing at the Project Level

Below the rule-making level is the project level: a particular study, a particular protocol. At this level there is a seat with real power, the ethics review board. It can approve, require modifications, and disapprove.

Its jurisdiction is drawn very clearly. Paper 1's Introduction cited the provision: the board "should not consider possible long-range effects of applying knowledge gained in the research (e.g., the possible effects of the research on public policy) as among those research risks that fall within the purview of its responsibility" (45 CFR 46.111(a)(2)). The text says should not: not "need not," but should not. As to the long-range effects of applying knowledge, which this paper asks about, the provision excludes them from this assessment of risk. An ethics review board's jurisdiction is not only risks and benefits: the equitable selection of subjects, informed consent, and the protection of privacy are in the other paragraphs of the same section; this paper asks only about the one item of long-range effects.

Methodology is drawn the same way. In 2024 the US Department of Health and Human Services' Secretary's Advisory Committee on Human Research Protections issued a recommendation on how research of no informational value should be reviewed. It says: "It is not the role of the IRB to either design or improve research methodology, but to identify research that it finds is unlikely to achieve its stated goals." The board's authority to disapprove research or require modifications is limited to cases where risks are unreasonable, or where modification is needed to meet the balance of risks and benefits (SACHRP 2024).

So this seat at the project level has teeth, but on the two matters this paper asks about, what it can bite is marked out: the long-range social effects of research, the text states, do not count among the risks it assesses; as to methodology, it neither designs nor improves it, only identifies research unlikely to achieve its stated goals, and its power to disapprove or require modifications is tied to risks and benefits. Paper 1 said that the federal regulation states "this is not handled here," without stating "who handles it." Stanford University's additional Ethics and Society Review makes completion of the Ethics and Society Review process, including consideration of the proposal's possible risks to society, a requirement for funding from participating programs (Bernstein et al. 2021): it takes up part of what is left, as a supplementary arrangement with its own scope, with funding as its gate.

S23 Narrowing at the project level. Ethics review at the project level has real power: it can approve, require modifications, and disapprove. As to the long-range effects of applying knowledge, the text excludes them from this assessment of risk; as to methodology, it neither designs nor improves it, only identifies research unlikely to achieve its stated goals; its authority to disapprove or require modifications is limited to cases of unreasonable risk or an imbalance of risks and benefits. This does not summarize the whole jurisdiction of ethics review.

§4 Comparing by Condition

With levels and stages aligned, what is compared? Column by column, by the four conditions of Paper 2, with several columns added. The columns of a comparison table can be laid out like this:

Most cells read "not checked." That is not a defect of the table but its use: it sets out what is not yet known, and does not let the sentence "one has decision-making power, the other can only advise" stand in for registering the four conditions item by item.

One point must be held to especially here: "advisory power only" is not by itself judged to be toothless. Can the advice require the other side to answer? Can it initiate a review? Can its views be set aside without reasons? Only once these three have been checked can one say whether advice has teeth. A position that can only advise but has the right to require a written answer point by point is not the same as one whose advice, once given, is the end of it.

§5 Conditions for Counterexamples

This paper's generalizations must be open to refutation.

For the same p, the same jurisdiction and the same stage, if there is an arrangement in which the four conditions for the corresponding function hold jointly and which is standing, it cannot be pointed to as an instance of the four conditions not being met, and it limits the range over which this paper's generalizations apply. It may still take an advisory or other organizational form: a standing advisory position can require responses, initiate reviews and stay independent, while not holding the final decision. Form and conditions are registered separately. A judgment about a field as a whole must be tested against the same sampling frame and coverage. Existential claims can also be refuted: if a particular case pointed to as "not met" in fact has the corresponding powers, protections and record of initiation, that identification does not stand.

There is already an example that limits this paper's generalizations. GRADE, the system for grading evidence, rates which outcomes count and how important each is before rating the certainty of evidence (GRADE Handbook). Its Evidence to Decision framework lists how people value the main outcomes as one of the judgments to be made in forming a recommendation (Alonso-Coello et al. 2016). This shows that, within this procedure, the importance of outcomes is not left unevaluated: this line on the normative ledger is placed at the start of the procedure. So the generalization "the function of questioning normative premises is absent everywhere" does not hold here.

Whether it meets the four conditions must be registered item by item: who rates the importance of outcomes; whether those affected can get in; whether the results of the rating can require a response, whether they can initiate a review. These items have not all been checked. Until they are, "not checked" is kept, and this section judges neither that it is a counterexample nor that it is not. It serves first as a positive example: a line on the normative ledger can have a place in the procedure.

Stanford's Ethics and Society Review is another kind: a newly established position that takes up part of the questions left at the project level, with completion of its review required for funding from participating programs (Bernstein et al. 2021). Its four conditions, too, are recorded separately item by item.

S25 Conditions for counterexamples. For the same p, J and stage, an arrangement in which the four conditions for the corresponding function hold jointly and which is standing cannot be pointed to as an instance of the four conditions not being met; it may still take an advisory or other organizational form, and the two are registered separately. Such an arrangement limits the range over which this paper's generalizations apply; judgments of coverage about a field must be tested against the same sampling frame and coverage. Existential claims can also be refuted: a particular case pointed to as "not met" may in fact have the corresponding powers, protections and record of initiation.

§6 Contrasts on the Government Side

The government side has also set up seats that question the ought. They are not part of this paper's main comparison, because they do not sit at the rule-making level of the disciplines; they serve as contrasts, to see how seats can be set up.

  • The US Congress's Office of Technology Assessment. Established by the Technology Assessment Act of 1972, with the statutory task of providing Congress with assessments of the consequences of technology (2 USC §472). In 1995 Congress voted to withdraw its funding, and the Office closed on 29 September of that year (OTA archive page); what stopped was the funding, and the provisions establishing it are still in the Code. Standing, mandated, set up and then stopped.
  • The German Ethics Council. Under the Ethics Council Act, it follows the ethical, social and legal questions raised by research in the life sciences, informs the public, issues opinions and makes recommendations; the Act states that it is independent in its work and bound only by the tasks the Act sets (Ethikratgesetz §§2–3). Standing, independent, able only to advise: of the "advisory only" type.
  • The German Ethics Commission on Automated and Connected Driving. Set up by the Federal Ministry of Transport to work on one topic, it delivered its report in 2017, proposing a set of ethical rules (Ethik-Kommission 2017). Of the "set up ad hoc for one topic" type.
  • Article 27 of the EU Artificial Intelligence Act. Certain deployers of high-risk AI systems must, before deployment, assess the system's impact on fundamental rights and notify the market surveillance authority of the results (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, Article 27). For high-risk systems listed in Article 6(2) and Annex III, this obligation applies, under the 2026 amending regulation, from 2 December 2027 (Article 113 as replaced by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744): the law already provides for it, and the obligation has not yet begun to apply. It places a normative assessment before deployment, on the adopting side; it does not belong to the five types of §2, and Paper 6 speaks further of the adopting side.

The first three belong to three of the forms in §2; the fourth belongs to none of the five types. What they show is that seats can be set up in many forms; this paper does not judge which is good.

Where This Paper Stops

This paper does not say where the function of the ought should be set up or what it should look like; that is the business of Paper 9, and there too it is offered only as suggestion; it does not judge which institution does badly.

What it hands to Paper 5: how a premise that the using side treats as absent goes on acting.

The remainder it leaves: the trial table in §4 has not yet been aligned to one premise, and most cells are "not checked"; GRADE's four conditions have not all been checked; the three further types in §2 have no rule-making instance, none of the five types has a sampling frame, and no claim about numbers is made.

摘要

第三篇说，错送能长久，与问应当的功能在哪里，有多大有关。这一篇看席位。席位指功能，不指机构：同一个机构里，几个委员会各有职权，碰的是同一类决定；哪一处在问规范账上的哪一行，按职权与实际处理的记录登记，不按名称分。比较要对齐层级，也要对齐功能：在同一层，按同一环节比；同一机构的几种功能分别登记（S21）。在这一篇查过的规则制定层案例里，看得到：证据功能常设，例行，有决定权；追问规范前提的功能，在所查的公众参与安排上只有咨询，承担它的委员会设过又停；申诉的辖域划窄到程序。另三型，不在场，为一个专题临时设立，出事之后才设立，只作类型登记，这一篇没有规则制定层的实例。这是存在式加类型，不作数量判断（S22）。项目层的伦理审查有实权；就知识应用的长远后果，条文把它排除在这一项风险评估之外（S23）。比较按条件逐列登记；"只有咨询权"不单独判作没有牙齿（S24）。四条件合取且常设的安排，不能被指认为不满足的实例，它仍可能属于咨询等组织形态；存在式也可以被反驳（S25）。全篇五条（S21 至 S25），各节末写出，篇末合列。

引言

英国国家卫生与临床优化研究所（NICE）评一项新药或新技术，由技术评估委员会看证据：临床效果，成本，成本效用；它也照研究所使用的阈值与方法作取舍。委员会的建议有后果：它决定这项技术在国民医疗服务体系里能不能用上。

同一个研究所，也设过一个公民委员会，由普通公民组成，就研究所的工作里那些社会价值的问题给出意见。研究所在《社会价值判断》这份原则文件里，吸收过公民委员会的报告（NICE 2008）。公民委员会只能建议。它 2015 年以后不再开会。研究所后来设了 NICE Listens，一个审议式的公众参与项目，第一项在 2021 年；研究所的年报写明：「NICE Listens replaces our Citizens Council.」（NICE 2022，印刷版 p.24）。它的建议也只供研究所的执行团队与董事会参考。其间这类追问有没有别的承担者，这一篇没有核；这里登记的是两项安排的职权与存续，不据此判定研究所的这一功能曾整体停止。

两个委员会碰的是同一类决定。技术评估委员会评证据，也照研究所使用的阈值与方法作取舍，建议有后果；公民委员会就这些取舍背后的社会价值给意见，只能建议，后来停了，由 NICE Listens 接替。哪一处在问规范账上的哪一行，不按名称分，照第四节的列逐项登记。这一篇从这里起。它不判 NICE 做得好不好；它问的是，要比较这样的安排，该怎样比。

读法照前置篇。这一篇管第二层，作用到人的标准（前置篇第五节）。第一节说比较要对齐层级与功能；第二节登记规则制定层的几种形态；第三节说项目层的划窄；第四节给比较的表列；第五节给反例条件；第六节列政府一侧的几个对照。

第一节　对齐层级与功能

一篇论文的审稿人，可以拒掉一篇用错了统计方法的稿子，却改不了"显著"的门槛。改得了学科前提的，在规则制定层：诊断手册的工作组，证据分级的工作组，标准委员会，指南的方法学手册的修订组。

所以比较先要对齐层级。拿审稿人去比标准委员会，比出来的差别只是层级的差别。

层级对齐了，还要对齐环节。一条标准从形成到起作用，经过几个环节：制定，建议，采用，执行，复核。同一层的机构，分别负责不同的环节。比较要在同一个环节上比：拿一个制定标准的委员会，去比一个复核标准执行的机构，比出来的又只是环节的差别。

同一个机构，常常兼几种功能。NICE 里，技术评估委员会与公民委员会职权不同，可两处都碰到规范账上的行：照一条阈值评成本效用，本身就是一次取舍（第六篇第三节）。所以这一篇说"证据席""应当席"，指的是功能，不是机构：一个机构可以兼有两种功能，也可以只有一种；登记时，同一机构的几种功能分开记。主比较放在同一个构里，因为那里层级与环节最容易对齐。

S21　对齐层级与功能。 单篇论文的审稿人改不了学科前提；改得了的在规则制定层：诊断手册的工作组，证据分级的工作组，标准委员会。比较要在同一层，按同一环节（制定，建议，采用，执行，复核）比；同一机构的几种功能分别登记。"证据席""应当席"指功能，不指机构。

第二节　规则制定层的形态

在这一篇查过的规则制定层案例里，证据功能是常设的，例行的，有决定权的：评证据的委员会定期开会，按手册办事，结论进入决定。追问规范前提的功能，查过的案例里看得到两种形态：

  • 只有咨询。 有一个位置可以发表意见，决定却在别处。NICE 的公民委员会，与接替它的 NICE Listens，都是这一型。
  • 设过又停。 设立过，运行过，后来停了。NICE 的公民委员会作为一项安排，是这一型；它的工作后来由 NICE Listens 接替，这里记的是这项安排的存续，不是研究所这一功能的存续。

另有三型，只作类型登记，这一篇没有规则制定层的实例：

  • 不在场。 程序里没有一个环节，专门处理规范账上的那一行。
  • 为一个专题临时设立。 一个问题来了，设一个委员会，问题答完，委员会解散。政府一侧有一例，见第六节。
  • 出事之后才设立。 一项争议闹大了，才设一个位置来处理它。

政府一侧也有"设过又停"的一例，美国国会的技术评估办公室，见第六节。

还有一种形态，落在申诉上。标准的制定有申诉席位，可它的辖域可以划窄到程序。美国国家标准学会的标准审查委员会，「does not evaluate the content of standards and does not hear appeals with regard to technical issues」；电气与电子工程师学会的标准协会规定，它「shall not provide an appeals process to resolve a disagreement about a technical decision」（ANSI；IEEE SA）。有申诉，申诉只问程序。作对照的是国际标准化组织与国际电工委员会：它们的工作指令允许就技术问题提出申诉（ISO/IEC Directives，第 1 部分）。

第三篇引过的红皮书，是这一层的一个近前身：它要求风险的评估与管理在概念上分开，却不主张把两者放进不同的组织（National Research Council 1983）。功能在概念上分开了，席位没有分开。

这些是存在式加类型：已有实例的两型照写，另三型只作类型。它不说"多数规则制定机构没有追问规范前提的功能"。要用"多数"一类量词，先要定抽样框，例如一个领域里全部写标准的机构，再逐一查；这一篇没有做这一步。

"出事之后才设立"一型，与第五篇相接：被默认值盖住的余项仍在账上；有人接手时，新设的追问是接手的一种样子。

S22　规则制定层的形态。 在这一篇查过的规则制定层案例里，看得到：证据功能常设，例行，有决定权；追问规范前提的功能，在所查的公众参与安排上只有咨询，承担它的委员会设过又停（NICE 公民委员会，及接替它的 NICE Listens）；申诉的辖域划窄到程序（ANSI，IEEE SA）。另三型只作类型登记，这一篇没有规则制定层的实例：不在场；为一个专题临时设立（政府一侧有一例）；出事之后才设立。这是存在式加类型，不作数量判断。

第三节　项目层的划窄

规则制定层以下，是项目层：一项具体的研究，一份具体的方案。这一层有一个有实权的席位，伦理审查委员会。它能批，能要求修改，能否决。

它的辖域划得很清楚。第一篇引言引过那一条：委员会「should not consider possible long-range effects of applying knowledge gained in the research (e.g., the possible effects of the research on public policy) as among those research risks that fall within the purview of its responsibility」（45 CFR 46.111(a)(2)）。条文用的是 should not，译作"不应"：不是"不必"，是不应。就这一篇所问的知识应用的长远后果，这一条款把它排除在这一项风险评估之外。伦理审查委员会的辖域不止风险与收益：受试者的公平选择，知情同意，隐私的保护，在同一条的其他各款里；这一篇只问长远后果这一项。

方法学也是这样划的。美国卫生与公众服务部的人类研究保护咨询委员会 2024 年有一份建议，讨论没有信息价值的研究该怎样审。它说：「It is not the role of the IRB to either design or improve research methodology, but to identify research that it finds is unlikely to achieve its stated goals.」委员会拒绝研究或要求修改的权限，限于风险不合理，或为满足风险与收益的平衡而须修改的情形（SACHRP 2024）。

所以项目层的这一席，有牙齿，可就这一篇所问的两件，它咬的范围是划好的：研究的长远社会后果，条文写明不算进它评的风险；方法学，它不设计也不改进，只识别不太可能达到所述目标的研究，拒绝与要求修改的权限系在风险收益上。第一篇说过，那条联邦法规写明了"这里不管"，没有写"归谁管"。斯坦福大学另设的伦理与社会审查，把研究对社会可能的风险列为申请资助的前置条件（Bernstein 等 2021）：它接住了一部分，是一项补充的安排，有它自己的范围，以资助为门。

S23　项目层的划窄。 项目层的伦理审查有实权，能批，能要求修改，能否决。就知识应用的长远后果，条文把它排除在这一项风险评估之外；方法学，它不设计也不改进，只识别不太可能达到所述目标的研究；拒绝与要求修改的权限，限于风险不合理或风险收益失衡的情形。这一条不概括伦理审查的全部辖域。

多数格子写着"未核"。这不是表的缺点，是表的用处：它把还不知道的地方摆出来，不让"一个有决定权，一个只能建议"这一句，替代四条件的逐项登记。

这里有一条要特别守住："只有咨询权"，不单独判作没有牙齿。咨询能不能要求对方答复？能不能发动一次复审？它的意见能不能被无理由地搁下？这三件查过了，才说咨询有没有牙齿。一个只能建议却有权要求逐条书面答复的位置，与一个建议了就算完的位置，不是一回事。

S24　按条件比。 比较表对齐这几列：p；J；环节；辖域权；回应权；复审权；独立条件；实际启动记录；另加常设性，触发方式，覆盖面。"只有咨询权"不单独判为没有牙齿：查咨询能否要求答复，能否发动复审，意见能否被无理由搁置。

第五节　反例条件

这一篇的概括，要能被反驳。

对同一个 p，同一个辖域，同一个环节，若有一个安排，相应功能的四条件合取成立，而且常设，它就不能被指认为四条件不满足的实例，并限制这一篇概括的适用范围。它仍可能属于咨询等组织形态：一个常设的咨询位置，可以要求回应，发动复审，保持独立，同时不掌握最后的决定。形态与条件，分别登记。关于某一领域整体的判断，要按同一抽样框与覆盖范围检验。存在式也可以被反驳：一个被指认为"不满足"的具体案例，若实际上有相应的权限，保护与启动记录，那一指认就不成立。

已经有一个例子，限制了这一篇的概括。证据分级的 GRADE，在评证据的确定性以前，先评哪些结局算数，各多重要（GRADE 手册）。它的"从证据到决定"框架，把人们怎样看重主要的结局，列为作建议时要判断的一项（Alonso-Coello 等 2016）。这说明，在这一套程序里，结局的重要性不是没有经过评价的：规范账上的这一行，被摆在了程序的开头。所以"追问规范前提的功能一概不在场"这一概括，在这里不成立。

它满不满足四条件，要逐项登记：谁来评结局的重要性；受影响的人进不进得去；评的结果能不能要求回应，能不能启动复审。这几项还没有核齐。核齐以前，保留"未核"，不按这一节判它是反例，也不判它不是。它先作一个正面的例子：规范账上的一行，可以在程序里有一个位置。

斯坦福的伦理与社会审查是另一种：一个新设的位置，以资助为前置条件，接住项目层留下的一部分问题（Bernstein 等 2021）。它的四条件，也逐项另记。

S25　反例条件。 对同一 p，J 与环节，相应功能四条件合取成立且常设的安排，不能被指认为四条件不满足的实例；它仍可能属于咨询等组织形态，两者分别登记。这样的安排限制本篇概括的适用范围；关于某一领域的覆盖性判断，须按同一抽样框与覆盖范围检验。存在式也可被反驳：被指认为"不满足"的具体案例，实际有相应权限，保护与启动记录。

第六节　政府一侧的对照

政府一侧也设过问应当的席位。它们不作这一篇的主比较，因为它们不在学科的规则制定层；作对照，看席位可以怎样设。

  • 美国国会技术评估办公室。 由 1972 年的《技术评估法》设立，法律给的任务，是向国会提供技术后果的评估（2 USC §472）。1995 年，国会投票撤回它的经费，办公室于当年 9 月 29 日关闭（OTA 存档页）；停的是经费，设立它的条文仍在法典里。常设，受托，设过又停。
  • 德国伦理委员会。 依《伦理委员会法》，它跟踪生命科学研究带来的伦理，社会与法律问题，向公众通报，发表意见，提出建议；法律写明它在工作上独立，只受这部法律所定的任务约束（Ethikratgesetz §§2–3）。常设，独立，只能建议：属"只有咨询"一型。
  • 德国自动驾驶与网联驾驶伦理委员会。 由联邦交通部设立，为一个专题工作，2017 年交出报告，提出一组伦理规则（Ethik-Kommission 2017）。属"为一个专题临时设立"一型。
  • 欧盟《人工智能法》第 27 条。 某些部署高风险人工智能系统的主体，在部署以前，要评估系统对基本权利的影响，并把结果通知市场监督机构（Regulation (EU) 2024/1689，第 27 条）。对第 6 条第 2 款与附件三所列的高风险系统，这一义务经 2026 年的修订条例，自 2027 年 12 月 2 日起适用（Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 替换后的第 113 条）：法律已有规定，义务尚未开始适用。它把一次规范的评估放在部署以前，放在采用的一方；它不属第二节的五型，第六篇再说采用的一方。

前三个分属第二节的三种形态，第四个不属五型。它们说明的是：席位的形态有许多种可设；这一篇不判哪一种好。

这一篇停在哪里

这一篇不说应当的功能该设在哪里，长什么样，那是第九篇的事，而且那里也只作建议；不判哪个机构做得不好。

交给第五篇的：用的一方当作没有的前提，怎样继续作用。

留下的余项：第四节的试填表还没有对齐到同一项前提，多数格子"未核"；GRADE 的四条件没有核齐；第二节另三型没有规则制定层的实例，五型都没有抽样框，不作数量判断。

  • S22　规则制定层的形态。 查过的案例里：证据功能常设，例行，有决定权；追问规范前提的功能，在所查的公众参与安排上只有咨询，承担它的委员会设过又停；申诉划窄到程序。另三型（不在场，专题临时，出事后才设）只作类型，没有规则制定层的实例。存在式加类型。（描述；类型登记）
  • S23　项目层的划窄。 伦理审查有实权；就知识应用的长远后果，条文把它排除在这一项风险评估之外；方法学不设计不改进，只识别不太可能达到所述目标的研究，拒改之权系在风险收益上；不概括伦理审查的全部辖域。（描述；45 CFR 46.111(a)(2)；SACHRP 2024）
  • S24　按条件比。 比较表的列；"只有咨询权"不单独判没牙齿。（记法：比较方法）
