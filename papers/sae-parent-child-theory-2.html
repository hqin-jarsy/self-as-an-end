Securing in the Parent-Child Relation
亲子关系的保障

Han Qin (秦汉) · Independent Researcher · 2026

Abstract

This paper reads the Base Layer in the parent-child relation this series reads: the securing a father or mother gives the child. It borrows the shape of the two layers already written out on the institutional side to read what lies between the parent and the child: the Base Layer is to be secured, the Emergence Layer supported; the two layers are written apart, matter equally, and neither is traded for the other. Protecting the child's basic rights lies on the side of anti-colonization and is not part of cultivation; acts of care and provision are not, in themselves, renamed cultivation; cultivation needs the two layers together, and the thickness of securing does not prove cultivation. The two layers are faces, not two heaps of things: one and the same giving can be read two ways. The original speaks of institutions toward persons; carrying it to the parent toward the child is this series' own undertaking.

The care and support a father or mother gives come from this end's own cannot-not: that the child needs someone to look after him is true; it enters the parent's conditions of occurrence and is avowed in his own law. This is this end's answering to its own law, not a duty owed to the child. The face of giving within it is read as entitlement, anchored in the giving the parent actually does; the child is the end this giving is directed at and does not hold it; the basic rights the child holds himself do not arise from this giving. How the child's basic rights are protected, and by what rules, belongs to the institutional side; on the occasion when a parent's effect crosses them, the relational side points out "this is a violation" and the boundary crossed; protection and disposition after the violation are handed over here, and what that effect left in the dealings is still read by this relation. Securing is not exchanged for the child's compliance, nor made conditional on his taking some particular road; the two layers are not traded.

The child's dependency on care is not a dependency that was made, and carries no direction of colonization; the kind that was made and that has locked the path to saying no carries the direction of colonization; where one first induces dependency and then withdraws, violation is checked separately at the layer of basic rights. Before 13DD, the child's crying and resistance are first read as signals to be answered: first check whether securing is lacking, and also check whether it is a prediction that has fallen through, or a negation that is coming into being; his crying alone does not certify which layer he is at. The content of the securing layer shifts with the child's phase transitions, and is not withdrawn with them; after the parents part, each one's relation with the child remains. This paper interfaces with the Analects, Locke and Hegel. It does not prove that parents owe the child some duty, nor that parents who give more care are better, nor that all dependency is harmless.

Introduction

The few days He Zhou had a fever, Lin Wan was away on a business trip, and He Chuan took leave and stayed home. The first night the fever ran very high; He Chuan got up every two hours to take his temperature, sponge him down and give him water; at daybreak he took him to the hospital, queued, had blood drawn, collected the medicine, and came home and cooked rice porridge. On the third day the fever broke; He Zhou sat up in bed wanting to watch cartoons, and He Chuan sat beside him, answering a few emails that had been waiting for two days.

Lin Wan phoned and asked He Chuan whether he was tired. He Chuan said: "Tired, sure. But with him burning up like that, what else was I going to do?"

Nothing needs saying about this as a rule: a child is ill, and the father looks after him. But what does He Chuan's "what else was I going to do" say? Were those nights of care something he owed He Zhou? If they were owed, could He Zhou claim them from him? If they were not owed, how is this care taken on? Where care is not delivered, how are the effects already left entered on the books? Had He Chuan not taken leave those days, and left He Zhou at home on his own, how would that be read, and by whom?

What this paper reads. The parent-child relation this series reads has as its premise that the end of the father or the mother is headed in the 15DD direction, and it is read relation by relation.1 This paper reads the Base Layer within such a relation: the securing a father or mother gives the child.

This paper reads seven things. First, the two layers: the Base Layer is to be secured, the Emergence Layer supported; where this shape is borrowed from, and how it is read once borrowed. Second, where securing comes from: this end's own cannot-not, and entitlement. Third, the child's standing and violation: what belongs to the institutional side, and how far the relational side reads. Fourth, that securing is not made conditional. Fifth, the two senses of dependency. Sixth, the child's signals before 13DD. Seventh, how securing shifts with the phase transitions, and what happens after the parents part.

On each of these, the direction of colonization can be read; violation is judged separately, and only at the layer of basic rights.2

Shared words. "Securing" and "support" are used apart, as on the institutional side: "securing" is used for supply in the Base Layer; giving conditions for a person's emergence is called "support."3 The institution's guarding of the child's basic rights this paper writes as "protection," not "securing," so that the two senses do not run together.

"Entitlement" follows the usage on the side of rights: it is anchored in the giving end; it is one person's giving to another, and the child is the end this giving is directed at.4 It is not something in the child's hands, nor a discounted right.

The word "duty" this series does not use between parents and child. What a father or mother gives is written as this end's own cannot-not. A legal duty of maintenance belongs to the institutional side.

Method. As in Paper 1. This paper borrows the two layers of the institutional side to read a relation between two persons; the original speaks of institutions toward persons, and carrying it to the parent toward the child is marked in the notes wherever it occurs, as this series' own undertaking.

§1 Two Layers

PC8 The shape of the two layers already written out on the institutional side is borrowed to read what lies between the parent and the child: the Base Layer is to be secured, the Emergence Layer supported. The two layers are written apart and matter equally, and neither is traded for the other; protecting the child's basic rights lies on the side of anti-colonization and is not part of cultivation; acts of care and provision are not, in themselves, renamed cultivation; cultivation needs the two layers together, and the thickness of securing does not prove cultivation. The two layers are faces, and one and the same giving can be read two ways. On the side of the person, cultivation has a further reading as stance, coming from the parent's own law. The original speaks of institutions toward persons; it is carried here to the parent toward the child

A borrowed shape

The institutional side reads how an institution treats the people within it and divides two layers: the Base Layer, where people must first live and be safe, is to be secured; the Emergence Layer, where people grow into themselves, is to be supported. The Base Layer does not rise or fall with assets and is not exchanged for achievement; what the Emergence Layer gives is conditions, the content being decided by people themselves. The demands of the two layers are written apart; the two layers matter equally, and neither is traded for the other.5

Borrowed into what lies between a parent and a child, this shape reads very directly: He Zhou needs to eat, to sleep, to be kept warm, to be seen to when ill; this is the Base Layer. He Zhou needs to learn things, to try things, to have things of his own; this is the Emergence Layer. The original speaks of institutions toward persons; here it is one person toward another; what is borrowed is the shape, and the step of carrying it over is this series' own undertaking.6

Securing is not part of cultivation

The institutional side has a sentence: securing basic rights lies on the side of anti-colonization and is not part of cultivation. The subject of this sentence is securing basic rights: guarding the line between person and person.7 Carried to what lies between parents and child: guarding the child's basic rights is the face that blocks colonization, and is not on that account entered as cultivation; acts of care and provision are not, in themselves, renamed cultivation either.8

So He Chuan's taking temperatures, sponging and cooking porridge through those nights, however thorough the care, is not on that account cultivation. This sentence does not diminish those nights. Cultivation needs the two layers together: securing in the Base Layer, support in the Emergence Layer; it fails if either is missing; securing is one of the preconditions of cultivation, not a part of it. The thickness of securing does not prove cultivation.9

Faces, not two heaps

What the two layers divide is two faces of one and the same effect, not the things of life sorted into two heaps. One and the same giving can be read two ways.10 With He Zhou's fever broken, He Chuan sits beside the bed watching cartoons with him: this afternoon holds up a child just past an illness who needs someone beside him, and that is the face of the Base; He Zhou asks why the sea in the cartoon is green, and He Chuan puts down his laptop and looks it up with him, which gives conditions for his unfolding, and that is the face of Emergence. The two faces are read separately, without first having to decide which heap this afternoon belongs to.

On the side of the person

When the institutional side reads the two layers, it reads conditions on the institution's side: whether they were given, whether enough was given, whether one layer was traded for the other. Cultivation on the side of the institution and on the side of the person are read apart.11 What this series reads is the side of the person: which way one effect of the parent is headed, coming from the parent's own law. So in reading securing this paper does not ask only how much was given; it also reads whether, in giving, this end was headed toward treating the child as an end, or merely as a means (Paper 1).

§2 Where Securing Comes From

PC9 In the parent-child relation this series reads, the care and support a father or mother gives come from this end's own cannot-not: that the child needs someone to look after him is true; it enters the parent's conditions of occurrence and is avowed in his own law. This is this end's answering to its own law, not a duty owed to the child. The face of giving within it is read as entitlement, anchored in the giving the parent actually does; the child is the end this giving is directed at and does not hold it; the face of giving in securing and in support alike can be read as entitlement, not only in the Base Layer. The basic rights the child holds himself do not arise from this giving, and how his standing is protected belongs to the institutional side

That the child needs looking after is true

He Chuan says: "With him burning up like that, what else was I going to do?" What this sentence speaks of is not a debt. That He Zhou, burning up like that, needs someone to look after him is true; this true thing enters He Chuan's conditions of occurrence, and he avows it in his own law: in this matter, he cannot not look after him.12 This cannot-not is He Chuan's. It goes out from He Chuan's end; it is not something He Zhou claimed from him.

This is said only within the parent-child relation this series reads. When the true thing that the child needs looking after is avowed in this end's own law, it becomes this end's own cannot-not. What is read here is how this one taking-on happens; a relation's direction premise does not guarantee that every piece of care has been delivered. Nor does the series say that this care is bound to be there: the face that must be there, how the child's basic rights are protected and the legal duty of maintenance, belongs to the institutional side (§3); what is said here is where, for a father or mother within scope, it comes from.

Not a duty owed

Writing this care as the parents' duty to the child looks weightier: the child has a right to demand it, the parents a responsibility to give it. This series has no concept of a duty toward others; it has only one inward duty: the subject's answerability to its own law.13 He Chuan's care through those nights is his answering to his own law, not his owing He Zhou something.

Written this way, the care is not made lighter. Commitment binds a person by where I myself stand and by the effects already left, not by my owing the other party something;14 parents looking after a child are the same: what binds this end is this end's own cannot-not, and the effects that have already landed on the child. The legal duty of maintenance is another matter, and belongs to the institutional side: whether the parents' duty holds is a matter of right.15

What is set out here is how He Chuan takes on care in his own law; it does not on this basis decide whether He Zhou can demand care. The looking after already taken on, and the actual dependency already formed, are not cleared from the books by writing them in another concept; whether He Zhou's basic rights are crossed is read by the next section; taking it on and then withdrawing is read by §5.

The face of giving is read as entitlement

What He Chuan gave through those nights has a face of giving: time, effort, the wages docked for his leave, that pot of porridge. This face is read as entitlement: entitlement is anchored in the giving end, in the giving, not in the end that receives.16 He Zhou is the end this giving is directed at, and does not hold it; it is not that he has one more thing in his hands, but that there is someone giving to him.

The face of giving is not only in the Base Layer. Feeding and rearing are giving; giving conditions for his unfolding, looking up with him why that sea is green, is giving too.17 In securing and in support alike, every face of giving can be read as entitlement, checked by the structure of each piece of giving.

Basic rights do not arise from giving

One thing is not in this giving: the child's own basic rights. He Zhou's share in not being harmed, not being starved, not being shut away is not something He Chuan gave, nor does it arise from He Chuan's care through those nights; its standing lies in He Zhou himself, he holds it himself, and it has been there from the start.18 How this share is protected, and by what rules, belongs to the institutional side (§3).

§3 Standing and Violation

PC10 The child's basic rights have their standing in himself and are not conferred by the parents; how they are protected and by what rules belongs to the institutional side, and applies whichever direction the parents are headed in. On the occasion when a parent's effect crosses them, the relational side points out "this is a violation" and the boundary crossed; protection and disposition after the violation are handed over here, and what that effect left in the parent-child dealings is still read on the relational side

Standing lies in himself

The child's basic rights have their standing in himself. This sentence runs the same way as the first book in Paper 1: his standing does not wait for him to grow up, and nor do his basic rights. They are not conferred by the parents, and so the parents cannot revoke them either.

How they are protected belongs to the institutional side

Who protects a child's basic rights, how a crossing is judged, whether to intervene and how: these belong to the institutional side, which has institutional rules. These rules apply whichever direction the parents are headed in: parents headed in the 15DD direction and parents not headed in it meet one and the same set of rules.19

The series does not write this set of rules, nor does it evaluate them. What it reads is the side between person and person: within an institution, the part of what passes between persons that the institution does not provide for, or that goes beyond its provisions.20

Reading as far as "this is a violation"

Violation and the direction of colonization are two lines; violation is judged only at the layer of basic rights.2 On the occasion when a parent's effect crosses the child's basic rights, the relational side reads out "this is a violation," and which boundary was crossed. Protection and disposition after the violation are handed over here to the institutional side.

What is handed over is protection and disposition, not what that effect left in the dealings. After that occasion, what the child is like when he sees this father, what this father did afterward, what that occasion left in this relation: these are still read on the relational side, and entered on the books within this relation. Where parents within scope fall back to this step, it is likewise a violation; after turning back, it is still a violation (Paper 1).

§4 Not Made Conditional

PC11 Securing is not exchanged for the child's compliance, nor made conditional on his taking some particular road; how much support is given is not used to make up for a lack of securing; the two layers are not traded

Securing is not traded for compliance

The Base Layer is not exchanged for achievement.5 Carried to what lies between parents and child: eating, sleeping, being kept warm, being seen to when ill are not traded for the child's doing as he is told. If the child does badly in an exam, there is still dinner; if he talks back, he is still taken to be seen when he falls ill.

This proposition speaks of the face of securing. Parents can make demands of a child, can set rules, can decline to agree to something else when he has not done what was asked (Paper 4); this proposition does not govern those. It says only: the Base Layer is not put on the scales.

Not conditional on any one road

Securing in the Base Layer is not conditional on a person's taking some particular road.5 What the child learns and where he goes are matters of the Emergence Layer; whether or not he takes the road his parents want, securing stays as it was. This proposition bears most weight once the child has grown a direction of his own (Paper 5): the direction differs, and securing is not withdrawn.

The two layers are not traded

That the two layers are not traded runs two ways. One way is trading securing away: trading food and clothing for compliance, trading safety for his taking a certain road. The other is making up with support: having signed him up for many classes and bought him many books, one need not look to whether he sleeps well at night. Both ways trade one layer for the other.

One evening He Zhou was slow over dinner, eating and looking out the window, and Lin Wan said: "If you don't eat properly, you're not going out to play." On this occasion, the meal on the table was not withdrawn; going out was made conditional. The arrangement linking these two matters is read in Paper 4. Whether it also touches securing depends on what going out held up at the time. On its face, it links going out with eating, as though it pressed against "the two layers are not traded"; but the two layers are faces, and are not sorted first by the names "going out" and "eating": what going out held up at the time, what the restriction rested on, and what it actually took away, all still have to be looked at. That evening, He Zhou finished his dinner and went down to ride his bike for half an hour, and Lin Wan did not bring it up again. These facts are not enough to judge this example a trading of the two layers.

To see securing traded away takes a structural fact. A child does not take the course of study his parents designated, and the parents therefore withdraw the basic provision for his living they had given him before: this is an arrangement that hangs the Base Layer on a road. This series only registers this arrangement, and does not unfold its details.

§5 The Two Senses of Dependency

PC12 The child's dependency on care is not a dependency that was made, and carries no direction of colonization; the kind that was made and that has locked the path to saying no carries the direction of colonization; where one first monopolizes, or first induces dependency, and then withdraws, violation is checked separately at the layer of basic rights. Actual dependency alone, or factual irreplaceability, does not on its own yield the conclusion of violation

The child depends on care

People depend on each other in any case: a child on care, a patient on treatment, an apprentice on a master.21 He Zhou's depending on He Chuan through those nights of fever is not a dependency He Chuan made; He Zhou needed looking after in any case. It carries no direction of colonization.

The kind that was made

What is judged to carry the direction of colonization is that kind alone: it was made, and it locked the channel of saying no.21 Something a child could do himself, the parents keep doing for him until he cannot, and then use his inability to stop him trying for himself; or the parents cut off, one by one, the child's dealings with other grown-ups until only they are left, and then use "you can't do without me" to block his "no." In these two, the dependency was made, and the path to saying no has been locked too.

This proposition reads the effect, not the mind. Parents doing something for a child can be no more than being short of time that day; to read out the kind that was made, one has to see where the arrangement has actually taken that path.

First inducing dependency, then withdrawing

There is one more kind, checked separately at the layer of basic rights: first monopolizing a person's dependency, or first inducing it, and then withdrawing.22 All of a child's care is in one person's hands, and this person withdraws while the child still cannot do without him, leaving no other care: this is checked for violation at the layer of basic rights.

Dependency is not in itself violation

Actual dependency alone, or factual irreplaceability, does not on its own yield the conclusion of violation.23 That He Zhou wants only He Chuan to tell him stories, and no one else will do, is not a violation; that in the year He Chuan was raising him alone he was in fact irreplaceable is not a violation either. To read out a violation takes an effect that crosses basic rights.

§6 Signals Before 13DD

PC13 Before 13DD, the child's crying and resistance are first read as signals to be answered, not as unreasonable fussing, nor brushed aside with "he's still little": first check whether care, safety and the rest of securing are lacking, and also check whether it is a prediction that has fallen through, or a negation coming into being; crying alone does not certify which layer he is at, or his particular reason

First read as signals to be answered

Before He Zhou could talk, he often woke in the night. For a while, even after being fed and changed he kept crying; Lin Wan walked him around a few times and the crying stopped, and as soon as she put him down he cried again. Later He Chuan found that the night-light had been broken those days, and the room was darker than usual. Once the light was replaced, he still woke in the night, but would whimper a little and go back to sleep.

For a child who cannot yet talk, crying and resistance are what he sends out. The series first reads them as signals to be answered: not as unreasonable fussing, nor brushed aside with "he's still little." Reading them as signals speaks of the parents' end: there is a sound to be answered, and someone must go and find out what has happened.

Checking all three

In answering, three things are all checked.

First, whether securing is lacking: hungry, cold, ill, uncomfortable somewhere. This is the Base Layer, and falls under securing.

Second, whether a prediction has fallen through: a familiar person has gone, a familiar thing has disappeared, something expected has not happened. That the night-light was broken and the room darker than usual is one clue bearing on a prediction falling through: once the light was replaced, he whimpered and went back to sleep; this is a clue, and cannot yet certify the particular reason He Zhou cried those nights. Refusing a specific object is, in the upstream decomposition, a predictive conflict, not a need of the Base Layer.24

Third, whether it is a negation coming into being: a slightly older child pushes away the spoon held out to him and turns his head aside. Negation first targeting the other is another matter (Paper 4).

The three are checked apart, but a single bout of crying is not first assigned to one class. Through those nights He Chuan and Lin Wan checked on his care and also changed his surroundings; these are things they did, and the child's particular reason at his end is still seen only in clues.

Crying does not certify

Crying itself certifies neither which layer the child is at nor his particular reason. A child crying can be hunger, can be the light, can be something else, can be all three at once. From the parents' end, only clues are seen (Paper 1).

Why not classify first

Reading crying at this time uniformly as a need of the Base Layer would mix the three into one. Being hungry, a familiar thing gone, pushing the spoon away: these three descriptions cannot stand in for one another; giving back the toy, holding out the spoon again, cannot on the strength of the act alone be called answering what he asked for. The upstream decomposition already writes securing safety apart from allowing predictions to fall through and leaving room for negation. At the other extreme, once he is fed and warm, the crying that remains is treated as nothing.

Checking the three first, and not classifying first, blocks these two extremes. Here how the parents check and how they answer are read apart, and no prediction about the child's later direction is made from these acts.

§7 Shifting with the Phase Transitions

PC14 The content of the securing layer shifts with the child's phase transitions, and is not withdrawn with them; how securing is drawn back once 14DD has come to stand is read in Paper 7. After the parents part, each one's relation with the child remains, and is not settled by the exit between the two; the side of shared child-rearing between the two belongs to Marriage Theory

Shifting, not withdrawing

What the securing layer has to do changes as the child grows. Taking the kind of order Paper 1 spoke of as an example: before 13DD, care, safety and answering his signals are what stands out on the face of securing, while support is there at the same time (the surroundings the parents give, read in Paper 3); once 13DD has emerged, safety gains one more thing: room for his "no" not to be punished; later still, as matters of his own multiply, securing gains one more thing: if something happens to him out there, there is a place he can come back to.25 The order is only an example; what shifts is the content, and the securing layer is not withdrawn with the phase transitions.

Later He Zhou took the bus to school on his own, and He Chuan no longer took him every day; when he ran a fever at night, he knew to get the thermometer from the bedside drawer himself. These shapes of care changed; that someone sees to He Zhou when he is ill, and that there is a place to come back to if something happens, did not.

How it is drawn back

Once the child's cannot-not has come to stand in one matter after another, how the securing layer is drawn back, Paper 7 reads. Here only one thing is said: drawing back, too, is not conditional on which road he takes (§4).

After the parents part

The parents' parting is a matter between two grown-ups. The side of shared child-rearing between the two is read in Marriage Theory.26 What this series reads is each one's relation with the child: He Chuan and He Zhou one relation, Lin Wan and He Zhou another. When the two part, these two relations remain, and are not settled by the exit between the two.27 Departure can happen; departure does not by itself settle that relation.28

§8 Interfacing with the Tradition

The Analects: nothing to worry about but illness. In the Analects, "Wei Zheng," Meng Wubo asks about filial conduct, and the Master answers: "Give your parents nothing to worry about but your illness." SAE's reading of the Analects has already annotated this chapter; it is pointed back to here, and not read again.29 The series and that chapter shed light on each other at one point only: a child's illness is something that falls at the parents' end and cannot be set down; He Chuan's "what else was I going to do" falls at the parents' end together with this sentence. Where they part: in its original place the chapter answers a question about filial conduct, and speaks from the child's end; this series does not read filial conduct, and reads only how the parents' end acts.

Locke: the parents' care. In Chapter 6 of the Second Treatise of Government, Locke says that parents take care of, nourish and educate their children out of the duty laid on them by nature, while the children cannot yet take care of themselves.30 The common question: where this care comes from. Where they shed light on each other: the care falls on the stretch in which the child cannot yet do things, and changes as he grows. Where they part: Locke writes it as a duty to the child; this series reads it as the parents' end's own cannot-not, and the legal duty of maintenance belongs to the institutional side.

Hegel: maintained from the family's common property. In the part of the Philosophy of Right on the family (§174), Hegel says that children have a right to be maintained and educated from the common property of the family.31 The common question: on what basis the child receives what he receives from the family. Where they shed light on each other: maintenance is not conditional on the child's first doing something. Where they part: Hegel writes it as a right of the child; this series leaves how the child's standing is protected, and the rules governing that protection, to the institutional side, and on the relational side reads the giving the parents' end does, as entitlement, which the child does not hold.

§9 Drawing Together, and Downstream

This paper has established seven propositions. Taken together, they say: the shape of the two layers is borrowed from the institutional side and carried to what lies between the parent and the child; the Base Layer is to be secured, the Emergence Layer supported; the two layers are written apart, matter equally, and are not traded; protecting the child's basic rights is not part of cultivation, acts of care are not renamed cultivation, and cultivation needs the two layers together; the two layers are faces, and one and the same giving can be read two ways; on the side of the person, cultivation has a further reading as stance. Securing comes from the parent's end's own cannot-not, is this end's answering to its own law, and is not a duty owed; the face of giving is read as entitlement, anchored in the giving, and the child does not hold it; the child's basic rights do not arise from the giving. How standing is protected belongs to the institutional side; the relational side reads as far as "this is a violation" and the boundary crossed; protection and disposition after the violation are handed over, and what that effect left is still read by this relation. Securing is not traded for compliance, nor hung on a road. Dependency has two senses: the child's dependency on care carries no direction of colonization, while the kind that was made and has locked the path to saying no does; first inducing dependency and then withdrawing is checked separately for violation; dependency is not in itself violation. Crying and resistance before 13DD are first read as signals to be answered; the three are all checked, and nothing is classified first. Securing shifts with the phase transitions, and is not withdrawn; when the parents part, each one's relation with the child remains.

This paper does not prove that parents owe the child some duty, nor that parents who give more care are better, nor that all dependency is harmless.

A question left open. Whether the crying and resistance of a child who cannot yet talk is a signal of need, a signal of a prediction falling through, or a boundary signal that cannot be overwritten: this paper has said only that the three are checked first and nothing is classified first; it has not said which of them cannot be overwritten, or in what sense.

What the next paper reads. He Zhou got better and began drawing a great deal; Lin Wan found him a teacher, and He Chuan said drawing won't put food on the table: what is it that this layer gives?

Downstream. Support in the Emergence Layer: teaching, nurturing and leading, the direct-write window, "for your own good," and support not being traded and not being used to possess, Paper 3 reads. The item in securing of "room for his 'no'," Paper 4 reads. How securing is drawn back, Paper 7 reads. Who among several caregivers looks after the child, and by way of whom, Paper 6 reads.

摘要

这一篇读这个系列所读的亲子关系里的基础层：父或母给孩子的保障。它借制度侧已经写定的两层的形状来读父或母与孩子之间：基础层要保障，涌现层要支持；两层分开写，同等重要，一层不拿去换另一层。保护孩子的基础权利在反殖民一边，不是涵育的一部分；照料与供给的施为本身不改名为涵育，涵育要两层加起来，保障之厚不证明涵育。两层是分面，不是两堆事：同一笔给付可以两读。原处说的是制度对人，推到父或母对孩子，由这个系列自担。

父或母给出的照料与支持，出自这一端自己的不得不：孩子要人照顾，这是真的；它进入父或母的发生条件，在他自己的法里被认下。这是这一端对自己的法负责，不是对孩子欠下的一项义务。其中的给予面照权益读，锚在父或母实际给出的付出上，孩子是这份付出的指向端，不持有它；孩子自持的基础权利不由这份付出产生。孩子的基础权利怎样护、规则怎样定，归制度侧；父或母的作用越过它的那一次，关系侧指出「这是侵」及所越的边界，侵后的保护与处置在此交接，那次作用在来往里留下了什么，仍照这一段读。保障不以孩子的顺从为交换，不以他走某一条特定的路为条件；两层不互换。

孩子依赖照顾，这一种依赖不是被造出来的，不带殖民的方向；被造出来、又锁住了说不之路的那一种，带殖民的方向；先致其依赖、再撤出的，在基础权利一层另核侵。13DD 之前，孩子的哭闹抗拒先读作要回应的信号：先核保障有没有缺，也核是不是预测落空、是不是正在出现的否定，不凭哭闹本身认证他在哪一层。保障一层的内容随孩子的相变挪移，不随相变撤掉；父母分开以后，各人与孩子的那一段照旧在。这一篇与《论语》、洛克、黑格尔对接。它不证明父母对孩子负有某项义务，不证明照料得多的父母更好，也不证明一切依赖都无害。

何舟发烧的那几天，林晚在外地出差，何川请了假在家。头一夜烧到很高，何川每隔两个小时起来量一次体温，给他擦身、喂水；天亮以后带他去医院，排队、抽血、取药，回家熬粥。第三天烧退了，何舟坐在床上要看动画片，何川坐在旁边，回了几封压了两天的邮件。

林晚打电话回来，问何川累不累。何川说：「累是累，可他烧成那样，我还能干别的？」

这件事平常不用说什么：孩子病了，父亲照看。可何川这一句「我还能干别的」，说的是什么？这几夜的照料，是他欠何舟的吗？若是欠的，何舟能不能向他讨？若不是欠的，这份照料怎样被承担？照料没有兑现时，已经留下的作用又怎样入账？何川要是那几天没有请假，把何舟丢在家里，这一件又怎样读，由谁读？

第一节 两层

借来的形状

制度侧读制度怎样对待它里面的人，分出两层：基础层，人先要活着、要安全，这一层要保障；涌现层，人要长成他自己，这一层要支持。基础层不随资产增减，不以成就交换；涌现层给的是条件，内容由人自己定。两层的要求分开写，两层同等重要，一层不拿去换另一层。5

这一形状借到父或母与孩子之间，读得很直接：何舟要吃、要睡、要穿暖、病了要看，这是基础层；何舟要学东西、要试、要有自己的事，这是涌现层。原处说的是制度对人，这里说的是一个人对另一个人；借的是形状，推及的那一步由这个系列自担。6

保障不是涵育的一部分

制度侧有一句：保障基础权利在反殖民一边，不是涵育的一部分。这一句的主词是保障基础权利：守住人与人之间的那条线。7 推到父母与孩子之间：护住孩子的基础权利，是挡住殖民的那一面，不因此记作涵育；照料与供给的施为本身，也不改名为涵育。8

所以，何川那几夜量体温、擦身、熬粥，照料得周全，不因此就是涵育。这一句不贬低那几夜。涵育要两层加起来：基础层有保障，涌现层有支持，缺一样都不行；保障是涵育的前提之一，不是涵育的一部分。保障之厚，不证明涵育。9

分面，不是两堆事

两层分的是同一次作用里的两面，不是把生活里的事分成两堆。同一笔给付可以两读。10 何舟烧退了，何川坐在床边陪他看动画片：这一下午，托住的是一个病刚好的孩子要人在旁边，这是基础那一面；何舟问动画片里的海为什么是绿的，何川放下电脑跟他一起查，这是为他展开给条件，是涌现那一面。两面分别读，不必先判这一下午属于哪一堆。

在人这一侧

制度侧读两层，读的是制度那一侧的条件：给没给，给得够不够，有没有拿一层去换另一层。涵育在制度一侧与在人一侧，分开读。11 这个系列读的是人这一侧：父或母那一次作用朝哪里去，出自父或母自己的法。所以这一篇读保障，不只问给了多少，也读这一端给的时候，是朝把孩子当作目的那一面去，还是只当作手段（第一篇）。

第二节 保障从哪里来

孩子要人照顾，这是真的

何川说：「他烧成那样，我还能干别的？」这一句说的不是一笔欠账。何舟烧成那样，要人照看，这是真的；这件真事进入何川的发生条件，他在自己的法里把它认下：在这一件上，他不得不去照看。12 这一份不得不是何川的。它从何川这一端发出，不是何舟向他讨来的。

这一句只在这个系列读的亲子关系里说。孩子要人照顾这件真事，在这一端自己的法里被认下时，成为这一端自己的不得不。这里读的是这一次承担怎样发生，不由一段的方向前提保证每一次照料都已兑现。这个系列也不说这份照料一定会有：一定要有的那一面，孩子的基础权利怎样护、法律上的抚养义务，归制度侧（第三节）；这里说的是，在射程里的父或母那里，它从哪里来。

不是欠下的义务

把这份照料写成父母对孩子的义务，看上去更有分量：孩子有权要，父母有责任给。这个系列没有对他者的义务这个概念；它只有一条朝内的义务：主体对自己的法负责。13 何川那几夜的照料，是他对自己的法负责，不是他欠何舟一笔。

这样写，不使照料轻了。承诺约束人，靠的是我自己的站与已经留下的作用，不是我欠对方一笔；14 父母照看孩子也是这样：约束这一端的，是这一端自己的不得不，与已经落在孩子身上的那些作用。法律上的抚养义务，是另一件，归制度侧：父母义务成不成立，是法权的事。15

这里说明何川怎样在自己的法里承担照料，不据此判定何舟能不能要求照料。已经承担的照看与已经形成的实际依赖，不因换一种概念写法就消账；是否越过何舟的基础权利，照下一节读；已经承担又撤出，照第五节读。

给予面照权益读

何川那几夜给出的，有一面是付出：时间、力气、请假扣掉的工资、熬的那锅粥。这一面照权益读：权益锚在给予的那一端，锚在付出上，不锚在被给予的那一端。16 何舟是这份付出所指向的那一端，不持有它；不是他手里多了一样东西，是有一个人在对他付出。

给予面不只在基础层。喂养、护育是付出，为他展开给条件、陪他查那片海为什么是绿的，也是付出。17 保障与支持里，凡是给予的那一面，都可以照权益读，按每一次付出的结构核。

基础权利不由付出产生

还有一样，不在这份付出里：孩子自己的基础权利。何舟不被伤害、不被饿着、不被关起来的那一份，不是何川给的，也不由何川那几夜的照料产生；它的份位在何舟本人，他自持着，从一开始就在。18 这一份怎样护、规则怎样定，归制度侧（第三节）。

第三节 份位与侵

PC10 孩子的基础权利，份位在他本人，不由父母授予；它怎样护、规则怎样定，归制度侧，不论父母朝哪个方向都适用。父或母的作用越过它的那一次，关系侧指出「这是侵」及所越的边界；侵后的保护与处置在此交接，那次作用在亲子来往里留下了什么，仍照关系侧读

份位在他本人

孩子的基础权利，份位在他本人。这一句与第一篇的第一本账同向：他的地位不等他长成，他的基础权利也不等。不由父母授予，所以父母也撤销不了。

怎样护，归制度侧

一个孩子的基础权利谁来护、越过了怎样判、要不要介入、怎样介入：这些归制度侧，有制度的规则。这些规则不论父母朝哪个方向都适用：在 15DD 方向上的父母，与不在这个方向上的父母，碰到的是同一套规则。19

这个系列不写这一套规则，也不评它。它读的是人与人之间的那一面：一个制度里人与人之间，制度没有规定或超出规定的那一部分。20

读到「这是侵」

侵与殖民的方向是两条线；侵只在基础权利一层判。2 父或母的作用越过孩子基础权利的那一次，关系侧读出「这是侵」，以及所越的是哪一条边界。侵后的保护与处置，在这里交接给制度侧。

交接的是保护与处置，不是那一次作用在来往里留下的东西。那一次之后，孩子见到这个父亲时是什么样子，这个父亲后来做了什么，那一次在这段关系里留下了什么：这些仍照关系侧读，在这一段里记账。射程里的父母回落到这一步，同样是侵；回头以后，仍是侵（第一篇）。

第四节 不挂条件

PC11 保障不以孩子的顺从为交换，不以他走某一条特定的路为条件；支持的多少，不拿来抵保障的缺；两层不互换

不拿保障换顺从

基础层不以成就交换。5 推到父母与孩子之间：吃、睡、穿暖、病了有人看，不拿来换孩子听话。孩子没有考好，饭照样有；孩子顶了嘴，病了照样有人带去看。

这一条说的是保障这一面。父母可以对孩子有要求，可以定规矩，可以在孩子没有做到的时候不答应他别的事（第四篇）；这一条不管那些。它只说：基础层不放到天平上。

不挂在哪一条路上

基础层的保障，不以人走某一条特定的路为条件。5 孩子要学什么、往哪里去，是涌现层的事；他走不走父母想要的那一条，保障照旧。这一条在孩子长到有自己的方向以后最吃重（第五篇）：方向不同，保障不收。

两层不互换

两层不互换，有两个方向。一个方向，是拿保障去换：拿吃穿去换顺从，拿安全去换他走某一条路。另一个方向，是拿支持去抵：给他报了很多班、买了很多书，就不用管他夜里睡不睡得好。两个方向都是一层拿去换另一层。

一天晚上，何舟吃饭吃得慢，一边吃一边看窗外，林晚说：「不好好吃饭，就别想出去玩。」这一次没有撤掉桌上的饭，出门被设了条件。它是一项把两件事连起来的安排，安排这一面照第四篇读；有没有同时触及保障，仍须看出门当时承担什么。字面上，它把出门与吃饭连在一起，像是贴着「两层不互换」；可两层是分面，不按「出门」「吃饭」的名称先分：出门在当时承担什么，限制的根据是什么，实际收掉了什么，都还要看。那一晚，何舟把饭吃完，出去在楼下骑了半小时车，林晚没有再提。这些事实，不足以把这一例判成两层互换。

要看保障被拿去换，得是结构上的事实。一个孩子没有走父母指定的学习方向，父母因此撤掉了原先给他的基本生活供给：这是一项把基础层挂在一条路上的安排。这个系列只登记这一安排，不展开它的细节。

第五节 依赖的两义

PC12 孩子依赖照顾，这一种依赖不是被造出来的，不带殖民这个方向；被造出来、又锁住了说不之路的那一种，带殖民这个方向；先独占或先致其依赖、再撤出的，在基础权利一层另核侵。仅有实际依赖，或事实上不可替代，不独自取得侵这一结论

孩子依赖照顾

人本来就互相依赖，孩子依赖照顾，病人依赖医治，学徒依赖师傅。21 何舟发烧那几夜依赖何川，这一种依赖不是何川造出来的；何舟本来就要人照看。它不带殖民的方向。

被造出来的那一种

被判为带殖民方向的，只有那一种：它是被造出来的，而且锁住了说不的通道。21 一个孩子本来可以自己做的事，父母一直替他做，做到他不会，再拿他的不会去拦他自己试；或者，父母把孩子与别的大人的来往一一断开，只剩自己一个，再拿「你离了我不行」去挡他的「不」。这两样，依赖是被造出来的，说不的路也被锁住了。

这一条读的是作用，不读心。父母替孩子做一件事，可以只是那一天赶时间；读出被造出来的那一种，要看安排在实际上把那条路弄到了哪里。

先致其依赖，再撤出

还有一种，在基础权利一层另核：先独占一个人的依赖，或先致其依赖，再撤出。22 一个孩子的全部照料都在一个人手里，这个人又在孩子还离不开的时候撤走，不留别的照料：这一样照基础权利一层核侵。

依赖本身不是侵

仅有实际依赖，或事实上不可替代，不独自取得侵这一结论。23 何舟只要何川给他讲故事，换了谁都不行，这一件不是侵；何川一个人带他的那一年，他事实上不可替代，这也不是侵。读出侵，要有越过基础权利的那一次作用。

第六节 13DD 之前的信号

PC13 13DD 之前，孩子的哭闹抗拒，先读作要回应的信号，不读作无理取闹，也不凭「他还小」抹去：先核照料、安全等保障有没有缺，也核是不是预测落空、是不是正在出现的否定；不凭哭闹本身认证他在哪一层或具体的理由

先读作要回应的信号

何舟还不会说话的时候，夜里常醒。有一阵，喂过奶、换过尿布，他还是哭；林晚抱着走了几圈，哭声停了，一放下又哭。后来何川发现，那几天夜灯坏了，屋里比平常黑。换了灯，他夜里还是醒，醒了哼两声，又睡了。

一个还不会说话的孩子，哭闹与抗拒是他发出来的。这个系列先把它读作要回应的信号：不读作无理取闹，也不凭「他还小」抹去。读作信号，说的是父母那一端：有一个声音要回应，要去查发生了什么。

三样都核

回应的时候，有三样都核。

一是，保障有没有缺：饿了，冷了，病了，哪里不舒服。这是基础层，落在保障。

二是，是不是预测落空：熟悉的人走开了，熟悉的东西不见了，期待的事没有发生。夜灯坏了、屋里比平常黑，是有关预测落空的一条线索：换了灯，他醒了哼两声又睡了；这是线索，还不能认证何舟那几夜哭的具体理由。拒绝一个具体的对象，在上游的拆分里，是预测的冲突，不是基础层的需要。24

三是，是不是正在出现的否定：稍大一点的孩子推开递过来的勺子，扭过头去。否定第一次指向他人，是另一件事（第四篇）。

三样分开核，但不凭一次哭闹先把它归定一类。夜里那一阵，何川与林晚查看了照料，也改了环境；这些是他们做过的事，孩子那一端的具体理由仍只见线索。

不凭哭闹认证

哭闹本身，不认证孩子在哪一层，也不认证他的具体理由。一个孩子哭，可以是饿，可以是灯，可以是别的，也可以三样同时。从父母这一端，只见线索（第一篇）。

为什么不先分类

把这时候的哭闹一律读作基础层的需要，会把三样混成一样。饿了、熟悉的东西不见了、把勺子推开，这三种描述不能互相替代；把玩具还回去、把勺子再递过去，也不能单凭动作本身叫作回应了他的要求。上游的拆分本来就把保障安全与允许预测落空、给否定留余地分开写。另一头，是吃饱穿暖以后，剩下的哭就当作没事。

先核三样，不先分类，挡的是这两头。这里分开读父母怎样查、怎样回应，不从这些动作预测孩子此后的方向。

第七节 随相变挪移

PC14 保障一层的内容随孩子的相变挪移，不随相变撤掉；14DD 站住以后保障怎样收，在第七篇读。父母分开以后，各人与孩子的那一段照旧在，不因两人之间的退出结清；两人之间共同养育的那一面归婚姻论

挪移，不撤

保障一层要做的，随孩子长而变。照第一篇说的那一种次序作示例：13DD 之前，照料、安全与信号回应是保障这一面突出的内容，支持同时在（父母给出的环境，第三篇读）；13DD 涌现以后，安全里多了一样：给他的「不」留出不被罚的余地；再往后，他自己的事一件一件多起来，保障里多了一样：他在外面出了事，有一个地方可以回来。25 次序只是示例；挪移的是内容，保障这一层不随相变撤掉。

何舟后来自己坐车去上学，何川不再每天送；他夜里发烧，自己知道去床头柜拿体温计。这一些照料的样子变了，何舟病了有人管、出了事有地方回，这一层没有变。

怎样收

孩子的不得不在一件件事上站住以后，保障这一层怎样收，第七篇读。这里只说一句：收，也不挂在他走哪一条路上（第四节）。

父母分开以后

父母分开，是两个大人之间的事。两人之间共同养育的那一面，在婚姻论里读。26 这个系列读的，是各人与孩子的那一段：何川与何舟一段，林晚与何舟一段。两人分开，这两段照旧在，不因两人之间的退出结清。27 离开可以发生，离开本身不使那一段结清。28

第八节 与传统对接

《论语》：父母唯其疾之忧。 《论语·为政》记孟武伯问孝，孔子答：「父母唯其疾之忧。」这一章，SAE 的《论语》解读已经注过，这里回指，不另读。29 这个系列只在一处相照：孩子病了，是落在父母那一端、放不下的事；何川那句「我还能干别的」，与这一句同落在父母那一端。分开的一处：这一章在原处是答孝，从孩子那一端说；这个系列不读孝，只读父母那一端怎样作用。

洛克：父母的照管。 洛克在《政府论》下篇第六章说，父母照管、抚养、教育孩子，出于自然加给他们的责任，在孩子还不能照管自己的时候。30 共同的问题：这份照管从哪里来。相照的一处：照管落在孩子还做不到的那一段，随他长成而变。分开的一处：洛克把它写成对孩子的义务；这个系列读作父母那一端自己的不得不，法律上的抚养义务归制度侧。

黑格尔：由家庭的共同财产抚养。 黑格尔在《法哲学原理》论家庭的部分（§174）说，孩子有权由家庭的共同财产来抚养和教育。31 共同的问题：孩子从家里得到的，凭的是什么。相照的一处：抚养不以孩子先做到什么为条件。分开的一处：黑格尔把它写成孩子的一项权利；这个系列把孩子份位的保护方式与规则交制度侧，关系侧读父母那一端给出的付出，照权益读，孩子不持有它。

第九节 收束与下游

这一篇立了七条。合起来说的是：两层的形状借自制度侧，推到父或母与孩子之间；基础层要保障，涌现层要支持，两层分开写、同等重要、不互换；保护孩子的基础权利不是涵育的一部分，照料的施为不改名为涵育，涵育要两层加起来；两层是分面，同一笔给付可以两读；在人这一侧，涵育另有姿态一读。保障出自父或母这一端自己的不得不，是这一端对自己的法负责，不是欠下的义务；给予面照权益读，锚在付出上，孩子不持有它；孩子的基础权利不由付出产生。份位怎样护归制度侧；关系侧读到「这是侵」及所越的边界，侵后的保护与处置交接，那次作用留下的仍照这一段读。保障不换顺从，不挂在一条路上。依赖分两义：孩子依赖照顾不带殖民方向，被造出来又锁住说不之路的那一种带；先致其依赖再撤出的另核侵；依赖本身不是侵。13DD 之前的哭闹抗拒先读作要回应的信号，三样都核，不先分类。保障随相变挪移，不撤；父母分开，各人与孩子的那一段照旧在。

这一篇不证明父母对孩子负有某项义务，不证明照料得多的父母更好，也不证明一切依赖都无害。

留下的问题。 一个还不会说话的孩子的哭闹抗拒，是需要的信号、预测落空的信号，还是一道不可覆写的边界信号：这一篇只说了先核三样、不先分类，没有说其中哪一种在什么意义上不可覆写。

下一篇读的。 何舟的病好了，开始常常画画，林晚给他找了老师，何川说画画不能当饭吃：这一层给的东西，又是什么？

下游。 涌现层的支持：教、涵育、引领，直写窗口，「为你好」，支持不拿来换、不拿来占有，第三篇读。保障里「给他的『不』留出余地」那一样，第四篇读。保障怎样收，第七篇读。几个照料者之间谁照看、经过谁，第六篇读。
