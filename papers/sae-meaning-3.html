The Half That Is Not Mine to Settle
不在我这里结账的那一半

Abstract

The first paper of this series established the value history and the taken-as history of one and the same bearer as a cross-temporal, first-person relation of carrying-forward; the second wrote the inward side of that relation in a finite bearer. This paper examines the outward side. Its object is conditional: for a bearer that has avowed, in the first person, the content the other as an end, this paper writes the outward form of the relation. This is not the other half that every meaning relation must have; it is the form taken once the outlet that structure never excluded has actually been obtained.

The paper first supplies a position that upstream left unnamed: recognition is a single first-person event of uptake, cultivation is that content in motion, and between two enactments a person still faces that way — this orientation, capable of lasting across time, is looking-toward. That there is no act of cultivation at this moment is not by itself enough to conclude that looking-toward has ceased; whether it still obtains is judged by a description of the cross-temporal relation, not replaced by a single action or by the standing of a content. Next come the three forms: formal orientation, anticipatory enactment and factual filling can coexist and do not form a fixed sequence of life-stages valid for all bearers; arrivals and departures on the side of the world change the facts and the conditions of action directly, while how the bearer takes them up remains its own first-person response; what anticipatory enactment leaves behind is conditions, not an orientation deposited elsewhere. The third section writes that support is not substitute constitution: the constitutive positions stand structurally and cannot be taken from outside; this paper sets three negative boundaries on the concept of support and issues no new ought; that the other is able to unfold without revolving around me is the hardest test in this row, and the test has two layers — the qualification layer says the other's constitutive position is neither conferred nor withdrawn by me, the relation layer asks whether the relation treats the other's refusal as a nullity of constitution; whatever another has obtained from me, once avowed in his own law, is his, and does not become a constitution I completed on his behalf. The fourth section writes that crossing is not merging: the life-orientations of several bearers can cross and point to one another while each relation remains anchored in its own bearer; whether a collective first person can stand is not prejudged by this series; crossing is not resonance.

This paper does not re-establish the content the other as an end, gives no procedure for determining whether looking-toward is present, adds no readings, and issues no norms.

Introduction

The first paper of this series, What Kind of Relation Meaning Is (hereafter the relation paper), established the relation; the second, The Finite Bearer's Carrying-Forward (hereafter the paper on finitude), wrote its inward side in a finite bearer. At its close the paper on finitude handed the outward side to this paper: the outward outlet of the relation is not structurally sealed; only when the corresponding other-directed contents have been avowed by the bearer in the first person, and actually take part, in cross-temporal carrying-forward, in organizing future possibility and the field of reasons, is that outward form discussed; avowal is its necessary condition, not its presence (Meaning Theory P2 §9). This paper begins from that sentence.

Its object is therefore conditional, and the quantifier of the whole paper is fixed accordingly:

For a bearer that has avowed, in the first person, the other-directed content in question, this paper writes the outward form of this relation.

This is not the other half that every meaning relation must have. Upstream, in establishing that content, wrote that the other can have values of his own and that this is not mine to give, and that this is a fact that holds prior to me; whether I avow it is a first-person occurrence on my side; so it is not something every bearer has by nature, and someone who has not avowed it does not thereby make the fact untrue (Value Theory P6 V73). The half in the title is the other half of the series' map, not a half that every meaning relation already actually has. This paper follows the current published text of that proposition and does not adopt any other reading in advance.

Three inputs that this paper does not itself prove are used here. First, that it is one and the same bearer — the relation paper's domain input. Second, the avowed other-directed content itself: Value Theory established it and hands it down; this paper does not re-establish it, and writes only its form on this relation (Value Theory P6 V73). Third, what the three forms are — formal orientation, anticipatory enactment, factual filling — each of which is given by Value Theory (Value Theory P1 V14, the discussion of the three forms); what this paper gives is the way they coexist and rewrite one another across a life.

The man who turned up his old notebook is still there. He once taught a student, and the student later took a road he had not foreseen and did not approve. He has a friend who repaired a window and called him over to look. There is one person he will never see again. Each of these people holds a position among the things under his name; how those positions enter his cross-temporal carrying-forward is what this paper writes.

The text follows the series disciplines: no external theorists are named, no historical events are invoked; technical terms are defined at first use; every call on an upstream proposition carries its paper and number. This series adds no readings, converts nothing into higher and lower, and issues no norms; wherever ought arises, it is routed to the Moral Law, Rights Theory, and Action Theory. Every description this series gives of carrying-forward, identification, orientation, growth and stance says only how something that has already occurred is registered or corrected; it does not require the subject to set it going, and it does not register not setting it going as failure or stagnation. What this paper says of looking-toward and of support falls under the same rule.

§1 Looking-Toward

Y22 Looking-toward is the outward direction of this relation; cultivation is that direction in motion

Upstream has two positions. Recognition is that stroke, a single first-person event of uptake: when a concrete other appears in the domain of bearers, the fact that this other too is an end already holds before my recognition; recognition falls on a fact already so, and what it does is to acknowledge this fact and take it up, not to make it (Value Theory P1 V14, third layer). Cultivation is a verb-position, the dynamic showing-forth, on the transformation-axis, of that content of recognition — the content in motion (Value Theory P1 V16).

The orientation that lasts between them has no name. A person between two enactments still faces that way: for years he has done nothing for that friend, and the friend still holds a place in his arrangements; when he thinks of next year, there is a person there. Without this position only two ways of writing remain, and both are wrong. One writes looking-toward as the sum of enactments: no action, no looking-toward. That compresses the direction of a relation into the frequency of actions, whereas a person may do nothing for many years while that person remains in his arrangements for next year. The other writes looking-toward as the shadow of a content's standing: as long as the content is still avowed, looking-toward obtains. That confuses a content long dormant and merely recallable with a direction that is still organizing the future, and in a person's life those are two different shapes. The position this proposition supplies lies between the two. It is this series' own product, not a borrowing: upstream established the other as an end as a content, and this series reads it, at the scale of a life, as a direction; the relation paper has already reserved the name for it (Meaning Theory P1 Y12).

Looking-toward is the outward direction of this relation. It is not sustained psychological attention, nor enactment at every moment; it is the avowed content the other as an end, which in cross-temporal carrying-forward not only remains recallable but still takes part, as an effective reference, in organizing future possibility and the field of reasons. The paper on finitude wrote this shape at its close, and this paper takes it over as it stands. The layer taking part as an effective reference in organizing the field of reasons is added because recallability alone cannot separate two things. That the friend's affairs are still avowed but merely recallable is one shape: he thinks of him now and then, and when he does, nothing moves with it; on next year's timetable there is no stretch kept for that person. That they still change next year's arrangements is another: he counts out next year's time, and that window and that person take up a stretch of it, other things shift around it, and before he answers a letter he first thinks how that person will read it. In both shapes the content is under his name; only the second is looking-toward.

Two things are kept apart.

First, cultivation is not a fifth value-source; there are only four rows of value-source (Value Theory P1 V16). The value-content on the source-row corresponding to the fifteenth structural level is that the other can have values of his own and that this is not mine to give (Value Theory P6 V73); looking-toward is its form at the scale of a life, cultivation is that content in motion. The noun-position and the verb-position are not merged.

Second, looking-toward and enactment are kept apart. The standing of a content and life-orientation are not the same subject: that a content is still avowed entails only that a single non-enactment may not be taken to show that the content has exited; it does not entail that this life-relation still actually presents an outward direction. The relation paper has already set the epistemic limitation, and this paper applies it as it stands:

That there is no act of cultivation at this moment is not by itself enough to conclude that looking-toward has ceased; and whether looking-toward still obtains is judged by a description of the cross-temporal relation, not replaced by a single action or by the standing of a content.

The outward side presents an outward direction only when the content the other as an end actually takes part in organizing the field of reasons; that the relation remains, that the content is still avowed, or that a single action is absent — none of these can stand in for that judgment on its own (Meaning Theory P1 Y12). So three things each keep their place. The standing of a content is not refuted by a single action; looking-toward is not automatically guaranteed by the standing of a content; and the relation paper's account of description remains in force — whether looking-toward still obtains can only be described fallibly, the third-person standpoint has no conclusive procedure, and the first person is granted no finality in advance (Meaning Theory P1 Y8, Y9).

This proposition sits on the same orientation axis as the inward side; they are two contents of one axis, not two pillars. The inward side asks about the relation of carrying-forward, the outward side asks whom a content is oriented toward, and the two sides may overlap in one and the same case without merging into one (Meaning Theory P1 Y12). Looking-toward may for a time produce no action. What is given here is only the minimal description at the level of the relation, not an external criterion that is sufficient and necessary.

Nor is looking-toward an ought. A bearer that has avowed that content may pass a stretch of days without looking outward; that is a rise and fall of running, which Value Theory has already cut apart from active shutting (Value Theory P1 V11, third case); this paper registers only whether looking-toward obtains, not whether it ought to.

§2 The Three Forms

Y23 The three forms can coexist and rewrite one another, and have no fixed order

Upstream gives what each of the three forms is. Formal orientation holds the formal other-position open for any end-bearer that might appear in future; anticipatory enactment is outward-turned action toward a possible future bearer — writing a book, spreading a thought, leaving a work or an arrangement; factual filling is a concrete other actually existing in the domain of bearers filling the fact-position (Value Theory P1 V14, the discussion of the three forms). What this paper gives is the way they coexist and rewrite one another across a life.

The three can coexist, and do not form a fixed sequence of life-stages valid for all bearers. While a real other is present, formal orientation can still be kept; anticipatory enactment can also occur while a real other is present; a person can at one and the same time keep a place for a friend, write a book for future readers, and hold the position open for anyone who has not yet appeared. This does not exclude that some life happens to unfold in that order, first formal, then anticipatory, then factual; and it does not withdraw the structural relation upstream established, that anticipatory enactment is led by the formal other-position (Value Theory P1 V14, the discussion of the three forms). No fixed order in a life does not mean no structural dependence.

Nor do the three lie on one level, and this paper does not recast them as three mutually exclusive states. Formal orientation is a position kept open, with its orientation; anticipatory enactment is a class of actions; factual filling is a real other filling the fact-position. Their combination across a life can be discussed, and action, fact and first-person response do not thereby become one kind of event.

Formal orientation: that position is an empty place the bearer can keep. It does not stand for every bearer: Value Theory's modal reserve refuses to assert that the remainder necessarily fires for every running at the fourteenth level (Value Theory P1 V14, first layer). Once the position has been obtained by the bearer's own first-person structure, factual filling is not a condition of its holding. Empty and lacking are two different things: it stands empty, and standing empty, the solitary subject is still self-sufficient (Value Theory P1 V14, second layer).

Anticipatory enactment: what is avowed is a conditional content — that any bearer who might appear in future can have values of his own, and that this is not mine to give. This content is real: Value Theory writes that once avowed in the first person it takes value-standing alongside the other avowed contents under this person's name (Value Theory P6 V73). It is led by the formal other-position and is still an anticipatory orientation; it does not make a not-yet-existing bearer a present fact (Value Theory P1 V14, the discussion of the three forms).

Factual filling: only a concrete other actually existing in the domain of bearers fills the fact-position. And the appearance of a concrete other only brings that position to bear on one person; whether the bearer avows this fact as its own content still requires a further first-person response. Upstream keeps the two steps clearly apart: the first is the fact, that this concrete other too is an end, which holds before I recognize it; the second is the response, to recognize and take up this fact from the first-person position (Value Theory P6 V73; P1 V14, third layer).

Change on the side of the world, and the bearer's taking it up, are two layers. Whether a real other exists, whether the original opportunity still stands, are not decided by the bearer's response; how changed conditions limit the fulfillment of existing arrangements is not created by the bearer's avowal either. Change in the world can directly alter factual filling and the conditions of action, and it does not thereby complete the bearer's first-person uptake of that change. What this paper calls rewriting refers only to the third layer: how the bearer responds to these changes in current carrying-forward, re-identification or new constitution. That response need not always be a new avowal; existing carrying-forward can go on organizing the future, and not every change of conditions need add an entry (Meaning Theory P1 Y12). To write the comings and goings on the side of the world directly as a change in the first-person form of the relation is to take the world's change as proof that the bearer has already avowed or revised.

A concrete other entering the field of the relation does not automatically bring the bearer's response to completion. Not avowing does not alter that fact, and avowing does not create it (Value Theory P6 V73). And an uncompleted response and active shutting are two different things. That a response is uncompleted is not by itself enough to judge it an active shutting; whether there has been an act of voiding this other's standing as an end within the bearer's own running must be looked for in the relevant facts. Non-uptake is not uniformly explained as attention not yet reaching there, nor is it promised to be a mere temporary lag that will sooner or later be completed. What Value Theory cut apart is the case in which the bearer, while keeping its fifteenth-level construction-history, wholly voids this other's standing as an end (Value Theory P1 V11, third case); from an uncompleted response alone, that case does not follow.

An other's departure or exit does not automatically turn the bearer toward a successor not yet arrived. The bearer may return to the formal empty position; may turn toward another real other; may thereafter present no outward direction. It may also go on facing the person it will never see again.

That case must be stated plainly. Memories of this other, the shared history and the unfinished matters can go on taking part in the bearer's field of reasons as associated material of the relevant entries; this is not memory standing in for a present other, nor is memory itself a new factual filling — the fact-position is filled only by a concrete other actually existing. These remain entries and material the bearer carries forward in its own constitutive history; and they present an outward direction at the same time, because within them the avowed content that person as an end actually takes part in organizing the future, and not merely as narrative material for telling who I am — the two sides overlap in one and the same case without merging into one (Meaning Theory P1 Y12).

A memory may merely be recalled, or it may enter future arrangements and the use of reasons as associated material of the relevant entry; whether a commemoration changes the calendar, or how often one recalls, are at most fallible clues, and what matters is still whether the avowed other-directed content actually does the work of an effective reference. Take two stipulated cases: in the first, a recollection does not bring the corresponding other-directed content into any future arrangement or use of reasons; in the second, when he rearranges some concrete affair, he still takes as an effective reference the avowed content that that person has values of his own and that this is not mine to give. The difference between the two is given by those two stipulations, not by the frequency of an action or the form of a ritual. Which of the two obtains is described under Y22's condition of organization, and is not proved by the mere fact that I have memories. None of these is automatic, and none is an ought.

And when the rewriting toward a successor does occur, it is a response, not a mechanical falling-back. A person who, at the end, hands conditions on to those who come after has done something, not fallen passively into a slot. This is exactly what the paper on boundaries needs, and it is more real as a response than as a falling-back.

Y24 What anticipatory enactment leaves behind is conditions, not an orientation deposited elsewhere

An act of anticipatory enactment raises three distinct questions: what conditional content was avowed; whether, on its basis, an enactment occurred; and what conditions that enactment actually left behind, and whether they remain accessible to later bearers. The three questions are answered separately. A man writes a book; the book is printed and left there; in the example it is stipulated that anyone who comes later may open it.

What is left behind is conditions; the avowal is the bearer's own from beginning to end, and no orientation is deposited in an object to run on its own.

The book does not do his looking-toward for him, and the work does not avow on his behalf. The structural positions of avowal, revision and exit belong to this bearer, and the position is not taken away from outside (Meaning Theory P1 Y4, second); a work is something carried by one of this bearer's constitutive events, and what it enters in the world of those who come later is conditions, not that later person's constitution. The converse holds too: an enactment toward future bearers may really have occurred while its product was not left behind, or later lost its accessibility; whether usable conditions were left behind does not decide whether anticipatory enactment occurred at the time, nor does it retroactively judge that the looking-toward of that time never occurred. This proposition is the upstream precedent for the paper on boundaries: what continues after the carrier departs calls on this proposition alone there, without proving it again.

§3 Not Occupying the Office of Constitution

Y25 Support is not substitute constitution

Support does not mean not occupying the other's constitutive position. The constitutive positions stand structurally and cannot be taken from outside: the structural positions of avowal, revision and exit belong to this bearer, and all colonization can do is make them inaccessible in running (Meaning Theory P1 Y4, second; Value Theory P3 V39). So the right way to say it is: not to pass off my conditions, judgments or arrangements as his constitution — not to occupy the office of constitution.

With the existing division of labor as its constraint, this paper sets three negative boundaries on the concept of support, and issues no new ought:

If a relation seals off the other's channels of refusal, revision, exit and change of direction; if it converts the conditions it provides into ownership of the other's first-person constitutive results; or if it declares, on the sole basis of my judgment of success, that the other's constitution has been completed — then at those positions it is no longer support but the occupation of the office of constitution.

Each of the three points at one place. In the first, what is sealed off is the channel of actual expression, revision, exit or change of direction, not those positions themselves, which cannot be taken; what is passed off is who has completed the final acknowledgment. In the second, converting the conditions provided into ownership of the other's first-person constitutive results is to enter what belongs on the side of conditions on the side of constitution, whereas constitution is not brought off on the side of conditions (Value Theory P6 V73). In the third, declaring on the sole basis of my judgment of success that his constitution is completed, or giving that judgment final effect, is to let a third-person reading testify in place of a first-person avowal — exactly the point at which the relation paper says the third-person standpoint has no conclusive procedure (Meaning Theory P1 Y9); observation from outside and the appraisals of others can enter as fallible material, and this proposition does not block material; it blocks taking material as a substitute warrant. These three are the boundaries of the concept of support; they are not requirements for action derived from recognizing the other as an end. Which action, if any, is to be taken remains a matter for the Moral Law, Rights Theory, and Action Theory.

This proposition gives only the necessary boundary of what support is not; it gives no sufficient criterion that support has occurred. Not sealing off those channels only shows that a relation has not yet passed itself off as constitution at those positions; indifference, neglect and abdication may also fail to occupy them directly. A teacher who never asks after his student is not occupying the student's office of constitution, and is not thereby supporting him.

A person has only the duty to keep his own law; there is no lateral duty: the root of recognizing the other is wholly within the bearer's own law, not a debt owed the other (Value Theory P1 V16). That sentence holds, and its ought half belongs to the Moral Law. The Moral Law's first theorem and Value Theory's remainder and response are of one source and one reference, two statements of the same thing from two sides; and it is not this series' external ground — to educate value by morality is to bring in a foreign measure (Value Theory P1 V14, closing). Meaning Theory draws the conceptual boundary; the Moral Law issues the ought.

The form of the case of applicability-conditions can be brought in as a reference, not as a ground. A bearer may not practice cultivation at every moment; but when a concrete other has already entered the field of the relation, and the bearer, while keeping its fifteenth-level construction-history, wholly voids this other's standing as an end, that is not a walking-back but an active shutting (Value Theory P1 V11, third case). Occupying the office of constitution is one of the shapes in which the other's position is voided. Value Theory assigns that case no place under direction-value, which belongs to Power Theory. This paper registers only the boundary of the concept of support; what direction-value such operations acquire in Power Theory is left to Power Theory, by its own objects and criteria; this paper does not borrow that case's reasons to decide on its behalf, and the two objects are not the same — there, a single bearer's one-sided running; here, a relation with two ends.

Y26 That the other is able to unfold without revolving around me is the hardest test in this row

The other's own constitutive results do not, by virtue of the relation of support, become the supporter's possession. Within the boundary of not infringing the positions of other subjects, the other may use the conditions I provide to go in directions I did not foresee, do not approve, and even no longer need me for.

The title of this proposition is in the mode of ability, not a statement of fact; and hardest is rhetoric — technically it is one necessary counterfactual boundary of support. The other may perfectly well continue, in his own law, the shared undertaking, the relation of care, or me as an important direction; the test is not that he must in fact leave me. That the student took a road the teacher did not approve is one shape; that another student walks the teacher's road all his life is another; both can pass this test; what would make either fail lies elsewhere.

Ability is limited to constitutive independence, not to real-world self-sufficiency. Infants, the severely disabled, and the subjects of long-term care cannot in reality do without those who support them, and this never gives the supporter the constitutive right over their values: in the domain of bearers, end-value is grounded in being an end, and the gate distinguishes only the mode of operation (Value Theory P1 V3). Two objects must be told apart here. My outward meaning relation may take an infant as the concrete other it looks toward; whether that infant is itself already within the domain of this series' meaning relation is another question — the relation paper places quasi-subjects, and complete subjects that have not yet obtained the fourteenth-level source-position, outside the domain of the relation, without touching their standing as bearers of end-value (Meaning Theory P1 Y3). For an other not yet within that domain, what this proposition protects is his existing standing as an end, that the source of his value is not placed outside him, and the openness of the conditions of first-person running; it does not use that protection to declare that his meaning relation is already established. For an other already within the domain whose real bandwidth is limited, it does not infer from dependence on care that qualification has vanished. The meaning predicates on my side are not copied onto the other.

The test has two layers, and without either it has no power to discriminate.

The qualification layer: whether or not support holds, the other's constitutive position is neither conferred by me nor withdrawn by me on account of his refusal. The relation layer: if the other refuses me, restates his own reasons, or changes direction, does this relation that calls itself support treat that response, which does not match my expectation, as grounds for declaring his first-person constitution invalid; does it on that account seal off his channels of actual expression, revision and change of direction; or does it make acceptance of my final verdict the condition of going on to recognize his first-person position? If so, this paper can no longer describe it, at that point, as support.

The qualification layer holds in any relation whatever; support and blockade pass it alike, so it is a boundary that no judgment may breach, not the layer that sorts out support. A teacher who, after his student changes direction, controls the student's channels of publication and study and demands that the student pass off the teacher's approved direction as his own — the student's constitutive position is still the student's, and the qualification layer is passed; the relation layer is not. The discriminating power lies in the relation layer. It remains a negative boundary, not a procedure that positively certifies support and not a final verdict signed on anyone's inner life; nor does it require the supporter to provide unlimited resources, to maintain every cooperation, or to endorse any harmful action — the real limits of resources, existing commitments and the rights of third parties are judged separately as before. What this proposition opposes is the rewriting of disagreement as nullity of constitution and as acknowledgment on the other's behalf.

This proposition establishes one ground; the paper on boundaries and what follows it write its forms elsewhere without proving it again:

Whatever another has obtained from me, once avowed in his own law, is his, and does not become a constitution I completed on his behalf. While I am still here, it is so; at the boundary, it is so; when I am gone, it is still so.

This sentence needs a boundary of settlement. It is his, left unqualified, would be read as the attribution of property, copyright, authorship, causal contribution, credit, proceeds or responsibility:

This proposition settles one account only, the attribution of constitution. Ownership of things, authorship, causal contribution, credit, proceeds and responsibility each have their own accounts, belonging respectively to Rights Theory, to law, and to Action Theory, and are not settled by this proposition automatically.

Three settlements are then plainly apart; they are three questions, not three new ledgers set beside the relation paper's two. The question of value-constitution: the one who comes later avows in his own law, and what he obtains is his own value-content. The question of meaning's carrying-forward: only when that content is carried into the later person's cross-temporal, first-person carrying-forward does it acquire meaning on his side. The question of rights and action: as above, and not settled automatically from the first two. Take the student. If the teacher once avowed, in his own law, supporting this student, that avowal can become an entry in the teacher's value history; the acts of teaching and their consequences are carried forward with the relevant entry under the conditions for associated material (Meaning Theory P1 Y2). That the student later takes a road the teacher does not approve does not retroactively erase the constitution the teacher completed; and the value-content the student obtains for himself does not thereby become a constitution the teacher completed on his behalf. The teacher has his own constitutive history, the student his; the relation of teaching does not let either end be written by the other. As for the copyright of the course, the authorship of the paper, and the credit for the result, each goes to its own place.

Does not become a constitution I completed on his behalf says this and no more. The other's own avowal and cross-temporal carrying-forward are not re-entered as my constitutive results because I provided conditions; whether my teaching, help, cooperation and contribution genuinely obtained is judged separately, by the relevant facts and norms. This proposition does not forbid me to be glad for him, does not deny actual causal effect, and does not prejudge the authorship of joint work; it refuses one sentence only: that because I provided conditions, the step by which he became himself was completed by me on his behalf.

A precedent in the same direction may be cited, not as a ground. Power Theory, writing of the decay of cultivating power: when a cultivating relation succeeds, the one under power rises into a higher level, the positional difference dissolves, and the relation dissolves with it — the student no longer needs the teacher, the recovered no longer need the physician, the editor withdraws behind the finished work; this dissolution is not the failure of cultivation but the mark of its success (Power Theory P5, criterion C3). That the other's no longer needing me is success rather than failure is of the same shape as this proposition; but what it speaks of is the actual dissolution of a local grid of relations, confined to the releasing orientation of asymmetric power, and it is narrower than this proposition, so it is listed as a precedent in the same direction, not as a ground.

This proposition and the paper on finitude's Y18 share a basis and run in opposite directions: there, what is blocked is my later-acquired meaning being counted back as the justification of an earlier harm; here, what is blocked is the step by which the other became himself being entered under my name (Meaning Theory P2 Y18). The two propositions cite each other and do not prove each other.

§4 Crossing and Merging

Y27 Crossing is not merging

The life-orientations of several bearers can cross and point to one another while each relation remains anchored in its own bearer. The people on the riverbank cleared the silt together, stopped a sand-dredging once, and pooled money to repair a stretch of embankment. Upstream uses them to say that sources do not merge, and there nothing is required about what they have avowed; this paper, using them, must add a stipulation: even if each of them has avowed the content the other as an end, and each has carried the river and the people on its bank into his own cross-temporal carrying-forward, so that their orientations cross densely in those few affairs, each person's meaning relation still has both ends in himself, and it is not fused above them into a collective meaning.

The main proof is in the relation paper: all the attribution endpoints of the relation as a whole do not pass beyond that one bearer (Meaning Theory P1 Y2, Y10). The contradiction can be stated directly: if two meaning relations were merged into one, the sentence that both ends belong to one person would be gone. Value Theory has established the precedent of the same shape: relational crossing, resonance, joint decision and joint arrangement are not by themselves enough to merge the value-sources the bearers already have; the crossing of orientations says where they point, not where they come from (Value Theory P6 V76). That proposition lands on sources, this one on the relation; the shapes are close, and neither replaces the other.

What this proposition blocks is a total orientation that takes in each person's orientation and replaces it; that another bearer might come to stand on other conditions and coexist with each of them is not within this reductio, and is left undecided as Value Theory leaves it. Whether a collective first person can stand must be shown separately, by its own conditions of constitution, and this series does not prejudge it. This non-prejudgment matches upstream: Value Theory asked whether another bearer might come to stand on some other conditions, wrote that it does not ask, and that even if one did, that would not mean the sources of the original people had been swallowed by it (Value Theory P6 V76); Value Theory's Paper 0 registers it as a remainder left by that construct, an explicit deferral that is at the same time a remainder (Value Theory Paper 0, §6, first cell).

Crossing must be cut apart from resonance on the spot. Resonance is Value Theory's word, and it falls on the layer of mutual recognition: for two bearers each able, within the relational domain in question, to exercise other-directed recognition at the fifteenth structural level, resonance is a mutual recognition between them, and what is recognized is that the other has values of his own; it constitutes no third value, no third source and no third bearer (Value Theory P6 V77). What this series calls the crossing of life-orientations is another thing. Long crossing does not guarantee mutual recognition — two people's orientations can cross on one affair for years without their ever recognizing each other; and a single occurrence of mutual recognition does not guarantee continued crossing — people who once recognized each other can afterward go their separate ways. The two are not equated by definition. This series neither re-establishes resonance nor borrows it.

第一节 看向

Y22 看向是这段关系朝外的那个方向；涵育是它动起来的样子

上游现有两格。承认是那一刀，一次第一人称的接续事件：当承担域里出现一个具体的他者，他亦是目的这一事实先于我的承认就已经是，承认落在这个已经是的事实上，认这个事实，接续这个事实，不造这个事实（价值论 P1 V14 第三层）。涵育是动词位，是承认这项内容在变换轴上的动力显形，是那项内容动起来的样子（价值论 P1 V16）。

中间那个可跨时持续的朝向没有名字。一个人在两次施为之间仍然朝着那里：他好些年没有为那位朋友做过什么，而朋友仍在他的安排里占一个位置，他想到明年的时候那里有一个人。没有这一格，只剩两种写法，两种都错。一种把看向写成施为的总和，不做，便不看向；那把一段关系的方向压成了行动的频率，而一个人可以许多年不做什么，那个人仍在他明年的安排里。另一种把看向写成内容地位的影子，只要那项内容仍被认领，看向便在；那把一项长期休眠仅可召回的内容，与一项仍在组织未来的方向混成一样，而两样在这个人的一生里是两种不同的样子。本条补的这一格，正在两种写法之间。这一格是本系列自己的产出，不是外借：上游把他者作为目的那一项立成内容，本系列把它在一生的尺度上读成方向；关系篇已为它留了名字（意义论 P1 Y12）。

看向（looking-toward）是这段关系朝外的那个方向。它不是持续的心理注意，也不是每时每刻的施为；它是他者作为目的的那项已认领内容，在跨时承接中不只保持可被召回，而且仍作为一个有效参照参与未来可能性与理由场的组织。这一形状有限篇末尾已写，本篇照承。加到有效参照参与理由场的组织这一层，是因为只写可召回分不开两样。那位朋友的事仍被他认领，只是可回想，是这样一种样子：他偶尔想起，想起时没有什么随之而动，明年的时间表上没有为那个人留下的一段。仍改变明年的安排，是另一种样子：他算下一年的时间，那扇窗和那个人占了里面的一段，别的事围着这一段挪，他回一封信之前先想那个人会怎么读。两种样子里那项内容都在他名下；只有后一种是看向。

两处要分开。

其一，涵育不是第五种价值源，价值源只有四行（价值论 P1 V16）。15DD 那一行上的价值内容是他者可以有他自己的价值而这不由我代给（价值论 P6 V73）；看向是它在一生尺度上的形态，涵育是它动起来的样子。名词位与动词位不合并。

其二，看向与施为分开。内容的地位与生命方向不是同一个主词：一项内容仍被认领，只推得出不得由一次未施为判定该内容已经退出，推不出这段生命关系仍实际呈现向外方向。关系篇已立这条认识上的限制，本篇照用：

此刻没有涵育行动，不能单凭这一点推出看向已经停止；而看向是否仍在，由跨时关系的描述判断，不由单次行动或内容地位代替。

向外面只有在他者作为目的的那项内容实际参与理由场组织时才呈现向外方向；关系仍在，内容仍被认领，或一次行动缺席，都不能单独替代这一判断（意义论 P1 Y12）。如此三件各守各的位置：内容地位不被单次行动反证；看向不被内容地位自动保证；关系篇的描述账继续有效，看向是否仍在只能被可错地描述，第三人称没有终局程序，第一人称不预授终审（意义论 P1 Y8，Y9）。

本条与向内那一面同在关系篇的指向轴上，是同一条轴的两种内容，不是两根柱子；向内那一面问的是承接关系，向外那一面问的是内容朝向谁，两面可以在同一案上重叠而不合成一面（意义论 P1 Y12）。它可以暂不产生行动。此处只是关系层的最低描述，不是外部的充分必要判据。

看向也不是一项应当。一个已经认领了那项内容的承担者，可以有一段日子不朝外看，那是运行上的起落，价值论已把它与主动的关闭切开（价值论 P1 V11 第三种）；本篇只登记看向在不在，不登记它该不该在。

第二节 三分形态

Y23 三分形态可以并存，可以相互改写，而没有固定次序

上游给的是三种形态各自是什么。形式朝向，是为任何将来若出现的目的承担者保留那个形式他者位；预期施为，是面向未来可能承担者的他向行动，写一本书，传播一种思想，留下作品或安排；事实充实，是承担域里一个现实存在的具体他者充实那个事实位（价值论 P1 V14 第二层）。本篇给的是它们在一生中的并存与改写关系。

三者可以并存，不构成适用于一切承担者的固定人生阶段序列。现实他者在场时，形式朝向仍可保留；预期施为也可以在现实他者在场时发生；一个人可以同时为一位朋友留着位置，为将来的读者写一本书，并为任何还没有出现的人保留着那个位。这不排除某一生恰好依次展开，先形式，后预期，再事实；也不撤销上游所立的结构关系，预期施为由形式他者位引导（价值论 P1 V14 第二层）。没有固定的人生时序，不等于没有结构上的依赖。

三者也不在同一个层次上，本篇不把它们改成三个互斥的状态。形式朝向是一个保留着的位置与取向；预期施为是一类行动；事实充实是现实他者对事实位的充实。它们在一生中的组合可以讨论，而行动，事实与第一人称回应不因此变成同一种事件。

形式朝向（formal orientation）：那个位置是承担者可以保留的空位。它不对一切承担者恒在，价值论的模态留白拒绝主张那道余项对一切 14DD 运作皆必发（价值论 P1 V14 第一层）。在该位已由其第一人称结构取得时，事实充实不是它成立的条件。空与缺是两回事：它空着，而它空着，孤独主体依然自足（价值论 P1 V14 第二层）。

预期施为（anticipatory enactment）：所认领的是一项条件式的内容，任何将来若出现的承担者，可以有他自己的价值而这不由我代给。这一项是实在的，价值论写着它经第一人称认领后与这个人名下别的已认领内容同取价值地位（价值论 P6 V73）。它由形式他者位引导，仍是预期性的朝向，不把一个尚不存在的承担者变成当前的事实（价值论 P1 V14 第二层）。

事实充实（factual filling）：只有承担域里一个现实存在的具体他者，才充实那个事实位。而具体他者的出现只使那个位落到一个人身上；承担者把不把这一事实认领为自己的那一项，仍须另发生一道第一人称的回应。上游把两步分得清楚：第一件是事实，这个具体的他者也是目的，在我承认之前就是；第二件是回应，从第一人称的位置承认并接续这个事实（价值论 P6 V73；P1 V14 第三层）。

世界那一侧的变化，与承担者对它的接续，是两层。现实他者是否存在，原机会是否仍在，这些不由承担者的回应决定；条件的变化怎样限制既有安排的兑现，也不由承担者的认领创造。世界的变化可以直接改变事实充实与行动条件，而它不因此完成承担者对这一变化的第一人称接续。本篇所说的改写专指第三层：承担者在当前承接，重新辨认或新构中怎样回应这些变化。这一回应不必都是一次新的认领，已有的承接可以继续组织未来，不必每一次条件变化都新增一个账条（意义论 P1 Y12）。把世界那一侧的进出直接写成第一人称关系形态的转换，就是把世界的变化当成了承担者已经认领或修订的证明。

一个具体他者进入关系场，不自动使承担者完成那一道回应。不认领不改变那个事实，认领也不创造那个事实（价值论 P6 V73）。而回应未完成与主动的关闭是两件事。回应未完成这一事实，本身不足以判为主动的关闭；有没有把这个他者作为目的的地位在自身运行中作废的动作，须另看相应的事实。未接续不一概解释成注意未及，也不许诺它只是暂时的滞后，迟早会完成。价值论所说的那一种，是承担者一面保有着它 15DD 的构造史，一面把这个他者作为目的的地位彻底作废（价值论 P1 V11 第三种）；单凭未完成，推不出这一种。恒在的位置归属，与运行上此刻做了什么，是两层；第三节的硬检验也用这两层说话。

一个他者离开或退场，不自动使承担者转向尚未到场的后来者。他可以回到形式空位；可以转向另一位现实他者；也可以此后不再呈现向外方向。他也可以继续面向那个再也见不到的人。

这一种要说清。关于这位他者的记忆，共同的历史与未结的事，可以作为相应条目的关联材料继续参与他的理由场；这不是由记忆替代一位在场的他者，也不是记忆本身成了一次新的事实充实，事实位只由现实存在的具体他者充实。这仍是由他在自己的构成史中承接的条目与材料；它同时呈现向外方向，是因为其中那个人作为目的的那项已认领内容实际参与未来的组织，而不只是把那个人当作说明我是谁的叙事材料，两面在同一案上重叠而不合成一面（意义论 P1 Y12）。

记忆可以仅被想起，也可以作为相应条目的关联材料进入未来的安排与理由的使用；纪念是否改变日程，想起是否频繁，至多是可错的线索，关键仍是已认领的他向内容有没有实际发挥有效参照的作用。设两种情形：其一，某次想起没有使相应他向内容进入任何未来的安排或理由的使用；其二，他重排一件具体的事务时，仍以那个人有他自己的价值而这不由我代给这项已认领内容作有效参照。两种情形的差别由这两句设定给出，不由动作的频率或仪式的形态给出。哪一种在场，依 Y22 的组织条件描述，不由我有记忆这一件单独证明。这几样里没有一样是自动的，也没有一样是应当的。

而朝向后来者这一改写若确实发生，它是一次回应，不是机械回落。一个人临终把条件交给后来者，是做了一件事，不是被动落格。边界篇所需的正是这一条，而它作为一次回应比作为一次回落更实。

Y24 预期施为留下的是条件，不是被寄放的方向

一次预期施为可以分三问：所认领的条件式内容是什么；据此有没有发生施为；该施为实际上留下了什么条件，以及这些条件后来是否仍可进入。三问各归各。一个人写了一本书，书印出来放在那里，例子里设定任何一个后来的人可以打开它。

留下来的是条件，那道认领自始至终是承担者自己的，没有任何取向被寄放在物件里自行其是。

书不替他看向，作品不替他认领。认领，修订与退出的结构位归该承担者，位置不由外部取走（意义论 P1 Y4 二）；一件作品是这个承担者某一道构成事件所携带的东西，它进后来者的世界，进的是条件，不是那个后来者的构成。反过来也成立：一次面向未来承担者的施为可以真实发生，而它的产物没有留下来，或后来失去了可进入性；有没有留下可用的条件，不决定当时有没有发生预期施为，也不倒判那时的看向从未发生。本条是边界篇的上游先例：承载者退场之后什么继续，那里只调用这一条，不重证。

第三节 不占构成之职

Y25 支持不是替代构成

支持（support）说的不是不占据他的构成位。构成位在结构上恒在，外部取不走：认领，修订与退出的结构位归该承担者，殖民所能做的只是使它们在运行上不可达（意义论 P1 Y4 二；价值论 P3 V39）。所以正确的说法是：不把我的条件，判断或安排冒充为他的构成，不占着构成之职。

本篇以既有的分工为约束，在支持这个概念上另立三条否定边界，不发新的应当：

一项关系若封住他者拒绝，修订，退出与改向的通道，若把所给条件换算成对他者第一人称构成成果的占有，或仅凭我的成功评价就替他认定构成已经完成，那么它在这些位置上已经不再是支持，而是在占着构成之职。

三条各指一处。第一条，被封住的是实际表达，修订，退出或改向的通道，不是那几个位置本身，位置取不走；被冒充的是谁已经完成了最后认可。第二条，把所给条件换算成对他者第一人称构成成果的占有，是把条件那一侧的东西记到构成那一侧，而构成不在条件那一侧做成（价值论 P6 V73）。第三条，仅凭我的成功评价就替他认定构成已经完成，或赋予这份评价终局效力，是让第三人称的读数替第一人称的认领作证，那正是关系篇说第三人称没有终局程序的地方（意义论 P1 Y9）；外部的观察与他人的评价可以作可错的材料进入，本条不拦材料，拦的是把材料当成替代的凭据。这三条是支持这个概念的边界，不是从承认他者为目的推出的行动要求。是否采取何种行动，仍交道德律，权利论与行动论。

本条只给支持不是什么的必要边界，不给支持已经发生的充分判据。不封那些通道，只说明一项关系尚未在这些位置上冒充构成；冷漠，放任与撤责也可能并不直接占据它们。一个从不过问学生的老师，没有占着学生的构成之职，也不因此就在支持他。

人只有遵守自己的法的义务，没有横向的义务：承认他者的根全在承担者自己的法里，不是一项欠他者的债（价值论 P1 V16）。这一句成立，而它的应当那一半归道德律。道德律的第一定理与价值论那道余项和回应同源同指，二者是同一件事在两侧的陈述，而它不是本系列的外部根据，拿道德去教化价值就是引了外来的尺（价值论 P1 V14 末段）。意义论给概念分界，道德律发应当。

适用条件的形态可以引来作参照，不作根据。一个承担者可以不时时地实行涵育；但当一个具体的他者已经进入了关系场，承担者一面保有着它 15DD 的构造史，一面把这个他者作为目的的地位彻底作废，那不是回走，是主动的关闭（价值论 P1 V11 第三种）。占着构成之职，是把他者的位置作废了的一种样子。价值论不为那一情形指定方向价值上的归属，方向价值归权力论。本篇只登记支持这个概念的边界；占职这类操作在权力论中取得何种方向价值，交权力论按它自己的对象与判据处理，本篇不借那一情形的理由代判，两处对象也不相同：那里是一个承担者单侧的运行，这里是两端的关系。

Y26 他者能够不再围绕我而展开，是这一行最硬的检验

他者自己的构成成果，不因支持关系而归支持者占有。在不侵害其他主体位置的边界内，他者可以使用我提供的条件，走向我没有预料，不同意，甚至不再需要我的方向。

本条的标题是能力式的，不是事实断言；最硬也是修辞，技术上它是支持的一项必要反事实边界。他者完全可以在自己的法中继续共同的事业，继续照料的关系，继续以我为一个重要方向；这道检验不是他事实上必须离开我。那个学生走了一条老师不同意的路，是一种样子；另一个学生一辈子在老师的路上走，也是一种样子；两种样子都过得了这道检验，过不了的是另一件事。

能够，限为构成独立，不是现实自足。婴幼儿，重度残障者，长期照料关系里的主体，在现实中脱离不了支持者，而这绝不使支持者取得其价值的构成权：承担域的奠基在是目的，门只区分运行（价值论 P1 V3）。这里要分开两个对象。我的向外意义关系可以以一个婴幼儿为所看向的具体他者；这个婴幼儿自身是否已在本系列意义关系的定义域内，是另一问，关系篇把准主体与尚未取得 14DD 源位的完备主体排在定义域外，而不动它们作为目的价值承担者的地位（意义论 P1 Y3）。对尚未进入定义域的他者，本条保护的是其既有的目的地位，价值来源不外置，与第一人称运行的开放条件，不借这项保护宣告其意义关系已经成立；对已经进入定义域而现实带宽受限者，不从照料依赖推出资格消失。我这一侧的意义谓词，不复制到对方身上。

检验分两层，缺一层就没有区分力。

资格层：无论支持是否成立，他者的构成位置都不由我授予，也不由我因其拒绝而撤销。 关系层：若他者拒绝我，重述自己的理由或改向，这段以支持为名的关系是否把这种不合我期待的回应处理成他的构成无效，是否据此封住他实际表达，修订与改向的通道，或者把接受我的最后裁定当作继续承认其第一人称位置的条件。若是，本篇便不能在该处继续把它描述为支持。

资格层在任何关系里都成立，支持与封锁同过，所以它是任何判定都不能破坏的边界，不是筛出支持的那一层。老师在学生改向之后控制他发表与求学的通道，要他把老师认可的方向冒认为自己的方向，学生的构成位置仍是学生的，资格层过；关系层不过。区分力在关系层。它仍是一项否定边界，不是充分认证支持的程序，不替任何人的内心签终审；也不要求支持者无限提供资源，维持一切合作，或认可任何有害的行动，现实资源的边界，既有的承诺与第三方的权利照旧另判。本条反对的是把分歧转写为构成无效与代为认可。

本条立的是一条根据，边界篇与其后各写它在别处的形态，不重证：

凡他人从我这里得着的，一经他自己的法认领，就是他的，不成为我替他完成的构成。我还在，是这样；我在边界上，是这样；我不在了，还是这样。

这句要加一道结算边界。就是他的，若不加限，会被读成物权，著作权，作者身份，因果贡献，功劳，收益或责任的归属：

本条只结算构成归属这一笔。物的所有权，作者身份，因果贡献，功劳，收益与责任，各有各的账，分别归权利论，法与行动论，不由本条自动结算。

三类结算当场分明，它们是三个问题，不是在关系篇两账旁边新增的三本账。价值构成这一问：后来者以自己的法认领，所得是后来者自己的价值内容。意义承接这一问：该内容被担进后来者跨时的第一人称承接时，才在后来者一侧取得意义。权利与行动这一问：如上，不由前两问自动结出。拿那个学生说。若老师曾在自己的法中认领支持这个学生，这道认领可以成为老师的价值史条目；教学的行为及其后果，按关联材料的条件随相应条目被承接（意义论 P1 Y2）。学生后来走向老师不同意的道路，不追溯抹去老师已经完成的那道构成；学生自己取得的价值内容，也不因此成为老师替他完成的构成。老师有老师自己的构成史，学生有学生自己的构成史，教学关系不使任何一端被另一端代写。至于那门课的版权，那篇论文的署名，那项成果的功劳，各归各处。

不成为我替他完成的构成，说到这里为止。他者自己的认领与跨时承接，不因我提供了条件而转记为我的构成成果；我的教学，帮助，合作及其贡献是否真实成立，按相应的事实与规范另判。本条不禁止我为他高兴，不否认实际的因果作用，也不预判共同作品的署名；它只拒绝一句话：我提供过条件，所以他成为自己的那一步是我替他完成的。

同向的先例可以引，不作根据。权力论写涵育性权力的衰减：一段涵育关系成功时，被权力者升入更高层级，位差消解，关系随之解体，学生不再需要老师，康复者不再需要医生，编辑退到完成的作品之后；这一消解不是涵育的失败，是它的成功标志（权力论 P5 判据 C3）。那是他者不再需要我即是成功而非失败，与本条同形；但它说的是一张局部关系网格的实际解除，限于非对称权力的释放取向，比本条窄，故列同向先例，不作根据。

本条与有限篇的 Y18 同源异向：那里拦的是我后来取得的意义被倒算成先前伤害的正当，这里拦的是他者成为自己的那一步被记到我名下（意义论 P2 Y18）。两条互引，不互证。

第四节 交错与合并

Y27 交错不合并

多个承担者的生命方向可以交错，可以互指，而各自的关系仍锚在各自身上。河边那些人一起清过淤，拦下过一次采砂，凑钱修了一段护坡。上游用他们说源不合并，那里不要求他们认领了什么；本篇用他们，要加一句设定：哪怕他们各自都已认领了他者作为目的那项内容，各自都把这条河与河边的人担进了自己的跨时承接，各人的方向在那几件事上交错得很密，每一个人的意义关系，两端仍是他自己，不在他们之上熔成一个集体的意义。

主证在关系篇：整段关系的所有归属端点，不越出这一位承担者（意义论 P1 Y2，Y10）。反证可以直接转写：若两段意义关系并成一段，两端同属一人那一句就没了。价值论已立同形的先例：关系性交叉，共鸣，共同决定与共同安排本身，都不足以使各承担者既有的价值源自动合并为一；指向交错说的是朝哪里，不说从哪里出来（价值论 P6 V76）。那一条落在源上，本条落在这段关系上，形状相近，一条不替另一条。

本条拦的是一个总的方向把各人的方向收下并替下它们；另有一个承担者另行成立而与各人并存，不在本条的反证之内，与价值论一样留着不判。集体第一人称若能成立，须另以自己的构成条件证明，本系列不预判。本条的不预判与上游相同：价值论问过另一个承担者能不能在别的条件下另行成立，写着不问，就算有，也不等于原来那些人的源被它吞掉了（价值论 P6 V76）；价值论 Paper 0 把它登记为那次构留下的余项，一处明文的留置同时也是一处余项（价值论 Paper 0 第六节第一格）。

交错须当场与共鸣切开。共鸣是价值论的词，落在相认那一层：对两个在该关系域均能运行 15DD 他向承认的承担者，共鸣是双方之间的相认，所认的是对方有对方自己的价值，它不构成第三项价值，第三个源，或第三个承担者（价值论 P6 V77）。本系列所说的生命方向交错是另一件事。长期的交错不保证相认，两个人的方向可以在一件事上交错多年而从未相认；发生过一次相认也不保证此后持续交错，相认过的人可以此后各走各路。二者不作定义上的等同。本系列不重立共鸣，也不搬用它。

第五节 射程外地址与手递

本节不产命题。

两个承担者之间是否存在一种更高阶的关系条件，本系列不裁，按全体系既有的处理排除出本系列的范围。此处只标地址，不以确定语气把工作交给任何后续。

手递：这段关系指出了自身之外，而两端仍是同一个承担者。那么这个承担者停下的时候，什么停下，什么继续，归边界篇。
