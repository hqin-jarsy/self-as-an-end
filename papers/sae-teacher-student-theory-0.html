Abstract

This is Paper 0 of Teacher-Student Theory, written last and placed first. It sets up no propositions, and does only a few things for the reader of this series: it says where the series stands within SAE, writes down how far it reads and what its main line is, sets out the way of reading and the shared words that the papers have in common, interfaces as a whole with several traditions, says once, together, the directions of colonization the papers read, lists together the thirty-eight propositions the series has set up, and registers the remainders it leaves unsettled and the questions still to be argued.

Teacher-Student Theory is on the relational side of SAE, and follows Parent-Child Theory; what it takes up is the half that Cultivation Theory handed on: the back-and-forth between two people. The series does not define all teacher-student relations; the teacher-student relation it reads has as its premise that the teacher's end is headed in the 15DD direction: across one matter after another, headed toward treating this student as an end, and not merely as a means; people are all mixed, falling back is read as it occurs, and after falling back this end can turn back. Where it can be read that the student is treated merely as a means, the relation is not within scope; where someone who teaches a course only teaches the course well, it cannot be read which kind this is; it is not judged, nor is it within scope. The teacher is a party in a relation, determined by the matter, not a post and not a title; schools and universities lie on the institutional side. The series follows the student up to coming of age. The main line, said plainly: some of what the teacher teaches he cannot test for the present, and he takes it in first: he remembers it, he can do it. Working out why, and going back to see whether it is right, he has to do himself; the teacher gives him the time for this, and also ways to examine and test what he has been taught. Where he himself is going, the teacher does not decide for him. The seven papers read in turn: the teacher in a relation; teaching; checking; expectation; space; collision; handing over.

This paper interfaces as a whole with the Analects, the "Record of Learning" in the Book of Rites, Han Yu, Xunzi, Zhu Xi, Plato, Augustine, Locke, Rousseau, Nietzsche and Freire; developmental psychology and research on education are not interfaced with, and are taken only as material. The directions of colonization the papers read borrow things that are already there, or may form, between teacher and student: the student's standing, not being able to check, the rightness of the content, questions, expectation, dependency, the road the teacher chooses, support, the teaching received. These things carry no direction in themselves; the direction is read on the one effect in which they are put to some use. This paper proves no proposition at the object layer.

Introduction

The seven papers that follow each read one thing. Paper 1, "The Teacher in a Relation," reads what the teacher-student relation this series speaks of looks like: scope, the teacher determined by the matter, provisions and authority, each relation, the two books at the student's end, his never having chosen and not being able to walk away, the object layer and the operation layer. Paper 2, "Teaching," reads teaching itself: the two layers it reaches, taking in first and checking later, where the direction of teaching is read, teaching and indoctrination, what is written in along with it, and teaching that was wrong. Paper 3, "Checking," reads what follows once the student begins to scrutinize what he has taken in: his questions, the three signals, what can be given and what cannot be done for him, the student's finding that the teacher was wrong, and what follows once he can check. Paper 4, "Expectation," reads what grows out of the back-and-forth: expectation, hanging an expectation on "what kind of person you are," compliance not standing as proof of avowal, the two senses of dependency, using one student as the measure of another. Paper 5, "Space," reads what follows once the thing the student himself cares about has come up: the one thing that cannot be taught, one teacher and many students, the three forms, negating a false cannot-not, not turning every time together into an occasion for growth. Paper 6, "Collision," reads the student's own cannot-not meeting the teacher's law: what the teacher's end looks like, mutual chiseling, the threshold of the Conflict Treatise, not agreeing and surpassing. Paper 7, "Handing Over," reads the last stretch: the boundary of coming of age, the test that lies on the student's side, having taught not being entered as repayment, ending early, and by whom what follows is read.

Written this way, several things are left with no paper to look after them: why this series is here, and how far it reads; how the main line strings the seven papers together; the way of reading the papers share, and what the same word means in which paper; several traditions whose question is put to the series as a whole; what the directions of colonization the papers read look like when set together; and which questions are still to be argued. This paper looks after these.

The method is that of the other papers: the conclusions of upstream papers are said in plain words; sources and extensions go in the notes. This paper sets up no propositions, and alters no sentence of the papers that follow. How the pieces upstream handed to this series are placed is given in the appendix at the end.

§1 Place

SAE has three sides: the side of the subject, the relational side, and the institutional side. The relational side is unfolded by Relation Theory, and downstream of it there are already Love Theory, Marriage Theory, Cooperation Theory and Parent-Child Theory. Teacher-Student Theory is a further branch on the relational side, following Parent-Child Theory.

The division of labor between the two levels Relation Theory has written down: Relation Theory in the abstract establishes only what all relations have; what holds only in one kind of relation is handed to the series on that kind of relation to argue.1 What has already been set up at the layer of relation this series uses directly and does not rewrite: the minimal conditions of a relation, the two directions of cultivation and colonization, expectation, the sediment of dealings, asymmetry, ending.

With Cultivation Theory. Cultivation Theory writes a single act from the giving end; the back-and-forth between two people it does not develop, and it writes that the setting belongs to the side that writes about what passes between people.2 It is exactly this half that Teacher-Student Theory takes up: a question has come in, how the teacher responds, and what conditions of the student's next asking the response has changed; how expectation grows after one sentence of praise; what the things taught turn into in his hands; how this relation comes to its end. What Cultivation Theory has already set up this series refers back to and does not rewrite; merely replacing the examples of upstream propositions with a teacher and a student is not what this series is for.

With Parent-Child Theory. Parents teaching their own child is read in Parent-Child Theory, and this series does not enter it. The two series use the same axis of stages and the same boundary of coming of age. They differ in the frame: Parent-Child Theory borrows the two layers of the institutional side and reads securing and support; between teacher and student there is no main business at the layer of securing, the teacher does not bear the student's base layer, and the main business is teaching. So the frame of this series is another one: taking in first, checking later.

With the example in Relation Theory. Relation Theory Paper 5 read a veteran technician at a mill and his apprentice; that is Relation Theory's example, and this series refers back to it and does not read it again.

With the institutional side. Schools and universities are constructs. Marking, promotion, streaming, the school system, appointment, are provisions of a construct, which this series acknowledges but does not judge; of the authority a construct gives the teacher, the source and the force are not judged. Guardianship belongs to the institutional side;3 where the teacher's effect crosses the student's basic rights, this series reads as far as "this is violation," and protection and disposition after the violation lie on the institutional side. What responsibility comes with the position of teaching a course also lies on the institutional side. The university is not in this series, and is handed to the institutional side.

With the papers on the developmental side. SAE has already read, from the side of development and learning, how a person grows: the paper on education divides teaching, nurturing and leading; the three papers on the Terrible Twos, the Terrible Teens and the Terrible Telos read the genesis of self-awareness, purpose and recognition; the Learning series reads how each layer learns.4 This series does not read these again, and calls on them where they are used; what it reads is how the teacher's end acts, and how far the student has grown serves only as a condition of that end's acting.

With the subject that comes after this series. How the teacher himself lives and how he treats people is seen by the student, and the education paper calls this leading. This face is there all along; how it is passed on, how the one who was taught later teaches others, and how cultivation is passed on between people, are subjects for after this series, and are only registered here.

§2 Scope and Main Line

Scope. There are seven items.

One, not defining all teacher-student relations. To say that a relation is not within the teacher-student relation this series reads is not to say that it is not a teacher-student relation (Paper 1, TS1).

Two, the teacher's end headed in the 15DD direction. In SAE's levels, the layer at which the other is recognized as an end too is written 15DD.5 The teacher-student relation this series reads has as its premise that the teacher's end is headed in this direction: across one matter after another, headed toward treating this student as an end, and not merely as a means. The direction is a premise, not a component of the teacher-student relation. Where it can be read that the student is treated merely as a means, that relation is not within scope. Where someone who teaches a course only teaches the course well, it cannot be read which kind this is; it is not judged, nor is it within scope: that is teaching, and teaching can carry no direction at all; a relation in which the course is only taught well is not what this series sets out to read. What cannot be read does not fall, by default, on the side of means. So there are two things this series does not read further: where the student is read as merely a means, something has been read; where the course is only taught well, and where material is insufficient, nothing has been read; only the first carries a reading (Paper 1, TS1).

Three, direction, falling back, and turning back. There is no teacher who stands purely at 15DD; people are all mixed; the direction is read by one operation at a time, falling back is read as it occurs, and does not overturn the direction. After falling back, this end can turn back; where there is persistent refusal to turn back, the direction is no longer at 15DD. Reflection is read from words and deeds; it does not cancel a violation already read, nor leave the student owing forgiveness (Paper 1, TS1).

Four, the teacher as a party in a relation. Determined by the matter: one party teaches, the other learns, the teaching actually enters the conditions of occurrence at the learner's end, and in that matter there is a teacher-student relation; once is enough. Titles do not define it (Paper 1, TS2).

Five, each relation. A teacher and each student form one relation apiece, entered on separate accounts, pair by pair; a class or a school of followers is not read as a further bearer. Among teacher, student and parents, only the relation between teacher and student is read (Paper 1, TS4).

Six, the series follows the student up to coming of age. Coming of age is read as his own cannot-not having taken shape, arriving domain by domain; domains are drawn by the matters of avowal, not by the subjects taught. The main text gives no ages and no school years, and does not use graduation or completing an apprenticeship as the boundary (Paper 7, TS34).

Seven, the object layer and the operation layer. The object layer reads the relation within scope; at the operation layer the teacher confirms only his own end, and the student's end is seen only by clues (Paper 1, TS7).

Main line. Said plainly:

Some of what the teacher teaches he cannot test for the present, and he takes it in first: he remembers it, he can do it. Working out why, and going back to see whether it is right, he has to do himself; the teacher gives him the time for this, and also ways to examine and test what he has been taught. Where he himself is going, the teacher does not decide for him.

The frame is "taking in first, checking later." Four things, divided between two sides. The side of taking in has two layers: remembering, and having learned. The other side also has two things: understanding, and checking, both of them his own turning back to scrutinize what he has already taken in. What teaching reaches is the former side; the latter side he has to walk himself, and what the teacher gives is conditions (Paper 2, TS8 and TS9; Paper 3, TS16).

This is a line through time, not a rule: some of what is taught first enters his learning, and only later becomes something he can check for himself; it does not say that all teaching must be accepted first and examined afterward. Taking in is not holding it to be true, nor avowing. This series writes "take in"; it does not write "believe."

The last sentence of the main line governs direction: the student's own purpose cannot be taught, and the teacher's end gives space and does not decide it for him (Paper 5, TS24; Paper 7, TS36).

§3 Way of Reading

Asymmetry: three are read. Of the asymmetries between teacher and student, this series reads three, none of which is authority given by a construct: in knowledge and ability; not being able to check for the present; not yet being able to say no. Asymmetry in itself is not crossing the line (Paper 1, TS3; Paper 2, TS9).

The axis of stages. The student's phase transitions are used as an axis. In SAE's levels, memory is written 11DD, prediction 12DD, self-awareness 13DD, purpose 14DD. The table below is an example of one order, not a set of boxes, and not a definition; the main text gives no ages. Each paper writes only how this axis runs in the one thing that paper reads.

There is no 15DD of the student's on the axis; in this series 15DD appears only at the teacher's end.

The two uses of DD; the two books. At the teacher's end, 15DD is a stance: the direction in which one operation goes. At the student's end, DD reads emergence and maturation, and does not become a label; the main text writes "in this matter, he can already check for himself," and does not write what layer of student he is. The student's standing as an end is full from the start; what he can do and how much he understands grow as he learns; the two books are kept apart (Paper 1, TS5).

Teaching and nurturing; leading is not read. The education paper divides three things: teaching, nurturing and leading.6 Teaching is in Paper 2. Nurturing in the tripartite division is read in two stretches: the stretch facing self-awareness in Paper 3, and the stretch facing purpose in Paper 5. The tripartite division says how the three things differ in what they do; it does not say that teaching has in it no direction of cultivation in the broad sense. Leading this series only registers.

What is not read, and what is. The provisions of schools, universities and posts are acknowledged but not judged; of the authority a construct gives, the source and the force are not judged, and how the teacher uses it is read. Position is not unfolded here. The household is not read: the parents' arrangements are registered only as conditions, and where the parents' requirement passes through the teacher, that act is entered in the teacher's relation. Guardianship, and disposition after the line is crossed, belong to the institutional side. What comes after coming of age is not read. How things will go for any kind of teacher or any one student is not written.

Examples. Made-up examples are the main ones: Fang Xu teaches mathematics to Lu Miao, a neighbor's child, set outside school, with no appointment and no marks; Master Zheng, with three boys learning carpentry, appears in Papers 1 to 5. When the student appears, only words and deeds are written. Historical material uses only the childhood that an adult, now dead, wrote down of himself and that has already been made public, and reads only how the adult author writes. Contrasting sentences in the examples are not judged for direction by the form of the words alone: which way that occasion headed is read by what it actually opened or sealed at the time; what it sediments into later is read separately, and is not used to judge the occasion backward; where material is insufficient, the matter stays undetermined.

The normative layer gives only direction. This series has a few sentences of direction, for instance "hurried teaching, and teaching filled to the brim, are not the direction of cultivation." A sentence of direction does not itself stand in for a particular judgment: no one act of teaching is judged on the strength of it. How this occasion counts for this one student is for the teacher's end to judge by his situation, and this series gives no algorithm; this judgment is open to questioning (Paper 2, TS11).

§5 Interfacing as a Whole

Each paper has interfaced with one tradition or several; they are spoken of together here.

Pre-Qin and Confucian. The chapters of the Analects on teaching: walking three together; in teaching, no class of people is excluded; the bundle of dried meat; not opening the way until he is straining; not yielding even to one's teacher; Qiu holds back, so I urged him on; to take what you know as known. SAE's Reading the Analects has annotated these, and each paper refers back. The "Record of Learning" in the Book of Rites, on asking and awaiting the question, on leading without dragging, on the young listening and not asking, and on teaching and learning growing together, is divided among Papers 3, 4 and 6. The line on blue taken from the indigo plant, in Xunzi's "Exhortation to Learning," is in Paper 6. Han Yu's "On Teachers" is in Papers 1 and 6; Zhu Xi's division of lesser learning and greater learning is in Paper 2. In interfacing with the chapters of the Analects, only structure is read here.

Western. The geometry in Plato's Meno is in Paper 2, as a boundary case; Augustine's De Magistro is in Paper 3; Aristotle's passage on truth and friends is in Paper 6; Locke's section on reasoning with children is in Paper 4; the passage in Book II of Rousseau's Emile in which the tutor holds the conditions is in Paper 5; Nietzsche's "go away from me" is in Paper 7; Freire's criticism of banking education is in Paper 2. Rousseau and Kant on education have already been interfaced with in Parent-Child Theory and Cultivation Theory,8 and are referred back to here. Montessori, Pestalozzi, Tagore and Wang Shouren, with whom Cultivation Theory Paper 4 interfaced, are likewise referred back to.

Material. Developmental psychology and research on education are not interfaced with, and are taken only as material: research on teacher expectations is in Paper 4. The historical material in each paper reads only how an adult author writes of the teacher of his own childhood: Platter in Paper 1, Keller in Paper 2, Lu Xun in Paper 3, Carnegie in Paper 4, Hamerton in Papers 5 and 7, Lewis in Paper 6, and Camus's letter to Germain in Paper 7. One further piece of material has a different standing and is listed separately: Germain's letter to Camus of 1959 is a teacher's supplementary account of himself, does not fall under this rule, and is also in Paper 7.

The interfacing has one way of writing in common: the tension within the original is read along with it, and it is not excerpted into a set of well-known sentences that all head one way. The "Record of Learning" discusses asking well, and also says that the young only listen and do not ask; Locke argues for reasoning, and also lists the adult's displeasure as a reason; "On Teachers" says that the teacher is not determined by age, and also separates the teaching of sentence divisions from what it calls passing on the Way and resolving doubts.

One more thing has to be written down. That the student has to check for himself, that the student is not a vessel to be filled, that the teacher too is changed by the student: for each of these the traditions have very strong statements. This series does not write these general claims as if it were the first to make them. Its own work lies in three places: the reading by domain; keeping the accounts of different positions separately; and how the back-and-forth sediments.

§6 The Directions of Colonization the Papers Read, Said Once Together

The directions of colonization the papers read are read in the papers; only one layer is said together here: what they borrow are things that are already there, or may form, between teacher and student. These things carry no direction in themselves; whether there is one depends on that one effect, not on the name of the act:

  • The student's standing: where "he doesn't understand yet" is used to lower his standing as an end, or to read all his questions and his "no" as signs of immaturity (Papers 1 and 3).
  • The student as a condition: where it can be read that the student figures only as a condition of a signboard, of results, of labor, or of continuation; this kind has been read, lies outside scope, and is only registered. Only teaching the course well is a different case: nothing of means has been read, it is not judged, and the series does not read it further (Paper 1).
  • Not being able to check: where the teacher's end creates or increases obstacles to checking, or goes on holding shut a particular path of checking; where "the teacher said so" is set up as a ground that cannot be refuted. The original gap carries no direction in itself (Paper 2).
  • The rightness of the content: where the rightness of the content is used in heading toward avowing for him (Paper 2).
  • The window: where the high plasticity of the window is used to force in the script the teacher wants. The occasion on which fear is taught in along with the content is read by what it actually wrote in, and is only registered (Paper 2).
  • Questions: where the heading is toward making the student able to ask only this one teacher; where a cost is added to the speaking up that pointed out a mistake (Paper 3).
  • Expectation: where an expectation is hung on what kind of person he is; where the teacher's approval has become the measure of the student's judgment. Expectation carries no direction in itself (Paper 4).
  • Dependency: where it was made, and has locked the path to saying no; the student's depending on the teacher to teach carries no direction in itself (Paper 4).
  • Another student: where one student is set up as the measure of another student's whole person (Paper 4).
  • The road the teacher chooses: where it is treated as the direction the student cannot but take, or he is treated as the one to carry on the line; the teacher's deciding what to teach and what to teach first is not, in itself, this (Paper 5).
  • Support: where both the difficulties and other people's objections are fended off on his behalf; the increase or decrease of a single item of support is not enough to establish it (Paper 5).
  • Not agreeing, and surpassing: where not agreeing is read as betrayal, and surpassing as the teacher's achievement (Paper 6).
  • The teaching received: where it is used to hold a share in the student's direction (Paper 7).

The same sentence "you don't understand yet," the same word of praise, the same page of exercises, is in one place a faithful description, a sound of gladness, an arrangement, and only in another place the lowering of standing, the replacing of a measure, the filling of time. The direction is read on the one effect in which they are put to some use; whether it took hold is seen in how the student understands and acts afterward.

Crossing the line is another line. The occasion on which the effect crosses the student's basic rights is violation; violation is judged only at the layer of basic rights, is read apart from the direction of colonization, and one does not jump from the one line to the other.

People are all mixed, and falling back is read on that occasion; whether this end can turn back after falling back is read separately, by the words and deeds that follow. What this series reads is direction; it does not on that account say that being a teacher is dangerous, nor advise anyone how to be a teacher; it only reads.

§7 Remainders

Several things this series registers and does not settle.

One, leading. The face of the teacher that the student sees is there all along; how it is passed on is a subject for after this series.

Two, one minor teaching another. Only registered.

Three, books, courses and tools as teacher. The teacher of this series is a person. Books written for many, and borrowing tools, Cultivation Theory has already read;9 the student's borrowing tools to check what the teacher taught is mentioned in Paper 3 in one sentence only.

Four, compulsion when the teacher temporarily looks after the student. Looking after a person who cannot yet say no cannot do without compulsion; this compulsion is not called cultivation, and belongs to the institutional side.3 The teacher-student relation is not in itself guardianship.

Five, between students. Dealings among fellow students are read by Relation Theory; an effect that reaches another student by way of the teacher is read as in Papers 3 and 4.

Six, the question of when a class becomes a collective subject. Upstream does not address it, and neither does this series.

Seven, teacher and student in the university. The side that is a construct lies on the institutional side.

Eight, after coming of age. The same teacher teaching an adult is read as between adults.

§8 Questions Left Open

Seven things the papers have argued only halfway, and they are listed here as they stand.

One, the two models borrowed are both hypotheses. The sequence on which "taking in first, checking later" relies the Learning series lists as a temporal hypothesis, and writes that direct experiments have not yet been seen; the direct-write window the original paper lists as a structural hypothesis. This series uses them within the limits of the original papers, and does not vouch for them; if these two places are rewritten, the relevant passages of Paper 2 will have to change with them. As soon as there is one content the student cannot at present check independently, the question at the level of the relation is already there; this point does not rest on these two models.

Two, the dividing line between the two entries. Paper 2 says that where he cannot check two entries are made, that the second entry has to account for three things, and that acting and falling short are kept apart, and gives the case of the answer pages cut out. What is left is: how, in particular material, to tell apart a gap in ability, conditions not given, what should have been given and was not, and the continued blocking of a path by means of an arrangement already in place. The things to be accounted for are there; the criteria item by item have not been argued.

Three, how far questioning of the teacher's judgment can go. Paper 2 says that how to teach this time is for the teacher to judge, and that this judgment is open to questioning. Who supervises, and how, has been handed to the institutional side, and is not counted as an argument this series owes. What remains in this series is the half at the level of the relation: how far questioning between the two can go while the student cannot yet say no has not been argued.

Four, one teacher and many students. Paper 5 reads by borrowing the cut of "creating conditions, and questioning"; in the original it divides a person who is present from one who has not yet come. How a shared arrangement facing many enters each relation separately has been read only in examples, and has not been argued.

Five, at the edge of scope. This series reads one kind only: where it can be read that the teacher heads toward treating the student as an end. Where it can be read that the student is treated merely as a means, the relation lies outside scope; where the course is only taught well, and where material is insufficient, there is no judgment, and no further reading. When what is not judged becomes readable, and on which side it comes out, has been given only in examples, and has not been argued. What is owed here is not an algorithm for assigning the undetermined to one side or the other.

Six, how "the matter" and "the domain" are fitted together. The teacher-student relation is determined by the matter taught; coming of age arrives by the domains of the matters of avowal. Paper 7 says that domains are drawn by the matters of avowal, and that a matter can be identified before the student's own direction in it has come to stand; how "the matters of avowal that bear on this relation" are identified has been given only in examples, and has not been argued.

Seven, extensions that borrow a shape. In several places this series carries an upstream proposition to an object the original does not speak of, each marked in its place as this series' own undertaking: the cut of "creating conditions, and questioning" from Marriage Theory, carried to one teacher and many students (Paper 5); superego internalization from the Learning series, used to read the measure of judgment (Paper 4); the third signal of Value Theory V38, used to read what is said in teaching (Paper 2); Conflict Treatise §15, carried to the teacher's judgment of one act of teaching (Paper 2); a distinction from Cooperation Theory and the deferral of Cultivation Theory HY4, carried to scope (Paper 1); the falling back and turning back of Parent-Child Theory PC1, carried to the teacher's end (Paper 1); Political Economy E36 and E37, carried to teacher and student (Paper 7). Whether the shape in these places carries over has not been separately argued.

§9 The Propositions of the Whole Series

The propositions of the papers are listed together as they stand in the main text of each paper, with only the final full stop added; the arguments and qualifications are in the main text of each paper. The parts still to be argued are not here; see §8.

Paper 1　The Teacher in a Relation

TS1　This series does not define all teacher-student relations. The teacher-student relation it reads has as its premise that the teacher's end is headed in the 15DD direction: across one matter after another, headed toward treating this student as an end, and not merely as a means. There is no teacher who stands purely at 15DD; the direction is read by one operation at a time, falling back is read as it occurs, and it does not overturn the direction; after falling back, this end can turn back; where there is persistent refusal to turn back, the direction is no longer at 15DD. Where it can be read that the student is treated merely as a means, that relation is not within the teacher-student relation this series reads. Where a teacher who teaches a course only teaches the course well, it cannot be read which kind this is; it is not judged, nor is it within the scope of this series: that is teaching, and teaching can carry no direction at all (TS8); a relation in which the course is only taught well is not what this series sets out to read. What cannot be read does not fall, by default, on the side of means.

TS2　The teacher is a party in a relation, not a post. Where, in some matter, one party teaches and the other learns, and the teaching actually enters the conditions of occurrence at the learner's end, there is a teacher-student relation in that matter; once is enough. It is read matter by matter: the same person is the teacher in this matter and may not be in another. Titles do not define it: where there is a title and teaching and learning do not actually occur, the series does not read it; where there is no title and they actually occur, it is read all the same. The main material of this series is repeated dealings; a single act of teaching does not fall by default into either kind, and it may precisely be heading toward cultivation.

TS3　Posts, schools and their provisions are acknowledged but not judged. Of the authority a construct gives, the source and the force are not judged; how the teacher uses it, and how it enters the dealings that follow, are read. The asymmetries this series reads are three: in knowledge and ability; not being able to check for the present; not yet being able to say no.

TS4　A teacher and each student form one relation apiece, entered on separate accounts, pair by pair. The series does not read a class or a school of followers as a further bearer, and does not address the question of when either becomes a collective subject. Among teacher, student and parents, the series reads only the relation between teacher and student; the parents' arrangements are registered only as conditions of this relation; where the parents' requirement reaches the student by way of the teacher, the teacher's passing it on is entered in the teacher's relation.

TS5　The student's standing as an end is full from the start, and is not conferred by the teacher; what he can do and how much he understands grow as he learns. The two books are kept apart: "he doesn't understand yet" at most describes what, in this matter, he cannot yet do; it does not lower his standing, nor is it used to read all his questions and his "no" as signs of immaturity.

TS6　Where the student never chose this teacher, that does not give the teacher the position of deciding his direction for him. Where the student cannot decide for himself to walk away, that does not place his asking, his refusing and his not avowing, in particular matters, at the teacher's disposal. Who made the arrangement is acknowledged but not judged: what belongs to the household is in Parent-Child Theory, what belongs to the school is on the institutional side. His no longer asking, his no longer learning, are words and deeds; where it can be read that he is taking back his own side, it is read as exit, and carries no direction of colonization. Silence, no longer asking, or going on doing as told do not by themselves show what he has taken back or what he has avowed; what has not been expressed in word or deed is not read. The teacher's end adds no cost to this taking back. Whether in his heart he takes this teacher as his teacher is a matter at his own end; the teacher does not demand it, and it is not used to settle accounts.

TS7　The object layer reads the relation in which the teacher's end is headed in the 15DD direction; at the operation layer the teacher confirms only his own end. Which layer the student has reached in some matter can, from the teacher's end, be seen only by clues.

Paper 2　Teaching

TS8　Teaching is passing knowledge, skill and predictive capacity on to the student. What teaching reaches has two layers: remembering, and having learned; remembering does not of itself lead on to having learned. Understanding is not given directly by teaching: that step he has to take himself, and what the teacher gives is conditions. Teaching can be designed, and can be done for many at once. Teaching can carry both directions, and can also carry no direction at all; that a skill has been learned does not by itself show which.

TS9　Taking in first, checking later. Some of what is taught the student cannot check for the present, and he takes it in first. Taking in has two layers: remembering, and having learned. Taking in is not holding it to be true, nor avowing it as his own. Understanding and checking lie on the other side, and need his own reflecting, asking and going back to look; the two advance back and forth, with neither necessarily coming first. Related material and a first model give checking somewhere to start; remembering alone has not prepared the conditions for the checking that comes later. This is a line through time: some of what is taught first enters his learning, and only later becomes something he can check for himself; it does not lay down that all teaching must be accepted first and examined afterward. Where the material itself can show right and wrong, it can be tested on the spot. Where he cannot check for the present, two entries are made: on the original gap alone, it is not read as carrying the direction of colonization; whether the teacher's end has created or increased obstacles to checking, or gone on holding shut a particular path of checking, is entered separately.

TS10　That the content is right does not by itself show that this teaching heads toward cultivation; to read its direction, one looks at what it actually opened or sealed. This paper reads the face of it that lies on the path of checking: whether the reasons, the sources and the way to test are laid out; whether the path by which he may later go back and look is left open; whether "the teacher said so" has been set up as a ground that cannot be refuted. Whether the content is right, and whether the path is left open, are entered separately and do not offset each other. Whether a condition of return is hung on the teaching, and whether his own share is left to him, are two further places, referred back to. Not yet having the ability to check does not count as the teacher's having sealed the path; "left open" requires particular conditions, not a sentence like "you'll understand when you're older."

TS11　"Indoctrination" in this series follows Rights Theory F21 and F26 and Relation Theory Paper 1: where the effect itself already writes for the student from the teacher's position and crowds out his own unfolding, it is indoctrination, it is violation, and it also carries the direction of colonization; this series reads only as far as "this is violation." Where it is still only pushing that way, and he can still take it and can also put it down, it is not called indoctrination; it carries only the direction of colonization and is not violation. The judgment lies in writing for him and crowding out, not in the kind of content written, nor in motive and feeling: that the content is right, that the teacher sincerely means him well, that the student feels he has benefited, none of these changes the reading. Hurried teaching, and teaching filled to the brim, are not the direction of cultivation; they are a direction that heads toward indoctrination. This is a sentence of direction at the normative layer, not a verdict on any one act of teaching. For this one student, whether this occasion counts as hurried or as filled to the brim, and how to teach this time, is for the teacher's end to judge by this student's situation; this series gives no algorithm. This judgment is not made at will: it is open to questioning, and is not exempt from being looked at again because it comes from the teacher himself; who supervises it, and how, belongs to the institutional side. The cell that crosses the line does not move with this judgment: what already writes for him from the teacher's position and crowds out his own unfolding is read, as upstream reads it, as violation, and the reading does not change with how the teacher describes himself. A teacher's saying what he himself judges of the student is not thereby heading this way; the difference lies in the form in which it is handed over.

TS12　In the stretch that is still within the direct-write window, situation and content are written in together when he is taught, and he can only take it in. The occasion on which fear is taught in along with the content is read by what it actually wrote in; it is not closed out on the strength of "he learned it." What this proposition borrows is the direct-write mode, kept within the limits the original paper sets.

TS13　Where the content was taught wrong, the teacher's end, as soon as it finds this, points it out and corrects it, and does not blame the student. Where he has already taken it in and used it, correcting it does not rewrite its having happened; the effects already left are entered as they are. What the teacher does not know, he says he does not know.

Paper 3　Checking

TS14　The student begins to scrutinize what he has taken in: he asks why, asks how you know, says that is not right. This is the beginning of his checking. One question does not by itself certify the emergence of 13DD; reading every question as talking back, inattention or immaturity is exactly what the second of the three signals guards against. What the teacher's end reads is whether his question can land: it is heard, not punished, not laughed at; and where the question reaches a place the teacher would rather not answer, it is not evaded. When one student's question is answered in front of other students, whoever that answer actually reaches, an entry is made in that one's relation.

TS15　A teacher's teaching in one matter is read by three signals: whether there are particular arrangements by which the student gradually becomes able to check for himself; whether his challenges are answered on their content and, where there is reason, really change what is said or how it is taught; whether the reasons for what is taught and the conditions under which it applies can be examined, or whether the teacher's identity as teacher has become a ground that cannot be refuted. A conclusion that already has sufficient proof can be taught as certain; its premises, its proof and its range of application remain open to examination. Where his objection is mistaken, and the teacher has answered it in full and the conclusion stands, his words have landed all the same: that something was changed on this occasion is not made the condition of his question's counting.

TS16　The methods, materials and exercises of checking can be taught; the conditions under which scrutiny and understanding happen can be given: there is something to scrutinize, a question can land, a mistake has somewhere to show itself, and there is room to stop. That act of scrutiny, that step of understanding, is still completed by the student himself. Where, in this matter, the student can already raise, refuse and revise for himself, that position becomes his own at once; it is not conferred by the teacher, and does not wait for his cannot-not to come to stand. Handing over means that the part the teacher had actually been exercising, arranging or holding on his behalf is withdrawn domain by domain, not all on one day. Heading toward making the student able to ask only this one teacher carries the direction of colonization.

TS17　Where the student finds that the teacher was wrong: it is entered that what was said was changed, that how it is taught was changed, not as anyone's winning or losing. The teacher's end does not on that account add disadvantage in other matters.

TS18　Once the student can check in this matter, the teacher may still teach. Being able to check is not having checked every time; how he takes in and how he scrutinizes from then on remain at his end. "He no longer asks me" is not taken as evidence that the teaching succeeded; "he keeps asking me" is not taken as evidence that it went wrong; whether he asks next time is only material.

Paper 4　Expectation

TS19　Expectation between teacher and student grows out of the dealings: what the teacher has praised and what he has frowned at, which time the student got it right and which time he did not, all become material. The teacher's expectation is a construct left at his own end; it does not turn into the student's duty, and does not certify that the student has avowed anything.

TS20　Saying an expectation out loud is a form of questioning. To hang an expectation on what kind of person the student is, for instance "a good student wouldn't do this" or "you have talent, you shouldn't be like this," is to fix for him what something means to him, and slides in the direction of colonization; whether that takes hold depends on his later understanding and action.

TS21　Compliance does not stand as proof of avowal: the student's saying "all right," doing as told, and doing it well are all only clues. Where the teacher's approval has become the measure of the student's own judgment, this is read by borrowing "superego internalization" from Learning III; from outside only clues are seen, and confirmation lies at his end.

TS22　The student's depending on the teacher to teach is the kind of dependency that is there in any case, and carries no direction of colonization. The kind that was made, and that has locked the path to saying no, for instance "without me you'll never learn it," or not letting him go to anyone else, carries the direction of colonization. The direction is to make him no longer held by this one teacher; this is only a direction, and is not used as a measure.

TS23　Where one student is used as the measure of another, for instance "look at so-and-so," what is read first is how the sentence lands with the student who hears it. If it also actually alters the situation of the one used as the measure, that relation is entered separately; the line is not drawn by whether he knows, nor is the effect entered as having reached him merely because his name was mentioned. Using a piece of homework to show one way of doing something, and setting up one student as the measure of another student's whole person, are read apart.

Paper 5　Space

TS24　The student's own purpose and his cannot-not cannot be taught; he has to chisel them out himself. What the teacher's end can do is give space: not suppress, and not block that signal. This is the 14DD stretch of the one thing that the tripartite division of the education paper specifically calls "nurturing"; the 13DD stretch is in Paper 3.

TS25　What is done facing many is creating conditions for cultivation. Creating conditions can face many; questioning is put to one person who can hear and can answer, and how one responds after he has answered falls in his relation. One act can reach many at once, and is read separately as it lands in each relation: withdrawing a requirement, giving out material, answering one student's question, can each act on several people at the same time. A shared arrangement cannot take the place of a response to a particular question, and one method cannot vouch for, or complete, the growing and the avowing of many. The teacher's time and strength are limited: whether the other is willing, whether it suits him, whether the moment is ripe; where these three do not all hold, or where he judges that this time he cannot give, not giving this time is not losing one's ground, and this judgment too can be questioned; nor is not giving the safer side in advance. Whether he would drain himself dry is for his own end to assess. Where the time in which everyone has class together is taken to give to this one, the other students' share is in it too.

TS26　The three forms of colonization at 14DD each have a shape between teacher and student. Direction substitution is treating the road of study or of life that the teacher has chosen for the student as the direction he himself cannot but take, or treating him as the one to carry on the teacher's own line; the teacher's deciding what to teach and what to teach first is not, in itself, this. Superego internalization: see TS21. Full-support resistance removal is fending off, on his behalf, both the difficulties and other people's objections; the increase or decrease of a single item of support is not enough to establish it. The student does not figure only as the teacher's continuation.

TS27　Cultivation in this stretch is negating a false cannot-not, not implanting a direction. The teacher can withdraw a requirement he himself added, and say "you need not," not "you should not"; which cannot-not is false is for the student to judge.

TS28　The teacher does not turn every time together into an occasion for growth. The student may refuse to be treated continually as someone yet to grow.

Paper 6　Collision

TS29　At the 14DD turn, the student's own cannot-not crystallizes and begins to meet the teacher's law and the teacher's plans for him. Rebellion is not the mechanism by which 14DD comes about; it is often its behavioral by-product.

TS30　In this stretch, the teacher's end heading toward 15DD in one operation is read, in this matter, as: acknowledging that this cannot-not is the student's own, even where, as the teacher sees it, that direction will fail. The teacher may state his disagreement in full; he does not hand it over as a piece of self-knowledge the student has already completed.

TS31　Mutual chiseling is judged by process, not by identity: where teacher and student, in some matter, enter one and the same reciprocal process and actually chisel open each other's judgments, there is mutual chiseling in that matter. The matter in which the teacher is chiseled open by the student is read by the process on that occasion. That there is mutual chiseling does not certify that the student is already headed in the 15DD direction.

TS32　Where the requirement at the student's end and the teacher's law require incompatible actions in the same situation: whether that matter enters the Conflict Treatise is checked item by item against its two gates, and is not settled by the teacher-student identity or by incompatibility itself. What does not enter is read as dealings and collision.

TS33　The student's not agreeing with the teacher in some matter, or surpassing him: this is not read as betrayal, nor is it used to certify that this relation heads toward cultivation or that the teacher's end is headed in the 15DD direction. That this skill was taught by this teacher is entered as fact. "The blue that comes from the indigo plant" is not used as the measure of this relation.

Paper 7　Handing Over

TS34　This series reads up to the student's coming of age: read as in Parent-Child Theory, by the person's domains, arriving domain by domain. Domains are drawn by the matters of avowal, not by the subjects taught, and a further domain is not drawn because he meets a problem he cannot yet do; a matter can be identified before the student's own direction in it has come to stand. Being able to check is not this boundary (TS16, TS18). If the relation ends before the student has come of age in the matters of avowal that bear on it, it is read by TS37; a relation is not kept on the near side of coming of age merely because what was taught has not become the student's own cannot-not. On the side that has come of age, what follows is read as giving and dealings between adults, and the same teacher may still teach him. Graduation, completing an apprenticeship, and coming of age in law are acknowledged but not judged. This is the boundary of how far this series reads; it does not say that teacher-student relations in reality stop here.

TS35　The test lies on the student's side: he is able to unfold without revolving around this one teacher. This is a test; it is not that he must in fact leave, nor that from now on he needs no one. What this test looks at is whether the teacher has occupied the student's own constitutive position; "no longer revolving around the teacher" is not used to certify that his cannot-not has come to stand, nor to settle the account of the teacher's teaching.

TS36　The student's thanks, his achievement, his staying are not entered as repayment for the teaching, and are not used to settle accounts; the teacher does not, for having taught, hold a share in the student's direction. This proposition settles only to whom the student's direction belongs; tuition fees and credit for the teacher's contribution are reckoned separately. One may remember, and one may be glad for him.

TS37　Where the relation ends before the student comes of age: the teacher leaves, the student leaves, the teacher is changed. Exit does not settle the accounts, nor pass final judgment on this person; leaving the shared matters between the two room for natural development when one exits points in the direction of cultivation. What the teacher had meant to finish teaching does not on that account become a shared matter that remains after he has gone; what has been taught stays in the student's hands, for him to use as it is, to change, or to put down. Who arranged the change is acknowledged but not judged.

TS38　What lies between these two people from then on is read by Relation Theory, Cooperation Theory and Cultivation Theory. The face of how the teacher himself lives, as seen by the student, is handed to the series on how cultivation is passed on; the stretch in the university, on its side as a construct, is handed to the Theory of the University.

§10 Drawing Together

This paper sets up no propositions, and proves no proposition at the object layer. What it says is: Teacher-Student Theory is on the relational side, follows Parent-Child Theory, and takes up the half that Cultivation Theory handed on; it does not define all teacher-student relations, and reads the teacher-student relation whose premise is that the teacher's end is headed in the 15DD direction; people are all mixed, falling back is read as it occurs, and after falling back this end can turn back; the relation in which it can be read that the student is treated merely as a means is not within scope, and where the course is only taught well there is no judgment, nor is the relation within scope; the teacher is a party in a relation, determined by the matter; the series reads relation by relation, up to the student's coming of age. The main line is: some of what the teacher teaches he cannot test for the present, and he takes it in first; working out why, and going back to see whether it is right, he has to do himself, and the teacher gives him the time for this and also ways to examine and test what he has been taught; where he himself is going, the teacher does not decide for him. The directions of colonization the papers read borrow things that are already there, or may form, between teacher and student; the direction is read on the one effect in which they are put to some use.

摘要

这一篇是师生论的 Paper 0，最后写，最先放。它不立命题，只为读这个系列的人做几件事：说明这个系列在 SAE 里的位置，写明它读到哪里、主线是什么，交代各篇共用的读法与同字的词，与几家传统作总对接，把各篇读到的殖民方向合说一次，合列全系列已经立下的三十八条命题，登记留下来不结的余项与仍待论证的问题。

师生论在 SAE 的关系一侧，接在亲子论之后；它承的是涵育论交出来的那一半：两个人之间的来回。这个系列不给一切师生关系下定义；它读的师生关系，以老师那一端在 15DD 方向上为前提：在一件件事上，朝把这个学生当作目的那一面去，而不只把他当作手段；人都是混合的，回落照读，回落了能回过头来。读得出学生只被当作手段的，不在射程里；教课的人只把课教好的，读不出是哪一种，不判，也不在射程里。老师是关系里的一方，按事定，不是职位，也不是名分；学校与大学在制度一侧。学生写到成年。主线的平实说法是：老师教的，有些他当下验不了，先接下来：记住，会做。弄明白为什么，回头查对不对，要他自己来；老师给他这个工夫，也给他查的办法。他自己要往哪里去，老师不替他定。七篇依次读：关系里的老师；教；核对；期待；空间；碰撞；交出。

这一篇与《论语》、《礼记·学记》、韩愈、荀子、朱熹、柏拉图、奥古斯丁、洛克、卢梭、尼采、弗莱雷作总对接；发展心理学与教育研究不作对接，只作材料。各篇读到的殖民方向，借的是师生之间已经在或可能形成的件：学生的地位、核对不了、内容的对、问、期待、依赖、老师选的路、支持、教过的恩。这几件本身不带方向，方向在它们被拿来做什么的那一次作用上读。这一篇不证明任何对象层的命题。

引言

后面七篇各读一件。第一篇《关系里的老师》读这个系列所说的师生关系是什么样子：射程，老师按事定，规定与权限，每一段，学生那一端的两本账，他没有选过、不能走开，对象层与操作层。第二篇《教》读教这件事本身：教到的两层，先接下、后核对，教的方向读在哪里，教导与灌输，一同写进去的东西，教错了。第三篇《核对》读学生开始审视接下来的东西以后：他的问，三个信号，给得了的与替不了的，学生查出老师错了，会核对以后。第四篇《期待》读一来一回里长出来的东西：期待，把期待挂到「你是什么样的人」上，顺从不代证认领，依赖的两义，拿一个学生作另一个的尺子。第五篇《空间》读学生自己在意的东西冒出来以后：教不了的那一件，一位老师与许多学生，三种形态，否定假的不得不，不把每一次相处都安排成成长。第六篇《碰撞》读学生自己的不得不与老师的法相遇：老师那一端的样子，互凿，冲突论的门槛，不认同与超过。第七篇《交出》读最后一段：成年这条界，检验在学生那一侧，教过不记作偿还，提前结束，此后归谁读。

这样写下来，有几件事没有一篇来管：这个系列为什么在这里，读到哪里为止；主线怎样把七篇串起来；各篇共用的读法，同一个字在哪一篇是什么意思；有几家传统，问的是全系列；各篇读到的殖民方向放在一起是什么样子；哪些问题仍待论证。这一篇管这几件。

写法同各篇：上游的结论用平实的话说，出处与推及放在注释。这一篇不立命题，不改动后面各篇的任何一句。上游交给这个系列的各件怎样落址，放在篇末的附录里。

第一节 位置

SAE 有三侧：主体侧，关系侧，制度侧。关系侧由关系论展开，下游已经有爱情论、婚姻论、合作论、亲子论。师生论是关系侧的又一支，接在亲子论之后。

两层的分工，关系论写明了：抽象的关系论只立所有关系都具备的；只在某一种关系里成立的，交那一种关系的系列论证。1 关系一层已经立下的，这个系列直接用，不重写：关系的起码条件，涵育与殖民两个方向，期待，来往的沉积，不对称，结束。

与涵育论。 涵育论从给的一方那一端写一次作为；两个人之间的来回，它不展开，并写明场合归写人与人之间的那一侧。2 师生论接的就是这一半：问进来了，老师怎样回，回了以后改变了学生下一次发问的什么条件；夸过一次以后，期待怎样长；教过的东西在他手里变成什么；这段关系怎样到头。涵育论已经立下的，这个系列回指，不重写；只把上游的命题换成老师与学生作例子，不是这个系列要做的事。

与亲子论。 父母教自己的孩子，在亲子论里读，这个系列不进。两个系列用同一条阶段轴，同一条成年的界。不同在骨架：亲子论借制度侧的两层，读保障与支持；师生之间没有保障那一层的主业，老师不担学生的基础层，主业是教。所以这个系列的骨架是另一条：先接下，后核对。

与关系论的例子。 关系论第五篇读过厂里一位老技工与他的学徒；那是关系论的例子，这个系列回指，不重读。

与制度一侧。 学校与大学是构。评分、升学、分班、学制、聘任，是构的规定，这个系列认而不判；构给老师的权限，来源与效力不判。监护归制度侧；3 老师的作用越过学生基础权利的那一次，这个系列读到「这是侵」，侵以后的保护与处置在制度侧。教课这个位置带来什么责任，也在制度侧。大学不在这个系列里，交制度侧。

与发展一侧的几篇。 SAE 已经从发展与学习的一侧读过人怎样长：教育的那一篇分出教、涵育、引领；Terrible Twos、Terrible Teens、Terrible Telos 三篇读自意识、目的、承认的发生；学习系列读各层怎样学。4 这个系列不重读这些，在用到处调用；它读的是老师那一端怎样作用，学生长到哪一步，只作这一端作用时的条件。

与这个系列之后的题目。 老师自己怎样过、怎样待人，被学生看在眼里，教育那一篇把它叫作引领。这一面一直在；它怎样传下去，被教过的人后来怎样教别人，涵育在人与人之间怎样传，是这个系列之后的题目，这里只登记。

第二节 射程与主线

射程。 有七条。

一，不给一切师生关系下定义。说一段关系不在这个系列读的师生关系里，不是说它不是师生关系（第一篇 TS1）。

二，老师那一端在 15DD 方向上。在 SAE 的层级里，认对方也是目的的那一层，记作 15DD。5 这个系列读的师生关系，以老师那一端在这个方向上为前提：在一件件事上，朝把这个学生当作目的那一面去，而不只把他当作手段。方向是前提，不是师生关系的组分。读得出学生只被当作手段的，这一段不在射程里。教课的人只把课教好的，读不出是哪一种，不判，也不在射程里：那是教，教可以什么方向都不带；只把课教好的这一段，不是这个系列要读的。读不出的，不默认落在手段一边。这个系列不往下读的有两样：读得出只当手段的，是读出来的；只把课教好的，与材料不够的，是没有读出来；带着一项读数的只有前一样（第一篇 TS1）。

三，方向，回落，与回头。没有纯在 15DD 上的老师，人都是混合的；方向按一次运作读，回落照读，不推翻方向。回落了，这一端能回过头来；坚持不回头的，方向已不在 15DD 上。反省从言行里读；它不撤销已读出的侵，也不使学生欠下原谅（第一篇 TS1）。

四，老师是关系里的一方。按事定：一方教，另一方学，教实际进入了学的那一端的发生条件，这一件事上就有师生关系；一次也是。名分不定义它（第一篇 TS2）。

五，每一段。一位老师与每一个学生各成一段，两两记账；不把一个班、一门当作另一个承担者来读。老师、学生、父母之间，只读老师与学生这一段（第一篇 TS4）。

六，学生写到成年。成年读作他自己的不得不成型了，一域一域到；域照认领的事项划，不照所教的科目划。前台不写年龄与年级，也不拿毕业、出师作界（第七篇 TS34）。

七，对象层与操作层。对象层读射程里的那一段；操作层老师只确认自己这一端，学生那一端只见线索（第一篇 TS7）。

主线。 平实的说法是：

老师教的，有些他当下验不了，先接下来：记住，会做。弄明白为什么，回头查对不对，要他自己来；老师给他这个工夫，也给他查的办法。他自己要往哪里去，老师不替他定。

骨架是「先接下，后核对」。四样东西，分在两侧。接下的一侧有两层：记住，与学会。另一侧也是两样：理解，与核对，都是他自己回头审视已经接下的东西。教到的是前一侧；后一侧要他自己走，老师给的是条件（第二篇 TS8、TS9；第三篇 TS16）。

这是一条历时的线，不是一道规定：有些教来的东西先进入他的学习，后来才成为他能自行核对的对象；它不说一切教都得先接受、后检查。接下不是认作真的，也不是认领。这个系列写「接下」，不写「信」。

主线的后一句管方向：学生自己的目的教不了，老师这一端给空间，不替他定（第五篇 TS24；第七篇 TS36）。

不对称，读三样。 师生之间的不对称，这个系列读三样，都不是构给的权限：知识与能力上的；当下核对不了；还不能说不。不对称本身不是越线（第一篇 TS3；第二篇 TS9）。

阶段轴。 用学生的相变作一条轴。SAE 的层级里，记忆记作 11DD，预测记作 12DD，自意识记作 13DD，目的记作 14DD。下表是一种次序的示例，不是格子，也不是定义；前台不写年龄。各篇只写这条轴在本篇那件事上怎样走。

例子。 自设例为主：方叙教邻居家的孩子陆苗数学，放在校外，没有聘任，没有分数；郑师傅带三个少年学木工，在第一至第五篇出场。学生出场只写言行。史料只用已故的成年人写下、已经公开的自己的童年，只读成年的作者怎样写。例子里对照的话，不由话的样式独自判方向：那一次朝哪里去，照当时实际打开或封住了什么读；后来沉积成什么，另读，不拿来倒判当时；材料不足，停在未定。

规范层只给方向。 这个系列有几句方向句，例如「教得急、教得满，不是涵育的方向」。方向句本身不替具体的判断：不凭这一句判哪一次教。对这一个学生这一次怎么算，由老师这一端照他的情况判断，这个系列不给算法；这一判要受追问（第二篇 TS11）。

「灌输」一词，上游另有几处用法，各在原处读，不只凭同字补出侵或不是侵的判断：涵育论前置篇里「老师把正确的知识灌输给学生」一句，承权利论，落在借内容的对、替对方认下这一处，它原是一喻；学习系列另用「殖民性灌输」；涵育论引解论语的一句，「不当说而说，是灌输；当说而不说，是废教」。7 「理解」一词，上游也另有几处：学习系列第一篇里带引号的一处，第四篇的「理解他者」，关系论里「对方此后的理解与行动」，各是各的。

第五节 总对接

各篇各自对接了一家或几家；这里合说。

先秦与儒家。 《论语》论教的几章：三人行，有教无类，自行束脩，不愤不启，当仁不让于师，求也退故进之，知之为知之；SAE 的解论语已注，各篇回指。《礼记·学记》论问与待问、「道而弗牵」、「幼者听而弗问」、「教学相长」，分在第三、四、六篇。《荀子·劝学》的「青，取之于蓝」，在第六篇。韩愈《师说》在第一、六篇；朱熹分小学与大学，在第二篇。《论语》各章的对接，这里只读结构。

西方。 柏拉图《美诺》里的那一段几何，在第二篇作边界的例子；奥古斯丁《论教师》在第三篇；亚里士多德论真理与朋友的一段，在第六篇；洛克向孩子说理的一节，在第四篇；卢梭《爱弥儿》第二卷里导师掌着条件的一段，在第五篇；尼采的「离开我」，在第七篇；弗莱雷批评储蓄式的教育，在第二篇。卢梭与康德论教育，亲子论与涵育论已经对过，8 这里回指。涵育论第四篇对过的蒙台梭利、裴斯泰洛齐、泰戈尔与王守仁，也回指。

材料。 发展心理学与教育研究不作对接，只作材料：教师期待的研究在第四篇。史料各篇都只读成年的作者怎样写自己小时候的老师：普拉特在第一篇，凯勒在第二篇，鲁迅在第三篇，卡内基在第四篇，哈默顿在第五、七篇，刘易斯在第六篇，加缪写给热尔曼的信在第七篇。另有一份身份不同的材料，单列：热尔曼 1959 年写给加缪的信，是老师的补充自述，不在这一口径里，也在第七篇。

对接有一条共同的写法：连原文里的张力一起读，不摘成一组同向的名句。《学记》既论善问，也说年少的只听不问；洛克既主张说理，也把大人的不悦列作理由；《师说》既说师不按长幼定，也把句读之教与其所说的传道解惑分开。

还有一句要写明。学生要自己核对，学生不是装东西的容器，老师也被学生改变：这几样，传统里都有很强的说法。这个系列不把这些一般的主张写成自己首次提出。它自己的工作在三处：分域的读法；不同的位置分开记账；一来一回怎样沉积。

第六节 各篇读到的殖民方向，合说一次

各篇读出的殖民方向，读法在各篇，这里只合说一层：它们借的，是师生之间已经在或可能形成的件。这几件本身不带方向；带不带，看那一次作用，不看动作的名称：

  • 学生的地位：拿「他还不懂」降他作为目的的地位，或把他的问与「不」一律读作不懂事时（第一篇、第三篇）。
  • 学生作为条件：读得出学生只作为招牌、成绩、劳力或延续的条件时；这一种是读出来的，在射程以外，只登记。只把课教好的是另一样：没有读出手段，不判，这个系列也不往下读（第一篇）。
  • 核对不了：老师这一端制造或加重核对的障碍，或继续压住具体的核对路径时；把「老师说的」立成不可反驳的根据时。原有的差距本身不带方向（第二篇）。
  • 内容的对：借内容的对，朝替他认下去时（第二篇）。
  • 窗口：借窗口的高可塑性强行写入老师想要的脚本时。把害怕一同教进去的那一次，照它实际写入了什么来核，只登记（第二篇）。
  • 问：朝着使学生只能问这一位老师去时；给指出错的那一声追加代价时（第三篇）。
  • 期待：把期待挂到他是什么样的人上时；老师的赞许成了他判断的尺子时。期待本身不带方向（第四篇）。
  • 依赖：被造出来、又锁住了说不之路时；学生依赖老师教，本身不带方向（第四篇）。
  • 另一个学生：把一个学生立成另一个学生整个人的尺度时（第四篇）。
  • 老师选的路：把它当成学生非如此不可的方向，或把他当作传衣钵的人时；老师定教什么、先教什么，本身不是（第五篇）。
  • 支持：把难处与别人的异议都替他挡掉时；单凭一项支持的增减，不足以认定（第五篇）。
  • 不认同与超过：把不认同读作背叛，把超过读作老师的成绩时（第六篇）。
  • 教过的恩：拿它在学生的方向里占一份时（第七篇）。

同一句「你还不懂」，同一声夸，同一页习题，在一处是如实的描述、一声高兴、一项安排，在另一处才是地位的降低、尺子的替换、时间的填满。方向在它们被拿来做什么的那一次作用上读；成没成，看学生此后怎样理解、怎样做。

越线是另一条线。作用越过学生基础权利的那一次，是侵；侵只在基础权利一层判，与殖民这个方向分开读，不从一条线跳到另一条线。

人都是混合的，回落照那一次读；回落以后能不能回过头来，另照此后的言行读。这个系列读的是方向；它不因此说做老师危险，也不劝人怎样做老师；它只读。

第七节 余项

几件事，这个系列登记，不结。

一，引领。老师被学生看在眼里的那一面一直在；它怎样传下去，是这个系列之后的题目。

二，一个未成年人教另一个未成年人。只登记。

三，书、课程与工具作老师。这个系列的老师是一个人。写给众人的书，借工具，涵育论已经读过；9 学生借工具核对老师教的，第三篇只提了一句。

四，老师临时照看学生时的强制。照看一个还不能说不的人，免不了强制；这份强制不叫涵育，归制度侧。3 师生这一关系本身不等于监护。

五，学生之间。同学之间的来往照关系论读；经过老师到了另一个学生那里的作用，照第三、四篇读。

六，一个班什么时候成了集体的主体。上游没有碰，这个系列也不碰。

七，大学里的师生。构的一面，在制度侧。

八，成年以后。同一位老师教一个成年人，照成年人之间读。

第八节 留下的问题

七件，各篇只论到一半，这里如实列出。

一，所借的两个模型都是假说。「先接下，后核对」所靠的先后，学习系列列为时序假说，并写明直接的实验还没有见到；直写窗口，原篇列为结构假说。这个系列照原篇的限度用，不替它们作证；这两处若被改写，第二篇的相关段落要跟着改。只要有一项内容学生目前还不能独立核对，关系上的问题就已经有了；这一点不压在这两个模型上。

二，两笔账的分界。第二篇说，核对不了记两笔，第二笔要交代三件，并分开动手与不及，给了裁掉答案页一例。留下的是：在具体的材料里，怎样分开能力上的差距、条件没有给、该给而没有给，与借现成的安排持续堵住一条路。要交代的事项有了，逐项的判据没有论证。

三，老师的判断受追问，能做到哪一步。第二篇说，这一次怎样教由老师判断，这一判要受追问。由谁监督、怎样监督，已经交给制度一侧，不算这个系列欠着的论证。留在这个系列里的是关系上的一半：学生还不能说不的时候，两个人之间的追问能做到哪一步，没有论证。

四，一位老师与许多学生。第五篇借「创造条件，与追问」这一刀来读；原处分的是一个在场的人与一个还没有来的人。面向许多人的共同安排怎样分别进入各段关系，只在例子里读过，没有论证。

五，射程的边上。这个系列只读一种：读得出老师朝把学生当作目的那一面去的。读得出学生只被当作手段的，在射程以外；只把课教好的，与材料不够的，不判，也不往下读。不判的什么时候读得出来、从哪一边出去，只给了例子，没有论证。这里欠的不是一套把未定分到某一边的算法。

六，「事」与「域」怎样对上。师生关系按所教的事定，成年按认领事项的域到。第七篇说域照认领的事项划，事项可以在还没有站住的时候被指认；「与这一段有关的认领事项」怎样指认，只给了例子，没有论证。

七，借形的推及。这个系列有几处把上游的一条用到原处没有说的对象上，各在当处标明自担：婚姻论「创造条件，与追问」那一刀，用到一位老师与许多学生上（第五篇）；学习系列的超我内化，用来读判断的尺子（第四篇）；价值论 V38 的第三个信号，用来读教的说法（第二篇）；冲突论 §十五，用到老师对一次教的判断上（第二篇）；合作论的一处区分、涵育论 HY4 的暂缓，用到射程上（第一篇）；亲子论 PC1 的回落与回头，用到老师那一端（第一篇）；政治经济学 E36、E37，用到老师与学生之间（第七篇）。这几处的形状是不是带得过来，没有另作论证。

第九节 全系列命题

各篇的命题，照各篇正文的命题原句合列，只在句末补句号；论证与限定在各篇正文。仍待论证的部分不在这里，见第八节。

第一篇　关系里的老师

TS1　这个系列不给一切师生关系下定义。它读的师生关系，以老师那一端在 15DD 方向上为前提：在一件件事上，朝把这个学生当作目的那一面去，而不只把他当作手段。没有纯在 15DD 上的老师；方向按一次运作读，回落照读，不推翻方向；回落了，这一端能回过头来；坚持不回头的，方向已不在 15DD 上。读得出学生只被当作手段的，这一段不在这个系列读的师生关系里。教课的老师只把课教好的，读不出是哪一种，不判，也不在这个系列的射程里：那是教，教可以什么方向都不带（TS8）；只把课教好的这一段，不是这个系列要读的。读不出的，不默认落在手段一边。

TS2　老师是关系里的一方，不是一个职位。在某一件事上，一方教，另一方学，教实际进入了学的那一端的发生条件，这一件事上就有师生关系；一次也是。它按事读：同一个人在这件事上是老师，在另一件事上可以不是。名分不定义它：有名分而教与学没有实际发生的，这个系列不读；没有名分而实际发生的，照读。这个系列的主材料是反复的来往；一次教不默认落在哪一种里，它恰恰可能是涵育的。

TS3　职位、学校与它们的规定，认而不判。构给的权限，来源与效力不判；老师怎样用它、它怎样进入此后的来往，照读。这个系列读的不对称有三样：知识与能力上的，当下核对不了，还不能说不。

TS4　一位老师与每一个学生各成一段，两两记账。这个系列不把一个班、一门当作另一个承担者来读；它什么时候成了集体的主体，不碰。老师、学生、父母之间，这个系列只读老师与学生这一段；父母的安排只作这一段的条件登记；父母的要求经过老师到了学生那里的，老师转过去的那一下记在老师这一段。

TS5　学生作为目的的地位，从一开始就是满的，不由老师授予；他会什么、懂多少，随学随长。两本账分开记：「他还不懂」，至多描述他在这件事上还做不到什么，不降他的地位，也不拿来把他的问与「不」一律读作不懂事。

TS6　学生没有选过这位老师的，这不使老师取得替他定方向的位置。学生不能自己决定走开的，这不使他在具体事项上的发问、回绝与不认领归老师支配。是谁安排的，认而不判：家里的在亲子论，学校的在制度侧。他不再问、不再学，是言行；读得出是他收回自己这一侧的，照退出读，不带殖民这个方向。单凭沉默、不再问或继续照做，不独自说明他收回了什么、认领了什么；没有以言行表出来的，不读。老师这一端不给这种收回追加代价。他心里认不认这位老师，是他那一端的事，不由老师要求，也不拿来结算。

TS7　对象层读老师那一端在 15DD 方向上的那一段；操作层老师只确认自己这一端。学生在某一件事上到了哪一层，从老师这一端只见线索。

第二篇　教

TS8　教，是把知识、技艺与预测能力传给学生。教到的有两层：记住，与学会；记住不自动通向学会。理解不由教直接给到：那一步要他自己走，老师给的是条件。教可以设计，可以对许多人同时做。教可以两向都有，也可以什么方向都不带；学会了一样本事，不独自说明哪一向。

TS9　先接下，后核对。教来的东西，有些学生当下核对不了，先接下来。接下分两层：记住，与学会。接下不是认作真的，也不是认领为自己的。理解与核对在另一侧，要他自己反思、提问、回头查；两样来回推进，不分先后。相关的素材与初步的模型，使核对有了着手处；单有记住，并没有替后来的核对备好条件。这是一条历时的线：有些教来的东西先进入他的学习，后来才成为他能自行核对的对象；它不规定一切教都得先接受、后检查。材料自己给得出对错的，当下就验得了。当下核对不了，记两笔：单凭原有的差距，不判殖民这个方向；老师这一端有没有制造或加重核对的障碍，或继续压住具体的核对路径，另记。

TS10　内容对，不独自说明这一次教朝涵育去；读它的方向，看它实际打开或封住了什么。这一篇读它在核对的路上的那一面：理由、出处、怎样验，摆不摆出来；他日后回头查的路，留没留着；「老师说的」有没有被立成不可反驳的根据。内容对不对，与路留没留着，分开记，不相抵。教上挂没挂回报的条件，他自己那一份留没留，是另两处，回指。暂时还没有本事核对，不算老师封了路；「留着」要有具体的条件，不是一句「长大就懂了」。

TS11　这个系列的「灌输」，照权利论 F21、F26 与关系论第一篇：作用本身已经以老师的位置替学生书写，挤占了他自己的展开，是灌输，是侵，也带殖民这个方向；这个系列只读到「这是侵」。还只是朝那里推，他仍接得住，也放得下的，不叫灌输，只带殖民这个方向，不是侵。判在替他书写与挤占，不在所写内容的类，也不在动机与感受：内容对，老师真心为他好，学生觉得受益，都不改读数。教得急、教得满，不是涵育的方向，是朝灌输那边去的方向。这是规范层的方向句，不是对哪一次教下的判。对这一个学生，这一次算不算急、算不算满，这一次怎样教，由老师这一端照这个学生的情况判断，这个系列不给算法。这一判不是随便判：它要受追问，也不因出自他本人就免于回看；由谁监督、怎样监督，归制度侧。越线的那一格不随这一判移动：已经以老师的位置替他书写、挤占了他自己展开的，照上游读作侵，不因老师怎样自述而改变读数。老师说出自己对他的判断，不就是朝这边去；分别在交过去的样子。

TS12　仍在直写窗口的那一段，教的时候情境与内容一同写入，他只能接下。把害怕一同教进去的那一次，照它实际写入了什么来核；不凭「他学会了」结清。这一条借的是直写模式，照原篇的限度保留。

TS13　内容教错了，老师这一端一经发现就指出、改正，不怪学生。他已经接下去用了的，改正了，也不改写它发生过；已经留下的作用照记。老师不知道的，说不知道。

第三篇　核对

TS14　学生开始审视接下来的东西：问为什么，问你怎么知道，说不对。这是他核对的开始。一次发问不独自认证 13DD 涌现；把问一律读成顶嘴、不专心、还不懂，正是三个信号里第二个所挡的。老师这一端读的是他的问落不落得下：被听见，不受罚，不被笑；问到老师不想答的地方，不回避。当着别的学生回一个学生的问，这一回答实际到了谁那里，谁那一段记一笔。

TS15　读一位老师在一件事上的教，照三个信号：有没有使学生逐渐能够自行核对的具体安排；他的质疑有没有按内容得到回应，并在有理由时真改动说法或教法；所教内容的理由与适用条件能不能受查，还是老师的身份成了不可反驳的根据。已经有充分证明的结论，可以确定地教；它的前提、证明与适用范围仍可受查。他的反驳是错的，老师答足了、结论照留，他的话也落下了：不以这一次改了什么，作他发问有效的条件。

TS16　查验的办法、材料与练习可以教；使审视与理解发生的条件可以给：有东西可审，问落得下，错处有地方显出来，有停下来的空。那一次审视、那一步理解，仍由学生自己完成。学生在这件事上已经能自行提出、回绝与修订的，那一位置即刻归他本人，不由老师授予，也不等他的不得不站住。交出，说的是老师先前实际代行、安排或把持的那一部分，一域一域撤出，不是某一天一起撤。朝着使学生只能问这一位老师去的，带殖民这个方向。

TS17　学生查出老师错了：记说法被改了、教法被改了，不记作谁输谁赢。老师这一端不因此在别的事上追加不利。

TS18　学生在这件事上会核对了，老师仍可以教。会核对，不等于每一次都已核对；此后怎样接下、怎样审视，仍在他这一端。「他不再问我」不作教成了的证据；「他一直问我」也不作教坏了的证据；他下一次还问不问，只作材料。

第四篇　期待

TS19　师生之间的期待从来往里长出来：老师夸过什么、对什么皱眉，学生哪一次做对、哪一次没做到，都成为材料。老师的期待是他这一端留下的构，不落成学生的义务，也不认证学生认领了什么。

TS20　说出期待是追问。把期待挂到学生是什么样的人上，例如「好学生不会这样」「你是有天分的，不该这样」，是替他定下了一件事对他意味着什么，滑向殖民的方向；成没成，看他此后的理解与行动。

TS21　顺从不代证认领：学生说「好」、照做、做得好，都只是线索。老师的赞许成了他自己判断的尺子的，借学习导论三的「超我内化」读；从外面只见线索，确认在他那一端。

TS22　学生依赖老师教，是本来就有的那一种依赖，不带殖民这个方向。被造出来、又锁住了说不之路的那一种，例如「离了我你学不成」、不让他去找别人，带殖民这个方向。方向是让他不再受制于这一位老师；这只是方向，不作尺子。

TS23　拿一个学生作另一个学生的尺子，例如「你看人家」，先读这句话怎样落到听这句话的那一个学生那里。若也实际改动了被拿来作尺子的那一个的处境，那一段另记；不以他知不知道为界，也不只凭名字被提到，就记成作用已经到了他那里。用一份作业示范一种做法，与把一个学生立成另一个学生整个人的尺度，分开读。

第五篇　空间

TS24　学生自己的目的与不得不，教不了，得由他自己凿出来。老师这一端能做的是给空间：不压制，不挡住那个信号。这是教育篇三分里专门叫作「涵育」的那一件的 14DD 一段；13DD 一段在第三篇。

TS25　面向许多人做的那一下，是为涵育创造条件。创造条件可以面向许多人；追问是对着一个听得见、答得了的人问，他答了以后怎样回，落在他那一段。一个动作可以同时到许多人那里，落到每一段各读：撤回一个要求，给出材料，回一个学生的问，都可能同时作用于几个人。共同的安排不能代替对具体问题的回应，一个方法也不能替许多人担保或完成各自的生长与认领。老师的时间与心力有限：对方愿不愿意、适不适合、时机成没成，三样不齐，或者判断自己这一次给不了，这一次不给不是失守，这一判也可以被问；不给，也不是预先更安全的一边。会不会把自己耗干，由他这一端评估。拿大家一起上课的时间去给这一个，别的学生那一份也在里面。

TS26　14DD 上殖民的三种形态，在师生之间各有样子。方向替代，是把老师替学生选定的学习或生活的路，当成他本人非如此不可的方向，或把他当作传自己衣钵的人；老师定教什么、先教什么，本身不是。超我内化，见 TS21。全支持式阻力清除，是把难处与别人的异议都替他挡掉；单凭一项支持的增减，不足以认定。学生不只作为老师的延续。

TS27　这一段的涵育，是否定假的不得不，不是植入方向。老师可以撤回自己加上去的要求，说「不必」，不说「你不该」；哪一条不得不是假的，由学生判。

TS28　不把每一次相处都安排成成长。学生可以拒绝被持续当作有待成长的人。

第六篇　碰撞

TS29　14DD 翻转时，学生自己的不得不结晶出来，开始与老师的法、老师对他的打算相遇。叛逆不是 14DD 的发生机制，常是它的行为副产品。

TS30　这一段，老师那一端在一次运作里朝 15DD 去，在这件事上读作：认这条不得不是他自己的，即使在老师看来那个方向会失败。老师可以把自己的不同意说足；不把它当作学生已经完成的自我认识交过去。

TS31　互凿按过程判，不按身份判：师生在某一件事上进入同一个互向的过程，并实际互相凿开对方的判断，那一件事上就有互凿。老师被学生凿开的那一件，照那一次过程读。有互凿，不认证学生已在 15DD 方向上。

TS32　学生那一端的要求与老师的法在同一情境里要求不相容的行动：那一件事是否进入冲突论，照它的两道门逐项核，不由师生身份或不相容本身完成认定。没有进入的，照来往与碰撞读。

TS33　学生在一件事上不认同老师、超过老师：不读作背叛，也不拿来认证这段关系朝涵育去，或老师那一端在 15DD 方向上。那一样本事是这位老师教会的，照事实记。「青出于蓝」不作这段关系的尺子。

第七篇　交出

TS34　这个系列读到学生成年：照亲子论读，按人的域，一域一域到。域照认领的事项划，不照所教的科目划，也不因遇到一道还不会的题另划一域；事项可以在还没有站住的时候被指认。会核对不是这条界（TS16、TS18）。与这一段有关的认领事项还没有到成年、关系先结束的，照 TS37 读；不因所教的内容没有成为学生自己的不得不，就把这一段一直留在成年以前。成年了的那一面，此后照成年人之间的给与来往读，同一位老师仍可以教他。毕业、出师、法律上的成年，认而不判。这是这个系列读到哪里的界，不是说现实里的师生关系到这里就没有了。

TS35　检验在学生那一侧：他能够不再围绕这一位老师而展开。这是检验，不是他事实上必须离开，也不是他从此谁也不需要。这里核的是老师有没有占住学生自己的构成位置；不凭「不再围绕老师」认证他的不得不已经站住，也不拿它给老师的教结算。

TS36　学生的感谢、成就、留下，不记作对教的偿还，也不拿来结算；老师不因教过而在他的方向里占一份。这一条只结算他的方向归谁；学费与功劳另算。可以记得，可以为他高兴。

TS37　关系在学生成年之前就结束的：老师走了，学生走了，换了老师。退出不结清，也不终审这个人；退出的时候，给两个人之间共同的事留出自然发展的空间，朝涵育这个方向。老师原先打算教完的东西，不因此成为他走后仍在的共同的事；教过的留在学生手里，由他照用、改或放下。是谁安排的换，认而不判。

TS38　此后这两个人之间，照关系论、合作论与涵育论读。老师自己怎样过被学生看见的那一面，交涵育的传播；大学里的那一段，构的一面交大学论。

第十节 收束

这一篇不立命题，不证明任何对象层的命题。它说的是：师生论在关系侧，接在亲子论之后，承的是涵育论交出来的那一半；它不给一切师生关系下定义，读的是以老师那一端在 15DD 方向上为前提的师生关系，人都是混合的，回落照读，回落了能回过头来；读得出学生只被当作手段的不在射程里，只把课教好的不判、也不在射程里；老师是关系里的一方，按事定；它按每一段读，读到学生成年为止。主线是：老师教的，有些他当下验不了，先接下来；弄明白为什么，回头查对不对，要他自己来，老师给他这个工夫，也给他查的办法；他自己要往哪里去，老师不替他定。各篇读到的殖民方向，借的是师生之间已经在或可能形成的件；方向在被拿来做什么的那一次作用上读。
