Abstract

This paper asks what users are taken to be by a company. In 1982, seven people in the Chicago area died after taking Tylenol; the medicine had been taken from store shelves by someone and laced with cyanide, and the danger was not caused by Johnson & Johnson, yet Johnson & Johnson stopped production and advertising and removed the product from shelves nationwide. Users are people, each with his own law; what a company sells them is what it does; the users themselves, their attention and the data about them are not the goods in this transaction. Users can leave: whether data can be taken along, and whether unsubscribing is no harder than subscribing, are the two places the normative layer asks about first; the costs and conditions the company sets do not seal off the channels for exiting, refusing, revoking, or passing into review. Recommending is permitted, and whether to choose is the user's own affair; interfaces are not designed to deceive him or to get around his judgment; the closer to a chokepoint, the stronger the checks (GS8). The EU's data portability, the reduction of switching charges to zero, Germany's cancellation button and the Digital Services Act's provisions on interfaces show how the rule layer writes this; the ranking on Crossways Market and the deletion of reviews on Echo Wall show what it is to judge the operation, not the person. Products do not trade harm to people's health and safety for profit; when a danger appears the company stops first, and the cost falls on what the company does; the accountability to stop first is not limited by fault (GS8a). Advertising, free services in exchange for data, subscriptions: the names are not judged, the execution is: as far as users enter an exchange of data and attention, being informed, being able to refuse and not being held fast make it an exchange in the Emergence Layer, and where these are not met the line is crossed; being able to refuse must be real (GS9). The European Commission's non-compliance decision against Meta in 2025 judged precisely a practice in which "being able to refuse" had not been made real. Finally the paper speaks of direction: giving the real thing, and giving users more of a way forward. The paper has three propositions (GS8, GS8a, GS9), each stated at the end of its section and listed together at the end.

Introduction

In 1982, seven people in the Chicago area died after taking Tylenol. Tylenol is a painkiller made by Johnson & Johnson. Johnson & Johnson "stopped production, suspended advertising, and removed products from shelves nationwide"; it recalled thirty-one million bottles, at a cost of about one hundred million dollars. It was later established that "Authorities discovered that the Tylenol had been removed from store shelves and laced with poisonous cyanide." (The Christian Science Monitor, 2012-12-12) The danger was not caused by Johnson & Johnson; it still bore the cost on its own side: what stopped was its production, its advertising and the goods on its shelves, and what was spent was its money.

Johnson & Johnson has a credo, Our Credo, written in 1943 and revised a number of times since; in 1987 "fathers" was added to its first paragraph (jnj.com, "8 fun facts about the Johnson & Johnson Credo"). The first sentence of the current text is about the people who use its things: "We believe our first responsibility is to the patients, doctors and nurses, to mothers and fathers and all others who use our products and services." (jnj.com, "Our Credo")

Only this one practice and the original text of the credo are taken here. Johnson & Johnson's later lawsuits and controversies are outside the judgment of this paper: the practice is judged, and no company is characterized. One more thing must be made clear: the credo ranks the parties, with shareholders last ("Our final responsibility is to our stockholders."); this series does not rank the parties. The credo serves here only as an example at the rule layer: how a company writes users into its documents.

Hence the question: what are users taken to be by a company? §1 says who users are; §2 and §3, no locking in, no manipulation, no deception; §4, that health and safety are not traded for profit; §5 takes up models; §6 speaks of direction.

The reading follows the Prequel: the construct takes the ought, the position takes accountability, the person takes his own law.

§1 Who Users Are

Users are people, each with his own law. This series writes no oughts for users: what they buy and do not buy, how long they use it, why they switch, is their own affair.

What a company sells users is what it does: products and services. The price falls on what is done. The user himself is not the goods in this transaction; nor are his attention and the data about him. Using them in an exchange is permitted, but only by the three things of §5: being informed, being able to refuse, not being held fast.

Whether users are inside the company is first identified by the two branches of the Prequel, §7. The difficulty of leaving counts only the part that can be examined: what switching to another provider costs, whether data can be taken along, whether a contract can be cancelled, whether there are other choices. The harder it is to leave, the more explanation and routes of questioning the company owes him (own undertaking); and the ease of leaving does not cancel his standing to have the line guarded and to question.

If a company becomes a place users cannot get around in going about their lives, so that paying, communicating and finding work all have to pass through it, it has come near a chokepoint. Political Economy has laid down: "A position others cannot get around on the way to sustaining their lives or unfolding their own paths is a chokepoint"; "Whether it is a chokepoint depends on whether it can actually be got around, not on whether there is nominally another way. To identify a chokepoint is only to identify where questions are to be asked, not to judge that it crosses the line" (Political Economy Paper 2, E7). As it comes near a chokepoint, the checks strengthen accordingly (Political Economy Paper 3, E14). How chokepoints are identified, and who reviews them, is handed to a later theory of the market; this paper uses the notion only to settle in which direction the checks are strengthened.

§2 No Locking In

Normative layer. Users can leave. Brought down to a company's relation with its users, the normative layer first asks about two places: whether data can be taken along; whether unsubscribing is no harder than subscribing.

Beyond these two places there is one general boundary: the costs and conditions the company sets do not seal off the channels for exiting, refusing, revoking, or passing into review. Political Economy's first gate states "sealing off" precisely: "That a relation has terms, conditions, procedures or exit costs does not mean that exit has been sealed off; sealing off means that the person no longer holds a structural channel for refusing, revoking a standing bond, or passing into a reviewable procedure" (Political Economy Paper 4, E21). An annual contract or an early-termination fee is not in itself a sealing off; what must be looked at is whether, taken together, they leave a person with actually no road out.

Two kinds of difficulty in leaving. A user may be unable to leave a company for two kinds of reason (own undertaking).

One comes from the thing itself. Everyone else is here; switching means learning afresh; all one's contacts are on one messaging app, and changing apps means finding people again. This kind is not, by its origin, judged a line-crossing by the company: no position made it happen; it is the combined result of many people's separate choices. But it brings the company nearer a chokepoint, and the checks and the accountability of supply strengthen accordingly.

The other kind is set up by the company. Data cannot be taken along, the way to unsubscribe is hidden, switching to another provider incurs a fee. This is the kind the normative layer governs. Whether the line has been crossed is examined by whether it seals off the channels for exiting, refusing, revoking, or passing into review; the word "artificial" does not settle it: terms, conditions and exit costs as such do not mean that exit has been sealed off (Political Economy Paper 4, E21; Paper 2, E12a).

Examples at the rule layer. The EU has written "being able to leave" into rules in several places.

Article 20(1) of the General Data Protection Regulation: where the processing of personal data is based on consent or on a contract and is carried out by automated means, the data subject has the right to receive the personal data concerning him that he has provided to a controller "in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format and have the right to transmit those data to another controller without hindrance" (Regulation (EU) 2016/679, Art. 20(1)).

Article 6(9) of the Digital Markets Act requires more of the large platforms designated as "gatekeepers": at the end user's request, effective portability free of charge, covering not only the data the user has provided but also "data provided by the end user or generated through the activity of the end user" on the platform (Regulation (EU) 2022/1925, Art. 6(9)). The nearer a chokepoint, the heavier the obligation.

Article 29(1) of the Data Act: from January 12, 2027, providers of data processing services "shall not impose any switching charges on the customer for the switching process" (Regulation (EU) 2023/2854, Art. 29(1)).

Section 312k of the German Civil Code: except in the cases the section itself sets out, for continuing consumer contracts that can be concluded through a website and under which a business provides performance for payment, the business must ensure that the consumer can declare termination on the website through a "termination button"; the button may bear only the words "cancel contracts here" ("Verträge hier kündigen") or an equally unambiguous wording (BGB §312k Abs. 1, 2). Where one can sign up, one must also be able to get out.

Article 25(3) of the Digital Services Act lists the practices on which the European Commission may issue guidelines, among them, in point (c), "making the procedure for terminating a service more difficult than subscribing to it" (Regulation (EU) 2022/2065, Art. 25(3)(c)). This paragraph sets out the subjects of guidance, not a list of prohibitions; what it shows is where the rule layer puts its pen.

These provisions are all examples, not rules this series sets for any state.

Judgment layer. Whether the costs and conditions the company sets have sealed off the channels; the scope and format of what can be taken along; who decides, who is accountable.

§3 No Manipulation, No Deception

Normative layer. Recommending is permitted; whether to choose is the user's own affair (A Way Out, a Way Forward, E41). Interfaces and procedures are not designed to deceive him or to get around his own judgment. What the company tells users is the same story it tells investors and employees (Paper 1, GS2).

It judges the operation, not the person. This proposition looks at how the interface is designed, what the ranking is based on, whether reviews have been deleted or altered; it does not ask whether the company "meant to deceive."

The rule layer happens to be written in the same way. Article 25(1) of the Digital Services Act: providers of online platforms "shall not design, organise or operate their online interfaces in a way that deceives or manipulates the recipients of their service or in a way that otherwise materially distorts or impairs the ability of the recipients of their service to make free and informed decisions" (Regulation (EU) 2022/2065, Art. 25(1)). Paragraph 3 of the same article lists practices on which the European Commission may issue guidelines, such as giving one option more prominence when a choice is being made, or repeatedly requesting that the user make a choice already made. The provision too judges the interface, not the motive.

Two constructed examples show how the normative layer lands. For these two kinds of matter, ranking and the deletion of reviews, this paper has not found a usable judgment or regulatory finding, so constructed examples are used.

On Crossways Market in the Kingdom State, search results are ranked by Crossways. In Year 22 a statistician in Apple City took a sample: among goods of the same kind with similar prices and ratings, Crossways Home, Crossways' own brand, appeared on the first page at three times the rate of other sellers. Crossways answered: "Ranking looks at buyer satisfaction; Crossways Home has fewer returns." Ranking can have grounds; this proposition does not require a company not to rank, nor that its own brand be treated just like everyone else's. What it requires is that the grounds can be stated and can be questioned. Crossways stated a ground; whether it holds, and whether the threefold difference all comes from the return rate, must be examined further, and this one ratio is not filled out into a conclusion.

The review platform Echo Wall was acquired by Crossways Group in the spring of Year 23. In Year 24 the same statistician took another sample: the proportion of negative reviews on Crossways Home product pages on Echo Wall deleted on the ground of "violating community norms" rose from three percent before the acquisition to eleven percent. Reviews are information users give one another, and whether a person buys may depend on what others say. The ratio rose, and what remains are the places to ask: whether the grounds for deletion can be explained, whether they favor the company's own brand, whether the reasons and the outcomes of deletion can be questioned. It is not filled out into "all the deletions are stamping out questioning."

A conditional sentence can be written here: if review deletion is used to manufacture a false unanimity of praise, that is deceiving users through other people's mouths (own undertaking). This sentence is a reading at the normative layer, and does not reach a conclusion for this case.

GS8 No locking in, no manipulation, no deception. Whether data can be taken along, and whether unsubscribing is no harder than subscribing, are the two places the normative layer asks about first; the costs and conditions the company sets do not seal off the channels for exiting, refusing, revoking, or passing into review (per Political Economy E21, E12a). Recommending is permitted; whether to choose remains his own affair; interfaces are not designed to deceive him or to get around his judgment. The closer to a chokepoint, the stronger the checks (per Political Economy E7, A Way Out, a Way Forward, E41, E41a; own undertaking).

The root of the last sentence lies in the Specialist Paper's requirement on positions that cannot be got around: "A position others cannot get around (Paper 2, E7) makes rules for many people: how ranking, access, settlement and appeals are handled falls on their way to sustaining their lives or unfolding their own paths. The scope of these rules is drawn by the institution, not fixed and changed by the rule-making party on its own"; "The closer to a chokepoint, the stronger this requirement." (A Way Out, a Way Forward, E41a) The nearer a company is to a chokepoint, the more its ranking, its deletion of reviews and its appeals must be explicable and open to questioning.

§4 Health and Safety Are Not Traded for Profit

Normative layer. Products do not trade harm to people's health and safety for profit. This is a line, not weighed on the scale of profit; it is written in the same way as Paper 1, GS3: the line is there before any weighing (Institution Theory Z9).

Stopping first. When a danger appears, the company first stops and isolates the dangerous product, stage or practice, and does not let the danger continue for the sake of keeping its profit. The cost falls on what the company does, its output, its advertising, its shelves, its costs; not on users.

How to stop, and how supply is taken over. One situation must be taken up on its own. Political Economy has laid down a kind of position: "The sole position of supply that touches the life-substrate, for which others have no affordable substitute" (Political Economy Paper 2, E11a). If a company is such a position, say the sole supplier of piped water in a place, then when a danger appears what it must stop is the danger, not the supply. The arrangements for how to stop and how supply is taken over are examined together under E11a: "how to withdraw is examined separately, through a procedure that does not seal off others' way of taking over and in which the manager arranges succession" (ibid.). Stopping the danger is not read as withdrawing the necessary supply along with it.

The accountability to stop first is not limited by fault (own undertaking). That the original source of the danger is not the company does not by itself relieve it of its accountability, as provider, for protection and handling. The Tylenol was taken from the shelves by someone and poisoned, and the danger landed on Johnson & Johnson's product; in this series' reading, its stopping first, as a step, is within this proposition; stopping as far as removing the product nationwide and halting advertising is a matter for the judgment layer, "how far to stop," and this paper does not judge it. Acting first and the final allocation of compensation and losses are two separate things: the latter each have their own grounds, and are left to the rule layer and the judgment layer.

Tendencies at the rule layer. Recall mechanisms; disclosure on the company's own initiative; prompt notification once a danger is discovered. Only tendencies are written here; particular regulations are not cited.

Judgment layer. Whether the danger is real, and how great; when to stop, and how far; who decides, who is accountable, and how remedy is made afterwards; how compensation and recourse are divided.

Last, a discipline. Whether Johnson & Johnson's brand recovered after that recall, and how quickly, this paper does not use as argument. Stopping first is because of the line, not because companies that stop first do well afterwards. Outcomes are not taken as rules.

GS8a Health and safety are not traded for profit. Products do not trade harm to people's health and safety for profit; this is a line, not weighed on the scale of profit (per Institution Theory Z9). When a danger appears, the company first stops and isolates the dangerous product, stage or practice, and the cost falls on what the company does, not on users; where it is the sole position of supply at the life-substrate, how it stops and how supply is taken over are examined under Political Economy E11a. The accountability to stop first is not limited by fault: if the danger was not caused by the company but lands on its product, it still stops first; compensation and the allocation of losses have their own grounds (own undertaking).

§5 A Model Is Judged by Its Execution, Not by Its Name

Normative layer. Advertising, free services in exchange for data, subscriptions, memberships: none of these names does this series judge. The advertising and attention models as such are not the problem; the problem lies in how they are executed, and what their structural effects are.

As far as users enter an exchange of data and attention, three things are judged: being informed, being able to refuse, not being held fast. Where they are met, it is an exchange in the Emergence Layer, and is left open as usual; where they are not, the line is crossed.

This test governs the exchange itself. Other harms to the Base Layer, effects on third parties and chokepoint structures are examined separately under the corresponding lines; passing this test does not certify the model as warranted in every respect. This formulation has the same form as the sentence on Political Economy's two gates: "not running into the two gates does not certify a transaction as warranted in every respect" (Political Economy Paper 4, E24; own undertaking).

Attention, and data about a person, are not sold as commodities unless he is informed and able to refuse.

Being able to refuse must be real. Being able to refuse must be made real in three places: refusing is no harder than agreeing; a refusal still leaves an option that is not worse; those who have refused are not asked again and again.

Structural effects. The same model, used by a company that has already become a chokepoint, has a different reach, and the checks strengthen accordingly (Political Economy Paper 2, E7; Paper 3, E14).

Examples at the rule layer. Article 5(2) of the Digital Markets Act and its Recitals 36 and 37 match "being able to refuse must be real" almost point by point. A gatekeeper combining or cross-using personal data must first give the user the specific choice and obtain consent ("unless the end user has been presented with the specific choice and has given consent"); after the user refuses or withdraws, the request for the same purpose may not be made "more than once within a period of one year" (Art. 5(2)). A less personalized but equivalent option should be provided, and consent may not be made a condition of using the core platform service or some of its functions ("offering a less personalised but equivalent alternative, and without making the use of the core platform service or certain functionalities thereof conditional upon the end user's consent") (Recital 36); and: "Not giving consent should not be more difficult than giving consent." (Recital 37) The provisions do not prohibit the advertising model; they judge the execution.

A real case. In November 2023, Meta introduced in the EU a binary "Consent or Pay" advertising model: users of Facebook and Instagram could either consent to the combination of their personal data and see personalized ads, or pay a monthly fee and see no ads. On April 23, 2025, the European Commission announced its non-compliance decision on it: "Meta breached the DMA obligation to give consumers the choice of a service that uses less of their personal data," with a fine of two hundred million euros. The Commission's reason was that "it did not give users the required specific choice to opt for a service that uses less of their personal data but is otherwise equivalent to the 'personalised ads' service." The period found ran from March 2024, when these obligations became legally binding, to November 2024 (European Commission press release IP/25/1085, 2025-04-23).

What was judged was this one practice. Advertising was not judged, nor was paid subscription as such. In this series' reading, this is "being able to refuse" not made real: the road of refusal had only paying left on it, with no option that used less data and was otherwise equivalent (own undertaking). This reading does not extend to every arrangement in which refusal costs money crossing the line. It is stated as it stands: as of that announcement, the new version Meta introduced in November 2024 was still being assessed by the Commission.

GS9 A model is judged by its execution, not by its name. As far as users enter an exchange of data and attention, being informed, being able to refuse and not being held fast make it an exchange in the Emergence Layer; where these are not met, the line is crossed. Other harms to the Base Layer, effects on third parties and chokepoint structures are examined separately (the same form as Political Economy E24; own undertaking). Being able to refuse must be real: refusing is no harder than agreeing, a refusal still leaves an option that is not worse, and those who have refused are not asked again and again. Attention, and data about a person, are not sold as commodities unless he is informed and able to refuse.

§6 Direction: Giving the Real Thing, Giving More of a Way Forward

This section speaks of the direction of giving conditions, and also of the baseline that must still be kept while giving them.

Giving the real thing. The product does what it says. This is the plainest thing between users and a company, and also the company's main contribution to its users.

Giving more of a way forward. Give him information, give him comparisons, give him exit. A company that makes it easier for users to compare it with others, and easier to leave with their data, is giving them conditions.

AI helping users think clearly. Economics P6 has written of a direction: AI helping a person think clearly about what he really wants, rather than thinking for him and pushing him to buy. This paper takes that direction; that paper's judgment serves only as corroboration.

Users' voice. Users can question the company, and asking has efficacy; this is examined by the three questions: who can activate it, what actual efficacy it has, and how the party questioned cannot cancel it on its own (Political Economy Paper 3, E16). This is a tendency at the rule layer; the nearer a chokepoint, the more real appeals must be. There is one more case on Echo Wall. In the autumn of Year 23, the kiln workshop opened by the veteran kiln worker from Bluekiln received more than a hundred one-star reviews within a week, all from newly registered accounts; it appealed to Echo Wall, and three weeks later Echo Wall deleted sixty of them, saying that "the rest do not constitute violations." The appeal was handled by Echo Wall's own content review team, which gave no reasons. The appeal had some effect; whether this channel is functionally independent of the party questioned must still be examined by actual control (Institution Theory Z34). This case still does not by itself prove that the route of questioning has been sealed off.

In giving, the company does not set a person's direction for him. Paper 2 said that in giving conditions a company does not set a person's direction for him, and that deciding a person's own affairs for him under the guise of cultivation is colonization in the narrow sense. This is extended here to users (own undertaking): recommendations and "for your own good" designs that, in the form of cultivation, decide a user's own affairs for him are colonization in the narrow sense (A Way Out, a Way Forward, E41; Rights Theory P6 F26). The boundary must be made clear: an interface that gets around his judgment without passing itself off as cultivation is manipulation, belongs to §3, and is called line-crossing (the Prequel, §7).

Discipline. The content of contribution is a direction, not a duty; keeping promises, keeping open the channels of questioning and not setting a person's direction for him are still examined by the earlier sections and by Paper 2. Outcomes are not taken as rules.

Where This Paper Stops

This paper has laid down three propositions: no locking in, no manipulation, no deception (GS8); health and safety are not traded for profit, when a danger appears the company stops first, and the accountability to stop first is not limited by fault (GS8a); a model is judged by its execution, not by its name, and being able to refuse must be real (GS9).

What it has not proved: how data protection law should be designed; how chokepoints are identified and how competition is reviewed, which are handed to a later theory of the market; the particular regulations on recalls.

Handed to the later papers: the sellers on Crossways Market, in Paper 4; the accountability of the sole position of supply at the life-substrate on the side of society, in Paper 5, §4; how the channels that keep the direction are independent of the party questioned, in Paper 6, §2.

Remainders left: when the two kinds of difficulty in leaving are interwoven, which part the company has set up must be examined item by item, and there is no general way of dividing them (§2); how far to stop first, and how losses are divided, the normative layer has given only a direction (§4); how far "an option that is not worse" must be not worse is left to the rule layer and the judgment layer (§5).

摘要

这一篇问用户在公司那里被当作什么。1982 年，芝加哥一带有七人服用泰诺后死亡；药是被人从货架上取下、掺了氰化物的，危险不是强生造成的，强生仍停了生产与广告，把货从全国的货架上撤下。用户是人，各有自己的法；公司卖给他的是事，他本人、他的注意力、关于他的数据，都不是这笔交易里的货。用户离得开：数据能不能带走，退订是否不比订阅难，是规范层先问的两处；公司设的成本与条件，不封住退出、拒绝、撤回或转入审查的通道。推荐可以，选不选是他自己的事；界面不设计成骗他，或绕过他的判断；越靠近关口，制衡越强（GS8）。欧盟的数据可携、转换费归零、德国的解约按钮与《数字服务法》对界面的规定，示范规则层怎样写；通衢市集的排序与回音壁的删评，示范判操作、不判人。产品不以伤害人的健康与安全换利润；危险出现时先停，代价落在公司的事上；先停的职责不以过错为限（GS8a）。广告、免费换数据、订阅，名称都不判，判执行：就用户进入数据与注意力的交换而言，知情、能拒、不被收住，是涌现层的交换，做不到就越线；能拒要实（GS9）。欧盟委员会 2025 年对 Meta 的不合规决定，判的正是一项"能拒"没有落实的做法。最后说方向：给真东西，让他更有的选。全篇三条（GS8、GS8a、GS9），各节末写出，篇末合列。

引言

1982 年，芝加哥一带有七人服用泰诺（Tylenol）后死亡。泰诺是强生（Johnson & Johnson）的止痛药。强生停了生产，停了广告，把货从全国的货架上撤下（"stopped production, suspended advertising, and removed products from shelves nationwide"）；召回三千一百万瓶，花费约一亿美元。后来查明，药是被人从店里的货架上取下、掺了氰化物的（"Authorities discovered that the Tylenol had been removed from store shelves and laced with poisonous cyanide."）（The Christian Science Monitor，2012-12-12）。危险不是强生造成的；它仍把代价担在了自己一侧：停下的是它的生产、它的广告、它货架上的货，花掉的是它的钱。

强生有一份 1943 年写成、后经多次修订的信条（Our Credo）；1987 年，第一段里加进了"父亲"（jnj.com，"8 fun facts about the Johnson & Johnson Credo"）。现行文本的第一句写的就是用它东西的人：我们相信，我们首要的责任是对病人、医生与护士，对母亲与父亲，以及所有使用我们产品与服务的人（"We believe our first responsibility is to the patients, doctors and nurses, to mothers and fathers and all others who use our products and services."）（jnj.com，"Our Credo"）。

这里只取这一项做法与信条的原文。强生后来的诉讼与争议，不在这一篇的判断之内：判做法，不给一家公司定性。还有一处要说清：信条给各方排了先后，出资人在最后（"Our final responsibility is to our stockholders."）；这个系列不给各方排序。信条在这里只作规则层的一个例子：一家公司怎样把用户写进它的文件。

由此问：用户在公司那里，被当作什么。第一节说用户是谁；第二、三节说不锁死、不操纵、不欺瞒；第四节说不以健康与安全换利润；第五节说模式；第六节说方向。

读法照前置篇：构用应当，位置用职责，人用自己的法。

第一节　用户是谁

用户是人，各有自己的法。这个系列不对用户写应当：他买什么，不买什么，用多久，为什么换，是他自己的事。

公司卖给用户的是事：产品与服务。价格落在事上。用户本人不是这笔交易里的货；他的注意力，关于他的数据，也不是。要拿它们来交换，可以，但要照第五节的三件来：知情，能拒，不被收住。

用户在不在公司的内部，先照前置篇第七节的两支识别。离开的难度只算能核的部分：换别家要付什么，数据能不能带走，合约能不能退，有没有别的选。越难离开，公司欠他的说明与问路越多（自担）；离开容易，也不取消他守线与追问的资格。

一家公司若成了用户过日子绕不开的地方，付钱、通讯、找活，都要经过它，它就近了关口。政治经济学定过：「他人维持生活或展开自己的路上绕不过去的那个位置，是关口」；「是不是关口，看实际绕不绕得过去，不看名义上有没有别的路。认出一处关口，只是认出要问的地方，不是判它越线」（政治经济学第二篇 E7）。近了关口，制衡随之加强（政治经济学第三篇 E14）。关口怎样认、由谁来审，交日后的市场论；这一篇只用它定制衡往哪个方向加。

第二节　不锁死

规范层。 用户离得开。落到一家公司对用户的关系上，规范层先问两处：数据能不能带走；退订是否不比订阅难。

这两处之外，有一条总的界：公司设的成本与条件，不封住退出、拒绝、撤回或转入审查的通道。政治经济学的第一道闸把"封住"写准了：「关系里有期限、条件、程序或退出成本，不等于退出已被封死；封死，指人不再保有拒绝、撤回持续绑定或转入可审查程序的结构通道」（政治经济学第四篇 E21）。一份年度合约，一笔提前解约的费用，本身不等于封死；要看它们合起来，是否让人实际没有了走的路。

两种难离开。 用户离不开一家公司，可以有两种来由（自担）。

一种来自事本身。别人都在这里，换了要重新学，一个通讯软件上联系人都在，换一个就要重新找人。这一种不凭来由判公司越线：没有哪一个位置让它发生，是许多人各自的选择合起来的结果。可它让公司更近关口，制衡与供给的职责随之加强。

另一种是公司设的。数据带不走，退订的入口藏起来，换别家要收一笔费用。规范层管的是这一种。越没越线，核的是它是否封住了退出、拒绝、撤回或转入审查的通道，不凭"人为"二字终判：期限、条件、退出成本本身，不等于退出已被封死（政治经济学第四篇 E21；第二篇 E12a）。

规则层的例子。 欧盟在几处把"离得开"写成了规则。

《通用数据保护条例》第 20 条第 1 款：在个人数据的处理以同意或合同为基础、并以自动化方式进行的时候，数据主体有权以结构化、通用、机器可读的格式，取得他提供给控制者的、关于他的个人数据，并有权把这些数据转给另一个控制者，不受阻碍（"in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format and have the right to transmit those data to another controller without hindrance"）（Regulation (EU) 2016/679, Art. 20(1)）。

《数字市场法》第 6 条第 9 款，对被认定为"守门人"的大平台要求得更多：应终端用户的请求，免费提供有效的可携，不只是用户提供的数据，也包括他在平台上活动生成的数据（"data provided by the end user or generated through the activity of the end user"）（Regulation (EU) 2022/1925, Art. 6(9)）。越近关口，义务越重。

《数据法》第 29 条第 1 款：自 2027 年 1 月 12 日起，数据处理服务的提供者，不得就转换过程向客户收取任何转换费（"shall not impose any switching charges on the customer for the switching process"）（Regulation (EU) 2023/2854, Art. 29(1)）。

德国《民法典》第 312k 条：除本条另列的情形外，对于可经网页订立、约定由企业有偿提供给付的持续性消费者合同，企业要确保消费者能在网页上经一个"解约按钮"作出解约的表示；按钮上只能写"在此解约"（"Verträge hier kündigen"）或同样明确的字样（BGB §312k Abs. 1, 2）。订得进来的地方，也要退得出去。

《数字服务法》第 25 条第 3 款列了欧盟委员会可以就哪几种做法出指引，其中第 (c) 项是让终止一项服务的程序比订阅更难（"making the procedure for terminating a service more difficult than subscribing to it"）（Regulation (EU) 2022/2065, Art. 25(3)(c)）。这一款是指引的对象，不是禁止的清单；它示范的是规则层在哪里下笔。

这几条都是例子，不是本系列替各国定的规。

判断层。 公司设的成本与条件，封没封住通道；带走的范围与格式；谁定，谁负责。

第三节　不操纵，不欺瞒

规范层。 推荐可以；选不选，是他自己的事（专论 E41）。界面与程序不设计成骗他，或绕过他自己的判断。对用户说的，与对出资人、员工说的，是同一个故事（第一篇 GS2）。

判操作，不判人。 这一条看的是界面怎样设计，排序依什么，评价有没有被删改；不问公司"是不是想骗"。

规则层的写法正好也是这样。《数字服务法》第 25 条第 1 款：在线平台的提供者，不得把在线界面设计、组织或运营成欺骗或操纵服务的接受者，或以其他方式实质扭曲、妨碍他们作出自由而知情的决定（"shall not design, organise or operate their online interfaces in a way that deceives or manipulates the recipients of their service or in a way that otherwise materially distorts or impairs the ability of the recipients of their service to make free and informed decisions"）（Regulation (EU) 2022/2065, Art. 25(1)）。同条第 3 款列了欧盟委员会可以出指引的做法，比如在作选择时给某一选项更显眼的位置，反复请求用户作已经作过的选择。条文判的也是界面，不是动机。

两件设例，看规范层怎样落。排序与删评这两类事，本文没有核到可用的判决或监管认定，所以用设例。

王国州的通衢市集（Crossways Market），搜索结果由通衢排序。第 22 年，苹果城一位统计学者抽样：同类商品价格与评分相近时，通衢的自营品牌"通衢家用"（Crossways Home）出现在首页的比例，是其他卖家的三倍。通衢答："排序看的是买家满意度，通衢家用退货少。"排序可以有依据；这一条不要求一家公司不排序，也不要求自营与别家一个样。它要求的是：依据说得出，也可以被问。通衢说出了一个依据；它站不站得住，三倍的差是不是都出自退货率，要再核，不由这一个比例补成结论。

点评平台回音壁（Echo Wall）第 23 年春被通衢集团收购。那位统计学者第 24 年又抽样：回音壁上"通衢家用"商品页的差评，以"违反社区规范"为由被删的比例，由收购前的百分之三升到百分之十一。评价是用户之间互相给的信息，一个人买不买，可以要看别人怎么说。比例升了，留下的是要问的地方：删评的依据能不能说明，是否偏向自营，理由与删除的结果能不能受问。不由此补成"所有的删除都在消灭追问"。

这里可以写一句条件句：若借删评制造出虚假的一致好评，就是借别人的嘴欺瞒用户（自担）。这一句是规范层的读法，不替这一件下结论。

GS8　不锁死，不操纵，不欺瞒。数据能不能带走、退订是否不比订阅难，是规范层先问的两处；公司设的成本与条件，不封住退出、拒绝、撤回或转入审查的通道（承政治经济学 E21、E12a）。推荐可以，选不选还是他自己的事；界面不设计成骗他，或绕过他的判断。越靠近关口，制衡越强（承政治经济学 E7、专论 E41、E41a；自担）。

最后一句的根，在专论对绕不过去的位置的要求：「一个别人绕不过去的位置（第二篇 E7），为许多人立规：排序、准入、结算、申诉怎样办，落在他们维持生活或展开自己的路上。这些规的范围由制度划，不由立规的一方独自定、独自改」；「离关口越近，这一要求越强。」（专论 E41a）一家公司越近关口，它的排序、它的删评、它的申诉，越要说得出、问得到。

第四节　不以健康与安全换利润

规范层。 产品不以伤害人的健康与安全换利润。这是线，不在利润的天平上称；写法与第一篇 GS3 一样：线在称之前就在（制度论 Z9）。

先停。 危险出现时，先停止、隔离危险的产品、环节或做法，不以保住利润为由让危险延续。代价落在公司的事上，产量、广告、货架、成本；不落在用户身上。

停法与接续。 有一种情形要单独说。政治经济学定过一种位置：「触及生命基底、别人没有可负担替代的唯一供给位置」（政治经济学第二篇 E11a）。一家公司若是这样的位置，比如一处地方唯一的自来水，危险出现时，它要停的是危险，不是供给。停法与接续的安排，照 E11a 一并核：「怎样撤出，经不封他人接续之路、由管理者安排接续的程序另核」（同上）。不把停止危险，读成一并撤去必要的供给。

先停的职责不以过错为限（自担）。 危险的原始来源不是公司，不独自免除它作为提供者的防护与处置之责。泰诺是被人从货架上取下掺毒的，危险落在强生的产品上；在这个系列的读法里，它先停这一步，在这一条之内；停到全国下架、停广告，是判断层"停到哪一步"的事，这一篇不判。先行处置，与最终的赔偿、损失由谁分担，是两件事：后者各有根据，交规则层与判断层。

规则层的倾向。 召回的机制；主动公开；发现危险以后及时通报。这里只写倾向，不引具体法规。

判断层。 危险是不是真的，有多大；什么时候停，停到哪一步；谁定，谁负责，事后怎样补救；赔偿与追偿怎样分。

最后是一条纪律。强生那一次召回以后，品牌有没有恢复、恢复得多快，这一篇不拿来作论证。先停是因为线，不是因为先停的公司后来过得好。结果不当规则。

GS8a　不以健康与安全换利润。产品不以伤害人的健康与安全换利润；这是线，不在利润的天平上称（承制度论 Z9）。危险出现时，先停止、隔离危险的产品、环节或做法，代价落在公司的事上，不落在用户身上；是生命基底处唯一供给位置的，停法与接续照政治经济学 E11a 核。先停的职责不以过错为限：危险不是公司造成的，落在它的产品上，它也先停；赔偿与损失分配另有根据（自担）。

第五节　模式不判名称，判执行

规范层。 广告，免费换数据，订阅，会员：这些名称，这个系列都不判。广告和注意力的模式本身不是问题；问题在它怎样执行，结构性的影响是什么。

就用户进入数据与注意力的交换而言，判三样：知情，能拒，不被收住。做到了，是涌现层的交换，照常放开；做不到，就越线。

这一关管的是这一项交换本身。别的基础层侵害、对第三人的影响、关口的结构，照相应的线另核；过了这一关，不认证这项模式在一切方面都正当。这一写法与政治经济学两道闸的那一句同形：「没有撞上两道闸，不认证一次交易在一切方面正当」（政治经济学第四篇 E24；自担）。

注意力和关于他的数据，不在他不知情、不能拒绝的时候被当成商品卖出。

能拒要实。 能拒，有三处要落实：拒绝不比同意难；拒了，还有一个不差的选项；拒过的，不反复来问。

结构性影响。 同一种模式，用在一家已经成了关口的公司，影响面不同，制衡随之加强（政治经济学第二篇 E7；第三篇 E14）。

规则层的例子。 《数字市场法》第 5 条第 2 款与序言第 36、37 段，几乎一处一处对上了"能拒要实"。守门人合并、交叉使用个人数据，要先给用户"具体的选择"并取得同意（"unless the end user has been presented with the specific choice and has given consent"）；用户拒绝或撤回以后，同一目的的请求一年内不得超过一次（"more than once within a period of one year"）（Art. 5(2)）。应提供"较少个性化但同等"的选项，且不得以同意作为使用核心平台服务或其某些功能的条件（"offering a less personalised but equivalent alternative, and without making the use of the core platform service or certain functionalities thereof conditional upon the end user's consent"）（Recital 36）；而且："不给同意，不应比给同意更难"（"Not giving consent should not be more difficult than giving consent."）（Recital 37）。条文不禁止广告模式，判的是执行。

一件真实的事。 2023 年 11 月，Meta 在欧盟推出二选一的"同意或付费"（"Consent or Pay"）广告模式：Facebook、Instagram 的用户，或者同意合并个人数据、看个性化广告，或者按月付费、不看广告。2025 年 4 月 23 日，欧盟委员会公布对它的不合规决定：Meta 违反了《数字市场法》中"给消费者选择一项使用较少个人数据的服务"的义务（"Meta breached the DMA obligation to give consumers the choice of a service that uses less of their personal data"），罚款两亿欧元。委员会的理由是，它没有给用户"选择一项使用较少个人数据、其余与'个性化广告'服务同等的服务"所需的具体选择（"it did not give users the required specific choice to opt for a service that uses less of their personal data but is otherwise equivalent to the 'personalised ads' service"）。认定的期间，是 2024 年 3 月这些义务开始具有法律约束力起，到 2024 年 11 月（欧盟委员会新闻稿 IP/25/1085，2025-04-23）。

判的是这一项做法。广告没有被判，付费订阅本身也没有。照这个系列的读法，这是"能拒"没有落实：拒绝的那条路只剩付钱，没有一个使用较少数据、其余同等的选项（自担）。这一读不推到一切付费拒绝的安排都越线。照实写明：截至那一次公告，Meta 2024 年 11 月推出的新版本，委员会仍在评估。

GS9　模式不判名称，判执行。就用户进入数据与注意力的交换而言，知情、能拒、不被收住，是涌现层的交换；做不到，就越线。其他基础层侵害、第三人影响与关口结构另核（与政治经济学 E24 同形，自担）。能拒要实：拒绝不比同意难，拒了还有不差的选项，拒过的不反复来问。注意力和关于他的数据，不在他不知情、不能拒绝时被当成商品卖出。

第六节　方向：给真东西，让他更有的选

这一节说给条件的方向，也说给条件时仍须守住的底线。

给真东西。 产品做到它说的那样。这是用户与公司之间最朴素的一件事，也是公司对用户主要的贡献。

让他更有的选。 信息给他，比较给他，退出给他。一家公司让用户更容易比较它与别家，更容易带着数据离开，是在给他条件。

AI 帮用户想清楚。 经济学第六篇写过一种方向：AI 帮人想清楚他真正想要什么，而不是替他想，推他买。这一篇取这个方向，那一篇的判断只作旁证。

用户的声音。 用户能问公司，问了有实效；照三问核：谁能启动，有什么实际效力，被问的一方怎样不能独自取消它（政治经济学第三篇 E16）。这是规则层的倾向；越近关口，申诉越要实。回音壁上还有一件。第 23 年秋，青窑镇老窑工开的那家窑坊，一周内收到一百多条一星差评，都来自新注册的账号；它向回音壁申诉，三周后，回音壁删了其中六十条，说"其余不构成违规"。申诉由回音壁自己的内容审核组处理，不说理由。申诉有过一部分效力；这条通道在功能上是否独立于被问的一方，还要照实际控制核（制度论 Z34）。这一件仍不独自证明问的路已被封死。

给的时候不替人定方向。 第二篇说，给条件的时候，不替人定方向；装成涵育、替人定他自己的事，是窄义的殖民。这一句推到用户（自担）：推荐与"为你好"的设计，以涵育的形态替他定他自己的事的，是窄义的殖民（专论 E41；权利论第六篇 F26）。分界要说清：界面绕过他的判断、并不装成涵育的，是操纵，归第三节，称越线（前置篇第七节）。

纪律。 贡献的内容是方向，不是义务；兑现承诺、保有问的通道、不替人定方向，仍照前几节与第二篇核。结果不当规则。

这一篇停在哪里

这一篇立了三条：不锁死，不操纵，不欺瞒（GS8）；不以健康与安全换利润，危险出现时先停，先停的职责不以过错为限（GS8a）；模式不判名称，判执行，能拒要实（GS9）。

它没有证明的：数据保护的法律怎样设计；关口怎样认定、竞争怎样审查，交日后的市场论；召回的具体法规。

交给后面各篇的：通衢市集的卖家，在第四篇；生命基底处唯一的供给位置对社会一侧的职责，在第五篇第四节；守住方向的通道怎样独立于被问的一方，在第六篇第二节。

留下的余项：两种难离开交织在一起时，哪一部分是公司设的，要逐项核，没有一个总的切法（第二节）；先停到哪一步、损失怎样分，规范层只给了方向（第四节）；"不差的选项"不差到什么程度，交规则层与判断层（第五节）。

本篇命题（合列）

GS8　不锁死，不操纵，不欺瞒。数据能不能带走、退订是否不比订阅难，是规范层先问的两处；公司设的成本与条件，不封住退出、拒绝、撤回或转入审查的通道（承政治经济学 E21、E12a）。推荐可以，选不选还是他自己的事；界面不设计成骗他，或绕过他的判断。越靠近关口，制衡越强（承政治经济学 E7、专论 E41、E41a；自担）。

GS8a　不以健康与安全换利润。产品不以伤害人的健康与安全换利润；这是线，不在利润的天平上称（承制度论 Z9）。危险出现时，先停止、隔离危险的产品、环节或做法，代价落在公司的事上，不落在用户身上；是生命基底处唯一供给位置的，停法与接续照政治经济学 E11a 核。先停的职责不以过错为限：危险不是公司造成的，落在它的产品上，它也先停；赔偿与损失分配另有根据（自担）。

GS9　模式不判名称，判执行。就用户进入数据与注意力的交换而言，知情、能拒、不被收住，是涌现层的交换；做不到，就越线。其他基础层侵害、第三人影响与关口结构另核（与政治经济学 E24 同形，自担）。能拒要实：拒绝不比同意难，拒了还有不差的选项，拒过的不反复来问。注意力和关于他的数据，不在他不知情、不能拒绝时被当成商品卖出。
