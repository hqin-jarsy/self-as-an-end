The Place of the Propagation of Cultivation

Han Qin (秦汉) · Independent Researcher · 2026
DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.24351749 · Full PDF on Zenodo · CC BY 4.0
Abstract

This series reads one thing: how cultivation passes downstream through the relations between people. What it reads is the person in the middle of two connected relations: in the earlier one he is at the receiving end, and in the later one at the giving end. This is the series' Paper 0, written last and placed first. It sets out no new propositions; it says where the series stands within SAE, what it reads and does not read, and how it reads, and it gathers the propositions of the seven papers in one place.

The main line is three sentences. Propagation is not anyone's purpose; it is a result of people having come into relation. On the side of cultivation it is unsought, and read only in looking back. What passes on can go toward colonization or toward cultivation. A person who is willing to receive, whose own judgment opens up, and who then goes on to cultivate another is a link in the chain along which cultivation propagates. A practice carrying the direction of colonization repeats downstream without requiring that the person in the middle first open up a judgment of his own, and whether it takes hold in the next person is read separately; for cultivation to pass downstream by one link, three things of the middle person's own have to happen. What can be taught is content; what can be given is conditions; what can be seen is how a person stands. At every link, the step downstream starts again from the person in the middle.

The series is against the propagation of colonization, and "against" falls only on two things in the first person: not letting it occupy oneself, and not doing it to others. It identifies no person and no tradition. It reads only adults, and only the road that runs through relations; propaganda and the rules of platforms lie on the institutional side. It reads one link at a time, and no link is guaranteed; the level of the community and trends across generations are another scale, read by the Moral Law series, and the two are not on one level and do not cancel.

§1 Place

SAE writes about the single person, about institutions, and about what passes between people. On that third side, Relation Theory set down what all relations have, and its branches read love, marriage, cooperation, parent and child, teacher and student. Cultivation Theory opened a separate line, reading how one person gives conditions for another person's own law.

All of these read one relation: two ends, and what happens between them. One thing falls into the gap between them. Cultivation Theory handed it over in its abstract: the sources of cultivation are written at both ends, while its setting and the way it is passed on belong to the side that writes about what passes between people.1 Teacher-Student Theory registered it: how the one who was taught later teaches others, and how cultivation is passed on between people, are subjects for afterward.2 A special paper of Relation Theory registered it too: the spread of cultivation is to be treated separately.3

This series takes up those three registrations. What they call the way cultivation is passed on, or its spread, is what is called propagation here. It does not read one relation; it reads the point at which two relations join in one person. It is therefore not one more branch of Relation Theory: it does not read a kind of relation, and reads something that lies between any two relations. Nor is it a sequel to Cultivation Theory: that series reads how to give; this one reads what, after the giving, arrives at the next person by way of the one who received.

§2 Range

What is read. A toward B, and later B toward C; B is read.

Adults only. In the two relations this series reads, the receiver is already an adult; the lower bound is university students. The Theory of the University has said that cultivation has no end point, and occurs between adults too.4 If a person's earlier relation took place before he was an adult, that stretch is not read here: Parent-Child Theory and Teacher-Student Theory have written it, and it is acknowledged as they read it. He can be a link all the same; the reading picks up from his adulthood, and uses only material he wrote as an adult. A childhood memoir written in adulthood does not bring the childhood events it describes into the range read here.

The side of colonization, for contrast only. One paper, Paper 2.

Three units. One act: which way this end is headed in this matter, and what was actually opened or sealed. One relation: which acts took place between two positions. One link, spoken of by person: how those acts join up in one person as being willing to receive, judgment opening up, and going on to give. The giving and receiving positions are recorded by a single operation; A, B and C are not presupposed to be three different people, nor three generations. C can be A; the structure does not exclude it; the series takes its examples only from cases where C is not A.

§3 Stance

This series has a stance: against the propagation of colonization.

"Against" falls only on two things in the first person. Against being colonized oneself: not letting what lands on one come to occupy the place of one's own judgment. Against colonizing others oneself: not pouring it in as it was received, and not pouring in its opposite either. Together, Paper 3 calls these two breaking.

So the series pins no labels. It does not attach "colonization" to a person, a tradition, a trade or an account, and is not written as criticism of how others pass things on. Paper 2 uses made-up examples, no historical ones, and identifies no actual person. Directions are not labels;6 there is no criterion guaranteed to settle which place has taken hold of a person, and others do not judge it for him.

Nor does the stance issue oughts. The series does not say that a person who received cultivation ought to give onward, nor that anyone ought to break anything. It reads structure: what each side requires of the person in the middle when it goes downstream.

§4 How It Reads

The ways of reading used across the seven papers are gathered here.

Three things pass through a person. Content: knowledge, skill, sayings, rules; it can be taught and written down. An occupied place: what was poured in and has come to occupy the place of judgment. A way of standing: giving to another person without doing it for him. The three can be present in one person together; they are read apart and do not cancel one another.

A chain can be spoken of; it is not a bearer. A chain is broken down into acts between pairs, entered on separate accounts; passing through a third person does not release the earlier stretch.7 No overall direction is totalled for a chain and no overall account is kept; a school of masters and pupils, a school of thought, a tradition, a generation is not made the subject of a sentence.

How relations connect requires material. That two relations each hold and are alike in direction is not enough to say that the earlier one entered the later by way of B. Historical material is read by this rule: where there are only a few names and a few meetings, no link is recorded.

Conclusions in made-up examples are stipulations. Whether conditions opened, whether a judgment was formed, whether a place was occupied, are given by the example; a sentence, an operation, or the absence of something can be material, and does not by itself yield those readings. Where the material is not enough to divide the cases, "not yet known" is recorded. Where there is no suitable actual case, the series uses made-up examples.

Records, and no summing. Content being carried on can be arranged, checked and recorded; such records do not measure, certify or sum what is gained on the ontological side. The body papers do not speak of the layer of cultivation in headcounts.

Downstream is not used to rejudge upstream. How B later acts toward C does not rejudge what A did toward B at the time. New material that appears later can correct a reading; that is not rejudging.

No necessity written, no comparison of strength. It is not written that colonization necessarily passes downstream, nor that it necessarily takes hold in the next person; it is not written that cultivation cannot be passed on, nor that it always will be. The body papers do not compare the two sides as easier or harder, faster or slower, stronger or weaker. One sentence in the foundation papers speaks of an asymmetry between the two sides;8 this series uses it only as a citation, and what it writes itself is the structure at that point: the difference lies in the person in the middle; one side does not wait for him, and the other waits for three things of his.

Carried over from upstream. DD names a stance, not a label on a person, and layers are recorded by a single operation; the payoff layer and the ontological layer are two books, each kept on its own; provisions themselves are acknowledged but not judged; a reading states toward whom, in which matter, over which period, on what material, and where material is lacking it stays undetermined; no mind-reading; historical material is limited to what an adult author wrote and made public, and only how he wrote it is read.

§5 Shared Words

  • Propagation / transmission. Propagation in this series means from one relation to the next, by way of a person. The "transmission chain" of the foundation papers means an effect passing from the institutional layer through the relational layer to a person.9 The two words are not interchanged. "Propagation" in the information-theory and physics papers, and transmission at the scale of civilizations in the Anthropology series, are other senses.
  • Chain, link, starting point. A link means that receiving and giving have joined up in one person. On the path being read, the way of standing of the person at the starting point was not received from an earlier act of cultivation; he is not a link, and what he may have received elsewhere is read separately. A chain is not a bearer.
  • Three sets of three. The three things in a link are the middle person's own: being willing to receive, judgment opening up, going on to give. Two other sets of three appear: in Cultivation Theory and Action Theory, the three a giver does not answer in the other's place when assessing, namely whether he is willing, whether he is suited, whether the time is ripe; and, in this series, the three that pass through B: content, an occupied place, a way of standing.
  • Colonization. Two senses. Relation Theory takes the wider sense, the direction of an act; Value Theory takes a narrow sense in the field where value occurs, a state, and states that the narrow sense is one particular way the wider one falls in that field.10 When this series contrasts colonization with cultivation it uses the wider sense; when it speaks of the place that has taken hold in a person it borrows the narrow one, as an extension. Value Theory says in the same place that the opposite of cultivation is not colonization; that is said on the narrow sense's table, which is not the table of the contrast drawn here.
  • Indoctrination. Reserved for the special sense of Teacher-Student Theory: where the act has already written for the other and crowded out his own unfolding, which is violation. This series' main material is what has not crossed that line; for direction it says "pouring in and occupying."
  • Breaking. Its two first-person sides. Upstream writes of a breaking of intergenerational transmission that falls at the first choice, in a conflict, not to colonize; the two connect and are not the same.
  • Seek. The narrow sense only: fixing in advance, producing to that specification, inspecting against it. What has to be checked is what is being inspected: whether the other's own judgment and way of standing have been made into a standard for inspection. Settling, doing and checking that are aimed at conditions, technical content, or particular arrangements both have avowed are not called seeking. Open hoping is not included.
  • A way of standing. As the Theory of the University uses the phrase in speaking of leading. Two other places upstream use it for a stance the author himself has set up; the sense differs.
  • Being seen / observation at a distance. Being seen is the side that leading is, and lands on the seer's knowing of one more position. Observation at a distance, in the Moral Law series, is discernment, and lands on a judgment about the person observed.
  • Cultivation. As in Cultivation Theory: one person giving conditions to another. The education paper divides teaching, nurturing and leading into three, and "nurturing" there is a special sense of the same Chinese word; where this series cites that passage, it says so on the spot.
  • Gain. On the ontological side, the unfolding of legislative activity; gains and losses at the payoff layer are entered as payoff.

§6 The Boundary with Growth Necessity

The Moral Law series makes a strong claim: that the growth of 14DD toward 15DD is structurally necessary at long, cross-generational scales.11 Every paper of this series says "not guaranteed": a person who received is not necessarily willing to receive; one who is willing does not necessarily find his judgment opening up; one whose judgment opens up does not necessarily go on to give; and when he gives, the other does not necessarily take it up. How do the two sit together?

Three sentences only.

The first: this series reads one link, and no link is guaranteed. That is said at the scale of one person and two relations.

The second: what that Moral Law paper speaks of is an aggregate tendency at the level of the community and across generations, and it states its own scale: many generations, not an individual lifetime; and "Growth necessity is a cross-generational statistical operator — not necessity at every specific node or specific era".12 Its necessity rests on four roots: the inexhaustibility of the thing-in-itself, the structure of recognition, subjectivity as activity, and an economic a priori mechanism.13 None of the four is that every act of cultivation is passed on. At the cross-generational layer what it writes is that the next generation grows up in an environment where 15DD is denser; there is person-to-person accumulation in that layer, and it is spoken of at the scale of the aggregate, not brought down to any one link.

The third: the two are not on one level and do not cancel. From one link's not being guaranteed it does not follow that the tendency fails; from the tendency's holding it does not follow that any one link succeeds. This series does not prove that paper's claim a second time, and does not revise it from a single link. How many links aggregate into a tendency, and what lies in between, this series has not read. The Moral Law series speaks of person-to-person accumulation in two places, both at the scale of the aggregate.14

Moral Law, Paper 9, at the scale of institutional architecture, has one more sentence that can stand here as the other side of the boundary: "macroscopic phase transition requires waiting, but microscopic recognition is never delayed."15 Everything this series reads lies on the microscopic side: this time, toward this person.

§7 Division of Labor

With Cultivation Theory. Cultivation Theory reads how to give: how far, how without doing it for the other, when not to give. This series reads what comes after the giving. It uses Cultivation Theory's three questions, its assessing first, and its "inwardly a cannot-not, toward the other not a debt" as they stand, and does not set them up again.

With Teacher-Student Theory and Parent-Child Theory. Each reads one kind of relation, with the stretch before adulthood as its home ground. This series picks up from adulthood, acknowledges what they have read, and does not go back over it. Teacher-Student Theory handed over leading, and Paper 5 takes it up.

With Relation Theory and Positive-Sum, Unsought. Relation Theory supplies the threshold of relation, the two directions, pairwise accounting, and the reading of sediment; this series uses them as they stand. Positive-Sum, Unsought supplies the narrow sense of seeking, "carried out," the separation of two layers, and no book-keeping and no offsetting; this series carries them over to "passing on," marks each extension, and bears each itself. B giving back to A is that paper's business.

With the Theory of the University. That series reads how a construct leaves room for cultivation and leading; this one reads the level of persons. Its definition of judgment, its point that judgment cannot be handed over along with rules, and its point that leading is not registered or assessed are used here as they stand.

With the institutional side. Propaganda, the rules of platforms, and the operations and succession arrangements of institutions are all handed over. The theory of propaganda has not been written; what is handed over is only registered, and none of its conclusions are borrowed.

§9 Remainders and Open Questions

When the series has been read through, these remain.

  1. Collision at the starting point. A chain has a starting point, and the person there came by his way of standing through collision in actual dealings. How collision brings a person to stand in that direction, this series only cites from upstream and has not read.
  2. The receiving side: loosening afterward. Upstream says that internal colonization cannot be broken from within alone, and lists three conditions under which an intervention from outside can work. How those three conditions hold between adults, this series has not checked one by one.
  3. How the stretch before adulthood connects to what comes after. This series acknowledges that stretch and does not read it. How what a person received before adulthood entered his giving as an adult requires material for the connection; how such material is to be read remains open.
  4. Giving back. B giving back to A is registered only.
  5. The limits of the historical material. A link takes three kinds of material: the stretch of receiving, the stretch of giving, and how they connect, each written down by the person himself or by someone present. Among the material this series found, none has all three. Whether this is because such material is hard to come by, or because few people have ever written such a thing down, is not judged here.
  6. Many links and a tendency. No link is guaranteed, and the tendency lies at another scale. How one gets from the one to the other has not been read. The note to §6 points to two places upstream that speak of person-to-person accumulation.
  7. The theory of propaganda is unwritten. What that theory would read is the provisions themselves. For an actual case, the method for checking apart the prescribed part and what a person added is already upstream: first find out what the provision is; where it cannot be obtained, register that the background is unchecked. What is lacking is particular material, not that theory.
  8. AI. A tool does not occupy the middle position. Where a person gives by way of AI, what passes through him is registered and not developed.

§10 The Propositions of the Series

Paper 1　Propagation Is a Result

CB1　Propagation is a result, not a purpose. Once people have relations with one another, what an earlier relation left in a person enters his dealings with someone else. What enters can head toward colonization or toward cultivation.

CB2　Three things pass through the person in the middle, and they are read apart: content can be taught and written down; the downstream repetition of an occupied place does not require that his own judgment has first opened up; a way of standing cannot be taught. The three can be present in one person, and none cancels another.

CB3　A chain has a starting point. On the path being read, the starting person's way of standing was not received from an earlier act of cultivation; he is not a link in the sense of Paper 4. Its source is collision in actual dealings. What he received in other relations and other matters is read separately. So the propagation of cultivation is not the only path to this direction, and a chain that breaks off does not mean there is nothing afterward.

CB4　The unit of reading is the place where two relations meet in one person. A acts toward B; B later acts toward C; B is read. The positions of giver and receiver are recorded by a single operation; A, B and C are positions in two relations, and presuppose neither three different people nor three generations. B is one person: at the receiving end the first time, at the giving end the second. A link is spoken of by person, and the opening of judgment in between is read in looking back.

CB5　One may speak of a chain, but a chain is not a bearer. A chain is broken down into effects between pairs, each entered on its own account; passing through B does not release A from his part; a chain is given no overall direction and no overall account.

CB6　That two relations each hold and head in similar directions is not yet enough to say the earlier one passed through B into the later one; material is needed that shows how they connect. It need not be the only source, and B need not copy A.

CB7　The handing-on of content can be arranged, whether it was learned can be checked, and where it has reached can be recorded; such records do not measure, certify, or add up what is gained on the ontological side. A way of standing and each single effect are read one by one, and "how many people were cultivated" is not used as a measure of completion. That content has spread widely does not show the way of standing arrived with it; that no one took up the content does not show nothing was left.

CB8　What happens downstream is not used to rejudge what happened upstream. How B later acts toward C does not rejudge what A did toward B; A's gain from that occasion lies in that occasion, and is neither increased nor reduced by whether anything was passed on. New material that surfaces later can correct a misreading of that occasion.

Paper 2　How Colonization Propagates

CB9　Colonization has two senses. Value Theory Paper 3, on the field where value occurs, takes the narrow sense: colonization describes a state, in which some content occupies a position. This series borrows that sense for what has taken hold in a person (an extension, on this series' own account); when it says which way an act is headed, it follows Relation Theory H8: a direction, not a label.

CB10　A practice headed toward colonization can be repeated downstream without B first opening up his own judgment, avowing what is passed on, or having any motive to pass it on. B may do the same to C, or may not; whether it comes to occupy C's position of judgment is read separately, at C's end.

CB11　What was poured in can carry with it a line that says "this is how people are to be treated": content and practice travel downstream together. "Indoctrination" keeps the sense of Teacher-Student Theory TS11, where the subject is the teacher's position; applied between adults it is an extension.

CB12　An occupied place is read by positions: which are open and which are sealed, written by content-domain and period, with no overall direction assigned to a person or a relation. It can come about without conflict; that a stretch of dealings shows no dispute is not evidence that no place has been occupied.

Paper 3　The Propagation of Cultivation: Breaking

CB13　Breaking has two sides, both in the first person: not letting what lands on me occupy the place of my own judgment; and not colonizing others, whether by pouring in the same thing or its opposite. Merely having no occasion, pausing for a while, or switching to another way of managing people is not recorded as breaking on appearance alone.

CB14　The two sides need not arrive together. What took hold in a person may still be there while he no longer pours it into the next person: the giving side can come first. Not pouring in does not by itself mean being able to recognize the other.

CB15　The sources of the two sides are read apart, and do not exclude each other. The giving side is a choice a person makes when facing another person; declining to pass it on this time need not come from having been cultivated earlier. On the receiving side, where something has already taken hold, upstream writes that internal colonization cannot be broken from within alone; here conditions given by another person can be read. Either side may have conditions given by someone else, and on either side the step remains the person's own.

CB16　Breaking is not, in itself, cultivation; structurally it is read as a step in the 15DD direction. The breaking that upstream describes falls within a conflict: choosing, for the first time, not to colonize and not to withdraw recognition of the other; it requires only the entry, not maturity. Breaking in this series is further divided into a receiving side and a giving side, with direction read by one operation at a time; no single instance of declining to repeat certifies which layer the whole person is at. His own end is for him to confirm.

CB17　On the receiving side, there is no criterion guaranteed to close the question of how one knows that such a place in oneself has been occupied. The way of looking back follows Aesthetics Ray 13: try taking away the yardstick that person gave; this can be mistaken. No one else makes the judgment for him.

Paper 4　The Propagation of Cultivation: A Link

CB18　A person who is willing to receive, whose own judgment opens up, and who then goes on to cultivate another person is a link in the chain along which cultivation propagates.

CB19　All three rest with the person in the middle: whether he is willing to receive, whether his judgment opens up, whether he goes on to give and, in giving, whether he takes over the step that is the other's to take. They are not the three things Cultivation Theory speaks of (whether the other is willing, whether he is suited, whether the time is ripe): those are what a giver, in assessing, does not answer in the other's place; these are what a link requires.

CB20　Being willing to receive is not taking everything as given. The receiver can disagree, can change it, can take only part.

CB21　Judgment is read by Action Theory's definition. It cannot be delivered along with rules; it can only be practised. Doing as told, or arriving at the same conclusion, does not prove that it has formed.

CB22　A link is reckoned up to "carried out": he took on the direction of cultivation, and his act actually opened conditions for another person. That B's giving toward C was carried out does not mean C has become the next link; whether C is willing to receive, whether his judgment opens up, whether he later gives, are each read separately. Where something was given and the conditions did not actually open, it is recorded as it is.

CB23　A single act of B's toward C can carry both directions: making space in one place, pouring in and occupying in another. The two are recorded separately and are not netted out.

CB24　Giving is also practice: to give to someone, one has to judge where he is stuck and how far to give. The third of the three returns to the second.

CB25　Giving onward is not a debt: having received, one does not thereby owe anyone. For a person standing in the 15DD direction, cultivation is inwardly his own cannot-not; toward the other it is a direction, not a debt. Whether to give this time, to this person, is first assessed, and what is judged is conditions; that the time is not ripe means not doing it this time, and does not mean he no longer stands in this direction. Where he did not give and, looking back, the judgment was wrong, or where he judged that this was his to give, the judgment still stood, and he did not give, Cultivation Theory records it as failing to do his part; this entry comes from his own judgment, not from having received. That his judgment opened up and he did not go on to give does not rejudge the earlier cultivation as having come to nothing, and says nothing about anyone's character.

CB26　Each link has a separate account of time and effort, and this end's gain is not used to offset it.

Paper 5　The Propagation of Cultivation: Leading

CB27　Leading is the side of a person's living out recognition, in his own life and dealings, that another person sees. Having activities of one's own is not in itself leading.

CB28　Leading lets someone know that the position exists; it does not produce 15DD. Giving conditions, being seen, and collision in actual dealings are read apart, and are not assumed to come from the same person in the same encounter; the later person's step remains his own.

CB29　Leading is not registered, not assessed, not inspected. When a practice is fixed as a template for the other to follow, what is passed on is content that can be followed. When a person deliberately makes public how he judges and how he answers disagreement, whether there is leading in it still depends on the actual effect and on what the other saw.

CB30　What is seen includes how one turns back after falling back.

CB31　What is seen through particular practices is a way of standing that need not be copied: the later person need not treat people the way the earlier one did. Through a book or a screen a way of standing may also be seen: in how the author treats the reader here and now, in an act that has itself been left behind, and also by way of an account of an actual encounter; an account has to be checked for its grounds. That a later reader saw something is not read back into something the author did toward that reader at the time. How a person treats others as mere means is seen just the same; that is not called leading, and belongs to Paper 2.

Paper 6　The Propagation of Cultivation: Independent Creators

CB32　Where a person's speech, writing, or something he leaves behind identifiably and actually enters the conditions of occurrence of a reader or viewer, there is a relation between the two ends; a one-way relation counts. Where one person addresses many, each relation holds separately and is entered on its own account. The same goes for writing books, giving talks, and the work of independent creators.

CB33　An independent creator stands in the middle position: what he receives and what he gives are read apart. On his side, what is read is whether the yardstick of traffic has come to occupy the place where he himself judges what to make and how. What he has really avowed is not for others to judge; whether positions are open or sealed in his public work and dealings can be read. This paper takes its material from made-up examples only. The line between person and institution is drawn not by account but by a single operation: the part of the content an institution prescribes goes to the institutional side; what the person on camera adds of his own is looked at separately.

CB34　Traffic lies at the payoff layer; it can be recorded and can be sought. The layer of cultivation is not on that table. Seeking traffic, seeking commercial success, or using retention data does not by itself show that the audience enters his table only as an item; what matters is how the particular work and dealings treat the audience's judgment.

CB35　"How many people were cultivated" is not used as a measure. What he can settle, do and check is whether each piece leaves room: whether it gives reasons, whether it leaves a place for the viewer's disagreement, whether it answers in the viewer's place.

CB36　A single act can open conditions in many relations. In each relation where it actually opens conditions, the act is recorded as carried out; whether a viewer later becomes a link rests with each viewer. Where there is no feedback, the matter stays undetermined, and it is not turned round into a claim that nothing happened.

CB37　On a platform, too, one may be seen: how he treats his audience, how he owns a mistake. This connects to Paper 5.

Paper 7　The Propagation of Cultivation: Unsought

CB38　Cultivation does not seek success. Whether the other's judgment opens up, whether he gives onward, is neither fixed in advance nor inspected. What can be settled, done and checked is possibility: whether this act of mine left room, and what it opened.

CB39　Carried over to propagation: one does not seek that it be passed on; what one can do is see that the conditions for its being passed on are there. On the side of cultivation, propagation is read only in looking back, in the same form as the "unsought" of Positive-Sum, Unsought: where it can be read, it is recorded as it is; where it cannot, that too is recorded as it is; looking back can be mistaken.

CB40　The seeking excluded here is taken in the narrow sense: fixing in advance, producing to that specification, inspecting against it. What has to be checked is what is being inspected: whether the other's own judgment and way of standing have been made, in advance, into a standard for production and inspection; if so, calling it by another name leaves it excluded. Checking conditions, technical content, and particular arrangements both have avowed is read separately. Hoping that involves no inspection is not included.

CB41　Fixing in advance how the other is to treat the next person carries the direction of colonization at that point, and motive does not change this reading. The giver does not, for having given, hold a share in how the other acts toward the next person.

CB42　The handing-on of a craft can be discussed and arranged, and whether its content was learned can be checked; none of this fixes in advance how the other is to treat the next person. To fix that in advance, produce to it and inspect against it falls within the seeking excluded here. Tying it to a conferred title is one way of doing so; whether there is a title is not itself the criterion. Seeking, and getting, someone to carry the content on does not show that the way of standing arrived too.

CB43　That something was passed on does not offset the harm done at any link; what was not passed on is said, as it is, not to have been, and is not topped up into "some influence always remains"; no one is certified as being at any layer.

CB44　Not seeking is not a mark of anything. Not seeking guarantees nothing in itself, direction included; colonization, too, can pass downstream without being anyone's purpose. The difference lies not in whether there is a wish for it to be passed on, but in where the step downstream falls.

摘要

「涵育的传播」读一件事：涵育怎样经过人与人的关系往下传。它读的是接在一起的两段关系中间的那个人：他在前一段里是受的一端，在后一段里是给的一端。这一篇是系列的 Paper 0，最后写，最先放；它不立新命题，说明这个系列在 SAE 里站在哪里、读什么、不读什么、怎样读，并把七篇的命题汇在一处。

主线三句。传不是谁的目的，是人与人有了关系以后的结果；涵育这一边的传不求，只在回看里读；传过去的可以朝殖民，也可以朝涵育。一个人肯受，长开了自己的判断，再去涵育另一个人，是涵育传播链上的一环；带殖民方向的做法往下重复，不以中间那个人先长开自己的判断为条件，到了下一个人那里占没占住另读；涵育往下一环，要等中间那个人自己的三件。能教的是内容，能给的是条件，能被看见的是一个人怎样站；往下的那一步，每一环都从中间那个人自己重新开始。

这个系列反殖民的传播，「反」只落在第一人称的两件上：不让它占住自己，自己不去这样对别人。它不指认哪一个人、哪一门传承。它只读成年人，只读经过关系的那一路；宣传与平台的规则在制度一侧。它读一环，每一环不保证；共同体层、跨世代的趋势是道德律系列读的另一个尺度，两处不在一层，不相抵。

第一节 位置

SAE 写一个人，写制度，也写人与人之间。写人与人之间的那一侧，关系论立了所有关系都具备的，各支读了爱情、婚姻、合作、亲子、师生。涵育论另起一线，读一个人怎样为另一个人自己的法给条件。

这些都读一段关系：两端，之间发生了什么。有一件事落在它们的缝里。涵育论在摘要里把它交了出来：涵育的来源写两端，场合与传播链归写人与人之间的那一侧。1 师生论登记过：被教过的人后来怎样教别人，涵育在人与人之间怎样传，是之后的题目。2 关系论的一篇专论也登记：涵育的传播，另写。3

这个系列接这三处登记。它不读一段关系，读两段关系在一个人身上相接的那一处。它因此不是关系论的又一支：它不读某一种关系，读的是随便哪两段关系之间的一件事。它也不是涵育论的续篇：涵育论读怎样给，这里读给过以后，经过受的那个人，到了下一个人那里的是什么。

第三节 立场

这个系列有一个立场：反殖民的传播。

「反」只落在第一人称的两件上。反自己被殖民：不让落到自己身上的占住自己判断的位置。反自己去殖民别人：照样灌不做，反着灌也不做。这两件合起来，第三篇叫打断。

所以这个系列不扣帽子。它不给一个人、一门传承、一个行当、一个账号贴「殖民」，不写成对别人怎样传的批评。第二篇用的是设例，不用史例，不指认真实的人。方向不是标签；6 哪一处占住了一个人，没有可保证闭合的判准，别人不替他判。

这个立场也不发应当。这个系列不说受过涵育的人应当往下给，不说谁应当打断什么。它读结构：两边往下的时候，在中间那个人身上各要什么。

第四节 读法

七篇里用到的读法，汇在这里。

经过一个人的三样。 内容：知识、技艺、说法、规矩，可以教，可以写下来。占住的那一处：灌进来并占住了判断位置的。站法：朝另一个人给而不替。三样可以同在一个人身上，分开读，不互相抵销。

链可以说，不作承担者。 一条链拆成两两之间的作用分别记账；经过第三人，不免除前面那一段。7 不给一条链合总方向，不记总账；师门、学派、传统、一代人不作主词。

怎样相接要有材料。 两段各自成立、方向相似，不足以说前一段经过乙进了后一段。史料照这一条读：只有几个名字和几次交往的，不记作一环。

设例里的结论是设定。 条件开没开、判断成没成、位置占没占，由例子设定；一句话、一次操作、一件事的缺席，可以是材料，不独自给出上述判断。材料不够分的，记目前还不知道。没有合适的真实案例，这个系列用设例。

记录与不加总。 内容的接续可以安排、核、记录；这些记录不量出、不认证、也不加总本体一侧的所得。正文不用人数说涵育那一层。

不拿下游倒判上游。 乙后来怎样对丙，不改判甲当初对乙的那一次。后来出现的新材料可以纠正读数；这不是改判。

不写必然，不比强弱。 不写殖民必然往下传，不写它到了下一个人那里必然占住；不写涵育传不下去，也不写它总会传下去。正文不比两边的难易、快慢、强弱。基础论文里有一句说到两边的不对称，8 这个系列只把它作引文，自己写的是那一处结构：差别在中间那个人身上，一边不等他，一边等他三件。

沿用的。 DD 是姿态，不是人的标签，层按一次运作记；收益层与本体层两本账，各记各的；规定本身认而不判；读数写明对谁、哪件事、哪个时段、凭什么材料，材料不够停在未定；不读心；史料只读成年作者自己写下并公开的，只读他怎样写。

第五节 同字的词

  • 传播 / 传导。 「传播」在这个系列指从一段关系到下一段关系，经过一个人。基础论文里的「传导」指一次作用从制度一层经关系一层到一个人身上。9 两词不换用。信息论、物理各篇里的「传播」，人类学系列里文明尺度上的「传播」，是另外的义。
  • 链、一环、起点。 一环指一个人身上受与给接上了；在所读的这一条来路上，起点上那个人的站法不是从前一段涵育接来的，他不是一环，他在别处受过什么另读；链不作承担者。
  • 三件 / 三样。 三件是一环里中间那个人自己的：肯受，长开，去给。三样有两处：涵育论与行动论里给的人评估时不替对方答的愿不愿意、适不适合、时机成没成；这个系列说的经过乙的内容、占住的那一处、站法。
  • 殖民。 两义。关系论取宽义，是一次作用的方向；价值论在价值发生场上取窄义，是一个状态，并写明窄义是宽义在那个场上的一个特定落法。10 这个系列拿殖民与涵育对照，用宽义；说落在一个人身上的那一处，借窄义，是推及。价值论同处说涵育的对立面不是殖民，那是窄义那张表上的话，与这里的对照不在一张表上。
  • 灌输。 留给师生论的专义：已经替对方书写、挤占了他自己的展开，是侵。这个系列的主材料是没有越线的那些；说方向时用「灌入并占住」。
  • 打断。 指第一人称的两面。上游写代际传导的那一种打断，落在冲突里第一次选择不殖民；两处相接而不相同。
  • 求。 只取窄义：预定，照它制作，照它验收。要核的是验的是什么：有没有把对方自己的判断与站法做成验收的标准。对着条件、技术内容、两人认下的具体安排的定、做、核，不叫求。开放的盼望不在其中。
  • 站法。 取大学论讲引领时的那一义。上游另有两处指作者自己立的一套姿态，义不同。
  • 被看见 / 远距离观察。 被看见是引领的那一面，落在看的人多知道了一个位置上。道德律系列里的远距离观察是辨识，落在对被看者的判断上。
  • 涵育。 照涵育论：人对人给条件。教育篇把教、涵育、引领分作三件，那里的涵育是专义；这个系列引到那一处时，就地标明。
  • 所得。 本体一侧的，立法活动的展开；收益层上的得失照收益记。

第六节 与成长必然性的界

道德律系列有一个强的主张：14DD 向 15DD 的成长，在长期、跨世代的尺度上是结构必然的。11 这个系列每一篇都在说「不保证」：受过的人不一定肯受，肯受的不一定长开，长开的不一定去给，给了对方也不一定接。两处怎样相处？

只说三句。

第一句：这个系列读一环，每一环不保证。这是在一个人、两段关系的尺度上说的。

第二句：道德律那一篇说的是共同体层、跨世代的汇总趋势，它自己写明了尺度：多代，不是个体的生命时间；成长必然性是跨世代的统计算子，不是每个具体节点、每个具体时代的必然。12 那一篇的必然立在四个根源上：物自体不可穷尽，承认结构，主体性作为活动，经济先验机制。13 四条里没有一条是每一次涵育都传下去。跨世代那一层，它写的是下一代成长在 15DD 密度更高的环境里；这一层里有人到人的累积，它在汇总的尺度上说，不落到哪一环。

第三句：两句不在一层，不相抵。一环不保证，推不出趋势不成立；趋势成立，也推不出哪一环一定成。这个系列不替那一篇另证一次，也不由一环去改它。许多环怎样汇成一个趋势，中间隔着的东西，这个系列没有读。道德律系列有两处说到人到人的累积，都在汇总的尺度上。14

道德律 Paper 9 在机构的尺度上还有一句，可以放在这里作界的另一边：宏观的相变需要等待，微观的承认绝不等待。15 这个系列读的全在微观那一边：这一次，对这一个人。

第七节 分工

与涵育论。 涵育论读怎样给：给到哪里，怎样不替，什么时候不给。这个系列读给过以后。它照用涵育论的三问、先评估、对内是不得不朝外不是债，不重立。

与师生论、亲子论。 两个系列各读一种关系，都以未成年的一段为主场。这个系列从成年以后接着读，认它们已读的，不回头读。师生论把「引领」交了过来，第五篇接。

与关系论、《恰为正和》。 关系论给了门槛、方向的两义、两两记账、沉积的读法，这个系列照用。《恰为正和》给了求的窄义、做出了、两层分开、不记账不抵账，这个系列把它们推到「往下传」上；每一处推及都标了，由这个系列自己担。乙回过来给甲，是那一篇的事。

与大学论。 大学论读构怎样为涵育与引领留出地方，这个系列读人这一层。大学论的判断力定义、判断不能随规则交付、引领不登记不考核，这里照用。

与制度一侧。 宣传、平台的规则、机构的运营与传承安排，都交过去。宣传论还没有写；交过去的各项只登记，不借它的结论。

第九节 余项与留着的问题

这个系列读完以后，留着这样几处。

  1. 起点上的碰撞。 链有起点，起点上的人的来路是实际来往里的碰撞。碰撞怎样使一个人站到那个方向上，这个系列只引了上游，没有读。
  2. 受的一面，事后松开。 上游说内在殖民不能仅从内部打破，并列了外部介入成立的三个条件。成年人之间，这三个条件怎样成立，这个系列没有逐条核。
  3. 未成年时的那一段怎样接到成年以后。 这个系列认它，不读它。一个人未成年时受的，怎样进了他成年以后的给，中间的相接要有材料；这样的材料怎样读，留着。
  4. 回过来的那一种。 乙回过来给甲，只登记。
  5. 史料的限度。 一环要三样材料：受的一段、给的一段、怎样相接，都要本人或当事人写下。这个系列找到的史料里，三样齐的没有。这是材料难得，还是这件事本来就少有人写下，这里不判。
  6. 许多环与趋势。 一环不保证，趋势在另一个尺度上。中间怎样过去，没有读。第六节注里指了上游说到人到人累积的两处。
  7. 宣传论未写。 那一论要读的是规定本身。真实的个案里，规定的部分与个人另添的部分怎样分开核，方法上游已有：先查规定是什么；拿不到规定的，登记背景未核。缺的是具体的材料，不是那一论。
  8. AI。 工具不占中间那个位置。一个人借 AI 去给，经过他的是什么，登记，不展开。

第十节 全系列命题

第一篇　传是结果

CB1　传是结果，不是目的。人与人有了关系，前一段关系留在一个人身上的，会进入他对另一个人的那一段。进去的可以朝殖民，也可以朝涵育。

CB2　经过乙的有三样，分开读：内容可以教，可以写下来；占住的那一处往下重复不以乙先长开为条件；站法教不了。三样可以同在一个人身上，不互相抵销。

CB3　链有起点。在所读的这一条来路上，起点上那个人的这一种站法，不是从前一段涵育接来的；他不是第四篇所说的一环。它的来路是实际来往里的碰撞。他在别的关系、别的事上受过什么，另读。所以涵育的传播不是这个方向唯一的来路；一条链断了，不等于此后没有。

CB4　读的单位是一个人身上两段关系相接的那一处。甲对乙，乙后来对丙，读乙。给与受两个位置按一次运作记；甲、乙、丙是两段关系里的位置，不预设三个不同的人，也不预设三代。乙是同一个人：前一次在受的一端，后一次在给的一端。一环按人说，中间的长开在回看里读。

CB5　链可以说，不作承担者。一条链拆成两两之间的作用分别记账；经过乙，不免除甲那一段；不给一条链合总方向，不记总账。

CB6　两段各自成立、方向相似，还不足以说前一段经过乙进了后一段；要有材料读出它怎样相接。不要求它是唯一的来源，也不要求乙照搬甲。

CB7　内容的接续可以安排，学会没有可以核，传到哪里可以记；这些记录不量出、不认证，也不加总本体一侧的所得。站法与每一次作用逐件读，不拿涵育了多少人作完成的指标。内容传得广，不由此读出站法也到了；内容没有人接，也不由此读出什么都没有留下。

CB8　不拿下游倒判上游。乙后来怎样对丙，不改判甲当初对乙的那一次；甲那一次的所得在那一次里，不因传没传下去而添减。后来出现的新材料，可以纠正对那一次的误读。

第二篇　殖民怎样传

CB9　殖民有两义。价值论第三篇在价值发生场上取窄义：殖民述的是一个状态，一项内容占住了位置。这个系列借这一义说落在一个人身上的那一处（推及，自担）；说一次作为朝哪里去，照关系论 H8，是方向，不是标签。

CB10　殖民性的做法往下重复，不以乙先长开自己的判断、认下所传的东西、或有传的动机为条件。乙可以照样对丙，也可以不照做；到了丙那里占没占住他的判断位置，照丙这一端另读。

CB11　灌进来的东西里可以连着一句对人就该这样：内容与做法一起往下。「灌输」取师生论 TS11 的义，那里的主词是老师的位置；用到成年人之间，是推及。

CB12　占住的那一处用位置读：哪几位开着，哪几位封着，按内容域与时段写，不给一个人、一段关系一个总的方向。它可以不经过冲突；一段来往里没有争执，不作那一处没有被占住的证据。

第三篇　涵育的传播：打断

CB13　打断有两面，都是第一人称的：不让落到自己身上的占住自己判断的位置；不去殖民别人，照样灌是，反着灌也是。只是没有机会、暂时停下、或换了一种管法，不凭外观记作打断。

CB14　两面可以不同时到。落在身上的那一处还在，对下一个人已经不去灌：给的一面可以先到。不去灌，不等于已经能承认。

CB15　两面的来路分开读，不互相排除。给的一面，是这个人面对另一个人时的一次选择；这一次不往下灌，不必先由前一段涵育接来。受的一面，已经占住的那一处，上游写内在殖民不能仅从内部打破；这里可以读到另一个人给的条件。哪一面都可以有别人给的条件，哪一面的那一步都仍是他自己的。

CB16　打断本身还不是涵育；结构上读作朝 15DD 方向去的一步。上游的那一种打断，落在冲突里第一次选择不殖民、不撤回对对方的承认，只要入口，不要成熟。这个系列的打断另分受与给两面，按各次运作读方向；不由哪一次不再照做，认证他整个人在哪一层。他自己这一端，由他自己确认。

CB17　受的一面怎样知道那一处占住了自己，没有可保证闭合的判准。回看的办法照美学射线十三：拿掉那个人给的尺子试试；可以看错。别人不替他判。

第四篇　涵育的传播：一环

CB18　一个人肯受，自己的判断长开了，再去涵育另一个人，是涵育传播链上的一环。

CB19　这三件都在中间那个人自己：肯不肯受，长不长开，去不去给、给的时候替不替。它们不是涵育论说的那三样（愿不愿意、适不适合、时机成没成）：那三样是给的人评估时不替对方答的，这三件是一环成立要有的。

CB20　肯受不等于照单全收。受的人可以不同意，可以改，可以只取一部分。

CB21　判断力照行动论的定义读。它不能随规则一并交付，只能练；照做、结论一致，不证明这一件已经成了。

CB22　一环算到「做出了」为止：他承担了涵育的方向，他的作为也实际打开了另一个人的条件。乙向丙这一端做出了，不等于丙已经接成下一环；丙肯不肯受、长不长开、后来去不去给，各自另读。给了而条件没有实际打开的，照实记。

CB23　乙对丙的同一次作为可以两向都有：对一处腾出了空间，对另一处灌入并占住。两向分开记，不合成净值。

CB24　去给也是练：给一个人，要判断他卡在哪里，给到哪一步为止。第三件回到第二件上。

CB25　往下给不是债：受过，不因此欠着谁。站在 15DD 方向上的人，涵育对内是他自己的不得不，朝对方是方向，不是债。这一次、对这一个人给不给，先评估，判的是条件；时机未成，是这一次先不做，不因此就不在这个方向上。没有给，回看下来若是判错了，或者判了由自己给、这一判还在却没有给，照涵育论记没做到；这一笔由他自己的那一判来，不由受过来。长开了而没有去给，不倒判前一次涵育落空，也不说明谁的品格。

CB26　每一环另有一本时间与心力的账，不拿这一端的所得去抵。

第五篇　涵育的传播：引领

CB27　引领是一个人在自己的生活与来往里活出承认、被另一个人看见的那一面。有自己的活动，不直接就是引领。

CB28　引领让人知道那个位置在；它不生出 15DD。给条件、被看见、实际来往里的碰撞，分开读，不预定三样由同一个人在同一次来往里齐备；后一个人的那一步仍是他自己的。

CB29　引领不登记，不考核，不验收。把一套做法定成对方照做的样板，传下去的是可以照做的内容。一个人有意把自己怎样判断、怎样回应不同意公开出来，其中有没有引领，仍看实际的作用与对方看见了什么。

CB30　被看见的也包括回落以后怎样回头。

CB31　由具体的做法看见的，是一种不必照搬的站法：后一个人待人不必像前一个人。隔着书与屏幕，也可能看见一种站法：在作者当下怎样待读者里，在留下来的作为本身里，也可以经由对一次实际来往的记述；记述要核它的根据。后来的读者看见了，不倒推作者当时朝这位读者做了什么。一个人怎样只把人当手段，同样被看见，那不叫引领，归第二篇。

第六篇　涵育的传播：自媒体

CB32　一个人的言说、写作或留下的东西，可指认地实际进入一位读者、观众的发生条件，两端就有一段关系；单向的也算。一个人面向许多人，各段关系分别成立，分别记账。写书、讲演、自媒体同此。

CB33　做自媒体的人是中间那个位置：他受什么，他给什么，分开读。他这一侧，读的是流量这把尺子占没占住他自己判断做什么、怎样做的位置。他真正认下的是什么，别人不替他判；公开的片子与来往里位置的开合，读得到。这一篇取材只用设例。个人与机构的界不按账号分，按一次运作分：内容由机构规定的部分交制度一侧；出镜的人另添了什么，另看。

CB34　流量在收益层上，可以记，可以求；涵育那一层不在那张表上。求流量、求商业上做成、用留存数据，都不独自说明观众只作为一项进入他的表；要看具体的写法与来往怎样对待观众的判断。

CB35　不拿涵育了多少人作指标。能定、能做、能核的，是每一件东西留没留空间：给不给理由，留不留观众不同意的位置，替不替他先答。

CB36　一个动作可以在许多段关系里打开条件。对哪一段实际打开了，哪一段就记做出了；观众后来接不接成一环，各在各人。没有反馈时，停在未定，不反过来说什么都没有发生。

CB37　平台上也可能被看见：他怎样待观众，怎样认错。接第五篇。

第七篇　涵育的传播：不求

CB38　涵育不求成功。对方长不长开、往不往下给，不预定，不验收。能定、能做、能核的，是可能：自己这一次作为留没留空间、打开了什么。

CB39　推到传播上：不求它传下去；能做的是让它传得下去的条件在。涵育这一边的传只在回看里读，与《恰为正和》的恰同形：读得出的，照实记；读不出的，也照实记；回看可以看错。

CB40　这里排除的求取窄义：预定，照它制作，照它验收。要核的是验的是什么：有没有把对方自己的判断与站法，预先做成一份制作与验收的标准；有，换了叫法也在排除之内。对条件、对技术内容、对两人认下的具体安排的核，另读。没有验收的盼望，不在其中。

CB41　替对方预定他怎样待下一个人，这一处带殖民这个方向，动机不改变这一读。给的人不因给过而在对方怎样对下一个人上占一份。

CB42　一门手艺的接续可以商量、安排，教的内容学会没有也可以核；这些不替对方预定他怎样待下一个人。把后一件预定下来，照它制作、照它验收，就落在这里排除的求里。以名分相系是其中一种做法，有没有名分本身不作判据。求到内容有人接，不由此读出站法也到了。

CB43　传下来了，不抵其中哪一环的伤害；没有传下去的，照实说没有，不补成总有影响留下；不认证谁在哪一层。

CB44　不求不是记号。不求本身不保证什么，也不保证方向；殖民往下传，也可以不是谁的目的。区别不在有没有想它传下去的愿望，在往下的那一步落在哪里。
