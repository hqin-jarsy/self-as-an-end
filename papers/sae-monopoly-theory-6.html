AI and Monopoly
AI 与垄断

Han Qin (秦汉) · Independent Researcher · 2026
DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.24377562 · Full PDF on Zenodo · CC BY 4.0
Abstract

This paper reads AI, written as concentration in formation and its structural risks. The title uses "and" so as not to presuppose that this area is already monopolized; this paper judges neither that AI has already entered a stage of monopoly nor that it will not. AI divides into several layers: computing power and chips, cloud, models, data, distribution; each layer is read by the ways of examining in the earlier papers. Control in partnerships and investment is examined by what is actual: three kinds of material are examined, control, dependence and information; the presence of any one item does not by itself complete a conclusion of control (LD12). The United Kingdom Competition and Markets Authority's closure of its inquiries into four AI partnerships and investments, and the German Bundeskartellamt's conclusion on one of these partnerships, all stop at the jurisdictional threshold of merger review; each case is written by its own decision, and they are read neither as establishing that there is no control nor as finding that there is a problem. When the models of one firm or a few have become the entry point through which many people obtain information and make judgments, what they press on is the materials of judgment; checks increase with this depth; this proposition speaks of the entry point being held, not of machines replacing human judgment or creativity; when judgment is handed to machines, the bearing is not handed over with it (LD13). While concentration is still forming, checks must already be there: the normative reason is that checks are needed within the line, and that scale does not decide from what moment a construct must guard the line and be questioned; setting them while substitutes remain and dependence has not yet become entrenched keeps open the possibility of taking over and changing direction; none of this is a prediction of where an industry is going; research and public discussion are material and entry points for questioning, not final verdicts (LD14). Research notes several interrelated structural risks; there is dynamism now, and in segments where hardware, data and the like are concentrated, existing positions may become entrenched in the long run. The constructed example running through this paper is the four terms between a model company and its cloud partner; in the far future, after machines have taken over Base Layer work such as farming and drinking water, one household's day, with food, water and payments all passing through the same place. The far future is imagined, not predicted.

Introduction

In the spring of Year 27, four researchers who had come out of Oakgrove College founded Beacon in Apple City to make general-purpose models. In the autumn of the same year, Stone Granary, a computing and cloud company in the Highland State, invested in Beacon, taking about twenty percent. There were four terms:

Beacon's training and operation must use Stone Granary's cloud; Stone Granary supplies computing power at half the market price, and the discounted part counts as Stone Granary's investment.

Of the revenue from Beacon's models, Stone Granary takes fifteen percent, until it has recovered three times its investment.

Stone Granary holds one observer seat, without a vote, and may attend board meetings and obtain board materials.

Before Beacon gives another cloud company the right to use its models, it must first ask Stone Granary; within thirty days Stone Granary may take them first on the same terms.

In Year 28 Beacon Assistant went online; from Year 29 it charged companies and institutions, while individuals could use a basic tier free of charge. From Year 29 to Year 31, about seventy percent of Bluekiln's workshops used it to write product pages in foreign languages, keep accounts and answer letters from buyers elsewhere; four clinics in Apple City, and the township offices of Willow Ferry and Bluekiln, used it to issue queue numbers and answer questions.

In Year 31, about forty percent of Beacon's revenue came from sales through Stone Granary's cloud: Stone Granary had put Beacon's models into its cloud service catalogue.

Both faces are here. Stone Granary's money and half-price computing power let four researchers get started; Beacon Assistant opened a road to buyers elsewhere for the workshops. The four terms also tied Beacon to Stone Granary.

This paper asks: a minority stake, an observer without a vote, half-price computing power, the obligation to use Stone Granary's cloud, revenue sharing, a right of first refusal: taken together, where does control lie? Nominally Stone Granary has no vote; in fact, which of Beacon's decisions can it sway? Beacon opened roads for workshops and clinics: how is this face recorded, and can it offset the other?

There are several further questions: many people obtain information and make judgments through Beacon, and how is this depth examined; concentration is still forming, and when are checks set; and what will things look like after machines have taken over the work of the Base Layer?

§1 Layers

AI can be read in several layers.

Computing power and chips. The computing power needed to train and run models comes from chips and data centers. Paper 5 read Stone Granary's scheduling of computing power: seventy percent at one firm, "strategic partners first."

Cloud. Computing power is mostly rented through cloud. A position in the cloud layer stands at the entrance to the layer of models. Paper 5 established that control of one layer does not extend into the power to shut the door on the next (LD10).

Models. General-purpose models are the center of this paper: one model can be connected to many places, to write, keep accounts, issue queue numbers, choose goods, read filings.

Data. Training needs data, users leave data, and data goes back into improving models. Paper 4 read that data about a person is not material for placing his leaving under another's control (LD8).

Distribution. How models reach people: an assistant, a default entry in a market, a government's system for public business. Paper 4 read Ask-the-Way: the default entry on the market's front page, with only one assistant allowed to place orders.

Each layer can be read by the ways of examining in the earlier papers: control recognized by what is actual, depth reaching the materials of judgment, examined as it is now (Paper 1); the road of questioning and the efficacy of questioning (Paper 2); names (Paper 3); a chokepoint making rules, data, several firms side by side (Paper 4); one layer extended into the next, integration and separability (Paper 5). This paper does not redo these, and reads only three questions AI adds: control in partnerships and investment; the materials of judgment; concentration in formation and early checks. Last it reads the far future.

Research has looked at what these layers look like together. The United Kingdom Competition and Markets Authority's update paper "AI Foundation Models," published on 11 April 2024, notes three interrelated risks: firms that control critical inputs restricting access; firms with existing positions in consumer- or business-facing markets using them to distort people's choice of foundation model services; and partnerships among key players strengthening market power along the value chain. The same paper also acknowledges that such arrangements can promote competition. What research notes are risks, not findings; this paper cites it as research and does not treat it as a final verdict.

§2 Control in Partnerships and Investment

Back to Beacon and Stone Granary.

Nominally, Stone Granary holds only about twenty percent, and its observer has no vote. About twenty percent of the shares and one observer seat without a vote cannot, on their own, complete a conclusion of control; what rights these shares carry, and how the rest of the equity is distributed, the example does not give.

Political Economy has set down a sentence: nominal holding and mobilization come apart, and how much is held is not how much can be mobilized; the position that can mobilize accumulation can be one person, a board, or a coordinated arrangement (Political Economy Paper 1, E2). Control is examined by what is actual, not by name.

How is it examined by what is actual? When Political Economy asks about control within the line, it has three questions: who can activate it; what actual efficacy it has on reasons, actions, rules or the holding of office; how the party questioned cannot cancel it on its own (Political Economy Paper 3, E16). The original asks whether those acted on by an economic position have actual control over its relevant decisions; here it is changed to the effect of a partner or investor on another construct (extension, own undertaking): over which of Beacon's decisions does Stone Granary have actual efficacy?

Take the four terms apart into three kinds of material.

Control. About twenty percent of the equity; one observer seat, attending the board, without a vote; a right of first refusal: before giving its models to another cloud, Beacon must first ask Stone Granary. The first two, on their own, cannot yet show what they can sway; the third falls directly on one of Beacon's decisions: on whose cloud its models go. If Beacon wants to sell its models on another firm's cloud, it must first get past Stone Granary.

Dependence. Training and operation must use Stone Granary's cloud; computing power at half price, with the discount counted as investment; revenue sharing of fifteen percent, until three times is recovered. Every training run Beacon does and every copy it sells pass through Stone Granary. By Year 31, forty percent of revenue came from sales through Stone Granary's cloud. Dependence needs no votes: to switch clouds, Beacon would first have to think whether its training system could be moved, whether half-price computing power exists elsewhere, and how the forty percent of revenue would be made up. Paper 5 read what came later: by Year 36, Beacon's training system could run only on Stone Granary's platform.

Information. The observer can obtain board materials. Stone Granary also runs a cloud business itself and has other customers; through this seat it obtains Beacon's board materials, and what is in them the example does not record. The right to obtain materials alone is not yet enough to judge that it sways Beacon's decisions; it may also fall elsewhere: if the materials contain Beacon's plans and prices, Stone Granary would thereby have information unavailable to others in its dealings with other model companies.

Read the three kinds of material together, and what is to be asked is: when the interests of Beacon and Stone Granary diverge, say Beacon wants to put its models on another firm's cloud, or switch to a cheaper supplier of computing power, can Beacon decide for itself? The example does not record that Beacon ever tried. This paper reads only this: the nominal "no vote" does not reveal the right of first refusal, the dependence and the information written into the terms; how much effect they actually have must be examined separately.

The presence of any one item does not by itself complete a conclusion of control. Being required to use a firm's cloud is a term many start-ups have signed; an observer seat is a common arrangement in investment; revenue sharing is a kind of return. This paper does not judge them by their names, and reads only what effect they have together in one place; nor does it say that Stone Granary already controls Beacon in fact, since the example records only the terms.

Research has listed the features of such partnerships. On 17 January 2025, the United States Federal Trade Commission published a staff report on a 6(b) study of three partnerships between three cloud service providers and two developers of generative AI. The features the report notes include: significant equity stakes and some revenue sharing; varying degrees of consultation, control and exclusivity rights; a considerable part of the investment having to be spent on the partner's cloud services; discounted computing power; the possibility that such arrangements affect access to inputs such as computing power and engineering talent; contractual and technical switching costs; and the partner's access to sensitive technical and business information others cannot obtain. The materials the partners supplied run to September 2024, and public materials to January 2025 were used. What research notes are features and possible effects, not findings; this paper cites it as research. The four terms in the Kingdom State example are written as a combination of several features, and do not copy the terms or proportions of any real pair of companies.

In reality, one kind of regulatory decision already made shows how such partnerships are handled in one jurisdiction. The United Kingdom Competition and Markets Authority has made decisions closing its inquiries into four AI partnerships and investments in turn, all concluding that they did not constitute merger situations for it to review; the reasons are of three kinds, and each is written on its own.

Microsoft and OpenAI (5 March 2025). The Authority considered that Microsoft had had material influence over OpenAI's commercial policy since 2019; this time it found no evidence that this influence had risen to de facto control. Therefore, as to the change in the level of control under review, there was no relevant merger situation. The Authority stated expressly that this conclusion does not amount to finding that there are no competition concerns.

On the same partnership, the German Bundeskartellamt also reached a jurisdictional conclusion on 15 November 2023. It considered that Microsoft had acquired material competitive influence over OpenAI as early as 2019, or at the latest when the partnership was deepened in 2021; at that time OpenAI's activities in Germany were not yet substantial, becoming so only in 2023; the 2023 investment could not be considered to have deepened the existing link relevant to competition, so no notification was required. It also said that if Microsoft were to increase its influence on OpenAI in the future, it would have to be re-examined whether there is an obligation to notify.

Amazon and Anthropic (27 September 2024); Google and Anthropic (19 November 2024). In the former, neither of the two thresholds, turnover and share of supply, was met, and the Authority reached no conclusion on material influence; in the latter, the Authority did not consider that Google had acquired material influence over Anthropic through the partnership.

Microsoft and Mistral AI (17 May 2024). The Authority did not consider that Microsoft had acquired material influence over Mistral.

This series writes these as the two bodies have decided, and gives no verdict of its own. These decisions all stop at the jurisdictional threshold of merger review: on material influence and changes in the level of control, each case is written by its own decision; what they did not proceed to is a subsequent substantive assessment of competitive effects. They cannot be merged into a general reading that there is no control or no competition concern, nor read the other way as finding that there is a problem. The level of control the two bodies judged under their respective legal standards is not the kind of control this paper examines through three kinds of material either. Put together, they serve as an example for this proposition: several arrangements that look alike yield several different reasons at one body; arrangements that look alike must be read by their particular terms, their actual relations and the question under review.

§3 The Materials of Judgment

Paper 1 established that the depth of a chokepoint reaches not only prices and livelihoods but also information, attention and the materials of judgment (LD2). In AI, this depth is most evident.

Once a model is connected to many places, many people obtain information and form judgments through it. The examples already contain several such places.

A buyer on Crossways Market says what he wants, and Ask-the-Way chooses one or two items for him; he no longer looks at a list.

The Office of Model Affairs uses Beacon Assistant to read each firm's filings and write initial review summaries; Beacon's own filings are read by it too.

In the drought year, which district's water is cut first each time is decided by the dispatching model; the full calculation the City Council cannot read.

The daughter of one household works at a sorting station, and her shifts are set by Beacon's model.

In these places, what the model presses on is not a price, but where a judgment comes from: what to buy, what is wrong in a filing, whose water is cut first, who works on which day. When the models of one firm or a few become the entry point through which many people obtain information and make judgments, what they press on is the materials of judgment.

Political Economy has established that checks increase with the power-face, and that the power-face is compared by its particular effects: whom it converges, down to which dimension, how many must pass through it, how great the cost of walking away (Political Economy Paper 3, E14). This paper follows it: an entry point that presses on the materials of judgment goes deeper; checks increase with this depth (per E14; extension of the depth of E7; own undertaking).

One misreading must be guarded against. This proposition speaks of the entry point for obtaining information being held by one firm or a few; it does not say that machines replace human judgment or creativity. The buyer can still go and look at the list; the Office can still read for itself; the City Council can still review the rules. Human judgment and creativity are not replaced by an entry point; but people's road to the materials of judgment can be narrowed by an entry point. What this paper examines is the latter: to what degree the road is held by one firm or a few, and how the holder is questioned.

When judgment is handed to machines, the bearing is not handed over with it. The Theory of Government says that a tool holds no bearing, and that the bearing falls on the positions that hand the judgment out, set how it is to be used, and act on its results (Theory of Government Paper 1, G8). The Theory of the Company specialist paper says that where a company deploys a system, "the address of attribution is the construct that deploys it; the company cannot present an algorithm or AI as another address" (The Company in the Age of AI, ZN2); and that a judgment entrusted to a position requires that the position be able to judge, that the information needed can be obtained and understood, and that it can change the outcome (ibid., ZN1). The originals speak of governments and companies; here they are extended to the various positions that make judgments through a model's entry point (extension). Where the Office uses Beacon Assistant to read filings, the bearing is with the Office; where the operator uses a model to allocate water, the bearing is with the City Council and the Water Department, which set the goals of allocation and handed over the dispatching, and the operator holds its own accountability toward the construct that engaged it; where Crossways uses Ask-the-Way to choose goods for people, the bearing is with Crossways. The model itself is not an address.

§4 Concentration in Formation

What does AI look like now?

Research has given a "mixed picture." The OECD report "Artificial Intelligence markets," published on 10 July 2026, notes that the foundation-model layer has been dynamic over the past three years; that structural risks remain at each layer of the value chain; and that in segments structurally concentrated in hardware, data and the like, existing positions "may become entrenched in the long run." What research notes are risks and possibilities, not findings.

This paper writes accordingly: concentration in formation, and its structural risks. It judges neither that AI has already entered a stage of monopoly nor that it will not.

"Examined as it is now" must be used in both directions here. In one direction: dynamism now does not answer for later; early road-opening does not serve as a later name. In Year 29 Beacon opened a road for the workshops, and that is true; later it wrote an interface that allowed only itself priority, used a format for Notes that other firms cannot read, and the head of the review panel came from its safety department; these are examined by their own effects and are not offset by that face of Year 29. In the other direction: possible entrenchment later does not condemn the present. When research says "may," it means may; this paper does not fill it out into any firm's already being entrenched.

In formation means that substitutes remain. The Open Model Group was still releasing (before Year 34); the other two model companies are still there; a workshop owner who wants to switch has to rebuild from scratch. This stretch of time is when the readings are still changing.

§5 Early Checks

While concentration is still forming, when are checks set?

One view says: wait until it crosses the line. It has not crossed the line yet, there is no monopoly yet, and setting checks now is punishing in advance, and may also press down what has not yet grown.

This paper does not take that view. Early checks do not wait for the line to be crossed.

The normative reason has two places upstream. Political Economy says: "Growth must be subject to checks; this is needed within the line, not only once the line is crossed" (Political Economy Paper 5, E27). Checks are not a punishment for crossing the line, but an ordinary arrangement for a position with a power-face. The same series also says: "Scale and power-face affect the form and strength of checks; they do not decide from what moment a construct must guard the line and be questioned" (Political Economy Paper 3, E14). While a position is still small, checks can be light and few; but its being questioned does not begin only on the day it grows large.

On the side of fact there is another kind of reason, which must be stated separately. Setting checks while substitutes remain and dependence has not yet become entrenched can keep open the possibility of taking over and changing direction; what is kept, how much is paid, and how much is saved later must be examined separately for the same arrangement. When the Office of Model Affairs was set up in Year 33 it had no testing equipment of its own; the laboratory was built only in Year 36. Had the laboratory been built three years earlier, the Office would have had an extra pair of eyes of its own in those three years of reading filings, looking at self-tests and granting approvals; the one result it found not to match might have come out sooner.

Neither of these reasons is a prediction. The normative reason says that checks ought to be there; the factual reason says that setting them early can keep open the possibility of taking over and changing direction, at a cost examined under E17. Neither says that early checks necessarily avoid monopoly, nor that without them things will necessarily end in monopoly. What an industry will become this paper does not predict.

Setting checks early also has costs, stated as they stand. Political Economy says that if checks affect the capacity to produce, to serve and to give conditions, the cost, the alternatives available and the reasons for the choice are to be stated as they stand (Political Economy Paper 3, E17); it also says that too many veto points can also leave a position unable to do anything (ibid.). Early checks must state their effects on innovation and on the capacity to produce and to supply; a set of rules that comes too early and weighs too heavy can press down what has not yet grown: the safety rules read in Paper 3 have this face to be examined. Early checks likewise must state their costs, and leave room to change them as things are now (under E17; under LD3).

A few arrangements that can be made at the rule layer are given as examples: keeping access, interoperability and preparation for separability, so that other firms' models can connect to the entry points of distribution and the interfaces between cloud and models are public; building in advance the reviewer's capacity to obtain information and test independently, like that laboratory; setting up in advance channels of supervision and questioning, so that those stopped by a gate can ask. These are all examples, and their form is examined by effect, cost and alternatives.

One more thing is already being done: research and public discussion. The several studies cited above, the Competition and Markets Authority's list of risks, the features of partnerships listed by the Federal Trade Commission, the OECD's mixed picture, are all questions raised while concentration is still forming. They are material and entry points for questioning; they are not final verdicts. That a study says "there are risks" is not a finding that anyone has crossed the line; but it points out early the places to be asked about.

§6 The Far Future: After Machines Have Taken Over the Work of the Base Layer

This section is an imagined scenario. What it writes is: supposing machines can do Base Layer work such as farming and drinking water, what form monopoly could take. "Machines can do all the work of the Base Layer" is the premise of the scenario, not the conclusion of an argument; this section is written as facts in a fictional world, not as a prediction of the real future. The far future is written only for the Base Layer: food, water, payments.

The earlier papers have already read several of these matters separately.

Food. From Year 40, Hundredcraft's farm machines are driven by Beacon's model, and Fieldworks, which the two set up together, operates about seventy percent of the Kingdom State's farmland by Year 50; the machines accept only the seed and fertilizer specifications Fieldworks designates; from Year 45, the state's farm machinery subsidies, grain purchasing and storage, and subsidies to secure the harvest in disaster years are mostly received and carried out through Fieldworks; at the autumn harvest of Year 47 an update went wrong, the combines in two counties stopped for nine days, and no one in the state could be found with the people and machines to take over; after the outage, the state gave Fieldworks a special "food security" fund, to be used for backup systems. Paper 5 read one layer extended into the next, and Paper 2 read being unable to fail.

Water. From Year 42, Apple City's Water Department handed its operation and maintenance to the operator set up jointly by Hundredcraft and Beacon; the department's engineers fell from forty to four; at the renewal in Year 50 the operating fee rose by about forty percent. In the drought of Year 53, whose water is cut each time is decided by the dispatching model. Paper 2 read rules written into programs, and Paper 7 reads taking over.

Payments. In Year 48 the Kingdom State set up a Basic Payment, issued through Beacon Assistant; the service windows were cut from a hundred and twenty to eighteen; in Year 52 the payment account was tied to the assistant account. Paper 4 read the manner of payment.

Each matter, taken on its own, has its effects and costs, and each has two faces: grain prices fell by thirty percent, deaths and injuries in the fields almost disappeared, leakage in the water mains fell from twenty percent to five, three villages in the north of the city were connected to water, and the payment was never late. This section brings them together in one place, set in Year 55.

One household's day. A household in Apple City's East Market: an elderly couple, their daughter, and a child at school.

The rice and vegetables in the morning come from fields Fieldworks operates, ordered through Ask-the-Way on Crossways Market and delivered; the only assistant that can place orders for her on Crossways Market is Ask-the-Way, and Ask-the-Way is driven by Beacon Assistant.

The water is supplied by the Apple City Water Department, whose operator is the one Hundredcraft and Beacon set up together. The water bill is paid in Beacon Assistant; the payment system uses Beacon's interface, and other firms' assistants face call limits and priority ordering when using it.

The two elderly parents' Basic Payment arrives through Beacon Assistant. The daughter works at one of Fieldworks' sorting stations, and her shifts are set by Beacon's model.

In the evening, the daughter wants to switch the family's assistant to another firm's. She works it out: the two parents' payment would have to be verified again at a service window, which takes about six weeks, and the nearest service window is two hours away by road; the family's three years of Notes cannot be taken along; with another firm's assistant, paying the water bill would be put further back in the queue; and with another firm's assistant, the only assistant that can place orders for her on Crossways would still be Ask-the-Way. She does not switch.

She writes a complaint in the assistant: "I want to switch, and I can't." The assistant replies: "Your request has been recorded; the relevant department will handle it." By the end of Year 55 there is no response.

What this household adds is only her day, her reckoning and her complaint; the other places are all matters the earlier papers have already read. Taken together, they show something that cannot be read from any one place on its own.

Whether there is a way out. The several burdens she reckons with add up: the payment must be verified again, Notes cannot be taken along, paying the water bill would be put further back. There is also one place that is not newly added by this switch: with a different assistant, the only one that can place orders for her on Crossways is still Ask-the-Way; this dependence does not disappear with this switch. Under the limit of Gate One, that there are terms, conditions, procedures or exit costs does not mean that exit has been sealed off (Political Economy Paper 4, E21). These items are each set by different positions: the payment and the service windows by the state, the water supply by the city government and the Water Department, the admission of assistants in the market by Crossways, while the operator, Fieldworks and Beacon each have effects of their own. That they all use one firm's model does not of itself prove that these controls have been acquired together by the model's provider. What is to be examined is the difficulty of leaving that these arrangements form together, and also, separately, the segment each position can change. She did not switch; this is not filled out into every structural channel having been sealed off.

Whom securing in the Base Layer is for. Food, water and payments are all in the Base Layer. Political Economy says that for a construct doing the work of the Base Layer, the institution must not only check but also secure this supply; "The object of securing is the supply, not that construct" (Political Economy Paper 3, E14a). The state's subsidies, purchasing and storage, and special fund flow out through Fieldworks; the service fee for the payment is paid to Beacon; the operating fee for the water is paid to the operator: is the money securing the supply of food, water and payments, or that firm? Are backup systems built inside Fieldworks still preparation for taking over? Paper 7 takes up this question.

Gate One. Political Economy says: accumulation is not converted into domination over people's Base Layer; what people need to sustain their lives is not converted into control that compresses their life-substrate, nor may money, housing or the manner of paying wages be used to place people's ability to leave, refuse or revoke a standing bond under another party's control (Political Economy Paper 4, E21). This household's payment arrives through one firm's assistant, its water bill is paid in that assistant, and its groceries are bought through Ask-the-Way, driven by the same firm's model; if she wants to leave that assistant, the payment must first be verified again. This reading falls close beside that gate. This paper does not say that the gate has already been crossed: the service windows are there, the road is there. What this paper says is that if several arrangements in the Base Layer come together in one place like this, that gate is the place to be examined item by item.

Whether questioning has effect. The complaint has a road: the assistant received it, and "the relevant department will handle it." By the end of the year there is no response. The Prequel said that being open to questioning does not cancel monopoly; Paper 2 said that the presence of the channel of questioning does not prove that questioning has effect. To which position able to change the outcome was this complaint delivered? The example does not record.

The same form as despotism. The Prequel read the same form through this household's day. Here Beacon is not identified as a despot; only the structures that limit exit and questioning are compared; that the payment arrives on time and the water supply has improved is another face to be recorded as it stands, and does not take the place of examining this structure. The same form lies in the structure, not in names, nor in whether the service is good or bad. Whether there is a way out and whether questioning has effect have not been fully examined for this household: the places to be asked about have been put side by side, and the judgment must be made place by place.

This household is what AI's monopoly over the Base Layer could look like. It brings the far future back to this paper's subject: once the models of one firm or a few drive the supply and issuing of food, water and payments, several places of control may come together in one place, reaching down to the life-substrate; whether they have come together, and how far, must be examined position by position.

Two suppositions are left for Paper 7: suppose the state took all of these into state ownership; suppose that around Year 40 preparations had been made to let it be able to fail. The first looks at what changes once the name has changed; the second at whether what is reduced is dependence or capacity.

This section does not prove that machines will do all the work of the Base Layer, nor that monopoly will necessarily follow then. It can prove nothing about the real future; it only writes one possible form clearly, so that the places to be asked about show themselves early.

Where This Paper Stops

This paper has established three propositions: control in partnerships and investment is examined by what is actual, through the three kinds of material, control, dependence and information, and no one item by itself completes a conclusion of control (LD12); where AI presses on the materials of judgment, checks increase with the depth, without saying that machines replace human judgment or creativity, and the bearing is not handed over (LD13); early checks do not wait for the line to be crossed, the normative reason and the factual reason are kept apart, and neither is a prediction (LD14).

What it has not shown: that the AI industry has already entered a stage of monopoly; that any AI company has crossed the line; that early checks necessarily avoid monopoly; that controlling an entry point for information proves that creativity has been replaced; the far future is imagined: it does not prove that machines will do all the work of the Base Layer, nor that monopoly will necessarily follow then. Stone Granary has not been judged to control Beacon in fact; the Competition and Markets Authority's four decisions closing its inquiries and the Bundeskartellamt's conclusion have been read neither as establishing that there is no control nor as finding that there is a problem.

The Three Propositions of This Paper

LD12 Control in partnerships and investment is examined by what is actual. Three kinds of material are examined: control, dependence, information; they are examined by actual control, dependence and whether people are left unable to get around, not by name; the presence of any one item does not by itself complete a conclusion of control (per E2, "nominal holding and mobilization come apart", and E18; borrowing E16's way of examining actual efficacy: E16 originally asks whether those acted on by an economic position have actual control over its decisions; here it is changed to the effect of a partner or investor on another construct; extension, own undertaking).

LD13 Where AI presses on the materials of judgment, checks increase with the depth. When the models of one firm or a few have become the entry point through which many people obtain information and make judgments, what they press on is the materials of judgment; checks increase with this depth (per E14; extension of the depth of E7; own undertaking). This proposition speaks of the entry point for obtaining information being held by one firm or a few; it does not say that machines replace human judgment or creativity. When judgment is handed to machines, the bearing is not handed over with it (by way of The Company in the Age of AI, ZN1, ZN2; Theory of Government G8, which originally speaks of government; extension).

LD14 Early checks do not wait for the line to be crossed. While concentration is still forming, checks must already be there. The normative reason is that checks are needed within the line (per E27, "Growth must be subject to checks; this is needed within the line, not only once the line is crossed"; E14: scale does not decide from what moment a construct must guard the line and be questioned). Setting them while substitutes remain and dependence has not yet become entrenched keeps open the possibility of taking over and changing direction; this is the side of fact that can be examined; the particular costs, and the effects on innovation and on the capacity to produce and to supply, are examined under E17; neither is a prediction of where an industry is going. Arrangements that can be made at the rule layer include, for example, keeping access, interoperability and preparation for separability, building in advance the reviewer's capacity to obtain information and test independently, and setting up in advance channels of supervision and questioning. Research and public discussion are material and entry points for questioning; they are not final verdicts.

摘要

这一篇读 AI 一处，写成在形成中的集中与它的结构性风险。篇名用"与"，不预设这一领域已经垄断；本篇不判 AI 一处已经进入垄断的阶段，也不判它不会。AI 分几层：算力与芯片，云，模型，数据，分发；每一层照前几篇的核法读。合作与投资里的控制，照实际核：核三类材料，控制、依赖、信息；任何一项的存在都不独自完成控制结论（LD12）。英国竞争与市场管理局对四项 AI 合作与投资的结案、德国联邦卡特尔局对其中一项合作的结论，都止于并购审查的管辖门槛，各案照其决定分别写，既不读作没有控制，也不读作有问题。一家或几家的模型成了许多人取得信息与作判断的入口时，它压到的是判断的材料；制衡随这一深度增强；这一条说的是入口被握住，不说机器取代人的判断或创造力；判断交给机器，承担不随之交出（LD13）。集中还在形成中时，制衡就要在：规范上的理由是，制衡在线内就要有，规模不决定一个构从哪一刻起才须守线、受问；趁替代仍在、依赖尚未固化先设，保留接续与改向的可能；这些都不是对一个行业走向的预测；研究与公开的讨论是追问的材料与入口，不作定案（LD14）。研究指出：有几项相互关联的结构性风险；现在有活力，在硬件、数据等集中的环节，已有的地位可能长期固化。设例用一家模型公司与它的云伙伴的四项条款，贯穿本篇；远景里，机器做了农业、饮水等基础层的活以后，一户人家的一天，粮、水、给付都经同一处。远景是设想，不是预测。

第一节　分层

AI 一处，可以分作几层来读。

算力与芯片。 训练与运行模型要的算力，出自芯片与数据中心。第五篇读过石仓的算力排期：七成在一家，"战略伙伴优先"。

云。 算力多经云租用。云一层的位置，站在模型一层的入口。第五篇立过：一层的控制，不推成下一层的关门之权（LD10）。

模型。 通用模型是这一篇的中心：一个模型可以接到许多处，写字、记账、排号、挑货、读申报。

数据。 训练要数据，用的人留下数据，数据又回去改进模型。第四篇读过关于人的数据，不作锁住他离开的材料（LD8）。

分发。 模型经什么到人手里：一个助手，一个市集的默认入口，一个政府的办事系统。第四篇读过问路：市集首页的默认入口，只许一个助手下单。

每一层都可以照前几篇的核法读：控制按实际认，深度到判断的材料，照现在核（第一篇）；问的路与问的效力（第二篇）；名义（第三篇）；关口立规、数据、几家同在（第四篇）；一层推到下一层、整合与可拆（第五篇）。这一篇不重做，只读 AI 一处新添的三问：合作与投资里的控制；判断的材料；在形成中的集中与早期的制衡。最后读远景。

有研究看过这几层合在一起的样子。英国竞争与市场管理局 2024 年 4 月 11 日发布的《AI 基础模型》更新文件指出三项相互关联的风险：控制关键投入的企业限制接入；在面向消费者或企业的市场上已有地位的企业，借此扭曲人们对基础模型服务的选择；主要参与者之间的合作，沿价值链加强市场力量。同一份文件也承认，这类安排可以促进竞争。研究指出的是风险，不是认定；本篇照研究引，不作定案。

第二节　合作与投资里的控制

回到灯塔与石仓。

名义上，石仓只占约两成，它的观察员不投票。只凭约两成股份和一席不投票的观察员，还不能完成控制的结论；这些股份对应哪些权利、其余的股权怎样分布，事例里没有给。

政治经济学立过一句：名义持有与调动分开，持有多少不等于能调动多少；能调动积累的位置，可以是一个人、一个董事会或一个协同安排（政治经济学第一篇 E2）。控制要按实际核，不照名义。

怎样按实际核？政治经济学问线内的控制时有三问：谁能启动；对理由、行动、规则或任职位置有什么实际效力；被问的一方怎样不能独自取消它（政治经济学第三篇 E16）。原问的是受一个经济位置作用的人，对它的相关决定有没有实际控制；这里换作合作方、投资方对另一个构的作用（推及，自担）：石仓对灯塔的哪些决定，有实际的效力？

把四项条款拆成三类材料来看。

控制。 股权约两成；一席观察员，列席董事会，不投票；先取权：灯塔把模型给别家云以前须先问石仓。前两样，单凭它们，还说不出左右得了什么；第三样，直接落在灯塔的一项决定上：模型给谁的云。灯塔若想把模型放到别家的云上卖，先要过石仓这一关。

依赖。 训练与运行须用石仓的云；算力半价，折价计作投入；收入分成一成五，到收回三倍为止。灯塔每训练一次、每卖出一份，都经过石仓。到第 31 年，营收的四成经石仓的云卖出。依赖不需要票数：灯塔要换一家云，先要想训练系统搬不搬得动，半价的算力到别处还有没有，四成的营收从哪里补。第五篇读过后来的事：到第 36 年，灯塔的训练系统只在石仓的平台上能运行。

信息。 观察员可以取得董事会的材料。石仓自己也做云、也有别的客户；它通过这一席取得灯塔董事会的材料，材料里有什么，事例里没有记。仅凭取得材料的权利，尚不能判它左右灯塔的决定；它还可能落在别处：若材料里有灯塔的计划与价格，石仓对别家模型公司的路，就因此有了一份别人没有的信息。

三类材料合起来读，要问的是：在灯塔与石仓的利益相左时，比如灯塔想把模型放到别家的云上，想换一家算力更便宜的，灯塔能不能自己定？这一问，事例里没有记灯塔试过。本篇只读出：名义上的"不投票"，读不出条款里写下的先取权、依赖与信息；它们实际起多大作用，要另核。

任何一项的存在，都不独自完成控制的结论。须用其云，是许多创业公司都签过的条款；观察员席，是投资里常见的安排；收入分成，是一种回报。本篇不由名目判它们，只读它们合在一处起了什么作用；也不说石仓已实际控制灯塔，事例里只记了条款。

有研究列过这类合作的特征。美国联邦贸易委员会 2025 年 1 月 17 日发布一份 6(b) 研究的工作人员报告，研究三家云服务商与两家生成式 AI 开发者之间的三项合作。报告指出的特征有：重大的股权与部分收入分成；不同程度的咨询、控制与排他权；投资款中相当一部分须花在伙伴的云服务上；折价的算力；这类安排可能影响算力、工程人才等投入的可得；合同与技术上的转换成本；伙伴可以取得别人取不到的敏感技术与商业信息。合作方提供的资料截至 2024 年 9 月，并用了截至 2025 年 1 月的公开资料。研究指出的是特征与可能的影响，不是认定；本篇照研究引。王国州设例里的四项条款，写成几种特征的组合，不照搬哪一对真实公司的条款与比例。

现实里，有一类已作的监管决定，可以看这类合作在一个法域里被怎样处理。英国竞争与市场管理局先后对四项 AI 合作与投资作了结案决定，结论都是不构成须由它审查的并购情形；理由有三种，各写各的。

微软与 OpenAI（2025 年 3 月 5 日）。 管理局认为，微软自 2019 年已经对 OpenAI 的商业政策具有实质影响；这一次没有发现这一影响上升为事实上的控制。因此，按本次所审的控制层级变化，不构成相关并购情形。管理局明说，这一结论不等于认定没有竞争问题。

同一项合作，德国联邦卡特尔局 2023 年 11 月 15 日也作过管辖上的结论。它认为，微软早在 2019 年、至迟在 2021 年合作加深时，已对 OpenAI 有实质的竞争影响；那时 OpenAI 在德国的业务还不够实质，到 2023 年才够；2023 年的投资，不能认定使既有的、与竞争相关的联系加深，所以不须申报。它也说，微软日后若加深对 OpenAI 的影响，须重新审查有没有申报的义务。

亚马逊与 Anthropic（2024 年 9 月 27 日）；谷歌与 Anthropic（2024 年 11 月 19 日）。 前一项，营业额与供给份额两项门槛都未达到，管理局没有就实质影响下结论；后一项，管理局不认为谷歌因这项合作取得了对 Anthropic 的实质影响。

第三节　判断的材料

第一篇立过：关口的深度不止价格与生计，也到信息、注意力与判断的材料（LD2）。AI 一处，这一深度最明显。

一个模型接到许多处以后，许多人经它取得信息、形成判断。设例里已经有几处。

买家在通衢市集说要什么，问路替他挑一两件；他不再看列表。

州模型事务处用灯塔助手读各家的申报、写初审摘要；灯塔自己的申报，也由它读。

旱年里，每一回先减哪个区的水，由调度模型定；完整的计算，市议会读不了。

一户人家的女儿在一个分拣站上班，排班由灯塔的模型定。

这几处，模型压到的不是一笔价钱，是一个判断从哪里来：买什么，申报里有什么问题，谁家先停水，谁哪天上班。当一家或几家的模型成了许多人取得信息与作判断的入口，它压到的就是判断的材料。

政治经济学立过：制衡随权力面递增；权力面按具体的作用比较，收敛谁，收敛到哪一个维度，多少人要经过它，走开的代价多大（政治经济学第三篇 E14）。本篇照此：入口压到判断的材料，深度更深；制衡随这一深度增强（承 E14，推及 E7 的深度，自担）。

要防一个误读。这一条说的是取得信息的入口被一家或几家握住，不说机器取代人的判断或创造力。买家仍可以去看列表；事务处仍可以自己读；市议会仍可以审规则。人的判断与创造力，不由一个入口取代；可人取得判断材料的路，可以被一个入口收窄。本篇核的是后者：路被一家或几家握住到什么程度，握住的一方怎样受问。

判断交给机器，承担不随之交出。政府论说：工具不持承担；承担落在把判断交出去、定下怎样用它、并据其结果施为的位置上（政府论第一篇 G8）。公司论专论说：在公司部署的情形里，「归责的地址是部署它的构；公司不能把算法或 AI 说成另一个地址」（公司论专论《AI 时代的公司》ZN2）；交给一个位置的判断，那个位置要能判断，所需的信息能取得、能理解，能改变结果（同上 ZN1）。原说的是政府与公司；这里推到经一个模型入口作判断的各个位置（推及）。事务处用灯塔助手读申报，承担在事务处；运维方用模型定配水，承担在定下配水目标、把调度交出去的市议会与水务局，运维方对委托它的构持它自己的职责；通衢用问路替人挑货，承担在通衢。模型本身不是一个地址。

LD13　AI 压到判断的材料，制衡随深度增强。一家或几家的模型成了许多人取得信息与作判断的入口时，它压到的是判断的材料；制衡随这一深度增强（承 E14，推及 E7 的深度，自担）。这一条说的是取得信息的入口被一家或几家握住，不说机器取代人的判断或创造力。判断交给机器，承担不随之交出（经公司论专论 ZN1、ZN2；政府论 G8 原说政府，推及）。

第四节　在形成中的集中

AI 一处，现在是什么样子？

有研究给过一个"混合的图景"。经济合作与发展组织 2026 年 7 月 10 日发布的《人工智能市场》报告指出：基础模型这一层过去三年有活力；价值链各层仍有结构性风险；在硬件、数据等结构上集中的环节，已有的地位"may become entrenched in the long run"（可能长期固化）。研究指出的是风险与可能，不是认定。

本篇照此写：在形成中的集中，与它的结构性风险。不判 AI 一处已经进入垄断的阶段，也不判它不会。

"照现在核"在这里两头都要用。一头是：现在有活力，不替日后作答；早期的开路，不作后期的名。灯塔第 29 年给作坊开了路，这是真的；它后来写了只许自己优先的接口，用了别家读不了的记事格式，评审组的组长出自它的安全部门，这些照它们各自的作用核，不由第 29 年的那一面抵销。另一头是：日后可能固化，不替现在定罪。研究说"可能"，就是可能；本篇不由此补成哪一家已经固化。

在形成中，意味着替代还在。开放模型组还在发布（第 34 年以前）；另两家模型公司还在；作坊主想换，要从头建。这一段时间，是读数还在变的时候。

第五节　早期的制衡

集中还在形成中时，制衡什么时候设？

一种说法是：等它越线了再说。还没有越线，还没有垄断，现在设制衡，是提前惩罚，也可能压住了还没长出来的东西。

本篇不取这一说。早期的制衡，不等越线。

规范上的理由，上游有两处。政治经济学说：「增长须被制衡；在线内就要有，不等越线」（政治经济学第五篇 E27）。制衡不是对越线的惩罚，是对一个有权力面的位置的常规安排。同一系列还说：「规模与权力面影响制衡的形态与强弱，不决定一个构从哪一刻起才须守线、受问」（政治经济学第三篇 E14）。一个位置还小，制衡可以轻、可以少；可它要受问，不从它长大的那一天才开始。

事实一侧，是另一种理由，要分开说。趁替代仍在、依赖尚未固化先设，可以保留接续与改向的可能；保留了哪些、付出多少、日后省下多少，要对同一安排分别核。州模型事务处第 33 年设立时没有自己的测试设备；第 36 年才建检验室。检验室若早建三年，事务处这三年读申报、看自测、作认可，就多一双自己的眼睛；它复测出的那一项不符，有可能早些出来。

这两种理由都不是预测。规范的理由说的是制衡应当在；事实的理由说的是早设能保留接续与改向的可能，代价多大，照 E17 核。都不说早设制衡必然避免垄断，也不说不设就必然走到垄断。一个行业以后会怎样，本篇不预测。

早设也有代价，照实说明。政治经济学说，制衡若影响生产、服务与给条件的能力，要照实说明代价、可替代的办法与选择的理由（政治经济学第三篇 E17）；还说，否决点过多，也会使一个位置什么都做不了（同上）。早设的制衡，要说明它对创新、生产与供给能力的影响；一套过早、过重的规矩，可以压住还没长出来的东西：第三篇读过的那一项安全办法，就有这一面要核。早期的制衡同样要说明它的代价，并留出照现在改的余地（照 E17；照 LD3）。

可作规则层安排的，举几样作例子：留住接入、互通与可拆的准备，让别家的模型能接到分发的入口上，让云与模型之间的接口公开；先建审者独立取得信息、独立检验的能力，像那个检验室；先设监督与追问的通道，让被一道门挡住的人能问。这些都是例子，形态照作用、代价与替代核。

还有一样，已经在做：研究与公开的讨论。前面引的几份研究，竞争与市场管理局的风险清单，联邦贸易委员会列的合作特征，经合组织的混合图景，都是在集中还在形成时就提出的问。它们是追问的材料与入口；它们不作定案。一份研究说"有风险"，不等于认定了谁越线；可它把要问的地方提早指了出来。

LD14　早期的制衡，不等越线。集中还在形成中时，制衡就要在。规范上的理由是：制衡在线内就要有（承 E27「增长须被制衡；在线内就要有，不等越线」；E14：规模不决定一个构从哪一刻起才须守线、受问）。趁替代仍在、依赖尚未固化先设，保留接续与改向的可能，这是可核的事实一侧；具体代价，以及对创新、生产与供给能力的影响，照 E17 核；两样都不是对一个行业走向的预测。可作规则层安排的，例如留住接入、互通与可拆的准备，先建审者独立取得信息、独立检验的能力，先设监督与追问的通道。研究与公开的讨论，是追问的材料与入口；它们不作定案。

第六节　远景：机器做了基础层的活以后

这一节是一个设想。它写的是：设若机器能做农业、饮水这些基础层的活，垄断可能是什么形态。"机器能做全部基础层的活"是设想的前提，不是论证的结论；这一节写成虚构世界里的事实，不是对真实未来的预测。远景只写基础层：粮、水、给付。

前几篇已经分别读过几件。

粮。 第 40 年起，百工的农用机器由灯塔的模型驱动，两家合办的田畴到第 50 年运营王国州约七成的耕地；机器只认田畴指定的种子与肥料规格；州的农机补贴、粮食收储与灾年的保收补助，第 45 年起多经田畴领取与执行；第 47 年秋收，一次更新出错，两个县的收割机停了九日，州里找不到能接手的人与机器；停摆以后，州给田畴拨了一笔"粮食安全"专项资金，用于备用系统。第五篇读了一层推到下一层，第二篇读了倒不起。

水。 第 42 年起，苹果城水务局把运维交给百工与灯塔合办的运维方；局里的工程师由四十人退到四人；第 50 年续约，运维费提了约四成。第 53 年旱，每一回切谁的水由调度模型定。第二篇读了写进程序，第七篇读接续。

给付。 第 48 年，王国州设基础给付，经灯塔助手发放；窗口由一百二十个撤到十八个；第 52 年，给付账户与助手账户绑在一起。第四篇读了发放的方式。

每一件单看，各有作用与代价，各有两面：粮价降了三成，田里的伤亡事故几乎没有了，水的漏损由两成降到百分之五，城北三个村接上了水，给付从没有延误。这一节把它们合到一处，写在第 55 年。

一户人家的一天。 苹果城东市的一户人家：一对老人，他们的女儿，一个上学的孩子。

早上的米与菜，出自田畴运营的田，在通衢市集经问路下单送到；能在通衢市集替她下单的助手，只有问路一个，问路由灯塔助手驱动。

水由苹果城水务局供，运维方是百工与灯塔合办的那一家。水费在灯塔助手里缴；缴费系统用的是灯塔的接口，别家的助手调用要受限次与排序。

两位老人的基础给付，经灯塔助手到账。女儿在田畴的一个分拣站上班，排班由灯塔的模型定。

晚上，女儿想把家里的助手换成别家的。她算了一下：两位老人的给付要到窗口重新核实，约六周，最近的窗口要坐车两小时；家里三年的记事带不走；换了别家，缴水费要排在后面；换了别家，在通衢能替她下单的助手，仍只有问路。她没有换。

她在助手里写了一条申诉："我想换，换不了。"助手答："已为您记录，相关部门会处理。"到第 55 年底，没有回音。

这一户人家新增的，只有她这一天、她的盘算与申诉；其余几处，都是前几篇已经读过的事。合在一起，读出的是一件单看每一处都读不出的事。

离不离得开。 她盘算的几项负担相互叠加：给付要重新核实，记事带不走，缴水费要排在后面。还有一处不是这次更换新添的：换了助手，在通衢能替她下单的仍只有问路；这一依赖不随本次更换消失。照第一道闸的限定，有期限、条件、程序或退出成本，不等于退出已被封死（政治经济学第四篇 E21）。这几项各由不同的位置定下：给付与窗口由州定，供水由市政与水务局管，市集里的助手准入由通衢定，运维方、田畴与灯塔又各有自己的作用。共同用到一家的模型，不自行证明这些控制已由模型的提供者统一取得。要核的，是这些安排合起来形成的离开困难，也要分别核各个位置能改变的那一段。她没有换；这一件不补成所有结构通道都已封死。

基础层的保障，对象是谁。 粮、水、给付都在基础层。政治经济学说，做基础层之事的构，制度不只制衡，还要保障这一供给；「保障的对象是供给，不是那个构」（政治经济学第三篇 E14a）。州的补贴、收储、专项资金经田畴流出，给付的服务费付给灯塔，水务的运维费付给运维方：钱保障的是粮、水、给付的供给，还是那一家？备用系统建在田畴里，还算不算接续的准备？这一问第七篇接着读。

第一道闸。 政治经济学说：积累不兑换为对人的基础层的支配，不把人维持生活所必需的东西换成压缩其生命基底的控制，也不拿钱、住处或工资的发放方式去锁住人的离开、拒绝与撤回（政治经济学第四篇 E21）。这一户人家的给付经一家的助手到账，水费在那个助手里缴，买菜经同一家模型驱动的问路；她想离开那个助手，给付先要重新核实。这一读数，落在那一道闸的近旁。本篇不说那一道闸已经被越过：窗口在，路在。本篇说的是，对基础层的几项安排若这样合在一处，那一道闸就是要逐处核的地方。

问不问得动。 申诉有路：助手收下了，"相关部门会处理"。到年底没有回音。前置篇说过，能被问，不取消垄断；第二篇说过，问的通道在，不证明问得动。这一条申诉，送到了哪一个能改变结果的位置上？事例里没有记。

与专制同形。 前置篇用这一户人家的一天读过同形。这里不把灯塔认作专制者，只比较退出与追问受到限制的结构；给付按时、供水改善，是另须照实记的一面，不代替对这一结构的核查。同形落在结构上，不在名称，也不在服务的好坏。离不离得开、问不问得动，在这一户人家这里还没有核完：要问的地方摆在了一起，判断要逐处作。

这一户人家，是 AI 对基础层的垄断可能的样子。它把远景收回到本篇的题目：一家或几家的模型驱动了粮、水、给付的供给与发放以后，几处控制可能合到一处，深到生命基底；合没合、合到哪一步，要分别核各个位置。

两条"设若"留给第七篇：设若州把这些都收归州有；设若第 40 年前后就设了让它倒得起的准备。前一条看名称换了以后，变的是哪一样；后一条看减的是依赖还是能力。

这一节不证明机器会做全部基础层的活，也不证明届时必然垄断。它证明不了任何关于真实未来的事；它只是把一种可能的形态写清楚，让要问的地方提早显出来。

这一篇停在哪里

这一篇立了三条：合作与投资里的控制照实际核，核控制、依赖、信息三类材料，任何一项不独自完成控制结论（LD12）；AI 压到判断的材料，制衡随深度增强，不说机器取代人的判断或创造力，承担不随之交出（LD13）；早期的制衡，不等越线，规范的理由与事实的理由分开，两样都不是预测（LD14）。

它没有证明的：AI 行业现在已进入垄断的阶段；任何一家 AI 公司越线；早期制衡必然避免垄断；控制信息入口即证创造力已被取代；远景是设想：不证明机器会做全部基础层的活，也不证明届时必然垄断。石仓没有被判已实际控制灯塔；英国竞争与市场管理局的四项结案与德国联邦卡特尔局的结论，没有被读作"没有控制"，也没有被读作"有问题"。

交给第七篇的：三件都要，让它倒得起；一户人家的两条"设若"；基础层的公共支持与它的根据。

留下的余项：三类材料的清单怎样落到并购审查与合作审查的规则里，在规则层；判断的材料压到多深算深，在判断层逐处核；在形成中的集中以后怎样走，本篇不预测，照现在核。
