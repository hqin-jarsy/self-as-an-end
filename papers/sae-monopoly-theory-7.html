Monopoly: Remedies
垄断的药方

Han Qin (秦汉) · Independent Researcher · 2026
DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.24377564 · Full PDF on Zenodo · CC BY 4.0
Abstract

The first six papers diagnosed how monopoly forms, how it is exempted from questioning, and in what name; and what the digital, technology and AI each look like. This paper gives the remedies. The remedies are three: checks before the fact, so that structurally no one firm or few firms may monopolize the power to shut the door, and economic and political positions stand apart; supervision during, so that records can be reviewed, the account does not belong to the party whose accounts are kept, and the position of supervision does not depend on the supervised party and has the capacity to obtain information and test independently; questioning after the fact, with the channel of questioning functionally independent of the party questioned. Achieving one does not substitute for the other two; where questioning has no effect, the gap is filled by going back to checks before the fact. The three divide their work by their main effect and are not three mutually exclusive stages; too many veto points can also leave a position unable to do anything (LD15). The remedies must let it be able to fail: what is reduced is dependence, not capacity; preparation for taking over, interoperability, substitution and separability; breakup is the last resort (LD16). That the rules have not caught up does not mean it is not questioned: the requirements of the normative layer do not wait for the rule layer to set them out before taking effect; being questioned is explaining and answering, while penalties and remedies still require grounds and procedures at the rule layer; the channels of questioning are set up without waiting for detailed rules to be complete; neither early permissions nor early constraints can be carried over directly, and where a position no longer controls, constraints and remedies are examined again as it is now and can be withdrawn (LD17). Suggestions at the rule layer serve only as examples: interoperability and data portability, no self-preferencing, structural separation, obligations before the fact, merger review covering acqui-hires and minority stakes, access to computing power and models, resolution plans, independent audit, cooling-off periods and recusal or disclosure of conflicts of interest, public backup capacity and rehearsals of taking over, and breakup as the last resort. The constructed example is a city's water in the far future: the city-owned Water Department handed over its operations, its engineers fell from forty to four, the operating fee rose by forty percent at renewal, and a council member who voted in favor said "Without renewal, there is no water." The far future also has two suppositions: suppose the state owned it; suppose it were able to fail. In reality: the United Kingdom Competition and Markets Authority's finding that acquiring a team can fall within merger review; the technical committees and monitor in the two Google cases; the European Union's obligations before the fact; the breakup and separation of Standard Oil and of AT&T.

Introduction

What this passage writes is an imagined far-future scenario, not a prediction of the real future.

The Apple City Water Department is owned by the city and supplies the whole city's drinking water. Its head is nominated by the mayor and appointed by the City Council; water prices are reviewed and voted on by the City Council.

From Year 42, the Water Department handed the operation and maintenance of its treatment plants and mains to an operator: a company set up jointly by Hundredcraft and Beacon, under a ten-year contract. Hundredcraft's machines do the maintenance, and Beacon's model does the dispatching.

The department's engineers retired year by year and were not replaced: forty in Year 42, four in Year 50, and all four manage the contract rather than the operations.

The data formats and control interfaces of the operations and maintenance system are not public. In Year 49 the Water Department asked another operator for an estimate for taking over. That operator answered: its machines cannot read this system; taking over would require retrofitting first, and it estimated that water would be cut district by district across the city for about two months.

At the renewal in Year 50, the operator raised its operating fee by about forty percent. The City Council voted it through. A council member who voted in favor said: "Without renewal, there is no water."

The other face is recorded too: from Year 42 to Year 50, leakage in the mains fell from about twenty percent to about five; every water-quality sample in the city passed; three villages in the north of the city that had never been connected to the mains were connected to water.

The roads of questioning were there all along: the Water Department is owned by the city, the City Council reviews water prices and votes on renewals, and the department's head is nominated by the mayor and appointed by the City Council. On the day of renewal the City Council voted it through; how far its questioning went, the example does not record. But when it made its decision, what did it still have in hand?

This paper asks: once the city-owned Water Department had handed its capacity away, did it still "have" water? On the day of renewal, who was questioning whom? What did the line "Without renewal, there is no water" answer? Where questioning has no effect, to which year must one go back to fill the gap?

§1 All Three Are Needed

The remedies are three: checks, supervision, questioning.

Checks come before the fact. Structurally, no one firm or few firms may monopolize the power to shut the door. Political Economy says that no economic position may monopolize the power to shut the door, and that review before the fact, and breakup or reorganization after it, are means by which the rule layer keeps positions from being final (Political Economy Paper 3, E18). Checks before the fact mean making sure, before a position becomes the side that shuts the door, that the door is not in its hands alone: interfaces public, so that other firms can connect; someone able to take over; economic and political positions standing apart. Political Economy has fixed the two directions of political–economic separation: "Money does not buy that share of political power which reviews and questions it," and decisions made in a position are not cashed in, either, as wealth later obtained from the party the position questioned (Political Economy Paper 4, E23).

Supervision comes during. Records can be reviewed, and the account does not belong to the party whose accounts are kept. Political Economy says: "The account does not belong to the party whose accounts are kept, nor to any single keeper of accounts"; a record must have a clear scope and a reviewable provenance (Political Economy Paper 4, E22). The position of supervision does not depend in substance on the supervised party for its income, term or prospects (A Way Out, a Way Forward, E43; Theory of the Market Paper 6, SC12). Paper 2 added one more thing: the position of supervision must have the capacity to obtain information and test independently; capability dependence is set up as a separate category (LD5).

Questioning comes after the fact. The channels of questioning are as in Paper 2: the scope is drawn by the institution, not fixed and changed by the party questioned on its own; the channel is functionally independent of it; for rules written into programs, the entry points for questioning are set with reference to every run of the program (LD4).

Achieving one does not substitute for the other two. Having questioning after the fact does not mean that checks before the fact are unnecessary; having supervision during does not mean that questioning after the fact is unnecessary. Each of the three covers its own place, and each has places it cannot catch. Questioning after the fact cannot catch things when questioning has no effect: Paper 2 read that the channel of questioning being there does not prove that questioning has effect. Where questioning has no effect, the gap is filled by going back to checks before the fact.

Read it through the water.

Questioning after the fact was there: at the renewal in Year 50 the City Council voted; how far its questioning went, the example does not record. What is recorded is what it had in hand when it voted: another operator's estimate that taking over would first require retrofitting, with water cut district by district for about two months; only four people left in the department managing the contract; and a council member who voted in favor saying "Without renewal, there is no water." Voting it through does not mean that questioning has no effect; but when what the questioning side can use has dwindled this far, part of the efficacy of questioning is pledged into the hands of the party questioned. Whether this has already left a particular questioning without efficacy requires examining what the City Council can require, what it can change, and whether these powers can actually be set in motion.

And supervision during? The department's four people manage the contract, not the operations; the formats and interfaces of the operations system are not public. Can it understand the operator's accounts, test the state of the treatment plants for itself, review the results the dispatching model gives? The example does not record that it did. That it is not recorded does not prove that it can only borrow the eyes of the supervised party; a lack of operating capacity and a lack of independent review capacity must also be examined separately: the latter can be obtained through a separate testing position, without the department's four people running the operations themselves. Only one place to be examined is read here: under Paper 2, if what the position of supervision looks at and how it measures can rely only on the supervised party, that is a kind of capability dependence.

Checks before the fact lay in Year 42. On the day the contract was signed, was it required that the interfaces of the operations system be public and that the data formats be readable by others? Was it required that the department keep a team able to take over? Was it agreed how handover would take place when the contract expired? The example records none of these. By Year 50, what questioning could use had dwindled; if what was missing was interfaces, retained staff and handover, that piece lay in Year 42.

So, where questioning has no effect, the gap is filled by going back to checks before the fact. This reading does not assess whether the City Council of Year 50 did its duty; what it reads is what the City Council of Year 50 still had in hand.

The three divide their work by their main effect; they are not three mutually exclusive stages. Supervision and questioning also enter before the fact: before the contract is signed, the City Council can already ask how the operator intends to set up its interfaces; and questioning after the fact can in turn push for new checks: if at renewal it is found that the efficacy of questioning is limited by the conditions for taking over, the City Council can be pushed to write public interfaces, retained staff and handover into the new contract. The three speak of where the main effect falls, not of three non-overlapping periods.

All three are needed, and overreach is to be guarded against too. Political Economy says that too many veto points can also leave a position unable to do anything (Political Economy Paper 3, E17). If an operating contract required a City Council vote for every dispatch and a third-party retest for every repair, the treatment plants could not run. All three are needed means that all three places must be present, not that all three must be heavy. Where to be light and where heavy is examined by effect, cost and alternatives.

§2 Let It Be Able to Fail

The Prequel said that too big to fail is a structural influence that concentration itself brings; Political Economy also says that checks fall on how it is used, not aimed at lowering capacity (Political Economy Paper 3, E17). The two sentences must stand together.

The way for them to stand together is to let it be able to fail: what the remedy reduces is dependence, not capacity.

The operator's capacity is real: leakage fell from twenty percent to five, and three villages were connected to water. The remedy does not ask it to hand these capacities back, nor ask the Water Department to return to forty engineers and twenty percent leakage. What the remedy reduces is the dependence spoken of in the line "Without renewal, there is no water": to leave it, the Water Department would, by the other operator's estimate, first have to pay the cost of retrofitting and of cutting water district by district.

Reducing dependence relies on preparation: for taking over, interoperability, substitution and separability. Paper 5 established that preparation for separability and for taking over comes before the fact (LD11); Paper 6 established that early checks do not wait for the line to be crossed (LD14). Here they are gathered into one item of the remedy.

For the water, preparations for being able to fail could be these, all as examples: the interfaces and data formats of the operations system public, so that any licensed operator can read and connect to them; the Water Department keeping a team able to take over, not necessarily large, but truly capable; a rehearsal of taking over once a year, to see whether that team and another operator can take over a treatment plant within a few days; and the contract stating how handover takes place at expiry, and who is responsible during the handover.

The direction of these preparations is not to reduce the operator's capacity: its machines go on with maintenance, and its model goes on dispatching. Whether a particular arrangement can preserve capacity without loss is still examined under E17: public interfaces and rehearsals of taking over both have costs, which must be stated as they stand. What is to be reduced is the degree to which the Water Department cannot leave it. On the day of renewal, when the City Council asks the operator "why a forty percent increase," it has more in hand: without renewal, there may be someone else able to take over. The efficacy of questioning can therefore not be wholly pledged into the hands of the party questioned.

Suppose it were able to fail. The far future has one supposition that shows what things look like once this is done. Suppose that around Year 40 the state had already required: that the interfaces of machines and operations systems be public, so that any licensed operator could take over any treatment plant or farm; that the state keep a team of people and a public backup system, with a rehearsal of taking over once a year; that the Basic Payment have three roads of issuing, one of which does not pass through any company; and that Notes could be exported in a format other firms can read. By Year 55: the same machines, the same Beacon; in the household read in Paper 6, the daughter wants to switch assistants, and she can.

This supposition reduces dependence, not capacity: the machines and the model still belong to the same firm. Nor does it prove that early checks necessarily avoid monopoly: it is a supposition, not a prediction; Paper 6 said that the reason for early checks is normative, not a prediction of where an industry is going.

Suppose the state owned it. The other supposition looks at names. Suppose that from Year 45 the state took Fieldworks, the operator and the issuing of the payment into state ownership, run exclusively by one state-owned company, with Beacon's model bought outright by that company. In Year 55, in that household's day, everything passes through the same place; complaints go to the state-owned company, whose chair is appointed by the governor.

The name has changed: from several private firms to one state-owned firm. Everything still passes through the same place; what has changed is that economic and political positions no longer stand apart: complaints go to the state-owned company, whose chair is appointed by the governor. The Theory of the Market has fixed: "The line does not change with ownership"; "State ownership is not automatically in the direction of the Kingdom of Ends, nor does it automatically cross the line" (Theory of the Market Paper 1, SC3); the position that reviews a publicly run supply is functionally independent of the supplier and of its owner, and where the owner is among the party questioned, independence must be examined again against the owner (Theory of the Market Paper 6, SC13). In this supposition, the channel of questioning must be examined again against the owner. It proves neither that state ownership is worse nor that it is better. What happens once things are joined in one place is handed to the Theory of Despotism; this series only leaves an interface here.

Public support in the Base Layer. Paper 6 read that the state's subsidies, purchasing and storage, and subsidies to secure the harvest in disaster years are mostly received and carried out through Fieldworks; after the outage of Year 47, the state gave Fieldworks a special "food security" fund for backup systems. In the example, supply in the Base Layer is for the most part not borne by private firms alone; the state's money has been in it all along.

Here two sentences of Political Economy are read. One: "public support obtained by scale itself needs a ground of its own" (Political Economy Paper 4, E25). The other: "The object of securing is the supply, not that construct" (Political Economy Paper 3, E14a). A sentence from the Theory of Government in its original sense belongs here too: a guarantee is not an endorsement (Theory of Government Paper 3, G22).

Read it through the special fund. The money was allocated for the supply of food, and its ground lies in food: in Year 47 the harvest in two counties lost twenty percent, and the state wanted to secure food. This ground is real. The backup systems are built inside Fieldworks. If the backup fails along with Fieldworks, it may reduce failures in operation but does not necessarily solve taking over after Fieldworks withdraws; if other qualified operators can also use it, it provides one condition for taking over, and whether it can sustain the supply of food is still to be examined separately. For the same money, what must be examined is the supply it secures under which risks, and who has both the right and the capacity to use the backup if the original operator fails. The object of securing is the supply; this sentence limits the object of securing, and does not choose for this paper any one form of ownership or of shared use.

After its three days down, Beacon made two requests to the state government: support for "backup and resilience," and no liability for breach for outages caused by force majeure within a year. Paper 2 read that if they are granted, their ground must be set somewhere other than its being unable to fail. Here one sentence of remedy is added, as an example at the rule layer: if granted, it can be asked under which risks the share given secures supply, and who can use it if the original operator fails; backup that is public and usable by whoever takes over is one way of doing it, not the only way.

Breakup is the last resort. The Theory of the Market says: "Breakup is used only when lighter means are not enough, and when used it states its effect, cost and alternatives" (Theory of the Market Paper 4, SC9); the Political Economy specialist paper says that at the rule layer, breakup is the last resort (A Way Out, a Way Forward, E41a). Preparation for being able to fail does not break anything up; it only leaves conditions for taking over and separation later; it can make some lighter means possible, and whether those means are enough to deal with the road-closing uses concerned, and what they cost, is still to be examined separately.

In reality, breakup and separation have been accomplished. In the Standard Oil case, the United States Supreme Court in 1911 upheld the dissolution decree, and the company was broken into more than thirty companies; in United States v. AT&T, a settlement was reached in 1982 and the breakup took effect on 1 January 1984, separating the local telephone operating companies from long-distance service and equipment. These historical examples show that even positions of very great scale have been broken up and separated; their costs and consequences have since been much discussed, and this series does not assess them here. When to use breakup is still examined by whether lighter means are enough, and by the arrangements of effect, cost and alternatives.

One reference point. The financial industry has had a practice of the same form. The United States Dodd–Frank Act requires large bank holding companies and certain other companies to submit resolution plans periodically to the Federal Reserve and the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation, commonly called "living wills," explaining how they would be resolved rapidly and in an orderly way in the event of severe financial distress or failure. This reference point shows that "able to fail" can be written as an obligation before the fact; its effectiveness, and the conditions for carrying it over to other industries, were not examined at the time of writing, and it does not show that it can be copied elsewhere.

§3 That the Rules Have Not Caught Up Does Not Mean It Is Not Questioned

AI moves very fast. Laws, regulations and standards at the rule layer often cannot keep up: a company grows large in a field where there is not yet a dedicated law, and someone will say: the law sets nothing down, I have broken no law, on what ground do you question me?

This paper's answer is: that the rules have not caught up does not mean it is not questioned.

There is a sentence of the same form upstream. Reading the sole position of supply at the life-substrate, Political Economy says that this supply is part of the accountability of the institution or government that manages it, that this layer is there whether or not there is a permit, and that "the law's not covering it does not cancel it" (Political Economy Paper 2, E11a). The original speaks of the sole position of supply at the life-substrate; here it is extended to every position that enters this series' diagnosis (extension, own undertaking): the requirements of the normative layer do not wait for the rule layer to set them out before taking effect.

The general root lies in the paper on checks. Political Economy says that scale and power-face do not decide from what moment a construct must guard the line and be questioned (Political Economy Paper 3, E14); measures, forms and thresholds lie at the rule layer, and "Below the threshold, guarding the line and being questioned are not weaker" (Political Economy Paper 3, E19). A position still below the threshold of the rules, or one for which the rules have not yet set a threshold, guards the line all the same and can be questioned all the same.

Being questioned is not being penalized. This must be kept clear. Being questioned is explaining and answering: the side asked must say what it is doing, why, and on whom the cost falls. Penalties and remedies are different: fines, injunctions and breakups still require grounds and procedures at the rule layer. That the rules have not caught up does not allow anyone to be penalized on the strength of a paper; nor does it allow the claim that, because the rules have not caught up, no one may be questioned. Questioning comes before penalties, and also stands apart from them.

The channels of questioning do not wait for detailed rules to be complete. Paper 6 read about the laboratory: in the three years in which the Office had no equipment of its own, the Audit Office inspected and pointed out that the test methods were all provided by the party inspected. Channels of questioning can be set up first: a road by which those stopped can go and ask, a position able to test independently, a statement of reasons. Where these involve actual powers, such as compelling the production of material or compelling disclosure, corresponding authorization and procedure are still required; not waiting for detailed rules to be complete does not mean not asking where those powers come from and how far they reach. Setting up channels is itself a step at the rule layer; rough ones can be set first and detailed ones added later.

Neither early permissions nor early constraints can be carried over directly. Paper 1 established that the same power is examined as it is now (LD3). In the remedies, this proposition is used in both directions.

In one direction, permissions. An early permission, an approval, a decision to let something through, answers the matter of its time; it cannot directly stand in for examining the later actual effect. The safety rules read in Paper 3: suppose a model has been released after approval; how many places it has been connected to since approval, and what effect it has had, must be examined as it is now, and that one approval does not answer for it. The United Kingdom Competition and Markets Authority's decisions closing its inquiries into several AI partnerships answered the jurisdictional threshold of the time; Paper 6 said that those decisions stop at the jurisdictional threshold and do not answer for later.

In the other direction, constraints. What once controlled and no longer does has the constraints and remedies laid on it early examined again as it is now too, and they can be withdrawn. The supposition in the Prequel: in Year 24, the Competition Review Division's report recommended separating Crossways' marketplace from its own retail; suppose that by Year 33 Crossways Market held only about twenty percent of the Kingdom State's online retail, and sellers in the state who sold only on Crossways were under ten percent. Examined as it is now, whether this recommendation is still needed, and to what extent, is examined again by the structure of Year 33; if the readings have changed, it can be narrowed, and it can also be withdrawn. Constraints are not for life; they follow the effects they are meant to deal with.

This direction too has its measure: examining it as it is now is not exempting it from examination. Crossways in Year 33 is still to be questioned; what is withdrawn is that one constraint, not questioning itself.

Fast innovation lacking checks and regulation falls under this proposition. Speed is not the problem; being so fast that the roads of questioning have not yet been built is the problem. The roads of questioning are not built only once the rules are built.

§4 Suggestions at the Rule Layer, as Examples

The normative layer sets out only direction and floor; how the rule layer carries them out, this series offers only as suggestion and example. The items below are means the rule layer can use. They are in no order of priority and are not a checklist; each is examined by effect, cost and alternatives, and there are always remainders and exceptions; a remainder yields no permission.

Interoperability and data portability. Records can be exported in formats other firms can read, assistants can read one another's records, and interfaces connect by public specifications (Paper 4, LD8). In reality, the remedy in the Google search case requires Google to share parts of its index and user-interaction data with qualified competitors (final judgment 5 December 2025; both sides on appeal, not yet decided). Costs: engineering, and new problems of privacy and security.

No self-preferencing. The side that runs the venue does not place its own goods in front, nor its own assistant ahead of others' (Papers 4 and 5). In reality, in September 2025 the European Commission found that Google had favored itself in advertising technology and ordered it to stop (Commission decision; Google has brought an action before the General Court).

Structural separation. The venue and those selling within it belong to two firms; the upstream layer and the downstream layer belong to two firms. The Competition Review Division's recommendation to Crossways in Year 24 is of this kind; in reality, the breakup of AT&T is a concluded example. Costs: costs of coordination after separation, and integration efficiencies that may be lost.

Obligations before the fact. Without waiting for a finding of illegality after the fact, a few obligations are set in advance for a class of positions. In reality, the European Union's Digital Markets Act sets obligations before the fact for designated "gatekeepers"; in April 2025 the Commission found that Apple had breached its anti-steering obligation, and that Meta's "consent or pay" model of the time breached the obligation to give users an equivalent option using less personal data (Commission decisions; Apple and Meta have both brought actions before the General Court). Cost: a one-size-fits-all obligation may fall where it should not.

Merger review covering acqui-hires and minority stakes. Paper 6 established that control in partnerships and investment is examined by what is actual (LD12). In reality, on 4 September 2024 the United Kingdom Competition and Markets Authority found that Microsoft's hiring of Inflection AI's staff and obtaining a license to its intellectual property constituted a merger situation subject to review, and said that acquiring a team with relevant know-how, even without other assets, can fall within the jurisdiction of merger review; at the same time it found that the transaction did not give rise to a realistic prospect of a substantial lessening of competition, and cleared it. This series uses it only to show that acqui-hires can enter review; it does not say that every hiring away of a team is a merger.

Access to computing power and models. The scheduling rules for computing power are public; a partner's accounts are kept separately from other firms'; models can connect to other firms' entry points of distribution (Paper 5, LD10).

Resolution plans and additional requirements. A position so large that its failure would press on many people submits a resolution plan in advance, saying how things would be taken over if it failed, and by whom; with reference to the financial industry (§2 of this paper).

Independent audit. The reviewing position has its own people and its own equipment, its reports go out directly, and its funding and term are not in the hands of the party reviewed (the laboratory in Paper 2). The Theory of the Market has fixed that the questioning carried out by rating, record-keeping and audit is not controlled by the party questioned on its own (Theory of the Market Paper 3, SC7).

Cooling-off periods; recusal or disclosure of conflicts of interest. How long after leaving office a person in a reviewing position may not take a post with the party reviewed; when a reviewer has a relation with the party being evaluated, whether recusal or disclosure applies (the review under the safety rules in Paper 3). Whether recusal or disclosure applies belongs to the rule layer. The Political Economy specialist paper says that how caps, disclosure, recusal and cooling-off periods are set lies at the rule layer (A Way Out, a Way Forward, E43).

Public backup capacity and rehearsals of taking over. For supply in the Base Layer, keep a team able to take over and a public backup system, with a rehearsal once a year (§2 of this paper, suppose it were able to fail).

Breakup as the last resort. Used only when lighter means are not enough, and when used it states its effect, cost and alternatives (Theory of the Market Paper 4, SC9).

In reality, supervision during also has one form: the Google search case set up a technical committee to assist with enforcement; the remedies opinion in the advertising technology case also calls for a monitor and a technical committee, while the final judgment has not yet been entered (as of 29 October 2026; status as in Paper 2). They are what supervision during looks like when it lands in a court judgment; what actual efficacy they have remains to be seen later, and is examined separately.

None of these examples is a design this series advocates as best. Which is best, where it is used and to what extent, is set by those who make rules at the rule layer, who must be able to state their grounds; those it acts on can question it.

§5 Interfaces

Across borders. When one monopoly spans several states, each state's measures on the side of access are looked at by the cross-border way of examining in Theory of the Market Paper 7: who makes the rules, whom they bind, how they are carried out, and where the accounts fall. How states check, supervise and question together is handed to the Theory of Inter-State Cooperation. How cross-border mechanisms are designed is a remainder registered by Theory of the Market Paper 7, and this series does not take it up. The same advertising technology structure read in Paper 5, on which an American court and the European Commission took different directions, is only pointed to here.

The Theory of Despotism. The Prequel set out the same form shared by monopoly and despotism; Paper 2 wrote questioning without effect; this paper's "suppose the state owned it" wrote economic and political positions joined in one place. What despotism itself is, and why states fail, are handed to the Theory of Despotism. This series leaves only these three interfaces.

Where This Paper Stops

This paper has established three propositions: checks, supervision and questioning, all three are needed, achieving one does not substitute for the other two, and where questioning has no effect, the gap is filled by going back to checks before the fact (LD15); let it be able to fail, reducing dependence and not capacity, with breakup as the last resort (LD16); that the rules have not caught up does not mean it is not questioned, being questioned is not being penalized, and early permissions and constraints alike are examined again as it is now (LD17).

What it has not shown: which design at the rule layer is best; in which case breakup should be used; that having all three in place guarantees that there will be no monopoly. The operator has not been judged to have used the renewal to raise its price; whether the amount of the increase is reasonable lies at the rule layer and the judgment layer; the City Council has not been judged to be held by the operator; the two suppositions are only suppositions.

The remainders it leaves: for each example at the rule layer, how it is set, where its threshold lies and how its cost is measured, is handed to the rule layer; the form of resolution plans outside the financial industry was not examined at the time of writing; the design of cross-border mechanisms is still a remainder of Theory of the Market Paper 7.

Where this series stops. The Prequel cleared away what a monopoly is not; Papers 1 to 3 diagnosed how it forms, how it is exempted from questioning, and in what name; Papers 4 to 6 read the digital, technology and AI; this paper gives the remedies. All seventeen propositions are at the normative layer: what they criticize is the direction of a structure, a direction that serves the controller rather than everyone, and "replacing questioning in the name of X"; they do not criticize control as such, nor bigness, nor success. Design at the rule layer is offered only as suggestion; judgment at the judgment layer is made by the positions and persons present, and a paper cannot take their place. Meeting the tradition is in Paper 0.

The Three Propositions of This Paper

LD15 Checks, supervision, questioning: all three are needed. Checks come before the fact: structurally, no one firm or few firms may monopolize the power to shut the door, and economic and political positions stand apart (per E18, E23). Supervision comes during: records can be reviewed, and the account does not belong to the party whose accounts are kept (per E22); the position of supervision does not depend in substance on the supervised party for its income, term or prospects (per E43; Theory of the Market SC12), and also has the capacity to obtain information and test independently (under LD5's category of capability dependence). Questioning comes after the fact: its channels are as in LD4. Achieving one does not substitute for the other two; where questioning has no effect, the gap is filled by going back to checks before the fact. The three divide their work by their main effect: supervision and questioning also enter before the fact, and questioning after the fact can in turn push for new checks. All three are needed, and also under E17: too many veto points can also leave a position unable to do anything.

LD16 Let it be able to fail. The remedy reduces dependence, not capacity: preparation for taking over, interoperability, substitution and separability (per E17; the preparation per LD11, LD14). Breakup is the last resort (per Theory of the Market SC9; per E41a).

LD17 That the rules have not caught up does not mean it is not questioned. The requirements of the normative layer do not wait for the rule layer to set them out before taking effect; this has the same form as E11a, "the law's not covering it does not cancel it" (extension, own undertaking: E11a originally speaks of the sole position of supply at the life-substrate); the general root lies in E14 and E19: "Below the threshold, guarding the line and being questioned are not weaker". Being questioned is explaining and answering; penalties and remedies still require grounds and procedures at the rule layer. The channels of questioning are set up without waiting for detailed rules to be complete. Neither early permissions nor early constraints can be carried over directly: a permission cannot directly stand in for examining the later actual effect; where a position no longer controls, constraints and remedies are examined again as it is now, and can be withdrawn (per LD3). Fast innovation lacking checks and regulation falls under this proposition.

引言

这一段写的是一个远景的设想，不是对真实未来的预测。

苹果城水务局是市属的，供全城的饮用水。局长由市长提名、市议会任命；水价由市议会审议、表决。

第 42 年起，水务局把净水厂与管网的运维交给了一家运维方：百工与灯塔合办的一家公司，签十年的合同。百工的机器做检修，灯塔的模型做调度。

局里的工程师逐年退休，不再补人：第 42 年四十人，第 50 年四人，四人都管合同，不管运维。

运维系统的数据格式与控制接口不公开。第 49 年，水务局请另一家运营商估价接手。那一家答：它的机器读不了这套系统，接手要先改造，估计全城要分片停水约两个月。

第 50 年续约，运维方把运维费提了约四成。市议会表决通过。一位赞成的议员说："不续，就没有水。"

另一面也照记：第 42 年至第 50 年，管网的漏损由约两成降到约百分之五；全城的水质抽检全部合格；城北三个以前没有接上管网的村，接上了水。

问的路一直都在：水务局是市属的，市议会审水价、表决续约，局长由市长提名、市议会任命。续约那一天，市议会表决通过；它问到了哪一步，事例里没有记。可它作决定的时候，手里还有什么？

这一篇问：市属的水务局把能力交了出去以后，它还"有"水吗？续约那一天，谁问谁？"不续，就没有水"这一句，答了什么？问不动的地方，要回到哪一年去补？

第一节　三件都要

药是三件：制衡、监督、追问。

制衡在事前。 结构上不让一家或几家独占关门之权。政治经济学说：没有一个经济位置可以独占关门之权；事先的审查与事后的拆分、重组，是规则层让位置不终局的手段（政治经济学第三篇 E18）。事前的制衡，是在一个位置成为关门的那一方以前，先让门不只在它一处：接口公开，别家能接；接续有人；经济与政治的位置分开站。政治经济学定过政经分离的两个方向：「钱不买成审它、问它的那份政治权力」，位置上作出的决定也不变现为日后从被它问过的一方拿到的财富（政治经济学第四篇 E23）。

监督在事中。 记录可复核，账不归被记账的一方。政治经济学说：「账不归被记账的一方，也不归任何单一的记账者」；记录要有明确的范围与可复核的来路（政治经济学第四篇 E22）。监督的位置，不在收入、任期、前程上实质依赖被监督者（政治经济学专论 E43；市场论第六篇 SC12）。第二篇还添了一样：监督的位置要有独立取得信息、独立检验的能力；能力依赖另立一类（LD5）。

追问在事后。 问的通道，照第二篇：范围由制度划，不由被问的一方独自定、独自改；通道在功能上独立于它；写进程序的规则，问的入口照每一次运行设（LD4）。

一件做到，不顶替另两件。 有了事后的追问，不能说事前就不必制衡；有了事中的监督，不能说事后就不必追问。三件各管一处，各有各接不住的地方。事后的追问，在问不动的时候接不住：第二篇读过，问的通道在，不证明问得动。问不动的地方，要回到事前的制衡去补。

拿水来读。

事后的追问是在的：第 50 年续约，市议会表决了；它问到了哪一步，事例里没有记。记下的是它表决时手里有什么：另一家估计接手要先改造、分片停水约两个月，局里只剩四个管合同的人，一位赞成的议员说"不续，就没有水"。表决通过，不等于问不动；可问的一方手里能用的东西少到这一步，问的效力就有一部分押在了被问的一方手里。是否已使具体的追问失去效力，要核市议会能要求什么、能改变什么，这些权能能否实际发动。

事中的监督呢？水务局四个人管合同，不管运维；运维系统的格式与接口不公开。它能不能看懂运维方的账，能不能自己测一遍水厂的状况，能不能复核调度模型给出的结果？事例里没有记它做过。没有记，不等于已证它只能借被监督者的眼睛；运维能力不足与独立审查能力不足，也要分别核：后者可以另由一个检验的位置取得，不必由局里的四个人自己做运维。这里只读出一个要核的地方：照第二篇，若监督的位置看什么、怎样测都只能靠被监督的一方，那就是一种能力依赖。

事前的制衡，在第 42 年。合同签下的那一天，有没有要求运维系统的接口公开、数据格式能被别家读？有没有要求局里留一支能接手的人？有没有约定合同到期时怎样交接？事例里没有记这些。到第 50 年，追问的手里能用的东西少了；若缺的是接口、留人与交接，那一块在第 42 年。

所以说，问不动的地方，回到事前的制衡去补。这一读不评第 50 年的市议会尽没尽责；它读的是第 50 年的市议会手里还剩什么。

三件按主要作用分工，不是互斥的三段。 监督与追问也进入事前：合同签下以前，市议会就可以问运维方的接口打算怎样设；事后的追问又可以推动新的制衡：续约时若发现追问的效力受接续条件限制，可以推动市议会在新合同里写进接口公开、留人与交接。三件说的是主要作用落在哪里，不是三个不相交的时段。

三件都要，也要防过头。 政治经济学说，否决点过多，也会使一个位置什么都做不了（政治经济学第三篇 E17）。一项运维的合同，若每一次调度都要市议会表决，每一次检修都要第三方复测，水厂就动不了。三件都要，是说三处都要有，不是三处都要重。在哪里轻、在哪里重，照作用、代价与替代核。

LD15　制衡、监督、追问，三件都要。制衡在事前：结构上不让一家或几家独占关门之权，经济与政治的位置分开站（承 E18、E23）。监督在事中：记录可复核，账不归被记账的一方（承 E22）；监督的位置不在收入、任期、前程上实质依赖被监督者（承 E43、市场论 SC12），也有独立取得信息、独立检验的能力（照 LD5 能力依赖一类）。追问在事后：通道照 LD4。一件做到，不顶替另两件；问不动的地方，回到事前的制衡去补。三件按主要作用分工：监督与追问也进入事前，事后的追问又可以推动新的制衡。三件都要，也照 E17：否决点过多，也会使一个位置什么都做不了。

第二节　让它倒得起

前置篇说过，大到不能倒，是集中本身带来的结构性影响；政治经济学又说，制衡落在怎样使用上，不以压低能力为目的（政治经济学第三篇 E17）。两句要并存。

并存的办法，是让它倒得起：药方减的是依赖，不是能力。

运维方的能力是真的：漏损由两成降到百分之五，三个村接上了水。药方不要它把这些能力交回去，不要水务局回到四十个工程师、漏损两成的样子。药方要减的，是"不续，就没有水"这一句所说的依赖：水务局要离开它，照另一家的估计，得先付改造与分片停水的代价。

减依赖，靠的是准备：接续、互通、替代与可拆。第五篇立过可拆与可接续的准备在事前（LD11）；第六篇立过早期的制衡，不等越线（LD14）。这里把它们收成药方的一样。

拿水来说，倒得起的准备，可以是这几样，都作例子：运维系统的接口与数据格式公开，任何持证的运营方都读得了、接得上；水务局留一支能接手的人，人数不必多，但要真的会；每年演练一次接手，看一看那支人与另一家运营方能不能在几天里接住一座水厂；合同里写明到期怎样交接，交接期间谁负责。

这些准备，方向是不减运维方的能力：它的机器照样检修，它的模型照样调度。具体的安排能否无损保全能力，仍照 E17 核：接口公开、接手演练都有代价，要照实说明。要减的，是水务局离不开它的程度。到续约那一天，市议会问运维方"为什么提四成"，手里就多了东西：不续，还有别人可能接得住。问的效力，因此可以不全押在被问的一方手里。

设若倒得起。 远景里有一条"设若"，可以看这样做以后的样子。设若第 40 年前后，州就要求：机器与运维系统的接口公开，任何持证的运营方都能接手任何一座水厂与农场；州留一支人的队伍与一套公共的备用系统，每年演练一次接手；基础给付有三条发放的路，其中一条不经任何公司；记事可以导出成别家读得了的格式。到第 55 年，同样的机器，同样的灯塔；第六篇读过的那一户人家，女儿想换助手，换得了。

这一条"设若"减的是依赖，不是能力：机器与模型都还是那一家的。它也不证明早设制衡必然避免垄断：它是一个设若，不是预测；第六篇说过，早期的制衡的理由是规范的，不是对行业走向的预测。

设若州有。 另一条"设若"，看的是名称。设若第 45 年起，州把田畴、运维方与给付的发放收归州有，由一家州属公司独家运营，灯塔的模型由州属公司买断。到第 55 年，那一户人家的一天，事事经同一处；申诉交州属公司，州属公司的董事长由州长任命。

名称换了：从私营的几家，换成了一家州属。事事仍经同一处；变了的是经济与政治的位置不再分开站：申诉交给州属公司，它的董事长由州长任命。市场论定过：「线不随所有制变」，「国有不自动在目的王国方向上，也不自动越线」（市场论第一篇 SC3）；审公营供给的位置，在功能上独立于供给者与它的主人，主人在被问的一方里，独立要对着主人重核（市场论第六篇 SC13）。这一条"设若"里，问的通道就要对着主人重核。它不证明州有更坏，也不证明更好。合在一处以后会怎样，交专制论；本系列只在这里留一个接口。

基础层的公共支持。 第六篇读过：州的补贴、收储与灾年的保收补助，多经田畴领取与执行；第 47 年停摆以后，州给田畴拨了一笔"粮食安全"专项资金，用于备用系统。基础层的供给，在设例里多半不是纯私营全负责；州的钱一直在里面。

这时照政治经济学两句读。一句：「规模本身换来的公共支持，要另立根据」（政治经济学第四篇 E25）。一句：「保障的对象是供给，不是那个构」（政治经济学第三篇 E14a）。政府论原说的一句也在这里：保证不是背书（政府论第三篇 G22）。

拿专项资金来读。钱是为粮的供给拨的，根据在粮：第 47 年两个县的收成损了两成，州要保粮。这一根据是实的。备用系统建在田畴里。若备用随田畴失效，它可能减少运行中的故障，却未必解决田畴退出以后的接续；若别的合格运营方也能使用，它提供了接续的一个条件，能否持续供粮仍须另核。同一笔钱，要核它保障了哪些风险下的供给，以及原运营方失效时，谁有权、也有能力使用备用。保障的对象是供给；这一句限定的是保障的对象，没有替本篇选定一种归属或通用的形式。

灯塔停摆三日以后，向州政府请了两项：一笔"备份与韧性"的支持；一年里不可抗力造成的停摆不担违约责任。第二篇读过：若给，根据要另立在它倒不起以外的地方。这里补一句药方，作规则层的例子：若给，可以问给的那一份在哪些风险下保住供给，原运营方失效时谁能用它；公共的、接手者能用的备用是一种做法，不是唯一的做法。

拆分是最后的手段。 市场论说：「较轻的办法不够时才拆分，用时说明作用、代价与替代」（市场论第四篇 SC9）；政治经济学专论说，在规则层，拆分是最后的手段（政治经济学专论 E41a）。倒得起的准备不拆，只为将来的接续与分离留出条件；它可以使一些较轻的办法成为可能，这些办法是否足以处理相关的关路用法、付出多少，仍须另核。

现实里，拆分与分离做成过。标准石油一案，美国最高法院 1911 年维持了解散令，公司拆成三十余家；美国诉 AT&T 一案，1982 年和解，1984 年 1 月 1 日分拆生效，本地电话的运营公司与长途、设备分开。这些历史例子说明，规模极大的位置也曾实现拆分与分离；它们的代价与后果，后来有许多讨论，本系列不在这里评。何时使用，仍照较轻的办法是否足够，以及作用、代价与替代的安排来核。

一个参照。 金融业有过同形的做法。美国的《多德—弗兰克法》要求大型银行组织与某些其他公司，定期向美联储与联邦存款保险公司提交处置计划，俗称"生前遗嘱"，说明自己陷入严重财务困境或倒闭时，怎样快速、有序地处置。这一参照说明，"倒得起"可以写成一项事前的义务；它的成效，以及移到别的行业的条件，写作时未及查核，不说明别处照搬。

LD16　让它倒得起。药方减的是依赖，不是能力：接续、互通、替代与可拆的准备（承 E17；准备照 LD11、LD14）。拆分是最后的手段（照市场论 SC9；承 E41a）。

第三节　规则没跟上，不等于不受问

AI 一处走得很快。规则层的法、条例与标准，常常跟不上：一家公司在一个还没有专门的法的领域里做大了，有人会说：法没有规定，我没有违法，凭什么问我？

本篇的回答是：规则没跟上，不等于不受问。

上游有一句同形的话。政治经济学读生命基底处唯一的供给位置时说：这一供给是管理它的制度或政府职责的一部分，有无许可，这一层都在，「法律没有覆盖也不取消」（政治经济学第二篇 E11a）。原说的是生命基底处唯一的供给位置；这里推到一切进入本系列诊断的位置（推及，自担）：规范层的要求，不等规则层立出来才生效。

一般的根，在制衡一篇。政治经济学说，规模与权力面不决定一个构从哪一刻起才须守线、受问（政治经济学第三篇 E14）；量法、形态与门槛在规则层，而「门槛以下，守线与受问不弱」（政治经济学第三篇 E19）。一个位置还在规则的门槛以下，或者规则还没有为它立门槛，它照样守线，照样可以被问。

受问，不是受罚。 这一句要分清。受问是说明与作答：被问到的一方，要说出它在做什么、为什么、代价落在谁身上。处罚与救济不同：罚款、禁令、拆分，仍须有规则层的根据与程序。规则没跟上，不能凭一篇论文去罚谁；可也不能因规则没跟上，就说谁都不能问。问在罚之前，也在罚之外。

问的通道，不等细的规则齐备才设。 第六篇读过检验室：事务处没有自己的设备的那三年里，审计署查过，指出测试方法都由被查方提供。问的通道，可以先设：一条被挡住的人能去问的路，一个能独立检验的位置，一份说明。其中涉及调取资料、强制公开等实际权力的，仍须有相应的授权与程序；不等细则齐备，不等于不问权力的来源与范围。设通道本身也是规则层的一步，可以先立粗的，再补细的。

早期的许可与约束，都不能直接沿用。 第一篇立过：同一份权力，照现在核（LD3）。这一条在药方里两头都用。

一头是许可。一项早期的许可，一次认可，一份放行的决定，答的是当时的事；它不能直接代替对后来实际作用的核查。第三篇读过的那项安全办法：设若一个模型经认可发布了，认可以后它被接到了多少处、起了什么作用，要照现在核，不由那一纸认可替它作答。英国竞争与市场管理局对几项 AI 合作的结案，答的是那时的管辖门槛；第六篇说过，那些结案止于管辖门槛，不替以后作答。

另一头是约束。从前控制、现在不再控制的，早期加给它的约束与救济，同样照现在重核，可以退出。前置篇的那一条"设若"：第 24 年，竞争审查处的报告建议通衢的场与自营分开；设若到第 33 年，通衢市集只占王国州线上零售的约两成，州内只在通衢卖的卖家不足一成。照现在核，这一建议要不要、要到哪一步，要照第 33 年的结构重核；读数若变了，它可以收窄，也可以退出。约束不是终身的；它跟着它要处理的作用走。

这一头也有分寸：照现在核，不是免核。第 33 年的通衢仍要受问；退出的是那一项约束，不是问本身。

缺少制衡与监管的快速创新，落在这一条。快不是问题；快到问的路还没修好，才是问题。问的路，不等规则修好才修。

LD17　规则没跟上，不等于不受问。规范层的要求不等规则层立出来才生效，与 E11a「法律没有覆盖也不取消」同形（推及自担：E11a 原说生命基底处唯一的供给位置）；一般的根在 E14、E19：「门槛以下，守线与受问不弱」。受问是说明与作答；处罚与救济，仍须有规则层的根据与程序。问的通道不等细的规则齐备才设。早期的许可与约束都不能直接沿用：许可不能直接代替对后来实际作用的核查；位置不再控制的，约束与救济照现在重核，可以退出（照 LD3）。缺少制衡与监管的快速创新，落在这一条。

第四节　规则层的建议，作例子

规范层只立方向与底线；规则层怎样落，本系列只作建议与例子。下面几样，是规则层可以用的办法。它们不排次序，不是清单；每一样都照作用、代价与替代核，永远有余项与例外；余项推不出许可。

互通与数据可携。 记录能导出成别家读得了的格式，助手之间能互读，接口按公开的规范相连（第四篇 LD8）。现实里，谷歌搜索一案的救济，要谷歌向合格的竞争者共享部分索引与用户交互数据（最终判决 2025 年 12 月 5 日；双方上诉中，未判）。代价：工程，隐私与安全的新问题。

不自我优待。 场的一方不把自家的货排在前面，不把自家的助手放在别家前面（第四篇、第五篇）。现实里，欧盟委员会 2025 年 9 月认定谷歌在广告技术上自我优待，命它结束（委员会决定；谷歌已诉至普通法院）。

结构分离。 场与在场里卖的分属两家；上一层与下一层分属两家。竞争审查处第 24 年给通衢的建议就是这一种；现实里，AT&T 的分拆是已结的一例。代价：分开以后的协调成本，可能失去的整合效率。

事前义务。 不等事后认定违法，先给一类位置定下几样义务。现实里，欧盟《数字市场法》给被指定的"守门人"定下事前义务；委员会 2025 年 4 月认定苹果违反反引导义务、Meta 当时的"同意或付费"模式违反给用户一项使用较少个人数据的等效选择的义务（委员会决定；苹果与 Meta 都已诉至普通法院）。代价：一刀切的义务，可能落在不该落的地方。

并购审查涵盖人才收购与少数股权。 第六篇立过：合作与投资里的控制，照实际核（LD12）。现实里，英国竞争与市场管理局 2024 年 9 月 4 日认定，微软聘走 Inflection AI 的人员并取得其知识产权授权，构成须审查的并购情形，并说，收购一支有相关技术的团队，即使没有别的资产，也可以落入并购审查的管辖；同时认定这一交易没有现实的显著减损竞争的可能，放行。本系列只用它说明：人才收购可以进入审查；不说一切挖团队都是并购。

算力与模型的接入。 算力的排期规则公开；伙伴与别家分开记账；模型能接到别家的分发入口上（第五篇 LD10）。

处置计划与附加要求。 规模大到倒了会压到许多人的位置，事先提交处置计划，说明倒了怎样接、谁来接；参照金融业（本篇第二节）。

独立审计。 审的位置有自己的人、自己的设备，报告直接交出去，经费与任期不在被审者手里（第二篇的检验室）。市场论定过评级、记账与审计的追问不由被问的一方独自控制（市场论第三篇 SC7）。

冷却期；利益冲突的回避或披露。 审的位置上的人离任以后，多久不去被审的一方任职；评审的人与被评的一方有关系时，是回避还是披露（第三篇的安全办法评审）。是回避还是披露，属规则层。政治经济学专论说，上限、公开、回避与冷却期怎样设，在规则层（政治经济学专论 E43）。

公共的备用能力与接手演练。 基础层的供给，留一支能接手的人与一套公共的备用系统，每年演练一次（本篇第二节，设若倒得起）。

拆分作最后的手段。 较轻的办法不够时才用，用时说明作用、代价与替代（市场论第四篇 SC9）。

现实里，事中的监督也有一种样子：谷歌搜索一案设技术委员会协助执行；广告技术一案的救济意见也要设监督员与技术委员会，最终判决尚未作出（截至 2026 年 10 月 29 日；状态见第二篇）。它们是事中的监督落在一个法院判决里的样子；实际效力怎样，要看以后，另核。

这些例子，都不是本系列主张的最好的设计。哪一种最好，在哪里用，用到什么程度，由规则层的立规者定，说得出根据；被它作用的人可以问。

第五节　接口

跨境。 一家垄断跨好几国时，各国准入一侧的措施，照市场论第七篇的跨境核法看：规则由谁立，约束谁，怎样落地，账落在哪里。国与国之间怎样协作制衡、监督与追问，交国家间协作论。跨境机制怎样设计，是市场论第七篇登下的余项，本系列不接。第五篇读过的同一个广告技术的结构，美国法院与欧盟委员会取向不同；这一例在这里只作指引。

专制论。 前置篇立了垄断与专制的同形；第二篇写了问不动；本篇的"设若州有"，写了经济与政治的位置合在一处。专制本身是什么，国家为什么失败，交专制论。本系列只留这三处接口。

这一篇停在哪里

这一篇立了三条：制衡、监督、追问三件都要，一件不顶替另两件，问不动的地方回到事前的制衡去补（LD15）；让它倒得起，减依赖不减能力，拆分是最后的手段（LD16）；规则没跟上，不等于不受问，受问不是受罚，早期的许可与约束都照现在重核（LD17）。

它没有证明的：哪一种规则层的设计最好；拆分在哪一案该用；三件齐备即保证不垄断。运维方没有被判借续约提价；提价的数额合不合理，在规则层与判断层；市议会没有被判被拿住；两条"设若"都只是设若。

留下的余项：规则层的每一样例子，怎样设、门槛在哪里、代价怎样量，交规则层；处置计划在金融业以外的形态，写作时未及查核；跨境机制的设计，仍是市场论第七篇的余项。

这个系列停在哪里。 前置篇清出垄断不是什么；第一至第三篇诊断它怎样形成、怎样免问、以什么为名；第四至第六篇读数字、技术、AI 三处；这一篇开药方。十七条命题，都在规范层：批的是结构性的方向，为了控制者、而非每个人的方向，与"以 X 为名，换掉了问"；不批控制本身，不批大，不批成功。规则层的设计只作建议；判断层的判断，由在场的位置与人作出，论文不能代替。与传统的对接，在 Paper 0。
