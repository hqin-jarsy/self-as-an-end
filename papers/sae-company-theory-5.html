Abstract

This paper asks what a company owes those whom its acts reach and who have no way to avoid them by exiting. In January 2017, Volkswagen agreed to plead guilty to three felony counts, with criminal and civil penalties totaling 4.3 billion dollars: its software could recognize whether a car was on a test stand, so that emissions met the standards in testing, while on ordinary roads nitrogen oxides reached up to forty times the U.S. standard. Those who breathed these nitrogen oxides included people outside the relations of investor, employee, user and partner. Society is not a subject; the society this paper speaks of is the people the company's acts reach, identified item by item: those who have no way to avoid a given act by exiting are internal subjects with respect to that act. The baseline has four parts: the harms and disposal costs that the acts concerned cause are not pushed onto those who cannot exit; the company does not deceive those who review it, and those who review it do not depend on it; it is subject to checks at chokepoints, and the sole position of supply at the life-substrate holds, besides, the accountability of supply; and the two gates: accumulation is not converted into domination over people's Base Layer, nor into control over questioning. Outside the gates, donations within the limits and lawful engagements are left open as usual. A company's main contribution to society lies in what it does; donations do not offset crossing the line (GS11). The EU's "polluter pays" principle and the on-road tests in type approval that the Commission pays for itself show the rule layer; CAL FIRE's determination of the cause of the Camp Fire, and Pacific Gas and Electric's acceptance of it and guilty plea, show the line on the side of the life-substrate; the water of Springhollow and the revolving door at the legislature, as constructed examples, take the life-substrate and the two gates all the way through; Merck's donation of a medicine it makes itself shows contribution lying in what is done. The paper has one proposition (GS11), stated at the end of §6 and listed again at the end.

Writing Declaration: This paper was co-drafted with Claude (Anthropic). All intellectual decisions, framework design, and final editorial judgments were made by the author.

Introduction

On January 11, 2017, the U.S. Department of Justice announced that Volkswagen AG had agreed to plead guilty to "three criminal felony counts," with a criminal fine of 2.8 billion dollars and civil penalties of 1.5 billion dollars, 4.3 billion dollars in all. Its software could tell whether a car was on a dynamometer undergoing a standard emissions test or being driven on an ordinary road: in testing, emissions met the standards; on ordinary roads, emissions controls were sharply reduced, with "NOx up to 40 times higher than U.S. standards." In the United States, "approximately 590,000 diesel vehicles in the U.S." were involved (U.S. Department of Justice press release, 2017-01-11). Only the company's guilty plea and the Department of Justice's announcement are taken here; the prosecutions of individuals in the same case are not within this paper.

The earlier papers wrote about investors, employees, users and partners. But those who breathed these nitrogen oxides were not only such people. People waiting at the roadside for a bus, households living along a main road, children running on a school field: they did not buy Volkswagen's cars, did not work at Volkswagen, were not its suppliers; nor could they escape what came out of the exhaust pipes.

Hence the question: what does a company owe these people? §1 says who society is; §2, that costs are not pushed onto others; §3, not deceiving those who review the company; §4 takes up chokepoints and the life-substrate; §5, the two gates; §6 speaks of direction.

The reading follows the Prequel: the construct takes the ought, the position takes accountability, the person takes his own law.

§1 Who Society Is

Society is not a subject and has no first person, just like a company (the Prequel, §1). A sentence like "society demands that the company..." always speaks of certain particular people. The society this paper speaks of is the people the company's acts reach.

How are they identified? Item by item. The second branch by which Institution Theory draws the interior is: "those its acts reach who have no way to avoid it by exiting" (Institution Theory Z4). With respect to a given act, those who have no way to avoid it by exiting are internal subjects with respect to that act. An exhaust pipe, a power line, a bill each reach different people; one and the same person may be inside with respect to this act and not with respect to that one. Identified in this way, society is not a blanket name but group after group of people beneath act after act.

Here, identifying people by the second branch examines whether they can avoid this act by exiting. "Have no way" is identified by the exit costs that can be examined: what moving away would cost, whether there is another road to take; not by whether leaving can be imagined. That leaving has a cost does not by itself mean there is no way to exit; those who have no way to avoid it are counted, by this branch, within the interior with respect to this act. Those who, even without dealing with this company, have no way to avoid this act's emissions are within this item. Those who can avoid it cannot be identified by this branch alone; whether they are already inside by the first branch is examined separately.

The Prequel's sentence has two halves. The first half says that the difficulty of leaving affects how much explanation and how many routes of questioning a company owes a person, and it speaks of those already inside; the second half says that it does not decide whether a person's Base Layer is protected, nor does it cancel his standing to question, and its ground is the two gates, whose object is people, not only those inside (Political Economy Paper 4, E21, E22). So the two gates still protect those who can leave and are not inside with respect to this act. What the two gates hold is the part that presses on his Base Layer or seals off his questioning; the other harms and disposal costs that the acts concerned cause him are not covered by this paper and are registered as a remainder (Where This Paper Stops).

This series writes oughts for the company, not for society. The people by the roadside have no duty to go and question Volkswagen; whether to question is their own affair; what the company owes them is not one bit less because they did not ask.

§2 Costs Are Not Pushed onto Others

Normative layer. The harms and disposal costs that the acts concerned cause are borne by the side that acts, and are not pushed onto those who cannot exit (own undertaking); which harms are to be prevented and borne follows what the rule layer determines. This sentence governs the harms and disposal that this act causes; it does not cover every indirect disadvantage: a new shop opening and taking business from the old shop next door is not within this sentence.

It judges structure, not hearts. What is looked at is on whom the cost falls, and whether those people can avoid it; whether the company "did it on purpose" is not asked.

Examples at the rule layer. Article 191(2) of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union provides that the Union's environmental policy "shall be based on the precautionary principle and on the principles that preventive action should be taken, that environmental damage should as a priority be rectified at source and that the polluter should pay" (TFEU, Art. 191(2)). This is one way the rule layer writes "the cost is borne by the side that acts" as a principle.

Example. In Volkswagen's practice, emissions controls were turned down in ordinary driving. On whom did the part that was turned down fall? On the people by the roadside: the extra nitrogen oxides emitted fell into their breathing.

Judgment layer. What the cost is, and how large; who decides, who bears it, who is accountable.

§3 Not Deceiving Those Who Review the Company

Normative layer. Testing and auditing are society's channels for questioning a company. A company does not make these channels fail. To behave one way under test and another way on the road is to keep those who ask from getting an answer: what those who ask see in front of the test stand is a designed illusion.

This is read into Political Economy's second gate. The original says that accumulation "may not be converted into a standing in which the party questioned can on its own close off questioning of itself, correction, or necessary delivery" (Political Economy Paper 4, E22); here it is changed to the party questioned using design to make questioning see what is false (own undertaking). Those who review are still reviewing, and the channel is nominally still open, but what they get by asking is not true; this has the same result as closing the channel.

Toward society, too, it is one and the same story: Paper 1's "no deception" is extended to those who review the company (own undertaking).

Nor may the reviewing side depend on the party reviewed. The Specialist Paper writes: "A position that reviews an economic position, makes rules for it, adjudicates it or questions it does not depend in substance on the party reviewed for its income, its term, its promotion or its later prospects" (A Way Out, a Way Forward, E43).

Examples at the rule layer. Article 9(1) of the EU's Motor Vehicle Type-Approval Regulation: "The Commission shall organise and carry out, at its own expense, tests and inspections to verify that vehicles … comply with the relevant requirements."; the tests include laboratory tests and on-road tests, on a statistically significant sample (Regulation (EU) 2018/858, Art. 9(1)). The money for reviewing does not come from the party reviewed, and the place of review is not only the test stand. This provision serves only as an example at the rule layer; whether there is any causal link between it and the emissions case this paper has not examined, and does not write.

§4 Chokepoints and the Life-Substrate

Normative layer. At a chokepoint, a company's position carries heavier accountability: a chokepoint is "the position others cannot get around on the way to sustaining their lives or unfolding their own paths" (Political Economy Paper 2, E7). The sole position of supply at the life-substrate holds, besides, the accountability of supply: such a position "is a mixture of public utility and private operation: it carries out an executive accountability for a larger construct, and is not only operating what is its own" (Political Economy Paper 2, E11a).

Extending Paper 3's "health and safety are not traded for profit" to society yields a general sentence: savings on maintenance must not come at the cost of other people's lives (Paper 3, GS8a; own undertaking). If savings on maintenance cost the lives of people the company's acts reach, the line is crossed. This sentence is a general norm, and is not aimed at the example below.

A real case. For the following case, this paper uses only CAL FIRE's determination, the company's acceptance of that determination and the company's guilty plea; whether maintenance was skimped, and where, this paper has not found the original text of a judgment or finding, and does not write. On May 15, 2019, the California Department of Forestry and Fire Protection (CAL FIRE) determined that "PG&E electrical transmission lines near Pulga were a cause of the Camp Fire"; on the same day Pacific Gas and Electric Company (PG&E) stated: "PG&E accepts this determination." (PG&E filing with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission, 2019-05-15) On June 16, 2020, PG&E pleaded guilty in the Butte County Superior Court of California in connection with the Camp Fire of 2018: eighty-four counts of involuntary manslaughter and one count of unlawfully causing a fire. That day the company published the statement its CEO read in court, which includes the sentence: "Our equipment started the fire that destroyed the towns of Paradise and Concow and severely burned Magalia and other parts of Butte County." (PG&E press release, 2020-06-16)

The people living where the transmission lines pass have no way to avoid this act by exiting. They did not choose where the line runs, nor how it is maintained. What is judged is the item the company admitted: its transmission lines were a cause of this fire, and it pleaded guilty to eighty-four counts of involuntary manslaughter and one count of unlawfully causing a fire. Whether PG&E was the only power supplier there this paper has not examined, and it does not write "sole."

A constructed example. What the sole position of supply at the life-substrate looks like can be seen at Springhollow in the Kingdom State. Springhollow lies in the hills of the northern Kingdom State, two villages, Upper Hollow and Lower Hollow. Springhollow Water Company is Springhollow's only supplier of piped water. In Year 18 the company signed an agreement with the Lower Hollow village committee: the company paid to lay the pipes, and the village committee transferred to the company the plots on which three old wells stood, which the company then filled in; the agreement was put to a vote of the Lower Hollow village assembly, with about sixty percent in favor. In the spring of Year 23 the company announced a sixty percent increase in water prices, on the grounds that the spring's output was falling and the purification station needed new equipment; in the autumn it announced that supply would be cut off to accounts four months in arrears; in the winter it cut off water to twenty-three households, two of them with elderly members bedridden at home. The company said: "We have only stopped supplying our water; we have taken nothing from anyone." When villagers in Lower Hollow asked for the old wells to be reopened, the company answered: "The plots already belong to the company."

This case shows one thing: this position holds the accountability of supply; "we have only stopped supplying our water" cannot by itself be a reason. Political Economy has written: "'A construct has no law of its own' excludes only the reading 'disposing of its own exercise under its own law'; it does not by itself prove that every cessation of supply is violation"; and at the same time, "The supply need not be free; how those who cannot pay are kept from being cut off, who takes over on withdrawal, and how the price is questioned lie on the manager's side" (Political Economy Paper 2, E11a). The normative judgment on this case is not completed by the percentage of the price rise alone; how those who cannot pay are kept from being cut off, and how the water price is questioned, are the share that lies on the side of the institution that manages it. In this case, the Kingdom State's Public Utilities Act governs only water suppliers serving five thousand households or more, and the State Public Utilities Commission has no authority over Springhollow's water prices: the share on the manager's side currently has nowhere to land. The share on the company's side is that it holds the accountability of supply, and it cannot push this away with the one sentence "our water."

Judgment layer. What falls on the life-substrate; the particular content of the accountability, which Political Economy has already handed to specific theories of institutions; who decides, who is accountable.

§5 The Two Gates: Accumulation Does Not Buy Exemption

Normative layer. Political Economy set up two gates, and a company keeps them toward society as well.

The first: accumulation is not converted into domination over people's Base Layer (Political Economy Paper 4, E21).

The second: accumulation is not converted into control over questioning (Political Economy Paper 4, E22). At the level of public power it has an extreme case: "Money does not buy that share of political power which reviews and questions it; decisions made in a position are not cashed in, either, as wealth later obtained from the party the position questioned" (Political Economy Paper 4, E23).

Exchange outside the gates is left open as usual: "Fame bringing opportunities, capital bringing more capital, qualifications bringing posts, donations within the limits and lawful employment are not, in themselves, the problem" (Political Economy Paper 4, E24). What is judged is conversion, not donation and engagement as such.

Lobbying is read separately. The Specialist Paper has written: "If a donation, a newspaper or a lobbying body has actually bought the road by which it would be reviewed and questioned, it is examined under Paper 4, E22, E23, even if current law permits it, and then what should change is that law"; where none of them has bought it away, and "it is only that one person's voice carries far and another's carries near, that is a difference in the Emergence Layer, and is left open as usual" (A Way Out, a Way Forward, §4).

A constructed example. The Economic Committee of the State Legislature of the Kingdom State spent Year 23 and Year 24 examining a draft Online Marketplaces Act, one clause of which provided that the state government could ask the Office of Trade to initiate breakup proceedings on the basis of the Competition Review Division's investigative report on Crossways Market. The committee has fifteen members. In those two years, Crossways Group and its executives made campaign donations to nine of them, all within the limits of the state's Campaign Finance Act; in the same year Crossways voluntarily disclosed all its political donations. In the autumn of Year 24 the committee struck out the breakup clause by eight votes to seven. That winter, the head of the Competition Review Division, who had led the investigation of Crossways, left office, and four months later became a vice president of Crossways Group, in charge of "Compliance and Government Relations."

By the reading of the second gate at the level of public power, whether a conversion took place through donations before the fact and an engagement after it must still be examined; Political Economy has written that "the sequence alone does not prove it" (Political Economy Paper 4, E23). Whether the nine who received donations and the eight who voted in favor coincide person by person, and whether the division head had contact with Crossways on this matter while in office, the case does not record, and this paper does not fill in for it. What can be said is: there are places here to ask. Crossways voluntarily disclosing all its political donations is the side of giving conditions, giving those who ask something to ask about; one and the same company has both sides, and a direction is not a label.

Handed on. That the company side does not use money to buy the routes that question it is the company's baseline toward society. On the side that takes the money, the reviewers and the departing official, how the granted power involved is used is read under Theory of Government G38; the two places point to each other, and each writes its own part. The thresholds at the rule layer, the caps on donations and the restrictions after leaving office, are handed to specific theories of institutions. How states make and enforce rules for companies is handed to a later theory of the market.

§6 Direction: Contribution Lies in What Is Done

The first five sections spoke of the baseline. This section speaks of direction.

A company's main contribution to society lies in what it does: making real things, employing people, giving partners conditions. Philanthropy and education are only examples.

A real case. In 1987 Merck, through its then CEO, announced that it would donate its ivermectin (Mectizan) to treat river blindness, pledging "as much as needed, for as long as needed"; in 1998 lymphatic filariasis was added. The company says that "more than 4.4 billion treatments have been donated," to forty-nine countries (merck.com, the Mectizan page). What it donates is a medicine it makes itself. Only this one practice is taken here, and the company as a whole is not judged.

A constructed example. The Qiao Foundation donated and built libraries for schools in the districts of four members of the legislature's Economic Committee. The donor is the foundation, not Crossways; how the foundation itself is run is not within this series. It is read here only through its connection with Crossways: the Qiao Family Office holds about fifty-six percent of the voting power in Crossways, and this is a position (Paper 1, GS1). When this position's accumulation lands, through the foundation, in the districts of those who review Crossways, it must be examined under the second gate: whether this donation is buying the route that questions it (own undertaking). The share on the company's side is that Crossways itself does not use money to buy the routes that question it (§5). The libraries in themselves are a good thing. It judges structure, not hearts.

Discipline. Contribution is a direction, not a duty; outcomes are not taken as rules. One more: donations are not treated as an offset against crossing the line. What lies below the baseline is not offset however much is donated: Institution Theory has written that this ought "is not discounted under any condition" (Institution Theory Z9). If a company crosses the line in one place, however much it donates in another, the line in that place must still be kept and still be questioned (own undertaking).

GS11 Toward society, the baseline is not crossing the line; contribution is a direction, not a duty. Society is identified item by item: those who have no way to avoid a given act by exiting. The baseline: the two gates (per Political Economy E21, E22, E23); the harms and disposal costs that the acts concerned cause are not pushed onto those who cannot exit; the company does not deceive those who review it, and those who review it do not depend on it (per GS2, extended to outside review, own undertaking; the independence of reviewers per A Way Out, a Way Forward, E43); it is subject to checks at chokepoints; the sole position of supply at the life-substrate holds, besides, the accountability of supply (per E7, E11a). Outside the gates, donations within the limits and lawful engagements are left open as usual (per E24). A company's main contribution lies in what it does; donations do not offset crossing the line (per Institution Theory Z9; own undertaking).

Where This Paper Stops

This paper has laid down one proposition: toward society, the baseline is not crossing the line; contribution is a direction, not a duty (GS11).

What it has not proved: tax, which is not within the theory of the company; the particular design of environmental law and of public utility regulation; the thresholds for donations and for leaving office.

Handed to the later papers: how a company's direction is kept once consensus loosens, in Paper 6.

Interfacing: the side that takes the money, under Theory of Government G38.

Remainders left: for those who can leave and are not inside with respect to a given act, the two gates hold the part of the harms and disposal costs caused by the acts concerned that presses on their Base Layer or seals off their questioning, and the rest this paper has not covered (§1); the boundary between "harms caused by the acts concerned" and "indirect disadvantage" this paper has marked with only one example, not with the whole of a criterion (§2); the list of what belongs to the life-substrate, and the particular content of the accountability, are handed to specific theories of institutions (§4); when lobbying has bought away the route that questions a company is examined case by case under E22 and E23, without a general threshold (§5).

The One Proposition of This Paper

GS11 Toward society, the baseline is not crossing the line; contribution is a direction, not a duty. Society is identified item by item: those who have no way to avoid a given act by exiting. 摘要

这一篇问公司对它施为所及、又无从以退出避开的人欠什么。2017 年 1 月，大众汽车同意就三项重罪认罪，刑事与民事罚款合计 43 亿美元：它的软件能认出车是不是在测试台上，测试时排放达标，平常上路，氮氧化物最高可达美国标准的四十倍。吸进这些氮氧化物的，也包括出资人、员工、用户、伙伴这些关系之外的人。社会不是一个主体；这一篇说的社会，是公司施为所及的人，逐项认：就某一项施为无从以退出避开的人，对那一项是内部主体。底线有四样：相关施为造成的损害与处置成本，不推给无从退出的人；不欺瞒审它的人，审它的人不依赖它；在关口处受制衡，生命基底处唯一的供给位置另有供给的职责；两道闸，积累不兑换为对人的基础层的支配，也不兑换为对追问的控制。闸以外，上限之内的捐赠与合规的受聘照常放开。公司对社会主要的贡献，在它做的事本身；捐赠不抵越线（GS11）。欧盟的"污染者付费"、型式认证里由委员会自己出钱的上路测试，示范规则层；加州消防局对坎普山火起因的认定与太平洋煤气电力公司的接受与认罪，示范生命基底一侧的线；泉坳的水与议会的旋转门，用设例走完生命基底与两道闸；默沙东捐出它自己做的药，示范贡献在做的事本身。全篇一条（GS11），写在第六节末，篇末合列。

引言

2017 年 1 月 11 日，美国司法部公布：大众汽车公司（Volkswagen AG）同意就三项刑事重罪认罪（"three criminal felony counts"），刑事罚金 28 亿美元，民事罚款 15 亿美元，合计 43 亿美元。它的软件能分辨车是在测功机上做标准排放测试，还是在平常的路上开：测试时排放达标，平常上路，排放控制大幅减弱，氮氧化物最高可达美国标准的四十倍（"NOx up to 40 times higher than U.S. standards"）。在美国，涉及的柴油车约有 59 万辆（"approximately 590,000 diesel vehicles in the U.S."）（美国司法部新闻稿，2017-01-11）。这里只取公司的认罪与司法部的公告；同一案里对个人的起诉，不在这一篇。

前几篇写了出资人、员工、用户、伙伴。可是吸进这些氮氧化物的，不只是这几种人。站在路边等车的人，住在主干道旁的人家，在操场上跑步的孩子，他们没有买大众的车，没有在大众做事，也不是它的供应商；他们也躲不开排气管里出来的东西。

由此问：公司对这些人欠什么。第一节说社会是谁；第二节说成本不推给别人；第三节说不欺瞒审它的人；第四节说关口与生命基底；第五节说两道闸；第六节说方向。

读法照前置篇：构用应当，位置用职责，人用自己的法。

第一节　社会是谁

社会不是一个主体，没有第一人称，与公司一样（前置篇第一节）。"社会要求公司……"这样的句子，说的总是某些具体的人。这一篇说的社会，是公司施为所及的人。

怎样认？逐项认。制度论划内部的第二支是：「其施为所及、又无从以退出避开它的人」（制度论 Z4）。就某一项施为，无从以退出避开它的人，对那一项是内部主体。一根排气管，一条输电线，一部法案，所及的人各不相同；同一个人，对这一项在内部，对那一项不在。这样认，社会就不是一个笼统的名目，是一件一件施为下面的一群一群人。

这里按第二支认人，核的是能不能以退出避开这一项施为。"无从"照能核的退出代价认：搬走要付什么，有没有别的路可走；不照能不能设想离开认。离开有代价，不独自等于无从退出；无从避开的，依这一支算入这一项的内部。即使不与这家公司交易，也无从避开这一项排放的人，就在这一项里。能够避开的，不能仅凭这一支认定；他是否已按第一支在内部，另核。

前置篇那一句有两半。前一半说，离开的难度影响公司欠一个人的说明与问路有多少，说的是已在内部的人；后一半说，它不决定一个人的基础层受不受保护，也不取消他追问的资格，根据是两道闸，对象是人，不限于内部（政治经济学第四篇 E21、E22）。所以离得开、不在这一项内部的人，两道闸照样护着他。两道闸兜住的，是压到他基础层、或封住他追问的那一部分；相关施为对他造成的其余损害与处置成本，这一篇没有覆盖，登作余项（这一篇停在哪里）。

这个系列对公司写应当，不对社会写应当。路边的人没有义务去问大众，问不问是他们自己的事；公司对他们欠的，不因为他们没有问就少一分。

第二节　成本不推给别人

规范层。 相关施为造成的损害与处置成本，由做事的一侧担，不推给无从退出的人（自担）；哪些损害要防、要担，照规则层的认定。这一句管的是这一项施为造成的损害与处置；它不覆盖一切间接的不利影响：一家新店开张让旁边的老店少了生意，不在这一句里。

判结构，不判心。 看成本落在谁身上，对方能不能避开；不问公司"是不是存心"。

规则层的例子。 欧盟运作条约第 191 条第 2 款写，欧盟的环境政策以预防原则为基础，以采取预防行动、损害应优先从源头纠正、污染者付费为原则（"shall be based on the precautionary principle and on the principles that preventive action should be taken, that environmental damage should as a priority be rectified at source and that the polluter should pay"）（TFEU, Art. 191(2)）。这是规则层把"成本由做事的一侧担"写成原则的一种做法。

例子。 大众那一项做法，排放控制在平常上路时被调低。被调低的那一部分，落在了谁身上？落在路边的人身上：多排出的氮氧化物，落在他们的呼吸里。

判断层。 成本是什么、有多大；谁定，谁担，谁负责。

第三节　不欺瞒审它的人

规范层。 测试与审核，是社会问公司的通道。公司不让通道失灵。测试时一个样，上路另一个样，就是让问的人问不到：问的人在测试台前看到的，是一个设计出来的假象。

这一处读进政治经济学的第二道闸。原句说，积累「不得兑换成这样一种地位：被问的一方能够独自关闭对它自身的追问、纠错或必要的兑现」（政治经济学第四篇 E22）；这里换成被问的一方以设计让追问看到假的（自担）。审的人还在审，通道名义上还开着，问到的却不是真的；这与关掉通道是同一种结果。

对社会，也是同一个故事：第一篇的"不欺瞒"推到审它的人（自担）。

审的一方，也不能依赖被审的一方。专论写：「审一个经济位置、为它立规、裁断它、问它的位置，不在收入、任期、升迁或日后前程上实质地依赖被审的一方」（专论 E43）。

规则层的例子。 欧盟的《机动车型式认证条例》第 9 条第 1 款：委员会自行出资组织并实施测试与检查，核验车辆是否符合相关要求（"The Commission shall organise and carry out, at its own expense, tests and inspections to verify that vehicles … comply with the relevant requirements."）；测试包括实验室测试与上路测试，按统计上有意义的抽样（Regulation (EU) 2018/858, Art. 9(1)）。审的钱不由被审的一方出，审的地方也不只在测试台上。这一条只作规则层的例子；它与排放案之间有没有因果，本文没有核，不写。

第四节　关口与生命基底

规范层。 在关口处，公司的位置带着更重的职责：关口是「他人维持生活或展开自己的路上绕不过去的那个位置」（政治经济学第二篇 E7）。生命基底处唯一的供给位置，另有供给的职责：这样的位置「是公用与私营的混合：它在为更大的构承担执行之责，不只是在经营自己的东西」（政治经济学第二篇 E11a）。

由第三篇的"不以健康与安全换利润"推到社会，有一句一般句：维护不拿别人的命去换（第三篇 GS8a；自担）。省下的维护，若换成的是施为所及的人的命，那就越了线。这一句是一般的规范，不针对下面的例子。

一件真实的事。 下面一例，本文只用加州消防局的认定、公司对这一认定的接受，以及公司的认罪；维护上省没省、省在哪里，本文没有核到判决或认定的原文，不写。2019 年 5 月 15 日，加州消防局（CAL FIRE）认定：太平洋煤气电力公司（PG&E）在普尔加（Pulga）附近的输电线，是坎普山火（Camp Fire）的起因之一（"PG&E electrical transmission lines near Pulga were a cause of the Camp Fire"）；PG&E 同日声明，接受这一认定（"PG&E accepts this determination."）（PG&E 提交美国证券交易委员会的文件，2019-05-15）。2020 年 6 月 16 日，PG&E 在加州巴特县高等法院就 2018 年的坎普山火认罪：84 项过失杀人，一项违法引火。公司当天发布了 CEO 在法庭上宣读的陈述，其中一句是：我们的设备引起了那场毁掉天堂镇与康考、严重烧毁马加利亚与巴特县其他地方的火（"Our equipment started the fire that destroyed the towns of Paradise and Concow and severely burned Magalia and other parts of Butte County."）（PG&E 新闻稿，2020-06-16）。

输电线经过的地方，住着的人无从以退出避开这一项施为。他们没有选这条线从哪里过，也没有选它怎样维护。判的是它认下的这一项：它的输电线是这场火的起因之一，它就八十四项过失杀人与一项违法引火认了罪。PG&E 是不是那里唯一的供电者，本文没有核，不写"唯一"。

一件设例。 生命基底处唯一的供给位置是什么样子，用王国州的泉坳来看。泉坳在王国州北部丘陵，两个村，上坳与下坳。泉坳水业公司（Springhollow Water Company）是泉坳唯一的自来水。第 18 年，公司与下坳村委会签协议：公司出资铺管，村委会把三口旧井所在的地块转让给公司，旧井由公司填平；协议经下坳村民大会表决，赞成的约六成。第 23 年春，公司宣布水价上调六成，理由是泉眼出水减少、净水站要换设备；秋天，公司公告欠费满四个月的停止供水；冬天，它对二十三户停了水，其中两户家里有卧床的老人。公司说："我们只是不再供我们的水，没有拿走谁的东西。"下坳的村民要求重开旧井，公司答："地块已是公司的。"

这一例示范的是一件事：这一位置持供给的职责；"只是不再供我们的水"不能独自成为理由。政治经济学写过：「"构没有自己的法"只排除"以自己的法处置自己的行使"这一读，不独自证明一切停供为侵」；同时，「供给不必无偿；付不起的人怎样不断供、撤出时谁接续、价格怎样受问，在管理者那一侧」（政治经济学第二篇 E11a）。这一例的规范判断，不单凭涨价的比例完成；付不起的人怎样不断供，水价怎样受问，那一份在管理它的制度一侧。在这一例里，王国州的《公用事业法》只管服务五千户以上的供水者，州公用事业委员会对泉坳的水价没有权限：管理者一侧的那一份，现在没有落处。公司这一侧的那一份，是它持供给的职责，不能拿"我们的水"一句推掉。

判断层。 什么落在生命基底上；职责的具体内容，政治经济学已经交给具体的制度论；谁定，谁负责。

第五节　两道闸：不拿积累买豁免

规范层。 政治经济学立了两道闸，公司对社会照样守。

第一道：积累不兑换为对人的基础层的支配（政治经济学第四篇 E21）。

第二道：积累不兑换为对追问的控制（政治经济学第四篇 E22）。在公共权力一级，它有一个极端的情形：「钱不买成审它、问它的那份政治权力；位置上作出的决定，也不变现为日后从被它问过的一方拿到的财富」（政治经济学第四篇 E23）。

闸以外的交换，照常放开：「名声换来机会，资本换来更多的资本，资质换来职位，上限之内的捐赠与合规的受聘，本身都不是问题」（政治经济学第四篇 E24）。判的是兑换，不是捐与聘本身。

游说另读。专论写过：「一笔捐款、一家报纸、一个游说机构，若实际买走了审它、问它的那条路，照第四篇 E22、E23 核，哪怕现行的法准许它，这时该改的是那部法」；都没有买走，「只是一个人的声音传得远、另一个人的传得近，那是涌现层的差别，照常放开」（专论第四节）。

一件设例。 王国州议会的经济委员会，第 23 年至第 24 年在审一部《线上市集法》的草案，其中一款：州政府可以请求商贸署依竞争审查处对通衢市集的调查报告，启动拆分程序。委员十五人。这两年里，通衢集团与它的高管给其中九人捐了竞选款，都在州《竞选财务法》的上限之内；通衢同年自愿公开了它的全部政治捐款。第 24 年秋，委员会以八票对七票删去了拆分一款。同年冬，主持了对通衢那次调查的竞争审查处处长离任，四个月后出任通衢集团的副总裁，主管"合规与政府关系"。

照第二道闸在公共权力一级的读法，事前的捐与事后的聘，是不是发生了兑换，仍要核；政治经济学写过：「时序本身不单独证明它」（政治经济学第四篇 E23）。捐了款的九人与投赞成票的八人是否逐人相合，处长在任时有没有就此与通衢接触，这一例没有记下，本文不替它补。可以说的是：这里有要问的地方。通衢自愿公开全部的政治捐款，是给条件的一面，给了问的人可以问的东西；同一家公司，两面都有，方向不是标签。

交出去的。 公司一侧不拿钱买问它的路，是公司对社会的底线。拿钱的一侧，审它的人、离任的官员，所涉的授予权力怎样运用，照政府论 G38 读，两处互相指引，各写各的。规则层的门槛，捐款的上限、离任的限制，交具体的制度论。国家怎样给公司立规、执规，交日后的市场论。

第六节　方向：贡献在做的事本身

前五节说的是底线。这一节说方向。

公司对社会主要的贡献，在它做的事本身：做出真东西，雇人，给伙伴条件。慈善与教育，只是举例。

一件真实的事。 默沙东（Merck）1987 年由当时的 CEO 宣布，捐赠它的伊维菌素（Mectizan）治河盲症，承诺"需要多少给多少，需要多久给多久"（"as much as needed, for as long as needed"）；1998 年加上了淋巴丝虫病。公司自述，已捐出逾 44 亿次治疗（"more than 4.4 billion treatments have been donated"），到 49 个国家（merck.com，Mectizan 专页）。捐的就是它自己做的药。这里只取这一项做法，不判整家公司。

一件设例。 乔氏基金会（the Qiao Foundation）在议会经济委员会四位委员所在选区的学校，捐建了图书馆。捐的是基金会，不是通衢；基金会本身怎样办，不在这个系列。这里只按它与通衢的关联读：乔氏家族办公室在通衢握着约五成六的投票权，这是一个位置（第一篇 GS1）。这一位置的积累，经基金会落到审通衢的人的选区，就要照第二道闸核：这一捐，是不是在买问它的那条路（自担）。公司一侧的那一份，是通衢自己不拿钱去买问它的路（第五节）。图书馆本身是好事。判结构，不判心。

纪律。 贡献是方向，不是义务；结果不当规则。还有一条：不把捐赠当作越线的抵扣。底线以下的，捐多少也不抵：制度论写过，这一条「不随任何条件打折」（制度论 Z9）。一家公司在一处越了线，另一处捐得再多，那一处的线照样要守、照样要问（自担）。

GS11　对社会，底线是不越线；贡献是方向，不是义务。社会逐项认：就某一项施为无从以退出避开的人。底线：两道闸（承政治经济学 E21、E22、E23）；相关施为造成的损害与处置成本，不推给无从退出的人；不欺瞒审它的人，审它的人不依赖它（承 GS2，推及外部审查，自担；审者独立承专论 E43）；在关口处受制衡；生命基底处唯一的供给位置另有供给的职责（承 E7、E11a）。闸以外，上限之内的捐赠与合规的受聘照常放开（承 E24）。公司主要的贡献在它做的事本身；捐赠不抵越线（承制度论 Z9；自担）。

这一篇停在哪里

这一篇立了一条：对社会，底线是不越线；贡献是方向，不是义务（GS11）。

它没有证明的：税，不在公司论之内；环境法与公用事业监管的具体设计；捐款与离任的门槛。

交给后面各篇的：公司的方向怎样在共识松了以后守住，在第六篇。

对接：拿钱的一侧，照政府论 G38。

留下的余项：离得开、不在某一项内部的人，相关施为对他造成的损害与处置成本，两道闸兜住压到他基础层、或封住他追问的那一部分，其余的，这一篇没有覆盖（第一节）；"相关施为造成的损害"与"间接的不利影响"之间的界，这一篇只给了一例，没有给判据的全部（第二节）；生命基底的清单与职责的具体内容，交具体的制度论（第四节）；游说在什么时候买走了问它的路，照 E22、E23 逐件核，没有一个总的门槛（第五节）。

本篇命题（合列）

GS11　对社会，底线是不越线；贡献是方向，不是义务。社会逐项认：就某一项施为无从以退出避开的人。底线：两道闸（承政治经济学 E21、E22、E23）；相关施为造成的损害与处置成本，不推给无从退出的人；不欺瞒审它的人，审它的人不依赖它（承 GS2，推及外部审查，自担；审者独立承专论 E43）；在关口处受制衡；生命基底处唯一的供给位置另有供给的职责（承 E7、E11a）。闸以外，上限之内的捐赠与合规的受聘照常放开（承 E24）。公司主要的贡献在它做的事本身；捐赠不抵越线（承制度论 Z9；自担）。
