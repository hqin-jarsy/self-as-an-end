SAE Theory of the Company · Paper 6

Keeping the Direction
守向

Han Qin (秦汉) · Independent Researcher · 2026
DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.24045739 · Full PDF on Zenodo · CC BY 4.0
Abstract

The earlier papers wrote what a company oriented toward the Kingdom of Ends does toward five parties. This paper asks what keeps that direction. In February 2020, Wells Fargo paid 3 billion dollars, through a deferred prosecution agreement and a civil settlement, to resolve the criminal and civil investigations into its sales practices; it admitted that from 2002 to 2016, under the Community Bank's onerous sales goals and accompanying management pressure, customers were charged fees and interest they should not have been charged. Internal investigators had called it a "growing plague" as early as 2004, yet the Community Bank's senior leaders did not change that sales model. The problem had been seen early; why was it not changed? This paper does not answer for this case; what it asks is what keeps a company's direction. Consensus loosens: people change, companies are acquired, and a written consensus slowly comes to exist only on paper; and a company cannot require people to agree with it. So keeping the direction cannot presuppose consensus, and can rest only on constructs and positions: the baseline is written into the articles and into decision procedures, and changing it must be public and open to questioning; self-correcting channels are independent of the party questioned and are examined by the three questions, and those who ask are not penalized for asking; incentive structures do not override the baseline, and the position that sets a target is accountable for where the target presses (GS12). The John Lewis Partnership Council's power to dismiss the chairman, the Delaware public benefit corporation's periodic reports and the stockholders' power to sue, and the external channels run by competent authorities and the ban on retaliation in the EU Whistleblower Directive each show one kind of arrangement; John Lewis's plan to shrink its Partnership Council, and the third generation of Crossways, in this series' constructed examples, changing the composition of the Seller Appeals Committee, show that the channels that keep the direction can themselves be changed. The judgment of hard times has reasons that can be given and can be questioned; if the judgment is wrong, the position that made it bears the corrections and the consequences of accountability that correspond to it, and individual responsibility beyond that requires independent grounds (GS13). Finally the paper speaks of direction: keeping the direction is also leaving conditions for those who come later. The paper has two propositions (GS12, GS13), each stated at the end of its section and listed together at the end.

Introduction

On February 21, 2020, the U.S. Department of Justice announced that Wells Fargo had agreed to pay 3 billion dollars to resolve the criminal and civil investigations into its sales practices. In law this was a deferred prosecution agreement and a civil settlement, not a criminal conviction. Among the facts the company admitted: from 2002 to 2016, under "The Community Bank's onerous sales goals and accompanying management pressure," the company "collected millions of dollars in fees and interest to which the Company was not entitled," harmed the credit ratings of some customers, and unlawfully used customers' sensitive personal information (U.S. Department of Justice press release, 2020-02-21).

The problem had been seen early. According to the same material, internal investigators had called these practices a "growing plague" as early as 2004, and in 2005 said they were "spiraling out of control"; yet the Community Bank's senior leaders "refused to alter the sales model."

The problem had been seen early; why was it not changed? This paper does not answer for this case. What it asks, from here, is what keeps a company's direction.

What is judged here is this practice and its structure, not the whole bank, and minds are not read. §1 says that consensus loosens; §2 takes up the arrangements that keep the direction; §3, judging hard times and answering for the judgment; §4 speaks of direction.

The reading follows the Prequel: the construct takes the ought, the position takes accountability, the person takes his own law.

§1 Consensus Loosens

The Prequel said that consensus is identified by where it lands: written into the articles and into decision procedures, able to be invoked when decisions are made, with positions holding their accountability by it. Even once it has landed, it can loosen. People change, CEOs change, companies are acquired, hard times come. A written consensus slowly comes to exist only on paper: the sentence is still in the articles, but no one invokes it when deciding; or it is invoked, but no one uses it to question anything.

There is one more thing that makes keeping the direction harder. A company cannot require employees to agree inwardly with the consensus written into the company: "A construct may require a position to do something; it may not require a person to think something." (F6 §3.3; Paper 2, GS6) This proposition protects people, and this series does not take it back. But it has a consequence: keeping the direction cannot presuppose that "everyone still agrees." New employees may not agree, the next CEO may not agree; if the direction is still to be kept, it can rest only on constructs and positions (own undertaking).

One more thing must be kept apart. A company that has deliberately changed its direction is outside this series' scope (Paper 1, §3). What this paper speaks of is the kind that no one meant to change, but that has slowly loosened.

§2 The Arrangements That Keep the Direction

Normative layer. Of the arrangements that keep the direction, this series requires only four things.

First, the baseline is written into the articles and into decision procedures. What is written in, if changed, must be changed publicly, and can be questioned.

Second, self-correcting channels are independent of the party questioned. Independence is examined by Institution Theory's three conditions: the channel "is independent of the party questioned, internal subjects can actually initiate it, and initiating it brings no retaliation" (Institution Theory Z34). Whether the channel has efficacy is examined by the three questions: who can activate it; what actual efficacy it has on reasons, actions, rules, or the holding of office; how the party questioned cannot cancel it on its own (Political Economy Paper 3, E16). Where the party questioned can close on its own the route that questions it, this is read into the second gate (Political Economy Paper 4, E22).

Third, those who ask are not penalized for asking (Institution Theory Z34).

Fourth, incentive structures do not override the baseline. That a company sets targets for a position is not in itself the problem; where those targets press is the problem. Political Economy writes that the rate of return set in the place of the sole goal "provides a signal to be examined; if it further acquires a standing that overrides guarding the line, cancels the questioning and change of direction of those concerned, or decides for the investee its own ends, it is judged by these actual effects" (Political Economy Paper 5, E27). The original speaks of the return targets an investor sets for the construct invested in; here it is changed to the targets a company sets for a position (own undertaking). The third of the original's three branches, "decides for the investee its own ends," cannot be extended to a position: a position has accountability, not ends of its own; here it is read as deciding for the person behind the position his own affairs (Paper 2, GS6). That the position that sets a target is accountable for where the target presses is not in E27's original sentence; its ground lies in the accountability of positions (Political Economy Paper 1, E2; Institution Theory Z6; own undertaking).

Contrast: being asked does not prove the channel effective. In the Wells Fargo case, internal investigations had already pointed out the problem. The Department of Justice's material says: "Despite knowledge of the illegal sales practices, Community Bank senior leadership failed to take sufficient action."

What this case shows is: being asked does not prove the channel effective. Who could compel correction, and whether the party questioned could cancel the necessary effects, require a further examination of structure. The first of the three questions has an answer here: internal investigators could activate it, and did. What remains to be examined are the latter two: what actual efficacy asking had, and whether the party questioned could stop it on its own. The material this paper has examined reads only as far as "being asked does not prove the channel effective," and gives no answer to these two questions. The Department of Justice's material writes the Community Bank's senior leaders, the group's management and the board separately; nor are they pressed here into one undifferentiated "party questioned": the latter two questions must be examined layer by layer.

Examples at the rule layer.

John Lewis Partnership has a Partnership Council elected by employees. Under Rule 10 of its current constitution (the August 2026 edition), "If the Council judges that the Chairman has failed to fulfil, or is no longer a suitable person to fulfil, the responsibilities of the office as laid down in the Constitution, it may pass a 'Resolution upon the Constitution' to dismiss the Chairman according to the Articles of Association of John Lewis Partnership Trust Limited." In May 2023 the Council held a confidence vote on the then chairman's leadership; that vote was not itself binding (ITV News, 2023-05-10). Dismissal is a channel in the constitution; the confidence vote is something else; the two are read separately.

A Delaware public benefit corporation "shall no less than biennially provide its stockholders with a statement" setting out the public benefit objectives the board has set, the standards adopted to measure progress, objective factual information based on those standards, and an assessment of how far the objectives have been achieved (Delaware Code, Title 8, §366(b)); stockholders owning together at least two percent of the corporation's outstanding shares (for a listed corporation, two percent or shares with a market value of two million dollars, whichever is less) can sue to enforce the balancing requirement (§367). Read by the three questions: who can activate it, stockholders who meet the threshold can; on the basis of being an employee, a user or a member of society alone, one cannot activate this suit (own undertaking). These standings may overlap with being a stockholder who meets the threshold; nor does this provision sum up other channels of questioning. Whether the statement is made public may be provided for separately by the certificate of incorporation or the bylaws.

The EU's Whistleblower Protection Directive requires that "legal entities in the private sector with 50 or more workers" set up internal reporting channels (Art. 8(3)); that a reporting person may report first through internal channels, "or by directly reporting through external reporting channels" (Art. 10); that reports made through external channels are received and followed up by authorities designated by Member States ("Member States shall designate the authorities competent to receive, give feedback and follow up on reports") (Art. 11(1)), with Article 12(1) further defining the "independent and autonomous" character of external channels by standards such as the completeness, integrity and confidentiality of information; and it prohibits "any form of retaliation," suspension and dismissal included (Art. 19) (Directive (EU) 2019/1937). This case shows two things: external channels are run by competent authorities, not by the entity reported on, and so are independent of the party questioned; and those who ask are not penalized for asking.

Two things used earlier are also arrangements that keep the direction: Patagonia placing its voting shares in a trust set up to safeguard the company's purpose, and Bosch separating voting rights from earnings (Paper 1).

Judgment layer: the channels that keep the direction can themselves be changed. A June 2025 report (FashionUnited, citing The Daily Telegraph) said that John Lewis was preparing to cut its Partnership Council from fifty-seven members to forty-three; the reason the company gave employees was that the existing structure relied "too heavily on hierarchy and escalation." This is a plan in a report; whether it has taken effect this paper has not examined.

This paper does not judge this adjustment. Whether a council should have fifty-seven members or forty-three is not something the normative layer can answer. Only three things are asked here: whether the decision to change the channel is public; whether it can be questioned; whether, among those asking, there is anyone other than the party questioned. The channels that keep the direction are constructs too, and must also be open to questioning.

Among the examples set up in this series there is also a case that fits "people change." At Crossways Group in Apple City, in the summer of Year 24, the second generation of the Qiao family retired, the third generation took over as chair of the Family Office and chair of the Crossways board, and the voting rights of the Class B shares were thereupon exercised by him. In Year 25 the board chaired by the third generation stopped the Small Shop Starter Program, which had run since Year 15; and the two members of the Seller Appeals Committee elected by and from among the Gold Sellers were changed to appointment by management. What the previous generation had loosened was taken back in the hands of the next; and the one that changed the composition of the channel was the party questioned itself. These two are not one kind of thing: the Small Shop Starter Program is giving conditions, and belongs to direction; the two seats on the appeals committee are a channel of questioning, of the kind that the second requirement in this section requires to be independent of the party questioned. This case shows one thing: if a channel of questioning can be changed at will by the party questioned, then when people change, it can be taken back (Political Economy Paper 3, E16). It is not the slow loosening that §1 speaks of; the party questioned changed the channel directly; nor does it show what kind of person the third generation is, or that every route of questioning at Crossways has been closed (Paper 4, §4).

GS12 Consensus loosens: the arrangements that keep the direction do not presuppose consensus. A company cannot require people to agree, so keeping the direction rests only on constructs and positions: the baseline is written into the articles and into decision procedures, and changing it must be public and open to questioning. Self-correcting channels are independent of the party questioned and are examined by the three questions; those who ask are not penalized for asking. Incentive structures do not override the baseline: the position that sets a target is accountable for where the target presses (per Institution Theory Z34, Political Economy E16, E22, E27, How Is Institution Possible §3.3; own undertaking).

§3 Judging Hard Times, and Answering for the Judgment

Paper 2 said that the cost of hard times falls on what is done; but whether the hard times are real, and how the cost is divided, who has the say?

Normative layer. Whether the hard times are real, how much is borne and for how long: the reasons can be given, and can be questioned. Who decides and who is accountable is written down clearly; the CEO is among them.

How a wrong judgment is borne. Judgments of hard times can be wrong: what was thought impassable could in fact be passed; it was thought that one round of layoffs would be enough, and then another was needed. If the judgment is wrong, the position that made it explains its reasons, submits to review, and bears the corrections and the consequences of accountability that correspond to the position. The construct's account still belongs to the construct. Whether an individual bears responsibility beyond that requires independent grounds, and is not automatically transferred onto some person because the outcome shows the judgment wrong; the CEO is examined in the same way. Political Economy has written: "the construct's account, the position's accountability and individual responsibility with independent grounds are examined separately: the construct's name does not shield the last, and the construct's failure is not automatically transferred onto any one person" (Political Economy Paper 1, E2); Institution Theory writes: "The construct's name does not shield an individual responsibility that has independent grounds, and the construct's failure is not automatically transferred onto any one person" (Institution Theory Z6). The two sentences are two sides of one line: the position bears what is the position's, and the person bears the share that has independent grounds.

Tendencies at the rule layer. What to do in hard times is written down in advance. Lincoln Electric guaranteed employment from 1958, and employees agreed to accept necessary adjustments to posts and schedules (Paper 2, §3): how hard times would be borne was agreed before they came. The determination that hard times exist can be reviewed by a party independent of management (tendency; own undertaking). The two tendencies of Paper 2, §3 also apply here: sharing should be progressive, with the CEO among those who share; and employees take part in deciding how to share.

Example. Writing about that hard time, Barry-Wehmiller's CEO said that at first "I was poised to react as I always had," and then changed to company-wide unpaid furloughs (the Prequel, Introduction). A judgment can change; and he stated the reason for the change.

Contrast. In Year 25 Bluekiln Porcelain Works shut two kiln lines and laid off nine hundred people; the company said cheap porcelain from elsewhere had taken its market. A reason was given. Whether it could be questioned, and by whom, the case does not record. This is exactly what this section asks: once a reason has been given, is there someone who is not the party that judged who can use it to ask, and does asking have any effect?

Judgment layer. Who determines that hard times exist; who reviews afterwards; if the judgment is wrong, what the position bears, and whether an individual has independent grounds for bearing further responsibility.

GS13 Judging hard times, and answering for the judgment. Whether hard times are real, how much is borne and for how long, who decides and who is accountable: these are written down clearly, and the CEO is among them. The reasons can be given and can be questioned; if the judgment is wrong, the position that made it bears the corrections and the consequences of accountability that correspond to it; individual responsibility beyond that requires independent grounds and is not automatically transferred because the judgment was wrong (per Political Economy E2; Institution Theory Z4, Z6).

§4 Direction: Keeping the Direction Is Also Giving Conditions

Keeping the direction is leaving conditions for those who come later: new employees, the next CEO, later users and partners. When they come in, this company is still a company oriented toward the Kingdom of Ends, not because everyone still remembers that sentence, but because that sentence is still in the articles, in the decision procedures, in places where it can be taken up to ask.

Make "open to questioning" the company's own habit: give reasons publicly, review afterwards, keep a record of corrections. A company's record of corrections is the most solid condition it gives to those who come later: they know what has gone wrong here, how it was corrected, and what they can take up to ask with in future.

Two disciplines. The arrangements that keep the direction do not guarantee that it is kept; outcomes are not taken as rules. There will always be times when it is not kept. The Specialist Paper says that the normative layer acknowledges that it has a remainder, and that the remainder yields no permission (A Way Out, a Way Forward, E41); extended to the arrangements that keep the direction (own undertaking): where a set of arrangements does not reach, that it does not reach there does not make anything permissible.

Where This Paper Stops

This paper has laid down two propositions: consensus loosens, and the arrangements that keep the direction do not presuppose consensus (GS12); judging hard times, and answering for the judgment (GS13).

What it has not proved: the particular forms of corporate governance; the design of whistleblowing law; the legal arrangements for acquisition and succession.

With this, the five parties and keeping the direction have been written for the whole series. How it interfaces with existing theories of the company, what it has handed on, and what accounts it still holds itself, are handed to Paper 0.

Remainders left: at what point an incentive structure counts as "overriding the baseline," the normative layer has given a direction, and the judgment must be made case by case by actual effect (§2); when the channels that keep the direction are changed, which ways of changing them themselves cross the line this paper has given only three questions, not a list (§2); who reviews the determination of hard times is written only as a tendency (§3).

摘要

前几篇写了一家目的王国方向的公司对五方怎样做。这一篇问：这个方向靠什么守住。2020 年 2 月，富国银行付 30 亿美元，以暂缓起诉协议与民事和解了结销售行为案的刑事与民事调查；它承认，2002 年到 2016 年，社区银行繁重的销售指标与随之而来的管理层压力下，客户被收了不该收的费用与利息。内部调查者 2004 年就称之为"蔓延的瘟疫"，社区银行的高层却没有改那套销售模式。问题早被看到了，为什么没有改？这一篇不替这一案作答；它问的是，一家公司的方向靠什么守住。共识会松：人会换，公司会被收购，写下的共识会慢慢只剩在纸上；公司又不能要求人认同它。所以守向不能以共识为前提，只能落在构与位置上：底线写进章程与决策程序，改了要公开、可被问；自我修正的通道独立于被问的一方，照三问核，问的人不因问而受罚；激励的结构不压过底线，定指标的位置对指标压到了哪里负责（GS12）。约翰·刘易斯的合伙委员会能罢免主席，特拉华公益公司要定期报告、股东可以起诉，欧盟的吹哨人指令设了由主管机关来办的外部渠道、禁止报复，各示范一种安排；约翰·刘易斯缩编合伙委员会的计划，与本系列设的通衢第三代改动卖家申诉委员会的组成，示范守向的通道本身也会被改。难关的判断，理由说得出来、可以被问；判错了，作判断的位置担与它相应的纠错与职责后果，个人另负责任须有独立的根据（GS13）。最后说方向：守向也是给后来的人留条件。全篇两条（GS12、GS13），各节末写出，篇末合列。

引言

2020 年 2 月 21 日，美国司法部公布：富国银行（Wells Fargo）同意付 30 亿美元，了结销售行为案的刑事与民事调查。法律上，这是暂缓起诉协议与民事和解，不是刑事定罪。在公司承认的事实里：从 2002 年到 2016 年，社区银行"繁重的销售指标与随之而来的管理层压力"（"The Community Bank's onerous sales goals and accompanying management pressure"）之下，公司向客户收了它无权收取的数以百万计美元的费用与利息（"collected millions of dollars in fees and interest to which the Company was not entitled"），损害了一些客户的信用评分，非法使用了客户敏感的个人信息（美国司法部新闻稿，2020-02-21）。

问题早被看到了。据同一份材料，内部调查者 2004 年就称这些做法为一场"蔓延的瘟疫"（"growing plague"），2005 年说它"正在失控"（"spiraling out of control"）；社区银行的高层却"拒绝改变销售模式"（"refused to alter the sales model"）。

问题早被看到了，为什么没有改？这一篇不替这一案作答。由此问的是：一家公司的方向，靠什么守住。

这里判的是这一项做法与它的结构，不判整家银行，也不读心。第一节说共识会松；第二节说守向的安排；第三节说难关的判断与负责；第四节说方向。

读法照前置篇：构用应当，位置用职责，人用自己的法。

第一节　共识会松

前置篇说，共识按落处认：写进章程与决策程序，做决定时可以被援引，位置照它持职责。落下来了，也会松。人会换，CEO 会换，公司会被收购，难关会来。写下的共识，会慢慢只剩在纸上：章程里那一句还在，决定的时候没有人援引；援引了，也没有人拿它去问。

还有一件让守向更难。公司不能要求员工内心认同写进公司的那个共识：「构可以要求一个位置做什么，不能要求一个人想什么」（F6 §3.3；第二篇 GS6）。这是护人的一条，这个系列不收回。可它带来一个后果：守向不能以"人人仍然认同"为前提。新来的员工可以不认同，下一任 CEO 可以不认同；方向还要守得住，就只能落在构与位置上（自担）。

还要分开一件事。有意改了方向的，出了这个系列的范围（第一篇第三节）。这一篇说的，是没有打算改、却慢慢松了的那一种。

第二节　守向的安排

规范层。 守向的安排，这个系列只要求四样。

第一，底线写进章程与决策程序。写进去的，改了要公开，可以被问。

第二，自我修正的通道独立于被问的一方。独立照制度论的三项条件核：通道「独立于被追问的一方，内部主体实际可以发动，发动不被报复」（制度论 Z34）。通道有没有实效，照三问核：谁能启动；对理由、行动、规则或任职位置有什么实际效力；被问的一方怎样不能独自取消它（政治经济学第三篇 E16）。被问的一方能独自关掉问它的路，就读进第二道闸（政治经济学第四篇 E22）。

第三，问的人不因问而受罚（制度论 Z34）。

第四，激励的结构不压过底线。公司给一个位置定的指标，本身不是问题；它压到了哪里，才是问题。政治经济学写：回报率被置于唯一目标的位置，「提供一项须核的信号；若它进一步取得压过守线、取消相关主体的追问与改向、或替被投资者决定其自身目的的地位，就按这些实际作用判」（政治经济学第五篇 E27）。原句说的是投资者给被投资的构所定的回报目标；这里换成公司给一个位置定的指标（自担）。原句三支里的第三支，"替被投资者决定其自身目的"，推不到一个位置上：位置有职责，没有自身的目的；它在这里读作替位置后面的人定他自己的事（第二篇 GS6）。定指标的位置，对指标压到了哪里负责：这一句 E27 原句里没有，根据在位置的职责（政治经济学第一篇 E2；制度论 Z6；自担）。

对照：问到了，不足以证明通道有效。 富国银行一案里，内部调查已经指出了问题。司法部的材料写："尽管知道这些违法的销售做法，社区银行的高层没有采取足够的行动"（"Despite knowledge of the illegal sales practices, Community Bank senior leadership failed to take sufficient action."）。

这一件说明的是：问到了，不足以证明通道有效。谁能迫使改正，被问的一方能不能取消必要的效力，要再核结构。三问里的第一问，在这里有了答案：内部调查者能启动，也启动了。要再核的是后两问：问了以后有什么实际效力，被问的一方能不能独自让它停下。本文核到的材料，读到"问到了不足以证明通道有效"为止，不替这两问作答。司法部的材料把社区银行的高层、集团的管理层与董事会分开写；这里也不把它们压成一个不分层级的"被问的一方"：后两问要一层一层核。

规则层的例子。

约翰·刘易斯合伙公司有一个员工选出的合伙委员会。照现行章程（2026 年 8 月版）第 10 条，委员会若判断主席未能履行、或已不再适合履行章程所定的职责，可以通过一项"章程决议"，依约翰·刘易斯合伙信托有限公司的公司章程罢免主席（"If the Council judges that the Chairman has failed to fulfil, or is no longer a suitable person to fulfil, the responsibilities of the office as laid down in the Constitution, it may pass a 'Resolution upon the Constitution' to dismiss the Chairman according to the Articles of Association of John Lewis Partnership Trust Limited."）。2023 年 5 月，委员会就时任主席的领导举行过一次信任表决；那一次表决本身没有约束力（ITV News，2023-05-10）。罢免是章程里的一条通道，信任表决是另一件事；两件分开读。

特拉华州的公益公司，至少每两年要向股东提交一份报告，写明董事会定的公益目标、衡量进展的标准、依这些标准的客观事实与对达成目标的评估（"shall no less than biennially provide its stockholders with a statement"）（Delaware Code, Title 8, §366(b)）；合计持有公司已发行股份至少百分之二的股东（上市公司为百分之二或市值两百万美元，取低者），可以起诉，要求执行平衡的义务（§367）。照三问读：谁能启动，达到门槛的股东能；仅凭员工、用户、社会这些身份，不能启动这一项诉讼（自担）。这些身份可能与达门槛的股东重叠；这一条也不概括其他追问的渠道。报告向公众公开与否，可以由章程或附则另定。

欧盟的《吹哨人保护指令》要求：50 名以上劳动者的私营单位设内部举报渠道（"legal entities in the private sector with 50 or more workers"）（Art. 8(3)）；举报人可以先经内部渠道，也可以直接向外部渠道举报（"or by directly reporting through external reporting channels"）（Art. 10）；外部渠道由成员国指定的主管机关接收与跟进（"Member States shall designate the authorities competent to receive, give feedback and follow up on reports"）（Art. 11(1)），第 12 条第 1 款再以信息的完整、不被篡改与保密等标准，界定外部渠道的"独立与自主"（"independent and autonomous"）；禁止任何形式的报复（"any form of retaliation"），停职、解雇都在其中（Art. 19）（Directive (EU) 2019/1937）。这一例示范两件：外部渠道由主管机关来办，不由被举报的单位来办，所以独立于被问的一方；问的人不因问而受罚。

前面用过的两件，也是守向的安排：巴塔哥尼亚把带表决权的股份放进一个为守住公司目的而设的信托，博世把表决权与收益分开（第一篇）。

判断层：守向的通道本身也会被改。 2025 年 6 月的报道说（FashionUnited，引《每日电讯报》），约翰·刘易斯正准备把合伙委员会由 57 人缩到 43 人；公司对员工说明的理由，是原来的结构"过于依赖层级与上报"（"too heavily on hierarchy and escalation"）。这是报道里的计划；是否已经生效，本文没有核到。

这一篇不判这次调整。一个委员会是 57 人好还是 43 人好，不是规范层能答的。这里只问三件：改通道的决定，是否公开；是否可以被问；问的人里，有没有被问的一方以外的人。守向的通道也是构，也要可被追问。

本系列设的例子里也有一件，正合"人会换"。苹果城的通衢集团，第 24 年夏，乔家第二代退休，第三代接任家族办公室主席与通衢董事长，B 类股的投票权随之由他行使。第 25 年，第三代主持的董事会停了第 15 年起的"小店扶持计划"；卖家申诉委员会里两名由金牌卖家互选的委员，改由管理层任命。上一代放宽的几条，在下一代手里收了回去；改通道组成的，正是被问的一方。这两样不是一种东西：小店扶持是给条件，属方向；申诉委员会的两席是问的通道，是本节第二项要它独立于被问的一方的那一种。这一例说明一件：问的通道若由被问的一方说改就改，人一换，它就可以被收回（政治经济学第三篇 E16）。它不是第一节说的慢慢松了，是被问的一方直接改了通道；它也不说明第三代是什么样的人，不说明通衢一切问的路都已关上（第四篇第四节）。

GS12　共识会松：守住方向的安排，不以共识为前提。公司不能要求人认同，守向只落在构与位置上：底线写进章程与决策程序，改了要公开、可被问。自我修正的通道独立于被问的一方，照三问核；问的人不因问而受罚。激励的结构不压过底线：定指标的位置，对指标压到了哪里负责（承制度论 Z34、政治经济学 E16、E22、E27、《制度如何可能》§3.3；自担）。

第三节　难关的判断与负责

第二篇说，难关的代价落在事上；可是难关是不是真的，代价分多少，由谁说了算？

规范层。 难关是不是真的，担多少，担多久：理由说得出来，也可以被问。谁定，谁负责，写清；CEO 也在其中。

判错了怎样担。 难关的判断会错：以为过不去的，其实过得去；以为只要裁一批，结果还要再裁。判错了，作判断的位置说明理由、接受复核，担与这个位置相应的纠错与职责后果。构的账仍归构。个人是否另负责任，须有独立的根据，不因结果判错就自动转记到某个人身上；CEO 同受这套核法。政治经济学写过：「构的账、位置的职责与个人有独立根据的责任分别核，构的名义不遮蔽后者，构的失责也不自动转记给某个人」（政治经济学第一篇 E2）；制度论写：「构的名义不遮蔽有独立根据的个人责任，构的失责也不自动转记到某个人身上」（制度论 Z6）。两句是同一条线的两面：位置担位置的，人担人有独立根据的那一份。

规则层的倾向。 难关时怎么办，事先写清。林肯电气 1958 年起保障就业，员工同意接受必要的岗位与排班调整（第二篇第三节）：难关来了怎样担，在难关来以前就约好了。难关的认定，可以由独立于管理层的一方复核（倾向，自担）。第二篇第三节的两样倾向也在这里：分担要累进，CEO 也在其中；怎样分担，让员工参与。

例子。 Barry-Wehmiller 的 CEO 写那一次难关，说他起初"照以往那样"准备应对（"I was poised to react as I always had."），后来改作全员无薪休假（前置篇引言）。判断可以改；改的理由，他也说出来了。

对照。 青窑瓷业第 25 年关了两条窑线、裁员九百，公司说外地的低价瓷抢走了市场。理由说了。能不能被问，由谁来问，这一例没有记下。这正是这一节要问的地方：一个理由说出来以后，有没有一个不是作判断的那一方的人，能拿它去问，问了有没有用。

判断层。 谁认定难关；过后谁复盘；判错了，位置担什么，个人有没有独立的根据另负责任。

GS13　难关的判断与负责。难关是不是真的，担多少、多久，谁定、谁负责，写清；CEO 也在其中。理由说得出来，也可以被问；判错了，作判断的位置担与它相应的纠错与职责后果，个人另负责任须有独立根据，不因判错自动转记（承政治经济学 E2；制度论 Z4、Z6）。

第四节　方向：守向也是给条件

守住方向，是给后来的人留条件：新来的员工，下一任 CEO，以后的用户与伙伴。他们进来的时候，这家公司还是一家目的王国方向的公司，不是因为每个人都还记得那句话，是因为那句话还在章程里、在决策的程序里、在可以拿来问的地方。

把"可被问"做成公司自己的习惯：公开理由，复盘，留下纠错的记录。一家公司的纠错记录，是它给后来的人最实在的一份条件：他们知道这里出过什么错，怎样改的，以后可以拿什么去问。

两条纪律。守向的安排不保证守得住；结果不当规则。永远有守不住的时候。专论说，规范层自认有余项，余项推不出许可（专论 E41）；推到守向的安排上（自担）：一套安排没有管到的地方，不因为没有管到就可以随意。

这一篇停在哪里

这一篇立了两条：共识会松，守住方向的安排不以共识为前提（GS12）；难关的判断与负责（GS13）。

它没有证明的：公司治理的具体形式；举报法的设计；收购与继任的法律安排。

全系列到这里，五方与守向写完。与已有的公司理论怎样对接，交出去了什么，自己还留着什么账，交给 Paper 0。

留下的余项：激励的结构压到哪里算"压过底线"，规范层给了方向，判断要照实际作用逐件做（第二节）；守向的通道被改时，哪些改法本身越了线，这一篇只给了三问，没有给清单（第二节）；难关认定的复核由谁做，只写了倾向（第三节）。

本篇命题（合列）
