Abstract

This paper is Paper 0 of Parent-Child Theory: written last, placed first. It lays down no propositions; it only does a few things for those who read this series: it states this series' place in SAE, sets out how far it reads and what its main line is, gives the method and the shared words the papers have in common, interfaces as a whole with several traditions, says once, all together, the directions of colonization read in the papers, lists together the forty-two propositions the whole series has established, and registers the remainders that are left over and not closed out, and the questions that still await proof.

Parent-Child Theory is the last branch downstream of SAE Relation Theory, following Love Theory, Marriage Theory and Cooperation Theory. This series does not define all parent-child relations; the parent-child relation it reads has as its premise that the end of the father or the mother is headed in the 15DD direction: across one matter after another, headed toward treating the child as an end, and not merely as a means; people are all mixed, falling back is read as it occurs, and having fallen back, one can turn back. It reads relation by relation: father and child one relation, mother and child another. It borrows the two layers of the institutional side to read what parents give the child: the Base Layer is to be secured, the Emergence Layer supported; it takes the child's phase transitions as one axis along which to read how the parents' end acts, the order being only an example; it reads until the child's cannot-not has come to stand in one matter after another, until handing over and leading. The main line, put plainly: from the start he is another end; the parents guard him, give him conditions, and one matter at a time give back to him what they held for him. The seven papers read, in order: Another End; securing; support; arrangements; collisions; several caregivers; handing over and leading.

This paper interfaces as a whole with Locke, Rousseau, Kant, Hegel, the Analects and the Mencius, and Gibran; developmental psychology is not interfaced with, and the several SAE papers on the developmental side are pointed back to. The directions of colonization read in the papers borrow things that are, or may come to be, between parents and child: the child's standing, securing, dependency, the window, "for your own good," the parents' meaning, giving, arrangements, the child's "no," resistance, a third person, leading. These things carry no direction in themselves; the direction is read on the one effect in which they are taken up to do something. This paper proves no proposition at the object layer.

Introduction

The seven papers that follow each read one thing. Paper 1, Another End, reads what the parent-child relation this series speaks of looks like: the premise, falling back and turning back, relation by relation, the three kinds in a relation, the two books at the child's end, his never having chosen to enter, the two uses of DD for parents and for the child, the object layer and the operation layer. Paper 2, Securing in the Parent-Child Relation, reads the Base Layer: where the shape of the two layers is borrowed from, where the securing parents give comes from, the child's standing and violation, securing not made conditional, the two senses of dependency, the child's signals before 13DD, and how securing shifts with the phase transitions. Paper 3, Support in the Parent-Child Relation, reads the Emergence Layer: setting, not source; the three things, teaching, nurturing and leading; the direct-write window; "for your own good"; meaning not copied; support not entered as repayment. Paper 4, Arrangements in the Parent-Child Relation, reads the household's rules: today's arrangements for tomorrow, the three questions by which an arrangement is read, the domain-by-domain handing over of the authority to interpret, that "no," the ex ante structural condition, and pre-occupation. Paper 5, Collisions in the Parent-Child Relation, reads the meeting of the child's own cannot-not, once it has grown, with the parents' law: the 14DD turn and rebellion, what the parents' end looks like in this stretch, the three forms of colonization at 14DD, the threshold of the Conflict Treatise, mutual chiseling, and collision running two ways. Paper 6, Several Caregivers, reads the case in which a child has more than one caregiver: each its own relation, by way of a third person, caregivers questioned and mutual questioning, one party treating the child merely as a means, the child brought in by remarriage and grandparents. Paper 7, Handing Over and Leading, reads the last stretch: coming of age, raising and having it come to stand, "being able to unfold without revolving around me" on the child's side, leading, and the turn to family affection.

Written this way, several things are left that no single paper takes care of: why this series is here, and how far it reads; how the main line strings the seven papers together; the method the papers share, and what the same word means in which paper; several traditions whose questions concern the whole series; what the directions of colonization read in the papers look like when set side by side; and which questions still await proof. This paper takes care of these.

Method as in the papers: the conclusions of upstream papers are said in plain words, and sources and extensions go in the notes. This paper lays down no propositions, and alters no sentence of the papers that follow. How the pieces upstream handed to this series are placed goes in the appendix at the end.

§1 Place: The Last Branch Downstream on the Relational Side

SAE has three sides: the subject side, the relational side and the institutional side. The relational side is unfolded by Relation Theory; once Relation Theory is written, there are several branches downstream: Love Theory, Marriage Theory, Parent-Child Theory and Cooperation Theory.1 Love Theory, Marriage Theory and Cooperation Theory come first; this series is the branch written last.

The division of labor between the two layers Relation Theory has written out: Relation Theory in the abstract establishes only what all relations have; what holds only in one kind of relation is handed to the series on that kind of relation to argue.2 Whatever has already been laid down at the layer of relation, this series uses directly and does not rewrite: the minimal condition of a relation, the two directions of cultivation and colonization, expectation, commitment, the sediment of dealings and ending are all read by Relation Theory. Of what Relation Theory handed to this series, several things it gave first itself: acknowledgment that requires no qualification and the realization of recognition kept on separate books; the two senses of dependency; today's arrangements for tomorrow; effects that carry the direction of colonization without passing through conflict (Appendix).

With Marriage Theory. Marriage Theory reads two people; once the two have a child, the side of shared child-rearing between the two is in Marriage Theory; what lies between the child and the parents is handed to this series; where three or more are involved, accounts are kept pair by pair.3 Marriage Theory reads preparing for someone who has not yet come; once the child has come, the relation between the father or the mother and the child is read from this series on. Marriage Theory's scope takes both parties headed in the 15DD direction; this series takes the end of the father or the mother; this difference can be traced to the kind of relation: the two ends of the parent-child relation do not start symmetrically, and the child never chose to enter (Paper 1).

With Cooperation Theory. Of a thing parents and their adult children do together, the face of doing it together is in Cooperation Theory; what lies between parents and child belongs here.4 Cooperation Theory's scope further requires that both avow this undertaking and actually take part; this series makes no further requirement that both sides avow it, for the same reason.

With Rights Theory and the institutional side. The child's basic rights have their standing in himself; how they are protected and by what rules belongs to the institutional side; guardianship, recognition-based rights held on his behalf, and the delegated holding and replacement on the side of caregivers' converging the child's action belong to Rights Theory and the institutional side.5 This series reads only the side between person and person: the part the institution does not provide for, or that goes beyond its provisions. On the occasion when a parent's effect crosses the child's basic rights, this series reads as far as "this is a violation" and the boundary crossed; protection and disposition after the violation are handed over there, and what that effect left in the dealings is still read here (Paper 2).

With Political Economy. The two layers, the Base Layer to be secured and the Emergence Layer supported, were written out by Political Economy for institutions;6 this series borrows their shape to read what lies between the parent and the child, and the step of carrying it over is this series' own undertaking. Political Economy writes that cultivation on the side of the institution and on the side of the person are read apart; this series reads the side of the person (Paper 2).

With the several papers on the developmental side. SAE has already read the child from the side of development and learning: the education paper divides teaching, nurturing and leading; the three papers on the Terrible Twos, the Terrible Teens and Terrible Telos read the genesis of 13DD, 14DD and 15DD; the Learning series reads how each layer learns.7 This series does not read these again, and calls on them where they are used; what it reads is how the end of the father or the mother acts, and how far the child has grown is only a condition of that end's acting.

With a future series on relations of care. Cases needing long-term care after coming of age, and parents grown old needing care from their adult children, this series does not read; they are handed to a future series on relations of care (Paper 7).

§2 Scope and Main Line

Scope. There are six items.

First, it does not define all parent-child relations. To say that a relation is not within the parent-child relation this series reads is not to say that it is not a parent-child relation; the subject is that relation, not that person (Paper 1, PC1, PC3).

Second, the end of the father or the mother headed in the 15DD direction. In SAE's levels, the layer at which the other is recognized as an end too is written 15DD. 15DD is non dubito: "it is not only I who am an end; the other, too, is an end in itself."8 The parent-child relation this series reads has as its premise that the end of the father or the mother is headed in this direction: across one matter after another, headed toward treating the child as an end, and not merely as a means. The direction is a premise, not a component; the child never chose to enter, and no further requirement is made here that both sides avow it (Paper 1).

Third, direction, falling back, and turning back. There is no parent who stands purely at 15DD; people are all mixed; the direction is read by one operation at a time, falling back is read as it occurs, and does not overturn the direction. After falling back, this end can turn back, own up to that occasion, and make some change in the arrangements and dealings that follow; where there is persistent refusal to turn back, the direction is no longer at 15DD. Reflection is read from words and deeds, and is confirmed only at the person's own end; it does not cancel a violation already read, nor leave the child owing forgiveness; "persistent" means continuing not to turn back (Paper 1, PC1).

Fourth, relation by relation, and the three kinds in a relation. Father and child one relation, mother and child another, grandparents and other caregivers each their own; a single parent is read the same way. The three kinds apply to a relation as a whole, not as a sieve that lets each matter in or out of scope: a relation headed toward treating the child as an end is within scope, and every matter in it is read; the relation that treats the child merely as a means, and the relation that does not treat him merely as a means yet is not headed toward treating him as an end either, are both relations that on the whole remain in the 14DD direction and are not headed toward 15DD (this says where a relation is headed; it does not grade a person); they are not within scope, and are only registered; the occasion that crosses basic rights is, separately, a violation (Paper 1, PC2, PC3; Paper 6).

Fifth, the object layer and the operation layer. The object layer reads the relation within scope; at the operation layer the parent confirms only his own end, and of the child's end, and of another caregiver's end, sees only clues. If it is later confirmed that the direction premise of the relation being read did not obtain, the series does not unfold it as an object within scope, and does not turn and judge; one falling back within the relation is no ground for exit (Paper 1, PC7).

Sixth, the upper bound and the edges. This series reads until the child's cannot-not has come to stand in one matter after another, until handing over and leading, until this relation becomes one face of family affection between adults; the child's own 15DD is his own affair, and is not in this series (Paper 7). What this series does not read: guardianship, and the side of the family as a construct; the protection of the child's standing and its rules; disposition after a line has been crossed; the side between husband and wife; long-term care after coming of age.

Main line. In plain prose it is one sentence: from the start he is another end. The parents guard him, give him conditions, and one matter at a time give back to him what they held for him.

Put technically, as the main text and the abstracts of the papers use it: the father or the mother, headed in the 15DD direction, secures the child's Base Layer and supports his Emergence Layer; from before his own cannot-not has come to stand, the reading goes on to handing over and leading once it has come to stand domain by domain. His standing as an end is not conferred by the parents; what he can do grows with his phase transitions; the conditions the parents give do not avow his direction for him. The first half is Papers 2 and 3: the two layers. The middle is Papers 4 and 5: arrangements and collisions, and the domain-by-domain handing over of the authority to interpret. The second half is the two books of Paper 1 and the handing over and leading of Paper 7.

At the core of the passage on scope in Paper 1 is the one sentence that states scope: at least one father or mother, across one matter after another, is headed toward treating the child as an end; and having fallen back, can turn back. This sentence sums up the entrance; it is not an equivalent substitute for all the conditions of scope.

What also strings the seven papers together is the division into two books: the child's standing as an end is full from the start; what he can do grows layer by layer with his phase transitions. The first book does not wait for the second; the second does not lower the first. Every effect of the parents' end that the papers read is read under these two books.

§3 Method

A layer records one operation; stance, and emergence. DD has two uses for parents and for the child: at the parent's end, 15DD is a stance, the direction in which one operation goes; at the child's end, DD reads emergence and maturation, and does not become a label. The maturation of the first-person position is checked by content-domain; the present operation follows its object, and is read separately; whether a piece of learning is still within the direct-write window is read separately again; the three do not certify one another. Whether the emergence of 13DD itself arrives at once or domain by domain, this series does not settle (Paper 1, PC6).

The order is only an example; what is read is the parents' end. The papers take the child's phase transitions as one axis: before 13DD, the emergence of 13DD, 13DD in operation and 14DD budding, the 14DD turn, 14DD established. This order is only one example, not a definition of development, nor does it sort the child into cells. What this series reads is how the end of the father or the mother acts; the child's development is read only as a condition of that end's acting, not as a study of developmental stages. So the main text gives no particular ages; ages in upstream papers and in research material appear only in the notes, as a posterior reference.

Speak of structure, not of good and bad; issue no ought. A parent-child relation is not scored by its length, by success and failure, or by what the child later became. The main text is not written as "what parents should do," but as how the parents' end acts.

Do not read minds; do not predict. Inference goes by structure, or by the original sentences of upstream; how a person thinks is not said for him. Of the child, only what he says and does is written. No prediction is made about how one kind of parent or one child will turn out. The care seen from outside does not certify the parents' inner direction.

Only the layer between person and person. Guardianship, the family as a construct, the legal duty of maintenance, and the protection of the child's standing and its rules belong to the institutional side; they are acknowledged but not judged, and registered as conditions of the effect.

"Cannot-not," not "duty." The care and support a father or mother gives come from this end's own cannot-not, and are this end's answering to its own law; this series has no concept of a duty toward others. The face of giving is read as entitlement, anchored in the giving (Paper 2).

Cultivation and colonization in the broad sense. They name the direction of one effect, not labels stuck on persons or on a relation; the direction is read on this one effect, and whether it took hold is seen in the child's later understanding and action; the measure of outcomes is not used to judge direction. Where the reading reaches the value layer (incomplete cultivation is not colonization; pre-occupation), colonization takes the value layer's narrow sense instead, marked on the spot.9 Violation is judged separately, and only at the layer of basic rights.

Examples. A single family runs through the whole series: He Chuan, Lin Wan, and their child He Zhou; side examples are Song Lan and her daughter Song Nian, and Xu Ning, Ji Ping and Xu Dong. These are made-up examples, used only to see structure: in the examples, that He Chuan and Lin Wan are headed in the 15DD direction is a set premise; the examples write their falling back, and also their turning back afterward, and the two can question each other. When the child appears, only words and deeds are written; harm is only registered structurally, not written as plot or detail. Historical examples do not use children in history as the persons concerned; they may use the childhoods that adults have themselves written down and that have been made public, read only for how the adult later wrote, not rewritten as a record of the child's inner life at the time, nor used to certify the parents' direction; private letters and diaries published after death are marked as such in the notes. Only the deceased, whose records are public, are used; living real persons are not.

Research material. Where no suitable example is to hand, research in developmental psychology can serve as material: following published reports, naming no living individual, giving the source in the notes, and reporting sample ages truthfully; findings are not used as a step of argument, and the structural reading is this series' own undertaking.

Interfacing with the tradition. The papers each interface with figures by question, and this paper interfaces as a whole. Interfacing reads only structure, and does not evaluate traditions. Developmental psychology is not interfaced with: the several SAE papers on the developmental side have already been in conversation with it, and they are pointed back to here (§5).

Sources and extensions. The conclusions of upstream papers are said in plain words in the main text; sources, original sentences and the proposition numbers of other papers go in the notes. Where upstream words are carried beyond their original place, from institutions toward persons to parents toward the child, from between adults to before adulthood, from one and the same bearer to the parents' today and the child's tomorrow, from the gate of recognition-based rights to what lies between parents and child, it is stated on the spot to be an extension, this series' own undertaking.

§4 Shared Words

Securing, support; protection. Used apart, as in Political Economy: "securing" is used for supply in the Base Layer, and giving conditions for a person's emergence is called "support."10 The "negative boundaries of support" Marriage Theory speaks of are the same "support." The institution's guarding of the child's basic rights is written "protection," not "securing."

Entitlement; duty. "Entitlement" follows the usage on the side of rights: anchored in the giving end, in the giving; the child is the end this giving is directed at, and does not hold it.11 "Duty" this series does not use between parents and child: there is only one inward duty, the subject's answerability to its own law.12 A legal duty of maintenance belongs to the institutional side.

15DD, 14DD, 13DD; coming to stand. At the parents' end, these speak of stance; at the child's end, of the layer that has already emerged, or already matured, in some matter. The main text writes "in this matter, his cannot-not has already come to stand," not "this is a 13DD child."

Coming of age. Coming of age structurally is read as the child's 14DD coming to stand, arriving domain by domain; coming of age in law is a line drawn by institutions, acknowledged but not judged (Paper 7).

Cultivation; teaching, nurturing, leading. Where not otherwise marked, cultivation follows Relation Theory's broad sense: one effect goes toward making space. Where the education paper's tripartite division of "teaching, nurturing, leading" is cited, its "nurturing" follows that paper's special usage: giving space for the subject to chisel out self-awareness and purpose on its own. What the Learning series calls nurturing during the window period is that series' usage. The tripartite division says that what is done differs, and is not used to cancel cultivation in the broad sense elsewhere. "Leading" is not leadership, not a position with authority: the adult lives out recognition, the child sees the direction, and it can be done only by personal demonstration.

Arrangement; rules. An arrangement is something parents set for a child that governs him for some time afterward; rules are the kind of arrangement a household states out loud and follows again and again. Both are read as the parents' today's arrangements for the child's tomorrow (Paper 4).

The authority to interpret; handing over; raising and having it come to stand. The authority to interpret is who says how a matter is to be read and what it counts as. "Handing over" means the interpretations and avowals the parents' end used to hold for the child being transferred domain by domain; the initiation of questioning is not within "handing over": it becomes his own as soon as the negation-gate runs of itself in a matter. Being able to raise something is not the same as having it come to stand (Paper 4, Paper 7). "Handing over" differs from "withdrawing" in Cooperation Theory: withdrawing is a party withdrawing his own side.

Pre-occupation. Following the usage at the value layer: a not-yet-running position filled before it begins to run. Marking an arrangement as a final content the child will be unable to refute in future is a signal of pre-occupation, not a judgment of it (Paper 4).

The gate: the negation-gate; running of itself, exercise on his behalf. Following the usage on the side of value and of rights: the negation-gate is the link in a person's own law by which he says no to content imposed on him, and questions and negates what has been produced.13 Where, in a matter, this gate can be run by himself in the first person, it is said to run of itself; the side that cannot yet, and is exercised for him by those who care for him, is exercise on his behalf; the range in which it runs of itself shifts gradually with the matter and over time.14 Where the gate runs of itself in a matter, the position of raising and refusing in that matter becomes his own at once (Paper 7).

Collision: conflict, mutual chiseling. Following the usage at the layer of relation: collision has two subclasses, conflict and mutual chiseling. "Conflict" is used only after the two gates of the Conflict Treatise have been passed; what has not passed them is read as dealings and collision. Mutual chiseling is judged by process, not by identity (Paper 5).

Guardianship. A word of the institutional side, not read by this series.

Family. This series does not read the family as a construct, nor does it merge a family into one bearer for the purpose of keeping accounts; the family and the interests of its members are not read as one indivisible whole.15 The effects that the rules "our family" has set have on the child are still read separately in each person's relation. Whether a family can separately be established as a bearer is left as a remainder (§7).

Caregiver. Said on its own, it refers broadly to whoever actually cares for a child, parents included; said alongside the parents, as "other caregivers," it refers to grandparents on either side, nannies, relatives and the like, and also includes the party who, after remarriage, actually takes on care. Each has a relation with the child (Paper 6).

Subject-to-be. Following the usage of Value Theory and of the side of rights: a bearer whose equal standing as an end is already in place, while the first-person running of its negation-gate is still in genesis. When applied to particular matters, it is checked by domain; his standing as an end does not wait for him to grow up.16

Family affection. Only a name: the dealings between parents and adult children, read by Relation Theory and Cooperation Theory; this series gives it no definition of its own.

§5 Interfacing as a Whole

Locke: paternal power. In Chapter 6 of the Second Treatise of Government, on paternal power, Locke says that children are not born in this full state of equality, though they are born to it; the power parents have over their children is to take care of them while they cannot yet use their own reason, and it ends as the child grows up.17 This series and Locke shed light on each other at two points: the two books, being born in and being born to; and the parents' letting go, which comes when the child can judge for himself. They part at two points too: Locke begins from the parents' power, while this series does not establish an authority of parents to govern the child, and reads the effects between person and person; Locke explains by the ability to use reason when parental government ends, while this series instead reads, matter by matter, how the first-person position operates, and when the position of raising and refusing becomes the person's own (Papers 1 and 7).

Rousseau: negative education; dependence on things alone. In Émile, Rousseau holds that the first education should be negative, and that the child should be made to depend on things alone, not on people.18 This series and Rousseau shed light on each other in leaving room (Paper 3); where they part is that Rousseau's tutor designs a whole "necessity of things" for the child, while what this series reads is exactly who sets an arrangement and whether the child can change it (Paper 4).

Kant: brought into the world without consent; freedom under constraint; paternal government. In the Doctrine of Right, Kant says that the parents, without the child's consent, have brought a person into the world, and thereby bear an obligation; the child, as a person, is not a thing; in On Pedagogy he asks how freedom is to be cultivated under constraint; in an essay of 1793 he says that a government that, as a father toward his children, decides its subjects' happiness for them out of benevolence is the greatest conceivable despotism.19 These three places this series reads in Papers 1, 3, 5 and 7: the child never chose to enter, and his constitutive position does not pass to the parents, while this series does not derive from this a duty toward others; two laws meeting in one matter; in a matter that is already an adult child's own, still treating him as a child, where what is borrowed is only the shape, not that kind of government. Marriage Theory and Political Economy have already interfaced with two of these places, and they are pointed back to.

Hegel: children are not things; discipline and self-will. In the sections on the family in the Philosophy of Right, Hegel says that children are in themselves free, and are not things; that children have a right to be maintained and educated from the common property of the family; and, in the main text of §174, that the parents' right over the children's arbitrary will has as its end to discipline and to educate them, while the Addition (Zusatz) to §174 says that the purpose of discipline is to break the child's self-will (Eigenwille).20 This series and Hegel shed light on each other at the first point (Paper 1); at the second, this series leaves how the child's standing is protected, and the rules governing that protection, to the institutional side, and on the relational side reads the giving the parents' end does (Paper 2); at the third, the antecedents of "self-will" and of "a cannot-not of one's own" are checked first, and the one is not translated directly into the other (Paper 5). Marriage Theory has already cited Hegel's sections on the family, and they are pointed back to.

The Analects and the Mencius. SAE's reading of the Analects has already annotated the chapters on filial conduct; this series sets them against itself at only two points: "give your parents nothing to worry about but your illness" falls on what the parents' end cannot set down (Paper 2); "in serving your parents, remonstrate gently": the child's "no" falls, in that chapter, as gentle remonstrance (Paper 4). The Mencius's "between father and son, affection," and the five relationships, Relation Theory Paper 0 has already set alongside the differential mode of association (Paper 6).21 Interfacing reads only structure, and does not evaluate traditions.

Gibran: on children. In The Prophet, "On Children," Gibran says that children come through their parents but not from them; you may give them your love but not your thoughts; the parents are the bows from which the children as living arrows are sent forth.22 These sentences and this series shed light on each other at three points: "from the start he is another end," "meaning is not copied," and "being able to unfold without revolving around me." In the poem, the parents are the bow and the one who aims is an archer; where they part is that Gibran writes counsel, and hands the aiming to an archer, while this series reads only structure, sets up no such archer, and reads apart giving conditions, deciding his direction for him and personal demonstration, without taking the poem's image directly as a structural criterion.

Developmental psychology: pointed back to. The Terrible Twos paper has already been in conversation with Spitz, Winnicott, Mahler, Piaget, Erikson and attachment research; the Terrible Teens paper with Erikson, Kohlberg and Marcia.23 This series does not interface again; where it uses research, it uses it only as material (§3).

§6 The Directions of Colonization Read in the Papers, Said Once Together

How the directions of colonization the papers have found are read lies in each paper; here only one layer is said together: what they borrow are things that are, or may come to be, between parents and child. These things carry no direction in themselves; whether they carry one is seen from the antecedents of that one effect, not from the name of the act:

  • The child's standing: when "he's still little, he doesn't understand" is used to lower his standing as an end, or his refusals are read uniformly as not yet understanding (Papers 1 and 4).
  • The child as a condition: when the child figures only as the parents' continuation, a link in continuing the line, a security for old age, or a condition for keeping or for using against the other party; a sentence is read by that sentence itself, and what is read is the arrangement (Papers 1 and 6).
  • Securing: when securing is traded for compliance, hung on the child's taking some road, or a lack of securing is made up for by how much support is given (Paper 2).
  • Dependency: when it was made and has locked the path to saying no; the child's depending on care carries no direction in itself, nor is dependency in itself a violation (Paper 2).
  • The window: when the high plasticity of the window period is used to force-write a script the parents designate; the parents' choosing what to teach is not, on that account, this (Paper 3).
  • "For your own good": when it is used to decide the child's direction for him; the difference lies in that one effect (Paper 3).
  • The parents' meaning: when the meaning on the parents' side is copied onto the child (Paper 3).
  • Giving: when the child's compliance or achievement is entered as repayment, or giving is used to possess him or to decide his direction for him; support can have content, a term and conditions (Paper 3).
  • Arrangements: when a present arrangement is marked as a final content the child will be unable to refute in future, it is a signal of pre-occupation; reading pre-occupation further requires that the position be sealed shut before the gate runs of itself (Paper 4).
  • Resistance: direction substitution, superego internalization, and full-support resistance removal, which appears in the guise of support (Paper 5).
  • A third person: when effects on the child are had by way of another caregiver, taking oneself to have no part in that entry (Paper 6).
  • Leading: when leading is made into a position with authority, and an adult child is still treated as a child in a matter that is already his own (Paper 7).

One and the same "for your own good," one and the same "no screens before bed," one and the same fee paid, is in one place a piece of support or an act of maintaining, and only in another the replacement of a direction or the pre-occupation of a position. The direction is read on the one effect in which they are taken up to do something; whether it took hold is seen in how the child afterward understands and acts.

These things do not in themselves decide the direction of an effect; the several effects within one relation are read separately. People are all mixed (Paper 1); falling back is read by that one occasion; whether, after falling back, one can turn back is read separately, by the words and deeds that follow. What this series reads is direction; it does not on this account say that being a parent is dangerous, nor does it advise anyone how to be a parent; it only reads.

§7 Remainders

Several things this series registers, and does not close out.

First, whether a family can separately be established as a bearer. Marriage Theory has already left this as a remainder; this series does not judge it, does not merge a family into one bearer for the purpose of keeping accounts, and reads only each person's relation with the child.

Second, whether the emergence of 13DD itself arrives at once, or domain by domain. This series does not settle it, and only cites the upstream description (Paper 1).

Third, adoption. Marriage Theory has read preparing to adopt; what lies between the adoptive father or mother and the child afterward is read by the same scope and the same reading; the papers have not written about it separately.

Fourth, between siblings. This is not a parent-child relation, and is read by Relation Theory; where it appears as a condition on the child's side, it is only registered.

Fifth, government protection of children. The Theory of Government has read a case in which a child is removed from the home and the matter goes to the family division of the court, and entitlements under government guardianship; that is the institutional side, and this series only registers it.24

Sixth, that the principles of marriage do not cover children. Foundational Paper 6, How Is Institution Possible, Appendix IV, writes that the side of children in a marriage is "a vast remainder, left for those who have children to chisel on their own";25 what this series reads is the side between person and person in one shape of what is chiseled out, and it does not set rules for that side.

Seventh, long-term care after coming of age, and parents grown old needing care from their children: handed to a future series on relations of care.

Eighth, care between humans and AI, and between humans and animals: a choice of range, not a determination about ontology.

§8 Questions Left Open

Two things the papers argue only halfway; they are listed here as they stand.

First, whether the crying and resistance of a child before 13DD can be read as a boundary signal that cannot be overwritten. Paper 2 says only this: they are first read as signals to be answered; first check whether securing is lacking, and also check whether it is a prediction that has fallen through, or a negation coming into being; crying alone does not certify which layer he is at (PC13). Which kind of signal it is, and in what sense it cannot be overwritten, has not been argued.26

Second, how far lateral cross-checking among several caregivers can go in the general case. Paper 6 says only this: a caregiver's reasons, interpretations and adjudications are not placed beyond examination, and being questioned is his own being questioned; another caregiver is one source of external questioning; where an institution exists that is fit for purpose, available, and not controlled by one party to the dispute, the principal screening may be borne by it, and where none is present, deferring, referring on and setting boundaries are not upgraded into a permanent exemption; when both parents are headed in the 15DD direction they can question each other; when there is no other co-caregiver, the external question is looked for among people and procedures actually available (PC35). How far lateral cross-checking among caregivers can generally go, between a person questioning himself and a fit institution, has not been argued.26

§9 The Propositions of the Whole Series

The propositions of the papers are listed together in the very words of each paper's propositions, with only a full stop added at the end; argument and qualification are in each paper. What still awaits proof is not here; see §8.

Paper 1, Another End

PC1　This series does not define all parent-child relations. The parent-child relation it reads has as its premise that the end of the father or the mother is headed in the 15DD direction: across one matter after another, headed toward treating the child as an end, and not merely as a means. The 15DD direction is a premise, not a component of the parent-child relation; the child never chose to enter, and no further requirement is made here that both sides avow it. There is no parent who stands purely at 15DD; people are all mixed; the direction is read by one operation at a time, falling back is read as it occurs, and it does not overturn the direction; after falling back, this end can turn back, own up to that occasion, and make some change in the arrangements and dealings that follow; where there is persistent refusal to turn back, the direction is no longer at 15DD.

PC2　Read relation by relation: father and child one relation, mother and child another; a single parent is read the same way. The relation headed in the 15DD direction is within scope; effects between the two relations are read by pairwise accounting, and the actual effect by which the other relation enters this one is entered in the instigator's relation.

PC3　The three kinds apply to a relation as a whole: a relation headed toward treating the child as an end is within scope, every matter within it is read, and the matter that falls back is read by its own direction and does not exit scope; a relation that treats the child merely as a means is not within scope, and the direction of colonization is read out by the reading of "only as"; a relation that does not treat him merely as a means, yet is not headed toward treating him as an end either, is also not within scope, and is only registered; these two kinds are both relations that on the whole remain in the 14DD direction and are not headed toward 15DD. In whichever kind, the occasion that crosses basic rights is, separately, a violation at the layer of basic rights. Scope is not the whole definition of the parent-child relation: not being unfolded in this series is no denial that it is a parent-child relation.

PC4　The child's standing as an end is full from the start; it is not conferred by the parents, nor revoked by them; what he can do grows, layer by layer, with his phase transitions. The two books are kept apart: "He's still little, he doesn't understand" at most describes what, in some matter, he cannot yet do; it does not lower his standing as an end, nor is it used to read his refusals uniformly as not yet understanding; nor, for the sake of recognizing the child, are the parents' own positions as subjects cancelled.

PC5　The child never chose to enter this relation, nor which conditions he would first enter; his own constitutive position does not on that account pass to the parents. That in reality he cannot do without the people who care for him does not give the caregivers the constitutive right over his value.

PC6　DD has two uses for parents and for the child: at the parent's end, 15DD is a stance, the direction in which one operation goes; at the child's end, DD reads emergence and maturation, and does not become a label. Whether the first-person position can already run of itself in a given domain is checked by domain; how a layer that has already emerged operates on the present occasion, facing this object, is read separately. The layer of a present operation is not used to judge maturation in its place, nor is it concluded from maturation by domain that the emergence of 13DD itself is by domain.

PC7　The object layer reads the relation in which the end of the father or the mother is headed in the 15DD direction; at the operation layer, the parent confirms only his own end. Which layer the child has reached in some matter is, from the parents' end, seen only in clues. If it is later confirmed that the direction premise of the relation being read did not obtain, the series does not unfold it as an object within scope, and does not turn and judge the other; one falling back within the relation is no ground for this exit, and the effects already read are kept on the books as before.

Paper 2, Securing in the Parent-Child Relation

PC8　The shape of the two layers already written out on the institutional side is borrowed to read what lies between the parent and the child: the Base Layer is to be secured, the Emergence Layer supported. The two layers are written apart and matter equally, and neither is traded for the other; protecting the child's basic rights lies on the side of anti-colonization and is not part of cultivation; acts of care and provision are not, in themselves, renamed cultivation; cultivation needs the two layers together, and the thickness of securing does not prove cultivation. The two layers are faces, and one and the same giving can be read two ways. On the side of the person, cultivation has a further reading as stance, coming from the parent's own law. The original speaks of institutions toward persons; it is carried here to the parent toward the child.

PC9　In the parent-child relation this series reads, the care and support a father or mother gives come from this end's own cannot-not: that the child needs someone to look after him is true; it enters the parent's conditions of occurrence and is avowed in his own law. This is this end's answering to its own law, not a duty owed to the child. The face of giving within it is read as entitlement, anchored in the giving the parent actually does; the child is the end this giving is directed at and does not hold it; the face of giving in securing and in support alike can be read as entitlement, not only in the Base Layer. The basic rights the child holds himself do not arise from this giving, and how his standing is protected belongs to the institutional side.

PC10　The child's basic rights have their standing in himself and are not conferred by the parents; how they are protected and by what rules belongs to the institutional side, and applies whichever direction the parents are headed in. On the occasion when a parent's effect crosses them, the relational side points out "this is a violation" and the boundary crossed; protection and disposition after the violation are handed over here, and what that effect left in the parent-child dealings is still read on the relational side.

PC11　Securing is not exchanged for the child's compliance, nor made conditional on his taking some particular road; how much support is given is not used to make up for a lack of securing; the two layers are not traded.

PC12　The child's dependency on care is not a dependency that was made, and carries no direction of colonization; the kind that was made and that has locked the path to saying no carries the direction of colonization; where one first monopolizes, or first induces dependency, and then withdraws, violation is checked separately at the layer of basic rights. Actual dependency alone, or factual irreplaceability, does not on its own yield the conclusion of violation.

PC13　Before 13DD, the child's crying and resistance are first read as signals to be answered, not as unreasonable fussing, nor brushed aside with "he's still little": first check whether care, safety and the rest of securing are lacking, and also check whether it is a prediction that has fallen through, or a negation coming into being; crying alone does not certify which layer he is at, or his particular reason.

PC14　The content of the securing layer shifts with the child's phase transitions, and is not withdrawn with them; how securing is drawn back once 14DD has come to stand is read in Paper 7. After the parents part, each one's relation with the child remains, and is not settled by the exit between the two; the side of shared child-rearing between the two belongs to Marriage Theory.

Paper 3, Support in the Parent-Child Relation

PC15　The parent-child relation is the setting in which cultivation may happen, not the source of cultivation. Support gives conditions, and the content grows out of the child himself; the completion of cultivation is not in the enactor's hands, and incomplete cultivation is not colonization (these two sentences belong to the value layer, and colonization here takes the value layer's narrow sense).

PC16　Support has three things in it: teaching, nurturing and leading, and the three cannot stand in for one another; with the child's phase transitions the emphasis shifts from one to the next, and the earlier is not on that account withdrawn. "Nurturing" in the tripartite division follows the special usage of the education paper; the tripartite division is not used to cancel the possibility of cultivation in the broad sense at other stages.

PC17　Learning before 13DD, if it is still within the direct-write window, is read by the model of the Learning series: content is written in together with the situation of the time; this model is a structural hypothesis, and is kept within the limits the original paper lists; the reading is borrowed here to read the environment the parents give and its effects. Support in this stretch is a rich and safe environment. Forcibly writing a script the parents designate by means of the window period's high plasticity is checked for the direction of colonization on that one effect; the judgment is not completed merely because the content was chosen by the parents.

PC18　"For your own good" can be cultivation's self-justification and can be colonization's disguise; the difference lies not in the wording but in that one effect. Cultivation at 14DD negates false cannot-nots and does not implant a direction: what can be negated is the necessity an external requirement claims over the child; parents can withdraw requirements they themselves imposed and put forward reasons, but cannot cancel a purpose the child avows merely by saying "that's false"; whether it is his own cannot-not is not certified by the parents.

PC19　The meaning predicates on the parents' side are not copied onto the child; the child does not figure only as the parents' continuation, a link in continuing the line, or a security for old age.

PC20　The support given does not enter the child's compliance or achievement as repayment, nor is it used afterward to possess him: however much has been given does not make the child's later direction pass to the parents, nor give the giving side an authorization to decide his direction. Support can have particular content, a term and conditions, and is checked separately by whether it seals refusal and change of direction, and whether it takes basic securing, or what is already due, in exchange.

Paper 4, Arrangements in the Parent-Child Relation

PC21　The arrangements parents set for a child, and the household's rules, are read here only on their side between person and person, the part the institution does not provide for or that goes beyond its provisions; provisions concerning guardianship, role authority and constructs are acknowledged but not judged. What is read is how they become today's arrangements for the child's tomorrow: the layer of relation reads one and the same bearer's today toward his tomorrow; here it is the parents' today toward the child's tomorrow.

PC22　An arrangement is read by three questions: whether there is an arrangement transferring the authority to interpret as the first-person position matures; whether the refusals, deviations and restructurings the child gradually produces can really alter that arrangement, or are uniformly read as not yet understanding; whether the present configuration is marked as a present arrangement open to revision, or as a final content irrefutable in future.

PC23　The authority to interpret is handed over domain by domain as the child matures in each matter, not all on one day. Using a line that is externally fixed, not defeasible by the facts of a case, and applied uniformly across matters, to determine once that he has arrived in every matter or in none, carries a risk of systematic misclassification; a judgment that can be overturned by the facts, made matter by matter, and kept open to appeal and review is not in this class.

PC24　When 13DD emerges, the child's "no" is where negation first targets the other and the "I" is chiseled out; one "no" does not by itself certify that 13DD has emerged. Reading it uniformly as not yet understanding is exactly what the second question blocks. At the end of the father or the mother, what is read here is: keeping one's own direction, while not maintaining it by pressing down the child's negation; how one answers on one occasion is read by words and deeds, and does not on that basis certify which layer the parent as a whole is at.

PC25　The ex ante structural condition of a cultivative intervention, carried to the child, is checked at the parent's end, without first judging which layer the child has reached: whether this end's arrangements compress his authority, channels and practical possibility of refusing, interrupting, revising, exiting and restating himself. Where there is something he cannot yet do, it is checked separately for pre-occupation: whether his position for later first-person running and revision is sealed in advance, before he can do it. That the child cannot do it for now neither falsely claims that he already has the real capacity, nor on that account hands that position to the parents to pre-occupy. Rules of guardianship, of exercise on his behalf and of particular disposition belong to the institutional side.

PC26　Marking a present arrangement as a final content the child will be unable to refute in future is a signal of pre-occupation; to read it as pre-occupation further requires that the not-yet-running position be sealed shut before the gate runs of itself: the difference between maintaining and fixing is what this whole judgment turns on. An arrangement does not become pre-occupation merely by constraining the child. Colonization here takes the value layer's narrow sense.

Paper 5, Collisions in the Parent-Child Relation

PC27　At the 14DD turn, the child's own cannot-not crystallizes out of the remainder of 13DD and begins to meet the parents' law. Rebellion is not the mechanism of 14DD's genesis, but frequently serves as its behavioral byproduct, and can function as one of its confirmation conditions in boundary encounters.

PC28　In this stretch, the end of the father or the mother heading toward 15DD in one operation is read, in this matter, as: acknowledging that the child's cannot-not is his own, even if, as the parents see it, that direction will fail. Upstream writes this as the condition of cultivation in this stretch; here it is read, by the series' scope, as the direction of one operation, and falling back is read as it occurs.

PC29　Colonization at 14DD takes three forms: direction substitution, superego internalization, and full-support resistance removal; the third appears in the guise of support.

PC30　When the child's end appears as a requirement he bears in the first person and by which he goes on organizing his action, and it requires action incompatible with the parents' law in the same situation: whether that matter enters the Conflict Treatise is checked item by item against its two gates: the first asks after structure, with the object layer and the operation layer kept apart; the second asks whether the handling of this matter would put at risk the parent's own capacity to go on making first-person revisions and to go on being open to questioning, or might put the parent in the position of constituting on the child's behalf; this is not settled by the parent-child identity or by incompatibility itself. The parents do not certify whether his end is already a law of his own, nor cancel it on his behalf for want of certification; if the object later fails to obtain, this matter is exited, and he is not turned on and judged as not qualifying. What has not entered is read as dealings and collision.

PC31　Mutual chiseling is judged by process, not by identity: where parents and child, in some matter, enter one and the same reciprocal process and actually chisel open each other's judgments, there is mutual chiseling in that matter; it is not established by the parent-child identity or by the name of a stage.

PC32　Collision runs two ways: parent-child dealings may also lead the parents to re-examine their own law; whether it is actually rewritten is read by the process on that occasion, and falling back is read as it occurs. Where the child's refusal really altered an arrangement, it is entered that the arrangement was changed, not as either side's winning or losing.

Paper 6, Several Caregivers

PC33　Father and child, mother and child, grandparents and other caregivers and the child, each form a relation of their own; an arrangement set jointly is still read person by person in each relation. "For the child's sake" does not legislate for the two grown-ups.

PC34　An effect one caregiver has on the child by way of another caregiver does not release the instigator from his part.

PC35　A caregiver's reasons, interpretations and adjudications toward the child are not placed beyond examination: there is at least one route of external review not wholly under his control; being questioned is his own being questioned, and gives him no standing to interrogate another caregiver. Another caregiver can be one source of external questioning; where an institution exists that is fit for purpose, available, and not controlled by one party to the dispute, the principal screening may be borne by the institution; where none is present, a caregiver may defer, refer on, set boundaries, but may not upgrade these arrangements into a permanent exemption. When both parents are headed in the 15DD direction, the two can question each other: each places his own reasons, interpretations and adjudications under the other's questions; this gives neither the standing to interrogate the other; reflection after falling back may also be brought out this way. If no other co-caregiver can provide questioning of this kind, the source of external review is looked for separately among people actually available, or in fit procedures; the independence and effect of the route are checked by the particular arrangement.

PC36　Where one end is headed in the 15DD direction and the other treats the child merely as a means: the relation headed in the 15DD direction is read, and the other is only registered; registering the other only does not mean that the conditions it creates for the child stay out of the main text; the actual effect by which it enters this relation is read by pairwise accounting. The other relation's effects are entered in the instigator's relation, and the end headed in the 15DD direction does not avow or settle it on its behalf; what he does to block the other's acts is read by the particular arrangements and effects, and protection has grounds of its own; the side involving the replacement of guardianship belongs to the institutional side. The party that uses the child against the other party, where it falls to "only as," carries the direction of colonization.

PC37　Between a child one party brings in on remarrying and the later party there is another relation, with a different starting point: what each has avowed, and from when, is read by that relation. Grandparents and other caregivers are read the same way.

Paper 7, Handing Over and Leading

PC38　Coming of age is read structurally as the child's 14DD coming to stand: his own cannot-not has taken shape. It arrives domain by domain, not all on one day; coming of age in law is acknowledged but not judged.

PC39　Where, in some matter, his negation-gate can already run of itself, the position of raising and refusing in that matter becomes his own at once, and no one may seal it; this is not further conditioned on his 14DD already having come to stand. Being able to raise something is not the same as having it come to stand: whether the arrangement in that matter changes, if it also involves the parents' end or others, is read by each party's own law and by the dealings, and is not decided by any one party alone. In matters of his own direction, the parents do not interpret or avow in his place; once his cannot-not has come to stand in that matter, leading takes up from there. How the securing layer is drawn back is not conditional on which road he takes; in drawing back, actual dependency, the arrangements already formed, and the two senses of dependency are checked. On the side that has already turned into a relation between adults, what the parents give from then on is read as help between adults, or as something done together.

PC40　On the child's side, the test is that he is able to unfold without revolving around his father, and also able to unfold without revolving around his mother, read person by person; this is a test, not that he must in fact leave; being able is limited to constitutive independence, not real-world self-sufficiency.

PC41　Once 14DD has come to stand, what remains is mainly leading: the effect of leading can only be by personal demonstration; it is not a position with authority, and does not extend the structure of the parent-child relation. This does not widen into all later dealings between parents and adult children being allowed only demonstration; ordinary exchange and things done together are read by Relation Theory and Cooperation Theory. The child's own 15DD is his own affair.

PC42　Leading is read until it becomes one face of family affection between adults; no endpoint is set. From then on, what lies between parents and adult children is read by Relation Theory and Cooperation Theory, and conflict by the Conflict Treatise; the case of long-term care needed after coming of age is handed to a future series on relations of care.

§10 Drawing Together

This paper lays down no propositions, and proves no proposition at the object layer. What it says is this: Parent-Child Theory lies downstream on the relational side, the branch written last; it does not define all parent-child relations, and reads the parent-child relation whose premise is that the end of the father or the mother is headed in the 15DD direction; people are all mixed, falling back is read as it occurs, and having fallen back, one can turn back; it reads relation by relation, with the three kinds applied to a relation; it borrows the two layers of the institutional side to read the securing and support parents give the child, and takes the child's phase transitions as one axis along which to read how the parents' end acts, the order being only an example; it reads until the child's cannot-not has come to stand in one matter after another, until handing over and leading, until this relation becomes one face of family affection between adults. The main line is: from the start he is another end; the parents guard him, give him conditions, and one matter at a time give back to him what they held for him. The directions of colonization read in the papers borrow things that are, or may come to be, between parents and child; the direction is read on the one effect in which they are taken up to do something.

摘要

这一篇是亲子论的 Paper 0，最后写，最先放。它不立命题，只为读这个系列的人做几件事：说明这个系列在 SAE 里的位置，写明它读到哪里、主线是什么，交代各篇共用的写法与同字的词，与几家传统作总对接，把各篇读到的殖民方向合说一次，合列全系列已经立下的四十二条命题，登记留下来不结的余项与仍待论证的问题。

亲子论是 SAE 关系论下游的最后一支，接在爱情论、婚姻论、合作论之后。这个系列不给一切亲子关系下定义；它读的亲子关系，以父或母那一端在 15DD 方向上为前提：在一件件事上，朝把孩子当作目的那一面去，而不只把他当作手段；人都是混合的，回落照读，回落以后能回过头来。它按每一段读，父与孩子一段，母与孩子一段。它借制度侧的两层读父母给孩子的东西：基础层要保障，涌现层要支持；它以孩子的相变作一条轴，读父母那一端怎样作用，次序只是示例；它读到孩子的不得不在一件件事上站住，读到交出与引领为止。主线的平实说法是：他从一开始就是另一个目的；父母护着他，给他条件，一件一件，把替他拿着的还给他。七篇依次读：另一个目的；保障；支持；安排；碰撞；几个照料者；交出与引领。

这一篇与洛克、卢梭、康德、黑格尔、《论语》《孟子》、纪伯伦作总对接；发展心理学不作对接，回指 SAE 已有的发展一侧的几篇。各篇读到的殖民方向，借的是亲子之间已经在或可能形成的件：孩子的地位、保障、依赖、窗口、「为你好」、父母的意义、付出、安排、孩子的「不」、阻力、第三人、引领。这几件本身不带方向，方向在它们被拿来做什么的那一次作用上读。这一篇不证明任何对象层的命题。

引言

后面七篇各读一件。第一篇《另一个目的》读这个系列所说的亲子关系是什么样子：前提，回落与回头，每一段，一段上三种，孩子那一端的两本账，他没有选过进入，DD 在父母与孩子身上的两种用法，对象层与操作层。第二篇《亲子关系的保障》读基础层：两层的形状从哪里借来，父母给的保障从哪里来，孩子的份位与侵，保障不挂条件，依赖的两义，13DD 之前孩子的信号，保障随相变怎样挪移。第三篇《亲子关系的支持》读涌现层：场合不是来源，教、涵育、引领三件，直写窗口，「为你好」，意义不复制，支持不记作偿还。第四篇《亲子关系的安排》读家里的规矩：今天对明天的安排，读一项安排的三问，解释权一域一域移交，那一声「不」，事前的结构条件，预占。第五篇《亲子关系的碰撞》读孩子自己的不得不长出来以后与父母的法相遇：14DD 翻转与叛逆，父母那一端在这一段的样子，14DD 上殖民的三种形态，冲突论的门槛，互凿，碰撞两向。第六篇《几个照料者》读一个孩子身边不止一个照料者：各成一段，经过第三人，照料者受问与互相追问，一方只把孩子当作手段，再婚带来的孩子与祖辈。第七篇《交出与引领》读最后一段：成年，提出与提出得成，「能够不再围绕我而展开」在孩子那一侧，引领，转为亲情。

两层的分工，关系论写明了：抽象的关系论只立所有关系都具备的；只在某一种关系里成立的，交那一种关系的系列论证。2 关系一层已经立下的，这个系列直接用，不重写：关系的起码条件，涵育与殖民两个方向，期待，承诺，来往的沉积，结束，都照关系论读。关系论交给这个系列的，有几件是它先给了的：不需要资格的认与承认的实现分开记账；依赖的两义；今天对明天的安排；不经过冲突而带殖民方向的作用（附录）。

第二节 射程与主线

射程。 有六条。

一，不给一切亲子关系下定义。说一段关系不在这个系列读的亲子关系里，不是说它不是亲子关系；主语是那一段，不是那个人（第一篇 PC1、PC3）。

二，父或母那一端在 15DD 方向上。在 SAE 的层级里，认对方也是目的的那一层，记作 15DD。15DD 是不疑：「不只有我是目的，他者也是目的本身」。8 这个系列读的亲子关系，以父或母那一端在这个方向上为前提：在一件件事上，朝把孩子当作目的那一面去，而不只把他当作手段。方向是前提，不是组分；孩子没有选过进入，这里不另要双方认下（第一篇）。

三，方向，回落，与回头。没有纯在 15DD 上的父母，人都是混合的；方向按一次运作读，回落照读，不推翻方向。回落以后，这一端能回过头来，认下那一次，在之后的安排与来往里有所改；坚持不回头的，方向已不在 15DD 上。反省从言行里读，确认只在本人一端；它不撤销已读出的侵，也不使孩子欠下原谅；「坚持」是持续地不回头（第一篇 PC1）。

四，每一段，与一段上三种。父与孩子一段，母与孩子一段，祖辈与其他照料者各一段；单亲同读。三种用在一段上，不作每一件进出射程的筛子：朝目的那一面去的一段在射程里，段内各件都照读；只把孩子当作手段的，与不只当作手段、也没有朝目的那一面去的，都是总体上停在 14DD 方向、没有朝 15DD 去的那一段（说的是一段关系朝哪里去，不给人定级），不在射程里，只登记；越过基础权利的那一次另是侵（第一篇 PC2、PC3；第六篇）。

五，对象层与操作层。对象层读射程里的那一段；操作层父或母只确认自己这一端，孩子那一端、另一位照料者那一端，只见线索。若后来确认所读这一段的方向前提并未成立，这个系列不把它作为射程内的对象展开，不倒判；段内一次回落不构成退出的根据（第一篇 PC7）。

六，上界与边。这个系列读到孩子的不得不在一件件事上站住，读到交出与引领，读到这段关系成为成年人之间亲情的一面为止；孩子自己的 15DD，是他自己的事，不在这个系列里（第七篇）。这个系列不读的：监护与家作为构的那一面；孩子份位的护与规则；越线以后的处置；夫妻之间那一面；成年以后的长期照料。

主线。 平实的一句是：他从一开始就是另一个目的。父母护着他，给他条件，一件一件，把替他拿着的还给他。

技术式的说法，各篇的正文与摘要照用：父或母在 15DD 方向上，为孩子保障基础层、支持涌现层；从他自己的不得不还没有站住，读到它一域一域站住以后的交出与引领。他作为目的的地位不由父母授予，他能做的随相变长出来，父母给的条件不替他认领方向。前一半是第二、三篇：两层。中间是第四、五篇：安排与碰撞，解释权一域一域移交。后一半是第一篇的两本账与第七篇的交出与引领。

第一篇讲射程那一段的核心，是射程式的一句：至少有一个父亲或母亲，在一件件事上，朝把孩子当作目的那一面去；回落了，能回过头来。这一句是入口的概括，不是射程全部条件的等价替换。

把七篇串起来的，还有两本账的分法：孩子作为目的的地位，从一开始就是满的；他能做的事，随相变一层一层长出来。前一本不等后一本；后一本不降前一本。各篇读父母那一端的每一次作用，都在这两本账底下读。

第三节 写法

层按一次运作记；姿态，与涌现。 DD 在父母与孩子身上两种用法：父母那一端的 15DD 是姿态，一次运作朝哪个方向去；孩子那一端用 DD 读涌现与成熟，不成标签。第一人称位的成熟按内容域核；当次的运作随对象走，另读；某项学习是否还在直写窗口，再另读；三件不互相代证。13DD 的涌现本身是一次到还是分域，这个系列不定（第一篇 PC6）。

次序只是示例；读的是父母那一端。 各篇以孩子的相变作一条轴：13DD 之前，13DD 涌现，13DD 运作与 14DD 萌芽，14DD 翻转，14DD 确立。这一次序只是一种示例，不是发展的定义，也不给孩子分格。这个系列读的是父或母那一端怎样作用；孩子的发展，只作这一端作用时的条件来读，不作发展阶段的研究。所以正文不写具体年龄；上游与研究材料里的年龄，只在注里，作后验的参考。

谈结构，不谈好坏；不发应当。 不以长短、成败、孩子后来成了什么给一段亲子关系打分。正文不写成「父母要做什么」，写成父母那一端怎样作用。

不读心，不预测。 照结构推，或照上游原句；一个人怎样想，不替他说。孩子只写他说了什么、做了什么。不对哪一种父母、哪一个孩子会怎样作预测。从外面看到的照料，不认证父母的内在方向。

只讲人与人之间那一层。 监护、家作为构、法定的抚养义务、孩子份位的护与规则，归制度侧，认而不判，作为作用的条件登记。

「不得不」，不写「义务」。 父或母给出的照料与支持，出自这一端自己的不得不，是这一端对自己的法负责；这个系列没有对他者的义务这个概念。给予的那一面照权益读，锚在付出上（第二篇）。

涵育与殖民取宽义。 说的是一次作用的方向，不是贴给人或一段关系的标签；方向在这一次作用上读，成没成，看孩子此后的理解与行动；结果的尺，不拿来判方向。读到价值层的话（未完成的涵育不是殖民；预占），殖民改用价值层的窄义，就地标明。9 侵另判，只在基础权利一层。

例子。 全系列用何川、林晚与他们的孩子何舟这一家贯穿；旁例有宋岚与女儿宋念，许宁、季平与许冬。这是自设的例子，只用来看结构：例子里，何川与林晚在 15DD 方向上是设定的前提；例子写他们的回落，也写回落以后的回头，两人可以互相追问。孩子出场只写言行；伤害只作结构登记，不写成情节与细节。历史例不用历史上的孩子作当事人；可用成年人写下、已经公开的自己的童年，只读他成年以后怎样写，不改写成当年孩子的内心实录，也不据此认证父母的方向；私信、日记死后才发表的，注里写明。只用已故、史料公开的人物；不用在世的真实人物。

研究材料。 没有合适的例子处，发展心理学的研究可作材料：照已发表的报告，不点名在世的个人，注里注明出处，如实报告样本年龄；研究所见不作论证的一步，结构的读法由这个系列自担。

传统对接。 各篇按问题分接各家，这一篇作总对接。对接只读结构，不评传统。发展心理学不作对接：SAE 发展一侧的几篇已经与它对过话，这里回指（第五节）。

出处与推及。 上游的结论在正文用平实话说；出处、原句与别篇的命题号放在注释。上游原话推到原处之外：制度对人推到父母对孩子，成年人之间推到成年之前，同一个承担者推到父母的今天与孩子的明天，承认性权利的门推到亲子之间：就地写明是推及，由这个系列自担。

第四节 同字的词

保障，支持；护。 照政治经济学分用：「保障」用于基础层的供给，为人的涌现给条件叫「支持」。10 婚姻论说的「支持的否定边界」，是同一个「支持」。制度对孩子基础权利的护，写「护」，不写「保障」。

权益；义务。 「权益」照权利一侧的用法：锚在给予的那一端，锚在付出上；孩子是这份付出的指向端，不持有它。11 「义务」这个系列不用在父母对孩子之间：只有一条朝内的义务，主体对自己的法负责。12 法律上的抚养义务，归制度侧。

15DD、14DD、13DD；站住。 父母那一端说的是姿态；孩子那一端说的是某一件事上已经涌现、已经成熟的那一层。正文写「在这件事上，他的不得不已经站住了」，不写「这是一个 13DD 的孩子」。

成年。 结构上的成年，读作孩子的 14DD 站住，一域一域到；法律上的成年，是制度划的一条线，认而不判（第七篇）。

涵育；教，引领。 不另标时，涵育照关系论的宽义：一次作用朝腾出空间去。引教育那一篇的三分「教、涵育、引领」时，其中的「涵育」照那一篇的专门用法：给空间，让主体自己凿出自意识与目的。学习系列在窗口期说的「涵育」，是那一篇的用法。三分说明所作之事不同，不用来取消别处宽义的涵育。「引领」不是领导，不是一个有权限的位置：大人活出承认，孩子看到方向，只能以身示范。

安排；规矩。 安排，是父母为孩子定下、管着他以后一段时间的东西；规矩，是家里明说出来、一再照着做的那一类安排。两者都读作父母的今天对孩子明天的安排（第四篇）。

解释权；交出；提出与提出得成。 解释权说的是一件事怎样读、算什么，由谁来说。「交出」说的是父母那一端原先替孩子拿着的解释与认领，一域一域移交；追问的发起不在「交出」里，它在一件事上的否定门自行时就归他本人。能提出，不等于提出得成（第四篇、第七篇）。与合作论的「收回」不同：收回是一方收回自己这一侧。

预占。 照价值一层的用法：一个尚未运行的位置，在它开始运行之前就被填满。把一项安排标为孩子将来不可反驳的最终内容，是预占的信号，不是判定（第四篇）。

门：否定门；自行，代行。 照价值一侧与权利一侧的用法：否定门是一个人在他自己的法里，对加于自身的内容说不、对已产出的东西追问并否定的那一环。13 在一件事上这道门能由他自己第一人称地运行，叫自行；还不能、由照料他的人替他行使的那一面，叫代行；自行的范围随事项、随时间渐进地移动。14 门在一件事上自行，那一件事上提出与回绝的位置即刻归他本人（第七篇）。

碰撞：冲突，互凿。 照关系一层的用法：碰撞分两个子类，冲突与互凿。「冲突」只在过了冲突论的两道门以后才用；没有过门的，照来往与碰撞读。互凿按过程判，不按身份判（第五篇）。

监护。 制度侧的词，这个系列不读。

家。 这个系列不把家读作一个构，也不把一家人合成一个承担者来记账；家与它的成员的利益，不读作一个不可分的整体。15 「我们家」定的规矩，落到孩子身上的作用，仍在各人那一段分别读。家能不能另成一个承担者，留作余项（第七节）。

照料者。 单说这个词时，泛指实际照料孩子的人，包括父母；与父母并列说「其他照料者」时，指祖父母、外祖父母、保姆、亲戚等，也包括再婚以后实际承担照料的那一方。各与孩子一段（第六篇）。

准主体。 照价值论与权利一侧的用法：作为目的的平等份位已经在，而否定门的第一人称运行仍在生成中的承担者。用到具体事项时，分域核；他作为目的的地位不等他长成。16

亲情。 只是一个名字：父母与成年子女之间的来往，照关系论与合作论读，这个系列不另给它下定义。

第五节 总对接

洛克：父权。 洛克在《政府论》下篇第六章论父权：孩子不是生来就处在完全平等的状态里，却是为这种平等而生；父母对孩子的那份权，是在孩子还不能运用自己理性的时候照管他，随孩子长成而止。17 这个系列与他在两处相照：两本账，生来与为之而生；收手落在孩子自己能判断的时候。分开处也在两处：洛克从父母之权说起，这个系列不建立父母管束孩子的权限，而读人与人之间的作用；洛克以能够运用理性说明父母管束何时终止，这个系列另按具体事项，读第一人称位置怎样运行、提出与回绝的位置何时归本人（第一、七篇）。

卢梭：消极的教育，只依赖于物。 卢梭在《爱弥儿》里主张最初的教育应当是消极的，又要让孩子只依赖于物、不依赖于人。18 这个系列与他在留出余地上相照（第三篇）；分开处在于，卢梭的导师替孩子设计了整套「物的必然」，这个系列读的正是安排由谁定、能不能被孩子改（第四篇）。

康德：未经同意被带到世上；强制之下的自由；父权式的治理。 康德在《法权论》里说父母未经孩子同意把一个人带到世上，由此负有一份责任，孩子作为人，不是物；在《论教育》里问怎样在强制之下培养自由；在 1793 年的一篇里说，像父亲待孩子那样以善意替臣民定幸福的政府，是可设想的最大专制。19 三处，这个系列分在第一、三、五、七篇读：孩子没有选过进入，他的构成位置不转归父母，这个系列不由此推出对他者的义务；两条法在同一件事上的相遇；在一件已经是成年子女自己的事上仍照孩子待他，这里借的只是形状，不是那一种治理。婚姻论与政治经济学已经对过其中两处，回指。

黑格尔：孩子不是物；管教与私意。 黑格尔在《法哲学原理》论家庭的几节里说，孩子自在地是自由的，不是物；孩子有权由家庭的共同财产抚养与教育；§174 正文说父母对孩子任性的那份权，目的在于管教与教育，§174 的补充（Zusatz）说管教的用意在于打破孩子的私意（Eigenwille）。20 这个系列与他在第一处相照（第一篇）；第二处，这个系列把孩子份位的保护方式与规则交制度侧，关系侧读父母那一端给出的付出（第二篇）；第三处，先核「私意」与「自己的不得不」各自的前件，不直接对译（第五篇）。婚姻论已经引过黑格尔论家庭的几节，回指。

《论语》《孟子》。 《论语》论孝的几章，SAE 的《论语》解读已经注过；这个系列只在两处对照：「父母唯其疾之忧」，落在父母那一端放不下的事（第二篇）；「事父母几谏」，孩子的「不」在那一章里落作委婉地劝（第四篇）。《孟子》的「父子有亲」与五伦，关系论 Paper 0 已经与差序格局一起对过（第六篇）。21 对接只读结构，不评传统。

纪伯伦：论孩子。 纪伯伦在《先知》「论孩子」里说，孩子经父母而来，却不是从父母而来；你可以给他们你的爱，不能给他们你的思想；父母是弓，孩子是射出去的活的箭。22 这个系列与这几句在「他从一开始就是另一个目的」「意义不复制」「能够不再围绕我而展开」三处相照；诗里父母是弓，瞄准的是一位弓手；分开处在于，纪伯伦写的是劝告，又把瞄准交给一位弓手，这个系列只读结构，不设这样一位，并把给条件、替他定方向与以身示范分开读，不把诗的意象直接当作结构判据。

发展心理学：回指。 Terrible Twos 一篇已经与斯皮茨、温尼科特、马勒、皮亚杰、埃里克森与依恋研究对过话；Terrible Teens 一篇与埃里克森、科尔伯格、马西亚对过话。23 这个系列不再对接；用到研究时，只作材料（第三节）。

第六节 各篇读到的殖民方向，合说一次

各篇读出的殖民方向，读法在各篇，这里只合说一层：它们借的，是亲子之间已经在或可能形成的件。这几件本身不带方向；带不带，看那一次作用的前件，不看动作的名称：

  • 孩子的地位：拿「他还小，还不懂」降他作为目的的地位，或把他的拒绝一律读作尚未懂事时（第一篇、第四篇）。
  • 孩子作为条件：孩子只作为父母的延续、继后世的一环、养老的保障，或留住、对付另一方的条件时；一句话照那一句本身读，读的是安排（第一篇、第六篇）。
  • 保障：拿保障换顺从，挂在孩子走某一条路上，拿支持的多少抵保障的缺时（第二篇）。
  • 依赖：被造出来、又锁住了说不之路时；孩子依赖照顾本身不带方向，依赖本身也不是侵（第二篇）。
  • 窗口：借窗口期的高可塑性强行写入父母指定的脚本时；父母选择教什么，不因此就是（第三篇）。
  • 「为你好」：以它替孩子定方向时；分别在那一次作用上（第三篇）。
  • 父母的意义：把父母这一侧的意义复制到孩子身上时（第三篇）。
  • 付出：把孩子的顺从或成就记作偿还，拿付出占有他、替他定方向时；支持可以有内容、期限与条件（第三篇）。
  • 安排：把当前的安排标为孩子将来不可反驳的最终内容时，是预占的信号；读成预占另要那个位置在门自行之前被封死（第四篇）。
  • 阻力：方向替代、超我内化，与以支持的样子出现的全支持式阻力清除（第五篇）。
  • 第三人：经过另一个照料者对孩子起作用，以为不在那一笔里时（第六篇）。
  • 引领：把引领做成一个有权限的位置，在一件已经是成年子女自己的事上仍照孩子待他时（第七篇）。

同一句「为你好」，同一条「睡前不看屏幕」，同一笔交了的学费，在一处是一次支持或一项维护，在另一处才是方向的替下或位置的预占。方向在它们被拿来做什么的那一次作用上读，成没成，看孩子此后怎样理解、怎样做。

这些件本身不决定作用的方向；同一段中的各次作用分别读。人都是混合的（第一篇），回落照那一次读；回落以后能不能回过头来，另照此后的言行读。这个系列读的是方向；它不因此说做父母危险，也不劝人怎样做父母；它只读。

第七节 余项

几件事，这个系列登记，不结。

一，家能不能另成一个承担者。婚姻论已经留作余项；这个系列不判，不把一家人合成一个承担者来记账，只读各人与孩子的那一段。

二，13DD 的涌现本身，是一次到，还是一域一域到。这个系列不定，只引上游的描述（第一篇）。

三，领养。婚姻论读过为领养做准备；领养以后的父或母与孩子之间，照同一个射程、同一套读法读，各篇没有另写。

四，兄弟姐妹之间。这不是亲子关系，照关系论读；它作为孩子那一侧的条件出现时，只登记。

五，政府的儿童保护。政府论读过一个孩子被带离家、交家事庭处理的例子，与政府监护下的权益；那是制度侧，这个系列只登记。24

六，婚姻原则没有覆盖孩子。基础论文《制度如何可能》附录四写过，婚姻里孩子那一面，「留给有了孩子的人自己去凿」；25 这个系列读的，是凿出来的一种样子里人与人之间那一面，不替那一面立规则。

七，成年以后的长期照料，父母老了要子女照料：交将来的照护关系系列。

八，人与 AI、人与动物之间的照料：范围的选择，不作本体论的裁决。

第八节 留下的问题

两件，各篇只论到一半，这里如实列出。

一，13DD 之前，孩子的哭闹抗拒能否读作不可覆写的边界信号。第二篇只说了：先读作要回应的信号；先核保障有没有缺，也核是不是预测落空、是不是正在出现的否定；不凭哭闹本身认证他在哪一层（PC13）。它是哪一种信号，在什么意义上不可覆写，没有论证。26

二，多个照料者之间的横向复核，一般情形下能做到哪一步。第六篇只说了：一个照料者的理由、解释与裁断不置于审查之外，受问是他自己受问；另一个照料者是外部追问的一个来源；适格、可用、且不被争议一方控制的制度存在时，主要的筛选可以由它负担，制度缺席时，延期、转介、设边界不升级为永久免检；父母都在 15DD 方向上时可以互相追问；没有另一位共同照料者时，外部的问另看实际可用的人与程序（PC35）。在一个人自己受问与适格的制度之间，照料者之间的横向复核一般能做到哪一步，没有论证。26

第一篇 另一个目的

PC1　这个系列不给一切亲子关系下定义。它读的亲子关系，以父或母那一端在 15DD 方向上为前提：在一件件事上，朝把孩子当作目的那一面去，而不只把他当作手段。15DD 方向是前提，不是亲子关系的组分；孩子没有选过进入，这里不另要双方认下。没有纯在 15DD 上的父母，人都是混合的；方向按一次运作读，回落照读，不推翻方向；回落以后，这一端能回过头来，认下那一次，在之后的安排与来往里有所改；坚持不回头的，方向已不在 15DD 上。

PC2　按每一段读：父与孩子一段，母与孩子一段；单亲同读。在 15DD 方向上的那一段在射程里；两段之间的作用，照两两记账读，另一段进入这一段的实际作用，记在始作俑者那一段。

PC3　三种用在一段上：一段朝把孩子当作目的那一面去的，在射程里，段内各件都照读，回落的那一件照它自己的方向读，不出射程；一段只把孩子当作手段的，不在射程里，照「只作为」的读法读出殖民方向；一段不只把他当作手段、也没有朝目的那一面去的，也不在射程里，只登记；这两种，都是总体上停在 14DD 方向、没有朝 15DD 去的那一段。不论哪一种，越过基础权利的那一次，另在基础权利一层是侵。射程不等于亲子关系的全部定义：不在这个系列展开，不因此否认它是一段亲子关系。

PC4　孩子作为目的的地位，从一开始就是满的，不由父母授予，也不由父母撤销；他能做的事随相变一层一层长出来。两本账分开记：「他还小，还不懂」，至多描述他在某一件事上还做不到什么，不降他作为目的的地位，也不拿来把他的拒绝一律读作尚未懂事；为了认孩子，也不取消父母自己的主体位置。

PC5　孩子没有选过进入这段关系，也没有选过最初进入哪些条件；他自己的构成位置，不因此转归父母。他在现实中脱离不了照料他的人，这不使照料者取得他价值的构成权。

PC6　DD 在父母与孩子身上两种用法：父母那一端的 15DD 是姿态，一次运作朝哪个方向去；孩子那一端用 DD 读涌现与成熟，不成标签。第一人称位在这一域是否已能自行，按域核；已经涌现的层在当次面对这个对象时怎样运作，另读。不拿当次层位代判成熟，也不由分域成熟断定 13DD 的涌现本身分域。

PC7　对象层读父或母那一端在 15DD 方向上的那一段；操作层父或母只确认自己这一端。孩子在某一件事上到了哪一层，从父母这一端只见线索。若后来确认所读这一段的方向前提并未成立，这个系列不把它作为射程内的对象展开，不倒判对方；段内一次回落不构成这一退出的根据，已经读出的各次作用照旧记账。

第二篇 亲子关系的保障

PC8　借制度侧已写定的两层的形状读父或母与孩子之间：基础层要保障，涌现层要支持。两层分开写，同等重要，一层不拿去换另一层；保护孩子的基础权利在反殖民一边，不是涵育的一部分；照料与供给的施为本身不改名为涵育，涵育要两层加起来，保障之厚不证明涵育。两层是分面，同一笔给付可以两读。在人这一侧，涵育另有姿态一读，出自父或母自己的法。原处说的是制度对人，推到父或母对孩子。

PC9　在这个系列读的亲子关系里，父或母给出的照料与支持，出自这一端自己的不得不：孩子要人照顾，这是真的；它进入父或母的发生条件，在他自己的法里被认下。这是这一端对自己的法负责，不是对孩子欠下的一项义务。其中的给予面照权益读，锚在父或母实际给出的付出上，孩子是这份付出的指向端，不持有它；保障与支持里的给予面都可以照权益读，不只在基础层。孩子自持的基础权利不由这份付出产生，份位怎样护归制度侧。

PC10　孩子的基础权利，份位在他本人，不由父母授予；它怎样护、规则怎样定，归制度侧，不论父母朝哪个方向都适用。父或母的作用越过它的那一次，关系侧指出「这是侵」及所越的边界；侵后的保护与处置在此交接，那次作用在亲子来往里留下了什么，仍照关系侧读。

PC11　保障不以孩子的顺从为交换，不以他走某一条特定的路为条件；支持的多少，不拿来抵保障的缺；两层不互换。

PC12　孩子依赖照顾，这一种依赖不是被造出来的，不带殖民这个方向；被造出来、又锁住了说不之路的那一种，带殖民这个方向；先独占或先致其依赖、再撤出的，在基础权利一层另核侵。仅有实际依赖，或事实上不可替代，不独自取得侵这一结论。

PC13　13DD 之前，孩子的哭闹抗拒，先读作要回应的信号，不读作无理取闹，也不凭「他还小」抹去：先核照料、安全等保障有没有缺，也核是不是预测落空、是不是正在出现的否定；不凭哭闹本身认证他在哪一层或具体的理由。

PC14　保障一层的内容随孩子的相变挪移，不随相变撤掉；14DD 站住以后保障怎样收，在第七篇读。父母分开以后，各人与孩子的那一段照旧在，不因两人之间的退出结清；两人之间共同养育的那一面归婚姻论。

第三篇 亲子关系的支持

PC15　亲子关系是涵育可能发生的场合，不是涵育的来源。支持给的是条件，内容由孩子自己长出来；涵育的完成不在施为者手里，未完成的涵育不是殖民（这两句是价值层的话，这里的殖民取价值层的窄义）。

PC16　支持那一层有三件：教、涵育、引领，三者不能互相替代；随孩子的相变，重点依次挪移，前一件不因此撤掉。三分里的「涵育」照教育那一篇的专门用法；三分不用来取消其他阶段宽义涵育的可能。

PC17　13DD 之前的相关学习，若仍在直写窗口，照学习系列的模型读：内容与当时的情境一同写入；这一模型是结构假说，照原篇所列的限度保留；这里借这一读法读父母给出的环境及其作用。支持在这一段是丰富且安全的环境。借窗口期的高可塑性强行写入父母指定的脚本，照那一次作用核殖民方向；不只凭内容由父母选择，就完成这一判断。

PC18　「为你好」可以是涵育的自我辩护，也可以是殖民的伪装；分别不在说法上，在那一次作用上。14DD 上的涵育，是否定虚假的「不得不」，不是植入方向：可否定的是外部要求对孩子声称的必然性；父母可以撤回自己施加的要求、提出理由，不能只凭自己说「这是假的」，取消孩子所认的目的；是不是他自己的不得不，不由父母认证。

PC19　父母这一侧的意义谓词，不复制到孩子身上；孩子不只作为父母的延续、继后世的一环或养老的保障。

PC20　给出的支持，不把孩子的顺从或成就记作偿还，也不在此后拿来占有他：付出过多少，不使孩子此后的方向转归父母，也不使给的一方取得替他定方向的授权。支持可以有具体的内容、期限与条件，另照它封不封拒绝与改向、拿不拿基础保障或已经应得的东西作交换来核。

第四篇 亲子关系的安排

PC21　父母为孩子定的安排、家里的规矩，这里只读其中人与人之间、制度没有规定或超出规定的那一面；涉及监护、角色权限与构的规定，认而不判。读的是它们怎样成为今天对孩子明天的安排：关系一层读的是同一个承担者的今天对明天，这里是父母的今天对孩子的明天。

PC22　读一项安排，照三问：有没有随第一人称位成熟而移交解释权的安排；孩子逐步出现的拒绝、偏离与重构，能不能真实地改变那个安排，还是一律被读作尚未懂事；当前的配置被标为一个可修订的当下安排，还是被标为未来不可反驳的最终内容。

PC23　解释权随孩子在各件事上的成熟，一域一域移交，不是某一天一起交出。用一道外部固定、不可由个案事实反驳、跨事项统一使用的线，一次判定他在所有事上都已到了或都未到，有系统性错分的风险；可被事实推翻、按事项分别判、留着申诉与复核的判断，不在此列。

PC24　13DD 涌现时，孩子那一声「不」，是否定首次指向他人、「我」被凿出来的地方；一次「不」不独自认证 13DD 涌现。把它一律读成尚未懂事，正是第二问所挡的。父或母这一端，这里读的是：保有自己的方向，同时不靠压掉孩子的否定来维持这一方向；一次怎样回应，照言行读，不据此认证父母整个人在哪一层。

PC25　涵育性介入的事前结构条件推到孩子，照父或母这一端核，不先判孩子到了哪一层：这一端的安排有没有压缩他拒绝、中断、修订、退出与重新表述的权限、通道及现实可行性。他还做不到的那一处，另照预占核：他此后第一人称运行与修订的位置，是不是在他能做之前就被预先封住。孩子暂时做不到，不冒称他已有现实能力，也不因此把那个位置交给父母预占。监护、代行与具体处置的规则，归制度侧。

PC26　把当前的安排标为孩子将来不可反驳的最终内容，是预占的信号；读成预占，另要那个尚未运行的位置在门自行之前就被封死：维护与定格之别，是这一判的全部所系。安排本身，不因约束了孩子而成为预占。这里的殖民取价值层的窄义。

第五篇 亲子关系的碰撞

PC27　14DD 翻转时，孩子自己的不得不从 13DD 的余项中结晶出来，开始与父母的法相遇。叛逆不是 14DD 的发生机制，常是它的行为副产品，并可在边界碰撞中充当确认条件之一。

PC28　这一段，父或母那一端在一次运作里朝 15DD 去，在这件事上读作：承认孩子的不得不是他自己的，即使在父母看来那个方向会失败。上游把它写作这一段涵育的条件；这里照射程读作一次运作的方向，回落照读。

PC29　14DD 上的殖民有三种形态：方向替代、超我内化、全支持式阻力清除；第三种以支持的样子出现。

PC30　孩子那一端的要求，以他第一人称承担并持续组织其行动的一项要求出现，与父母的法在同一情境中要求不相容的行动：那一件事是否进入冲突论，照它的两道门逐项核：第一道问结构，对象层与操作层分开；第二道问这件事的处理，会不会把父或母自己继续修订、继续受问的位置置于风险中，或可能使父或母占据替孩子构成的位置；不由亲子身份或不相容本身完成认定。父母不认证他那一端是否已经是他自己的法，也不以未能认证为由替他撤销；对象后来没有成立，退出这一件，不倒判他不够格。没有进入的，照来往与碰撞读。

PC31　互凿按过程判，不按身份判：父母与孩子在某一件事上进入同一个互向的过程，并实际互相凿开对方的判断，那一件事上就有互凿；不凭亲子身份或阶段名称认定。

PC32　碰撞是两向的：亲子来往也可能使父母重新审视自己的法；是否实际改写，照那一次过程读，回落照读。孩子的拒绝真实地改变了安排，就记安排被改了，不记作哪一方的输赢。

第六篇 几个照料者

PC33　父与孩子、母与孩子、祖辈与其他照料者与孩子，各成一段；共同定下的安排，在各段里仍逐人读。「为了孩子」不替两个大人立法。

PC34　一个照料者经过另一个照料者对孩子起的作用，不免除始作俑者那一段。

PC35　一个照料者对孩子的理由、解释与裁断，不置于审查之外：至少有一条不完全由他控制的外部复核路径；受问是他自己受问，不给他盘问另一个照料者的资格。另一个照料者可以是外部追问的一个来源；适格、可用、且不被争议一方控制的制度存在时，主要的筛选可以由制度负担；制度缺席时，照料者可以延期、转介、设边界，不能把这些安排升级为永久免检。父母都在 15DD 方向上时，两人可以互相追问：各自把自己的理由、解释与裁断放到对方的问下；这不给哪一方盘问对方的资格；回落以后的反省，也可能由此问出。若没有另一位共同照料者能提供这样的追问，外部复核的来源另看实际可用的人或适格程序；路径的独立性与作用，照具体安排核。

PC36　一方在 15DD 方向上、另一方把孩子只当作手段：在 15DD 方向上的那一段照读，另一段只登记；只登记另一段，不等于它对孩子造成的条件不进入正文，它进入这一段的实际作用，照两两记账读。另一段的作用记在始作俑者那一段，在 15DD 方向上的那一方不替它认领或结清；他挡下另一方的那些作为，照具体的安排与作用读，保护人另有自己的根据；涉及撤换监护的那一面，归制度侧。拿孩子作对付另一方的一方，落到「只作为」时，带殖民这个方向。

PC37　再婚时一方带进来的孩子，与后来的那一方之间是另一段，起点不同：各人认下了什么、从什么时候认下，照那一段读。祖辈与其他照料者同读。

第七篇 交出与引领

PC38　成年，结构上读作孩子的 14DD 站住：他自己的不得不成型了。它一域一域到，不是某一天一起到；法律上的成年，认而不判。

PC39　在某一件事上他的否定门已能自行，那一件事上提出与回绝的位置即刻归他本人，谁也封不得；这不另以他的 14DD 已经站住为条件。能提出，不等于提出得成：那件事上的安排改不改，若也牵着父母那一端或别人，照各方各自的法与来往读，不由哪一方单方说了算。涉及他自己的方向，父母不代替他解释与认领；他的不得不在那件事上站住了，引领从那里接上。保障一层怎样收，不挂在他走哪一条路上；收的时候，照实际依赖、已经形成的安排与依赖两义核。已经转成成年人关系的那一面，此后父母给的，照成年人之间的帮助或一起做的事读。

PC40　在孩子那一侧，检验是他能够不再围绕父亲而展开，也能够不再围绕母亲而展开，逐人读；这是检验，不是他事实上必须离开；能够，限为构成独立，不是现实自足。

PC41　14DD 站住以后，剩下的主要是引领：引领这一作用只能以身示范，不是一个有权限的位置，不把亲子的结构延长下去。这不扩大成父母与成年子女此后的全部来往都只准示范；普通的交流、一起做的事，照关系论、合作论读。孩子自己的 15DD 是他自己的事。

PC42　引领读到它成为成年人之间亲情的一面为止，不设终点。此后父母与成年子女之间，照关系论与合作论读，冲突照冲突论读；成年以后需要长期照料的情形，交将来的照护关系系列。

第十节 收束

这一篇不立命题，不证明任何对象层的命题。它说的是：亲子论在关系侧的下游，是最后写的一支；它不给一切亲子关系下定义，读的是以父或母那一端在 15DD 方向上为前提的亲子关系，人都是混合的，回落照读，回落以后能回过头来；它按每一段读，三种用在一段上；它借制度侧的两层读父母给孩子的保障与支持，以孩子的相变作一条轴读父母那一端怎样作用，次序只是示例；它读到孩子的不得不在一件件事上站住，读到交出与引领，读到这段关系成为成年人之间亲情的一面为止。主线是：他从一开始就是另一个目的；父母护着他，给他条件，一件一件，把替他拿着的还给他。各篇读到的殖民方向，借的是亲子之间已经在或可能形成的件；方向在被拿来做什么的那一次作用上读。
