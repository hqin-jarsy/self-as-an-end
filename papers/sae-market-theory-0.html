Theory of the Market and the Tradition: The Line and the Space
市场论与传统：线与空间

Han Qin (秦汉) · Independent Researcher · 2026
DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.24183388 · Full PDF on Zenodo · CC BY 4.0
Abstract

The Prequel and the seven papers of this series are written. They ask how a market in an institution whose consensus includes "a human being is an end" can, in a mixed reality, do two things at once: no person's Base Layer is violated, and the Emergence Layer has space and conditions within reach. The answer is gathered into one main-line sentence: the Base Layer is a line; the Emergence Layer is to have space and conditions: guarding the line does not seal off the space, and leaving it open does not eat into the line; where the two conflict, the line comes first. The subtitle is taken from this sentence. This paper lays down no propositions, and changes no sentence of the eight papers. It does four things. First, it stands outside the eight papers and gives the machine's account of itself: what it asks and what it does not, how its sentence form and three layers are arranged, and how it divides labor with upstream. Next, it interfaces with existing theories of the market, at two depths. Satz is examined to a whole chapter and Mazzucato to the full text, and the four steps are taken in full; Eucken rests on a few pages of an abridged English translation, Laffont and Tirole on a working paper, and Young and Rodrik are examined only to the abstract, and the four steps are taken on the sentences examined. Rawls and Lange reach only a table of contents, a book review or an encyclopedia entry, and go as far as the common question and the surface of contact. Stigler, already interfaced with by an earlier series, receives only one added question. The real-world interfaces are written from judgments, regulatory findings, statutory texts and arbitral documents, and serve only as interfaces, bearing no weight: the Google Shopping case and the Korean Naver Shopping case; France's social-housing quota; the North Carolina Dental Board case; an order of the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission against S&P; the Trados case; U.S. Customs' finding on Top Glove; the Azurix v. Argentina arbitration; Ireland's Electricity Regulation Act. Two questions are examined in interfacing: how an analysis of market arrangements, overall orders or structural outcomes lands on constructs open to questioning and their acts; and, in the choice of rules and measures, which requirements serve as constraints and which costs must be weighed. Which conversation falls on which question is written conversation by conversation; how this series answers them, the eight papers have already stated; the overlaps with each school, the questions added and the divergences are confirmed in each conversation separately, and where only the way of asking differs, this is written as such. Last comes the settling of accounts: how the items handed on by upstream land, what is adjacent and not taken up, what is handed downstream, and the six slots of remainders.

Writing Declaration: This paper was co-drafted with Claude (Anthropic). All intellectual decisions, framework design, and final editorial judgments were made by the author.

Introduction

This paper was written only after the eight papers were finished. A conversation is to be had once one's own object stands: only someone who knows what he himself is saying can tell where what others say differs from it.

The examples of the eight papers are all constructed examples: the Kingdom State, the Highland State and their towns, companies and bodies serve only as facts, and this series does not claim that any one of them is the best design. Real-world cases this series has from the start reserved for this paper: each conversation with a school is paired with a real-world interface. The facts of an interface are written from the original documents, with year, source and the tier to which they were examined; only the practice in question is judged, and no company or government is characterized; an interface only helps the reader see where in reality a conversation lands, and does not bear the weight of the eight papers' arguments. The constructed examples of the eight papers stand as they are, and are not replaced by these cases.

When this paper cites this series, it gives the paper and the proposition number; when it cites the schools, it says only what each holds and where, and gives the smallest source that can be examined. The schools are interlocutors, not premises of this series; this paper does not write of this series correcting anyone, nor of anyone anticipating this series. Its structure follows Theory of the Company Paper 0: the machine's account of itself, the method of interfacing, interfacing conversation by conversation, handover and remainders.

Outside texts are cited in four tiers, following Theory of the Company Paper 0:

  • Tier A: the sentence and its location have been confirmed in a reliable original; it is quoted as it stands, with the original and the location given;
  • Tier B: the quoted sentence has been examined in the original relied on, but the edition, the exact location or the full text has not yet been confirmed; it is quoted, with what it rests on and what has not been examined noted;
  • Tier C: it rests only on reliable secondary literature; it is only paraphrased, with what it rests on stated, and without quotation marks;
  • Cited at second hand: it rests only on what others have quoted; it follows the text relied on, is marked as cited at second hand, goes as far as the third step, and no objection is drafted;
  • What could not be confirmed is not cited.

Works in English are quoted in the original, and what was examined is the original as quoted. Real-world interfaces are marked by the same four tiers.

§1 What Kind of Machine This Is

I · What It Asks

How a market in an institution whose consensus includes "a human being is an end" can, in a mixed reality, do two things at once: no person's Base Layer is violated, and the Emergence Layer has space and conditions within reach. Where the two conflict, the Base Layer comes first, and the line is not on the scales; regulation is a necessity, and regulators themselves can also be questioned; the two layers holding each other up is a direction, not a guarantee.

It does not ask whether the free market is good, and does not take sides between market and government; "free market," "planning," "publicly run" and "privately run" are all names, and what is judged is structure, place by place (the Prequel, §2). It is not a theory of price, nor is it hands-off, nor is it planning (the Prequel, §3). It does not ask which form of ownership is best; ownership lies at the rule layer, and this series only suggests (Paper 1, SC2). Tax is not within this series.

II · Sentence Form and the Three Layers

The construct takes the ought, the position takes accountability, the person takes his own law (Institution Theory Z4). The market is not a construct: "the market is not even an address of attribution such as a mill is" (the Political Economy Prequel, §3). The ought is addressed to the four kinds of constructs that receive the ought in a market: the constructs that make and enforce rules; the constructs that organize and operate market infrastructure; suppliers and operators in the market; the bodies and institutions responsible for funding, subsidy and rescue (the Prequel, §6). Individual participants follow their own law, and this series writes no ought for them.

The three layers are the method: the normative layer gives the direction and the line; the rule layer only suggests, may give examples, and does not rank; the judgment layer states who decides and who bears the consequences, listing only the questions to be answered and the positions that answer them. There is always a remainder, and there are exceptions; a remainder yields no permission (the Prequel, §7). Judge structures, not names; judge operations, not persons.

III · The Main Line, and Where Each of the Eight Papers Lands

The main-line sentence: the Base Layer is a line; the Emergence Layer is to have space and conditions: guarding the line does not seal off the space, and leaving it open does not eat into the line; where the two conflict, the line comes first.

  • The Prequel: the market is not a construct; the market oriented toward the Kingdom of Ends; the two things; to whom the ought is addressed.
  • Securing: the Base Layer is a line, not on the market's scales (SC1); the closer to the life-substrate, the stronger the checks and supervision, and the more the question of how large the private share is must be something that can be asked about and answered for (SC2); the line does not change with ownership (SC3).
  • Space: above the line, things are left open as usual, leaving open is not leaving unquestioned, and conditions must be within reach (SC4); the two kinds of "excess" are not symmetrical, the way of examining and the order differ, and the two layers matter equally (SC5).
  • Market Chokepoints: for infrastructure that cannot be got around, the scope of the rules it makes is drawn by the institution, and those rules can be questioned (SC6); questioning through rating, record-keeping and audit is not controlled on its own by the party questioned (SC7).
  • Competition: competition is left open as usual and questioned as usual, and sealing off roads is a kind of effect to be examined with particular care (SC8); no position monopolizes the power to shut the door, and breakup is the last resort (SC9).
  • Funding: funding may carry requirements, but does not thereby acquire the authority to decide a person's direction (SC10); public funding is to be able to state its grounds, and is not an endorsement (SC11).
  • Regulation: regulation is a necessity, and regulators too are questioned (SC12); the position that reviews a publicly run supply is functionally independent of the supplier and of its owner (SC13).
  • Borders: the line does not change at borders, and responsibility goes with each party's own acts (SC14); where there is no one above, the channel of questioning does not belong to the party questioned either (SC15).

IV · The Division of Labor with Upstream

What Institution Theory, the Theory of Government, the Theory of the State, Political Economy and its Specialist Paper, and the Theory of the Company have already proved, this series does not prove again, and only lands on the structures of the market: the construct's acts cross no line and are not discounted under any condition (Institution Theory Z9); the channel is independent of the party questioned, and independence is functional (Institution Theory Z34); the Kingdom of Ends is a direction, not a state to be signed off (Institution Theory Z39); that basic rights may not be violated holds against everyone (Theory of Government G17); capture is judged by structure (Theory of Government Paper 6, G38); between states there is no third party that legislates for both (Theory of the State N24); the chokepoint (Political Economy Paper 2, E7); the level of a price does not, by itself alone, complete a normative judgment (E10); the object of securing is the supply, not that construct (Paper 3, E14a); the two gates and exchange outside the gates (Paper 4, E21–E24); only the inevitability of emergence is taken up, not the inevitability of dominance (Paper 6, E32); institutions give conditions, and conditions must be within reach (E34); the two layers matter equally (E35); support does not acquire the authority to decide a person's direction (E37); the rules made by a position that cannot be got around (A Way Out, a Way Forward, E41a); those who review do not depend on those they review (A Way Out, a Way Forward, E43); investors changing the direction (Theory of the Company GS3); the purchasing company and the supply chain (Theory of the Company GS10).

§2 The Method of Interfacing

I · The Four Steps

Each conversation takes four steps: first, a common question that can be examined is sought; next, whether the two speak of the same thing; next, how far agreement goes, and where the two part; last, the strongest objection from the other side is written, with this series' reply. The objection is drafted by this paper, marked as drafted, and as far as possible made to touch the other side's strength; pressure on this series already present in the other side's original text is transcribed from the original, marked as transcribed, and not drafted.

II · Two Depths

Where the original has been examined, the conversation is carried as far as the examination reaches. Where a whole chapter or the full text has been examined with page numbers checked, all four steps are taken: Satz, Mazzucato. Where only a few pages of a translation, a working paper or an abstract have been examined, the four steps are taken on the sentences examined, and it is stated that beyond them this paper does not answer for the other side: Eucken (from an abridged English translation), Laffont and Tirole (from a working paper; the journal version not examined), Young and Rodrik (from abstracts).

Where only a table of contents, a book review or an encyclopedia entry is reached, the conversation goes as far as the common question and the surface of contact: Rawls, Lange. In these two units, this paper writes what its paraphrase rests on, where the surface of contact lies, and which question is to be decided once the original book has been examined; it does not write a settled conclusion of "meeting" or "parting."

What earlier series have already interfaced with is only pointed to, not redone: the twenty thinkers Political Economy Paper 0 interfaced with (Smith, Kant, Marx, Hayek, Friedman, Khan, Hirschman, Hegel, Polanyi, Thompson, Berle and Means, Coase, Galbraith, Anderson, Nozick, Walzer, Sandel, Frankfurt, Piketty, Robeyns); those the Specialist Paper interfaced with, among whom those close to this paper are Lina Khan (the Khan of Political Economy Paper 0), Rahman, Narechania and Sitaraman, Stigler and Pettit; and the thinkers of Theory of the Company Paper 0. Where one of them is used again, this paper first registers how far upstream has gone, and which question this paper adds; this paper adds a question only for Stigler.

III · Real-World Interfaces

Each conversation is paired with a real-world interface (the Eucken conversation with a pair, and the two thinkers of the ownership conversation sharing one), under four rules:

  • the facts go back to the original documents: judgments, regulatory findings, statutory texts, arbitral documents; year, source and tier are stated;
  • only what the original documents wrote is written; what they did not write is written as not written;
  • what is judged is the practice in question, and no company or government is characterized;
  • they serve only as interfaces, bearing no weight: the argument does not rest on the reputation of that company or body, and if it later changes, the argument does not move.

IV · The Two Questions Examined in Interfacing

Two questions are examined in interfacing; which conversation falls on which question is written conversation by conversation.

The first question: how an analysis of market arrangements, overall orders or structural outcomes lands on constructs open to questioning and their acts. Taking an order, a market, or whether several institutional conditions are compatible as the object of analysis is not the same as treating the market as a subject that receives an ought; this question asks only on whose acts, and which of them, that analysis lands.

The second question: in the choice of rules and measures, which requirements serve as constraints, and which costs must be weighed.

How this series answers these two questions, the eight papers have already stated: the ought addressed to constructs lands on the constructs that make and enforce rules, operate infrastructure, supply and fund (the Prequel, §6); the Base Layer side is examined by the line, with no margin (Paper 2, SC5; the line in Paper 1, SC1); a securing measure that restricts others' Emergence Layer is examined by its effect, stating its cost, alternatives and reasons; where both fall on one and the same person, neither direction is permitted: his Emergence Layer is not traded for his securing, nor is the absence of securing in his Base Layer traded for more choice for him (Paper 2, SC5).

The overlaps with each school, the questions added and the divergences are confirmed in the conversations below one by one, each stating which passage of the original supports it. Where only the way of asking differs, this is written as a difference in the way of asking, not as a divergence.

§3 Order and Boundaries

I · Eucken (B, from an abridged English translation)

The original. An abridged English translation of Eucken's Die Wettbewerbsordnung und ihre Verwirklichung (ORDO, vol. 2, 1949), in Competition Policy International, vol. 2, no. 2 (2006), pp. 219–245, translated by Ahlborn and Grave. On p. 227, having said in turn that firms and workers each have their freedoms, he goes on: "Freedom of the consumer exists, but not the freedom to choose how to define the rules of the game or the forms which the economic process takes." On p. 232 he writes of "making the establishment of a functioning price system of complete competition the essential criterion of every economic measure." On p. 241: "The monopoly supervision should thus be transferred to a state monopoly office." This is "to withdraw it from the persistently dangerous influences exerted by interested parties"; it should be "an independent office which is only subject to the law," not set up as a division of the ministry of economics; "The monopoly office is just as indispensable as the Supreme Court." (From the abridged English translation, page numbers examined; the German original has not been examined; this paper reads only these pages, and beyond them does not answer for Eucken.)

The common question. Who sets and who adjudicates the basic rules of the market, and how the position that guards these rules keeps from being held by a party in the market.

Where they meet. Market participants cannot monopolize decision and adjudication on the basic rules: this series says that the scope of the rules made by a position that cannot be got around is drawn by the institution, and that those rules can be questioned (Paper 3, SC6, per A Way Out, a Way Forward, E41a). The supervising position must be withdrawn from the influence of interested parties: this series says that the constructs that make and enforce rules are functionally independent of the party they regulate, and do not depend in substance on the party reviewed for their income, term or prospects (Paper 6, SC12, per A Way Out, a Way Forward, E43). What Eucken asks here is precisely the concrete structure of power, not only form.

Where they part. In two places, both supported by the pages cited above. The first is in the criterion: Eucken takes the function of the competitive price system as the criterion of economic policy (p. 232); this series does not take approaching some market form as the ground for a judgment, and examines how an act guards the line and leaves space, and how that act is questioned (the Prequel, §2; Paper 2, SC5). The second is in how independence is written: Eucken gives an arrangement, subject only to the law and not within a ministry (p. 241); this series' independence is functional and does not look at where a body is set up: "Independence is functional and does not require being outside the institution" (Institution Theory Z34), and the arrangement is handed to the rule layer (Paper 6, SC13). His arrangement can be one form at the rule layer in this series; the difference here is in how it is written, not in direction (own undertaking).

The strongest objection from the other side (drafted by this paper, from the side of order). Without the criterion of a functioning competitive price system, each paper can only patch place by place: chokepoints, collusion and mergers are asked about separately, and the whole cannot be asked about. A market's being held by a few positions may not be any one place crossing the line, but the whole form having changed.

Reply. This series does not say that form does not matter. The closer to a chokepoint and the larger the power-face, the stronger the checks (Paper 3, SC6); buying a substitute that has not yet grown, giving one's own offerings preferential placement and sealing off access are a kind of effect to be examined with particular care (Paper 4, SC8). All these look at changes of form, only landing them on positions that can each be questioned: if the form has changed, on whose acts has it changed, and who can question them? Breakup is the last resort, stating its effect, cost and alternatives when used (Paper 4, SC9), and does not take the ideal of some form as its ground (own undertaking). This reply has its limit: it can say how each place is asked about, but cannot say at what step the form of the whole has already changed; that must be recognized at the judgment layer, place by place, by structure.

Real-world interface (B). On September 10, 2024, the Court of Justice of the European Union upheld the €2.4 billion fine the European Commission had imposed on Google. The press release writes that "it cannot be considered that, as a general rule, a dominant undertaking" which favors its own products or services departs from competition on the merits; that Google's favoring of its own comparison shopping service on its general results pages amounted to discriminatory treatment departing from competition on the merits was found "in the light of the characteristics of the market and the specific circumstances of the case" (from the Court's Press Release No 135/24; the press release states that it does not bind the Court, and the original pages of the judgment itself were not obtained for this paper).

Real-world interface (A). The Supreme Court of Korea, judgment of October 16, 2025 (2023Du32709): the court below, the Seoul High Court, had upheld the Korea Fair Trade Commission's disposition, finding that a platform's adjustment of its search algorithm to favor the goods of merchants listed on its own open market constituted three violations: discriminatory treatment as an abuse of market dominance, unfair discriminatory trading, and deceptive inducement of customers; the Supreme Court held that the court below had misunderstood the law in its findings on unfairness and effect and on deception, set aside its judgment, and remanded the case to the Seoul High Court (from the operative part of the original judgment). The Court's English summary answers one issue in the affirmative: the method for judging unfairness and intent applies equally to platforms that favor their own goods. Both the judgment and the summary refer to the parties by pseudonym; that one party is Naver rests on the case note of Kim & Chang, the law firm that represented it on appeal. The result after remand this paper has not examined, and it cannot be written that the court has found the conduct concerned lawful.

Reading. Neither place decides the case on the bare label "favoring its own side": one press release states expressly that it cannot be considered so as a general rule, and finds by the specific circumstances; the other remands because the lower court's findings on unfairness and effect were in error. This points the same way as Paper 4: giving one's own offerings preferential placement is a kind of effect to be examined with particular care, and is not judged to cross the line by its label (Paper 4, §2; SC8). In this conversation, this pair of interfaces says: when rules are made by the platform itself, there is a position outside the platform to question them, and what it asks is what effect that practice had in that market. The two places judge those two practices, and characterize neither company; nor does this paper infer from them anything about all self-preferencing.

II · Satz (A)

The original. Satz, Why Some Things Should Not Be for Sale: The Moral Limits of Markets, chapter 4, "Noxious Markets" (Oxford University Press, 2010, pp. 91–113). The PDF examined has at the head of the chapter a passage marked as an abstract (ABSTRACT), which asks several questions in a row, one of them: "Where and for what reasons is it appropriate to regulate a market, and when should we seek to block it?" (p. 91; whether the printed book has this passage, this paper has not examined). She gives four parameters, and herself divides them into two groups: "The first two parameters characterize the consequences of particular markets" (p. 94), namely extremely harmful outcomes for individuals and extreme harm to society; the latter two characterize their source, the situation of the market agents (p. 96), namely very weak or very asymmetric knowledge and agency, and the extreme vulnerability of one party to the exchange. In the same chapter she also writes that there is reason to "block certain market exchanges altogether if citizens are to be equals": for education, health care, opportunities, rights, liberties and personal security, "access to these goods should not depend only on individual preferences or income" (p. 102); she sets out "two different ideals of democratic citizenship," and goes on: "Both conceptions would block markets in votes, judicial offices, legislative offices, and voluntary slavery." (p. 103); "A commitment to equal citizenship, however, does presuppose that there are some rights that individuals cannot contract away." (p. 105); "Some markets trade in things that no democratic society can countenance" (p. 109). On the side of response, she says: "we cannot immediately conclude from the fact that a market is noxious that we ought legally to ban it" (p. 110); a response may be "legislating a safety net," or "instead of changing the market we might try to change the context" (p. 111).

The common question. When a market must be questioned, and how to respond once it is; which things may not be traded.

Where they meet. Neither side takes price and efficiency as the whole of the criterion: "The level of a price does not, by itself alone, complete a normative judgment" (Political Economy Paper 2, E10); the Base Layer is a line, not on the market's scales (Paper 1, SC1). Both keep identifying a problem apart from choosing a response: Satz first identifies by the four parameters, then chooses among banning, regulating, a safety net and changing the context (pp. 110–111); this series first recognizes at the judgment layer whether a matter falls on the line, and then examines securing measures that restrict others by their effect (Paper 1, SC1; Paper 2, SC5). Both sides also have things that may not be traded: the votes, offices and voluntary slavery she blocks on the ground of equal citizenship and of the two ideals of democratic citizenship (pp. 102–103), and the rights that cannot be contracted away (p. 105); this series' two gates, accumulation not converted into domination over people's Base Layer nor into control over questioning (Political Economy Paper 4, E21, E22), and the line (Paper 1, SC1). The line governs acts; it does not set any particular thing outside exchange: that the line is not discounted for price does not mean that the supply must be free of charge (Paper 1, SC1).

Whether they speak of the same thing. The way of asking differs. Satz evaluates a market: whether a market is noxious; her responses are addressed to institutions (pp. 110–111). This series writes no ought for the market (the Prequel, §1), and asks what the constructs that make and enforce rules have embedded and what they have done. Here the way of asking differs; this is not a divergence: her analysis of a market can be material this series looks at when it makes a finding. By this paper's division, three of her four parameters fall on persons, while "extreme harm to society" falls on society; this series does not set up a separate layer of society: where it presses on a person's Base Layer, it is examined there by the line; where it lets the party questioned control the channel of questioning, it is examined there by the second gate (Political Economy Paper 4, E22) (own undertaking).

What still needs telling apart. This series writes the requirements already found to fall on the Base Layer as constraints that later choices may not cross (Paper 1, SC1; Paper 2, SC5). Which parts of Satz's four parameters, and of her several places of "blocking altogether" on the ground of equal citizenship, coincide with this constraint and which still differ cannot be decided merely from the parameters' being matters of degree and responses' needing weighing; this paper does not decide it. One difference that can be written down lies in how the ground is written: in her arguments at those places for excluding exchanges, she grounds them in equal citizenship (pp. 102, 105); this series grounds them in a human being's being an end, with the Base Layer as the line (the Prequel, §5). Whether the two grounds draw the same range is to be examined separately.

The strongest objection from the other side (drafted by this paper). You say that the list lies at the rule layer, and that whether a supply falls on the life-substrate lies at the judgment layer (Paper 1, SC1). Since the content of the line is not at the normative layer, at the judgment layer you are still doing what I do: looking at how severe the harm is, how vulnerable the people are. Your so-called not entering the weighing only moves the weighing to the judgment layer.

Reply. What the judgment layer recognizes is whether a matter falls on the line, not how much the line is worth; once recognized, it is no longer weighed together with price, scale and efficiency (Paper 1, SC1). In recognizing it one does indeed look at material and circumstances; this series writes out the position that makes the finding, which explains it and can be questioned, and does not write the finding as the result of weighing. Satz's four parameters can be material the judgment layer looks at in making a finding; this use is this paper's (own undertaking). She herself has things that, once recognized, are blocked altogether (pp. 102–103, 105, 109); at this place, the two sides are closer than they look.

Real-world interface (A). French Code de la construction et de l'habitation, art. L302-5 (the text in force since January 1, 2023, as last amended by Article 65 of Law No. 2022-217 of February 21, 2022): the section applies to communes with at least 1,500 inhabitants in the Paris urban unit (unité urbaine de Paris) and at least 3,500 inhabitants on the rest of the territory, lying within an agglomeration of more than 50,000 inhabitants comprising at least one commune of more than 15,000 inhabitants, or within an intercommunal grouping with its own tax powers of such size, in which social rental housing is less than 25 percent of main residences; for certain areas listed by decree the ratio is set at 20 percent; another decree lists communes that are excluded, and communes more than half of whose urbanized area is barred from residential building are also exempt (from the article as published on Légifrance). The articles that follow in the Code set out separately the levy and the arrangements for catching up (from the account of France's Agence nationale pour l'information sur le logement, C).

Reading. This is an embedding: at the rule layer, it lays down for one kind of supply a ratio, a scope of application and exceptions, and fixes who bears it. It does not forbid the rental market, nor does it set rents for the market; placed in Satz's words, it is close to changing the context (p. 111), a reading that is this paper's (own undertaking). In this series, the housing section of Paper 1 writes only a direction sentence, and its negative example is against "no securing at all embedded at the rule layer" (Paper 1, §5). This instance is one form at the rule layer, not a rule this series lays down for any state, and its effectiveness and the strength of its enforcement are not assessed.

§4 Those Who Guard the Market

I · Laffont and Tirole (B, working paper)

The original. The abstract of Laffont and Tirole's 1988 working paper "The Politics of Government Decision-Making: A Theory of Regulatory Capture" (MIT Department of Economics Working Paper No. 506): "We develop an agency-theoretic approach to interest-group politics." In the introduction, section 1: "Congress relies on information supplied by the agency." In the conclusion, section 8: "The organizational response to the possibility of agency politics is to reduce the stakes interest groups have in regulation." (Cited by section; the later journal version and the 1993 book were examined only to the bibliographic record.)

The common question. Whether the position that guards the market will be held by the party it guards, and what to do if it is.

Where they meet. Neither side reads minds: capture is read from structure. Laffont and Tirole look at who holds information and whose stakes are tied to regulation; this series follows the Theory of Government: "Capture is judged by structure, not by the name of a body or where it sits" (Theory of Government Paper 6, G38; carried over in Paper 6, SC12). The responses too both land on structure: they reduce the stakes; this series requires that those who review not depend on those they review, looking at whether the payment can be withdrawn at any time, whether the reviewer has alternative support, and whether his term and later career rest on it (Paper 6, SC12, per A Way Out, a Way Forward, E43).

The question this series adds. The working paper analyzes information and collusion among Congress, the agency and firms, with Congress in the position of the principal. From this hierarchical arrangement at the level of the model it cannot be inferred that the authors hold that only Congress should have a channel of questioning. Here this series adds one question: whether those acted on by the relevant decisions can initiate effective questioning, and whether the channel is controlled by the party questioned (Paper 6, SC12, per Political Economy Paper 3, E16). The working paper examined gives neither an affirmative nor a negative answer to this normative question. How the organization is configured, this series places at the rule layer (Paper 6, SC13); reducing stakes can be one means at the rule layer (own undertaking).

The strongest objection from the other side (drafted by this paper). Letting all who are acted on initiate questioning itself gives interest groups a new entrance: those best at using channels of questioning are exactly the parties with the largest stakes. One more door is one more place that can be captured.

Reply. This series grants this point: the channel of questioning is itself questioned, and the decisions of those who check are also questioned by the three questions (Political Economy Paper 3, E16); that those who review do not depend on those they review holds equally for the positions that take up questioning. It does not on that account narrow who can initiate: initiating only lets a matter be asked about; what efficacy it has is explained by structure by the position that takes it up, which can itself be questioned (own undertaking). What this reply preserves is a requirement; whether such a channel is, in a particular arrangement, enough to guard against capture, this paper has not proved.

Real-world interface (A). North Carolina State Board of Dental Examiners v. Federal Trade Commission (Supreme Court of the United States, February 25, 2015, No. 13-534). The Board sent at least 47 cease-and-desist letters on its official letterhead to nondentist providers of teeth whitening. The Supreme Court held that a state board on which "a controlling number of decisionmakers" are active market participants in the occupation the board regulates must satisfy the requirement of "active supervision" in order to invoke the state-action immunity from antitrust law. Active supervision has substantive requirements: the supervisor "must review the substance of the anticompetitive decision, not merely the procedures followed to produce it"; it "must have the power to veto or modify particular decisions"; the mere potential for supervision is not an adequate substitute for a decision by the State; and "the state supervisor may not itself be an active market participant." The Court said that "Parker immunity does not derive from nomenclature alone," and affirmed the judgment below (from the full text of the judgment).

Reading. The Court did not grant immunity merely because the Board was created by state law and designated by the State as a state agency; it looked at who actually sits in the decision-making positions, and whether a position not belonging to the regulated party, able to review substance and to veto or modify, supervises. This points the same way as "capture is judged by structure, not by the name of a body" (Paper 6, SC12). The Court also did not say that market participants may not take part in occupational licensing; this series likewise does not say that concurrent office is in itself capture (Paper 6, §2). This instance judges whether that board and that set of cease-and-desist letters could obtain immunity, and does not characterize that profession.

II · Stigler (C, one added question)

How far upstream has gone. The Specialist Paper registered Stigler's theory of regulation (1971) as a near neighbor of "those who review do not depend on those they review": the literature on guarding against regulatory capture is earlier, and also broader; the Specialist Paper paraphrased it from the bibliographic record and the abstract, and wrote down there where the two part (A Way Out, a Way Forward, §9, item 5; E43). This paper points to it and does not redo it.

The question this paper adds. Whom regulation benefits. Paraphrased from the bibliographic record and abstract on RePEc: the article analyzes how public resources and powers can be used to improve the economic status of economic groups, the demand for regulation, and the political features that enable relatively small groups to obtain regulation (C). The abstract reaches only the side of benefit; on whom the burden falls is what this paper goes on to ask.

Real-world interface (A). The administrative order of the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission against Standard & Poor's Ratings Services of January 21, 2015 (Securities Act Release No. 9705; Exchange Act Release No. 74104). As the order records: rating fees for conduit/fusion commercial mortgage-backed securities were paid by the issuers; issuers engaged two rating agencies, generally choosing those that reported the lowest credible credit enhancement levels; after the crisis, S&P's share of this market did not return to its pre-2008 level; S&P changed its method of calculating debt service coverage ratios, using averages that S&P staff called "blended constants," which produced lower credit enhancement requirements. The order found that S&P's pre-issuance reports did not tell investors that the method had been changed to use these averages (para. 21), and that these statements were knowingly or recklessly false and misleading (para. 22); S&P did not follow its own internal policies and procedures when it changed this method. Three violations were found: Section 17(a)(1) of the Securities Act (para. 34); the internal-control requirements of Section 15E(c)(3) of the Securities Exchange Act (para. 35); and the recordkeeping rules of Rules 17g-2(a)(2)(iii) and 17g-2(a)(6) thereunder (para. 36). The order does not say that changing the method was in itself unlawful. S&P neither admitted nor denied the order's findings, except as to the Commission's jurisdiction over it and the subject matter of the proceedings and the facts set forth in Annex A; the order imposed a civil penalty of $35 million and disgorgement of $6.2 million plus $0.8 million in interest; S&P undertook not to rate new issues of this class of securities for twelve months (from the original order).

Reading. Stigler asks whom regulation benefits. This instance is not in public regulation but at a position of questioning: a rating asks "can it repay," the rating fee is paid by the party questioned, and the party questioned also chooses among several agencies. What this series additionally asks here is the other half of the same matter: whether the benefiting party can control the conclusion of the questioning. Paper 3's reading: payment by the party rated is dependence formed through payments, examined by three points; "that dependence is present does not mean that capture has occurred" (A Way Out, a Way Forward, E43); payment does not by itself produce the capacity to control conclusions (Paper 3, SC7). What the order found is that the method was changed, that credit enhancement requirements were lower as a result, and that the pre-issuance reports did not truthfully tell investors; besides, there were violations of internal-control and recordkeeping obligations. These findings do not automatically prove that issuers had acquired the capacity to control rating conclusions; that layer is to be examined separately by structure; on whom the burden fell, the order did not find item by item, and this paper does not supply it.

§5 The Direction of Funding

I · Mazzucato (A)

The original. Mazzucato, The Entrepreneurial State (Demos, 2011; the full text examined; this conversation cites only the pages below; the expanded edition of 2013 not examined). The cover line: "The state has not just fixed markets, but actively created them." On p. 53, the preceding sentence says that government has not only funded the riskiest research but has often been the source of the most radical innovations; it goes on: "To this extent it has actively created markets not just fixed them." In the example of pharmaceuticals and biotechnology, she writes that public laboratories take on the risky research while private drug companies make "me too" drugs, and that "private venture capitalists enter only once the real risk has been absorbed by the state" (pp. 21–22). In the section of recommendations: "When successful, a part of the return from investments made with significant public support should be returned to government." (p. 25) The chapter "Final thoughts on risk-taking in innovation: who gets the return?" says that "while the efforts are collective, the returns remain private" (p. 109), and that "we need social institutions that enable these risk-bearers to reap the returns from the innovation process" (p. 113).

The common question. Whether, and how, the public side's having borne risk constitutes a ground for claiming a return; what rights this ground gives it, and what authority it does not.

Whether they speak of the same thing. Two places must be kept apart. Mazzucato's "direction" is the direction of technology and of markets: what public funding chooses to do. What this series bars funding from claiming on someone's behalf is a person's direction of life: those who give conditions do not acquire the authority to decide a person's direction (Political Economy Paper 6, E37; Paper 5, SC10); a company's business direction and the direction of the Kingdom of Ends are kept apart separately under Paper 5, §3. "The state created markets" speaks of the role of public investment, procurement and technology in the formation of markets; this series says that the market is the result that emerges from many people's transactions taken together, and that no one sets it (the Prequel, §1), which speaks of how the market exists. The two sentences can hold at the same time.

Where they meet. Public funding does not only patch gaps: this series does not require support to be without content; support may have particular content (Political Economy Paper 6, E37), and funding may expect output and attach agreements on use and governance that have a source (Paper 5, SC10). On whom risk and return fall, both sides require to be stated: the public position that gives the support states its grounds, authority, object and cost, and can be questioned (Paper 5, SC11).

Asking further from here. On a public side's claiming a return because it has borne risk, what this series can answer is this: a claim to a return must state its source and can be questioned; where the claim rests on an agreement, it is examined separately by the source and actual effect of the agreement (Paper 5, §1; SC10; own undertaking); the proportion and form of the return lie at the rule layer. It does not thereby acquire the authority to decide a person's direction, nor does it endorse the existing acts and continued existence of those supported (SC10, SC11). This limit the pages of Mazzucato examined do not touch; this paper does not say that she denies it. Where there is no agreement, whether having borne risk alone can be a ground for claiming a return, the eight papers have not answered.

Pressure arising from the original (transcribed by this paper). In her own words: when successful, a part of the return should be returned to government (p. 25); the efforts are collective, while the returns remain private (p. 109). The pressure arising from this is: if a public risk-bearer's share was not written into an agreement beforehand, on what ground does this series support or reject its claim to a return? Requiring only that a claim state its source and accept questioning does not yet answer where the entitlement to a return comes from.

Reply. At the normative layer this series answers only that a claim to a return must state its source and can be questioned; proportion and form lie at the rule layer (Paper 5, §1; SC10; own undertaking). Where there is no agreement, whether bearing risk can alone be a ground, the eight papers have not answered, and this paper registers it as it stands (§8, IV, the sixth slot).

Real-world interface (B). The Trados case (Delaware Court of Chancery, August 16, 2013). In July 2005, SDL acquired Trados for $60 million in cash and stock. A management incentive plan took the first $7.8 million of the merger consideration; the preferred stockholders received $52.2 million; "The common stockholders received nothing." The court stated that without the management incentive plan the common stockholders would have received $2.1 million. The preferred stock's liquidation preference together with cumulative dividends came to about $57.9 million; the venture capital investors held preferred stock and had seats on the board. The sale was not approved by a majority of disinterested and independent directors, and the court therefore required the defendants to prove that the transaction was entirely fair. The court noted that the board had not followed a fair process, but held that the defendants had proved entire fairness: under Trados's business plan, the common stock had no economic value before the merger; in a separate analysis of appraisal, it found that the appraised value of the common stock was likewise zero (from the full text of the judgment; the text relied on has no page numbers).

Reading. In this instance, how the sale proceeds were divided involves several things: the liquidation preference funding acquired through terms, the management incentive plan, the interests linking directors with the preferred side, and the court's review. Paper 5's reading: terms such as liquidation preference cannot be judged to cross the line on their names alone; the control they acquire is examined by the three questions, whether those it acts on have actual control over the relevant decisions (Paper 5, §3; SC10). In this instance, the common stockholders went to court, and the court reviewed process and price for entire fairness; this road the party questioned could not close. How risk and return were divided was answered in this instance by the terms, the incentive plan and the court's review, and this series does not judge whether that division was right (own undertaking). Read together with the Mazzucato conversation, it asks about the private side of the same matter: the return the investing party obtains, by what agreement, and under what review.

I · Rawls (section titles from the table of contents; text cited at second hand)

What this paper examined is the table of contents of Justice as Fairness: A Restatement (2001): §41, "Property-Owning Democracy: Introductory Remarks," p. 135; §42, "Some Basic Contrasts between Regimes," p. 138. One sentence of Rawls is cited at second hand from Quish's review of Edmundson, John Rawls: Reticent Socialist (2017), which gives no source: welfare-state capitalism permits a small class to have a near monopoly of the means of production. No quotation marks are used here.

The surface of contact. Once the original book is examined, two questions are to be decided: whether what Rawls asks in comparing regimes is the same question as this series' "recognized by power-face, not by public or private" (SC3); whether the near monopoly of the means of production spoken of above is read in this series as positions of accumulation and chokepoints (Political Economy Paper 1, E2; Paper 2, E7).

II · Lange (C)

Paraphrased from the entry on Lange in Econlib's Concise Encyclopedia of Economics: Lange's scheme of 1936–37, answering Hayek, had the government own the major industries, with a central planning board setting prices for these industries, raising prices where there was a shortage and lowering them where there was a surplus in order to reach equilibrium; later he advocated only that the government set prices in industries with few firms. Of the original book, On the Economic Theory of Socialism (1938), this paper examined only the table of contents. Hayek's side follows Political Economy Paper 0, and is not rewritten.

The surface of contact. Lange separates ownership from allocation: industries are publicly owned, and prices are adjusted by the board according to supply and demand. This series separates at another place: ownership and accountability. A publicly run position of supply guards the line all the same; the position that reviews it is functionally independent of the supplier and of its owner (Paper 1, SC3; Paper 6, SC13). Once the original book is examined, what is to be decided is this: a central planning board setting prices is, in this series, a construct that makes and enforces rules; how its own pricing is questioned, and how Lange answers that.

III · Real-World Interface (B)

The arrangement formed by Ireland's Electricity Regulation Act 1999 and its later amendments. In the current revised text (as published by the Law Reform Commission), Schedule 1, paragraph 9 reads: "Subject to this Act, the Commission shall be independent in the performance of its functions." Section 14 was substituted in full in 2005, and the present wording of its subsection (1) was substituted in 2022: the Commission may grant licences to electricity undertakings, or to any person proposing to exercise the functions of an electricity undertaking; under subsection (2), the Commission may grant to "the Board" referred to in the Act, that is, the incumbent Electricity Supply Board (ESB), a licence to supply electricity to eligible customers. Section 10A (inserted in 2006): the Minister may give the Commission "general policy directions"; subsection (6) does not permit directions concerning the Commission's functions in relation to an individual energy undertaking or person. Section 10 of the original Act made separate provision for ministerial directions, and subsection (4) of the same section states that the section was repealed on February 19, 2000 (from the original Act as published in the Irish Statute Book). ESB's own page states that it was established in 1927 as a statutory corporation under the Electricity (Supply) Act, and that it is majority-owned by the Irish Government. The 1999 original text of the Schedule and of section 14 this paper has not compared.

Reading. The current arrangement writes three things apart: who holds the business of supplying electricity, the government-controlled incumbent supplier included; who can enter, with licences granted to electricity undertakings; who reviews, a commission independent in the performance of its functions, to which the Minister gives only general policy directions, with no directions concerning individual undertakings or persons. This points the same way as this series: the line does not change with ownership, and the position that reviews a publicly run supply is functionally independent of the supplier and of its owner (Paper 1, SC3; Paper 6, SC13). Whether the commission is actually independent is to be examined separately by structure. This instance serves only as an interface, and does not assess the performance of the Irish electricity market.

§7 Across Borders

I · Young (B, abstract)

The original. The abstract of Young's "Responsibility and Global Justice: A Social Connection Model" (Social Philosophy and Policy 23(1), 2006, 102–130): it asks what agents, individual or institutional, bear with respect to global structural processes that produce unjust outcomes. The social connection model holds that "all agents who contribute by their actions to the structural processes that produce injustice" have responsibilities to remedy it. It takes the transnational production, distribution and marketing of garments as its main example, and, contrasted with a liability model of responsibility, has five features: it does not isolate perpetrators, it judges the background conditions of action, it is more forward-looking than backward-looking, its responsibility is shared, and it can be discharged only through collective action. (From the abstract; the body of the article and the posthumous Responsibility for Justice of 2011 were not obtained for this paper. How responsibility is shared, and whether the parties' shares are equal, the abstract does not say, and this paper does not extrapolate from the abstract.)

The common question. For harms along cross-border supply chains, by what responsibility falls; to whom the responsibility for changing structures is addressed.

Where they meet. Distance does not make responsibility disappear: the line does not change at borders (Paper 7, SC14). An outcome with no single decider still has places that can be asked: the step forward the Prequel took asks what the constructs that make and enforce rules in the institution where the outcome lies have done with respect to it (the Prequel, §1; own undertaking). Neither side looks only for perpetrators: in this series the share of the importing side is its own rules and enforcement, and does not presuppose that it was at fault (Paper 7, SC14; own undertaking), which already sets it at a distance from a model that only pursues past fault.

Whether they speak of the same thing. "Shared" and "each to its own acts" are not necessarily opposed: responsibility can be borne in common and at the same time discharged separately by different subjects; each having a share can also look forward.

Where they part. The parting lies in whether the responsibility to change structures can be addressed directly to the individuals who take part in structural processes. Young addresses the responsibility to remedy injustice to the agents, individual or institutional, who take part in structural processes; it looks forward, and can be discharged only through collective action (abstract). This series writes institutional oughts for constructs, settling what each bears for its own acts; an individual's participation is understood under his own law. This series writes no ought directly for individual participants; the accountability of an individual's position when he holds office, and the rules that apply to his transactions, are examined separately at the corresponding layers (the Prequel, §6; Institution Theory Z4). What the two sides still have to tell apart is what kind of responsibility can be addressed directly to individuals, and how jointly changing structures finds those who carry out the action.

The strongest objection from the other side (drafted by this paper). If changing a structure requires organizing a collective action that has not yet happened, is it enough, to explain who initiates this change, to say only that the existing constructs answer for their own acts? Before any construct takes it up, do individuals have a responsibility for changing structures that can be addressed to them?

Reply. Here this series answers half. The constructs that make and enforce rules on the importing side guard the line by the unified sentence toward those whom their own acts reach and who have no way to avoid them by exiting (SC14, first item); this share of responsibility cannot be transferred to a party abroad. The purchasing company has a share of its own under Theory of the Company GS10. Whether an individual initiates is his own law, and this series does not write an institutional ought for it. Before any construct takes it up, who initiates a joint change and how it finds those who carry it out, the eight papers have not answered; this paper registers it as it stands (§8, IV, the sixth slot).

Real-world interface (A). On March 29, 2021, U.S. Customs and Border Protection published a notice of finding (CBP Dec. 21-08, 86 FR 16380): under the categories listed in the statute, certain disposable gloves were produced in Malaysia by Top Glove "with the use of convict, forced, or indentured labor"; the notice states that Customs found that Top Glove produced disposable gloves with forced labor. A notice of September 10 of the same year (CBP Dec. 21-13, 86 FR 50725), titled "Determination That Maintenance of Finding of March 29, 2021, Pertaining to Certain Disposable Gloves Produced in Malaysia, Is No Longer Necessary," determined that these goods were no longer so produced, applying to goods imported on or after that date (from the originals in the Federal Register).

Reading. This finding was made by the importing side's construct regarding its own access: it governs what goods may enter its market, and writes no ought for a factory abroad. Both the making of the finding and the later determination that it need not be maintained lie within the importing side's own rules and enforcement; this is exactly where the importing side's share in Paper 7 lands (SC14, first item). This instance judges access for that class of goods, and does not characterize that company.

II · Rodrik (B, abstract)

The original. The abstract of Rodrik's 2000 "How Far Will International Economic Integration Go?" (Journal of Economic Perspectives 14(1), 177–186): "international economic integration, the nation-state, and mass politics cannot co-exist"; "We have to pick two out of three." (From the abstract; the body was not obtained for this paper. Of the 2011 book The Globalization Paradox, this paper saw only the publisher's description, and does not cite it.)

The common question. When cross-border rules and accountability within one state are not on the same layer, to whom the channel of questioning belongs.

Where they meet. Cross-border arrangements and questioning within one state stand in tension: this series too says that at the level of state and state there is no third party that legislates for both, that treaties bind the parties within their scope of application, and that carrying out and questioning go through each party's own law (Paper 7, SC15, per Theory of the State N24).

Where they part. What Rodrik puts forward is a constraint of compatibility among institutional conditions (abstract). What this series gives at this place is a normative order and addresses of responsibility: the commitments of a treaty, domestic supply and regulation, and arbitration and its implementation are each explained by, and questioned at, the corresponding positions; a procedure that the people whose line it is cannot initiate is not their channel, and their road remains within this state (SC15). These three questions do not correspond one to one with Rodrik's three conditions; writing the three questions apart cannot answer whether the three conditions can be maintained at the same time. Such a distinction does not remove the difficulty of compatibility; how, in a particular conflict, commitments, institutional arrangements and modes of implementation are to be adjusted, this paper has no general solution. "The line does not enter the table of trade-offs" specifies what this series refuses to treat as expendable; it does not prove that the remaining institutional adjustments already have a solution (own undertaking).

The strongest objection from the other side (drafted by this paper). If the three institutional conditions cannot be maintained at the same time, the normative order of guarding the line still gives no compatible arrangement for achieving it. In a particular conflict, which commitment or institutional arrangement is in fact to be adjusted, and how, after adjustment, is the line still guarded? Writing out separately the addresses of responsibility and of questioning has not yet answered this. Where a treaty already binds this state, how does this state's channel of questioning deal with the difficulties these constraints bring to guarding the line?

Reply. This series takes this place as a limit: what arbitration can do, and where its limits and points of failure lie, is stated separately (the end of SC15). This state's road cannot change treaty obligations already formed; it can ask what this state has done with respect to this commitment, and what the supplier and its regulator have done with respect to supply; suspension or exit is examined separately under the rules between states (Theory of the State N24). Whether, in a particular conflict, what is adjusted is a commitment, an arrangement or a mode of implementation is explained by the position that makes the decision, and can be questioned; a general solution this paper does not have (own undertaking). Here Rodrik is an interlocutor who puts pressure on this series' conditions of achievement.

Real-world interface (A). Azurix v. Argentina (ICSID Case No. ARB/01/12), under the United States–Argentina bilateral investment treaty. The concession agreement granted Azurix's subsidiary ABA a thirty-year concession for the distribution of drinking water and the treatment of sewage in the Province of Buenos Aires (Award, para. 41). The award of July 14, 2006 (para. 442): Argentina did not breach Article IV(1) of the treaty (expropriation); it breached Article II(2)(a), failing to give fair and equitable treatment and full protection and security; it breached Article II(2)(b) by taking arbitrary measures; it was to pay compensation of US$165,240,753, with interest compounded semi-annually; all other claims were dismissed. The decision on annulment, dispatched on September 1, 2009: "The application for annulment of the Argentine Republic is dismissed in its entirety." The stay of enforcement of the award was terminated (from the English originals of the award and of the annulment decision).

Reading. The award answers whether the state's measures concerning this investment breached the treaty; the application for annulment having been dismissed, this decision is final in the case. This review can itself touch on price and regulatory measures. That this series writes the three questions apart is an arrangement of attribution, not a claim that the three matters never touch in fact. How the award was implemented, this paper has not examined, and does not write. This instance serves only as an interface, and is not written as support for or opposition to state regulation.

IV · Where This Series Stops

Registration is in six slots, following Political Economy Paper 0. What the six slots distinguish is the nature of what was not continued; they are not six blanks that every series is to fill.

The first slot: the remainder this construct itself has left. What arrangement takes up guarding the line and being questioned in the stretch below the threshold; how the constructs that make and enforce rules examine "whether it suffices" where there are several suppliers, and by what method (Paper 1). How the conditions of a place and the thresholds of that same place are to be read together, for which only a direction, not a method, was given; how, in a particular rule, the face that falls on the same person is to be separated from the face that falls on others (Paper 2). How "not deciding" at a chokepoint is questioned (Paper 3). Who reads an old rule again once it has come to fall on a new road; coordination that falls outside the four routes (Paper 4). Where dependence cannot be removed for the time being, how decisions are made and answered for (Paper 6). The step forward the Prequel took, that for an outcome with no decider one asks what the institution in which it lies has done, is tested in the examples of each paper, and has no general procedure of testing.

The second slot: objects adjacent to the jurisdiction. The theory of price; tax; tariffs, trade policy and exchange rates; sanctions and blockades; the system of cooperation among states; the interior of the company.

Eucken wants the position that supervises monopoly withdrawn from the influence of interested parties; Laffont and Tirole read capture from information and stakes; the North Carolina case looks at who actually sits in the decision-making positions, and at whether the supervisor can review substance; the differences lie mostly in how this is written: Eucken gives an arrangement, while this series lays down only functions.

第一节　这是一台什么样的机器

一 · 它问的是什么

一个以"人是目的"为共识的制度里的市场，在混合的现实中，怎样同时做到两件事：每一个人的基础层不受侵犯，涌现层有空间、有够得着的条件。两相冲突时基础层在先，线不在天平上；监管是必然，监管者自己也可被追问；两层互相托起，是方向，不是担保。

它不问自由市场好不好，不在市场与政府之间选边；"自由市场""计划""公营""私营"都是名称，判的是一处一处的结构（前置篇第二节）。它不是价格理论，也不是放任，也不是计划（前置篇第三节）。它不问哪一种所有制最好；所有制在规则层，本系列只作建议（第一篇 SC2）。税不在本系列之内。

二 · 句式与三层

构用应当，位置用职责，人用自己的法（制度论 Z4）。市场不是一个构：「市场连一个厂那样的归责地址都不是」（政治经济学前置篇第三节）。应当说给在市场里接应当的四类构：立规、执规的构；组织、运营市场基础设施的构；在市场里的供给者与经营者；负责资助、补贴、救助的机构与制度（前置篇第六节）。参与者个人照他自己的法，本系列不对他写应当。

三层是方法：规范层给方向与线；规则层只作建议，可以举例，不排次序；判断层写清谁做决定、谁承担后果，只列要答的问题与答它的位置。永远有余项，也有例外；余项推不出许可（前置篇第七节）。判结构，不判名称；判操作，不判人。

三 · 主线与八篇各落一处

主线句：基础层是线，涌现层要有空间、有条件：守线不封住空间，放开不吃掉线；两相冲突，线在先。

  • 前置篇：市场不是一个构；目的王国方向的市场；两件事；应当说给谁。
  • 保障：基础层是线，不在市场的天平上（SC1）；越接近生命基底，制衡与监督越强，私有占多少这一问越要问得出、答得出（SC2）；线不随所有制变（SC3）。
  • 空间：线以上照常放开，放开不等于不问，条件要够得着（SC4）；两个"过度"不对称，核法与次序不同，两层同等重要（SC5）。
  • 关口：绕不过去的基础设施，所立的规由制度划范围，可以被问（SC6）；评级、记账与审计的追问，不由被问的一方独自控制（SC7）。
  • 竞争：竞争照常放开，也照常受问，封路是须重点核的一类作用（SC8）；没有一个位置独占关门之权，拆分是最后的手段（SC9）。
  • 资金：资金可以带要求，不因此取得替人定方向的权限（SC10）；公共资金说得出根据，不作背书（SC11）。
  • 监管：监管是必然，监管者也受问（SC12）；审公营供给的位置，在功能上独立于供给者与它的主人（SC13）。
  • 国界：线不随国界变，责任各归各的施为（SC14）；没有上级的地方，问的通道也不归被问的一方（SC15）。

第二节　对接的方法

一 · 四步

每一场对话走四步：先找可核的共同问题；再看两家说的是不是同一件事；再说同意到哪里为止，从哪里分开；最后写对方一侧最强的一条反驳，与本系列的回应。反驳由本篇代拟，标明代拟，尽量触到对方的强处；对方原文里已有、正对着本系列的压力，据原文转写，标明转写，不代拟。

二 · 两种深度

原文核到的，按核到的范围推进。核到整章或全文、页码已核的，四步走完：萨茨、马祖卡托。只核到译本的几页、一篇工作论文或一篇摘要的，按核到的那几句走完四步，并写明那几句以外不替对方作答：欧肯（据英译节本）、拉丰与梯若尔（据工作论文，期刊刊本未核）、杨与罗德里克（据摘要）。

只到目录、书评或百科条目的，走到共同的问题与接触面为止：罗尔斯、兰格。这两个单元里，本篇写明据什么转述、接触面在哪里、原书核到以后要决定的是哪一问；不写"同在""分在"的定论。

前面系列已经对接过的，只照指，不重做：政治经济学 Paper 0 对接的二十家（斯密、康德、马克思、哈耶克、弗里德曼、可汗、赫希曼、黑格尔、波兰尼、汤普森、伯利与米恩斯、科斯、加尔布雷斯、安德森、诺齐克、沃尔泽、桑德尔、法兰克福、皮凯蒂、罗宾斯）；专论对接的各家，其中与本篇相近的有莉娜·汗（政治经济学 Paper 0 作"可汗"）、拉赫曼、纳雷查尼亚与西塔拉曼、施蒂格勒、佩蒂特；公司论 Paper 0 的各家。再用时先登上游已到哪一步，本篇新增哪一问；本篇只对施蒂格勒补一问。

三 · 现实接口

每一场配现实接口（欧肯一场配一对，所有制一场两家合配一个），照四条：

  • 事实回原始文件：判决、监管认定、法律原文、仲裁文书；写明年份、来源与档次；
  • 只写原始文件写了的；没写的，照写没写；
  • 判的是那一项做法，不给一家公司、一个政府定性；
  • 只作接口，不承重：论证不靠那一家的名声，那一家以后变了，也不动论证。

四 · 对接时要核的两问

对接时核两问；哪一场落在哪一问，各场照写。

第一问， 对市场安排、整体秩序或结构结果的分析，怎样落到可被追问的构及其施为上。以一种秩序、一个市场或几项制度条件能否兼容为分析的对象，不等于把市场当作承接应当的主体；这一问只问那一分析落到了谁的哪一项施为上。

第二问， 在规则与措施的选择里，哪些要求作为约束，哪些代价须作权衡。

这个系列怎样答这两问，八篇已经写明：说给构的应当，落在立规、执规、运营基础设施、供给与资助的构上（前置篇第六节）；基础层一侧照线核，不留量（第二篇 SC5；线见第一篇 SC1）；限制别人涌现层的保障措施照作用核，说明代价、替代与理由；落在同一个人身上的，两个方向都不许：不拿他的涌现层换他的保障，也不拿他基础层的没有保障换他更多的选（第二篇 SC5）。

与各家的重合、追加的问题与分歧，在下面各场分别确认，各自写明由哪一段原文支持。只是问法不同的，照写问法不同，不写成分歧。

第三节　秩序与边界

一 · 欧肯（B，据英译节本）

原文。 欧肯《竞争秩序及其实现》（Die Wettbewerbsordnung und ihre Verwirklichung，ORDO 第 2 卷，1949）的英译节本，载《竞争政策国际》（Competition Policy International）第 2 卷第 2 期（2006），pp. 219–245，Ahlborn 与 Grave 译。p. 227 上文依次说企业、劳动者各有自由，接着说：「有消费者的自由，却没有选择怎样规定游戏规则、经济过程取什么形式的自由」（"Freedom of the consumer exists, but not the freedom to choose how to define the rules of the game or the forms which the economic process takes"）。p. 232，他以建立一个完全竞争下有效运转的价格体系，作每一项经济措施的根本判准（"making the establishment of a functioning price system of complete competition the essential criterion of every economic measure"）。p. 241：「所以，对垄断的监督应当交给一个国家的垄断局。」（"The monopoly supervision should thus be transferred to a state monopoly office."）为的是使它摆脱利害相关者持续而危险的影响（"to withdraw it from the persistently dangerous influences exerted by interested parties"）；它应当是一个只服从法律的独立机构（"an independent office which is only subject to the law"），不设作经济部的一个司；「垄断局与最高法院同样不可少。」（"The monopoly office is just as indispensable as the Supreme Court."）（据英译节本，页码已核；德文原文未核，本篇只读这几页，以外不替欧肯作答。）

共同的问题。 市场的基本规则由谁定、谁裁，守这一规则的位置怎样不被市场里的一方拿住。

同在。 市场参与者不能独占基本规则的决定与裁断：这个系列说，绕不过去的位置所立的规，范围由制度划，可以被问（第三篇 SC6，承专论 E41a）。监督的位置要摆脱利害相关者的影响：这个系列说，立规、执规的构在功能上独立于被它监管的一方，不在收入、任期、前程上实质依赖被审者（第六篇 SC12，承专论 E43）。欧肯在这里问的正是具体的权力结构，不只问形式。

分在。 两处，都由上引的几页支持。第一处在判准：欧肯以竞争价格体系的功能作经济政策的判准（p. 232）；这个系列不以接近某一种市场形式作落判的根据，核的是一处施为怎样守线、留下空间，以及这一施为怎样受问（前置篇第二节；第二篇 SC5）。第二处在独立的写法：欧肯给了一种设置，只服从法律、不在部里（p. 241）；这个系列的独立是功能义，不看机构设在哪里，「独立是功能义，不要求在制度之外」（制度论 Z34），设置交规则层（第六篇 SC13）。他的设置在这个系列里可以是规则层的一种样子，这一处的差别是写法上的，不是方向上的（自担）。

对方最强的反驳（本篇代拟，从秩序一侧）。 不以有效运转的竞争价格体系作判准，各篇就只能一处一处地补：关口、合谋、并购分开问，问不出整体。一个市场被少数位置拿住，可以不是哪一处越了线，而是整个形式变了。

回应。 这个系列不说形式无关紧要。离关口越近、权力面越大，制衡越强（第三篇 SC6）；收购尚未长大的替代者、自营排前、封住准入，是须重点核的一类作用（第四篇 SC8）。这些看的都是形式的变化，只是落到一个个可以被问的位置上：形式变了，变在谁的施为上，谁能问它。拆分是最后的手段，用时说明作用、代价与替代（第四篇 SC9），不以某一形式的理想为根据（自担）。这一回应有它的限度：它说得出每一处怎样问，说不出整体的形式到哪一步已经变了；那要在判断层，照结构一处一处地认。

现实接口（B）。 欧盟法院 2024 年 9 月 10 日维持欧盟委员会对谷歌处以的 24 亿欧元罚款。新闻稿写，不能一般地认为（"it cannot be considered that, as a general rule, a dominant undertaking"），支配企业优待自家产品或服务，就出了以优劣取胜的竞争；谷歌在一般搜索结果页上优待自家比价购物服务的结果，是照市场的特点与本案的具体情形（"in the light of the characteristics of the market and the specific circumstances of the case"）认定为差别待遇、出了以优劣取胜的竞争的（据法院第 135/24 号新闻稿；新闻稿自注不约束法院，判决主文原页本篇未取到）。

现实接口（A）。 韩国大法院 2025 年 10 月 16 日的判决（2023Du32709）：原审首尔高等法院维持公平交易委员会的处分，认定一家平台调整搜索算法、优待在它自家开放市场入驻的商户的商品，构成三项违法：滥用市场支配地位的差别待遇，不正当的差别交易，欺骗性的顾客引诱；大法院认为原审在不当性与效果、欺骗的认定上误解了法理，撤销原判，发回首尔高等法院（据大法院判决书原件的主文）。大法院英文要旨答一个争点为肯定：判断不当性与意图的那一套办法，对优待自家商品的平台同样适用。判决书与要旨都以代称写当事方；当事一方是 Naver，据代理它上诉的律所 Kim & Chang 的案例说明。发回以后的结果本篇未核，不能写成法院已认定相关行为合法。

读法。 两处都没有只凭"优待自家一侧"这一名目定案：一处新闻稿明写不能一般地认为，照具体情形认定；一处以原审对不当性与效果的认定有误发回。这与第四篇同向：自营排前是须重点核的一类作用，不凭名目判越线（第四篇第二节；SC8）。在这一场里，这一对接口说的是：规则由平台自己立的时候，有不在平台里的位置去问它，问的是那一项做法在那一个市场里起了什么作用。两处判的是那两项做法，不给两家公司定性；本篇也不由这两处推出一切自营排前怎样。

二 · 萨茨（A）

原文。 萨茨《为什么有些东西不该出售：市场的道德界限》第四章《有害的市场》（Oxford University Press，2010，pp. 91–113）。所核的 PDF 本在章首有一段标作摘要（ABSTRACT）的文字，连问几句，其一是：「在哪里、以什么理由规制一个市场是适当的，什么时候我们应当设法阻止它？」（"Where and for what reasons is it appropriate to regulate a market, and when should we seek to block it?"）（p. 91；印本里有没有这一段，本篇未核）她给出四个参数，自己分作两组：前两项刻画特定市场的后果（"The first two parameters characterize the consequences of particular markets."）（p. 94），即极端有害的个人后果、对社会的极端伤害；后两项刻画它的来源，即市场主体的处境（p. 96），即知识与行动能力上的极弱或极不对称、交易一方的极端脆弱。同一章里她也写：若要公民平等，有理由把某些市场交换整个拦住（"block certain market exchanges altogether if citizens are to be equals"），教育、医疗、机会、权利、自由与人身安全，获得它们不应只取决于个人的偏好或收入（"access to these goods should not depend only on individual preferences or income"）（p. 102）；她分说两种民主公民身份的理想（"two different ideals of democratic citizenship"），接着说：「两种观念都会拦住选票、司法职位、立法职位与自愿为奴的市场。」（"Both conceptions would block markets in votes, judicial offices, legislative offices, and voluntary slavery."）（p. 103）；「对平等公民身份的承诺……预设了有些权利是个人不能以契约让渡的。」（"A commitment to equal citizenship, however, does presuppose that there are some rights that individuals cannot contract away."）（p. 105）；「有些市场交易的东西，是任何民主社会都不能容许的」（"Some markets trade in things that no democratic society can countenance"）（p. 109）。回应一侧，她说：「我们不能从一个市场有害，立刻推出我们应当在法律上禁止它」（"we cannot immediately conclude from the fact that a market is noxious that we ought legally to ban it"）（p. 110）；回应可以是以立法设一张安全网（"legislating a safety net"），也可以「不去改市场，而试着改背景」（"instead of changing the market we might try to change the context"）（p. 111）。

共同的问题。 一个市场什么时候须问，问了以后怎样回应；哪些东西不许换。

同在。 两边都不把价格与效率当作全部的判准：「一个价格的高低，单凭它不完成规范的判断」（政治经济学第二篇 E10）；基础层是线，不在市场的天平上（第一篇 SC1）。两边都把认出问题与选择回应分开：萨茨先用四个参数认，再在禁止、规制、安全网与改背景之间选（pp. 110–111）；这个系列先在判断层认一件事落没落在线上，限制别人的保障措施再照作用核（第一篇 SC1；第二篇 SC5）。两边也都有不许换的东西：她以平等公民身份、以两种民主公民身份的理想为根据拦住的选票、职位与自愿为奴（pp. 102–103），不能以契约让渡的权利（p. 105）；这个系列的两道闸，积累不兑换为对人的基础层的支配，也不兑换为对追问的控制（政治经济学第四篇 E21、E22），与线（第一篇 SC1）。线管的是施为，并不把哪一样东西划到交易之外：线不随价格打折，不是说供给须无偿（第一篇 SC1）。

说的是不是同一件事。 问法不同。萨茨评的是一个市场：一个市场有害不有害；她的回应是对制度说的（pp. 110–111）。这个系列不对市场写应当（前置篇第一节），问的是立规、执规的构载入了什么、作了什么。这一处是问法不同，不是分歧：她对一个市场的分析，可以是这个系列认定时要看的材料。照本篇的分法，她的四个参数里有三个落在人身上，"对社会的极端伤害"一项落在社会上；这个系列不单设社会一层：它在哪一处压到一个人的基础层上，就在那一处照线核；在哪一处使被问的一方控制了问的通道，就在那一处照第二道闸核（政治经济学第四篇 E22）（自担）。

还要辨清的。 这个系列把已认定落在基础层上的要求，写成后续选择不得越过的约束（第一篇 SC1；第二篇 SC5）。萨茨的四个参数、她以平等公民身份为根据的几处"整个拦住"，与这一约束之间哪些重合、哪些仍有差别，不能仅由参数有程度、回应须权衡判定；本篇不定。能写下的一处差别在根据的写法上：她在上述几处排除交易的论证中，以平等公民身份为根据（pp. 102、105），这个系列以人是目的、以基础层为线（前置篇第五节）。两种根据划出的范围同不同，要另核。

对方最强的反驳（本篇代拟）。 你们说清单在规则层，一项供给落没落在生命基底上在判断层（第一篇 SC1）。线的内容既然不在规范层，到了判断层，你们做的仍是我做的事：看伤害有多重，看人有多脆弱。所谓不进称量，只是把称量挪到了判断层。

回应。 判断层认的是一件事落没落在线上，不是线值多少；认出以后，它不再与价格、规模、效率放在一起称（第一篇 SC1）。认的时候确实要看材料、看情形；这个系列把认定的位置写出来，由它说明，可被问，不把认定写成称量的结果。萨茨的四个参数，可以是判断层认定时要看的材料；这一用法是本篇的（自担）。她自己也有认出以后整个拦住的东西（pp. 102–103、105、109）；在这一处，两边比看上去近。

现实接口（A）。 法国《建筑与住房法典》L302-5 条（2023 年 1 月 1 日起施行的文本，最近一次由 2022 年 2 月 21 日第 2022-217 号法律第 65 条修改）：巴黎城市单元（unité urbaine de Paris）里人口一千五百人以上、其余地方三千五百人以上的市镇，处在人口超过五万、含一个人口超过一万五千的市镇的城市聚集区，或这样规模的有税权的市镇联合体之内，社会租赁住房少于主要住房的 25% 的，适用这一节；法令所列的部分地区，比例定为 20%；另有法令列出排除在外的市镇，城市化地域超过一半禁建住宅的市镇也不适用（据 Légifrance 所载法条）。法典随后的条文另定征款与追赶的安排（据法国国家住房信息署的说明，C）。

读法。 这是一项载入：在规则层，为一类供给立了比例、适用范围与例外，定了由谁承担。它不禁止租房的市场，也不替市场定房租；放在萨茨的话里，它近于改背景（p. 111），这一读是本篇的（自担）。在这个系列里，第一篇住房一节只写方向句，反面例子反的是"规则层一项保障也不载入"（第一篇第五节）。这一例是规则层的一种样子，不是本系列替各国定的规，也不评它的成效与执行的强弱。

第四节　守市场的人

一 · 拉丰与梯若尔（B，工作论文）

原文。 拉丰与梯若尔 1988 年的工作论文《政府决策的政治：一个规制俘获的理论》（MIT 经济系工作论文第 506 号）摘要：「我们为利益集团政治建立一种代理理论的进路。」（"We develop an agency-theoretic approach to interest-group politics."）第一节引言里，「国会依赖机构提供的信息」（"Congress relies on information supplied by the agency."）。第八节结论：「对机构政治之可能的组织回应，是降低利益集团在规制中的利害。」（"The organizational response to the possibility of agency politics is to reduce the stakes interest groups have in regulation."）（按节引；后来的期刊本与 1993 年的书，本篇只核到书目。）

共同的问题。 守市场的位置会不会被它守的一方拿住，拿住了怎样办。

同在。 两边都不读心：俘获从结构读。拉丰与梯若尔看的是谁掌握信息、谁的利害系在规制上；这个系列照政府论：「判捕获看结构，不看机构的名称与设在哪里」（政府论第六篇 G38；第六篇 SC12 承之）。回应也都落在结构上：他们降低利害；这个系列要审者不依赖被审者，看给付能不能被随时撤回、审者有没有可替代的支持、任期与日后的职业靠不靠它（第六篇 SC12，承专论 E43）。

这个系列追加的一问。 工作论文分析的是国会、机构与企业之间的信息与串通，国会处在委托人的位置。从模型这一层级安排，推不出作者主张只有国会才该有追问的通道。这个系列在这里追加一问：受相关决定作用的人，能不能发动有效的追问，通道是否由被问者控制（第六篇 SC12，承政治经济学第三篇 E16）。所核的工作论文对这一规范问题没有给出肯定或否定的回答。组织怎样配置，这个系列放在规则层（第六篇 SC13）；降低利害可以是规则层的一种办法（自担）。

对方最强的反驳（本篇代拟）。 让受作用的人都能启动追问，本身就给了利益集团新的入口：最会用追问通道的，正是利害最大的那一方。多开一扇门，就多一处可被俘获的地方。

回应。 这一点这个系列认：问的通道自己也受问，制衡者自己的决定也照三问受问（政治经济学第三篇 E16）；审者不依赖被审者，对受理追问的位置同样成立。它不由此收窄谁能启动：启动只让一件事被问，效力怎样，由受理的位置照结构说明，它自己也可被问（自担）。这一回应保住的是一项要求；这样的通道在具体安排里够不够防住捕获，本篇没有证明。

现实接口（A）。 北卡州牙科委员会诉联邦贸易委员会案（美国最高法院，2015 年 2 月 25 日，第 13-534 号）。委员会以官方信笺向非牙医的牙齿美白服务者发出至少 47 封停止通知。最高法院认定：一个州委员会，作决定的人中有形成控制的数目（"a controlling number of decisionmakers"）是它所管行业里的在业者，就须满足"主动监督"的要求，才能援用州行为的反垄断豁免。主动监督有实质的要求：监督者要审查反竞争决定的内容，不只审程序（"must review the substance of the anticompetitive decision, not merely the procedures followed to produce it"）；要有权否决或修改具体决定（"must have the power to veto or modify particular decisions"）；只有监督的可能，不能代替州的决定；监督者自己不能是在业者（"the state supervisor may not itself be an active market participant"）。法院说豁免不只出自名目（"Parker immunity does not derive from nomenclature alone"）；维持下级法院的判决（据判决全文）。

读法。 法院没有只因它由州法设立、被州指定为州的机构，就给予豁免；看的是谁实际坐在作决定的位置上，有没有一个不在被管一方、能审内容、能否决或修改的位置在监督。这与"判捕获看结构，不看机构的名称"同向（第六篇 SC12）。法院也没有说在业者不能参与资格管理；这个系列同样不说兼任本身就是捕获（第六篇第二节）。这一例判的是那一委员会、那一组停止通知能不能得到豁免，不给那一行业定性。

二 · 施蒂格勒（C，补一问）

上游已到哪一步。 专论把施蒂格勒的规制理论（1971）登作"审者不依赖被审者"的近邻：防规制俘获的文献更早，也更宽；专论据书目与摘要转述，分开的地方写在那里（专论第九节第五条；E43）。本篇照指，不重做。

本篇新增一问。 规制让谁得益。据 RePEc 所载的书目与摘要转述：这篇文章分析公共资源与权力怎样可以被用来改善经济群体的经济地位，规制的需求，以及使相对小的群体能取得规制的政治特征（C）。摘要只到得益一面；负担落在谁身上，是本篇接着问的。

现实接口（A）。 美国证券交易委员会 2015 年 1 月 21 日对标普评级服务的行政命令（Securities Act Release No. 9705；Exchange Act Release No. 74104）。照命令所记：管道与融合型商业抵押贷款证券的评级费由发行人付；发行人请两家评级机构，一般选报出最低可信增信水平的那几家；危机以后，标普在这一市场的份额没有回到 2008 年以前的水平；标普改了偿债覆盖率的算法，用了标普的人称作"blended constants"的平均值，算出的增信要求较低。命令认定：标普发行前的报告没有告诉投资者方法已改用这种平均值（第 21 段），这些陈述是明知或轻率的虚假、误导（第 22 段）；标普改这一方法时没有照它自己的内部政策与程序。违法认定有三项：《证券法》第 17(a)(1) 条（第 34 段）；《证券交易法》第 15E(c)(3) 条关于内部控制的要求（第 35 段）；该法下第 17g-2(a)(2)(iii) 条与第 17g-2(a)(6) 条关于记录的规则（第 36 段）。命令没有说改方法本身违法。标普对命令的认定不承认也不否认，但承认委员会对它的管辖与本程序的事项，以及附件 A 所列的事实；命令处民事罚款三千五百万美元，退还所得六百二十万美元及利息八十万美元；标普承诺十二个月内不给新发行的这一类证券评级（据命令原件）。

读法。 施蒂格勒问规制让谁得益。这一例不在公共规制里，在一个问的位置上：评级问的是"还不还得起"，评级费由被问的一方付，被问的一方又在几家之间挑。这个系列在这里多问的，是同一件事的另一半：得益的一方能不能控制问的结论。第三篇的读法：被评一方付费，是经由给付形成的依赖，照三样核；「依赖在场，不等于已被捕获」（专论 E43）；付费不独自产生控制结论的权能（第三篇 SC7）。命令认定的是：方法改了，增信要求因此较低，发行前的报告没有如实告诉投资者；另有内部控制与记录义务上的违法。这些认定不自动证明发行人已经取得控制评级结论的权能，那一层要照结构另核；负担落在谁身上，命令没有逐一认定，本篇也不补。

第五节　资金的方向

一 · 马祖卡托（A）

原文。 马祖卡托《创业型国家》（Demos，2011；全文已核，这一场只引用下列几页；2013 年的扩充本未核）。封面题句：「国家不只是修补了市场，而是主动创造了市场」（"The state has not just fixed markets, but actively created them"）。p. 53 前一句说，政府不只出钱做最有风险的研究，也常是最激进的创新的来源；接着：「在这个意义上，它主动创造了市场，不只是修补了它们」（"To this extent it has actively created markets not just fixed them"）。在制药与生物技术的例子里，她写公共实验室承担有风险的研究，私人药企做"me too"药，「私人风险投资者只在真正的风险已被国家吸收之后才进入」（"private venture capitalists enter only once the real risk has been absorbed by the state"）（pp. 21–22）。建议一节：「成功时，得到大量公共支持的投资，其回报的一部分应当还给政府。」（"When successful, a part of the return from investments made with significant public support should be returned to government."）（p. 25）《关于创新中承担风险的最后几点想法：谁得到回报？》一章说，「努力是集体的，回报却仍是私人的」（"while the efforts are collective, the returns remain private"）（p. 109），又说需要能让这些承担风险者得到创新回报的社会制度（"we need social institutions that enable these risk-bearers to reap the returns from the innovation process"）（p. 113）。

共同的问题。 公共一方承担了风险，是否、以及怎样构成主张回报的根据；这一根据给它什么权利，不给它什么权限。

说的是不是同一件事。 两处要分开。马祖卡托的"方向"，是技术与市场的方向：公共资金选什么去做。这个系列限制资金替人认领的，是人的人生方向：给条件的不取得替人定方向的权限（政治经济学第六篇 E37；第五篇 SC10）；公司的经营方向与目的王国方向，另照第五篇第三节分开。"国家创造了市场"说的是公共投资、采购与技术在市场形成中的作用；这个系列说市场是许多人的交易合起来涌现的结果，没有谁定它（前置篇第一节），说的是市场怎样存在。两句可以同时成立。

同在。 公共资金不只补漏：这个系列不要求支持没有内容，支持可以有具体内容（政治经济学第六篇 E37），资金可以期待产出，附有来源的用途与治理约定（第五篇 SC10）。风险与回报落在谁身上，两边都要求说出来：作出支持的公共位置说明根据、权限、对象与代价，可被问（第五篇 SC11）。

由此再问。 公共一方因承担风险而主张回报，这个系列能答到的是：回报的主张须说明来源、可被问；主张基于约定的，照约定的来源与实际作用另核（第五篇第一节；SC10；自担）；回报的比例与形式在规则层。它不因此取得替人定方向的权限，也不给受支持者既有的施为与存续背书（SC10、SC11）。这一限，马祖卡托这几页没有碰到；本篇不说她否认它。没有约定时，单凭承担了风险，能不能成为主张回报的根据，八篇没有答。

由原文形成的压力（本篇转写）。 她的原话：成功时，回报的一部分应当还给政府（p. 25）；努力是集体的，回报却仍是私人的（p. 109）。由此形成的压力是：公共风险承担者的一份若没有写进事前约定，这个系列凭什么支持或驳回它的回报请求？只要求主张说明来源、接受追问，还没有回答回报的资格从哪里来。

回应。 这个系列在规范层只答到回报的主张须说明来源、可被问；比例与形式在规则层（第五篇第一节；SC10；自担）。没有约定时，承担风险能不能独自成为根据，八篇没有答，本篇照实登记（第八节第四小节第六格）。

现实接口（B）。 Trados 案（特拉华衡平法院，2013 年 8 月 16 日）。2005 年 7 月，SDL 以现金与股票共六千万美元收购 Trados。管理层激励计划先取得收购对价的头 780 万美元；优先股股东取得 5,220 万美元；「普通股股东一无所得」（"The common stockholders received nothing."）；法院写明，没有管理层激励计划，普通股股东本可分得 210 万美元。优先股的清算优先连同累积股息共约 5,790 万美元；风险投资方持有优先股，在董事会占有席位。批准这次出售的，不是由无利害、独立的董事构成的多数，法院因此要被告证明交易完全公平。法院指出董事会没有走一个公平的程序，但认定被告完成了完全公平的举证：照 Trados 的经营计划，普通股在合并以前没有经济价值；另在评估一项里认定，普通股的评估价值同样为零（据判决全文，所据文本无页码）。

读法。 这一例里，出售所得怎样分，牵着几样：资金借条款取得的清算优先，管理层激励计划，董事与优先股一方的利益关联，以及法院的审查。第五篇的读法：优先清算一类条款，不能仅凭名目判越线；它取得的控制照三问核，受它作用的人对相关决定有没有实际的控制（第五篇第三节；SC10）。在这一例里，普通股股东走到了法院，法院照完全公平审查了程序与价格；这一条路，被问的一方关不掉。风险与回报怎样分，这一例由条款、激励计划与法院的审查答，这个系列不判那一分法对不对（自担）。与马祖卡托一场合读，问的是同一件事的私人一面：出钱的一方取得的回报，由什么约定、经什么审查。

一 · 罗尔斯（节题据目录，正文转引）

本篇核到的，是《作为公平的正义：正义新论》（2001）的目录：§41"财产所有的民主：导言"，p. 135；§42"几种政体的基本对照"，p. 138。罗尔斯的一句，据 Quish 评 Edmundson《约翰·罗尔斯：缄默的社会主义者》（2017）的书评转引，书评未给出处：福利国家资本主义容许一小群人近乎垄断生产资料。这里不加引号。

接触面。 罗尔斯把所有制放进基本结构，比较几种政体；这个系列把所有制放在规则层，只作建议，规范层只说线不随所有制变（第一篇 SC2、SC3）。原书核到以后要决定两问：罗尔斯比较政体时问的，与这个系列"按权力面认，不按公私认"（SC3）是不是同一问；"近乎垄断生产资料"在这个系列里，是不是读作积累的位置与关口（政治经济学第一篇 E2、第二篇 E7）。

二 · 兰格（C）

据 Econlib《简明经济学百科》的兰格条目转述：兰格 1936—37 年的方案回应哈耶克，由政府拥有主要的产业，中央计划委员会为这些产业定价，为求均衡，有短缺就涨价，有过剩就降价；后来只主张政府在企业很少的产业里定价。原书《论社会主义的经济理论》（1938）本篇只核到目录。哈耶克一侧，照政治经济学 Paper 0，不重写。

接触面。 兰格把所有与配置分开：产业公有，价格由委员会照供求调。这个系列在另一处分开：所有与问责。公营的供给位置，线照样守；审它的位置在功能上独立于供给者与它的主人（第一篇 SC3；第六篇 SC13）。原书核到以后要决定：中央计划委员会定价，在这个系列里是一个立规、执规的构，它自己的定价怎样受问，兰格怎样答。

三 · 现实接口（B）

爱尔兰 1999 年《电力管制法》及其后续修订形成的安排。现行修订本（法律改革委员会所载）：附表 1 第 9 段：「除本法另有规定外，委员会在履行职能时独立。」（"Subject to this Act, the Commission shall be independent in the performance of its functions."）第 14 条 2005 年整条替换，第 (1) 款的现行措辞 2022 年换过：委员会可向电力企业或拟行使电力企业职能的任何人发许可证；第 (2) 款，委员会可向法中所称的"the Board"，即原有的电力供应局（ESB），发向合格用户供电的许可证。第 10A 条（2006 年插入）：部长可向委员会发一般政策指示（"general policy directions"）；第 (6) 款不许就委员会对个别能源企业或个人的职能发指示。原法第 10 条另有部长指示的规定，同条第 (4) 款写明本条于 2000 年 2 月 19 日废止（据爱尔兰法规库所载原法）。ESB 自己的页面写明，它于 1927 年依《电力（供应）法》设为法定法人，由爱尔兰政府控股。附表与第 14 条的 1999 年原文，本篇没有对。

读法。 现行的安排把三件事分开写：谁持有供电的业务，政府控股的原有供应者在内；谁可以进来，向电力企业发证；谁来审，一个独立履职的委员会，部长只发一般政策指示，不就个别企业与个人发指示。这与这个系列同向：线不随所有制变，审公营供给的位置在功能上独立于供给者与它的主人（第一篇 SC3；第六篇 SC13）。委员会实际独不独立，要照结构另核。这一例只作接口，不评爱尔兰电力市场的成效。

第七节　跨境

一 · 杨（B，摘要）

原文。 杨《责任与全球正义：社会联结模型》（Social Philosophy and Policy 23(1)，2006，102–130）的摘要：问的是行动者，个人或机构，对产生不正义结果的全球结构过程负有什么。社会联结模型说，「凡以自己的行动促成产生不正义的结构过程的行动者」（"all agents who contribute by their actions to the structural processes that produce injustice"）都负有纠正之责。它以服装的跨国生产、分销与营销为主例，与"责任的追究模型"对照，有五个特点：不单挑出加害者，看行动的背景条件，更朝前看而不只朝后看，责任分担，只能经集体行动履行。（据摘要；正文与 2011 年的遗著《正义的责任》本篇未取到。分担怎样分、各方的份是否相等，摘要没有说，本篇不由摘要外推。）

共同的问题。 跨境供应链上的伤害，责任按什么落；改变结构的责任向谁提出。

同在。 远不使责任消失：线不随国界变（第七篇 SC14）。找不到一个决定者的结果，也有可问的地方：前置篇往前走的那一步，问的是结果所在的制度里，立规、执规的构就它作了什么（前置篇第一节，自担）。两边都不只找加害者：这个系列里进口一侧的那一份，是它自己的规则与执行，不以它有过失为前提（第七篇 SC14；自担），这已与单纯追究既往过错的模型拉开了距离。

说的是不是同一件事。 "分担"与"各归各的施为"未必相反：责任可以共同承担，同时由不同的主体分别履行；各有一份，也可以朝前看。

分在。 分开的地方，在能不能直接向参与结构过程的个人提出改变结构的责任。杨向参与结构过程的行动者，个人或机构，提出纠正不正义的责任，它朝前看，只能经集体行动履行（摘要）。这个系列把制度的应当写给构，就各自的施为确定承担；个人的参与，依他自己的法理解。本系列不直接对参与者个人写应当；个人任职时的位置职责，与他的交易适用的规则，另依相应的层次核（前置篇第六节；制度论 Z4）。两边还要辨清的，是何种责任可以直接向个人提出，共同改变结构又怎样得到行动的承担者。

对方最强的反驳（本篇代拟）。 结构的改变若需要组织尚未发生的共同行动，只说明现有各构对自己的施为负责，够不够解释谁来发起这一改变？在还没有一个构承接以前，个人对改变结构有没有可被提出的责任？

回应。 这个系列在这里答到一半。进口一侧立规、执规的构，就它自己的施为所及、又无从以退出避开的人，照统一句守线（SC14 第一项）；这一份责任不能转给境外的一方。买货的公司照公司论 GS10 另有它自己的一份。个人发起与否，是他自己的法，本系列不替它写制度的应当。在还没有一个构承接以前，共同改变由谁发起、怎样得到承担者，八篇没有答；本篇照实登记（第八节第四小节第六格）。

现实接口（A）。 美国海关与边境保护局 2021 年 3 月 29 日发布认定通知（CBP Dec. 21-08，86 FR 16380）：照法条所列的类别，某些一次性手套由 Top Glove 在马来西亚"以囚犯、强迫或契约劳役劳动"（"with the use of convict, forced, or indentured labor"）生产；通知写明，海关认定 Top Glove 以强迫劳动生产一次性手套。同年 9 月 10 日的通知（CBP Dec. 21-13，86 FR 50725），题为《认定维持 2021 年 3 月 29 日关于马来西亚所产某些一次性手套之认定已无必要》，认定这些货品已不再以此生产，对当日及以后进口的货物适用（据联邦公报原件）。

读法。 这一认定是进口一侧的构就它自己的准入作出的：它管的是什么货能进它的市场，不对境外的工厂写应当。认定作出、后来又认定无须维持，都在进口一侧自己的规则与执行之内；这正是第七篇所说进口一侧那一份的落处（SC14 第一项）。这一例判的是那一类货物的准入，不给那家公司定性。

二 · 罗德里克（B，摘要）

原文。 罗德里克 2000 年《国际经济一体化会走多远？》（Journal of Economic Perspectives 14(1)，177–186）的摘要：「国际经济一体化、民族国家与大众政治不能共存」（"international economic integration, the nation-state, and mass politics cannot co-exist"）；「三者我们只能取其二。」（"We have to pick two out of three."）（据摘要；正文本篇未取到。2011 年的书《全球化的悖论》，本篇只见到出版物页的书介，不引。）

共同的问题。 跨境的规则与一国之内的问责不在同一层时，问的通道归谁。

同在。 跨境的安排与一国之内的追问有张力：这个系列也说，国与国这一层没有替双方立法的第三者，条约在适用范围内约束缔约各方，执行与追问经各方自己的法（第七篇 SC15，承国家论 N24）。

分在。 罗德里克提出的，是制度条件之间的兼容性约束（摘要）。这个系列在这一处给出的，是规范的次序与责任的地址：条约的承诺，国内的供给与监管，仲裁及其落实，分别由相应的位置说明、受问；线所在的人发动不了的程序，不是他们的通道，他们的路留在本国之内（SC15）。这三问与罗德里克的三个条件不一一对应；把三问分开写清，答不了三个条件能不能同时维持。这样的区分不消除兼容性的难题；在具体冲突里怎样调整承诺、制度安排与实现方式，本篇没有一般解。"线不进取舍表"说的是这个系列不把什么当作可牺牲的项，不证明其余的制度调整已经有解（自担）。

对方最强的反驳（本篇代拟）。 三项制度条件若不能同时维持，守线的规范次序仍没有给出相容的实现安排。在具体冲突里，究竟调整哪一项承诺或制度安排，调整以后怎样仍能守线？把责任与追问的地址分别写清，还没有回答这一点。条约已经约束本国时，本国的追问通道又怎样处理这些约束给守线带来的困难？

回应。 这一处这个系列认作限度：仲裁能做什么、限度与失灵处在哪里，分别写明（SC15 末）。本国的路改不了已成的条约义务；它能问本国就这一项承诺作了什么，问供给者与它的监管就供给作了什么；中止或退出另照国与国的规则核（国家论 N24）。在一项具体冲突里调整的是承诺、安排还是实现方式，由作出决定的位置说明，可被问；一般解，本篇没有（自担）。在这里，罗德里克是给这个系列的实现条件施压的对话者。

现实接口（A）。 Azurix 诉阿根廷（ICSID 案号 ARB/01/12），依美国—阿根廷双边投资协定。特许协议给 Azurix 的子公司 ABA 布宜诺斯艾利斯省为期 30 年的饮用水分配与污水处理特许（裁决第 41 段）。2006 年 7 月 14 日的裁决（第 442 段）：阿根廷不违反协定第 IV(1) 条（征收）；违反第 II(2)(a) 条，未给公平公正待遇，也未给充分保护与安全；违反第 II(2)(b) 条，采取了任意措施；赔偿 165,240,753 美元，付半年复利；其余请求驳回。2009 年 9 月 1 日送达的撤销决定：「阿根廷共和国的撤销申请全部驳回。」（"The application for annulment of the Argentine Republic is dismissed in its entirety."）并终止对裁决执行的中止（据裁决与撤销决定的英文原件）。

读法。 裁决答的是国家对这一项投资的措施违不违反协定；撤销申请被驳回，这一决定在本案终局。这一审查本身可以涉及价格与监管措施。这个系列把三问分开，是归责的安排，不是说三件事在事实上互不接触。裁决怎样落实，本篇没有核，不写。这一例只作接口，不把它写成支持或反对国家规制。

四 · 本系列停下的地方

分六格登记，照政治经济学 Paper 0。六格区别的是未继续的性质，不是每个系列都要填满的六个空格。

第一格，这一次构自己留下的余项。 门槛以下的那一段由什么安排承接守线与受问；多家供给时"够不够"由立规、执规的构怎样核，用什么方法（第一篇）。一处的条件与同一处的门槛怎样一起读，只给了方向，没有方法；落在同一个人身上的那一面，在一项具体的规则里怎样与落在别人身上的那一面分开（第二篇）。关口处"不作决定"怎样受问（第三篇）。一条旧规则落到新路上以后由谁来重读；协调落在四条路以外的（第四篇）。依赖一时除不掉时，决定怎样作、怎样答（第六篇）。进口一侧的构就它所及的境外的人"作了什么"怎样核（第七篇）。前置篇往前走的那一步，找不到决定者的结果问它所在的制度作了什么，在各篇的事例里受检验，没有一般的检验程序。

第二格，辖域相邻的对象。 价格理论；税；关税、贸易政策与汇率；制裁与封锁；国家之间的协作体系；公司内部。

第三格，已经到头的结论。 市场不是一个构，说给它的应当是空的（前置篇第一节）。目的王国方向的市场会不会占上风，这个系列不倚靠：「只承涌现的必然，不承主导的必然」（政治经济学第六篇 E32）。两层互相托起是方向，不是担保（前置篇第五节；第二篇 SC5）。这是这一层能给的，不是还没找到办法。

第四格，研究候选。 罗尔斯《正义新论》§41、§42 与兰格 1938 年的原书核到以后，所有制一节的两问（第六节）。萨茨以平等公民身份为根据的"整个拦住"，与这个系列的线划出的范围同不同（第三节第二小节）。杨的正文核到以后，分担怎样分、各方的份是否相等（第七节第一小节）。罗德里克的正文核到以后，三个条件各指什么（第七节第二小节）。欧肯的垄断局只服从法律，它自己的决定怎样受问，要读全书（第三节第一小节）。Naver 购物案发回以后的结果（第三节第一小节）。

第五格，已有明确地址的手递。 公司论的专论；税；具体的制度论；仲裁与跨境机制的设计，见本节第三小节。

第六格，对照各家才看出来的问题。 四问，八篇都没有答，是对接时才看清的：没有约定时，承担风险能不能独自成为主张回报的根据（马祖卡托一场）；在还没有一个构承接以前，改变结构的共同行动由谁发起、怎样得到承担者（杨一场）；跨境制度条件不能兼容时，具体冲突里调整什么，没有一般解（罗德里克一场）；受作用的人能发动的追问通道，在具体安排里够不够防住捕获，只立了要求（拉丰与梯若尔一场）。另有一处多是同向：守市场的人也受问。欧肯要监督垄断的位置摆脱利害相关者的影响，拉丰与梯若尔从信息与利害读俘获，北卡一案看谁实际坐在作决定的位置上、监督者能不能审内容；差别多在写法上，欧肯给出一种设置，这个系列只立功能。

这一篇停在哪里

本篇不立命题，没有改动八篇的任何一句。它把这个系列与已有的市场理论对接了一遍，照实登记了每一家核到哪一层：萨茨核到整章，马祖卡托核到全文；欧肯据英译节本的几页，拉丰与梯若尔据一篇工作论文，杨、罗德里克据摘要；罗尔斯、兰格只到目录、书评与百科条目；施蒂格勒照专论，只补一问。现实接口各照原始文件写，档次分别标明。对接时核了两问，哪一场落在哪一问，各场照写；重合、追加的问题与分歧分别写明出处；只是问法不同的，照写问法不同。

它没有做的：没有证明哪一种市场形式、哪一种所有制、哪一种监管形态最好，这个系列不在名称之间选；没有替任何一家作完整的回应，核到的范围以外的论证都没有进；伯姆、米勒-阿尔马克、卡恩、博克、凯森与特纳、奥斯特罗姆，以及斯洛博迪安的《全球主义者》，本篇只到转引或没有取到原文，不引。

留下的：第八节的六格，其中第六格的四问，是对接时才看清的。
