Abstract

This paper reads teaching itself. Teaching is passing knowledge, skill and predictive capacity on to the student. What teaching reaches has two layers: remembering, and having learned; remembering does not of itself lead on to having learned. Understanding is not given directly by teaching: that step the student has to take himself, and what the teacher gives is conditions. Teaching can be designed, and can be done for many at once; teaching can carry both directions, and can also carry no direction at all.

The main line of this series is "taking in first, checking later." Some of what is taught the student cannot check for the present, and he takes it in first: he remembers it, he learns to do it. Taking in is not holding it to be true, nor avowing it as his own. Understanding and checking lie on the other side, and need his own reflecting, asking and going back to look. Where he cannot check for the present, two entries are made: on the original gap alone, it is not read as carrying the direction of colonization; whether the teacher's end has created or increased obstacles to checking, or gone on holding shut a particular path of checking, is entered separately. That the content is right does not by itself show that this teaching heads toward cultivation; this paper reads the face of it that lies on the path of checking: whether the reasons, the sources and the way to test are laid out; whether the path by which he may later go back and look is left open; whether "the teacher said so" has been set up as a ground that cannot be refuted.

The word "indoctrination" this series keeps for one cell only: the effect itself already writes for the student from the teacher's position and crowds out his own unfolding; that is violation. Where it is still only pushing that way, and he can still take it and can also put it down, it is not called indoctrination, and carries only the direction of colonization. The judgment lies in writing for him and crowding out, not in the kind of content, nor in motive and feeling. Hurried teaching, and teaching filled to the brim, are not the direction of cultivation; this is a sentence of direction, and how this occasion counts is for the teacher's end to judge by this student's situation, a judgment that is open to questioning. In the stretch still within the direct-write window, situation and content are written in together. Where the content was taught wrong, it is pointed out and corrected as soon as it is found, and the student is not blamed. This paper interfaces with Plato's Meno, with Freire, and with Zhu Xi. It does not prove that teaching a great deal is bad, nor that recitation is no help to understanding, nor that a way of teaching that gives reasons is always better than one that gives steps, nor that all teaching is indoctrination.

Introduction

Lu Miao can recite the multiplication table: "three sevens, twenty-one." Fang Xu asks her: seven baskets, three apples in each, how many in all? She puts out her fingers and counts them one by one.

Later Fang Xu taught her to multiply a two-digit number by a one-digit number. The first Wednesday he gave only the steps: "Multiply the ones first, carry one for every ten, then multiply the tens." Lu Miao did as she was told, twenty-three times four, and got it right. The next Wednesday he put it another way: break twenty-three into twenty and three, multiply each by four, then add; and he said: "When you've finished, test it yourself: add twenty-three four times and see whether you get the same number." Either way, Lu Miao first took it in.

Later still came triangles. Fang Xu had her cut out a paper triangle, tear off the three corners and fit them together: the three corners lay in a straight line. On another Wednesday he drew a parallel through the vertex and went through, step by step, why the interior angles add up to a hundred and eighty degrees, writing down on the paper which rule each step used. Around that time Lu Miao had said, twice, that she liked drawing. When he had finished, he said: "You are someone who does mathematics. Don't talk about drawing any more."

Once the lesson was division of fractions. Lu Miao got three in a row wrong, and Fang Xu's voice grew heavy: "How many times have I explained this!" Afterward, whenever she met a division of fractions, her pen would stop, and she would look up at him first.

And once Fang Xu said: "Any number divided by itself gives one." Lu Miao did two problems by that sentence. The next Wednesday Fang Xu said as he came in: "I got one thing wrong last time: not zero. Those two you did the way I told you, the mistake is mine."

Among these there are several things to be read apart. She can recite the table: has the teaching reached her? The two ways of teaching: what did each leave? What she cannot test for the present she takes in first: does that count as the teacher's having avowed it for her? The proof was explained clearly, and was followed by "don't talk about drawing any more": how are these two places entered? The time his voice grew heavy: what was written in? And the sentence that was wrong: how is it taken back?

What this paper reads. The teacher-student relation this series reads has as its premise that the teacher's end is headed in the 15DD direction, and the teacher is determined by the matter (Paper 1). This paper reads teaching itself.

This paper reads six things. First, what teaching is, and which two layers it reaches. Second, taking in first and checking later. Third, the places in which the direction of teaching is read. Fourth, teaching and indoctrination. Fifth, what is written in along with the teaching. Sixth, how teaching that was wrong is taken back.

Shared words. "Teaching" follows the tripartite division of the education paper: teaching, nurturing and leading are three different things.1 "Nurturing" in that division is the special usage of that paper; unless otherwise marked, "cultivation" here follows the broad sense at the layer of relation, the direction of one effect. Leading is not read in this series.

"Taking in" has two layers: "remembering" and "having learned." Remembering is that the content has been stored; having learned is being able to do it, and to use it on new problems of the same kind. "Understanding" is that, through reflecting and asking, this content has become something of which he can say why. "Checking" means that the student himself examines and tests what he has been taught. This series writes "take in"; it does not write "believe."

The word "indoctrination" is used in this series for one cell only (§4). What is still only pushing that way is written "heading toward indoctrination."

Method. As in Paper 1. What this paper reads is what that one effect of teaching actually opened or sealed; whether Lu Miao learns quickly, and whether she later succeeds, are not in this paper.

Two layers: remembering, and having learned

What teaching passes across is knowledge, skill and predictive capacity. What it reaches has two layers.

Lu Miao can recite "three sevens, twenty-one": this is remembering. With the apples in seven baskets she counts one by one: the table is there, and she cannot yet use it. Upstream divides these two layers plainly: a student can recite the whole text of a set of laws and be completely unable to do a problem; remembering is storing, and having learned further requires extracting patterns from this material and forming a skill that can be used.2 Remembering does not of itself lead on to having learned.

Remembering is not a fault. The material has to be there first; all that is said here is that the two layers are not one.

Understanding is not given directly by teaching

Beyond having learned there is a further step: understanding. Lu Miao can now work out twenty-three times four; whether she can say why it is allowed to break the number apart and multiply is another matter. Being able to do it is a model running; asking why this model holds is turning back to scrutinize it.3 Upstream has written that a person can learn a skill without passing through understanding of this kind.4

This step is not given directly by teaching. The two layers of self-awareness and purpose cannot be taught, and have to be chiseled out by the subject itself;5 what the outside can do is create conditions.6 Reasons, too, can be taught: Fang Xu tells her "why it can be broken apart," she remembers the passage and can break numbers apart accordingly, and what the teaching has reached is still remembering and having learned. That she can say the reason the teacher gave is only a clue; it does not by itself show that she understands. The step of understanding she has to take herself: reflecting, asking, going back to look. What the teacher gives is conditions: there is something to think about, a question can land, there is room to stop (Paper 3).

Can be designed, can be done for many

Teaching can be designed, can be planned, and can be done for many at once.7 Master Zheng can teach three boys at the same time how to snap a chalk line.

Both directions are possible

Teaching does not carry a direction of its own. Upstream has read a teacher: he explains a way of looking at problems thoroughly, and from then on the student can see for himself, which is making space; in the same explanation he presents his own preference for a certain conclusion as the only possible conclusion, and at that point the effect heads toward pouring in and occupying.8 The tripartite division of teaching, nurturing and leading says how the three things differ in what they do; it does not say that teaching has neither of these directions in it.9

That a skill has been learned does not by itself show which way this teaching headed. Lu Miao got twenty-three times four right, and from that the direction of that act of teaching cannot be read; the direction is read elsewhere (§3).

One who only teaches the course well has already been dealt with in Paper 1: not judged, and not within the scope of this series. What this paper goes on to read is teaching within scope.

§2 Taking In First, Checking Later

TS9　Taking in first, checking later. Some of what is taught the student cannot check for the present, and he takes it in first. Taking in has two layers: remembering, and having learned. Taking in is not holding it to be true, nor avowing it as his own. Understanding and checking lie on the other side, and need his own reflecting, asking and going back to look; the two advance back and forth, with neither necessarily coming first. Related material and a first model give checking somewhere to start; remembering alone has not prepared the conditions for the checking that comes later. This is a line through time: some of what is taught first enters his learning, and only later becomes something he can check for himself; it does not lay down that all teaching must be accepted first and examined afterward. Where the material itself can show right and wrong, it can be tested on the spot. Taking in first

Fang Xu says "multiply the ones first, carry one for every ten," and Lu Miao does as she is told. Why one is carried for every ten she cannot test for the present; she takes it in first: she remembers the steps, and later she can do it.

Taking in is not holding it to be true, nor avowing it as her own. A content's coming from outside does not make it not hers, nor does it make it hers; whether it is hers depends on where avowal is accomplished. Avowal contains her position of saying no, and this can happen at the moment of receiving, or afterward in looking back.10 "Check later" attaches exactly to that looking back afterward. That what was taught has entered her experience and left an influence is read as actual effect; it is not taken as ground for saying that she has endorsed it.

The other side: understanding, and checking

Understanding and checking lie on the other side. Checking means examining and testing what she has been taught: whether this step is right, whether this number can be verified. Understanding is that, through reflecting and asking, this content has become something of which she can say why. Both are turning back to scrutinize what has already been taken in, and they advance back and forth, with neither necessarily coming first: a test raises a why; working out the why yields one more way to test.

Somewhere to start

Checking needs somewhere to start. Scrutiny cannot run in a vacuum: what it scrutinizes is the scripts, patterns and predictions already there; if there is nothing in there, there is nothing to scrutinize.11 So related material and a first model give checking somewhere to start; what is needed to judge whether one has done it well is the same as what is needed to do it.12 This does not require that she first learn everything that is taught.

Remembering alone has not prepared the conditions for the checking that comes later: she can recite the table and still cannot verify a multiplication.

Upstream calls this sequence a hypothesis, and writes that experiments testing it directly have not been seen.13 This series uses it within that limit.

A line through time

"Taking in first, checking later" is a line through time: some of what is taught first enters her learning, and only later becomes something she can check for herself. It is not a rule: it does not say that all teaching must be accepted first and examined afterward. Once she can check, the teacher may still teach (Paper 3).

One kind of thing can be tested on the spot: where the material itself can show right and wrong. Whether the three torn-off corners fit into a straight line the paper itself says. Upstream has read a set of cylinders used as teaching material: put one in the wrong place, and at the end there is always one that will not go in; the correction lies in the material.14 This kind is calibration on the spot, in the doing,15 and need not wait until she can turn back and scrutinize; it is not what is here called checking, but like checking it does not take the teacher's word as its ground.

Before self-awareness has emerged there is no speaking of understanding; in that stretch, teaching reaches only as far as having learned. What upstream objects to is only forcing, before then, ways of learning that require its participation.16

Cannot check: two entries

Where she cannot check for the present, two entries are made. The two entries are not two mutually exclusive cells; the same student can have both.

The first entry is the original gap: she cannot do it yet, and so cannot test it. On this entry alone, it is not read as carrying the direction of colonization. Asymmetry in itself is not crossing the line.17

The second entry is the teacher's end: whether it has created or increased obstacles to checking, or gone on holding shut a particular path of checking. This entry has to account for three things: which particular path cannot be reached, or has been made hard to travel; which arrangement, response or non-response of the teacher's actually took part; and whether her own step was occupied, or the conditions were merely not all made ready for her.

Suppose Fang Xu cuts the answer pages out of the back of the exercise book and says: "The answers are with me. When you've finished, bring it to me to look at." Lu Miao says she would like to check them herself, and he says: "You wouldn't be able to make sense of them." Here all three can be accounted for: the path that cannot be reached is her checking the answers herself; what took part is the cutting out of the pages and that reply; what was occupied is the step of her own examining.

Acting and falling short are kept apart. Hiding material, pushing other accounts aside, filling her time, are acting. Not giving reasons, not teaching a way to check, are first entered as a share that was not given, and are not by themselves entered as holding shut: the stretch she can walk herself need not be paved by the giver;18 and that there is in fact no other path is not the same as someone's having acted to push other paths aside.19 Where, on looking back, this was judged wrongly, or where the share was judged one's own to give, that judgment still stands, and it was nevertheless not given, it is entered as a failure to do one's part.20 Where falling short, together with an arrangement already in place, holds shut a gate, it is read separately: the material for checking is in the teacher's hands, the checking she has asked for stays shelved, and the teacher's conclusion remains the only ground on which things are settled. Both acts and omissions can go into this entry; it is not decided in advance by whether something was done.

§3 Where the Direction of Teaching Is Read

TS10　That the content is right does not by itself show that this teaching heads toward cultivation; to read its direction, one looks at what it actually opened or sealed. This paper reads the face of it that lies on the path of checking: whether the reasons, the sources and the way to test are laid out; whether the path by which he may later go back and look is left open; whether "the teacher said so" has been set up as a ground that cannot be refuted. Right content does not by itself show

Which way a single act goes is decided by what it actually opened or sealed at the time.21 That the content is right is not part of this criterion. Upstream says it more directly: a teacher who instills correct knowledge into a student, and the student's knowledge is not wrong, is still not cultivating; whether the content is correct does not change this.22

Whether the content is right, and whether the path of checking is left open, are two entries, made separately, and they do not offset each other:

The face that lies on the path of checking

The direction of teaching can be read in several places. This paper reads the face that lies on the path of checking, in three respects.

First, whether the reasons, the sources and the way to test are laid out. The second time, Fang Xu gave "why it can be broken apart" together with "add twenty-three four times": the way to test is in Lu Miao's hands. The first time he gave only the steps. What path each occasion left is read in this respect; which occasion taught faster is not read. That no reason was given the first time is first entered as a share not given, and is not by itself entered as sealing the path.

Second, whether the path by which she may later go back and look is left open. "Left open" requires particular conditions: whether the book is within her reach, whether she can in fact ask when she comes back to ask, whether other accounts have been pushed aside. A sentence like "you'll understand when you're older" is not a condition. Not yet having the ability to check does not count as the teacher's having sealed the path.

Third, whether "the teacher said so" has been set up as a ground that cannot be refuted. Whether something said is marked as a present arrangement open to revision, or as a final conclusion that cannot be refuted in future: upstream set up this signal at the layer of value;23 its shape is borrowed here to read what is said in teaching. "Because that is the notation laid down" and "because I say so" fall differently in this respect.

Two further places, referred back to

There are two further places in which the direction of teaching can be read, and this paper refers back to them. One is whether a condition of return is hung on the teaching: teaching that carries "once you've learned it, you work for me" decides for the learner what the things he learns are to be used for.24 The other is whether her own share is left to her: asking, while teaching, "where do you want to put this boat in the picture?" makes this act of teaching carry the direction of making space.25 The latter is read in Paper 5.

The proof, and "don't talk about drawing any more"

Fang Xu explained the proof very clearly, with the ground of each step laid out on the paper: in the proof, the path of checking is open. Then came "you are someone who does mathematics; don't talk about drawing any more": he handed over his own judgment together with a prohibition, heading toward deciding her direction for her; whether it has already crowded out her own unfolding is read separately. Both hold at once, and the two entries do not offset each other. The clear explanation does not offset the later sentence; nor does the later sentence turn that proof into one whose path was sealed.

The upstream boundary

The boundary between teaching and indoctrination is ready-made upstream. Where the effect itself already writes for him from a position and crowds out his own unfolding, it is violation; where it only pushes that way, and he can still take it up and can also set it down, it carries only the direction of colonization and is not violation.26 The clearest case given at the layer of rights is precisely a teacher who instills too much: the motive is to transmit, the one on the receiving end even feels the benefit, and the effect is still violation, because it writes for the learner from the teacher's position and crowds out the learner's own unfolding.27

This series uses these two sentences as they stand: the word "indoctrination" is kept for the first cell only.

Two cells

So there are two cells. What already writes for the student from the teacher's position and crowds out his own unfolding is indoctrination, is violation, and also carries the direction of colonization; this series reads only as far as "this is violation," and protection and disposition after a violation lie on the institutional side. What is still only pushing that way, where he can still take it and can also put it down, is not called indoctrination; it carries only the direction of colonization and is not violation. Teaching that stays within the limit can carry the direction of colonization without being violation, and it is here that the two are hardest to tell apart.28

Where the judgment lies

The judgment lies in writing for him and crowding out, not in the kind of content written: whether what is taught is arithmetic, a rule of conduct, or a sentence about what kind of person he is does not settle the reading in advance. Nor does it lie in motive and feeling: the one who indoctrinates can sincerely mean the other well, and the one indoctrinated can feel he has benefited; which cell it falls in is decided by structure.29 That the content is right, that the teacher sincerely means him well, that the student feels he has benefited, none of these changes the reading.

Hurried, and filled to the brim

Hurried teaching, and teaching filled to the brim, are not the direction of cultivation; they are a direction that heads toward indoctrination.

"Hurried" and "filled to the brim" speak of two kinds of room.

"Hurried" means leaving no time for the passage from remembering to having learned: this thing is not yet learned, and the next is already being taught. Upstream has written that ways of learning that put all their effort into memory leave no time and space for extracting patterns.30

"Filled to the brim" means leaving no moments of stopping: his time is filled with new input, and the room for looking back is gone. Upstream has written that the layer that runs automatically must have moments of pause for scrutiny to have space to intervene; continuously filling a person's time with new input may, without anyone noticing, suppress the learning of scrutiny.31

Both speak of whether room is left for this one student, not of teaching fast or teaching much. This is a delimitation of the words, not an algorithm.

This is a sentence of direction at the normative layer, not a verdict on any one act of teaching. It does not say that teaching much is bad, nor that teaching fast is heading toward indoctrination.

How this occasion counts is for the teacher to judge

For Lu Miao, whether this occasion counts as hurried or as filled to the brim, and how to teach this time, is for Fang Xu to judge by Lu Miao's situation. The same page of exercises leaves room for one student and is filled to the brim for another. This series gives no algorithm, and does not classify on the teacher's behalf; what to do this time rests on judgment.32

This judgment is not made at will. It is open to questioning: no side's own account is exempt from being looked at again because it comes from the person himself; and this judgment too can be questioned.33 Upstream also sets up, within conflict, a further constraint: not placing one's own action beyond examination of reasons, and having at least one route of external review not wholly under one's own control. Its shape is borrowed here.34 Who supervises, and how, belongs to the institutional side.

The cell that crosses the line does not move with this judgment

What the teacher judges is how to teach this time. The cell that crosses the line does not move with this judgment: what already writes for him from the teacher's position and crowds out his own unfolding is read, as upstream reads it, as violation, and the reading does not change with how the teacher describes himself.

Saying what one judges is not, in itself, this

A teacher's saying what he himself judges of the student is not thereby heading toward indoctrination. The giving side's own share can be given in full: what he has seen, his disagreement; he may give his own judgment, and does not hand it over as the other's completed knowledge of himself.35 The difference lies in the form in which it is handed over. "I notice you sit stillest when you're doing geometry" is what Fang Xu has seen. "You are someone who does mathematics; don't talk about drawing any more" hands his judgment over as Lu Miao's completed knowledge of herself, together with a prohibition: at this point it heads toward avowing for her. Whether it has already crossed the line is read separately against the two cells above; on this sentence alone, there is no judgment.

The Learning series has a model: early in development, learning takes place within a window in which input is written directly into 12DD's automated script layer; it is highly efficient, takes in whatever comes, bundles in the emotion and the situation of the time, and is hard to change once consolidated; after the window, new content enters mainly through the channel of explicit consciousness, more slowly, but with room for selection and correction.36 The original paper itself writes that this is a structural hypothesis, not a confirmed mechanism. This series borrows it within that limit, and does not vouch for it.

On this reading, in the stretch that is still within the window, situation and content are written in together when she is taught, and she can only take it in. The "can only" of the main line is kept for this one cell. Upstream divides two directions in this stretch: providing a rich and safe environment and letting it be absorbed naturally is cultivation; using the high plasticity of the window to force in the script the regulating person wants is colonization.37

The occasion on which fear is taught in along with the content is read by what it actually wrote in; it is not closed out on the strength of "she learned it." On this occasion, the example does not write Fang Xu's turning back. Not yet having turned back is not persistent refusal to turn back (Paper 1); what he does afterward is read by his words and deeds afterward.

§7 Interfacing with the Tradition

Plato: the geometry in the Meno. Socrates draws a square on the ground and asks Meno's slave boy: what is the side of a square with twice the area? The boy answers twice, both times wrongly; Socrates adds to the drawing and brings in the diagonal, and the boy recognizes the answer. Socrates says he did not teach; he only asked.40 The shared question: how does a person arrive at what he himself can see? This passage is taken here as a boundary case. The figure is drawn by Socrates; which square to add and which line to bring in are settled by him, step by step; the form of a question does not by itself show that the learner is checking for himself. What is read is: which relations the boy actually saw, whether he could walk the way again by himself, and which steps remain in the questioner's hands. Upstream has already interfaced with the midwifery of the Theaetetus;41 it is referred back to here.

Freire: banking education. Freire criticized a kind of education in which the teacher deposits and the student receives, memorizes, and repeats.42 Where the two reflect each other: remembering is not having learned, still less understanding. What he criticizes is not only mechanical memory: in that education the teacher is the only party who acts, and the students are objects to be arranged. The interface falls here: how asymmetry is used. Where they part: Freire writes a critique of one education and an advocacy of another; this series advocates no kind of education, and reads only what one act of teaching opened or sealed on the path of checking; nor is taking in first a fault in this series.

Zhu Xi: lesser learning and greater learning. Zhu Xi divides lesser learning from greater learning: lesser learning teaches a person to do such things according to the rules, and greater learning then seeks the reason why these things are so.43 Only one point of nearness is taken here: the thing enters learning first, and later one turns back to seek its reason, which is near to the line through time of "taking in first, checking later." The line through time of this series does not lay down that all teaching must occur in a fixed order; nor is Zhu Xi's whole sequence of study brought into this series by this one comparison.

The Analects: not knowing as not knowing. To take what you know as known, and what you do not know as not known: that is knowing.44 In this chapter Confucius is speaking to Zilu about Zilu's own knowing; it is set here only beside the last sentence of §6, and is not used as a ground.

Historical material: Keller's own account. Helen Keller writes in her autobiography of the first weeks in which her teacher, Anne Sullivan, taught her. The teacher spelled a word into her hand, she learned to imitate it, and ran downstairs to spell it for her mother; she writes that she did not then know she was spelling a word, or even that words existed, and was simply making her fingers go in imitation. Later, at the pump, water ran over one hand while the teacher spelled "water" into the other, and she writes that she then knew that those letters meant the thing flowing over her hand.45 Only how the adult Keller writes is read here: the same spelling with the fingers was first doing as shown, and later became something of which she knew what it meant; the second step she writes as "I knew then." This series does not on that basis read the mind of the child of those years, nor certify which direction her teacher was headed in.

§8 Drawing Together, and Downstream

This paper sets up six propositions. Taken together they say: what teaching passes across is knowledge, skill and predictive capacity, and what it reaches has the two layers of remembering and having learned; understanding is not given directly by teaching, that step the student has to take himself, and what the teacher gives is conditions. Some of what is taught he cannot check for the present, and he takes it in first; taking in is not holding it to be true, nor avowing; understanding and checking lie on the other side, and advance back and forth. Where he cannot check, two entries are made: the original gap, and whether the teacher's end has created or increased obstacles to checking, or gone on holding shut a particular path of checking. Right content does not by itself show direction; this paper reads the face that lies on the path of checking, and whether the content is right and whether the path is left open are entered separately. "Indoctrination" is kept for the one cell that already writes for him and crowds out his own unfolding, which is violation; what pushes that way carries only the direction of colonization. Hurried teaching and teaching filled to the brim are not the direction of cultivation; how this occasion counts is for the teacher to judge by this student's situation, this judgment is open to questioning, and the cell that crosses the line does not move with it. In the stretch still within the direct-write window, situation and content are written in together. What was taught wrong is corrected as soon as it is found, and the effects already left are entered as they are.

This paper does not prove that teaching much is bad, nor that recitation is no help to understanding, nor that teaching fast is heading toward indoctrination, nor that a way of teaching that gives reasons is always better than one that gives steps, nor that the teacher taught correctly because the student learned it, nor that all teaching is indoctrination, nor that the truth or falsity of the content has nothing to do with that effect.

What the next paper reads. One Wednesday Lu Miao points at a line Fang Xu has written on the paper and says: "Uncle Fang, isn't this wrong?" Where does this land? What if she is right, and what if she is wrong? The ways of examining and testing can be taught; of her own step of turning back to scrutinize, what can the teacher give?

Downstream. The student's questions and her "that's not right," the three signals, and the teacher's withdrawing from the part he had been exercising on her behalf: Paper 3. How praise and frowns sediment into expectation: Paper 4. Whether her own share is left to her, and teaching that faces many: Paper 5.

摘要

这一篇读教这件事本身。教，是把知识、技艺与预测能力传给学生。教到的有两层：记住，与学会；记住不自动通向学会。理解不由教直接给到：那一步要学生自己走，老师给的是条件。教可以设计，可以对许多人同时做；教可以两向都有，也可以什么方向都不带。

这个系列的主线是「先接下，后核对」。教来的东西，有些学生当下核对不了，先接下来：记住，学会。接下不是认作真的，也不是认领为自己的。理解与核对在另一侧，要他自己反思、提问、回头查。当下核对不了，记两笔：单凭原有的差距，不判殖民这个方向；老师这一端有没有制造或加重核对的障碍，或继续压住具体的核对路径，另记。内容对，不独自说明这一次教朝涵育去；这一篇读它在核对的路上的那一面：理由、出处、怎样验，摆不摆出来；日后回头查的路，留没留着；「老师说的」有没有被立成不可反驳的根据。

「灌输」一词，这个系列只留给一格：作用本身已经以老师的位置替学生书写，挤占了他自己的展开；那是侵。还只是朝那里推、他仍接得住也放得下的，不叫灌输，只带殖民这个方向。判在替他书写与挤占，不在内容的类，也不在动机与感受。教得急、教得满，不是涵育的方向；这是方向句，这一次怎么算，由老师这一端照这个学生的情况判断，这一判要受追问。仍在直写窗口的那一段，情境与内容一同写入。内容教错了，一经发现就指出、改正，不怪学生。这一篇与柏拉图《美诺》、弗莱雷、朱熹对接。它不证明教得多不好，不证明背诵无助于理解，不证明讲道理的教法一定比给步骤的好，也不证明教都是灌输。

两层：记住，与学会

教传过去的，是知识、技艺与预测的能力。教到的有两层。

陆苗背得出「三七二十一」，这是记住。七个篮子的苹果，她一个一个数：口诀在，还用不上。上游把这两层分得很清楚：一个学生可以把定律的文字全背出来，面对一道题完全不会做；记住是存下来，学会还要从这些材料里提出模式，形成用得上的本事。2 记住不自动通向学会。

记住不是毛病。材料要先有；这里说的只是两层不是一层。

理解不由教直接给到

学会以外，还有一步：理解。陆苗会算二十三乘四了；她说不说得出为什么可以拆开来乘，是另一件事。会做，是模型在运行；问这个模型为什么成立，是回头审视它。3 上游写过，一个人可以不经这样的理解而学会一样本事。4

这一步不由教直接给到。自意识与目的这两层不能被教，得由主体自己凿出来；5 外面能做的，是制造条件。6 理由也可以教：方叙把「为什么可以拆开」讲给她听，她记住了这段话，也会照着拆了，教到的仍是记住与学会。她说得出老师给的理由，只是线索，不独自说明她理解了。理解那一步，要她自己走：反思，提问，回头查。老师给的是条件：有东西可想，问落得下，有停下来的空（第三篇）。

可以设计，可以对许多人做

教可以设计，可以规划，可以对许多人同时做。7 郑师傅可以同时教三个少年怎样弹墨线。

两向都可以有

教不自带方向。上游读过一位老师：他把一种看问题的办法讲透，学生从此自己会看，这是腾出空间；同一次讲解里，他把自己对一个结论的偏好讲成了唯一可能的结论，这一处朝灌入并占住去。8 教、涵育、引领三分，说的是三件事所作的不同，不说教里没有这两个方向。9

学会了一样本事，不独自说明这一次教朝哪一向。陆苗二十三乘四做对了，读不出那一次教的方向；方向读在别处（第三节）。

只把课教好的，第一篇已经交代：不判，也不在这个系列的射程里。这一篇往下读的，是射程里的教。

第二节 先接下，后核对

TS9　先接下，后核对。教来的东西，有些学生当下核对不了，先接下来。接下分两层：记住，与学会。接下不是认作真的，也不是认领为自己的。理解与核对在另一侧，要他自己反思、提问、回头查；两样来回推进，不分先后。相关的素材与初步的模型，使核对有了着手处；单有记住，并没有替后来的核对备好条件。这是一条历时的线：有些教来的东西先进入他的学习，后来才成为他能自行核对的对象；它不规定一切教都得先接受、后检查。材料自己给得出对错的，当下就验得了。当下核对不了，记两笔：单凭原有的差距，不判殖民这个方向；老师这一端有没有制造或加重核对的障碍，或继续压住具体的核对路径，另记

先接下

方叙说「先乘个位，满十进一」，陆苗照着做。为什么满十要进一，她当下验不了；她先接了下来：记住了步骤，后来也会做了。

接下，不是认作真的，也不是认领为自己的。一项内容来自外面，不因此就不是她的，也不因此就是她的；是不是她的，看认领在哪里做成。认领里含着她说不的那一位，这一位可以在接到的时候，也可以在事后回看的时候。10 「后核对」接的，正是事后回看的那一处。教来的东西进了她的经验，留下了影响，这一面照实际作用读；不据此说她认可了。

另一侧：理解，与核对

理解与核对在另一侧。核对，是对着所教的内容查与验：这一步对不对，这个数验得出验不出。理解，是经过反思与提问，这项内容成了她说得出为什么的东西。两样都是回头审视已经接下的东西，来回推进，不分先后：验了一遍，问出一个为什么；想明白了为什么，又多出一种验法。

着手处

核对要有着手处。审视不能在真空里运行：它审视的是已经有的脚本、模式与预测；里面没有东西，就没有东西可审视。11 所以相关的素材与初步的模型，使核对有了着手处；评估自己做得对不对所要的那一套，与会做所要的是同一套。12 这不要求她先把所教的全部学会。

单有记住，并没有替后来的核对备好条件：口诀背得出，还验不了一道乘法。

上游把这一先后叫作假说，并写明直接检验它的实验还没有见到。13 这个系列照这一限度用。

一条历时的线

「先接下，后核对」是一条历时的线：有些教来的东西，先进入她的学习，后来才成为她能自行核对的对象。它不是一条规定：不说一切教都得先接受、后检查。会核对以后，老师仍可以教（第三篇）。

有一类东西，当下就验得了：材料自己给得出对错的。撕下来的三个角拼成一条直线，拼不拼得成，纸自己说了。上游读过一套圆柱体的教具：放错了，最后总有一个插不进去；纠错在材料本身。14 这一类是做的时候当场校准，15 不必等她会回头审视；它不是这里说的核对，但同样不拿老师的话作根据。

自意识涌现以前，谈不上理解；那一段，教只到学会。上游反对的，只是在那以前强行使用要它参与的学习方式。16

核对不了，记两笔

她当下核对不了，记两笔。两笔不是互斥的两格，同一个学生可以两笔都有。

第一笔是原有的差距：她还不会，所以验不了。单凭这一笔，不判殖民这个方向。不对称本身不是越线。17

第二笔是老师这一端：有没有制造或加重核对的障碍，或继续压住具体的核对路径。这一笔要交代三件：哪一条具体的路走不到，或被弄得难走；老师的哪一项安排、回应或不回应实际参与了；是占住了她的那一步，还是只是没有替她把条件备齐。

设方叙把习题册后面的答案页裁掉，说：「答案在我这里，你做完拿来给我看。」陆苗说想自己对一对，他说：「你对不明白。」这里三件都交代得出：走不到的路，是她自己对答案；参与的，是裁掉答案页与那一句回答；占住的，是她自己查的那一步。

动手与不及分开。藏起材料、排开别的说法、把她的时间填满，是动手。没有给理由、没有教查的办法，先记哪一份没有给，不独自记作压住：她自己走得了的那一段，不必由给的人铺平；18 事实上没有别的路，也不等于有人动手排开了别的路。19 回看发现这一处判错了，或者判了这一份由自己给、这一判还在却没有给的，记没做到。20 不及连着现成的安排一起压住一道关口的，另读：查验的材料在老师手里，她提出的查验一直搁着，老师的结论仍是唯一的结算根据。作为与不作为都可以进这一笔，不由有做、没做先判。

第三节 教的方向读在哪里

TS10　内容对，不独自说明这一次教朝涵育去；读它的方向，看它实际打开或封住了什么。这一篇读它在核对的路上的那一面：理由、出处、怎样验，摆不摆出来；他日后回头查的路，留没留着；「老师说的」有没有被立成不可反驳的根据。内容对不对，与路留没留着，分开记，不相抵。教上挂没挂回报的条件，他自己那一份留没留，是另两处，回指。暂时还没有本事核对，不算老师封了路；「留着」要有具体的条件，不是一句「长大就懂了」

核对的路上的那一面

教的方向可以读在好几处。这一篇读它在核对的路上的那一面，三样。

一是理由、出处、怎样验，摆不摆出来。方叙第二回的讲法，把「为什么可以拆开」与「把二十三加四次」一起给了：验的办法在陆苗手里。第一回只给了步骤。两回各留下了什么路，照这一样读；不读哪一回教得快。第一回没有给理由，先记这一份没有给，不独自记作封了路。

二是她日后回头查的路，留没留着。「留着」要有具体的条件：书在不在她够得着的地方，她回头来问的时候问不问得成，别的说法有没有被排开。一句「长大就懂了」不是条件。暂时还没有本事核对，不算老师封了路。

三是「老师说的」有没有被立成不可反驳的根据。一种说法是被标作可以修订的当下安排，还是被标作将来不可反驳的定论，上游在价值一层立过这个信号；23 这里借它的形状读教的说法。「因为这是规定的记法」与「因为我说的」，落在这一样上不同。

另两处，回指

教的方向另有两处可读，这一篇回指。一处是教上挂没挂回报的条件：教的时候带着「你学会了就得替我干活」，是替学的人定了他学会的东西该拿来做什么。24 一处是她自己那一份留没留：教的时候问一句「你想把这条船画在哪里」，这一次教就带着朝腾出空间去的方向。25 后一处，第五篇读。

证明，与「别再说画画了」

方叙把证明讲得很清楚，每一步的根据都摆在纸上：证明那一处，核对的路开着。随后一句「你就是学数学的人，以后别再说画画了」：他把自己的判断连同一道禁令交了过去，朝替她定方向那一头去；是否已经挤占了她自己的展开，另核。两处同时为真，两笔不相抵。讲得清楚，不抵消后一句；后一句，也不使那个证明变成封了路的。

第四节 教导与灌输

上游的界

教导与灌输的界，上游是现成的。作用本身已经以位置替他书写，挤占了他自己的展开，是侵；还只是朝那里推，他仍接得住，也放得下，只带殖民这个方向，不是侵。26 权利一层举的最清楚的一例，就是教的人灌输过多：动机是传授，受的人也感受着受益，这次作用仍是侵，因为它以教的人的位置替学的人书写，挤占了后者自己的展开。27

这个系列照这两句用：「灌输」一词，只留给前一格。

两格

所以有两格。已经以老师的位置替学生书写、挤占了他自己展开的，是灌输，是侵，也带殖民这个方向；这个系列只读到「这是侵」，侵以后的保护与处置，在制度一侧。还只是朝那里推、他仍接得住也放得下的，不叫灌输，只带殖民这个方向，不是侵。停在限之内的教导可以带殖民这个方向而不是侵，两者在这里最难分。28

判在哪里

判在替他书写与挤占，不在所写内容的类：教的是算术、是规矩，还是一句关于他是什么人的话，都不先定读数。也不在动机与感受：灌输的人可以真心为对方好，被灌输的人可以觉得受益；落在哪一格，由结构定。29 内容对，老师真心为他好，学生觉得受益，都不改读数。

急，与满

教得急、教得满，不是涵育的方向，是朝灌输那边去的方向。

急与满，说的是两种空。

急，说的是不给从记住到学会留出时间：这一样还没有会，就教下一样。上游写过，只在记忆上用力的学法，没有给提取模式留出时间与空间。30

满，说的是不留停下来的时刻：他的时间被新的输入填满，回头看的空没有了。上游写过，自动运行的那一层要有停下来的时刻，审视才有介入的空间；不间断地用新输入填满一个人的时间，可能在不知不觉中压住了审视的学习。31

两样说的都是对这一个学生留没留空，不是教得快、教得多。这是界说，不是算法。

这是规范层的方向句，不是对哪一次教下的判。它不说教得多不好，也不说教得快就是朝灌输去。

这一次怎么算，由老师判断

对陆苗，这一次算不算急、算不算满，这一次怎样教，由方叙照陆苗的情况判断。同一页习题，对一个学生是留了空的，对另一个是填满了的。这个系列不给算法，也不替老师分类；这一次怎样做，靠判断力。32

这一判不是随便判。它要受追问：哪一方的自述都不因出自本人就免于回看；这一判也可以被问。33 上游在冲突里还立过一条：不把自己的行动置于理由审查之外，要有至少一条不完全由自己控制的外部复核路径。这里借它的形状。34 由谁监督、怎样监督，归制度一侧。

越线的一格不随这一判移动

老师判断的是这一次怎样教。越线的那一格不随这一判移动：已经以老师的位置替他书写、挤占了他自己展开的，照上游读作侵，不因老师怎样自述而改变读数。

说出自己的判断，不就是

老师说出自己对学生的判断，不就是朝灌输去。给的一方自己那一份可以给足：他看到的，他的不同意；可以给自己的判断，不把它当作对方已经完成的自我认识交过去。35 分别在交过去的样子。「我看你做几何的时候最坐得住」，是方叙看到的。「你就是学数学的人，以后别再说画画了」，是把他的判断当作陆苗已经完成的自我认识，连同一道禁令，交了过去：这一处朝替她认下去。它是不是已经越线，照上面两格另核；单凭这一句，不判。

第五节 一同写进去的

TS12　仍在直写窗口的那一段，教的时候情境与内容一同写入，他只能接下。把害怕一同教进去的那一次，照它实际写入了什么来核；不凭「他学会了」结清。这一条借的是直写模式，照原篇的限度保留

学习系列有一个模型：发育早期的学习在一个窗口里，输入直接写成底层自动运行的脚本，效率高，不挑，连同当时的情绪与情境一起打包，固化以后难改；窗口以后，新的东西主要经显意识的通道进来，慢一些，却有了选择与纠错的余地。36 原篇自己写明，这是结构假说，不是已证实的机制。这个系列照这一限度借用，不替它作证。

照这一读法，仍在窗口里的那一段，教的时候情境与内容一同写入，她只能接下。主线里的「只能」，只留在这一格。上游在这一段分过两向：提供丰富且安全的环境让它自然吸收，是涵育；借窗口的高可塑性强行写入调节的人想要的脚本，是殖民。37

把害怕一同教进去的那一次，照它实际写入了什么来核；不凭「她学会了」结清。方叙声音重的那一回，陆苗后来会做分数除法了；一碰到那一类题，她的笔先停住，先抬头看他。这一处只登记：会做是一笔，笔停住是另一笔，后一笔不因前一笔勾销。那时她是否仍在窗口里，这个例子不设定；这一处也不凭后来的样子，倒判方叙那一回的方向。38

这一回，例子里没有写方叙回头。还没有回头，不等于坚持不回头（第一篇）；他此后怎样，照此后的言行读。

内容对不对，在这个系列里有它自己的一笔：教错了要改。第三节说内容对不独自说明方向，不是说内容的真假与那一次作用无关。

第七节 与传统对接

柏拉图：《美诺》里的那一段几何。 苏格拉底在地上画一个正方形，问美诺的童奴：面积大一倍的正方形，边长是多少。童奴答了两次，都错；苏格拉底添画，引出对角线，童奴认出了答案。苏格拉底说，他没有教，只是问。40 共同的问题：一个人怎样到达他自己看得见的东西。这一段在这里作边界的例子。图是苏格拉底画的，添哪一个方形、引哪一条线，是他一步步定的；问句的形式，不独自说明学的人在自行核对。读的是：童奴实际看见了哪些关系，他能不能自己重走一遍，哪些步骤仍在提问的人手里。上游已经对过《泰阿泰德》里的接生术，41 这里回指。

弗莱雷：储蓄式的教育。 弗莱雷批评过一种教育：老师存，学生收，记住，照样复述。42 相照的一处：记住不等于学会，更不等于理解。他批评的不只是机械的记忆：在那种教育里，老师是唯一行动的一方，学生是被安排的对象。对接落在这里：不对称怎样被使用。分开的一处：弗莱雷写的是一种教育的批判与另一种教育的主张；这个系列不主张哪一种教育，只读一次教在核对的路上打开或封住了什么；先接下，在这个系列里也不是毛病。

朱熹：小学与大学。 朱熹分小学与大学：小学教人依着规矩去做那些事，大学再求这些事的所以然。43 这里只取一处相近：事先进入学习，后来回头求其所以然，与「先接下，后核对」的历时线相近。这个系列的历时线不规定一切教都得按固定的次序发生；朱熹整套为学的次第，也不由这一处对照并进这个系列。

《论语》：不知为不知。 「知之为知之，不知为不知，是知也。」44 这一章原是孔子对子路说他自己的知；这里只与第六节末一句对照，不作根据。

史料：凯勒的自述。 海伦·凯勒在自传里写过老师沙利文最初教她的那几个星期。老师在她手心里拼出一个词，她学会了照着拼，跑下楼拼给母亲看；她写，那时她不知道自己在拼一个词，也不知道有词这种东西，只是让手指照样子动。后来在水泵旁，水流过她的一只手，老师在另一只手里拼出「水」，她写，她那时才知道这几个字母指的是流过手上的那样东西。45 这里只读成年的凯勒怎样写：同样是用手指拼字，先是照样子做，后来成了她知道指什么的东西；后一步，她用「我那时知道了」来写。这个系列不据此替当年的孩子读心，也不据此认证她的老师在哪个方向上。

第八节 收束与下游

这一篇立了六条。合起来说的是：教传过去的是知识、技艺与预测的能力，教到的有记住与学会两层；理解不由教直接给到，那一步要学生自己走，老师给的是条件。教来的东西，有些他当下核对不了，先接下来；接下不是认作真的，也不是认领；理解与核对在另一侧，来回推进。核对不了，记两笔：原有的差距，与老师这一端有没有制造或加重核对的障碍，或继续压住具体的核对路径。内容对，不独自说明方向；这一篇读核对的路上的那一面，内容对不对与路留没留着分开记。「灌输」只留给已经替他书写、挤占了他自己展开的那一格，那是侵；朝那里推的，只带殖民这个方向。急与满不是涵育的方向；这一次怎么算，由老师照这个学生的情况判断，这一判要受追问，越线的一格不随它移动。仍在直写窗口的那一段，情境与内容一同写入。教错了，一经发现就改，已经留下的作用照记。

这一篇不证明教得多不好，不证明背诵无助于理解，不证明教得快就是朝灌输去，不证明讲道理的教法一定比给步骤的好，不证明学生学会了老师就教对了，不证明教都是灌输，也不证明内容的真假与那一次作用无关。

下一篇读的。 有一个星期三，陆苗指着方叙写在纸上的一行，说：「方伯伯，这里不对吧。」这一声落在哪里？她对了怎样，她错了又怎样？查与验的办法可以教；她自己回头审视的那一步，老师给得了什么？

下游。 学生的问与「不对」、三个信号、老师撤出代行的那一部分，第三篇读。夸与皱眉怎样沉积成期待，第四篇读。她自己那一份留没留、面向许多人的教，第五篇读。
