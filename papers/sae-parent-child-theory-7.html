Handing Over and Leading
交出与引领

Han Qin (秦汉) · Independent Researcher · 2026
DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.24121559 · Full PDF on Zenodo · CC BY 4.0
Abstract

This paper reads the last stretch of the parent-child relation this series reads: coming of age, handing over, and leading. Coming of age is read structurally as the child's 14DD coming to stand: his own cannot-not has taken shape; it arrives domain by domain, not all on one day; coming of age in law is acknowledged but not judged. Where, in some matter, his negation-gate can already run of itself, the position of raising and refusing in that matter becomes his own at once, and no one may seal it; this is not further conditioned on his 14DD already having come to stand. Being able to raise something is not the same as having it come to stand: whether the arrangement in that matter changes, if it also involves the parents' end or others, is read by each party's own law and by the dealings. In matters of his own direction, the parents do not interpret or avow in his place; how the securing layer is drawn back is not conditional on which road he takes, and in drawing back the two senses of dependency are checked.

On the child's side, the test is that he is able to unfold without revolving around his father, and also able to unfold without revolving around his mother, read person by person; this is a test, not that he must in fact leave; being able is limited to constitutive independence, not real-world self-sufficiency. Once 14DD has come to stand, what remains is mainly leading: the effect of leading can only be by personal demonstration; it is not a position with authority, and does not extend the structure of the parent-child relation; this does not widen into all later dealings being allowed only demonstration; ordinary exchange and things done together are read by Relation Theory and Cooperation Theory. The child's own 15DD is his own affair.

Leading is read until it becomes one face of family affection between adults; no endpoint is set. From then on, what lies between parents and adult children is read by Relation Theory and Cooperation Theory, and conflict by the Conflict Treatise; the case of long-term care needed after coming of age is handed to a future series on relations of care. This paper interfaces with Locke, Kant and Gibran, and registers the material of Fu Lei's Letters. It does not prove that a child ought to leave once grown, nor that leading is bound to be seen, nor that the parent-child relation has an endpoint.

Introduction

When He Zhou had finished his schooling, he went to a port city in the south and became an apprentice at a yard that built and repaired wooden boats. The day he left, Lin Wan packed two quilts for him, and He Chuan drove him to the station. At the station entrance, He Chuan said: "Call when you get there."

In the first few months, He Zhou once said on the phone that the yard paid its apprentices very little; the next day He Chuan phoned the owner of the yard himself, and asked whether the boy could be paid a bit more. The owner mentioned it to He Zhou. That evening He Zhou phoned home, his voice low: "Dad, this is my business." He Chuan was silent for a moment, then said: "I overstepped." He did not call again after that. In the first year, Lin Wan still sent him a little money every month; he also often asked her on the phone how to save money, and how to spend it.

More than a year later, He Zhou was about to sign a contract with a yacht company, and was unsure about one clause in it; he phoned He Chuan. He Chuan had handled contracts at his company for more than ten years, and could make sense of the clause; he gave his view, and then said: "Whether to sign is up to you."

That year, Lin Wan signed up for a drawing class at the cultural center near home, on Saturday mornings. On a video call she showed He Zhou the sea she had painted, said it wasn't good, and laughed at herself. He Zhou said: "Mom, the mast on that boat of yours is crooked." Lin Wan said: "It is crooked."

Several things in these have to be read apart. From what day was He Zhou "of age"; what was He Chuan's call to the owner of the yard; where does He Zhou's "this is my business" fall; was the contract a matter of He Chuan deciding for He Zhou, or something else; what does Lin Wan's learning to draw have to do with He Zhou; and are this family's later dealings still the parent-child relation this series reads?

What this paper reads. The parent-child relation this series reads has as its premise that the end of the father or the mother is headed in the 15DD direction, and it is read relation by relation.1 This paper reads the last stretch of this relation: once the child's cannot-not has come to stand in one matter after another, how the parents' end acts.

This paper reads five things. First, coming of age: what it is read as structurally, and where coming of age in law is placed. Second, raising and having it come to stand: when the position of raising and refusing becomes his, what is handed over, and how securing is drawn back. Third, how "being able to unfold without revolving around me" is read on the child's side. Fourth, leading. Fifth, this relation turning into family affection between adults.

Shared words. "Coming of age" has two senses in this series: coming of age structurally, read as the child's 14DD coming to stand; and coming of age in law, a line drawn by institutions, which this series acknowledges but does not judge. Where the main text says "coming of age" without further marking, it means the former.

"Handing over" means the interpretations and avowals the parents' end used to hold for the child being given back to him, domain by domain. The initiation of questioning is not within "handing over": it is not something the parents hand him; it is his as soon as the gate runs of itself.

"Leading" follows the education paper's usage: the adult lives out recognition, and the child sees the direction.2 It is not leadership, not a position with authority.

"Family affection" is only a name here: it means the dealings between parents and adult children, read by Relation Theory and Cooperation Theory; this series gives it no definition of its own.

Method. As in the previous six papers. This paper writes of the years after He Zhou left home; the main text gives no ages.

§1 Coming of Age

PC38 Coming of age is read structurally as the child's 14DD coming to stand: his own cannot-not has taken shape. It arrives domain by domain, not all on one day; coming of age in law is acknowledged but not judged

His own cannot-not has taken shape

The clues the parents' end has seen are these: stopped, quarrelled with, pressed down, it came back from elsewhere (Paper 5). What follows takes as its condition that He Zhou's cannot-not in boatbuilding has already taken shape, and reads how the parents' end acts; his sustained action before this is the clue the parents saw, and certification is not completed by the day he got on the train. The series reads coming of age as this: the child's 14DD comes to stand; his own cannot-not has taken shape.

This reading, like those of the earlier papers, reads the conditions at the parents' end: once the child's cannot-not has come to stand in a matter, how the parents act in that matter changes accordingly.

Domain by domain

Coming of age does not arrive all on one day. He Zhou's direction in boatbuilding cannot vouch for other matters as well. He asked Lin Wan how to save money, and asked He Chuan to look over a contract; these show only that he used other people's help, not that the position of raising, refusing and revising was still exercised by his parents on his behalf. How the position operates in each matter is still checked matter by matter. In a person who is still maturing, that gate can in some places already run of itself, while in others it must still be exercised by others for him.3 Using a line that is externally fixed, not defeasible by the facts of a case, and applied uniformly across matters, to judge in a single day that he has arrived in every matter, carries a risk of systematic misclassification.4 So when this series says "coming of age," it means coming of age in one matter after another.

Coming of age in law

How the law draws the line of coming of age, and how the corresponding authority changes, belongs to the institutional side; this series acknowledges but does not judge. Nor does it rule on whether any particular age set by a ruling is right: that is left to the specific disciplines and to social judgment.5 Coming of age in law and coming of age structurally need not fall on the same day; this series reads the latter, and does not use it to change the former.

§2 Raising, and Having It Come to Stand

PC39 Where, in some matter, his negation-gate can already run of itself, the position of raising and refusing in that matter becomes his own at once, and no one may seal it; this is not further conditioned on his 14DD already having come to stand. Being able to raise something is not the same as having it come to stand: whether the arrangement in that matter changes, if it also involves the parents' end or others, is read by each party's own law and by the dealings, and is not decided by any one party alone. In matters of his own direction, the parents do not interpret or avow in his place; once his cannot-not has come to stand in that matter, leading takes up from there. How the securing layer is drawn back is not conditional on which road he takes; in drawing back, actual dependency, the arrangements already formed, and the two senses of dependency are checked. On the side that has already turned into a relation between adults, what the parents give from then on is read as help between adults, or as something done together

When the gate runs of itself, the position is his

In a matter, once a child's negation-gate takes a stable first-person ownership, the initiation of questioning in that matter becomes his own at once.6 This position does not wait for his cannot-not to come to stand: before He Zhou had set out on the road of boatbuilding, he could already say "not wearing it" to that coat, and ask "why can't I watch for ten minutes." In these matters, the position of raising and refusing was already his, and no one may seal it; whoever seals his position of expression or channel of exit commits a violation.7

So handing over does not begin on the day of coming of age. The position of raising and refusing, in one matter after another, became his long before coming of age; what the parents' end hands over is something else: the interpretations and avowals it used to hold for him (Paper 4).

Raising, and having it come to stand

Being able to raise something is not the same as having it come to stand.8 For a while He Zhou talked of giving up his schooling and going straight to the yard. He could raise this, and no one could stop him saying it. But the matter involved other things: the family's money, the law at the parents' end, whether the yard would take an apprentice who had not finished his schooling. Whether the arrangement in that matter changes is read by each party's own law and by the dealings, and is not decided by any one party alone. That time, the three parties talked it over for a long while, and the result was that he finished. He Zhou raised it, and it did not come to stand; his position of raising was not sealed because of that.

It runs the other way too: that the gate runs of itself does not mean the child should decide everything himself; that would be counting the having-it-come-to-stand to him as well.8

His own direction

In matters of He Zhou's own direction, the parents do not interpret or avow in his place. What the yard pays its apprentices falls on the road he chose himself; it is a matter on that road. He Chuan's call to the owner of the yard raised on He Zhou's behalf something He Zhou had not raised himself: more pay. This matter, for He Zhou, was already his own; He Chuan raising it for him was avowing a matter of his own in his place, and was a falling back. He Zhou said "this is my business": this sentence falls exactly on this matter. He Chuan said "I overstepped," and did not call again: owning up to that occasion, and changing what he did afterward; this is a turning back (Paper 1).

Once his cannot-not has come to stand in that matter, leading takes up from there (§4).

How securing is drawn back

After He Zhou went to the yard, Lin Wan still sent him a little money every month; for the first half year or more, then less, then later he said there was no need. How these payments changed is checked against his actual dependency, the arrangements already formed, and how those arrangements changed; it is not inferred directly from his having come to stand in boatbuilding that securing may be drawn back.

Drawing back has two lines. First, it is not conditional on which road he takes: Lin Wan's giving this share is not conditional on He Zhou switching to engineering, nor is it withdrawn because he keeps on with boatbuilding; the particular payments are still checked against actual dependency and the arrangements already formed. Securing in the Base Layer is not conditional on a person's taking some particular road.9 Second, it is checked against dependency: in drawing back, one looks at whether he still actually relies on this share, and at what arrangements have already formed. Where one first induces dependency and then withdraws, violation is checked separately at the layer of basic rights (Paper 2).10 Had Lin Wan, in He Zhou's first month at the yard, suddenly given nothing at all, without a word, that would be checked by these two lines.

The side that has turned into a relation between adults

The contract: He Zhou came to ask He Chuan; He Chuan gave his view, and then said "whether to sign is up to you." This matter is no longer something the parents hold for the child; it is one adult asking another adult for help looking over a contract. On the side that has already turned into a relation between adults, what the parents give from then on is read as help between adults, or as something done together: of a thing parents and their adult children do together, the face of doing it together is in Cooperation Theory.11

§3 Being Able to Unfold Without Revolving Around Me

PC40 On the child's side, the test is that he is able to unfold without revolving around his father, and also able to unfold without revolving around his mother, read person by person; this is a test, not that he must in fact leave; being able is limited to constitutive independence, not real-world self-sufficiency

The test is on the child's side

The layer of meaning has a test: that the other is able to unfold without revolving around me is the hardest test in this row.12 Marriage Theory, reading two people and their child, left how this test is read on the child's side to Parent-Child Theory.13

Read on the child's side, the test is: He Zhou is able to unfold without revolving around He Chuan, and also able to unfold without revolving around Lin Wan. Reading person by person means checking separately whether He Chuan and Lin Wan, each in their own relation, occupy the constitutive position of He Zhou's value.14 That He Zhou still asks Lin Wan's advice and still takes her support is not enough to judge that he still revolves around her; what is checked is whether his direction and his first-person revision are still decided for him by her. Two relations, two readings.

Being able, not having to leave

This is a test, not that he must in fact leave.15 That He Zhou went south is his own affair; had he stayed at a yard near home and come back for dinner every weekend, the test could be read just the same: whether his affairs are able to unfold without revolving around his parents. Having left does not prove being able; not having left does not prove not being able.

Constitutive independence, not real-world self-sufficiency

Being able is limited to constitutive independence, not real-world self-sufficiency.16 In the first year, He Zhou's money did not go far enough and he asked home for some; when there was an accident at the yard, he phoned home. These do not make the test fail: that he cannot do without some real-world support does not give his parents the constitutive right over his value. What is read is whether who he is, and how his affairs go, are still decided by his parents.

§4 Leading

PC41 Once 14DD has come to stand, what remains is mainly leading: the effect of leading can only be by personal demonstration; it is not a position with authority, and does not extend the structure of the parent-child relation. This does not widen into all later dealings between parents and adult children being allowed only demonstration; ordinary exchange and things done together are read by Relation Theory and Cooperation Theory. The child's own 15DD is his own affair

Only by personal demonstration

Of teaching, nurturing and leading, leading comes last: the window for leading is after 14DD has stabilized. There is only one way to lead: the adult stands at the 15DD position, and the child, seeing this, knows that position exists. Leading is possible only by personal demonstration.17

Lin Wan went to the cultural center to learn drawing. As a girl she had wanted to learn to draw, and her family had not allowed it (Paper 3); that year, she went herself. She showed He Zhou her painting, He Zhou said the mast was crooked, and she answered "It is crooked." In this, there is not one "you should" said to He Zhou. This scene is first read as dealings between adults; whether there is leading in it as well depends on how she lives out recognition in her own life and dealings, and is not settled directly by her having an activity of her own. What He Zhou's end can see, and whether he saw it, lie with him.

Read by scope, "standing at the 15DD position" is read as which way operations go, one after another, and falling back is read as it occurs (Paper 1).

Not a position with authority

Leading is not a position with authority. It gives parents no standing to decide matters for an adult child; nor does it extend the structure of the parent-child relation: when the child has come of age and the parent still settles his affairs with "I'm your father," that structure is being stretched out. He Chuan phoning the owner of the yard was, in a matter that was already He Zhou's own, treating him as a child not yet of age. Treating an adult as a child who cannot yet judge for himself what is good for him has been read at the political layer: government that decides people's happiness for them out of benevolence is the greatest conceivable despotism.18 What Kant criticizes is government built on this principle; what is borrowed here is only the shape: treating an adult as a child who cannot yet judge for himself what is good for him. He Chuan's phone call was one operation of this shape, not that kind of government.

Not widened into all dealings

That leading can only be by personal demonstration speaks of the effect of leading. It does not widen into all later dealings between parents and adult children being allowed only demonstration. He Chuan and He Zhou talk on the phone about contracts, the weather, the food at the yard; Lin Wan talks with He Zhou on video about the sea she painted. These are ordinary dealings, read by Relation Theory; should He Chuan and He Zhou one day repair a small boat together, that is something done together, read by Cooperation Theory.

The child's own 15DD

The child's own 15DD is his own affair. 15DD cannot be taught, and cannot be nurtured; it emerges in the collision with another 14DD.19 "Nurtured" here follows the special usage of the education paper's tripartite division, and does not cancel other possibilities of cultivation in Relation Theory's broad sense. What the parents can do is live out recognition, so that he sees that the position exists; whether he gets there, and when, and in which matter, lies with him. The door opens from the inside.20

§5 Turning into Family Affection

PC42 Leading is read until it becomes one face of family affection between adults; no endpoint is set. From then on, what lies between parents and adult children is read by Relation Theory and Cooperation Theory, and conflict by the Conflict Treatise; the case of long-term care needed after coming of age is handed to a future series on relations of care

No endpoint is set

Leading is read until it becomes one face of family affection between adults. There is no date here: there is no day on which the parents stop leading, nor a day on which family affection begins. The evening Lin Wan was learning to draw and He Zhou said the mast was crooked was dealings between adults; whether there was leading in it as well is still checked by the particular effect. The series reads as far as this, and sets no endpoint.

From then on

From then on, what lies between parents and adult children is read by Relation Theory and Cooperation Theory. Parents and an adult child can, in some particular matter, enter the same reciprocal process, and in that matter there may be mutual chiseling;21 of a thing the two do together, the face of doing it together is in Cooperation Theory.11 Whether a later collision enters the Conflict Treatise is still checked by the two gates Paper 5 spoke of; what has entered is read by the Conflict Treatise.

Long-term care

After coming of age, some people, because of severe disability or for other reasons, need long-term care; some parents, grown old, need care from their adult children. These cases this series does not read; they are handed to a future series on relations of care.

§6 Interfacing with the Tradition

Locke: paternal power ends with the maturity of reason. In Chapter 6 of the Second Treatise of Government, Locke says that the power parents have over their children ends when the children come to be able to use their own reason; after that, the children still owe their parents honor, which is another matter.22 The common question: when, and where, the parents' end draws back. Where they shed light on each other: the drawing back comes when the child can judge for himself, not when the parents wish. Where they part: Locke explains by the ability to use reason when parental government ends; this series instead reads, matter by matter, how the first-person position operates, and when the position of raising and refusing becomes the person's own.

Kant: paternal government. In his 1793 essay on the common saying, Kant says that a government aiming at its subjects' happiness, exercising benevolence toward them as a father toward his children, is the greatest conceivable despotism. Political Economy Paper 6 has already interfaced with this passage, and it is pointed back to here.18 This series adds only one point: Kant uses the father toward his children as a comparison, and what he criticizes is government built on this principle, which rules adults as children; what this series reads is literal parents and children: in one matter after another the child has already come of age, and the parents still treat him as a child not yet of age; what is borrowed is only the same shape, not that kind of government; it is read by §4.

Gibran: the bow and the arrow. In The Prophet, "On Children," Gibran says: your children come through you but not from you; you are the bows from which your children as living arrows are sent forth.23 Gibran makes the parents the bow and the children the arrows loosed from it; where they shed light on each other is that the child is not the parents' possession, which runs the same way as this paper's "being able to unfold without revolving around me." In the poem, the one who aims is an archer, and the parents are the bow he bends; this series sets up no such archer, and further reads apart giving conditions, deciding his direction for him, and personal demonstration; it does not take the poem's image directly as a structural criterion.

Material registered: Fu Lei's Letters. The many letters Fu Lei wrote to his son Fu Cong, studying the piano abroad and already grown, were not published in Fu Lei's lifetime, and were collected after his death. The letters talk of music, of how to conduct oneself, and also of very small matters of daily life, often at great length.24 They were originally private correspondence; both men have died, and the book has been published. Here this material is only registered. A particular reading would have to fall on a selected letter, checking separately the giving, the requiring and the room left at the time of writing; it does not judge a direction from the book as a whole, nor does it certify ordinary coming of age as 14DD having come to stand.

§7 Drawing Together, and Downstream

This paper has established five propositions. Taken together, they say: coming of age is read structurally as the child's 14DD coming to stand, arriving domain by domain; coming of age in law is acknowledged but not judged. The position of raising and refusing becomes his own as the gate runs of itself, without waiting for his 14DD to come to stand, and no one may seal it; being able to raise something is not having it come to stand, and an arrangement that involves others is read by each party's law and by the dealings; in matters of his own direction, the parents do not interpret or avow in his place; how securing is drawn back is not conditional on which road he takes, is checked against actual dependency and the arrangements already formed, and is not inferred directly from his having come to stand in one matter; the side that has turned into a relation between adults is read as help or as something done together. The test is on the child's side: that he is able to unfold without revolving around his father, and is also able to unfold without revolving around his mother, read person by person; not that he must leave; constitutive independence, not real-world self-sufficiency. Once 14DD has come to stand, what remains is mainly leading: only by personal demonstration, not a position with authority, not extending the structure of the parent-child relation, and not widening into all dealings being allowed only demonstration; the child's own 15DD is his own affair, and the door opens from the inside. Dealings between adults and leading are read apart, and whether there is leading as well is checked by the particular effect. Leading is read until it becomes one face of family affection, and no endpoint is set; from then on, things are read by Relation Theory and Cooperation Theory, and whether a collision enters the Conflict Treatise is checked by the two gates; long-term care is handed to a future series on relations of care.

This paper does not prove that a child ought to leave once grown, nor that leading is bound to be seen, nor that the parent-child relation has an endpoint.

Downstream. This paper is the last of the papers of this series. The seven papers are listed together in Paper 0; Paper 0 states this series' place on the relational side, lists the shared words, and registers the remainders. The dealings between parents and adult children go back to Relation Theory; things done together go back to Cooperation Theory; conflict goes back to the Conflict Treatise; long-term care after coming of age is left to a future series on relations of care.

摘要

这一篇读这个系列所读的亲子关系的最后一段：成年，交出，与引领。成年，结构上读作孩子的 14DD 站住：他自己的不得不成型了；它一域一域到，不是某一天一起到；法律上的成年，认而不判。在某一件事上他的否定门已能自行，那一件事上提出与回绝的位置即刻归他本人，谁也封不得；这不另以他的 14DD 已经站住为条件。能提出，不等于提出得成：那件事上的安排改不改，若也牵着父母那一端或别人，照各方各自的法与来往读。涉及他自己的方向，父母不代替他解释与认领；保障一层怎样收，不挂在他走哪一条路上，收的时候照依赖两义核。

在孩子那一侧，检验是他能够不再围绕父亲而展开，也能够不再围绕母亲而展开，逐人读；这是检验，不是他事实上必须离开；能够，限为构成独立，不是现实自足。14DD 站住以后，剩下的主要是引领：引领这一作用只能以身示范，不是一个有权限的位置，不把亲子的结构延长下去；这不扩大成此后的全部来往都只准示范，普通的交流、一起做的事，照关系论、合作论读。孩子自己的 15DD 是他自己的事。

引言

何舟书念完，去了南边一座港口城市，进了一家修造木船的船厂当学徒。走的那天，林晚给他装了两床被子，何川开车送他到车站。车站门口，何川说：「到了打个电话。」

头几个月，有一回何舟在电话里说，船厂给学徒的钱少，何川第二天自己给船厂的老板打了电话，问能不能给孩子涨一点。老板跟何舟提了一句。何舟当晚打电话回来，声音不高：「爸，这是我的事。」何川停了一会儿，说：「是我多事了。」以后他没有再打。头一年，林晚每个月还给他打一点钱；他也常在电话里问林晚，钱怎样存、怎样花。

过了一年多，何舟要跟一家游艇公司签一份合同，拿不准里面一条，打电话问何川。何川在公司里管了十几年合同，这一条他看得懂；他说了他的看法，又说：「签不签，你定。」

那一年，林晚在家附近的文化馆报了一个画画的班，周六上午去。她在视频里给何舟看她画的海，说画得不好，自己笑了。何舟说：「妈，你那条船的桅杆歪了。」林晚说：「是歪了。」

这几件里，有几样要分开读。何舟是从哪一天起「成年」的；何川给船厂老板打的那个电话，是什么；何舟那一句「这是我的事」，落在哪里；那份合同，是何川在替何舟拿主意，还是别的；林晚去学画，与何舟有什么关系；这一家人此后的来往，还是不是这个系列读的亲子关系。

这一篇读什么。 这个系列读的亲子关系，以父或母那一端在 15DD 方向上为前提，按每一段读。1 这一篇读这一段关系的最后一段：孩子的不得不在一件件事上站住以后，父母那一端怎样作用。

这一篇读五件事。一是成年：结构上读作什么，法律上的成年怎样放。二是提出与提出得成：提出与回绝的位置何时归他，交出交的是什么，保障怎样收。三是「能够不再围绕我而展开」在孩子那一侧怎样读。四是引领。五是这段关系转为成年人之间的亲情。

同字的词。 「成年」在这个系列有两义：结构上的成年，读作孩子的 14DD 站住；法律上的成年，是制度划的一条线，这个系列认而不判。正文说「成年」，不另标时，说的是前一义。

「交出」说的是父母那一端原先替孩子拿着的解释与认领，一域一域还给他。追问的发起不在「交出」里：它不是父母交给他的，是门自行时就归他的。

「引领」照教育那一篇的用法：大人活出承认，孩子看到方向。2 它不是领导，不是一个有权限的位置。

「亲情」在这里只是一个名字：说的是父母与成年子女之间的来往，照关系论与合作论读，这个系列不另给它下定义。

写法。 同前六篇。这一篇写的是何舟已经离了家的那几年；正文不写年纪。

第一节 成年

PC38 成年，结构上读作孩子的 14DD 站住：他自己的不得不成型了。它一域一域到，不是某一天一起到；法律上的成年，认而不判

他自己的不得不成型了

父母这一端见到的线索是：拦过，吵过，压下去以后从别处回来过（第五篇）。以下以何舟在造船这件事上的不得不已经成型为条件，读父母这一端怎样作用；此前的持续行动是父母所见的线索，不由登车这一天完成认证。这个系列把成年读作这样一件事：孩子的 14DD 站住，他自己的不得不成型了。

这一读法与前面各篇一样，读的是父母那一端的条件：孩子在一件事上的不得不站住了，父母在那一件事上怎样作用，就跟着变。

一域一域到

成年不是某一天一起到的。何舟在造船这件事上的方向，不能替其他事项一并作证。他问林晚怎样存钱，也请何川看合同；这些只说明他使用了别人的帮助，不说明提出、回绝与修订的位置仍由父母代行。各事项上的位置怎样运行，仍各自核。一个正在成熟的人，那道门可以一部分已能自行，一部分还得别人代行。3 用一道外部固定、不可由个案事实反驳、跨事项统一使用的线，一天之内判定他在所有事上都到了，有系统性错分的风险。4 所以这个系列说「成年」，说的是一件件事上的成年。

法律上的成年

法律怎样划成年、相应权限怎样变化，归制度侧，这个系列认而不判。它也不裁某一个具体的判龄是不是对：那仍留给具体的学科与社会的判断。5 法律上的成年与结构上的成年，可以不在同一天；这个系列读后一件，不拿它去改前一件。

第二节 提出与提出得成

PC39 在某一件事上他的否定门已能自行，那一件事上提出与回绝的位置即刻归他本人，谁也封不得；这不另以他的 14DD 已经站住为条件。能提出，不等于提出得成：那件事上的安排改不改，若也牵着父母那一端或别人，照各方各自的法与来往读，不由哪一方单方说了算。涉及他自己的方向，父母不代替他解释与认领；他的不得不在那件事上站住了，引领从那里接上。保障一层怎样收，不挂在他走哪一条路上；收的时候，照实际依赖、已经形成的安排与依赖两义核。已经转成成年人关系的那一面，此后父母给的，照成年人之间的帮助或一起做的事读

门自行，位置就归他

一件事上，一个孩子的否定门一旦取得稳定的第一人称归属，那一件事上追问的发起就即刻归他本人。6 这一位置不等他的不得不站住：何舟在还没有走上造船这条路的时候，就已经会对那件外套说「不穿」，会问「为什么不能看十分钟」。在这些事上，提出与回绝的位置已经是他的，谁也封不得；谁封了他的表达位置或退出的通道，那就是侵。7

所以，交出不是从成年那一天开始的。提出与回绝的位置，在一件件事上，比成年早得多就归了他；父母那一端交出的，是另一样：原先替他拿着的解释与认领（第四篇）。

提出，与提出得成

能提出，不等于提出得成。8 何舟有一阵提过，书不念了，直接去船厂。这一句他提得出，谁也不能不让他说。可这件事牵着别的：家里的钱，父母那一端的法，船厂收不收没念完书的学徒。那件事上的安排改不改，照各方各自的法与来往读，不由哪一方单方说了算。那一回，三方说了很久，结果是念完。何舟提出了，没有提出得成；他提出的位置没有因此被封。

反过来也一样：门一自行，并不是说孩子就该自己决定一切；那是把提出得成也一并算给了他。8

他自己的方向

在何舟自己的方向上，父母不代替他解释与认领。船厂给学徒多少钱，落在他自己选的那条路上，是这条路上的一件事。何川给船厂老板打的那个电话，替何舟提了一件何舟自己没有提的事：涨钱。这一件，在何舟那里，已经是他自己的事；何川替他提，就是替他认领了一件他自己的事，是一次回落。何舟说「这是我的事」：这一句落在的，正是这一件。何川说「是我多事了」，以后没有再打：认下那一次，并改了此后的做法，这是一次回头（第一篇）。

他的不得不在那件事上站住了，引领从那里接上（第四节）。

保障怎样收

何舟去了船厂，林晚每个月还给他打一点钱，头半年多，后来少了，再后来他说不用了。这几次给付怎样变化，核的是他的实际依赖、已经形成的安排与这些安排怎样改变；不由他在造船这件事上站住，就直接推出保障可以收。

收，有两条线。一是不挂在他走哪一条路上：林晚给这一份，不以何舟改学工程为条件，也不因他继续学造船便撤掉；具体给付仍照实际依赖与既成安排核。基础层的保障，不以人走某一条特定的路为条件。9 二是照依赖核：收的时候，看他实际还靠不靠这一份，看已经形成的安排是什么。先致其依赖、再撤出的，在基础权利一层另核侵（第二篇）。10 何舟去船厂的头一个月，林晚若一下子什么都不给，又不说一声，那一件照这两条核。

转成成年人之间的那一面

那份合同，何舟来问何川；何川说了他的看法，又说「签不签，你定」。这一件，已经不是父母替孩子拿着的东西，是一个成年人请另一个成年人帮忙看一份合同。已经转成成年人关系的那一面，此后父母给的，照成年人之间的帮助读，或者照一起做的事读：父母与成年子女一起做的那件事，一起做的那一面在合作论里。11

第三节 能够不再围绕我而展开

检验在孩子那一侧

意义一层有一道检验：他者能够不再围绕我而展开，是这一行最硬的检验。12 婚姻论读两个人与孩子的时候，把这道检验在孩子那一侧怎样读，留给了亲子论。13

在孩子那一侧读，检验是：何舟能够不再围绕何川而展开，也能够不再围绕林晚而展开。逐人读，是分别核何川与林晚在各自那一段有没有占据何舟价值的构成位置。14 何舟仍请教林晚、仍收她的支持，不足以判定他还围绕她而展开；核的是他的方向与第一人称修订是否仍由她代定。两段，两次读。

能够，不是必须离开

这是检验，不是他事实上必须离开。15 何舟去了南边，是他自己的事；他若留在家附近的船厂，每个周末回家吃饭，这道检验照样读得出：他的事，是不是能够不再围绕父母展开。离开了，不证明能够；没离开，也不证明不能够。

构成独立，不是现实自足

能够，限为构成独立，不是现实自足。16 何舟头一年钱不够，跟家里要；船厂出了事故，他打电话回家。这些不使检验落空：他脱离不了一些现实的支持，不使父母取得他价值的构成权。读的是他是谁、他的事怎样走，是不是还由父母定。

教、涵育、引领三件里，引领在后：引领的窗口是 14DD 站稳之后。引领的方式只有一种：大人自己站在 15DD 的位置上，孩子看到了，知道那个位置存在。引领只能以身示范。17

照射程读，「站在 15DD 的位置上」读作一次次运作朝哪里去，回落照读（第一篇）。

引领不是一个有权限的位置。它不给父母一个替成年子女定事的资格；它也不把亲子的结构延长下去：孩子成年了，父母仍以「我是你爸」来定他的事，就是把那个结构拉长。何川给船厂老板打电话，是在一件已经是何舟自己的事上，把他当作还没有成年的孩子来待。把成年人当作还不能自己判断什么对自己好的孩子，政治的一层读过：以善意替人定幸福的那种治理，是可设想的最大专制。18 康德批的，是以这一原则立起来的治理；这里借的只是形状：把一个成年人当作还不能自己判断什么对自己好的孩子。何川那一通电话是这一形状的一次运作，不是那一种治理。

不扩大成全部来往

引领只能以身示范，说的是引领这一作用。它不扩大成父母与成年子女此后的全部来往都只准示范。何川与何舟在电话里说合同，说天气，说船厂的伙食；林晚跟何舟视频，说她画的海。这些是普通的来往，照关系论读；何川与何舟哪一天一起修一条小船，那是一起做的事，照合作论读。

孩子自己的 15DD，是他自己的事。15DD 不能教，也不能涵育；它是在与另一个 14DD 的碰撞里涌现的。19 这里的「涵育」照教育篇三分的专门用法，不取消关系论宽义涵育的其他可能。 父母能做的，是活出承认，让他看到那个位置存在；他走不走到那里，什么时候、在哪一件事上走到，在他那里。门从里面开。20

成年以后，有的人因为重度残障或别的原因，需要长期照料；有的父母老了，需要成年的子女照料。这些情形，这个系列不读，交给将来的照护关系系列。

第六节 与传统对接

洛克：父权止于理性成熟。 洛克在《政府论》下篇第六章说，父母对孩子的那份权，随孩子到了能自己运用理性的时候而止；此后孩子对父母仍有尊敬，那是另一件。22 共同的问题：父母那一端，到什么时候、在哪里收手。相照的一处：收手落在孩子自己能判断的时候，不落在父母的意愿上。分开的一处：洛克以能够运用理性说明父母管束何时终止；这个系列另按具体事项，读第一人称位置怎样运行、提出与回绝的位置何时归本人。

康德：父权式的治理。 康德在 1793 年论俗语的那篇文字里说，一个以臣民的幸福为目标、像父亲对孩子那样施行善意的政府，是可设想的最大专制。这一段，政治经济学第六篇已经对过，这里回指。18 这个系列只补一处：康德拿父亲对孩子作比，批的是以这一原则立起来、把成年人当作孩子来治的治理；这个系列读的是字面上的父母与孩子，一件件事上孩子已经成年，父母仍照未成年的孩子待他，借的只是同一个形状，不是那一种治理，照第四节读。

纪伯伦：弓与箭。 纪伯伦在《先知》「论孩子」里说：你们的孩子，经你们而来，却不是从你们而来；你们是弓，孩子是从弓上射出去的活的箭。23 纪伯伦让父母作弓，让孩子作离弦的箭；相照的一处，是孩子不归父母占有，这与这一篇「能够不再围绕我而展开」同向。诗里瞄准的是一位弓手，父母是被他拉弯的弓；这个系列不设这样一位弓手，进一步把给条件、替他定方向与以身示范分开读，不把诗的意象直接当作结构判据。

材料登记：《傅雷家书》。 傅雷写给在国外学琴、已经成年的儿子傅聪的许多信，傅雷生前未出版，身后结集。信里谈音乐、谈做人，也谈生活里很细的事，常常写得很长。24 这些原是私人的通信；两人都已去世，书已公开出版。此处只登记这部材料。具体读法须落到选定的一封信，分别核写信时的给予、要求与余地；不凭整部书判定方向，也不把通常的成年认证为 14DD 站住。

第七节 收束与下游

这一篇立了五条。合起来说的是：成年，结构上读作孩子的 14DD 站住，一域一域到；法律上的成年认而不判。提出与回绝的位置随门自行归他本人，不等他的 14DD 站住，谁也封不得；能提出不等于提出得成，牵着别人的安排照各方的法与来往读；他自己的方向，父母不代替解释与认领；保障怎样收，不挂在他走哪一条路上，照实际依赖与既成安排核，不由他在一件事上站住直接推出；已转成成年人关系的那一面，照帮助或一起做的事读。检验在孩子那一侧：他能够不再围绕父亲、也能够不再围绕母亲而展开，逐人读，不是必须离开，是构成独立，不是现实自足。14DD 站住以后剩下的主要是引领：只能以身示范，不是有权限的位置，不延长亲子的结构，也不扩大成全部来往都只准示范；孩子自己的 15DD 是他自己的事，门从里面开。成年人之间的来往与引领分开读，是否同时有引领，核具体作用。引领读到成为亲情的一面为止，不设终点；此后照关系论、合作论读，碰撞是否进入冲突论照两道门核；长期照料交将来的照护关系系列。

这一篇不证明孩子成年以后应当离开，不证明引领一定被看见，也不证明亲子之间有一个终点。

下游。 这一篇是这个系列各篇的最后一篇。七篇合起来，在第零篇合列；第零篇说这个系列在关系一侧的位置，列同字的词，登记余项。父母与成年子女之间的来往，回到关系论；一起做的事，回到合作论；冲突，回到冲突论；成年以后的长期照料，留给将来的照护关系系列。
