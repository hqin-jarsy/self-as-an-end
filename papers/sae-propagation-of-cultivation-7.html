The Propagation of Cultivation: Unsought
涵育的传播：不求

Abstract

This is the last of the body papers in the series. It reads where the giver stands with respect to "passing it on." Cultivation does not seek success: whether the other's judgment opens up, whether he gives onward, is neither fixed in advance nor inspected. What can be settled, done and checked is possibility: whether this act of mine left room, and what it opened. Carried over to propagation: one does not seek that it be passed on; what one can do is see that the conditions for its being passed on are there. On the side of cultivation, propagation is read only in looking back; where it can be read it is recorded as it is, where it cannot, that too is recorded as it is, and looking back can be mistaken.

The seeking excluded here is taken in the narrow sense: fixing in advance, producing to that specification, inspecting against it. What has to be checked is what is being inspected: whether the other's own judgment and way of standing have been made, in advance, into a standard for production and inspection; if so, calling it by another name leaves it excluded. Checking conditions, technical content, and particular arrangements both have avowed is read separately; hoping that involves no inspection is not included. Fixing in advance how the other is to treat the next person carries the direction of colonization at that point, and motive does not change this reading; the giver does not, for having given, hold a share in how the other acts toward the next person. The handing-on of a craft can be discussed and arranged, and whether its content was learned can be checked; none of this fixes in advance how the other is to treat the next person.

That something was passed on does not offset the harm done at any link; what was not passed on is said, as it is, not to have been, and is not topped up into "some influence always remains." Nor is not seeking a mark of anything: it guarantees nothing in itself, direction included. The difference lies not in whether there is a wish for it to be passed on, but in where the step downstream falls.

The paper refers back to a sentence from Wittgenstein's preface and paraphrases one remark from his notebooks. It does not show that what is unsought will be passed on, does not say that hoping for someone to carry it on is wrong, does not rate any way of handing things down, and does not identify any tradition as seeking.

Introduction

Fang Yan is close to retirement. The procedure for tracking down faults he has used for more than twenty years; Su Yao has changed it, and Cheng Xi has changed it again. He hopes someone will go on using it, and changing it.

He does several things. He writes the procedure out from the beginning, with the reason noted after every step; in a few places he writes in the margin: "I have never been sure of this step. If anyone has a better way, change it." He puts the document on the crew's shared drive, where anyone can open it. Someone asks whether he wants to put his name on it and fix a version; he says no.

Su Yao once asks him: "After you've gone, how am I supposed to train newcomers?" Fang Yan says: "That's for you to decide. The way you train Cheng Xi already isn't my way."

Stipulate another course, separate from the above. Fang Yan writes one more page, headed Rules for Training Newcomers: talk with the newcomer once a week; when the newcomer asks a question, ask back first; when the newcomer changes a step, report it to Fang Yan. He hands the page to Su Yao and says: "Promise me you'll train by this from now on." After that he comes back once a quarter, calls in the newcomers Su Yao is training, and asks them item by item. On one occasion Su Yao says: last month a newcomer came to ask about a wiring point, and that time she answered directly; the machine was live, and there was no time to let him think it through. Fang Yan does not ask about the state of that machine. He says: "The rules say ask back first. You didn't do that one. Put it right from now on." Su Yao says this feels like an assessment. Fang Yan says: "It's not an assessment. It's what I expect of you."

The company has plans of its own. It wants to make the procedure a standard process, name it "the Fang Method," and roll it out across the company; and it sets up a conferred title, "Successor," the first holder to be Su Yao. One of the conditions for the title reads: the process is not to be altered without authorization; any change is to be submitted to the technical department for approval.

And one thing from further back. In Paper 4, Su Yao said to Cheng Xi every time, "Three lines will do," and Cheng Xi eventually stopped recording even what he himself wanted to keep. Later, training a newcomer, Cheng Xi said the same.

Several things here need to be read apart. Fang Yan hopes someone will carry it on: is that seeking? Writing the procedure down and leaving it on the shared drive, and writing the Rules and asking Su Yao to promise: where is the division? When the Rules are renamed "what I expect of you," what has changed? The company makes the content a standard and sets up a title: which of these is ordinary business, and which item is not? This chain has passed through three people, and "three lines will do" came down it too: how is that entered? And Fang Yan seeks nothing: does that show he is doing it right?

What this paper reads. Paper 1 held that propagation is a result, not anyone's purpose. The papers since have read how a link holds, what being seen is, and what happens when one addresses many. This paper returns to that opening sentence and reads what it looks like at the giver's end. Seven things: not seeking success; carried over to propagation; the narrow sense of seeking and what has to be checked; fixing things in advance for the other; content being carried on; no offsetting and no topping up; not seeking is not a mark.

Shared words. Seek is used in this series in one narrow sense only, following the upstream paper on positive-sum: fixing in advance, producing to that specification, inspecting against it.1 In ordinary speech the word is far wider and can mean no more than where one puts one's effort; §1 uses it once in that ordinary way and says so on the spot. Hope means open hoping: hoping, with no when and no shape fixed, and not used to inspect anyone. Being carried on is said of content: someone goes on using it. A conferred title is a designation and position given by a construct or by a school of masters and pupils.

What it does not read. How the company sets its standard and how it awards the title "Successor" are arrangements inside a construct; the provisions themselves this series acknowledges and does not judge. This paper reads only the point where they come down to what passes between people.

§1 Not Seeking Success

CB38　Cultivation does not seek success. Whether the other's judgment opens up, whether he gives onward, is neither fixed in advance nor inspected. What can be settled, done and checked is possibility: whether this act of mine left room, and what it opened

Not fixed in advance, not inspected

Fang Yan taught Su Yao, and Su Yao's judgment opened up by a step. Was that step something Fang Yan "accomplished"?

No. Cultivation Theory divides confirmation into three questions, and the third, whether the other himself completed that step, "can only be done by the one who receives."2 What Fang Yan can settle is his own act; whether Su Yao's judgment opens up, he cannot settle. What cannot be settled cannot be fixed in advance or inspected either: he cannot write down beforehand, "in a year Su Yao will be able to change the process herself," and come back to check.

The upstream paper says it through: "What the other grows into is for the other to decide." Once it is fixed in advance, what has been fixed is a different thing: a standard that can be worked toward and checked against.3

The next link is the same. Whether Su Yao gives onward, and how, Fang Yan likewise cannot settle. Paper 4 held that all three things rest with the person in the middle.

So the first half of the proposition is: cultivation does not seek success. Whether the other's judgment opens up, whether he gives onward, is neither fixed in advance nor inspected.

Possibility

The second half is: what can be settled, done and checked is possibility.

On those occasions Fang Yan gave the reason for each step and made it clear she could change it. That he can settle: whether to give reasons, whether to leave her a place to change things. That he can do. And that he can check: looking back, did he finish her sentences for her that time; when she objected, did he press the objection back down?

Taken together these are a condition: under it, Su Yao's forming, avowing and revising a judgment of her own is possible. Whether she later took that step is her affair; that the step could be taken is what Fang Yan's end brought about.

It can be put in ordinary words: what cultivation seeks is not success but possibility. "Seeks" in that sentence is the ordinary usage, about where the effort goes. It is not the seeking this paper excludes: the upstream paper lists providing conditions explicitly among the things that are not seeking.4 So in this series' propositions it is not written "seek possibility"; it is written: does not seek success; what can be settled, done and checked is possibility.

Not doing nothing

"Not seeking" is easily heard as doing nothing and waiting.

The Moral Law series, at the scale of institutional architecture, has a sentence: "waiting is not non-action but non-colonizing preparation".5 That is said at another scale and is not carried over to a single link; but it blocks the same misreading. Fang Yan does not seek that Su Yao grow into anything in particular, and he does not do less on that account: reasons have to be given step by step; the place has to be actually kept open; after she changes something he has to go back and look again at his own sequence. All of that is work. Not seeking strikes out only one thing: treating her step as a deliverable of his own.

§2 Carried Over to Propagation

CB39　Carried over to propagation: one does not seek that it be passed on; what one can do is see that the conditions for its being passed on are there. On the side of cultivation, propagation is read only in looking back, in the same form as the "unsought" of Positive-Sum, Unsought: where it can be read, it is recorded as it is; where it cannot, that too is recorded as it is; looking back can be mistaken

Seeing that the conditions are there

Fang Yan hopes the procedure will go on being used and changed. By the previous section he does not seek that it be passed on: who takes it up, and how that person then trains the next, he does not fix in advance.

What he can do is see that the conditions for its being passed on are there. The things he does in the Introduction can be read this way.

He writes it down, with the reason after every step. Whoever takes it up gets not only the order but the why behind it; with the why, he is able to change it.

He marks the places where he himself is unsure, and writes "change it." That leaves the place for change on the page.

He puts it where anyone can open it, unsigned, with no fixed version. Whoever takes it up does not first need anyone's permission.

He says to Su Yao, "That's for you to decide." How to train the next person he states outright is not his to settle.

All of these are at his end: he can settle them, do them, check them. They do not guarantee that anyone takes it up. It may be that no one ever opens the document.

Read only in looking back

On the side of cultivation, propagation is read only in looking back. The upstream paper says this of ontological positive-sum: "where it can be read, it came unsought; where it cannot, it is recorded as it is; looking back can be mistaken."1 The form here is the same: whether it was passed on is not set up beforehand; it is looked back on afterward.

Where it can be read, it is recorded as it is. Su Yao trained Cheng Xi, and said herself at which point the earlier relation entered the later; Cheng Xi trained a newcomer, laying out two paths for him to choose between. Looking back, there are two links here.

Where it cannot be read, that too is recorded as it is. Suppose no one later opened the document, and no one after Su Yao trained people in that way. Looking back: it reached as far as her.

Looking back can be mistaken, and with a bias in one direction. Upstream describes a bias on the giver's side called over-cultivation: "reading the other's strategic performance as awakening."6 In looking back along a chain there is a bias of the same shape: reading "alike" as "passed on." The later person uses the same words and the same steps, and someone who hopes for a successor readily reads this as the way of standing having arrived too. The rule set in Paper 1 governs exactly here: that two relations are alike is not enough; material is needed that shows how they connect.

§3 The Narrow Sense of Seeking

CB40　The seeking excluded here is taken in the narrow sense: fixing in advance, producing to that specification, inspecting against it. What has to be checked is what is being inspected: whether the other's own judgment and way of standing have been made, in advance, into a standard for production and inspection; if so, calling it by another name leaves it excluded. Checking conditions, technical content, and particular arrangements both have avowed is read separately. Hoping that involves no inspection is not included

Three operations

The seeking excluded here is three operations joined together: fixing in advance, producing to that specification, inspecting against it.

On the other course, the Rules for Training Newcomers have all three. Fixing in advance: talk once a week; ask back first; report any step the newcomer changes. Producing to it: he asks Su Yao to promise she will train by this from now on. Inspecting against it: every quarter he comes back, calls in the newcomers, and asks item by item.

What is fixed in advance has to be seen clearly. It is not the procedure for tracking down faults; that is content, which §5 reads. What is fixed in advance is how Su Yao is to treat the next person.

What has to be checked: what is being inspected

Hoping and seeking are sometimes half a step apart. Fang Yan hopes Su Yao will go on leaving newcomers a place to change things: that is hoping. He writes it up as rules, asks her to promise, and comes back to check: that is seeking. Where is the division?

One answer is too coarse and is set aside first: wherever there is inspection, there is seeking. Not so. A technician's assessment inspects whether a repair meets the technical conditions. Two people agree that changes to the procedure will be logged, and go through the log together later. Both involve inspection, and both are ordinary business, read in §5.

What has to be checked is what is being inspected. This series words it: whether the other's own judgment and way of standing have been made, in advance, into a standard for production and inspection.

What the Rules inspect is not whether Su Yao knows the procedure; it is how she trains people; and, further, a list is being used to determine whether her judgment in training has been formed. The occasion in the Introduction shows it most clearly: she said why she answered directly that time; Fang Yan does not check that reason, and checks only whether she asked back first. Her reason was not treated as a reason.

Hoping that involves no inspection is not excluded. The upstream paper gives the case: Fang Yan hopes that Su Yao will one day find her own way of doing things; "when, and in what shape, he has not fixed, nor does he check her by it. This is not seeking."7 A hope can be spoken. Fang Yan can say to Su Yao, "I hope the people you train will dare to change yours too." Once said, it is a sentence; it has entered Su Yao's conditions, and what she does with it rests with her.

Calling it by another name leaves it excluded. Su Yao says this feels like an assessment, and Fang Yan says: "It's not an assessment. It's what I expect of you." The upstream paper reads exactly this move: if Fang Yan does not call the checklist a standard but calls it what he expects of her, and inspects by it, item by item, whether her judgment holds, "it is still the seeking spoken of here."8 The name has changed; none of the three operations has gone.

Set up, and actually inspected

Two further things are kept apart: an arrangement having been set up, and an inspection actually arriving.

At the moment Fang Yan hands over the rules and asks for her promise, how Su Yao is to train people has already been settled for her. Which way this act is headed can be read then, without waiting for the quarter. Whether all three operations are present can be checked separately; what has already been settled for her is there whether or not it has yet been inspected.

Of this proposition, "hoping that involves no inspection is not included" is found upstream. Writing what has to be checked as "what is being inspected" follows that paper's placing of the boundary at what the other grows into;3 the wording is this series' own undertaking.

§4 Fixing Things in Advance for the Other

CB41　Fixing in advance how the other is to treat the next person carries the direction of colonization at that point, and motive does not change this reading. The giver does not, for having given, hold a share in how the other acts toward the next person

Direction

What the Rules fix in advance is how Su Yao is to treat the next person. This series reads that point as carrying the direction of colonization.

The reason has appeared in each of the earlier papers. How to treat the next person is a matter for Su Yao's own judgment: for this newcomer, this time, whether to ask back or not, how far to give. Paper 4 said that giving is itself an exercise of judgment. To fix it in advance is to settle for her how this is to be done; on the items the rules lay down, compliance is all that is wanted, and her reasons do not count: that time she said why she had answered directly, and it was not checked. On those items her own way of training cannot come out.

The same three items, were they an arrangement Su Yao herself had avowed, with her reasons heard and checked as before, would be another matter: that is a particular arrangement both have avowed, read in the next section. An arrangement both have avowed can bind. What has to be checked is not whether she can change it alone at any time; it is how her reasons are dealt with, and whether conformity with the list is entered directly as whether her judgment has been formed.

This is the same structure Paper 2 read: a line about how people are to be treated travels downstream inside content. Only this time the content of the line happens to be "leave room" and "ask back first." The content points toward cultivation; the manner of handing it over goes toward pouring in and occupying. Paper 5 read what the same thing looks like inside a construct: "What do you think?" written into the Training Standard.

Motive does not change this reading

Fang Yan means well. He wrote that page because he hopes this way of training will not break off.

That does not change the reading. The upstream papers agree on this point: the judgment lies in what the act actually settled for the other and what it occupied, not in motive and feeling.9 Rules written with good intent and rules written with ill intent read the same at the position "can Su Yao still judge for herself how to train people."

One thing is worth seeing here: a person who cares whether cultivation gets passed on stands close to this step. Hoping that it will be passed on, he wants to pin it down; once it is pinned down, what gets passed on is a page of rules.

A way out

Two propositions of Cultivation Theory bear directly on this. "The other's share is not filled in for him: whether to avow, how to judge, whether to change."10 And when something was wrongly given, what has not yet reached into the other's share is taken back as soon as this is pointed out; what has already reached in, once taken back, is still not rewritten as never having happened.11

Su Yao's "this feels like an assessment" is the pointing out. Fang Yan changed the name and did not take it back. So this reading has a way out: he can withdraw the page. Once withdrawn, those quarters are recorded as they were; from then on, how to train people is back in Su Yao's hands.

No share

Teacher-Student Theory holds that the teacher "does not, for having taught, hold a share in the student's direction."12 Action Theory holds that "those who provided the conditions do not thereby become the ones who completed that step for him."13 Meaning Theory has a shorter sentence: "What anticipatory enactment leaves behind is conditions, not an orientation deposited elsewhere."14

Carried across two relations: the giver does not, for having given, hold a share in how the other acts toward the next person. Fang Yan taught Su Yao; Su Yao's training of Cheng Xi is between Su Yao and Cheng Xi. His having taught her gives him no special claim to a say in that stretch. He can have a view, and can state it; it is a colleague's view, not a right belonging to someone "upstream."

In the Introduction this is just how he answers: "That's for you to decide. The way you train Cheng Xi already isn't my way."

Meaning Theory also has a test: "That the other is able to unfold without revolving around me."15 Placed here: Su Yao can train people without mentioning Fang Yan at all and without resembling him, and this link holds all the same. That this is possible is one of the forms taken by Fang Yan's act having been carried out, and not its failure.

§5 Content Being Carried On

CB42　The handing-on of a craft can be discussed and arranged, and whether its content was learned can be checked; none of this fixes in advance how the other is to treat the next person. To fix that in advance, produce to it and inspect against it falls within the seeking excluded here. Tying it to a conferred title is one way of doing so; whether there is a title is not itself the criterion. Seeking, and getting, someone to carry the content on does not show that the way of standing arrived too

Ordinary business

Not seeking does not mean that it is all the same whether a craft finds anyone to carry it on. At the layer of content, things can be arranged.

The company makes the procedure a standard process and rolls it out; it arranges who teaches it, and after the teaching assesses whether it has been learned. All of this is ordinary business. The upstream paper says that matters at the payoff layer go on as usual, and that two people jointly settling on a particular improvement can also seek it; teaching an operation by a checklist and inspecting a repair against technical conditions are not in themselves the seeking it excludes.416

Fang Yan and Su Yao can likewise discuss who will maintain the procedure from now on, how changes are logged, which step a newcomer starts from. Particular arrangements both have avowed are not excluded.

None of this fixes in advance how Su Yao is to treat the next person. "A newcomer is to learn these twelve steps" is an arrangement about content; "you must train newcomers in this way" is something else.

What is added beyond the provision

One of the company's conditions for the title reads: the process is not to be altered without authorization; any change is to be submitted to the technical department for approval. That is a provision. The provision itself this series acknowledges and does not judge. What it governs is altering the text of the process without approval; pointing out that a step is wrong, giving reasons, and changing it through the procedure all remain available.

What this series reads is what is added, between people, beyond the provision. Suppose Cheng Xi finds that a step does not fit the newer model, writes up his reasons, and asks Su Yao to submit them.

On one course, Su Yao submits them.

On another, Su Yao says: "The process is not to be touched. If it's touched, I lose the Successor title. Don't raise this again." The provision does not say it may not be raised. She has said more than the provision: she has kept Cheng Xi's reasons out of a procedure that was available for them. What is read is this addition: the place from which Cheng Xi could propose a change has been sealed at her.

On paper Fang Yan wrote, "If anyone has a better way, change it." The same procedure can travel downstream joined to that sentence, or joined to "don't raise this again."

A title is not the criterion

Conferred titles such as "Successor" are found in crafts and schools of every kind. This series does not use the presence or absence of a title as a criterion.

Tying things to a title is one way of fixing the step downstream in advance: the title goes only to the one who complies, and whoever wants the title complies. But fixing in advance does not need a title. The Rules for Training Newcomers involve no title at all, and all three operations are present. Conversely, a title can come without any fixing in advance: a title that only registers "this person has learned the whole procedure," with nothing attached about how he is to act or train people afterward, is simply a record about content.

So what is read is not the title; it is whether the three operations are there. Upstream, in interfacing with one religious tradition, lineages of transmission and certification were read as governance arrangements at that layer;17 that is a reading on the side of constructs, and this series only registers it, without using it to identify any tradition.

Content carried on; a way of standing not thereby read

A last sentence connects to Paper 1. Securing that content finds someone to carry it on does not show that the way of standing arrived too. The company's standard process has been rolled out to every crew, and everyone can recite the Fang Method. That is one thing. Whether, in those crews, anyone says to a newcomer "this step is yours to decide" is another, and cannot be read from the first.

The reverse occurs as well. Paper 4 read Lu Xun writing about Fujino. In the same essay he records what Fujino hoped of him: in the small, that China would have a new medical science; in the large, that the new medical science would be carried to China.18 That is a hope about content. He later gave up medicine, and that road was not carried on; something else remained, which he set down himself, and which entered his later writing. That the road was not carried on does not show that nothing was left.

§6 No Offsetting, No Topping Up

CB43　That something was passed on does not offset the harm done at any link; what was not passed on is said, as it is, not to have been, and is not topped up into "some influence always remains"; no one is certified as being at any layer

No offsetting

This chain has passed downstream: Fang Yan, Su Yao, Cheng Xi, the newcomer Cheng Xi trains. Looking back, two links can be read.

Something else in the chain has gone downstream too. Su Yao's "three lines will do" occupied the domain of Cheng Xi's note-keeping; training a newcomer, Cheng Xi said the same.

That something was passed on does not offset the harm done at any link. One does not say, "on the whole this chain is one of cultivation, and that point hardly counts." The upstream paper holds that saying a stretch of dealings has ontological positive-sum "does not offset the account of harm."19 The Conflict Treatise is blunter: one may not use the claim of positive-sum "to deny that what was just said did in fact wound."20 The things Cheng Xi did not record are actually missing; that entry is made in the stretch between Su Yao and Cheng Xi, and is not cancelled by "this step is yours to decide" in the same stretch.

Paper 1 already set down the root of this reading: a chain is not a bearer, and no overall direction is totalled for a chain. Here is its use in looking back.

No topping up

At the other end: what was not passed on is said, as it is, not to have been.

Suppose no one after Su Yao trained people in that way. A certain kind of remark appears at this point: "the influence is always there, only unseen"; "the seed has been planted." This series does not say that. The upstream paper holds that one "does not add an unseen payoff"; "what was not obtained is said, as it is, not to have been obtained."19 Two cases are distinguished here. Where there is material confirming that it did not continue, one says, as it is, that it stopped here: the several people who trained newcomers after Su Yao can all be read, and none trained in that way. Where there is no material, one says only that this is as far as the reading goes for now; one says neither that it stopped here nor that it is still there. Neither case is topped up.

Not topping up is not pessimism. Paper 1 said that a chain's breaking does not mean there is none afterward; another starts elsewhere. But "another starts elsewhere" is a statement about elsewhere, not a continuation of this chain. How far this chain reached is written as far as it reached.

Nor does Fang Yan's gain on those occasions depend on topping up to be kept. Paper 1 held that A's gain on an occasion lies in that occasion and is neither increased nor reduced by whether anything was passed on. It never hung on what came after.

No certifying

A last "no": no one is certified as being at any layer. That a chain has reached a fourth person does not show that any one of them "is 15DD." What can be read is which way particular acts were headed and what they opened.

§7 Not Seeking Is Not a Mark

CB44　Not seeking is not a mark of anything. Not seeking guarantees nothing in itself, direction included; colonization, too, can pass downstream without being anyone's purpose. The difference lies not in whether there is a wish for it to be passed on, but in where the step downstream falls

No guarantee of direction

At this point a misreading has to be blocked: that not seeking is the sign of cultivation, so that one can tell which side a person is on by whether he seeks.

It is not so. The upstream paper says in its Introduction that "not seeking does not by itself guarantee that both ends gain."21 And where it interfaces with Adam Smith it says something of the same shape: not aiming at the total "is not in itself enough to identify 15DD."22

Lao Guo, in Paper 2, did not seek either. He never thought of passing "if the number looks bad, the person is no good" on to Wei Heng; nothing was fixed in advance, nothing inspected. The line reached Wei Heng all the same, and then Zhu Cheng. Colonization, too, can pass downstream without being anyone's purpose. Paper 1 wrote that sentence at its opening, and this is where it lands.

So not seeking is not a mark. From the fact that a person does not seek, it cannot be read which way what he gives is headed.

Nor is the wish

The converse fails too: from a person's wishing that it be passed on, it cannot be read that he is seeking.

Fang Yan hopes someone will carry it on. He has said so, and has done a good deal for it. By §3 he has not made Su Yao's judgment and way of standing into a standard for inspection, and this is not seeking. A person with a deep wish for something to be handed down, and a person who turns handing down into rules and assessments, are not told apart by whether the wish is there or how deep it goes.

Where the step falls

The difference lies in where the step downstream falls.

On the side of cultivation, the step downstream falls to the person in the middle: he is willing to receive, his judgment opens up, he goes on to give; what the earlier person does is make that step possible. On the side of colonization, the step downstream does not wait for the person in the middle: he does the same, and it has passed through. To fix in advance, produce and inspect the passing-on of cultivation is to do the first in the manner of the second: not waiting for the person in the middle. When that succeeds, what has been passed on is already something else.

This is the structure the series has read from beginning to end, as it looks at the giver's end.

§8 Historical Material: Two Remarks of Wittgenstein

A sentence in a preface. Cultivation Theory read the preface Wittgenstein wrote for the Philosophical Investigations, in which he says that he would not want his writing to save others the work of thinking, and that, if it were possible, he would want it to stir someone to thoughts of his own.23 Cultivation Theory read where that sentence points. Here it is read from another side: he wrote down the kind of "passing on" he did not want. Readers thinking along his conclusions is not what he hoped for; a reader's having thoughts of his own is. It is a hope, with no inspection behind it.

A remark in the notebooks. Among his notes of 1947 is a remark three sentences long. He asks whether he alone is unable to found a school, or whether that is beyond any philosopher. He then says that he cannot, since being imitated is not something he actually wants. The last sentence adds a qualification: not, at any rate, by people who write for the philosophy journals.24

Only how he wrote it is read here. The remark can be set beside a hope that does not take the copying of oneself for being carried on. It is said in the context of philosophical writing, and carries the qualification of its last sentence; it is not used here to sum up his attitude to everything later people did that resembled him, nor to say what a school is.

What cannot be read here is also stated. Whether he later had students who imitated him, and what became of them, cannot be read from these two places; nothing is said on this basis about what he achieved, and he is not certified as being at any layer.

§9 Drawing Together: How Far the Series Has Read

This paper has set out seven propositions. Together they say: cultivation does not seek success; whether the other's judgment opens up, whether he gives onward, is neither fixed in advance nor inspected; what can be settled, done and checked is possibility. Carried over to propagation, one does not seek that it be passed on, and what one can do is see that the conditions for its being passed on are there; on the side of cultivation propagation is read only in looking back, and looking back can be mistaken. The seeking excluded is taken in the narrow sense: fixing in advance, producing, inspecting; what has to be checked is what is being inspected, whether the other's own judgment and way of standing have been made into a standard for inspection. Fixing in advance how the other is to treat the next person carries the direction of colonization, and motive does not change this reading; the giver does not, for having given, hold a share in how the other acts toward the next person. Content being carried on can be discussed, arranged and checked, and none of this fixes in advance how the other is to treat the next person; tying it to a conferred title is one way of fixing in advance, and a title is not itself the criterion. That something was passed on does not offset the harm done at any link; what was not passed on is said, as it is, not to have been, and is not topped up. Not seeking is not a mark; the difference lies in where the step downstream falls.

The seven body papers together read one structure. Propagation is a result of people having come into relation. Three things pass through a person: content can be taught; the downstream repetition of an occupied place does not require the person in the middle to have opened up a judgment of his own; a way of standing cannot be taught and can be seen. A practice carrying the direction of colonization can stop at one person; that is breaking, which has two sides, each with its own sources. For cultivation to pass downstream by one link takes three things of the middle person's own; it is reckoned up to "carried out"; it is not a debt. Being seen lets the later person know that the position exists; coming to stand there remains his own affair. Addressing many is still relations one at a time. Toward all of this the giver does not seek success; what he does is make it possible.

What the series has not done: it has not shown that cultivation will be passed on, nor that it will not; it has not compared the two sides for which is easier or which is faster; it has not attached a direction to any tradition or any person; it has not read the stretch before adulthood, has not read propaganda or the rules of platforms, has not read how institutions arrange succession; it has not said that a person who received ought to give onward, nor that a giver ought, or ought not, to hope. The level of the community and trends across generations are another scale, read by the Moral Law series; the boundary with it is written in Paper 0.

摘要

这是「涵育的传播」的最后一篇正文，读给的人对「往下传」这件事站在哪里。涵育不求成功：对方长不长开、往不往下给，不预定，不验收。能定、能做、能核的，是可能：自己这一次作为留没留空间、打开了什么。推到传播上：不求它传下去；能做的是让它传得下去的条件在。涵育这一边的传只在回看里读，读得出的照实记，读不出的也照实记，回看可以看错。

这里排除的求取窄义：预定，照它制作，照它验收。要核的是验的是什么：有没有把对方自己的判断与站法，预先做成一份制作与验收的标准；有，换了叫法也在排除之内。对条件、对技术内容、对两人认下的具体安排的核，另读；没有验收的盼望不在其中。替对方预定他怎样待下一个人，这一处带殖民这个方向，动机不改变这一读；给的人不因给过而在对方怎样对下一个人上占一份。一门手艺的接续可以商量、安排，教的内容学会没有也可以核；这些不替对方预定他怎样待下一个人。

传下来了，不抵其中哪一环的伤害；没有传下去的，照实说没有，不补成总有影响留下。不求也不是记号：不求本身不保证什么，也不保证方向；区别不在有没有想它传下去的愿望，在往下的那一步落在哪里。

这一篇回指维特根斯坦的一篇序言，并转述他笔记里的一则。它不证明不求的就会传下去，不说盼着有人接是不对的，不评哪一种传承的办法好，也不指认哪一门传承是在求。

方砚快退休了。那套排查做法，他用了二十多年，苏遥改过，程溪又改过。他盼着它有人接着用，接着改。

有一回苏遥问他：「你走了以后，新人我该怎么带？」方砚说：「那是你的事。你带程溪的办法，就不是我的办法。」

设另一种走法，与上面分开。方砚另写了一页，题目是《带人守则》：每周与新人谈一次；新人提问，先反问；新人改了哪一步，要报给他。他把这一页交给苏遥，说：「答应我，以后照这个带。」此后每个季度，他回来一趟，把苏遥带的新人叫来，一条一条问。有一回苏遥说：上个月有个新人来问一处接线，那一回她直接答了，机器带着电，等不得他慢慢想。方砚没有问那台机器的情形。他说：「守则上写的是先反问。这一条你没有做到，以后改回来。」苏遥说这像是考核，方砚说：「不是考核，是我对你的期待。」

公司那边另有打算。公司想把这套做法定为标准流程，起名叫「方氏排查法」，在全公司推广；又设了一个名分，叫「传承人」，头一位定的是苏遥。评定的条件里有一条：不得擅自改动流程；要改，须报技术部核准。

再往回看一处。第四篇里，苏遥对程溪每回都说「三行够了」，程溪后来连自己想留的记录也不记了。这一处，程溪后来带新人的时候，也照样说了。

这一篇读什么。 第一篇立：传是结果，不是谁的目的。前几篇读了一环怎样成立、被看见是怎么回事、面向许多人又怎样。这一篇回到开头那一句，读它在给的人那里是什么样子。它读七件事：不求成功；推到传播上；求的窄义与要核的是什么；替对方预定；内容的接续；不抵与不补；不求不是记号。

第一节 不求成功

CB38　涵育不求成功。对方长不长开、往不往下给，不预定，不验收。能定、能做、能核的，是可能：自己这一次作为留没留空间、打开了什么

不预定，不验收

方砚教苏遥，苏遥长开了一步。这一步是不是方砚「做成」的？

不是。涵育论把确认分成三问，第三问，对方自己是否完成了那一步，只能由受的人自己做。2 方砚定得了的，是他自己的作为；苏遥长不长开，他定不了。定不了的东西，也就预定不了，验收不了：他不能事先写下「一年以后苏遥要会自己改流程」，到时候来查。

上游那篇专论把这一层说透了：对方长成什么样，由对方自己定；一这样预定，被预定的就换成了另一样东西，一个可以照着去做、去核的标准。3

往下一环同此。苏遥往不往下给、怎样给，方砚同样定不了。第四篇立过：这三件都在中间那个人自己。

所以这一条的前一半是：涵育不求成功。对方长不长开、往不往下给，不预定，不验收。

可能

后一半是：能定、能做、能核的，是可能。

方砚那几次教，每一步讲了理由，说明了她可以改。这是他定得了的：讲不讲理由，留不留她改的位置。这是他做得了的。这也是他核得了的：回头看，那一次他有没有替她把话说完，她提出异议的时候他有没有压回去。

这些合起来，是一个条件：在这个条件里，苏遥自己形成、认领、修订判断，是可能的。她后来走没走那一步，是她的事；那一步走得了，是方砚这一端做出来的。

可以用一句平常话来说：涵育求的不是成功，是可能。这一句里的「求」是平常的用法，说的是把力气用在哪里。它不是这一篇排除的那一种求：那篇专论把提供条件明列在求以外。4 所以在这个系列的命题里，不说「求可能」，说：不求成功；能定、能做、能核的，是可能。

不是不做

不求，容易被听成什么也不做，等着。

道德律系列在机构的尺度上写过一句：等待不是不行动，而是不殖民式准备。5 那是另一个尺度上的话，这里不搬到一环上；但它挡的是同一个误读。方砚不求苏遥长成什么，他并没有因此少做：理由要一步一步讲，位置要实际留着，她改了以后他要回头重看自己的顺序。这些都是事。不求划掉的只是一样：把她的那一步当成自己的工作成果去交付。

第二节 推到传播上

CB39　推到传播上：不求它传下去；能做的是让它传得下去的条件在。涵育这一边的传只在回看里读，与《恰为正和》的恰同形：读得出的，照实记；读不出的，也照实记；回看可以看错

让条件在

方砚盼着那套做法有人接着用，接着改。照上一节，他不求它传下去：谁来接、接了以后怎样带下一个人，他不预定。

他能做的，是让它传得下去的条件在。引言里他做的那几件，可以这样读。

写下来，每一步注理由。接的人拿到的不只是顺序，还有顺序后面的为什么；有了为什么，他才改得动。

标出自己不确定的地方，写明「改掉它」。这是把改的位置留在了纸上。

放在谁都打得开的地方，不署名，不定版本。接的人不必先得到谁的许可。

对苏遥说「那是你的事」。怎样带下一个人，他明说了不归他定。

这几件都在他这一端，定得了，做得了，核得了。它们不保证有人接。可能后来没有人打开过那份东西。

只在回看里读

涵育这一边的传，只在回看里读。那篇专论说本体正和只在回看里读：读得出的，是恰；读不出的，照实记；回看可以看错。1 这里与那一处同形：传没传下去，事先不设，过后回看。

读得出的，照实记。苏遥带了程溪，她自己说出了前一段进了后一段的哪一处；程溪带了新人，摆出两条路让人选。回看下来，这里有两环。

读不出的，也照实记。设那份东西后来没有人打开，苏遥之后没有人再那样带过人。回看下来：到她为止。

回看可以看错，而且有一个方向上的偏。上游写过给的人的一种偏误，叫过度涵育：把对方策略性的表演读成觉醒。6 回看一条链的时候，有同形的偏：把「像」读成「传下来了」。后一个人用着同样的话、同样的步骤，盼着有人接的人容易把这读成站法也到了。第一篇立的那一条正管这里：两段相似还不够，要有材料读出怎样相接。

三个动作

这里排除的求，是三个动作连在一起：预定，照它制作，照它验收。

另一种走法里的《带人守则》，三样都在。预定：每周谈一次，新人提问先反问，新人改了哪一步要报给方砚。照它制作：他要苏遥答应，以后照这个带。照它验收：每个季度他回来，把新人叫来，一条一条问。

预定的是什么，要看清。不是那套排查做法，那是内容，下一节读。预定的是苏遥怎样待下一个人。

要核的：验的是什么

盼望与求，有时只差半步。方砚盼着苏遥以后也给新人留改的位置，这是盼。他把它写成守则、要她答应、到时候来查，这是求。分在哪里？

先排掉一个太粗的答法：有验收的就是求。不是。技师考核，验一次维修合不合技术条件。两个人说好这套做法的改动要登记，到时候对一遍。这些都有验收，都是照常的事，下一节读。

要核的是验的是什么。这个系列的写法是：有没有把对方自己的判断与站法，预先做成一份制作与验收的标准。

《带人守则》验的不是苏遥会不会那套排查做法，是她怎样带人；而且是拿一份清单，来认定她带人的判断成没成立。引言里那一回最清楚：她说了那一回为什么直接答，方砚不核这个理由，只核有没有先反问。她的理由没有被当作理由。

没有验收的盼望，不在排除之内。那篇专论举过：方砚盼着苏遥有一天找到她自己的做法；什么时候，什么样子，他没有定，也不拿来验她；这不是求。7 盼望可以说出口。方砚可以对苏遥说「我盼着你带的人也敢改你的」。说出来，它是一句话，进了苏遥的条件，她怎样处置在她。

换了叫法也在排除之内。苏遥说这像考核，方砚说：「不是考核，是我对你的期待。」那篇专论读过一模一样的一步：不叫那份清单「标准」，改叫「我对你的期待」，照它一条一条验，仍是那里说的求。8 名字换了，三个动作一个没少。

设下了，与验到了

还要分开两件：安排已经设下，与检查实际来到。

方砚把守则交出去、要她答应的那一刻，苏遥怎样带人已经被替她定了。这一次作用朝哪里去，当时就读得出，不必等到季度。三个动作齐不齐，可以另核；已经替她定下的那一处，不因为还没有验而不在。

这一条里，「没有验收的盼望不在其中」上游有。把要核的写成「验的是什么」，照的是那篇专论把界限在对方长成什么上；3 写法是这个系列自己担的。

第四节 替对方预定

CB41　替对方预定他怎样待下一个人，这一处带殖民这个方向，动机不改变这一读。给的人不因给过而在对方怎样对下一个人上占一份

方向

《带人守则》预定的是苏遥怎样待下一个人。这一处，这个系列读作带殖民这个方向。

理由在前面几篇都出现过。怎样待下一个人，是苏遥自己判断的事：对这个新人、这一回，问还是不问，给到哪里。第四篇说，去给本身在用判断。把它预定下来，是替她定了这件事该怎样做；守则定下的那几项上，她照做就够，她的理由不作数：那一回她说了为什么直接答，没有被核。在这几项上，她自己的那一种带法出不来。

同样三项，若是苏遥自己认下的一份安排，她的理由照样被听、被核，就是另一回事：那是两个人认下的具体安排，下一节读。两个人认下的安排可以有约束。要核的不是她能不能随时一个人把它改掉，是她的理由怎样被处置，以及合不合清单是不是被直接记成了她的判断成没成立。

这与第二篇读的是同一个结构：一句「对人就该这样」连在内容里往下。只是这一回，那一句的内容碰巧是「要留空间」「要先反问」。内容朝涵育，递过去的方式朝灌入并占住。第五篇读过同一件事在构里的样子：「你说呢」写进了《带教规范》。

动机不改变这一读

方砚是好意。他是因为盼着这种带法不要断，才写了那一页。

这不改变读数。上游各篇在这一点上是一致的：判在作为实际替对方定下了什么、占住了什么，不在动机与感受。9 好意写成的守则，与恶意写成的守则，在「苏遥还能不能自己判断怎样带人」这一位上，读数相同。

这里有一处要看到：在意涵育能不能传下去的人，离这一步很近。盼着它传，就想把它定下来；一定下来，传下去的成了一页守则。

出路

涵育论有两条正对着这里。对方那一份不替他填：认不认，怎么判，改不改。10 给错了，还没伸进对方那一份的，一经指出就收回；已经伸进去的，收回了，也不改写它发生过。11

苏遥说「这像是考核」，就是指出。方砚换了个叫法，没有收回。所以这一读是有出路的：他可以收回那一页。收回了，那几个季度照实记；此后怎样带人，回到苏遥手里。

不占一份

师生论立过：老师不因教过而在学生的方向里占一份。12 行动论立过：给过条件的人，不因此成了替他完成那一步的人。13 意义论有一句更短：预期施为留下的是条件，不是被寄放的方向。14

推到两段关系上：给的人不因给过，而在对方怎样对下一个人上占一份。方砚教过苏遥；苏遥带程溪，是苏遥与程溪的事。方砚对那一段没有一份发言的资格。他可以有看法，可以说；那是一个同事的看法，不是一个「上游」的权利。

引言里他正是这样答的：「那是你的事。你带程溪的办法，就不是我的办法。」

意义论还有一句检验：他者能够不再围绕我而展开。15 放在这里：苏遥带人，可以完全不提方砚，不像方砚；这一环照样成立。能这样，是方砚那一次做出了的样子之一，不是它的失败。

第五节 内容的接续

CB42　一门手艺的接续可以商量、安排，教的内容学会没有也可以核；这些不替对方预定他怎样待下一个人。把后一件预定下来，照它制作、照它验收，就落在这里排除的求里。以名分相系是其中一种做法，有没有名分本身不作判据。求到内容有人接，不由此读出站法也到了

照常的事

不求，不是说一门手艺有没有人接都无所谓。内容那一层，是可以安排的。

公司把那套排查做法定为标准流程，在全公司推广；安排谁来教，教完考核学会了没有。这些都是照常的事。那篇专论说过：收益层的事照常，两人一起定一件具体的改善，也可以去求；照清单教一套操作，照技术条件验一次维修，本身不是它排除的那一种求。416

方砚与苏遥也可以商量：这套做法以后由谁来维护，改动怎样登记，新人从哪一步学起。两个人都认下的具体安排，不在排除之内。

这些都不替苏遥预定她怎样待下一个人。「新人要学会这十二步」是关于内容的安排；「你必须这样带新人」是另一回事。

规定以外多出来的

公司的评定条件里有一条：不得擅自改动流程；要改，须报技术部核准。这是规定。规定本身，这个系列认而不判。它管的是未经核准去改流程的文本；提出哪一步不对、说出理由、照程序去改，都还在。

这个系列读的，是规定以外、人与人之间多出来的那一处。设程溪发现有一步对新款机器对不上，写了理由，请苏遥报上去。

一种走法：苏遥报了。

另一种走法：苏遥说：「流程不许动。动了，我这个传承人就没了。这事别再提。」规定没有说不许提。她说的比规定多：她把程溪的理由拦在了本来可用的程序外面。读的是这多出来的一处：程溪提出改动的位置，在她这里封上了。

方砚在纸上写的是「谁有更好的办法，改掉它」。同一套做法，可以连着这一句往下，也可以连着「这事别再提」往下。

名分不作判据

「传承人」这样的名分，在各种手艺、各种师门里都有。这个系列不拿有没有名分作判据。

以名分相系，是把往下的那一步预定下来的一种做法：名分只给照做的人，要名分就得照做。但预定不必经过名分。《带人守则》里没有任何名分，三个动作齐全。反过来，名分也可以不带预定：一个名分若只登记「这个人学全了这套做法」，不附带他以后须怎样做、怎样带人，它就只是一条关于内容的记录。

所以读的不是名分，是那三个动作在不在。上游在对接一门宗教传统的时候，读过法脉与印可的谱系，把它读作那一层的治理安排；17 那是构一侧的读法，这个系列只登记，不拿它去指认哪一门传承。

接上了内容，读不出站法

最后一句接第一篇。求到内容有人接，不由此读出站法也到了。公司的标准流程推广到了每一个班组，「方氏排查法」人人会背。这是一件事。那些班组里，有没有人对新人说「这一步你来定」，是另一件事，从前一件读不出。

反过来的也有。第四篇读过鲁迅写藤野。同一篇里他写藤野对他的盼望：小而言之，是希望中国有新的医学；大而言之，是希望新的医学传到中国去。18 这是内容上的盼望。他后来不学医了，原来这条学医的路没有接下去；另一样留下了，他自己写了，进了后来的写作。这条路没有接下去，不由此读出什么都没有留下。

第六节 不抵，不补

CB43　传下来了，不抵其中哪一环的伤害；没有传下去的，照实说没有，不补成总有影响留下；不认证谁在哪一层

不抵

这条链往下传了：方砚，苏遥，程溪，程溪带的新人。回看下来，读得出两环。

链里也有另一样东西往下了。苏遥的「三行够了」占住了程溪记录这一域；程溪带新人，照样说了。

传下来了，不抵其中哪一环的伤害。不说「这条链总的来说是涵育的，那一处不算什么」。那篇专论立过：说一段来往有本体正和，不抵伤害的账。19 冲突论的原话更直：不得用正和去否认刚才那句话确实伤到了人。20 程溪那些没有记下来的东西，是实际少掉的；这一笔记在苏遥与程溪那一段，不因为同一段里有「这一步你来定」而消掉。

第一篇已经立过这条读法的根：链不作承担者，不给一条链合总方向。这里是它在回看时的用法。

不补

反过来的一头：没有传下去的，照实说没有。

设苏遥之后没有人再那样带过人。有一种说法会在这时候出现：「影响总是在的，只是看不见」「种子已经种下了」。这个系列不这样说。那篇专论立过：不补一份看不见的收益；没有得到的，照实说没有得到。19 这里要分两种。有材料确认没有继续的，照实说止于此：苏遥之后带人的那几位都读得到，没有一位那样带。没有材料的，只说目前读到这里；不说止于此，也不说还在。两种都不补。

不补，不是悲观。第一篇说过：一条链断了，不等于此后没有，别处会另起。但「别处会另起」是关于别处的话，不是这条链的延续。这条链到哪里，照实写到哪里。

方砚那几次的所得，也不靠补来保住。第一篇立过：甲那一次的所得在那一次里，不因传没传下去而添减。它本来就不悬在后面。

不认证

最后一个「不」：不认证谁在哪一层。一条链传到了第四个人，不说明其中哪一个人「是 15DD」。读得出的是哪几次作为朝哪里去、打开了什么。

第七节 不求不是记号

CB44　不求不是记号。不求本身不保证什么，也不保证方向；殖民往下传，也可以不是谁的目的。区别不在有没有想它传下去的愿望，在往下的那一步落在哪里

不保证方向

到这里，有一个误读要挡住：不求，是涵育的标志；看一个人求不求，就知道他在哪一边。

不是这样。那篇专论在引言里就写了：不求本身不保证两端都有所得。21 它在对接斯密的地方又说了一句同形的话：不以总量为目的，本身不足以识别 15DD。22

第二篇里的老郭，也没有求。他从来没有想过要把「数字难看就是人不行」传给魏衡，没有预定，没有验收。那一句照样到了魏衡那里，又到了祝澄那里。殖民往下传，也可以不是谁的目的。第一篇开头就写了这一句，这里是它的落处。

所以不求不是记号。一个人不求，读不出他给出去的东西朝哪一向。

愿望也不是

反过来也不成立：有想它传下去的愿望，读不出他在求。

方砚盼着有人接。他把这个盼望说了出来，为它做了许多事。照第三节，他没有把苏遥的判断与站法做成一份验收的标准，这不是求。一个对传承有很深愿望的人，与一个把传承做成守则和考核的人，分别不在愿望的有无、深浅。

落在哪里

区别在往下的那一步落在哪里。

涵育这一边，往下的那一步落在中间那个人自己：他肯受，他长开，他去给；前一个人做的是让这一步可能。殖民那一边，往下的那一步不等中间那个人：他照样做，就过去了。把涵育的传下去预定、制作、验收，是照后一种的样子来做前一种：不等中间那个人。做成了的时候，传下去的已经是另一样东西。

这就是这个系列从头到尾读的那一处结构，在给的人这里的样子。

第八节 史料：维特根斯坦的两句

序言里的一句。 涵育论读过维特根斯坦为《哲学研究》写的序言，其中一句是：他不想用他的书替别人省掉思考；如果可能，他想激发某个人有他自己的思想。23 涵育论读的是这一句的指向。这里读它的另一面：他写下了他不要的那种「传」。读者照着他的结论想，不是他盼的；读者有了自己的思想，才是。这是一句盼望，后面没有验收。

笔记里的一则。 他 1947 年的笔记里有一则，一共三句。他问：是只有他办不成一个学派，还是哲学家都办不成。他接着写，他办不成学派，因为他其实不想被人模仿。末一句加了限定：至少不要被那些在哲学刊物上发文章的人模仿。24

这里只读他怎样写。这一则可以与不把复制自己当作接续的盼望相照。它是哲学写作这个语境里的话，又带着末一句的限定；这里不由它概括他对后来的人一切相似做法的态度，也不拿它来说学派是什么。

这里读不到的也要说清。他后来有没有学生模仿他，那些学生怎样，从这两处读不出；这里不由此说他做到了什么，也不认证他在哪一层。

第九节 收束：全系列读到哪里

这一篇立了七条。合起来说的是：涵育不求成功，对方长不长开、往不往下给，不预定，不验收；能定、能做、能核的是可能。推到传播上，不求它传下去，能做的是让它传得下去的条件在；涵育这一边的传只在回看里读，回看可以看错。排除的求取窄义，预定、制作、验收；要核的是验的是什么，有没有把对方自己的判断与站法做成验收的标准。替对方预定他怎样待下一个人，带殖民这个方向，动机不改变这一读；给的人不因给过而在对方怎样对下一个人上占一份。内容的接续可以商量、安排、核；这些不替对方预定他怎样待下一个人；以名分相系是预定的一种做法，名分本身不作判据。传下来了，不抵其中哪一环的伤害；没有传下去的，照实说没有，不补。不求不是记号，区别在往下的那一步落在哪里。

七篇合起来，读的是一处结构。传是人与人有了关系以后的结果。经过一个人的有三样：内容可以教；占住的那一处往下重复，不等中间那个人；站法教不了，能被看见。带殖民方向的做法可以到一个人为止，那是打断，它有两面，各有来路。涵育往下一环，要中间那个人自己的三件；算到做出了为止；不是债。被看见让后一个人知道那个位置在，站过去仍是他自己的事。面向许多人，仍是一段一段的关系。给的人对这一切，不求成功，做的是让它可能。

这个系列没有做的：没有证明涵育会传下去，也没有证明它传不下去；没有比两边哪一边容易、哪一边快；没有给任何一门传承、任何一个人贴方向；没有读未成年时的那一段，没有读宣传与平台的规则，没有读机构怎样安排传承；没有说受过的人应当往下给，也没有说给的人应当盼或不应当盼。共同体层、跨世代的趋势，是道德律系列读的另一个尺度；与它的界，Paper 0 写。
