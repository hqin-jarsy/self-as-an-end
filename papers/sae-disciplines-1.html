Abstract

The Prequel said that a discipline is not an end. This paper says what parochialism is: a discipline has a construct but does not acknowledge its remainder. It opens with three rule texts side by side: a boundary on the responsibilities of research ethics committees in a United States federal regulation; a provisional report of the International Union of Pure and Applied Chemistry on group 3 of the periodic table; and a European Commission decision on a formal objection. How far each text can take the argument is settled separately later. The establishment of a discipline is a construct, a means; its boundary is the division it draws and maintains, and its purpose is the further development of scholarship (S1). Division leaves a remainder; this is the normal state of things, not a fault (S2). Parochialism is having a construct while not acknowledging its remainder; at the normative layer, the criterion is that a construct is registered as complete. The paper offers a chain for checking this from public records, as a suggested practice, then three contrasts, and real examples from several disciplines of remainders being acknowledged. In the public records this series has examined, no instance with all four steps was found, and the paper says so plainly (S3). One form of parochialism is refusing to take up a question on the grounds that the boundary must be preserved (S4). Normative questioning can be moved outside the boundary by professional affiliation (S5). The logical starting point of parochialism is not the division into disciplines but treating that division as an end (S6). When results come, a claim can be out of reach, or it can escape; escape is what parochialism looks like at the level of theory (S7). Finally, this series does not hold to parochialism itself, and its own rules of evidence have a remainder (S8). Eight propositions in all (S1 to S8), stated at the end of each section and listed together at the end.

Introduction

Start with three documents.

The first is a provision on research ethics review in a United States federal regulation. When an ethics review board (IRB) approves research involving human subjects, it must judge whether the risks to subjects are reasonable in relation to the anticipated benefits. The text then says:

"The IRB should not consider possible long-range effects of applying knowledge gained in the research (e.g., the possible effects of the research on public policy) as among those research risks that fall within the purview of its responsibility." (45 CFR 46.111(a)(2))

This is a clearly written boundary on the responsibilities of a review seat. The text does not name another position to receive questions of this kind.

The second comes from chemistry. Which elements belong in group 3 of the periodic table, below scandium and yttrium, lanthanum and actinium, or lutetium and lawrencium, has been argued over by chemists for decades. The International Union of Pure and Applied Chemistry (IUPAC) set up a project on it. The working group's provisional report says that they concluded "there is no objective means to adjudicate" the matter; that any resolution must grant the scientific community here "a certain degree of conventionality, or choice"; and the report also asks members of IUPAC and other readers for comment (Scerri 2021). IUPAC's current periodic table page still says that the question "has been debated from time to time" (IUPAC, periodic table page). The party that writes the standard has registered the point it cannot settle.

The third comes from the European Union. A harmonised standard for steel storage tanks (EN 12285-2:2005) had its reference published in the Official Journal, so that products made to it were presumed to conform to the relevant requirements. A member state raised a formal objection to the standard, including claims about how such tanks are installed and used. The Commission found that this part of the objection was "inadmissible in the context of formal objections," and would not be taken up in that procedure; the same decision states that member states may prohibit or restrict the installation and use of such tanks (Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2017/1996, recitals 8 and 11).

The three texts are set together not to compare who does better. Each shows something different: how rules mark the boundary of a power, and how an unresolved dispute can be kept on the record. How far each can take the argument is settled later. The first says "this is not handled here" without saying "who handles it"; the second registers the dispute; the third declines to take something up and states that the power lies elsewhere. What the first shows is a checkable form that this paper is concerned with; whether it is parochialism is checked separately by the criterion and checking practice of §3. Stanford University set up an additional Ethics and Society Review, which makes possible risks of research to society a precondition for funding applications (Bernstein et al. 2021), and so takes up part of the kind of question the first text leaves aside. That is a supplementary arrangement; it does not change how the federal regulation itself is written.

The first document concerns research that acts on persons, the second domain in the Prequel's terms. This paper covers every discipline; it opens with that document because its wording is the clearest.

The question of this paper is: when does a discipline that has a construct fail to acknowledge its remainder; what does that look like in institutions; and how can it be told apart from ordinary refusal and ordinary retention. The reading follows the Prequel. §1 says that the establishment draws divisions; §2, that division leaves a remainder; §3, parochialism and its criterion; §4, parochialism keeping questioning outside the boundary; §5 looks at the problem side in the literature of several disciplines; §6 says how normative questioning is moved outside the boundary; §7, how parochialism begins; §8 separates being out of reach from escape; §9 says that this series itself does not hold to parochialism.

§1 The Establishment Draws Divisions

The establishment of a discipline is a construct: a body of people, a course of training, a set of journals and societies, a curriculum, a chain of credentials by which people recognize one another as peers. It is stable and repeatable, carried by positions and procedures, and used to handle the same kind of matter again and again. How Is Institution Possible gives such an arrangement institutional identity, and says that this identity carries no endorsement: the name reports that the conditions of establishment are met; "classification issues no legitimacy" (F6 §2.2). To say that the establishment of a discipline is a construct is to say just this much; it is neither better nor worse for it.

A construct is a means. The first premise of How Is Institution Possible says: "Any institution, including the most elegant constitution, is a means, not an end." (F6 §2.1) This paper takes only one sense of the sentence: the establishment of a discipline does not have itself as its end. What is it for? For the further development of scholarship. Development means that a construct can still be chiseled, and that new constructs can be chiseled out of it (Methodology Paper 2, Chapter 5); it does not need a measure of better and worse fixed in advance. Extending the sentence from institutions to the establishment of a discipline is a step of this series (own undertaking).

A discipline's boundary is the division its establishment draws and maintains: which questions count as belonging to the discipline, which methods count, which results can be cited, who counts as a peer. Division as such is not wrong. For a discipline to go deep, something must first hold steady the premises it uses; it is by dividing into fields that each can go far. This series does not oppose the division into disciplines, and does not propose abolishing boundaries.

A discipline's boundary is the division the establishment draws and maintains. Its purpose is the further development of scholarship: the construct can still be chiseled, and new constructs chiseled out of it. This series does not oppose the division into disciplines and does not propose abolishing boundaries.

§2 Division Leaves a Remainder

Division leaves something behind. The chain of grounds for this sentence is written out in full here.

In the register of The First Critique, the operational postulate says: "this book understands object-cognition as demarcation"; the indestructibility postulate says: "the construct is cognition's one intake of 'this,' and intake must deviate," and the deficit of intake cannot be removed by any treatment (The First Critique, Chapter 4, §2). These two speak of cognition. How Is Institution Possible carried the registration of remainder over into the social order (F6 §2); Institution Theory applied it to the divisions institutions draw, with a restriction: "this generalization governs only this kind of division, and other acts are registered elsewhere" (Institution Theory Z35). The boundary a discipline's establishment draws is exactly this kind of division, made by category and belonging. So this paper takes over the general sentence and extends it to the establishment of a discipline (own undertaking).

That division leaves a remainder is the normal state of things, not a fault. A particular residue has to be recognized from the record, not proved from the postulates alone: the postulates say only "there must be," not "where."

By how a problem arises, two kinds are registered. One kind is what the rules do not cover: phenomena that fall outside the cells of a classification, raised by whoever runs into them. The other kind is produced by the division itself: cut one thing into two fields, and the seam between the two fields is made by that cut; this kind is for the establishment that drew the division to explain. This assignment follows the burden of explanation in Institution Theory Z35. Who raises a problem does not change its kind; the two kinds can also overlap.

Who is inside a standard? By the drawing of Institution Theory Z4: "The interior is drawn by entanglement: those in the entanglement the construct handles, together with those its acts reach who have no way to avoid it by exiting, make up the interior." Only this drawing is taken here; reach of influence is not reach of authority.

S2 Division leaves a remainder. A discipline's boundary divides by category and belonging and leaves its own unaccommodated residue; this is the normal state of things, not a fault. Particular residue is recognized from the record, not proved from the postulates alone. By how problems arise, two kinds are registered: what the rules do not cover, raised by people; what the division itself produces, explained by the establishment that drew it. Who raises a problem does not change its kind, and the two kinds can overlap. Who is inside a standard is drawn by two branches: those in the entanglement it handles, and those its acts reach who cannot avoid it.

§3 Parochialism

The criterion. To define something is itself an operation of non: to mark it off is also to mark off what lies outside it. The problem is not in definition but in what follows it, in taking the definition to be perfect. Then non is used only on others and is not turned back on oneself to ask about one's own remainder. The reflexivity postulate of The First Critique says: "negation can take its own performer and products as objects — tool and object may coincide." Parochialism is performing non without performing reflexivity.

At the normative layer, the criterion is: a construct is registered as complete. The derivation in the Prequel says that registering a construct as complete is filling in, at that point, the position of not-knowing; in its own procedures the construct no longer entertains questioning at that point, and cognition closes there (Prequel §4). To say that this "is a problem" uses the epistemic ought: for a construct whose proper work is cognition, acknowledging its remainder is a condition of its continuing to operate as cognition. This ought presupposes the indestructibility postulate and does not depend on "a human being is an end."

Describe first, then evaluate. A description has to come down to particulars: which premise, in which jurisdiction, at which step. A construct has no first person (F6 §2.3); at the level of an establishment, "not performing reflexivity" is read as there being no position in its arrangements that makes its own divisions the object of questioning. From one refusal it does not follow that an entire body has never turned back on itself.

One way of checking. Parochialism has one form in institutions that can be checked from public records: the chain below. The four steps are a suggested checking practice, and all four must be checkable:

  1. A standard does in fact adopt a premise p, and on that basis classifies, measures, evaluates or decides admission. The objects may be phenomena, instruments, methods, submissions, or persons; p may be a division of objects, a definition of measurement, a criterion of justification.
  2. Questions directed at p are excluded on grounds of disciplinary belonging, of the existing format of evidence, or of "this is not a technical question."
  3. Within the jurisdiction drawn, no other position that can actually be set in motion, and that can require a response or initiate a review, takes the question up. "Or" is used here deliberately: it is weaker than the joint four conditions of Paper 2, S12; this step serves only for recognition and does not imply an ought to set up these functions. Whether a receiving party that is not independent counts is left to Paper 8.
  4. An earlier review did not in fact deal with this challenge, or the scope its conclusion claims for itself falls short of what it is said to have answered, yet the conclusion continues to be used to register this challenge to p as already answered, and the case is closed on that basis.

Only when all four steps are present is an instance found in this way. If a step is not met or not fully checked, the case is not decided on the remaining steps, nor is it inferred that no other form of parochialism exists. Without step 4, the first three steps recognize "a problem with no position to receive it," which is the failure of seats discussed in Paper 4; on the first three steps alone, this chain does not establish parochialism. Other forms of parochialism, such as refusing on the grounds that the boundary must be preserved (next section), and claims of completeness at the level of rules (below), are checked separately against the criterion. Step 4 is the pushing back that Paper 3 discusses under misrouting.

Checkable forms. The following are materials to look at when checking: intake rules that list "not within this discipline" or "unscientific" as grounds for not taking a question up, without saying to whom it is referred; matters raised and left unanswered that are not registered; a standard's text that writes its own scope of warrant as complete, with no section on "limitations and open matters"; rule texts that state themselves that a certain kind of challenge has been answered by a certain review, or that there is nothing to ask outside their scope; initiation of revision restricted to holders of the discipline's credentials; refusal on the basis of the proposer's disciplinary identity.

Among these, writing that something "has been answered by review" or that "there is nothing to ask outside the scope" is a claim of completeness at the level of rules. Writing only a scope of application ("this standard applies to objects of such a kind"), only a procedural end ("this decision is final"), or only that this seat does not review a certain kind of question, does not count: these say "this is not handled here" or "the procedure ends here," not "this has been answered."

Each of these is evidence for the chain, and none decides a case on its own: a credential condition, a single refusal, or the absence of a "limitations" section is not enough. The places to check include the scope clauses of journals, the comment-handling rules of standards committees, the intake clauses of revision procedures, records of how public consultations were handled, the reasons given for returning submissions, and documents of authorization and adoption.

Three contrasts. Where refusal at this seat is accompanied by effective referral, and the receiving position has the capacities listed in step 3 (it can actually be set in motion and can require a response or initiate a review), this paper makes no finding of parochialism on the basis of that refusal. Where all that has been shown is that the rule states the power lies elsewhere, it is recorded as "destination of the power stated," possibly an effective referral, still to be checked. Retention of a premise after sufficient questioning is not judged, on that basis, to be a failure to acknowledge a remainder: staying is not closure; closure is the absence of any possibility of change. A review that gives only a conclusion of limited scope, whose conclusion has not been used to end questioning beyond that scope, is not judged to involve misrouting. A measurement definition with limited scope that claims no answers from outside its boundary is itself a contrast case.

What acknowledging a remainder looks like. At the level of an establishment, acknowledging a remainder can take three arrangements, which are modes at the rule layer: when not taking a question up, stating to whom it is referred; registering known limitations; responding item by item to outside comments. There are real examples in every discipline, and the series does not tar them all with one brush:

  • Chemistry: the provisional report of IUPAC's group 3 working group states that no objective means of adjudication can be found, that any resolution involves a measure of convention, and invites public comment (Scerri 2021).
  • Mathematics: discussion in set theory of whether to accept new axioms openly divides its reasons into two kinds, intrinsic reasons that come from the concept itself and extrinsic reasons that look to the consequences (Maddy 1988, p.482).
  • Medicine: before rating the evidence, GRADE rates which outcomes count and how important each is (GRADE Handbook). This example belongs to the second domain.
  • Physics: the redefinition of the second has a public roadmap and criteria (International Bureau of Weights and Measures). The Particle Data Group records inconsistency between measurements in the error of the average: when data are inconsistent, the error is enlarged by a scale factor (Particle Data Group, Review of Particle Physics).
  • Biology: the International Code of Zoological Nomenclature states in its preamble that its provisions do not restrict taxonomic judgment (ICZN 1999, Preamble).

These examples serve as contrasts, not as proof that "this discipline has no parochialism"; all they show is that acknowledging a remainder can be done in every discipline.

Joining Methodology Paper 2. Methodology Paper 2 says that people get locked into one quadrant, and "Being locked is not a loss of knowledge—it is the force of habit" (§6.1); it also gives "ignorance" an epistemological reading: "Ignorance = not treating the current quadrant's method as complete" (§6.3). What parochialism loses is precisely this ignorance. One possible form of parochialism in institutions is rules of training and evaluation that leave no exit for referral; this is only a checkable form, and on its own it does not establish parochialism.

The state of the instances. Rule texts can show the grounds for closing a case and their limits; to establish whether a particular case went through step 4, one also has to look at the actual record of what was cited and how the case was handled. In the public records this series has examined (scope in Note 1), no instance with all four steps was found; the existing material cannot establish why none was found. What was found are partial chains and contrasts.

One is the stakeholder comments table for the 2017 partial update of the guideline on heavy menstrual bleeding (NG88) by the UK National Institute for Health and Care Excellence (NICE). The table publicly records outside comments and the reply to each. Some replies in it state that a matter falls outside the scope of the 2017 partial update, and in those rows no one is named to whom it is referred (NICE 2017, p.175). They establish only the limited scope of this update, and the absence of a stated referral in those rows; this is close to part of the first checkable form (not taking a question up without saying to whom it is referred), not the whole of it: being outside the scope of this update is not the same as being refused as "not within this discipline" or "unscientific." The table records both challenge and reply publicly: a public record makes the handling checkable, while whether a particular residue is acknowledged is judged separately. So it serves only as an example of a form, not as a finding of parochialism.

Another is the European decision from the Introduction: the objection is not taken up, and the decision states that member states can regulate installation and use. The destination of the power is stated, and it may be an effective referral; whether the member-state position can actually be set in motion, and whether it can require a response or initiate a review, that is, the weaker threshold of step 3, the existing material has not checked.

There are also several kinds of rule provision that look close to step 4 but cannot establish it on the text alone. Step 4 has three elements: there is a challenge to p; the earlier review did not deal with it, or its scope fell short; and that conclusion is used to register the challenge as answered and to close the case. Taking the three elements in turn: NICE's guide to technology appraisal appeals does not allow a second appeal to raise again an appeal point rejected at the first appeal where the relevant content has not changed (PMG41 §4.5); what was handled earlier is exactly that point, so the second element is missing. The US advisory committee on human research protections recommends that an IRB accept the determinations of other review bodies set up for the purpose, "unless it has reason to question their competence or their objectivity" (SACHRP 2024); what was reviewed is the same question, so the second element is again missing. The presumption of conformity in Article 40(1) of the EU Artificial Intelligence Act is limited by its own text to what a standard covers, and likewise lacks the second element. Article 27(2) needs separate treatment: in similar cases a deployer may rely on a previously conducted fundamental rights impact assessment, or on an existing impact assessment carried out by the provider; where the deployer considers that any of the listed elements has changed or is no longer up to date, it must update (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, Article 27(2)). What it waives is an obligation to assess, not the closing of a challenge, so the first element is absent; the second may be present. It is recorded here as a neighboring case not yet settled.

None of these provisions supplies the complete case this paper requires. Whether a closure beyond scope took place still has to be established, for the same premise, by comparing the scope of the original review, the particular challenge, and how the conclusion was actually used; a provision for reopening does not substitute for that comparison.

At the normative layer the criterion is clear. That no complete instance was found shows at least one limit of checking from public records: step 4 lies in the records of particular cases; why none was found remains undetermined. §9 registers this limit as a remainder of the series' method.

S3 Parochialism: the criterion. Parochialism is having a construct while not acknowledging its remainder: using non only on others and not turning it back on oneself. At the normative layer, the criterion is that a construct is registered as complete; this is a problem because, for a construct whose proper work is cognition, acknowledging its remainder is a condition of its continuing to operate as cognition. Describe first from the record (for this premise, in this jurisdiction and at this step), then evaluate by the epistemic ought.

Suggested checking practice: parochialism has one form in institutions that can be checked from public records, a four-step chain (adopting p; questions about p excluded on grounds of belonging or format; no other position within the jurisdiction that can be set in motion and can require a response or initiate a review takes them up; a review conclusion that did not deal with this challenge is used to register it as answered, and the case is closed on that basis). Only when all four steps are present is an instance established; the first three steps alone do not decide a case, and other forms are checked separately against the criterion. Each checkable form is evidence, and no single one decides a case. Three contrasts: refusal with effective referral; retention after sufficient questioning; a review that gives only a conclusion of limited scope.

§4 Keeping Questioning Outside the Boundary

One common form of parochialism is refusing to take a question up on the grounds that the boundary must be preserved. A question is raised, and the answer is: "if this question were taken up, our discipline would no longer be our discipline."

A criterion is given here, following Institution Theory: "The construct's own continued existence is not an independent sufficient reason to close off questioning, or to continue a particular arrangement automatically" (Institution Theory Z38). In Institution Theory this rests on "a human being is an end" by way of Z2, so here it is taken over and rests on the end postulate. There is also a reason pointing the same way, at an earlier layer: a construct has no first person (F6 §2.3); the "I" in "I must continue to exist" cannot be found at the construct. It only points the same way and does not replace the ground above.

Paper 3's pushing back and Paper 8's S44 (membership) join here.

S4 Parochialism keeps questioning outside the boundary. A checkable form: refusing to take a question up on the grounds that the boundary must be preserved. Criterion: the construct's own continued existence is not an independent sufficient reason to close off questioning.

§5 The Problem Side in Several Disciplines

This section writes only of literature analyzing the problem side, as material for §7; positive examples have been listed in §3. It is written existentially and by type: which field has which kind of analysis, without comparing which is more serious.

Physics. In fundamental physics, "naturalness" was for a time a criterion for choosing theories: if a theory's parameters could match observation only through extremely fine cancellations, the theory was regarded as unnatural and as needing new physics to explain it. Fischer analyzed the situation of this criterion after the results of the Large Hadron Collider: finding no new physics weakened support for it, and its weight in argument declined; he also discussed reconsideration of its foundations (Fischer 2023). This is his analysis. Whether it is the escape discussed in §8 requires checking the original commitment to testing and the record of how it was handled afterwards; a decline in weight alone does not decide it. Ellis and Silk argued in Nature that physics must hold to empirical testability and cannot exempt a theory from testing because it is elegant (Ellis & Silk 2014). Several current project documents do not invoke naturalness as a justification.

Biology. Gould and Lewontin criticized a research programme that breaks an organism into separate traits and writes an adaptive story for each; when one story fails, another is substituted, and the question whether the trait is not a product of adaptation at all is seldom asked (Gould & Lewontin 1979, pp.586–587).

Mathematics. In mathematics, progress in research is often counted as the number of theorems proved. Thurston set this out as an assumption to be examined: "progress made by mathematicians consists of proving theorems"; he said that what mathematicians really aim to do is "advance human understanding of mathematics," and that "our strong communal emphasis on theorem-credits has a negative effect on mathematical progress" (Thurston 1994). The sociologist Merton had long before analyzed disputes over priority in science (Merton 1957). On how proofs are verified, Voevodsky wrote of his own experience and concerns (Voevodsky 2014). The credit standard of mathematics is only interfaced with here; how research is evaluated is a matter for the Theory of the University.

All of these are only interfaced with; no corresponding rule texts were found. Seen together, examples on the problem side come mostly from literature analysis, while examples on the positive side are mostly rule texts. The two sides differ in their sources and in their units of analysis; whether this distribution comes from differences in the objects themselves, from how things are recorded, or from how the examples were selected, the existing material cannot tell.

§6 Normative Questioning Moved Outside the Boundary

This section speaks only of the second domain, standards that act on persons, and offers only a conditional account.

Once fields are divided, the work of the normative layer can also be made into a field of its own: ethics, normative philosophy. If so, its questions can be published in its own journals; they are asked, but they cannot be asked into the procedures in which standards are written and adopted. Other disciplines can decline to take them up on the grounds that "this is not our discipline's question," which is exactly step 2 of §3; and normative research, for its part, may not regard entering those procedures as its business. Research that takes such questions up can exist, but it may not obtain the right of response and the right of review needed to enter those procedures.

This paper found no corresponding case records, so it goes only as far as the conditional. It has two neighbors. In his 1931 inaugural address as director of the Institute for Social Research, Horkheimer proposed sustained cooperation between philosophical questions and specialized empirical research as a way of organizing research (Horkheimer 1931). Habermas discussed philosophy's roles as "stand-in" and "interpreter" (Habermas 1983). Both are neighbors only: they concern the relation between normative work and the organization of fields; how they correspond to the right of response and the right of review spoken of here, this paper does not judge.

S5 Normative questioning is moved outside the boundary by professional affiliation. When the work of the normative layer is made into a field of its own, its questions can be asked but cannot be asked into the procedures in which standards are written and adopted; other disciplines can decline them as lying outside the boundary, and normative research may not enter either. Research that takes them up can exist, but may not obtain the right of response and the right of review needed to enter those procedures. This is a conditional account of a mechanism, for the second domain only.

§7 How It Begins

For how parochialism begins, this paper takes a logical starting point, not a historical one. The starting point is not the division into disciplines; it is treating the division as an end.

A common form is "science" turning from a method into a boundary: questions outside the boundary are called "unscientific," and so need not be answered. Gieryn called this kind of work boundary-work, and studied how scientists set their own activity apart from other kinds of knowledge (Gieryn 1983); his research is a source of evidence for this form.

This starting point shows itself in two ways, both written existentially and by type.

In the disciplines concerned with persons, one type can be seen: the a posteriori route supplants the a priori route, so that only the route from phenomena is recognized as knowledge, the route from principle is said not to be knowledge, and the route that asks about the ought from experience is not taken either, because it lies outside the scope of warrant. This paper gives no rule-text instance of this type.

In the literature of the natural sciences and mathematics there are analyses of two other forms. In one, the part not conferred by experience is not registered, and when results come they are not accepted; naturalness and the adaptationist programme in §5 are literature discussing this form, and whether they amount to escape is judged separately in §8 by the record of verification. In the other, a construct is treated as an end in itself, as in the credit standard of mathematics. These are all only interfaced with and have no corresponding rule texts; each discipline also has positive examples of acknowledging remainders (§3).

Motives and historical claims such as "disciplines wrote standards in order to qualify as science" are not relied on by this paper. How parochialism persists once begun is the business of Paper 5.

S6 How it begins. The starting point is taken logically, not historically: not the division into disciplines, but treating the division as an end; "science" turning from a method into a boundary, with questions outside it called "unscientific." In the disciplines concerned with persons, one visible type is the a posteriori route supplanting the a priori route, for which this paper gives no rule-text instance; in the literature of the natural sciences and mathematics there are analyses of the part not conferred by experience going unregistered and results not being accepted when they come, and of a construct being treated as an end in itself, all only interfaced with.

§8 Out of Reach and Escape

When results come, a claim can treat them in two ways.

Out of reach. The claim has not yet been tested, and it has not refused testing; when results come, it changes according to them. As an illustration, take the "island of stability" of superheavy elements: a region of nuclei predicted in theory to be longer-lived; as new data come in, the predicted center moves with the data. Such a claim is always waiting for its test, and when the test comes it lets the test count.

Escape. The original commitment to verification is no longer borne: the test comes, and the claim neither concedes nor revises; the open question is treated as needing no handling. A common sign is simply losing weight: not revised, not withdrawn, gradually no longer mentioned in later arguments. But losing weight does not decide a case on its own: a claim can lose weight because testing has worked as it should. For naturalness in §5, what Fischer analyzed is its decline in weight after the results; whether it is escape requires checking the original commitment to testing and the later record of its handling, which this paper has not done, so it makes no finding.

By the Prequel's derivation, escape is a claim registering itself as complete: it no longer bears the original test at that point, and at that point the position of not-knowing is filled in. So escape is what parochialism looks like at the level of theory; being out of reach is not.

One can slide into the other. If a center keeps moving to just where it is out of reach, so that every new set of data only pushes it to the next place still out of reach, being out of reach slides into escape.

How can the two be told apart from the record? A suggested practice: where the original conditions of verification and the open status are both retained, record it as out of reach; where the record shows that the original commitment to verification is no longer borne, that changes are unexplained, and that the open question is treated as needing no handling, record it as escape. Whether a claim changed, or whether its weight rose or fell, does not decide a case on its own (Note 2).

Paper 5's shelving is the persistence of that line on the normative ledger in standards that act on persons; escape is its counterpart in the first domain.

S7 Out of reach and escape. When results come, a claim can be out of reach: not yet tested, not refusing testing, changing according to results when they come. It can also escape: the original commitment to verification is no longer borne, and the open question is treated as needing no handling; a common sign is neither revising nor withdrawing, only losing weight, but losing weight does not decide a case on its own. Escape is the claim registering itself as complete, what parochialism looks like at the level of theory; being out of reach is not. Being out of reach can slide into escape.

Suggested checking practice: sort by the record of verification. Where the original conditions of verification and the open status are retained, record it as out of reach; where the original commitment to verification is no longer borne, changes are unexplained, and the open question is treated as needing no handling, record it as escape. Whether it changed, or whether its weight rose or fell, does not decide a case on its own.

§9 This Series Does Not Hold to Parochialism

This series does not look down on any discipline, does not rank philosophy above the others, and does not assign the position that questions the ought to philosophy. To acknowledge a kind of work that runs through every field is not to hand it, by disciplinary identity, to any one of them.

The seat that questions the ought is itself a construct, and it too has a remainder. The construct of the indestructibility postulate is cognition's intake of "this"; a seat is an institutional construct, and between the two lies the step in How Is Institution Possible that carried the registration of remainder over into the social order (Prequel §4). Institution Theory wrote of institutions that "so long as the institution is still dividing, it is still leaving unaccommodated residue" (Institution Theory Z35a); for seats, this paper follows it. A seat cannot treat its own boundary as an end; this is a demand on seats, set out in Paper 9, S52. The drawing of the two domains has also registered its own unaccommodated residue (Prequel §5).

The series' rules of evidence also have a remainder. They use only rule texts and public records. Rule texts can show the form of the first three steps of the chain: how intake rules are written, to whom revision procedures are open, how far a seat's jurisdiction extends. Step 4, however, requires looking at particular cases: rule texts can show the grounds for closing a case and their limits; whether a particular case went through step 4 depends on the actual record of what was cited and how it was handled. So one has to speak precisely: whether something is public cannot by itself decide whether a body acknowledges its remainder. Being publicly visible, acknowledging the particular residue of a given premise, and there being an effective receiver are three separate determinations. The existing material cannot explain why complete instances are lacking; this is evidence neither that parochialism is rare nor that it is common or hidden. That examples on the problem side of the first domain lie mostly in the literature and rarely in rule texts is partly also a product of these rules of evidence.

S8 This series does not hold to parochialism. It does not look down on any discipline, does not rank philosophy above the others, and does not assign the position that questions the ought to philosophy. The seat that questions the ought is also a construct, with a remainder of its own; it cannot treat its own boundary as an end. The series' rules of evidence also have a remainder: being publicly visible, acknowledging a particular residue, and there being an effective receiver are three separate determinations; the lack of complete instances is evidence neither that parochialism is rare nor that it is common.

Where This Paper Stops

This paper does not prove that any particular discipline holds to parochialism; it does not write how disciplines ought to be divided; it does not write the history of how things came to this point; it does not compare disciplines as to which is more serious. The four-step chain of §3 is a way of checking, not the definition of parochialism.

What it hands to the papers that follow: once fields are divided, every discipline has premises that must be held steady, and Paper 2 speaks of the position that holds them and the position that questions them; the pushing back of step 4 in §3 is in Paper 3; "how it persists" is in Paper 5, where the correspondence between escape and shelving is referred back to; membership and independence are in Paper 8; the remainder of the seat that questions the ought is in Paper 9.

The remainder it leaves: no complete four-step instance was found in the public records examined (§3); how the distribution in §5 arose, the existing material cannot say; the overlap of the two kinds of residue in §2 is only registered here, without a scale for telling them apart.

第一节　建制作出划分

学科的建制是一个构：一批人，一套训练，一组期刊与学会，一份课程表，一串把人认作同行的资格。它稳定，可重复，由位置与程序承载，用来反复处理同一类事。《制度如何可能》给这样的安排一个制度身份，并说明这一身份不带背书：名分报告的是成立条件被满足，「分类不发正当性」（F6 §2.2）。说学科的建制是一个构，说的就是这一层；它没有因此变好，也没有因此变坏。

构是手段。《制度如何可能》的第一条前提说：「任何制度，包括最优雅的宪法，都是手段而非目的。」（F6 §2.1）这一篇只取这句话的一义：学科的建制不以它自己为目的。它为了什么？让学术更有发展。发展说的是构还能被凿，凿出新的构（方法论第二篇第五章）；它不需要一把先定的好坏尺子。把这句话从制度推到学科的建制，是这个系列的一步（自担）。

学科的边界，是这个建制作出并维持的划分：什么问题算本学科的，什么方法算数，什么样的结果可以援引，谁算同行。划分本身没有错。一门学科要往深处走，先要有东西把它用的前提稳住；分了科，各门才能各自走远。这个系列不反对分科，也不主张取消边界。

S1　建制是构，边界是划分。 学科的建制是一个构，是手段，不是目的；学科边界是建制作出并维持的划分；它的目的，是让学术更有发展：构还能被凿，凿出新的构。这个系列不反对分科，也不主张取消边界。

第二节　划分留下余项

划分会留下东西。这一句有一条依据链，这里写全。

《第一批判》的登记簿上，操作论公设说：「本书把对象认识理解为划界」；不可消灭公设说：「构是认识对"这"的一次收纳，收纳必有偏差」，其中收纳之亏，不可由任何处理消去（《第一批判》第四章第二节）。这两条说的是认识。《制度如何可能》把余项的登记迁进了社会秩序（F6 §2）；制度论把它用到制度的划分上，并给了一个限定：「这一般化只管这一类划分，此外的施为另行登记」（制度论 Z35）。学科建制作出的边界，正是以类别与归属作出的这一类划分。所以这一篇承用这一般句，并把它推到学科的建制（自担）。

划分留下余项，是常态，不是过错。具体的未尽，要从记录里认，不单凭公设证：公设只说"必有"，不说"在哪里"。

按问题怎样形成，登记两类。一类是规则没覆盖到的：现象跑到了分类表的格子外面，由碰到它的人提出。另一类是分科自己造出来的：把一样东西切成两门，两门之间的那条缝，是切这一刀造出来的；这一类，由作出划分的建制说明。这一分派，承制度论 Z35 的说明之责。提出者是谁，不改变归类；两类也可以交叠。

谁在一条标准的内部？照制度论 Z4 的划法：「内部按纠缠划：构所处理的纠缠里的人，与其施为所及、又无从以退出避开它的人，合为内部」。这里只取这一划法；影响所及不等于权限所及。

S2　划分留下余项。 学科边界以类别与归属作划分，留下它自己的收纳未尽；这是常态，不是过错。具体的未尽从记录中认，不单凭公设证。按问题形成的方式登记两类：规则没覆盖到的，由人提出；分科自己造出来的，由分科的建制说明；提出者的身份不改变归类，两类可以交叠。谁在一条标准的内部，照两支划：处理的纠缠里的人，与施为所及又避不开的人。

第三节　本位

判据。 定义一个东西，本身就是一次非的操作：划出它，也就划出了它的外面。问题不在定义，在定义之后，认为这个定义是完美的。这时它把非只用在别人身上，不转回自己身上，去问自己的余项。《第一批判》的反身公设说：「否定能以自身的施行者与产物为对象——工具与对象可重合。」本位，就是施行了非，而没有施行反身。

在规范层，判据是：一个构被登记为完备。前置篇的推导说，把一个构登记为完备，就是把这一处不知的位置填上；这个构在它自己的程序里不再受理对这一处的追问，认知在这一处封闭（前置篇第四节）。说它"是问题"，用的是认知上的应当：对以认知为本职的构，承认余项是它继续作为认知运作的条件。这一应当以不可消灭公设为前提，不依赖"人是目的"。

先描述，再评价。描述要落到具体处：对哪一个前提，在哪一个辖域，哪一个环节。构没有第一人称（F6 §2.3）；"没有施行反身"在建制上，读作安排里没有一个位置，让自己的划分成为被问的对象。从一项拒收，推不出整个机构从未反身。

一种核查的做法。 本位在制度上有一种可以从公开记录核到的样子，是下面这条链。四步是一种建议的核查做法；四步都要可查：

  1. 某一标准确实采用了前提 p，并据此分类，计量，评价或决定准入。对象可以是现象，仪器，方法，投稿，也可以是人；p 可以是一种对象划分，一个计量定义，一条证成准则。
  2. 针对 p 的问题，以学科归属，现有证据格式，或"这不是技术问题"为由被排除。
  3. 在划定的辖域里，没有另一个实际可以发动，能要求回应或启动复审的位置承接它。这里用"或"，是有意的：比第二篇 S12 说的四条件合取要弱；这一步只作识别，不推出设置这些功能的应当。接收的一方不独立时算不算，交第八篇。
  4. 先前那项审查实际没有处理这项质疑，或者它的结论自述的范围不及所称回答的范围，却被继续用来把对 p 的这项质疑登记为已经回答，并据此结案。

四步全具，才这样核定一个实例；某一步不满足或没核足，不据其余几步定案，也不反推不存在别的本位形态。没有第 4 步，前三步认出的是"有问题，没有位置接"，那是第四篇说的席位不满足；单凭前三步，这条链不据以核定本位。本位别的样子，比如下一节说的以边界存续为由不受理，与下面说的规则一层的完备宣称，照判据另核。第 4 步就是第三篇要讲的错送里往回推的那一种。

可查的形态。 下面几样，是核查时要看的材料：受理规则把"不属本学科""不科学"列为不受理的理由，又不写明转给谁；提出而未应答的事项没有登记；标准文本把自己的保证范围写成完备的，没有一节写"局限与未尽"；规则文本自己写明某类质疑已由某项审查回答，或写明它的范围之外没有要问的；发起修订限于持本学科资格的人；以提出者的学科身份拒收。

其中写明"已由审查回答""范围之外没有要问的"，是规则一层的完备宣称。只写适用范围（"本标准适用于某类对象"），只写程序终结（"本裁决为终局"），只写本席不审某类问题，都不算：它们说的是"这里不管"或"程序到此为止"，没有说"已经答了"。

这几样各是那条链的证据，单凭其中一样，不定案：一条身份条件，一次拒收，或没有"局限"一节，都不够。可查的地方，有期刊的范围条款，标准委员会的意见处理规则，修订程序的受理条款，公开征求意见的处理记录，退回的理由，授权文件与采用文件。

三个对照。 本席拒收，而存在有效转介，即接收的位置具备第 3 步所列的能力（实际可以发动，能要求回应或启动复审），不判本位；只证到规则写明了权能在别处的，记作"权能去向已明示"，可能是有效转介，还要核。前提经过充分的追问以后保留，不判不承认余项：留下来不等于封闭，没有可能改变才是。审查只给限定范围的结论，没有被拿来结束范围之外的追问，不判错送。一个计量定义，范围有限，又没有冒领界外的答案，本来就是对照。

承认余项的样子。 落到建制上，承认余项可以表现为三样安排，它们是规则层的样态：不受理时写明转给谁；登记已知的局限；对外部意见逐条回应。各学科都有真实的例子，这个系列不一棍子打死：

  • 化学：IUPAC 第 3 族工作组的临时报告，写明找不到客观的裁定办法，任何解决都带几分约定，并公开征求意见（Scerri 2021）。
  • 数学：集合论讨论要不要接受新的公理，公开地把理由分作两种，出自概念本身的内在理由，与看它带来什么后果的外在理由（Maddy 1988，p.482）。
  • 医学：GRADE 在评证据以前，先评哪些结局算数，各多重要（GRADE 手册）。这一例属第二层。
  • 物理：秒的重新定义，有公开的路线图与准则（国际计量局）。粒子数据组把几次测量之间的不一致，记进平均值的误差：数据不一致时，用一个比例因子放大误差（粒子数据组《粒子物理评述》）。
  • 生物学：国际动物命名法规在序言里明说，它的条款不限制分类学上的判断（ICZN 1999，序言）。

这些例子作对照，不作"这一学科没有本位"的证明；它们说明的只是，承认余项在每一门学科里都做得到。

与方法论第二篇相接。 方法论第二篇说，人被锁在一个象限里，「被锁住不是知识的缺失，是习惯的力量」（§6.1）；它又给"无知"一个认识论的读法：「无知 = 不把当前象限的方法当作完备的」（§6.3）。本位丢掉的，正是这一份无知。本位在制度上可能的一种样子，是训练与评价的规则不留转介的出口；这只是一种可查的形态，单凭它不定本位。

实例的现状。 规则文本可以显示结案的依据及其边界；要核定某个案是否走完第 4 步，还要看实际的援引与处理记录。在这个系列查过的公开记录里（范围见注 1），没有找到四步完整的一例；现有材料不能确定没有找到的原因。找到的是部分链条与对照。

一份是英国国家卫生与临床优化研究所（NICE）2017 年月经过多指南（NG88）局部更新的征求意见答复表。表里公开登记了外部意见与逐条答复。其中有几行答复说，所提的事不在 2017 年这次局部更新的范围之内，这几行没有写转给谁（NICE 2017，p.175）。它们显示的，只是这一次更新的范围有限，与这几行没有写明转介；这近于可查形态第一种的一部分（不受理而不说转给谁），不是整个第一种：不在本次范围之内，不等于以"不属本学科""不科学"为由不受理。这张表把质疑与答复都公开登记了：公开记录使处理方式可以核，是否承认具体的未尽，另判。所以它只作形态的例子，不判本位。

另一份是引言里的欧盟决定：不予受理，同时写明成员国可以管安装与使用。权能去向已经明示，可能是有效转介；至于成员国那一处是否实际可以发动，能否要求回应或启动复审，也就是第 3 步那较弱的门槛，现有材料没有核到。

还有几类规则条文，看起来与第 4 步相近，单凭条文都不够认定。第 4 步有三个要件：有一项对 p 的质疑；先前的审查没有处理它，或范围不及；这一结论被用来把质疑登记为已答并结案。拿三件去对：NICE 技术评估申诉指南不许二次申诉重提首次已经驳回，所涉内容又没有改的申诉点（PMG41 §4.5），先前处理的正是这一点，缺第二件；人类研究保护咨询委员会建议，伦理审查委员会接受另设审查机构的认定，「unless it has reason to question their competence or their objectivity」（SACHRP 2024），审的是同一问，也缺第二件；《人工智能法》第 40 条第 1 款的符合推定，条文自己限于标准覆盖的范围，同样缺第二件。第 27 条第 2 款要单独交代：类似情形下，部署者可以依赖先前做过的基本权利影响评估，或提供者已有的影响评估；部署者认为所列的要素有变或过时，要更新（Regulation (EU) 2024/1689，第 27 条第 2 款）。它免的是一项评估义务，不是结一项质疑，第一件不在场；第二件可能在场。这里把它记作一个尚未核定的近邻。

这几类条款，都没有提供这一篇所需的完整个案。是否发生了范围之外的结案，仍要对同一项前提，比对原审查的范围，具体的质疑，与结论实际怎样被使用；条文里有重开的条件，也不代替这项比对。

判据在规范层是清楚的。找不到完整的实例，至少显出公开记录核查的一处限度：第 4 步在个案记录里；没有找到的原因仍不能确定。这一限度，第九节登记为这个系列方法的余项。

S3　本位：判据。 本位，是有了构却不承认它的余项：把非只用在别人身上，不转回自己身上。在规范层，判据是一个构被登记为完备；它是问题，因为对以认知为本职的构，承认余项是它继续作为认知运作的条件。先据记录描述（对这个前提，在这个辖域与环节），再据认知上的应当评价。

建议的核查做法：本位在制度上有一种可以从公开记录核到的样子，是一条四步的链（采用 p；对 p 的问题以归属或格式为由被排除；辖域内没有另一个可发动，能要求回应或启动复审的位置承接；一项未处理这一质疑的审查结论被用来把它登记为已经回答，并据此结案）；四步全具才核定；单凭前三步不定案，别的样子照判据另核。可查的形态各是证据，单凭一样不定案。三个对照：存在有效转介的拒收；经充分追问后的保留；只给限定范围结论的审查。

第四节　挡在界外

本位有一种常见的形态：以边界的存续为由不受理。一个问题提出来，回答是"这一问若受理，本学科就不成其为本学科了"。

这里给一条判据，承制度论：「构自身的存续，不构成关闭追问、或自动续存一项具体安排的独立充分理由」（制度论 Z38）。这一条在制度论里经 Z2 依"人是目的"，所以在这里是承用，依目的公设。还有一条同向的理由，在更早的一层：构没有第一人称（F6 §2.3），"我要存续"里的那个"我"，在构这一处找不到。它只作同向，不替代上面的依据。

第三篇的往回推，第八篇的 S44（成员身份），与这里相接。

S4　本位把追问挡在界外。 可查的形态：以边界的存续为由不受理。判据：构自身的存续，不构成关闭追问的独立充分理由。

第五节　几门学科的问题一侧

这一节只写问题一侧的文献分析，为第七节准备材料；正面的例子，第三节已经列过。写法是存在式加类型：哪一门有哪一类分析，不比哪一门更严重。

物理。 基础物理里，"自然性"一度是挑选理论的一条准则：一个理论的参数，若要靠极精细的抵消才能和观测对上，就被看作不自然，需要新的物理来解释。Fischer 分析过这条准则在大型强子对撞机的结果以后的处境：没有发现新的物理，削弱了对它的支持，它在论证里的分量下降了；他也讨论了对它的基础的重新反思（Fischer 2023）。这是他的分析。它是不是第八节说的逃开，要核原来的检验承诺与其后的处理记录，不能只凭分量下降判。Ellis 与 Silk 在《自然》上撰文，主张物理学要守住可经验检验这一条，不能因为一个理论优美，就免去检验（Ellis & Silk 2014）。现行的几份立项文件，不以自然性为理由。

生物学。 Gould 与 Lewontin 批评过一种研究纲领：把生物体拆成一个个性状，给每一个性状各编一个适应的故事；一个故事不成立，就换另一个，很少去问这个性状是不是根本不是适应的产物（Gould & Lewontin 1979，pp.586–587）。

数学。 数学里，研究的进展常被算作证出了多少定理。Thurston 把这一点列为一个待检验的假定：「progress made by mathematicians consists of proving theorems」；他说数学家真正要推进的，是「advance human understanding of mathematics」，并说「our strong communal emphasis on theorem-credits has a negative effect on mathematical progress」（Thurston 1994）。社会学家默顿早就分析过科学里的优先权之争（Merton 1957）。证明怎样被核验，Voevodsky 写过他自己的经历与担心（Voevodsky 2014）。数学的功劳标准，这一篇只作对接；科研评价怎样定，是大学论的事。

这几样都只作对接，没有找到对应的规则文本。并排看，问题一侧的例子多来自文献分析，正面一侧的例子多是规则文本。两侧的来源与分析单位不同；这种分布出自对象本身的差别，出自记录的方式，还是出自选取的过程，现有材料不能区分。

第六节　规范性的追问被移到界外

这一节只说第二层，作用到人的标准；而且只作一个条件性的说明。

分科以后，规范层的工作也可以被做成一门：伦理学，规范哲学。若是这样，它的问题可以发表在本门的期刊里，问了，却问不进写标准与采用标准的程序。别的学科可以拿"这不是本学科的问题"作理由不受理，那正是第三节第 2 步；规范研究的一侧，也未必把走进那些程序当作本门的事。承接它的研究可以存在，但未必取得进入那些程序的回应权与复审权。

这一篇没有找到对应的个案记录，所以只写到条件句为止。它有两个近邻。霍克海默 1931 年就任社会研究所所长的演讲，提出让哲学问题与专门的经验研究持续协作，作为一种研究组织的方案（Horkheimer 1931）。哈贝马斯讨论过哲学作为"占位者"与"解释者"的角色（Habermas 1983）。两者都只作近邻：它们说的是规范工作与学门组织的关系；它们与这里说的回应权，复审权怎样对应，这一篇不判。

S5　规范性的追问被按专业归属移到界外。 规范层的工作被做成一门时，它的问题可以问了，却问不进写标准与采用标准的程序；别的学科可以拿界外作理由不受理，规范研究的一侧也未必走进去。承接的研究可以存在，但未必取得进入那些程序的回应权与复审权。这是条件性的机制说明，只对第二层。

第七节　怎么开始的

本位怎么开始，这一篇取逻辑上的起点，不取史实。起点不是分科，是把分科当作目的。

一种常见的样子是，"科学"从一种方法，变成了一条边界：界外的问题被说成"不科学"，于是不必回答。Gieryn 把这一类工作叫作划界工作，研究科学家怎样把自己的活动与别的知识活动分开（Gieryn 1983）；他的研究是这一形态的证据来源。

这一起点有两种表现，都写存在式加类型。

在关于人的学科里，可以看到一种类型：后验一路顶替先验一路，只承认从现象出发的那一路是知识，从原理出发的那一路，被说成不是知识；从经验出发去问应当的那一路，也因为不在保证范围里而不走。这一篇没有给出这一类型的规则文本实例。

在自然科学与数学的文献里，有对另外两种样子的分析。一种是不由经验赋予的部分不登记，结果来了也不认；第五节的自然性与适应主义纲领，是讨论这一样子的文献材料，是否构成逃开，第八节照核验记录另判。另一种是一个构被当成了目的本身，例如数学的功劳标准。这些都只作对接，没有对应的规则文本；各学科也都有承认余项的正面例子（第三节）。

"学科为了取得科学的资格才去写标准"一类动机与史实，这一篇不靠它立论。本位开始以后怎样持续，是第五篇的事。

S6　怎么开始的。 起点取逻辑，不取史实：不是分科，是把分科当作目的；"科学"从一种方法变成一条边界，界外的问题被说成"不科学"。在关于人的学科里，可以看到的一种类型是后验一路顶替先验一路，这一篇没有给出规则文本的实例；在自然科学与数学的文献里，有不由经验赋予的部分不登记，结果来了也不认，以及构被当成目的本身的分析，都只作对接。

第八节　够不着与逃开

结果来了以后，一个主张怎样对待它，有两种样子。

够不着。 主张还没被检验到，它没有拒绝检验；结果来了，它照结果改。以超重元素的"稳定岛"作示意：一片理论上预言寿命较长的原子核区域，新的数据出来，预言的中心就跟着数据移动。这样的主张一直在等检验，检验来了，它就让检验算数。

逃开。 原来的核验承诺不再承担：检验来了，它既不认输，也不修正，未决的问题被当作不必处理的。常见的表现是只失去分量：不改，也不撤，在后来的论证里慢慢不再被提起。可失去分量本身不定案：一个主张失去分量，也可能是检验正常起了作用。第五节说的自然性，Fischer 分析的是它在结果之后分量下降；它是不是逃开，要核原来的检验承诺与其后的处理记录，这一篇没有核到，不作判定。

照前置篇的推导，逃开是一条主张把自己登记为完备：原来那一处检验，它不再承担，在这一处，不知的位置被填上了。所以逃开是本位在理论这一层的样子；够不着不是。

两者之间可以滑动。一个中心若一直移到刚好够不着的地方，每一次新数据都只把它推到下一处还够不着的位置，够不着就会滑向逃开。

怎样从记录里分辨？这里给一种建议的做法：保留原来的核验条件，也保留未决状态的，记作够不着；记录显示原来的核验承诺不再承担，变化没有说明，未决的问题被当作不必处理的，记作逃开。单凭改没改，或者分量升了降了，不定案（注 2）。

第五篇的搁置，是作用到人的标准里，规范账上那一行的持续；逃开是它在第一层的对应。

S7　够不着与逃开。 结果来了以后，一个主张可以够不着：还没被检验到，没有拒绝检验，结果来了照结果改；也可以逃开：原来的核验承诺不再承担，未决的问题被当作无须处理；常见的表现是不改，不撤，只是失去分量，但失去分量本身不定案。逃开是这一主张把自己登记为完备，是本位在理论这一层的样子；够不着不是。够不着可以滑向逃开。

建议的核查做法：按核验记录分，保留原核验条件与未决状态的，记作够不着；原核验承诺不再承担，变化未获说明，未决问题被当作无须处理的，记作逃开；单凭改没改或分量升降，不定案。

第九节　这个系列不守本位

这个系列不看低任何一门学科，不以哲学为高，也不把问应当的位置派给哲学。承认一项贯穿各门的工作，不等于按学科身份把它专派给哪一门。

问应当的席位，自己也是一个构，也有余项。不可消灭公设说的构，是认识对"这"的收纳；席位是制度的构，中间要经《制度如何可能》把余项登记迁入社会秩序的那一步（前置篇第四节）。制度论在制度上写过这一句：「制度还在划分，就还在留下收纳未尽」（制度论 Z35a）；席位这里承它。它不能把自己的边界当作目的；这是对席位的要求，在第九篇 S52 立。两层定义域的划界，也登记了它自己的收纳未尽（前置篇第五节）。

这个系列的证据规则也有余项。它只用规则文本与公开记录。规则文本显得出那条链前三步的形态：受理规则怎样写，修订程序向谁开放，席位的辖域划到哪里。第 4 步却要看个案：规则文本可以显示结案的依据及其边界，某个案是否走完第 4 步，要看实际的援引与处理记录。所以要把话说准：公开与否，不能单独判一个机构承不承认余项。公开可见，承认这一个前提的具体未尽，存在有效的承接，是三个不同的判项。现有材料不能说明缺少完整实例的原因；它既不作本位少见的证据，也不作本位多见或被藏起来的证据。第一层问题一侧的例子多在文献里，规则文本里少，部分也出自这一证据规则。

S8　这个系列不守本位。 不看低任何学科，不以哲学为高，不把问应当的位置派给哲学。问应当的席位也是构，也有余项；它不能把自己的边界当作目的。本系列的证据规则也有余项：公开可见，承认具体的未尽，存在有效的承接，是三个不同的判项；缺少完整实例，不作本位少见或多见的证据。

这一篇停在哪里

这一篇不证明某一门学科在守本位；不写学科应当怎样分；不写走到这一步的史实；不比学科之间谁更严重。第三节的四步链是一种核查的做法，不是本位的定义。

交给后面各篇的：分了科，每一门学科都有要稳定下来的前提，第二篇说稳住它的位置与问它的位置；第三节第 4 步的往回推，在第三篇；"怎样持续"，在第五篇，逃开与搁置的对应，在那里回指；成员身份与独立，在第八篇；问应当的席位自己的余项，在第九篇。

留下的余项：四步完整的实例，在查过的公开记录里没有找到（第三节）；第五节的分布是怎么来的，现有材料说不清；第二节两类未尽的交叠，这一篇只登记，不给判别的刻度。
