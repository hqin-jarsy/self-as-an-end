Abstract

This paper reads the layer of recognizing her as an end too: she has her own cannot-not. In SAE's levels, this layer is written 15DD. Love at this layer has two things in it: the cannot-not toward this one, irreplaceable, and the recognition of her as her own end; both hold on my side, and one-way is enough for it to hold. Inwardly, I do not need her to tell me that I love rightly; outwardly, I do not wait for her to love me first; my eyes need not keep returning to myself. She has her own cannot-not; I only recognize that it is there, and cannot read out its content; observation and communication can approach it, but reading it out is not possible.

Love at this layer is persistently exclusive and does not waver: this is this series' stance, and its ground lies in recognizing her as an end too: she is not my means of obtaining love; it is not derived from the general structure of this layer. This layer is not easy to achieve to begin with; it is a direction: in each moment in which this layer operates, the one loved is always she; operation may be interrupted, and falling back is very likely. Love at this layer does not demand exclusivity of her; whether she is exclusive can be decided only by her. Exclusivity belongs to love, not to recognition: the share of recognizing her as an end too does not grow smaller because one loves this one.

My cannot-not is not her must: her not loving me does not diminish my share of recognizing her; my loving her does not add a duty for her either; leaving to her is not submitting to whatever harm she does. The direction of colonization read from this layer is judging her law for her in the name of love, and demanding endless endurance in the name of love; the counterfeit that borrows this layer's name is accommodation, in which one's own share is handed over. Love at this layer can end; she leaves, and I acknowledge her leaving; exit does not delete recognition.

This paper interfaces with Kant, Velleman, Beauvoir, Murdoch, Fromm, Kierkegaard, Mencius with Yi Zhi, Zhuangzi and Wang Bi. It does not prove that unrequited love is higher than loving each other, does not prove that love at this layer is enough without needing a response, and does not prove that one ought to let go.

Introduction

She played the cello in an orchestra; he taught physics at a high school; they had been together four years. That spring, an orchestra in another city invited her. To go would mean two cities; not to go, and the chance would not come again.

She thought it over for a week. He did not think it over for her. One evening she asked him: do you want me to go? He said: I want you to stay; that's mine. Whether to go is yours.

Later, she went. In the stretch before she left, there were a few nights when he could not sleep; once, when she spoke of a colleague in that orchestra with whom she was to share the stage, a sentence rose in him, "You can only be mine", which he did not say aloud; after a while he himself felt it was wrong. After she left, he went on writing to her as before, and did not, in his letters, reckon up the accounts of the four years.

What this paper reads is that point at this layer: he loves her, and recognizes her as her own end; he said his own share, and left her share to her; he did not wait for her to love him first, nor did he use his love to add a duty for her.

The place of this layer in the series. Paper 4 reads the layer of purpose: with purpose, that cannot-not persists, and she has become where I am going. Paper 4 also reads, at that layer, the most refined direction of colonization: taking my direction as her ruler; what I cannot exchange, you may not exchange either. Aesthetics Ray 11 stops at the same place and leaves a question: my cannot-not is not your must; do others have their cannot-not too?1 This paper takes it up from there: she too has her cannot-not. The upstream work that reads this layer is Ray 12, The Beauty of Non Dubito: a person recognizes you as an end too, without waiting for you to recognize him first. Ray 12 asks what it looks like, when seen, for a person to stand and also to leave your share to you; this paper asks how such recognition operates in love. The ray reads recognition; this paper reads love.2 In SAE's levels, this layer is written 15DD.

The layers of this series are not a path that draws steadily closer to her. Papers 3 and 4 are the ones that most easily circle back to myself: in Paper 3 she becomes my mirror; in Paper 4 my direction becomes her ruler. Only in this paper does it come back to her as her own end. Irreplaceability at this layer is like this: she has her own cannot-not; I love her without waiting for her to love me first; it is persistently exclusive, and yet does not demand exclusivity of her.

Method. As in Paper 1: upstream conclusions are said in plain words; sources, the proposition numbers of other papers and the notes on extension go in the notes. A layer records one operation; it does not record a person, nor a love: this layer is not a truer love, and his few sleepless nights do not overturn anything. This paper reads only structure, and does not speak of good or bad. The cellist and the physics teacher run through the whole paper; §3 sets up a further example, of unrequited love.

§1 Love at This Layer

L33 Love at this layer has both the cannot-not toward this one, irreplaceable, and the recognition of her as her own end; I love her this way without waiting for her to love me this way first. That it holds one-way means that these two hold on my side; it does not mean that recognizing her as an end on its own constitutes love

The recognition Ray 12 writes of is this: "He recognizes you as an end too, without waiting for you to recognize him first."3 This recognition is not had only toward the one loved: a teacher recognizing a student as an end too, a person recognizing a stranger on the road as an end too, are all this recognition. So recognizing her as an end, on its own, is not yet love. Ray 13 says it clearly: the share of recognizing others as ends too does not grow smaller because one has a friend.4

Love at this layer has two things in it. One is what has been there since Paper 1: this one, at this moment, irreplaceable; exchanging her for someone just as good will not do. The other is had only at this layer: recognizing her as her own end; she is not there in order to fulfill this love of mine.5 The physics teacher's words have both. "I want you to stay; that's mine": she is irreplaceable to him; he cannot exchange her. "Whether to go is yours": her road is hers, not a part of this love of his.

The two together make love at this layer. If that cannot-not has already become a persisting direction, while at this moment she is not yet recognized as her own end, it is read by Paper 4. Without the share toward this one, irreplaceable, it is recognition, not the love meant here.

One-way is enough for it to hold. Both are on my side. Whether she loves him is not a condition of this layer's holding; this series reads love from the subject's side, as Paper 1 has already laid down.6 Relation Theory, reading mutuality, says that mutuality is two one-way recognitions meeting; if they do not meet, each one's share is still there as before.7 His share, if she does not love him, is still there as before.

How this layer is told apart from Paper 4. The same "I love you" can be at the layer of Paper 4, and it can also be at this layer. Upstream, reading laughter, divided at the same point: doing things for you is an investment at the layer of purpose; it is confirming that you are an independent end that is at this layer; which layer it is depends on this one operation, not on the name "love".8 The physics teacher did many things for her: he carried her cello, he revised her résumé; all of these can just as well be had at the layer of Paper 4; it was that evening's "Whether to go is yours" that was the instant of this layer. Which layer a moment is at is seen in that operation, not in whether the word "love" is used.

L34 Inwardly, I do not need her to tell me that I love rightly; outwardly, I do not wait for her to love me first. My eyes need not keep returning to myself

Ray 12 divides non dubito into two halves: inwardly, not waiting for your nod; outwardly, not waiting for you to recognize him first.9 This paper reads love by it.

The inward half is that he does not need her to confirm that he loves rightly. This does not mean he cannot be wrong: the "You can only be mine" that rose in him that night was wrong, and he saw it himself. Nor does it mean that her words do not count for him. Ray 12 divides "confirmation" into three senses: only when you have nodded can I judge; you come to check my reasons; my becoming someone who can judge depends on you. It takes away only the first.10 Love at this layer also takes away only the first: her response enters his conditions of occurrence, and is material, not a settling of the account.11 He loves her without taking as a precondition that she first say "You love rightly".

The outward half is not waiting for her to love him first. His saying "I want you to stay" is not an opening offer in an exchange: only if you love me this way too will I love you this way. She went, and his share was there all the same.

The two halves together are not waiting for her. SAE: The First Critique recorded the name of this station, on the side of doing, as forgetting the self.12 Ray 12 writes of this point: not waiting for your nod, nor for you to recognize him first, "he has no need to look back at himself".13 Love is like this too. Ray 12 says: "Someone who always needs people to nod, and needs others to recognize him first, has eyes that keep falling back on himself."14 In love, the one who always needs her to nod, and needs her to love him first, is like this too: whether I love enough, whether she is worth it, whether it is the same for her. Only by not waiting for her are the eyes free, to rest on how she thought it over that week, and on what she wants.

One thing must also be kept apart here. Not waiting for her does not mean not wanting her to love him. Of course he wants it; the wanting is his, and belongs to the persisting cannot-not of Paper 4. What this layer says is: he loves her without making her love a precondition.

L35 She has her own cannot-not; I only recognize that it is there, and cannot read out its content; loving her is not reading her through: observation and communication can approach it; reading it out is not possible

Why she wanted to go to that orchestra, he could not fully say. She had talked about its concertmaster, about its repertoire, about wanting to try once before she was thirty-five; he had listened to all of it, yet what it was that made her have to go, he could not read out.

Upstream puts the information of this layer precisely: in another person only one thing can be read out: that he is an end, that a purpose-source is present in him; what his own cannot-not holds cannot be read out. The part that cannot be read out is inexhaustible, yet it can be approached through observing his behavior, and even through communication.15

So loving her is not reading her through. What he recognizes is that a purpose-source is present in her, and that it is hers; what is in it, he can approach little by little, watching her practice, listening to her, asking her, and still not read it out. To say "I know what you want better than you know it yourself" is to speak of approaching as if it were reading out; once the words are out, they fix for her what she wants, and at that point they head in the direction of colonization (L40).16

§2 Persistent Exclusivity

L36 Love at 15DD is persistently exclusive and does not waver, because she is recognized as an end too and is not my means of obtaining love; this "because" is not read as deriving "does not waver" from "not a means": 15DD is not easy to achieve to begin with; it is a direction. Persistence means: in each moment in which 15DD operates, the one loved is always she, not alternating between two people; operation may be interrupted, and when it is, it is entered separately by the operation of that occasion and is not on that account called wavering; if the moments of love alternate between two people, it is still entered as wavering; falling back is very likely

Persistent exclusivity is this series' stance, not an inference. The "because" in the proposition states where this stance stands: she is recognized as an end too, and she is not my means of obtaining love.

The "because" of this sentence must be read exactly. Paper 4 reads wavering: two directions coexist, the moments of love alternate between two people, and it is still uncertain. The one who wavers does not thereby treat a person as a means; what treats a person as a means is "anyone will do", and that kind is in Paper 3. So "does not waver" is not derived here from "not a means": the one who wavers does not treat anyone as a means either.

The other half of this sentence is: 15DD is not easy to achieve to begin with; it is a direction. This "direction" means the place one is heading toward and has not yet reached, not something present at every moment. Ray 13, in one place, uses the word in the same sense: among many, it is still a direction.17 It differs from Paper 4's direction: Paper 4's direction is the one that has come and that one cannot not follow, and it belongs to the layer of purpose; it also differs from the two directions Relation Theory speaks of, cultivation and colonization, which are read on one effect.

Written this way, not alternating at this layer looks like this. Wavering is still choosing which one, and is still within the frame of the layer of purpose: for him neither of the two can be exchanged, and he has not yet decided which one he is going toward; Paper 4 has read this.18 In the moments when 15DD operates, he is not still choosing between two people; this does not deny that this love has a history of choice. What keeps others out is the cannot-not toward this one, irreplaceable; the share of recognizing her as her own end is not exclusive (L38). So "not alternating" describes what those moments look like; it is not derived from recognition.19

Nor is persistent exclusivity derived from the general structure of 15DD. One-way recognition is not exclusive (L38); persistent exclusivity does not follow from it; it is a stance, not an inference.

What persistence is. Persistence means: in each moment in which 15DD operates, the one loved is always she, not alternating between two people. It does not say that 15DD operates at every moment. Operation may be interrupted: when he is explaining a problem in class, when he is grading homework, at that moment, love is not operating at any layer. When it is interrupted, it is entered separately by the operation of that occasion, and is not on that account called wavering. If in those moments the moments of love really do alternate between two people, that is still wavering, and is read by Paper 4. Persistent exclusivity does not rule out other operations occurring during a falling back.

Falling back is very likely. The "You can only be mine" that rose in him that night was a moment of falling back to the layer of purpose: my direction became the ruler for where she ought to go. A fit of jealousy, a sentence one wanted to say and did not, may each be such a moment. That 15DD is not easy to achieve and that falling back is very likely: the two fit together. These moments do not overturn that love; they say only that in those moments it operated at another layer. From outside, these are all only clues; the sentence in his mind was never said aloud, and so it did not enter her conditions of occurrence either.

L37 Love at 15DD does not demand exclusivity of her; whether she is exclusive can be decided only by her. When each side operates at 15DD and each is exclusive, two one-way exclusivities have met; this has the same form as the mutuality Relation Theory speaks of, and is not obtained by exchange; mutual exclusivity in itself does not certify 16DD. Each pledging exclusivity, or coming to an agreement on it together, is commitment; using one's own love to set up a duty of exclusivity for her goes back to Paper 4

That he loves only her is his. Whether she too loves only him is hers, and can be decided only by her. He does not trade his exclusivity for hers.

When both are like this, each loving only the other, two one-way exclusivities have met. This has the same form as the mutuality Relation Theory speaks of: mutuality is two one-way recognitions meeting; if they do not meet, each one's share is still there as before; mutuality is not exchange.20 It is not obtained by exchange: it is not that only if you love only me will I love only you.

Mutual exclusivity in itself does not certify that both are at 15DD, still less does it certify that moment of 16DD. Mutual exclusivity can also come from exchange, from fear of loss, from two people's cutting themselves off from others. From outside, it looks the same.

Two people each pledging exclusivity, or coming to an agreement on exclusivity together, is commitment, and does not in every case carry the direction of colonization; how commitment binds a person, and how it does not lock tomorrow, is read by Relation Theory's paper on commitment, Paper 3.21 Exclusivity agreed on and further given institutional protection goes beyond the scope of this series, and belongs to Marriage Theory.

Using one's own love to set up a duty of exclusivity for her, "I love only you, so you have to love only me too", goes back to Paper 4: my direction has become her ruler.

L38 Love at 15DD has two things in it: recognizing her as an end too, a recognition that is not exclusive and does not grow smaller because one loves this one; and the cannot-not toward this one, irreplaceable, which is exclusive

Exclusivity belongs to love, not to recognition.

The physics teacher recognizes her as an end too; he also recognizes his students, his colleagues, a stranger in the street as ends too. This recognition does not grow the least bit smaller because he loves her. What Ray 13 speaks of is friends: the share of recognizing others as ends too does not grow smaller because one has a friend; here it is extended to loving this one.22

What is exclusive is the cannot-not toward this one: at that moment, exchanging her for someone just as good will not do, and in that moment there is no place for anyone else, as Paper 1 said.23

If this division is not written out, love falls into what Ray 13 calls preferential love: two people recognize each other and recognize no one else.24 Two people who love only each other have not fallen into preferential love; two people who recognize only each other have. Kierkegaard brings a pressure to bear here; §5 takes it up.

§3 Without Waiting for You, and Without Submitting to You

L39 My cannot-not is not her must: her not loving me does not diminish my share of recognizing her as an end too; my loving her does not add a duty for her either. Leaving to her is not submitting to whatever harm she does

A further example is set up here. A woman who works as a translator loves an editor with whom she has frequent dealings. He does not love her, and has said so clearly.

Ray 11, at the layer of purpose, saw one sentence: my cannot-not is not your must; it stopped there, and left whether others have their cannot-not too to the beauty of non dubito.25 This sentence was handed to this layer from the start.

His not loving her does not diminish her share of recognizing him as an end too. Her loving him is hers; his not loving her is his. She does not use his not loving her to change what she takes him to be.

Nor does her loving him add a duty for him. Relation Theory says that expectation does not turn into a duty, and that commitment does not turn into a duty to the other party either.26 Love comes earlier than both: she loves him, and he does not on that account owe her anything, not a response, not a love of the same kind.

Leaving to him is not submitting to whatever harm he does. For a while the editor, knowing how she felt, would often send her rush jobs late at night with a "You're the best", wanting them first thing the next morning. She recognizes him as an end too, but this recognition does not require her to take on everything. Ray 12 says: "Leaving to you is not submitting to whatever harm you do. Refusing, walking away, protecting oneself can all go together with recognizing you as an end too."27 She can decline that job, can stop answering messages at that hour, can walk away. Neither refusing nor leaving takes back her share of recognizing him.

L40 The direction of colonization read from this layer is judging her law for her in the name of love, and demanding endless endurance in the name of love. The counterfeit that borrows this layer's name is accommodation: one's own share handed over; it is not 15DD; it is not having stood. A long absence of quarrels is not evidence of cultivation

The direction of colonization read from this layer takes two shapes.

One is judging her law for her in the name of love. Back to the physics teacher. Suppose that what he said that evening was not "Whether to go is yours" but: "I'm saying this for your own good. That orchestra isn't right for you, so you can't go; if you still need to think about it, you're not acknowledging my love." This sentence borrows his real love to set up her accepting his judgment as the criterion of whether she acknowledges this love, and judges for her where she ought to go. Here the name of love is a reason that makes it hard for her to push back. Upstream has seen the same sentence elsewhere: "'Sooner or later you'll understand how much I love you' can also become a reason for not allowing the other to disagree".28 This sentence heads toward pouring in and occupying her own step of judging, and slides in the direction of colonization; whether it took hold is seen in how she afterward understands herself and how she acts. Saying only "I'm worried you'll regret it if you go", giving her the reason and allowing her to disagree, is not judged on these words alone to be judging on her behalf.

The other is demanding endless endurance in the name of love. The editor's "You're the best", as he said it, meant: since you love me, you should take on everything. Ray 12 saw the same shape in beauty: demanding endless endurance in the name of beauty.29 To use love as the reason for demanding that a person keep yielding up her own share is of the same type.

The counterfeit that borrows this layer's name: accommodation. The shape in which this layer is most easily counterfeited is going along with her in everything. Suppose that what the physics teacher said that evening was: "Whatever you say is fine; I'm fine either way." In his mind he wanted her to stay; so as not to make things hard for her, he said not a word of it. This looks like leaving to her, like not waiting for her, but it is not: his own share was not given. Ray 12 calls this kind accommodation: "the accommodating person has a judgment of his own, but keeps it to himself for the sake of peace".30 Here he is taken to have withdrawn his own judgment from the deliberation in order to keep the peace; not every moment of not speaking up is called accommodation: "when to speak and when not, is also each person's own judgment".31 The translator's side has it too: if, to keep the editor, she handed over her own judgment and her own time, that is her own share handed over. Accommodation is not 15DD; it is not having stood; it does not carry a direction of colonization toward her, but it is not love at this layer either.

A long absence of quarrels is not evidence of cultivation. That two people rarely argued in four years does not show that in these four years neither one's path was made hard to travel. Relation Theory says that the direction of colonization "can proceed without passing through conflict", and so "a long absence of conflict in a relation is not evidence of cultivation".32 The accommodating person is precisely the one who often does not quarrel.

What is read here is direction, not a label. It does not say what kind of man he is, nor what kind of four years these were; it reads only where that one sentence, that one demand, is headed.

§4 How Love at This Layer Ends

L41 Love at 15DD can end; where the other is truly recognized as an end, ending too is a sincere choice. She leaves, and I acknowledge her leaving; exit does not delete recognition; loving someone else after an ending is succession, not wavering; one cannot, merely because a new love has appeared, conclude backward that the old love had already ended

In the second year after she went to the other city, she wrote a long letter saying that she had fallen in love with someone there, and that she was ending this relationship. The letter was written clearly: she made her exit something he could clearly understand. How the promises and the reliance the two had over four years are to be entered, and how an exit is to be declared, Paper 4 has already read; here only this layer is read.

Love at this layer can end. Persistence is not permanence. Persistence means that in the moments in which 15DD operates, the one loved is always she; it does not say that it will not end. Where the other is truly recognized as an end, ending too is a sincere choice: her letter is her own cannot-not speaking.

She leaves, and I acknowledge her leaving. The physics teacher read the letter and wrote back a short one; he did not try to keep her, nor did he say anything like "You've lost nothing in these four years". Relation Theory says: "Exit does not delete recognition"; saying something of that kind at the time of an exit says for the other party what this exit amounts to, moves to occupy the other party's own step of understanding, and slides in the direction of colonization.33 She has gone; his share of recognizing her as her own end is still there as before.

Succession is not wavering. Some years later, he fell in love with someone else. This is succession, not wavering: after one love has ended, loving someone else. But one cannot reason the other way round: one cannot, merely because a new love has appeared, conclude backward that the old one has already ended. That way, wavering would be erased by definition: whatever alternates between two people could be described as "the earlier one has already ended". Whether the old one has ended is read from his side.

Letting go. She has clearly ended the dealings; if he still went again and again to the colleagues in her new orchestra to press them to persuade her to come back, or waited at the door of her rehearsal hall to block her from leaving, that is what is meant here by entanglement: what is directed at her path to third parties, or keeps watch on the road she means to take, making it hard to travel. Relation Theory reads this kind: what is used outward to seal the other party's channels, or to make them hard to travel, carries the direction of colonization; when one party exits, acting outward, by adding consequences, using the occasion to apply pressure or calling on others to isolate him, all carry the direction of colonization.34 Structurally sealing off her exit is, at the layer of basic rights, violation.35 What is spoken of here is structure; it does not say that one ought to let go.

Which kind of ending it is at this layer. This series divides ending into four kinds, which can fall out of step with one another: an appearance passes; my love ends; the shared dealings terminate; commitments and effects remain unsettled. What her letter ends is the shared dealings; whether his love has ended is his. The translator in unrequited love need not wait for an ending: her love never depended on his responding, nor ought it to end because he does not respond. When love at this layer has ended, the share of recognizing her as an end too is still there as before. When the one loved is gone, love does not end on that account; that is what Paper 2 has read; this series reads love from the subject's side.

§5 Interfacing with Tradition

Several have asked about this layer, one after another. As in Paper 1: for each, this paper writes faithfully what he sees and why he looks, where he presses on this paper, where they meet and where they part; other traditions appear in their own words.36

Love that can be commanded. In the First Section of the Groundwork of the Metaphysics of Morals, Kant says that the passages of Scripture commanding people to love their neighbor, even their enemy, are to be understood this way: love as an inclination cannot be commanded; but beneficence from duty, even when no inclination drives it, and even when a natural aversion that is hard to overcome resists it, is practical love, not pathological love; it lies in the will, not in a leaning of feeling, in principles of action, not in tender sympathy; and only this kind can be commanded.37 He looks in order to show that the moral worth of an action lies in its being done from duty. What he sees: the kind of love that can be required of people does not lie in feeling. He presses on this paper: this layer speaks of recognizing her as her own end; if this recognition simply is practical love, then it can be commanded, and love at this layer too becomes a duty. They meet in: the share of recognizing her as an end does not depend on the warmth of feeling; that evening the physics teacher was not happy, and that share was there all the same. They part at two points. First, love at this layer has two things in it; the cannot-not toward this one is not within what he calls practical love, nor can it be commanded. Second, this series issues no ought; of recognizing her as an end, this paper reads only how it operates in love, and does not set it up as a duty. Love and respect as duties of virtue toward others are a matter for the relational layer; Relation Theory has already interfaced with them, and this paper points back to that.38

Love and respect, toward the same value. In "Love as a Moral Emotion" (1999), Velleman reads both love and respect as responses to the dignity of a person: respect is the required minimum response, love the optional maximum response; respect arrests our self-love, while love disarms our emotional defenses and leaves us unguarded toward the other.39 He looks in order to put love back among the moral emotions in Kant's sense. What he sees: love is not recognition in general; it faces the same value as respect, yet is a different response. He presses on this paper: if love is only the maximal response to the dignity that everyone has, then "this one" is not in the value, but only in whom I happen to drop my defenses toward; does the cannot-not this layer speaks of then fall through? They meet in: love is not the same as recognition in general; L38 keeps the two apart, which runs in the same direction as his keeping love and respect apart. They part at: he speaks of love from a value that is appraised; this paper does not speak of love from the appraisal of value, and the cannot-not toward this one is not a response to a value everyone shares. Paper 4 interfaces with Frankfurt, and the question there is whether love or value comes first; the two are interfaced by question, and neither is assigned a layer.

The mutual recognition of two freedoms. Beauvoir, The Second Sex, "The Woman in Love": the woman in love sinks her own freedom in the one she loves, a total abdication; authentic love ought to be founded on the mutual recognition of two freedoms, each experiencing both itself and the other, neither giving up its transcendence, neither mutilating itself.40 For her, this handing over comes out of woman's situation. She looks in order to analyze woman's situation. What she sees: love can become the road by which one hands oneself over. They correspond at two points: the total self-abandonment she analyzes corresponds to the handing over of oneself in L40; neither giving up its transcendence corresponds to recognizing her as an end too at this layer, and to L39. The dependency that a situation produces is not all put down here to an individual's accommodation: how a social situation makes self-abandonment a road that is encouraged and passable is registered as a condition; this series is not limited by gender, reads only whether, in this one operation, one has stood, and acknowledges the institutional side without judging it. They part at two points: she asks that two freedoms recognize each other, while love in this paper holds already on my side; she says what authentic love ought to be, while this paper reads only structure. The mutual recognition of two freedoms is two-sided; this series reads it as two one-way recognitions meeting, and it falls to Paper 6. To correspond is not the same as to have the same criterion.

Something else is real. In "The Sublime and the Good" (1959), Murdoch says that love is an exceedingly difficult kind of knowledge: the realization that something other than oneself is real.41 Ray 12 has already taken up her passage on seeing the kestrel; this is another passage. She looks in order to show how a person sees morally. What she sees: the difficulty of love lies in moving one's eyes off oneself and letting something else stand there as real. They meet at two points: L35, a purpose-source is present in the one loved, and that is real, and that it cannot be read out is real too; L34, the eyes need not keep returning to oneself. They part at: what she calls "other than oneself" can be a bird or a work; this paper reads only toward this one person. She links this kind of knowledge with the good; this paper reads only structure.

Seeing him as he is. In The Art of Loving, Fromm says that respect, by the root of the word, is seeing a person as he is; the one I love, I want to grow and unfold for his own sake and in his own way, not in order to serve me.42 He looks in order to write love as an art that has to be learned and practiced; respect is one of the elements he lists. What he sees: there is one thing in love that is letting the other grow in his own way. They meet in: "Whether to go is yours"; L39, my loving her does not add a duty for her. They part at: he lists the elements of love and makes requirements, teaching people how to love; this paper makes no requirements, and reads only what love at this layer looks like.

Preferential love. In Works of Love, Kierkegaard says: "erotic love and friendship are preferential love, the passion of preferential love; Christian love is self-denial's love".43 He looks in order to show how the command "you shall love the neighbor" does its work in the world. What he sees: erotic love picks out one person, and everyone else is outside it. He presses on this paper, on L38: in loving this one, are the others shut out of recognition? They meet in: love at this layer is indeed exclusive; the cannot-not toward this one admits only this one. They part at: exclusivity belongs to love, not to recognition; the share of recognizing others as ends too does not grow smaller because one loves this one, so erotic love need not turn into preferential love. He fills the gap with a command, "you shall love"; this paper does not say ought. Ray 13 has already parted from him at the same place; that is invoked here.

Love without gradations, its practice beginning with kin. In the Mencius, "Teng Wen Gong I", the Mohist Yi Zhi says: "As I take it, love has no gradations, and its practice begins with one's kin." Mencius answers: "Moreover, when Heaven gives birth to things, it gives them a single root; Master Yi gives them two roots, and that is the reason."44 Yi Zhi speaks to show that his lavish burial of his parents does not go against the Mohist way; Mencius speaks to refute him. What Yi Zhi sees: love can be without gradations, yet in practice it always begins with those close to one. Mencius, by the difference between an elder brother's son and a neighbor's infant, and by the grief that wells up from within at the burial of one's parents, questions what root one's affection for kin has. They press on this paper, and press directly: L38 says that recognition is not exclusive and love is; is this another kind of two roots? This paper does not evade the questioning: recognition and love here each have their own source. Recognition is recognizing that others have purpose-sources of their own, and closeness and distance do not change this criterion; the irreplaceability of this one person is read on another ground, by that cannot-not of mine. Universal recognition does not by itself yield a particular love, and loving this one person does not lessen recognition of others either. This paper's answer stops at entering recognition and love separately: loving this one is not a way of practicing recognition. Whether each having its own source is enough to answer Mencius's question of the one root: that question remains. It is a real pressure on L38, and this paper does not use it to prove that love can have only so many objects. Gradation, that is, whether closeness and distance can serve as a criterion, lies at the relational layer: Relation Theory has already stated that the criterion does not change with closeness, identity or the name of the relation;45 the Mohist standard and the benefit of all under heaven, Value Theory has already interfaced with;46 these are invoked here. This paper takes up only the distinction between recognition and love.

Moistening one another with spittle is not as good as forgetting one another in the rivers and lakes. The "Great and Venerable Teacher" chapter of the Zhuangzi: "When the springs dry up, the fish are stranded together on the land; they blow damp breath on one another and moisten one another with spittle; that is not as good as forgetting one another in the rivers and lakes."47 It goes on: rather than praising Yao and condemning Jie, better to forget both and be transformed in the Way. It looks in order to show how a person is transformed in the Way. What it sees: being stranded in one place and living on each other's moisture is not as good as each being in the rivers and lakes, with no need to keep each other in mind. It presses on this paper: if this layer's not waiting for her is carried all the way through, is it then simply forgetting each other? They meet in: not waiting for her, the eyes need not keep returning to oneself, nor need they keep watching whether she gives a response. They part at: here forgetting each other is borrowed to bring to light the side that is not maintained by each other's responses; it is not taken directly as a criterion that love has already vanished; in this paper's not waiting, the share of her being irreplaceable to me and the share of my recognizing her as an end are still read separately. Nor does this paper say that there is anything wrong with the two fish moistening each other with spittle: this paper reads only structure.

Responding to things without being burdened. He Shao's "Biography of Wang Bi" records: He Yan held that the sage has no joy, anger, grief or delight; Wang Bi disagreed, holding that the sage is "the same as others in the five affections", and that "because he has the same five affections, he cannot be without grief and joy in responding to things; yet the affections of the sage respond to things without being burdened by things".48 They speak in order to argue whether the sage has affections. What Wang Bi sees: having affections and being occupied by what one is moved by are two things. He presses on this paper: does love always come with a burden? They meet in falling back: the "You can only be mine" that rose in him that night was exactly the instant of being occupied by the one he loves; that was being burdened; he saw it himself, and that moment passed. They part at: what Wang Bi speaks of is the sage's own affections, not recognizing another person as an end too; being without burden is not the same as love at this layer, and this paper borrows it only to bring falling back to light.

§6 Drawing Together and Downstream

This paper has laid down nine propositions. Taken together, they say: love at this layer has two things in it, the cannot-not toward this one, irreplaceable, and the recognition of her as her own end; both hold on my side, and one-way is enough for it to hold. Inwardly I do not wait for her nod, outwardly I do not wait for her to love me first, and the eyes are free. Her purpose-source is present; I recognize that it is there, cannot read out its content, and can only approach it. Love at this layer is persistently exclusive and does not waver, and this is this series' stance; 15DD is not easy to achieve; it is a direction, and falling back is very likely. It does not demand exclusivity of her; exclusivity belongs to love, not to recognition. My cannot-not is not her must: her not loving me does not diminish my recognizing her; my loving her does not add a duty for her; leaving to her is not submitting to whatever harm she does. The direction of colonization read from this layer is judging her law for her in the name of love, and demanding endless endurance in the name of love; accommodation borrows this layer's name, and is not having stood. Love at this layer can end; she leaves, and I acknowledge her leaving; exit does not delete recognition.

This paper does not prove that unrequited love is higher than loving each other, does not prove that love at this layer is enough without needing a response, and does not prove that one ought to let go.

The question left open. Before that long letter, there had been an evening. The two of them were walking outside her rehearsal hall; she did not ask whether he wanted her to go, and he did not say. She said: I know you want me to stay. He said: I know you have to go. They both gave a little laugh.

He recognized her as an end too, without waiting for her to recognize him first; that evening she recognized him this way too, and did not wait for him either. When both are like this, what is that? One person's side, this layer can see; what is between two people, this layer cannot contain. I love you without waiting for you to love me; if you too love me this way, what is that moment? That is Paper 6.

Downstream. The moment when two one-way loves meet, and the mutual recognition of two freedoms, are in Paper 6; for the point that mutual exclusivity does not certify that moment, Paper 6 uses this paper's L37. Persistent exclusivity, falling back and the distinction between the two things are listed together in Paper 0; the direction of colonization in the name of love is registered together with the most refined colonization read in Paper 4.

这一篇读认她也是目的的那一层：她有她自己的不得不。在 SAE 的层级里，这一层记作 15DD。这一层的爱有两样：对这一个不可替换的那一份不得不，与认她是她自己的目的；两样在我这一侧成立，单向就成立。对内，不需要她告诉我我爱得对；对外，不等她先爱我；眼睛不必时时回到自己身上。她有她自己的不得不，我只认它在，读不出它的内容；观察与交流可以逼近，读出不可以。

这一层的爱持续排他，不摇摆：这是这个系列的立场，理由在认她也是目的，她不是我得到爱情的手段；它不由这一层的一般结构推出。这一层本来就不容易做到，它是方向：在这一层运作的各个片刻里，爱的总是她；运作可能中断，回落是很可能的。这一层的爱不向她要排他，她排不排他，只能由她决定。排他排的是爱，不是认：认她也是目的，这一份不因为爱了这一个就少。

第一节 这一层的爱

L33 这一层的爱，既有对这一个不可替换的那一份不得不，也认她是她自己的目的；我这样爱她，不等她先这样爱我。单向成立，是这两样在我这一侧成立，不是单凭认她为目的就构成爱情

射线十二写的那一个认，是「他认你也是目的，不等你先这样认他」。3 这一认不只对爱的人有：老师认学生也是目的，一个人认路上的陌生人也是目的，都是这一认。所以单凭认她是目的，还不是爱情。射线十三说得清楚：认别人也是目的的那一份，不因为有了朋友就少。4

这一层的爱有两样。一样是第一篇起就有的那一份：这一个，在这一刻，不可替换，换一个同样好的人不行。一样是这一层才有的：认她是她自己的目的，她不是为了成全我这一份爱才在那里的。5 物理老师那句话里两样都有。「我想你留下，这是我的」：她对他不可替换，他换不掉她。「去不去，是你的」：她的路是她的，不是他这份爱的一部分。

两样合在一处，是这一层的爱。若那份不得不已经成为持续的方向，而这一刻还没有认她是她自己的目的，照第四篇读。缺了对这一个不可替换的那一份，则是认，不是这里的爱情。

单向就成立。 这两样都在我这一侧。她爱不爱他，不是这一层成立的条件；这个系列从主体这一侧读爱，这一点第一篇已经立下。6 关系论读双向时说，双向是两个单向碰上了，碰不上，各人那一份照旧在。7 他那一份，她若不爱他，照旧在。

这一层与第四篇怎样分。 同一句「我爱你」，可以在第四篇那一层，也可以在这一层。上游读笑时分过同一处：为你做事，是目的那一层的投注；确认你是一个独立的目的，才在这一层；哪一层，要看这一次的运作，不靠「爱」这个名字。8 物理老师为她做了许多事，替她搬过琴，替她改过简历，这些在第四篇那一层也都可以有；那一晚的「去不去，是你的」，才是这一层的那一下。哪一刻在哪一层，看那一次运作，不看用的是不是「爱」这个字。

L34 对内，不需要她告诉我我爱得对；对外，不等她先爱我。眼睛不必时时回到自己身上

射线十二把不疑分成两半：对内，不等你点头；对外，不等你先认他。9 这一篇照它读爱。

对内的一半，是他不需要她来确认他爱得对。这不是说他不会错：他那一晚冒出的「你只能是我的」，就是错，他自己看出来了。也不是说她的话对他不算数。射线十二把「确认」分成三个意思：你点了头，我才能判断；你来查我的理由；我成为一个能判断的人要靠你。它只拿掉第一个。10 这一层的爱也只拿掉第一个：她的回应进入他的发生条件，是材料，不是结算。11 他爱她，不以她先说「你爱得对」为前提。

对外的一半，是不等她先爱他。他说「我想你留下」，不是一笔交换的开价：你若也这样爱我，我才这样爱你。她去了，他那一份照样在。

两半合起来，是不等她。《SAE 第一批判》在这一站做事的一侧，记下的名字是忘我。12 射线十二写这一处：不等你点头，也不等你先认他，「他就用不着回头看自己」。13 爱也是这样。射线十二说：「时时要人点头、要人先认他的人，眼睛总落回自己身上」。14 在爱里，时时要她点头、要她先爱他的人，也是这样：我爱得够不够，她值不值，她是不是也一样。不等她，眼睛才空得出来，落在她那一个星期怎样想、她要什么上。

这里也要分清一件事。不等她，不是不想她爱他。他当然想；想是他的，是第四篇那份持续的不得不里的东西。这一层说的是：他爱她，不拿她的爱作前提。

L35 她有她自己的不得不，我只认它在，读不出它的内容；爱她不是读懂她，观察、交流可以逼近，读出不可以

她为什么要去那个乐团，他说不全。她说过那里的首席，说过那里的曲目，说过她想在三十五岁以前试一次；这些他都听了，可那一个让她非去不可的东西是什么，他读不出来。

上游把这一层的信息说得很准：别人身上可以读出的只有一件：他是目的，他那里有一个目的源在；他自己的不得不里装着什么，读不出来。读不出的那一部分不可穷尽，却可以经观察他的行为，甚至经交流，被逼近。15

所以，爱她不是读懂她。他认的是：她那里有一个目的源在，那是她的；那里面是什么，他可以看她练琴，听她说，问她，一点一点逼近，读不出来。说「我比你自己更懂你要什么」，是把逼近说成了读出；话一出口，就是替她定她要什么，那一处朝殖民的方向去（L40）。16

第二节 持续排他

L36 15DD 的爱持续排他，不摇摆，因为认她也是目的，她不是我得到爱情的手段；这一「因为」不读作由「不当手段」推出「不摇摆」：15DD 本来就不容易做到，是方向。持续说的是：在 15DD 运作的各个片刻里，爱的总是她，不在两个人之间轮流；运作可能中断，中断时按当次运作另记，不因此就叫摇摆，爱的片刻若在两个人之间轮流，仍记为摇摆；回落是很可能的

持续排他是这个系列的立场，不是推论。命题里的「因为」，交代的是这一立场站在哪里：认她也是目的，她不是我得到爱情的手段。

这一句的「因为」要读准。第四篇读摇摆：两个方向并存，爱的片刻在两个人之间轮流，还不确定。摇摆的人并不就把人当手段，把人当手段的是「谁都行」，那一种在第三篇。所以这里不是由「不当手段」推出「不摇摆」：摇摆的人也不当手段。

这一句的另一半是：15DD 本来就不容易做到，它是方向。这个「方向」，说的是朝着去、还没到的那一处，不是每一刻都在。射线十三在一处用的是同一个意思：在许多人那里，它还是方向。17 它与第四篇的方向不同：第四篇的方向是来了、不得不跟随的那一个，是目的那一层的；它也与关系论说的涵育、殖民两个方向不同，那两个在一次作用上读。

照此写，这一层的不轮流是这样的。摇摆是还在选哪一个，仍在目的那一层的框里：两个人在他那里都换不掉，他还没有定往哪一个那里去，第四篇读过。18 在 15DD 运作的那几刻，他不是还在两个人之间选；这不否认这份爱有过选择的来历。排开别人的，是这一个、不可替换的那一份不得不，认她是她自己的目的那一份并不排他（L38）。所以「不轮流」说的是那几刻的样子，不由认推出。19

持续排他也不由 15DD 的一般结构推出。单向的认不排他（L38），推不出持续排他；它是立场，不是推论。

持续是什么。 持续说的是：在 15DD 运作的各个片刻里，爱的总是她，不在两个人之间轮流。它不说每一刻都在 15DD 运作。运作可能中断：他在课堂上讲题的时候，在批改作业的时候，那一刻，爱在哪一层都没有在运作。中断时，按当次的运作另记，不因此就叫摇摆。若那几刻里，爱的片刻真在两个人之间轮流，那仍是摇摆，照第四篇读。持续排他不排除回落期间发生别的运作。

回落是很可能的。 他那一晚冒出的「你只能是我的」，是落回目的那一层的一刻：我的方向，成了她该往哪里去的尺子。一阵嫉妒，一句想说没说的话，都可能是这样的片刻。15DD 不容易做到，回落很可能，两样相合。这些片刻不推翻那份爱，只说那几刻在别的层上运作。从外面看，都只是线索；他心里那一句没有说出口，也就没有进入她的发生条件。

L37 15DD 的爱不向她要排他；她是不是排他，只能由她决定。两边各自以 15DD 运作、又各自排他时，是两个单向的排他碰上了，与关系论说的双向同形，不是交换来的；彼此排他本身不认证 16DD。各自许下或一起说定排他，是承诺；拿自己的爱替她立一份排他的义务，退回第四篇

他只爱她，这是他的。她是不是也只爱他，是她的，只能由她决定。他不拿自己的排他去换她的排他。

两个人都这样时，两个人都只爱对方，是两个单向的排他碰上了。这与关系论说的双向同形：双向是两个单向碰上了，碰不上，各人那一份照旧在；双向不是交换。20 不是交换来的：不是你只爱我，我才只爱你。

彼此排他本身，不认证两个人都在 15DD，更不认证 16DD 的那一刻。彼此排他也可以出于交换，出于怕失去，出于两个人与别人隔绝。从外面看，样子一样。

两个人各自许下排他，或一起说定排他，是承诺，不一律带殖民方向；承诺怎样约束人，又怎样不锁住明天，照关系论读承诺的那一篇。21 说定的排他再有制度保护，就出了这个系列的射程，归婚姻论。

拿自己的爱替她立一份排他的义务，「我只爱你，所以你也得只爱我」，就退回了第四篇：我的方向，成了她的尺子。

L38 15DD 的爱有两样：认她也是目的，这一认不排他，不因为爱了这一个就少；这一个、不可替换的那一份不得不，这一份排他

排他排的是爱，不是认。

物理老师认她也是目的；他也认他的学生、他的同事、街上一个陌生人也是目的。这一认，不因为他爱了她就少了一点。射线十三说的是朋友：认别人也是目的的那一份，不因为有了朋友就少；这里推到爱了这一个。22

排他的，是那一份对这一个的不得不：在那一刻，换一个同样好的人不行，那一刻里没有别人的位置，第一篇说过。23

这一分若不写明，爱就落进射线十三说的偏爱：两个人互相认，对别的人一概不认。24 两个人只爱对方，没有落进偏爱；两个人只认对方，落进了。克尔凯郭尔在这里有一份压力，第五节接。

第三节 不等你，也不任你

L39 我的不得不不等于她必须：她不爱我，不减我认她也是目的的那一份；我爱她，也不给她添一份义务。留给她，不等于任她伤害

另设一个例子。一位做翻译的女子，爱着常有来往的一位编辑。他不爱她，说得很清楚。

射线十一在目的那一层看见一句：我的不得不，不等于你必须；它在那里停下，把别人是不是也有他的不得不，留给了不疑之美。25 这一句原本就交到了这一层。

他不爱她，不减她认他也是目的的那一份。她爱他，是她的；他不爱她，是他的。她不拿他不爱她，去改认他是什么人。

她爱他，也不给他添一份义务。关系论说，期待不落成义务，承诺也不落成对对方的义务。26 爱比这两样更早：她爱他，他不因此欠她什么，不欠一句回应，也不欠一份相同的爱。

留给他，不等于任他伤害。 有一阵，那位编辑知道她的心意，便常在深夜把急稿发给她，一句「你最好了」，第二天一早就要。她认他也是目的，可这一认不要她什么都接。射线十二说：「留给你，不等于任你伤害。拒绝，离开，保护自己，都可以和认你也是目的同在」。27 她可以不接那一份稿，可以不再回那一个时间的消息，可以走开。拒绝与离开，都不收回她认他的那一份。

L40 从这一层读到的殖民方向，是以爱之名替她判她的法，以爱的名义要人无限承受。借这一层之名的冒充是迁就：自己那一份交了出去，不是 15DD，是没有站住。长期不吵架，不作涵育的证据

这一层读到的殖民方向有两样。

一样是以爱之名替她判她的法。回到那位物理老师。设想那一晚他说的不是「去不去，是你的」，而是：「我是为你好。那个乐团不适合你，所以你不能去；你若还要考虑，就是不认我的爱。」这一句借着他真的爱，把接受他的判断立为她认不认这份爱的判准，替她判了她该往哪里去。爱这个名字在这里，是让她不好反驳的理由。上游在另一处看见过同样的句子：「“你迟早会明白我有多爱你”，也可以成为不许对方异议的理由」。28 这一句朝灌入并占住她自己去判的那一步去，滑向殖民的方向；成没成，看她此后怎样理解自己，怎样做。单说「我担心你去了会后悔」，把理由给她、容许她不同意，不由这几个字就判为代判。

一样是以爱的名义要人无限承受。那位编辑的「你最好了」，说出来的意思是：你既然爱我，就该什么都接。射线十二在美那里看见同一个形状：以美的名义要人无限承受。29 拿爱作理由，要一个人把自己那一份一直让出去，是同型的。

借这一层之名的冒充：迁就。 这一层最容易被冒充成的样子，是什么都依她。设想物理老师那一晚说的是：「你说什么都好，我都行。」他心里想她留下，为了不让她为难，一句也没说。这看上去像留给，像不等她，其实不是：他自己那一份没有给出来。射线十二把这一种叫迁就：「迁就的人有自己的判断，只是为了和气没有说」。30 这里设定他为保住和气，把自己的判断撤出了商量；不是一时没有开口，就都叫迁就：「什么时候开口，什么时候不开口，也是各人自己的判断」。31 翻译那一面也有：她若为了留住那位编辑，把自己的判断、自己的时间都交了出去，那是自己那一份交了出去。迁就不是 15DD，是没有站住；它不带向她去的殖民方向，可它也不是这一层的爱。

长期不吵架，不作涵育的证据。 两个人四年里很少争执，不说明这四年里没有一方的路被弄得难走。关系论说：殖民这个方向「可以不经过冲突」，所以「一段关系长期无冲突，不作涵育的证据」。32 迁就的人，正是常常不吵的人。

这里读的是方向，不是标签。不说他是什么样的人，不说这四年是什么样的四年；只读那一句、那一个要求朝哪里去。

第四节 这一层的爱怎样结束

L41 15DD 的爱可以结束；真认对方是目的，结束也是一种真诚的选择。她走，我认她走，退出不删除承认；结束以后再爱别人，是先后，不是摇摆；不能仅因出现新爱，就倒认旧爱已经结束

她去了另一座城市以后的第二年，写来一封长信，说她在那里爱上了一个人，这段关系她要结束了。信写得很清楚，她把自己要退出这件事，让他能清楚地理解。四年里两个人有过的答应与依靠怎样记，退出要怎样表明，第四篇已经读过；这里只读这一层。

这一层的爱可以结束。 持续不是永久。持续说的是在 15DD 运作的那些片刻里爱的总是她，不说它不会结束。真认对方是目的，结束也是一种真诚的选择：她那封信，就是她自己的不得不在说话。

她走，我认她走。 物理老师读完信，回了一封短信，没有挽留，也没有说「这四年你什么也没损失」一类的话。关系论说：「退出不删除承认」；退出时说出那一类话，是替对方说了这次退出算什么，去占对方自己去理解的那一步，滑向殖民的方向。33 她走了，他认她是她自己的目的，这一份照旧在。

先后不是摇摆。 又过了几年，他爱上了另一个人。这是先后，不是摇摆：一份爱结束以后，再爱别人。可反过来不能这样推：不能仅因为出现了一份新的爱，就倒过来认定旧的那一份已经结束。那样，摇摆就被定义抹掉了：凡在两个人之间轮流的，都可以说成「前一份已经结束」。旧的那一份结没结束，从他这一侧读。

放手。 她已经明确结束来往，他若仍反复找到她新乐团的同事施压劝返，或者守在她的排练厅门口阻住她离开，这是这里所说的纠缠：冲着她向第三方的路去，或守在她要走的路上，把它弄得难走。关系论读这一类：被用来向外封住对方通道或把它弄得难走的，带殖民这个方向；一方退出的时候，向外做事，追加后果、借机施压、号召孤立，都带殖民这个方向。34 在结构上封死她的退出，在基础权利一层是侵。35 这里说的是结构，不说应当放手。

这一层结束的是哪一样。 这个系列把结束分成四样，它们可以不同步：一次显现过去；我的爱结束；共同的来往终止；承诺与作用仍未结。她那封信，结束的是共同的来往；他的爱结没结束，是他的。单恋的那位翻译，不必等一个结束：她的爱从来不靠他回应才成立，也不因为他不回应就该结束。这一层的爱结束了，认她也是目的的那一份照旧在。所爱的人不在了，爱不因此结束，那是第二篇读过的；这个系列从主体这一侧读爱。

第五节 与传统对接

这一层先后有几家问过。照第一篇的写法：每一家照实写他认出了什么、为了什么看，在哪里向这一篇施压，在哪里相遇，在哪里分开；别的传统用它自己的词出场。36

能被命令的爱。 康德在《道德形而上学奠基》第一章说，圣经里命令人爱邻人、甚至爱仇敌的那些段落，要这样理解：作为倾向的爱不能被命令；出于义务的行善，即使没有倾向推动，甚至有天然而难以克服的反感抵着，却是实践的爱，不是病理的爱，它在意志里，不在感觉的偏向里，在行动的原则里，不在温柔的同情里；只有这一种能被命令。37 他为说明行动的道德价值在出于义务而看。他认出：可以要求于人的那一种爱，不在感觉里。他向这一篇施压：这一层说认她是她自己的目的，这一认若就是实践的爱，它就是可以命令的，这一层的爱也就成了一项义务。相遇在：认她是目的的那一份，不靠感觉的温度；物理老师那一晚并不愉快，那一份照样在。分开在两处。一，这一层的爱有两样，那一份对这一个的不得不，不在他说的实践的爱里，也命令不来。二，这个系列不发应当；认她是目的，这一篇只读它在爱里怎样运作，不立为义务。爱与敬作为对他人的德性义务，是关系一层的事，关系论已对过，这里回指。38

爱与敬，对同一份价值。 威尔曼在〈爱作为一种道德情感〉（1999）里，把爱与敬都读成对人的尊严的回应：敬是所要求的起码的回应，爱是可选的、尽处的回应；敬挡住我们的自爱，爱卸下我们情感上的防备，使我们对对方没有遮拦。39 他为把爱放回康德式的道德情感里而看。他认出：爱不是一般的认，它与敬对着同一份价值，却是另一种回应。他向这一篇施压：若爱只是对人人都有的那份尊严的尽处回应，「这一个」就不在价值里，只在我碰巧对谁卸下了防备；这一层说的那一份不得不，是不是就落空了？相遇在：爱不等于一般的认，L38 把两样分开，与他把爱与敬分开同向。分开在：他从一份被评估的价值说爱；这一篇不从价值的评估说爱，那一份对这一个的不得不，不是对人人共有的价值的回应。第四篇接法兰克福，那里问的是爱与价值谁在先；两家按问题分接，不给谁判层。

两个自由的相互承认。 波伏瓦《第二性》〈恋爱中的女人〉：恋爱中的女人把自己的自由沉没在所爱的人身上，是一种全盘的让渡；本真的爱应当建立在两个自由的相互承认上，各自既经验到自己也经验到对方，谁也不放弃自己的超越，谁也不残损自己。40 在她那里，这份交出出自女人的处境。她为分析女人的处境而看。她认出：恋爱可以成为一个人交出自己的那条路。相照在两处：她所分析的全盘的自我放弃，与 L40 交出自己的那一份相照；谁也不放弃自己的超越，与这一层认她也是目的、与 L39 相照。这里不把处境造成的依赖全归作个人的迁就：社会处境怎样使自我放弃成为被鼓励、走得通的路，作为条件登记；这个系列性别不限，只读这一次运作有没有站住，制度那一面认而不判。分开在两处：她要两个自由互认，这一篇的爱在我这一侧就成立；她说本真的爱应当怎样，这一篇只读结构。两个自由的相互承认是两边的，这个系列读作两个单向碰上了，落第六篇。相照不等于判准相同。

别的东西是真的。 默多克在〈崇高与善〉（1959）里说，爱是一种极难的认识：认出自己以外的某样东西是真的。41 射线十二已经接过她看红隼的那一处，这里是另一处。她为说明人怎样在道德上看见而看。她认出：爱的难处在于把眼睛从自己身上移开，让另一个东西作为真的立在那里。相遇在两处：L35，她那里有一个目的源在，那是真的，读不出也是真的；L34，眼睛不必时时回到自己身上。分开在：她说的「自己以外」，可以是一只鸟、一件作品；这一篇只读对这一个人。她把这一种认识与善连在一起，这一篇只读结构。

看见他本来的样子。 弗洛姆在《爱的艺术》里说，尊重，照这个词的词根，是看见一个人本来的样子；所爱的人，我要他为他自己、照他自己的方式生长展开，不是为了服侍我。42 他为把爱写成一门要学、要练的艺术而看，尊重是他列的几样要素之一。他认出：爱里有一样，是让对方照他自己的样子长。相遇在：「去不去，是你的」；L39，我爱她，不给她添一份义务。分开在：他给爱列要素、提要求，教人怎样去爱；这一篇不给要求，只读这一层的爱是什么样子。

偏爱。 克尔凯郭尔在《爱的作为》里说：「情爱和友谊都是偏爱，是偏爱的激情；基督教的爱，是舍己的爱」。43 他为说明「你要爱邻人」这一命令怎样在世上做工而看。他认出：情爱挑出一个人，其余的人就在它之外。他向这一篇施压，压在 L38 上：爱了这一个，是不是就把别人排出了认？相遇在：这一层的爱确实排他，那一份对这一个的不得不，只认这一个。分开在：排他排的是爱，不是认；认别人也是目的的那一份，不因为爱了这一个就少，所以情爱不必落成偏爱。他用一项命令去补，「你要爱」；这一篇不说应当。射线十三已在同一处与他分开，这里调用。

爱无差等，施由亲始。 《孟子·滕文公上》里，墨者夷之说：「之则以为爱无差等，施由亲始。」孟子答：「且天之生物也，使之一本，而夷子二本故也。」44 夷之为说明自己厚葬其亲并不违墨家之道而说；孟子为驳他而说。夷之认出：爱可以不分等差，做起来却总从亲近的人开始。孟子以亲兄之子与邻之赤子之别、葬亲时内发的哀痛，追问所亲之本。他们向这一篇施压，压得很直接：L38 说认不排他、爱排他，这是不是另一种二本？这一篇不回避这个追问：认与爱在这里各有来处。认是承认他人有自己的目的源，亲疏不改这一判据；对这一个人的不可替换，另据我的那份不得不读。普遍的认不独自推出特定的爱，爱这一个人也不减少对别人的认。这一篇的回答止于认与爱的两样分记：爱这一个，不是认的一种施行。两样各有来处是否足以回应孟子的一本之问，这一问仍在。这一问是对 L38 的真压力，这一篇不借它证明爱的对象只能有几个。差等，即亲疏能不能作判据，在关系一层：关系论已写明，判据不因亲疏、身份或关系的名称而换；45 墨家的法与天下之利，价值论已对过，46 这里调用。这一篇只接认与爱的分别。

相濡以沫，不如相忘于江湖。 《庄子·大宗师》：「泉涸，鱼相与处于陆，相呴以湿，相濡以沫，不如相忘于江湖。」47 它接着说，与其称誉尧而非议桀，不如两忘而化于道。它为说明人怎样化于道而看。它认出：困在一处、靠彼此的湿气活着，比不上各在江湖里，用不着彼此记挂。它向这一篇施压：这一层的不等她，若走到头，是不是就是相忘？相遇在：不等她，眼睛不必时时回到自己身上，也不必时时盯着她给不给回应。分开在：这里借相忘照见不靠彼此回应维持的那一面，不把它直接当作爱已经消失的判准；这一篇的不等，她对我不可替换的那一份与我认她是目的的那一份，仍分别读。相濡以沫的那两条鱼，这一篇也不说它们不好：这一篇只读结构。

应物而无累。 何劭《王弼传》记：何晏以为圣人无喜怒哀乐；王弼不同意，以为圣人「同于人者五情也」，「五情同故不能无哀乐以应物，然则圣人之情，应物而无累于物者也」。48 他们为辩圣人有没有情而说。王弼认出：有情，与被所感的东西占住，是两件事。他向这一篇施压：爱是不是总带着累？相遇在回落：他那一晚冒出的「你只能是我的」，正是被所爱的人占住的那一下，是累；他自己看出来，那一刻就过去了。分开在：王弼说的是圣人自己的情，不是认另一个人也是目的；无累不等于这一层的爱，这一篇只借它照见回落。

第六节 收束与下游

这一篇立了九条。合起来说的是：这一层的爱有两样，对这一个不可替换的那一份不得不，与认她是她自己的目的；两样在我这一侧成立，单向就成立。对内不等她点头，对外不等她先爱我，眼睛空得出来。她的目的源在，我认它在，读不出它的内容，只能逼近。这一层的爱持续排他，不摇摆，这是这个系列的立场；15DD 不容易做到，是方向，回落是很可能的。它不向她要排他；排他排的是爱，不是认。我的不得不，不等于她必须：她不爱我，不减我认她；我爱她，不给她添义务；留给她，不等于任她伤害。从这一层读到的殖民方向，是以爱之名替她判她的法，以爱的名义要人无限承受；迁就借这一层的名，是没有站住。这一层的爱可以结束，她走，我认她走，退出不删除承认。

这一篇不证明单恋高于相爱，不证明这一层的爱不需要回应就够了，也不证明应当放手。

留下的问题。 那封长信以前，有过一个晚上。两个人在她的排练厅外面散步，她没有问他想不想她去，他也没有说。她说：我知道你想我留下。他说：我知道你得去。两个人都笑了一下。

他认她也是目的，不等她先认他；那一晚，她也这样认他，也不等他。两个人都这样了，那是什么？一个人这一边，这一层看得见；两个人之间，这一层收不住。我爱你，不等你爱我；你若也这样爱我，那一刻是什么，那是第六篇。

下游。 两个单向的爱碰上了的那一刻，两个自由的相互承认，在第六篇；彼此排他不认证那一刻，第六篇照这一篇的 L37 用。持续排他、回落、两样的分别，Paper 0 合列；以爱之名的殖民方向，与第四篇读到的最精致的殖民，一并登记。
