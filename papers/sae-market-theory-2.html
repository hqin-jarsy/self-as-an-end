The Market: Space
市场的空间

Han Qin (秦汉) · Independent Researcher · 2026
DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.24183370 · Full PDF on Zenodo · CC BY 4.0
Abstract

Paper 1 wrote the side of the Base Layer; this paper writes the side of the Emergence Layer, and how the two are examined when they collide. Above the line, exchange in the market is left open as usual: fame bringing opportunities and capital bringing more capital are not, in themselves, the problem, and are not sealed off on the ground that they "may be converted"; leaving open is not leaving unquestioned, and not running into the two gates does not certify a transaction as warranted in every respect. Beyond space there are conditions: institutions give general conditions and do not manufacture emergence; conditions out of reach do not count as already given. Whether the conditions in a place are within reach must be read together with the thresholds and costs of that same place: the state guarantees half of a small start-up loan for those laid off, and the approved temperature-control system later required in Bluekiln costs about a year of a small kiln workshop's revenue (SC4). Securing and space can conflict. In Year 28 the kiln of a small kiln workshop in Bluekiln burst at night, and the worker keeping the night watch was burned; the State Labor Agency then required all kilns to install approved temperature-control and alarm systems. A year later, four of the eleven small kiln workshops in town had stopped firing, two young kiln workers' applications to open kilns had gone no further, and kiln accidents across the state had fallen by about sixty percent. The two kinds of "excess" are not symmetrical, and the asymmetry lies in the way of examining and the order. The Base Layer side is examined by the line, with no margin; the safety of those who tend the fire does not wait until small kiln workshops can afford it; the line comes first. A securing measure that restricts others' Emergence Layer is examined by its effect: what effect it addresses, what it costs the capacity to produce, to serve and to give conditions, whether there are other means, and why this one was chosen; the securing measure itself also guards the line. Where both fall on one and the same person, neither direction is permitted: his Emergence Layer is not traded for his securing, nor is the absence of securing in his Base Layer traded for more choice for him. So tending the fire with a hired worker and tending it oneself are examined separately. "Priority" is the order in a conflict, not a matter of weight; the two layers matter equally. The two layers holding each other up is a direction, not a guarantee: a workshop installed a system, and the apprentices who had kept the night watch moved to trying new glazes, which is one shape it can take; the jobs at the Ridgefoot warehouse more than doubled, yet institutional securing for housing has still not been embedded: growth does not complete securing on the institution's behalf, and whether anyone is actually held up and whether the institution's securing has been carried out are judged separately (SC5).

Introduction

In the spring of Year 28, the kiln of a small kiln workshop in Bluekiln burst at night. A worker keeping the night watch was burned and taken to the hospital. This kiln had no temperature-control system; it relied on people to tend the fire.

In the autumn, the State Labor Agency issued the Kiln Safety Rules: from Year 29, all kilns in the state must install approved temperature-control and alarm systems. Approval rests on a technical review by the Kingdom State Ceramics Association, and the State Labor Agency places systems on a list on the basis of the review's opinion. The list has two suppliers, one of which is the equipment subsidiary of Bluekiln Porcelain Works; the head of the Association's technical review panel is a deputy general manager of Bluekiln Porcelain Works. The price of one approved system is about a year of a small kiln workshop's revenue in the town.

In Year 29, four of the eleven small kiln workshops the town had had stopped firing; two young kiln workers who had completed their apprenticeships had planned to open kilns, and their applications went no further. The same year, the State Labor Agency's statistics said that kiln accidents across the state were about sixty percent fewer than the year before. At a hearing of the State Legislature, the owner of a workshop that had stopped firing said: "Nobody wants another burn. But we can't afford this system, and there are only those two on the list." The Ceramics Association answered: "The review follows technical standards; anyone may apply for approval." That year another supplier applied for approval, and its review had not yet concluded.

Bluekiln, the Kingdom State, and the workshops, the association and the terms here are all constructed examples of this series.

The body of the one who tends the fire is in the Base Layer; whether a kiln can be opened, and whether a young kiln worker can set up a kiln of his own, are in the Emergence Layer. In this matter the two layers have collided. The Prequel said that the two layers matter equally, and that what is asymmetric is the way of examining and the order. This paper unfolds that sentence: how the space above the line is kept (§1); how conditions are given (§2); how the two are examined when they conflict (§3); how the Kiln Safety Rules read under this way of examining (§4); why the two layers holding each other up is only a direction (§5).

§1 Above the Line, Left Open as Usual

Paper 1 wrote of the line. What is above the line?

Political Economy has laid down: outside the gates, exchange in the Emergence Layer is left open as usual. "Fame bringing opportunities, capital bringing more capital, qualifications bringing posts, donations within the limits and lawful employment are not, in themselves, the problem"; "exchange in the Emergence Layer is not sealed off on the ground that it 'may be converted'" (Political Economy Paper 4, E24). That paper read this sentence through a porcelain exhibition in Apple City: the tickets carried the name of a person with nine hundred thousand followers, and attendance in the first week was four times that of a similar exhibition the year before; a veteran kiln worker who had fired kilns in Bluekiln for thirty-one years had two pieces in the second hall (Political Economy Paper 4, §4). This paper does not redo it.

In the market, this sentence has two faces.

One face is not sealing off. The constructs that make and enforce rules do not seal off exchange in the Emergence Layer on the ground that it "may be converted," nor do they seal off one inch more in the name of guarding the line: guarding the line sets limits on the use that crosses the line, not on everything above the line. Whatever additional restriction a securing measure places on the Emergence Layer must also state its relation to guarding the line; the part that goes beyond what is needed and cannot state its grounds cannot pass on "for the sake of securing" alone (own undertaking). §3 writes how this is examined.

The other face is being open. The space of a market is not only that existing exchanges are not sealed off, but also that new people can come in: new stalls, new kilns, new delivery services. The Prequel said that Crossways started from four stalls in the East Market; in a market, today's large player can be yesterday's newcomer. That the space is open means that this road in, above the line, is kept open by the rule-making constructs, and is not closed by those already inside against those who come later (own undertaking). Others closing this road is examined in Paper 4; the rule-making construct's own rules narrowing it is examined in this paper.

Leaving open is not leaving unquestioned. Political Economy states explicitly in the same place that the two gates speak only of the restrictions on conversion that paper handles: "not running into the two gates does not certify a transaction as warranted in every respect, nor does it cancel this series' requirements elsewhere of guarding the line, being open to questioning, securing, and giving conditions" (Political Economy Paper 4, E24). Exchange above the line is left open as usual, and can be asked about as usual: whether a transaction crosses the line or seals off a road elsewhere; when a position grows large, its power-face grows, and checks are strengthened accordingly (Political Economy Paper 3, E14). Leaving open means not sealing off; it does not mean not asking; asking means that one can ask, not that one first seals off.

Judgment layer: whether an exchange is above the line or on it, and whether a restriction is guarding the line or sealing off space, is explained by the construct that makes the rule; those it restricts can question it.

§2 Conditions Must Be within Reach

Beyond space there are conditions.

Political Economy has laid down: "Institutions give general conditions that do not designate content"; lists of this kind, such as time, learning, mobility, access to credit and basic livelihood, are examples at the rule layer; and "conditions out of reach do not count as already given" (Political Economy Paper 6, E34). That paper read this sentence through Bluekiln Porcelain Works' production cut of Year 25. To the nine hundred people laid off, the State Labor Agency gave twelve months of unemployment benefit, a tuition subsidy for any one vocational training course of their choice, a relocation subsidy, and a small start-up loan, half guaranteed by the state; applying did not ask where one was going. But the state's training courses were mostly held in Apple City, three hours each way, and fewer than fifteen percent of those laid off in town signed up for training. That paper writes that this fifteen percent does not in itself judge the state's success or failure; outcomes "can serve as clues for checking the conditions, not as direct verdicts of success or failure"; what it asks is whom this share of conditions reaches and whom it does not (Political Economy Paper 6, §3).

This paper asks about one more place: how the conditions of a place are to be read when they meet the thresholds of that same place.

The small start-up loan the state gives is a condition that lets people give something a try: a person who could not otherwise borrow can borrow a sum and start a livelihood. In Bluekiln, starting a livelihood can be precisely setting up a kiln. Four years later the Kiln Safety Rules took effect, and an approved system cost about a year of a small kiln workshop's revenue; in Year 29, two young kiln workers' applications to open kilns went no further. The example does not record these two things falling on the same person: what became of those who took that loan, and whether the two young kiln workers took it, are not recorded, and this paper does not link them. They merely fall on the same kind of road: setting up a kiln in Bluekiln.

So reading whether the conditions in a place are within reach cannot look only at what was given; they must be read together with the thresholds and costs of that place. Giving a loan is one position; setting an equipment requirement is another; looked at separately, each can be justified. But people walk one and the same road: whether the loan obtained is enough to clear that threshold. In one and the same institution, conditions given in one place can be eaten up by a threshold set in another; whether conditions are within reach must be read on the road, not on the books of the party that gives them (extension: E34 originally says that conditions out of reach do not count as already given; here it is extended to reading the conditions of a place together with the thresholds of that same place; own undertaking).

This is not to say that the threshold should not be set: the safety of those who tend the fire is read in §§3 and 4, and the line comes first. It is to say that the position that sets the threshold and the position that gives conditions must know about each other; if both are in the state, the state must be able to answer: between these two, can a person who wants to set up a kiln still get through?

To whom conditions are given, and how much, depends on what relation holds between the giving party and the person, and how wide a range has been undertaken. Institution Theory says that an institution provides conditions for those in its interior according to relational thickness, "concerned with conditions, not with outcomes" (Institution Theory Z23). In the market, those who give general conditions are, first of all, the constructs that make and enforce rules, and what they give is the share that everyone in a market can reach; what a company gives its employees falls under the Theory of the Company; what funding and subsidizing bodies give those they fund, Paper 5 writes as the condition of funding. What specifically is given, and how strongly, lies at the rule layer; whether it is within reach lies at the judgment layer.

A theoretical passage. The Prequel cited the three things Economics P3 lists that institutions can do at the group level: help those who have already grown find each other; protect those who have already grown so they can sustain themselves; punish 14DD behavior that harms others' interests (Economics P3 §5.2). On the Prequel's reading, the first two fall in this paper: letting people be seen and be found, and letting people leave (a reading; own undertaking). Read by these two, conditions in the market include, besides the list above, two things to be raised on their own. One is being seen: whether others can find what a person makes; the veteran kiln worker's glazes are steady, yet few outside the town know of him; at a porcelain exhibition in Apple City, two of his pieces were in the second hall. The other is being able to leave: when one can no longer continue with one company, whether the cost of moving to another company, another trade or another place pins a person in place. These two can be written in many ways at the rule layer, for instance badges that state where they come from, records that can be taken along, and switching suppliers without needless obstruction; these are only examples, not ranked. The language of dimensions is borrowed only in this passage, and the body of the text does not classify people by dimension.

Judgment layer: whether the conditions in a place are within reach is answered jointly by the position that gives conditions and the position that sets thresholds; those who cannot reach them can ask; outcomes serve as clues, not as verdicts.

SC4 Above the line, things are left open as usual; leaving open is not leaving unquestioned; conditions must be within reach. Outside the gates, exchange in the Emergence Layer is left open as usual, and is not sealed off on the ground that it "may be converted"; the two gates do not exhaust the requirements of guarding the line, securing and giving conditions, and exchange left open can still be questioned (per Political Economy E24). Institutions give general conditions; they do not manufacture emergence; conditions out of reach do not count as already given (per E34).

§3 Two Kinds of "Excess"

Securing and space can conflict. The conflict can be described from both ends: securing excessively restricts the Emergence Layer; the Emergence Layer excessively erodes the Base Layer. The two kinds of "excess" sound symmetrical, like the two ends of a balance beam. This paper's answer is: they are not symmetrical. What is asymmetric is not weight, but the way of examining and the order.

The Base Layer side is examined by the line, with no margin. Where it falls on a person, crossing the line is crossing the line; there is no room for "crossing it only a little." An institution's acts cross no line, and this "does not vary with relational thickness and is not discounted under any condition" (Institution Theory Z9); an economic position does not convert accumulation into domination over people's Base Layer (Political Economy Paper 4, E21). So the "excess" in "the Emergence Layer excessively erodes the Base Layer" is only a manner of speaking: erosion that falls on a person is already crossing the line, and no "moderate" erosion can be left standing.

A securing measure that restricts others' Emergence Layer is examined by its effect. A securing measure protects some people's Base Layer, and may restrict other people's Emergence Layer. What is to be examined here is not the line but the measure's effect. It must state four things: what effect it addresses; what it costs the capacity to produce, to serve and to give conditions; whether there are alternative means; why this one was chosen. The securing measure itself also guards the line: it cannot, in order to protect some people, press on the Base Layer of others.

This way of examining has the same form as Political Economy's checks on economic positions. That proposition says that checks fall on how something is used, not aimed at lowering capacity; where one place is at once production and choking, a check must state which effect it addresses and how it preserves as far as possible the capacity to produce, to serve and to give conditions; if changing the configuration also affects these capacities, it "states as it stands the cost, the alternatives available and the reasons for its choice" (Political Economy Paper 3, E17). The original speaks of checks on economic positions; here it is extended to the form of securing rules (own undertaking).

Where both fall on one and the same person, neither direction is permitted. A conflict can fall between different people, and can also fall on one and the same person. Political Economy has written: "the two layers matter equally, and neither is traded for the other; neither direction is permitted" (Political Economy Paper 6, E35). Falling on one person, this is two sentences: his Emergence Layer is not traded for his securing; nor is the absence of securing in his Base Layer traded for more choice for him. That paper also says: "if the Base Layer is held up thickly at the cost of restricting the Emergence Layer, that thickness is a boundary-crossing disguised as cultivation" (Political Economy Paper 6, §4). Protecting a person is not done at the cost of closing his own road for him; giving a person more choice is not done at the cost of removing his floor.

"Priority" is order, not weight. The "priority" here is what the main-line sentence calls "the line comes first." When the two conflict and fall between different people, the line comes first: protecting some people's Base Layer does not wait until other people's Emergence Layer is provided for. Political Economy says: "present harm cannot wait" (Political Economy Paper 6, E33). But "first" speaks of order; it does not say that the Base Layer is more important: the two layers matter equally, and that sentence does not move. Order decides whether to protect, without waiting; the examination of effect decides how to protect, and how far. That the line comes first does not give a securing measure a blank permission; that the securing measure is examined by its effect does not make the line wait either.

Paper 1 left one matter that can be tried by this way of examining. In Year 27 the Kingdom State lowered the threshold of the Public Utilities Act from five thousand households to five hundred. For Springhollow, this means that the Commission's standing review came to cover Springhollow Water Company; for the other small water suppliers between five hundred and five thousand households, it means one more place where they must answer, which is a cost. Examining by effect, one asks: the effect this change addresses is bringing the standing review of the State Public Utilities Commission to cover Springhollow Water Company, the water price included; the cost is the added burden of review on these small suppliers, how heavy the example does not record, and what the other small suppliers paid this paper does not supply; whether there were lighter means, such as a simpler form of review for small suppliers, lies at the rule layer; why this one was chosen must be answered by the State Legislature, which made this change. The examination stops here, judging it neither excessive nor warranted; the way of examining is these few questions.

Judgment layer: the effect, cost, alternatives and reasons of a securing measure are stated by the position that made it; those it protects and those it restricts can both question it.

§4 The Kiln Safety Rules

By the way of examining in the previous section, read the Kiln Safety Rules. This reading presumes neither that they are excessive nor that they are warranted.

The line comes first. A kiln bursts at night and the one keeping watch is burned: this is a matter of the Base Layer, the body of the one who tends the fire. The State Labor Agency makes a rule for it, making explicit that "kilns must have temperature control and alarms," and the rule must also be enforced: this is what securing looks like. The safety of those who tend the fire does not wait until small kiln workshops can afford a system, nor until there are more suppliers on the list; this face has no margin and does not wait. The owner of a workshop that stopped firing said so himself: "Nobody wants another burn."

The measure is examined by its effect. That the line comes first answers whether to protect; how to protect must be examined by effect.

First question: what effect does it address? A kiln without temperature control, relying on people to tend the fire, bursting at night and injuring those tending it. What the Rules require is a kind of equipment, approved through a list.

Second question: on whom does its cost fall? An approved system costs about a year of a small kiln workshop's revenue. In Year 29, four of the eleven small kiln workshops in town stopped firing, and two young kiln workers' applications to open kilns went no further. These fell in the same year as the Rules took effect; what caused each stoppage and each stalled application the example does not record, and this paper does not attribute them all to the Rules. The workshop owner's words are his own account: "We can't afford this system, and there are only those two on the list." The cost also has a place on the list: only two are approved, those buying a system can choose only between the two, and the price too is in these two suppliers' hands. Whether this place has become a door, Paper 4 asks when it reads access; that the head of the review panel is a deputy general manager of the parent company of one supplier on the list, Paper 6 asks about when it reads the independence of review. This paper records it only as part of the cost.

Third question: are there other means? The rule layer can take many forms; the following are only examples, not ranked, and nothing is said about which would suffice: approval by a performance standard, so that any system meeting it counts, not only those on a list; phased implementation, giving small workshops a transition period; purchase subsidies or low-interest loans; night monitoring shared by several kilns; separate requirements for tending the fire with a hired worker and tending it oneself. This paper does not choose for the State Labor Agency. Whether the Rules considered these, the example does not record.

Fourth question: why was this one chosen? This question must be answered by the State Labor Agency, at the place where it made the Rules: why a list, why review by this association, why uniform implementation in Year 29. What the Ceramics Association answered was "The review follows technical standards; anyone may apply for approval"; that year another supplier applied, and its review had not yet concluded. This sentence answers whether the list is open; it does not answer why a list is used.

The securing measure itself also guards the line. Where the livelihoods of the four that stopped firing landed afterwards, the example does not record. If a rule that protects people leaves others unable to hold even their Base Layer, that part is examined under Paper 1, and is not exempted from being examined against the line because it comes from a securing.

The accident figure. Kiln accidents across the state fell by about sixty percent. The statistics do not record the total number of kilns or the volume fired, so this figure cannot be rewritten as an accident rate, nor said to mean that each kiln's risk fell by sixty percent; kilns that stopped and kilns that fired less also make the number of accidents smaller. It does not by itself prove that the Rules are appropriate, nor that the fall in accidents came wholly from the Rules. By that sentence of Political Economy, outcomes are clues for examination, not verdicts: it points the same way as what the Rules set out to do, and is a clue, not a verdict; nor does it tell us whether the cost had to be paid.

Tending the fire with a hired worker and tending it oneself are examined separately. Of the four that stopped firing, three had been tended by the kiln owner and his family, and one had hired a worker to tend the fire. The one burned on the night of the burst was a worker keeping the night watch.

In the one that hired a worker to tend the fire, what is protected is the worker and what is restricted is the owner: the two fall on different people. Here the line comes first: the worker's body is not traded for the owner's kiln. The owner's Emergence Layer is restricted, and this restriction is examined by the four questions above.

The three tended by the owners themselves and their families must be divided once more. The face that protects the family falls, as with a hired worker, on different people. The face that protects the owner himself falls on one and the same person: the one protected is he, and the one restricted is also he. By the previous section, neither direction is permitted on this face: his road to opening a kiln is not traded for protection of himself, nor is his lack of protection traded for more choice for him. Put together, the two sentences ask: is there a way of protecting him that does not cost him his kiln? Good will does not judge on his behalf how much risk he is to take (Political Economy Paper 6, §6). Whether these two faces can be separated within one kiln, and how, lies at the rule layer and the judgment layer; this paper does not separate them in their place; it only says that the two faces are examined differently, and cannot be merged into the single sentence "for the sake of safety."

Read to this point, whether the Rules are excessive this paper has not judged, and cannot judge: the cost, the alternatives and the reasons must all be answered by the position that made them. But this reading has set out the questions to be answered: protecting those who tend the fire does not wait; how they are protected must be explainable.

Judgment layer: the form of the Rules, the pace of implementation, and how the list is set are decided by the State Labor Agency, which states the effect, cost, alternatives and reasons; those who tend the fire, kiln owners and young kiln workers can all question it; whether the reviewing position is independent, Paper 6 writes.

§5 The Two Layers Holding Each Other Up Is a Direction

The Political Economy Specialist Paper has written: "Where the Base Layer is guarded well, people dare to leave and dare to try, and the room in the Emergence Layer is large; where the Emergence Layer develops well and the economy is active, it can in turn hold up the Base Layer." It goes on to say: "This sentence is written as a direction, not as a guarantee" (A Way Out, a Way Forward, §2). The Prequel uses this sentence as it stands; this paper uses two matters to see why it can only be a direction.

One shape. In Year 26 the veteran kiln worker's workshop installed a temperature-control system, so that no one had to watch the kiln at night; the two apprentices who had taken turns keeping the night watch over the fire moved to trying new glazes. A specialist paper of the Theory of the Company has read this: what was done changed; the people were not taken away (The Company in the Age of AI, a specialist paper of the Theory of the Company). In this paper, it is one shape of the two layers holding each other up: an arrangement that lets those who tend the fire take less risk also frees up space for people to try.

It does not prove that the direction has been reached, nor does it guarantee that this will happen again. In Year 29, when the Kiln Safety Rules took effect, the system this workshop had installed in Year 26 was not on the approved list, and was replaced. Whether that system was safe, the example does not record; its not being on the list proves neither that it was unsafe nor that it was safe. In the same workshop, a rule that protects people and a space that had already grown collided once more. Holding each other up happens case by case; it is not a state achieved once and for all.

An instance of growth not completing securing on the institution's behalf. The Crossways warehouse on the outskirts of Ridgefoot was expanded in Year 25, and its employees went from about three thousand to about seven thousand. This is a development in the Emergence Layer: four thousand new jobs. Over the same period, the average rent for a one-bedroom apartment rose by about seventy percent; in Year 28 a warehouse sorter's monthly rent was about half a month of his wages; from Year 26 on, Crossways has given warehouse workers a monthly housing allowance worth about ten percent of the rent; in the city's rules, no securing for housing at all has been embedded (Paper 1, §5).

The warehouse expansion and the rise in rent are facts that stand side by side, not an established cause, and this paper does not attribute the rise in rent to the warehouse. What this instance can say is that the increase in jobs did not automatically write securing for housing into the local institution. Whose lives the new jobs and the allowance have actually held up, this instance has not established; wages, private supply, and the mutual help of family and neighbors may all actually hold some people up, and this face cannot be left out. Yet their existence does not complete, on behalf of the constructs that make and enforce rules, that accountability of securing housing. Whether anyone is actually held up and whether the institution's securing has been carried out are two matters, to be judged separately. So one cannot take a development in the Emergence Layer as directly establishing that institutional securing of the Base Layer has been achieved. Holding each other up is not a place the market will necessarily reach by itself; it can only be written as a direction; the institution's share is done at the rule layer.

Ridgefoot leaves one more question: whether, and how, accumulation has pressed on the conditions of housing and on the arrangements for securing it. For the first half, what effect the warehouse expansion had on renting in the city depends on who rents, where, and where the new employees live, none of which the example records; for the second half, the city council rejected the rent-filing proposal in Year 28, and whether influence bought with accumulation entered that vote the example does not record either. Both questions need material; this paper writes up to the question and stops. If one day material shows accumulation pressing on rule-making, that is for Political Economy's proposition of political–economic separation to examine (Political Economy Paper 4, E23), and is not supplied here.

The direction is not used as a demand on every place. The two layers holding each other up is worth striving for; it is not used to demand that every market and every company achieve it, and failing to achieve it is not thereby crossing the line. It can fall back, and falling back is recognized by what has happened.

Judgment layer: whether a place's development has in turn held up the Base Layer is looked at by that place's rule-makers and asked about by the people there; this paper does not answer for them.

SC5 The two kinds of "excess" are not symmetrical: the way of examining and the order differ; the two layers matter equally. The Base Layer side is examined by the line, with no margin: where it falls on a person, crossing the line leaves no room for "not excessive" (per Institution Theory Z9, Political Economy E21). A securing measure that restricts others' Emergence Layer is examined by its effect: it states the effect it addresses, its cost to the capacity to produce, to serve and to give conditions, the alternatives available and the reasons for its choice; the securing measure itself also guards the line (the same form as E17: the original speaks of checks on economic positions; here it is extended to the form of securing rules; extension; own undertaking). Where both fall on one and the same person, neither direction is permitted: his Emergence Layer is not traded for his securing, nor is the absence of securing in his Base Layer traded for more choice for him (per E35; Political Economy Paper 6, §4). "Priority" is the order when the two conflict; it is not to be read as "more important." The two layers holding each other up is a direction, not a guarantee (per A Way Out, a Way Forward, E40).

Where This Paper Stops

This paper has laid down two propositions: above the line, things are left open as usual, leaving open is not leaving unquestioned, and conditions must be within reach (SC4); the two kinds of "excess" are not symmetrical, the asymmetry lies in the way of examining and the order, the two layers matter equally, and the two layers holding each other up is a direction (SC5).

What it has not proved: rates of growth; which industrial policy is better; how the strength of securing measures is to be quantified; whether the Kiln Safety Rules are excessive or warranted; what caused the rise in rent in Ridgefoot.

Handed to later papers: whether an approved list of only two has become a door, in Paper 4; that the head of the review panel is a deputy general manager of the parent company of one supplier on the list, in Paper 6; funding as a kind of condition, the state's subsidies and private investment, in Paper 5.

Remainders left: how the conditions of a place and the thresholds of that same place are to be read together, for which this paper has given only a direction, not a method; how, in a particular rule, the face that falls on the same person is to be separated from the face that falls on others, at the rule layer and the judgment layer; the two questions of Ridgefoot, which need material.

引言

第 28 年春，青窑镇一座小窑坊的窑炉在夜里爆裂。守夜的一位工人烧伤，住进了医院。这座窑没有控温系统，靠人守火。

秋天，州劳动厅发布《窑炉安全办法》：州内一切窑炉，第 29 年起须装经认可的控温与报警系统。认可由王国州陶瓷业协会作技术评审，州劳动厅据评审意见列入名单。名单上有两家供应商，其中一家是青窑瓷业的设备子公司；协会技术评审组的组长，是青窑瓷业的副总经理。一套认可系统的价，约合镇上一座小窑坊一年的营收。

第 29 年，镇上原有的十一座小窑坊里，四座停了窑；两位满师的年轻窑工原本打算开窑，申请没有往下走。同一年，州劳动厅的统计说，全州窑炉事故数比上一年少了约六成。一位停窑的窑坊主在州议会听证时说："烧伤的事谁都不想再有。可这套系统我们买不起，名单上也只有那两家。"陶瓷业协会答："评审照技术标准，谁都可以申请认可。"那一年，另有一家供应商申请认可，评审尚未结束。

青窑镇、王国州与这里的窑坊、协会、条款，都是这个系列的设例。

守火的人的身体，在基础层上；一座窑开不开得成，一个年轻窑工能不能自己起一座窑，在涌现层。这一件里，两层碰在了一起。前置篇说过，两层同等重要，不对称的是核法与次序。这一篇把这一句展开：线以上的空间怎样留（第一节）；条件怎样给（第二节）；两相冲突时怎样核（第三节）；窑炉安全办法照这一核法怎样读（第四节）；两层互相托起，为什么只是方向（第五节）。

第一节　线以上照常放开

第一篇写的是线。线以上是什么？

政治经济学定过：闸以外，涌现层的交换照常放开。「名声换来机会，资本换来更多的资本，资质换来职位，上限之内的捐赠与合规的受聘，本身都不是问题」；「不以"可能被兑换"为由封住涌现层的交换」（政治经济学第四篇 E24）。那一篇用苹果城一次瓷展读过这一句：门票上印着一位有九十万关注者的人的名字，开展一周参观人数是上一年同类展的四倍；一位在青窑烧了三十一年窑的老窑工，有两件作品在第二厅（政治经济学第四篇第四节）。这一篇不重做。

在市场里，这一句有两面。

一面是不封。立规、执规的构不以"可能被兑换"为由封住涌现层的交换，也不以守线为名多封一寸：守线是对越线的那一种用法设限，不是对线以上的一切设限。一项保障措施对涌现层多出的限制，也要说明它与守线的关系；超出所需、又说不出根据的那一部分，不能只凭"为了保障"过关（自担）。第三节写怎样核。

另一面是开着。市场的空间，不只是现有的交换不被封住，还有新的人进得来：新的摊位，新的窑，新的配送。前置篇说过，通衢起于东市的四个摊位；一处市场里，今天的大户，也可以是昨天进来的新人。空间开着，是说这条进来的路，在线以上，由立规的构留着，不由已经在里面的人替后来的人关上（自担）。别人把这条路关掉，第四篇核；立规的构自己的规则把它收窄了，这一篇核。

放开不等于不问。政治经济学在同一处写明：两道闸只说那一篇处理的兑换限制，「没有撞上两道闸，不认证一次交易在一切方面正当，也不取消本系列别处守线、可被追问、保障与给条件的要求」（政治经济学第四篇 E24）。线以上的交换照常放开，也照常可以问：一项交易有没有在别处越线、封路；一个位置做大以后，权力面大了，制衡随之加强（政治经济学第三篇 E14）。放开说的是不封，不说不问；问说的是可以问，不说先封。

判断层：一项交换在线以上还是线上，一项限制是在守线还是在封空间，由立这项规则的构说明；受它限制的人可以问。

第二节　条件要够得着

空间之外还有条件。

政治经济学定过：「制度给通用的、不指定内容的条件」；时间、学习、流动、信贷可得、基本生计这一类清单，是规则层的例子；而且「够不着的条件不算已经给了」（政治经济学第六篇 E34）。那一篇用青窑瓷业第 25 年的减产读过这一句。被裁的九百人，州劳动厅给了失业金十二个月、任选一项职业培训的学费补贴、迁居补贴，还有一笔小额创业贷款，州担保一半；申领不问去向。可是州的培训课多开在苹果城，单程三小时，镇上被裁的人里报培训的不到一成五。那一篇写：这一成五本身不判州的成败，结果「可作核查条件的线索，不直接作成败判决」；它问的是这一份条件够得着谁，够不着谁（政治经济学第六篇第三节）。

这一篇多问一处：一处的条件，与同一处的门槛碰在一起时，怎样读。

州给的小额创业贷款，是一份让人试一试的条件：一个原本借不到钱的人，可以借一笔钱起一样营生。在青窑镇，起一样营生，可以就是起一座窑。四年以后，窑炉安全办法施行，一套认可系统约合一座小窑坊一年的营收；第 29 年，两位年轻窑工开窑的申请没有往下走。事例里没有记这两件落在同一个人身上：领过那笔贷款的人后来怎样，两位年轻窑工有没有领过，都没有记，本篇不把它们连到一起。它们只是落在了同一类路上：在青窑镇起一座窑。

所以，读一处的条件够不够得着，不能只看给了什么，还要连着那一处的门槛与代价一起读。给出一笔贷款的是一个位置，定下一项设备要求的是另一个位置；两个位置各自看，各自都可以说得过去。可人走的是同一条路：贷到的那一笔，够不够跨过那一道门槛。同一个制度里，一处给出的条件，可以被另一处立下的门槛吃掉；条件够不够得着，要在路上读，不在给的一方的账上读（推及：E34 原说够不着的条件不算已经给了；这里推到一处的条件与同一处的门槛合起来读，自担）。

这不是说门槛不该立：守火的人的安全，第三、四节要读，线在先。这是说，立门槛的位置与给条件的位置，要知道彼此；它们若都在州里，州要答得出：一个想起窑的人，在这两样之间，还走不走得过去。

条件给到谁、给多少，看给的一方与人之间是什么关系，承了多大的范围。制度论说，制度按关系厚度为内部的人提供条件，「只管条件，不管结果」（制度论 Z23）。在市场里，给通用条件的，首先是立规、执规的构，它给的是一个市场里人人都碰得到的那一份；一家公司给它的员工，照公司论；资助、补贴的机构给它资助的对象，第五篇写资金这一种条件。具体给什么、给多强，在规则层；够不够得着，在判断层。

理论段。 前置篇引过经济学第三篇所列制度在群体一级能做的三件事：让已经成长的人互相找到；保护已经成长的人自保；惩罚伤害他人利益的 14DD（经济学第三篇 §5.2）。照前置篇的读法，前两件落在这一篇：让人看得见、找得到，让人离得开（读法，自担）。照这两件读，市场里的条件，除了上面那份清单，还有两样要单独提出。一样是看得见：一个人做的东西，别人找不找得到；老窑工的釉色稳，镇外很少有人知道；苹果城的一次瓷展，第二厅里有他的两件作品。一样是离得开：在一家做不下去时，换一家、换一行、换一处的代价，压不压得住人。这两样在规则层可以有许多写法，比如标识标明它的来路，记录可以带走，换供给者不受多余的阻拦；这些只是例子，不排次序。维度的语言只在这一段借用，正文不按人的维度分类。

判断层：一处的条件够不够得着，由给条件的位置与立门槛的位置一起答；够不着的人可以问；结果作线索，不作判决。

SC4　线以上照常放开，放开不等于不问；条件要够得着。闸以外，涌现层的交换照常放开，不以"可能被兑换"为由封住；两道闸不穷尽守线、保障与给条件的要求，放开的交换照样可被追问（承政治经济学 E24）。制度给通用的条件，不制造涌现；够不着的条件不算已经给了（承 E34）。

第三节　两个"过度"

保障与空间会冲突。冲突可以从两头说：保障过度限制了涌现层；涌现层过度侵蚀了基础层。两个"过度"听起来对称，像一根秤杆的两头。这一篇的回答是：它们不对称。不对称的不是轻重，是核法与次序。

基础层一侧照线核，不留量。 落到一个人身上，越线就是越线，没有"越得不多"的余地。制度的施为不越线，「不随关系厚度调节，也不随任何条件打折」（制度论 Z9）；经济位置不以积累兑换对人的基础层的支配（政治经济学第四篇 E21）。所以"涌现层过度侵蚀基础层"里的"过度"，只是一种说法：侵蚀落到一个人身上，就已经是越线，没有一个"适度"的侵蚀可以留下。

限制别人涌现层的保障措施，照作用核。 一项保障措施，护的是一些人的基础层，限的可以是另一些人的涌现层。这时要核的不是线，是这项措施的作用。它要说明四件：它处理的是什么作用；它对生产、服务与提供条件的能力有什么代价；有没有可替代的办法；为什么选了这一种。保障措施自己也守线：它不能为了护一些人，压到另一些人的基础层上。

这一核法与政治经济学对经济位置的制衡同形。那一条说，制衡落在怎样使用上，不以压低能力为目的；同一处既是生产也是卡住时，制衡要说明它处理的是哪一项作用，怎样尽量保全生产、服务与给条件的能力，若改变配置也影响这些能力，「照实说明代价、可替代的办法与选择的理由」（政治经济学第三篇 E17）。原说对经济位置的制衡，这里推到保障规则的形态（自担）。

落在同一个人身上的，两个方向都不许。 冲突可以落在不同的人之间，也可以落在同一个人身上。政治经济学写过：「两层同等重要，一层不拿去换另一层，两个方向都不许」（政治经济学第六篇 E35）。落在一个人身上，就是两句：不拿他的涌现层去换他的保障；也不拿他基础层的没有保障去换他更多的选。那一篇还说：「基础层托得厚，若代价是限制涌现层，那份厚就是伪装成涵育的越界」（政治经济学第六篇第四节）。护一个人，不以替他关掉他自己的路为代价；给一个人更多的选，也不以撤掉他的底为代价。

"优先"是次序，不是轻重。 这里的"优先"，就是主线句说的"线在先"。两相冲突、落在不同的人之间时，线在先：护一些人的基础层，不等另一些人的涌现层有了着落。政治经济学说：「此刻的伤害不能等」（政治经济学第六篇 E33）。可"在先"说的是次序，不是说基础层更重要：两层同等重要，这一句不动。次序决定的是护不护，不等；作用核决定的是怎样护，护到哪里。线在先，不给保障措施一张空白的许可；保障措施照作用核，也不让线去等。

第一篇留下一件，可以照这一核法试一试。第 27 年，王国州把《公用事业法》的门槛由五千户降到五百户。对泉坳，这是委员会这一常设审查覆盖到了泉坳水业；对五百户到五千户之间的别的小供水者，这是多了一处要答的地方，是一份代价。照作用核，要问：这一改动处理的作用，是让州公用事业委员会的常设审查覆盖到泉坳水业，水价在内；代价是这些小供水者多了一份受审的负担，有多重，事例里没有记，别的小供水者付出了什么，本篇不补；有没有更轻的办法，比如对小供水者用更简的审法，在规则层；为什么选了这一种，要由作出这一改动的州议会答。核到这里，不判它过度，也不判它正当；核法就是这几问。

判断层：一项保障措施的作用、代价、替代与理由，由作出它的位置说明；受它护的人与受它限的人，都可以问它。

第四节　窑炉安全办法

照上一节的核法，读窑炉安全办法。这一读不预认它过度，也不预认它正当。

线在先。 一座窑在夜里爆裂，守夜的人烧伤，这是基础层的事：守火的人的身体。州劳动厅为此立一项规则，把"窑炉要有控温与报警"显明出来，并且要执行，这是保障的样子。守火的人的安全，不等小窑坊买得起系统，也不等名单上有更多家供应商；这一面不留量，不等。停窑的那位窑坊主自己也说："烧伤的事谁都不想再有。"

措施照作用核。 线在先，回答的是护不护；怎样护，要照作用核。

第一问，它处理的是什么作用？是窑炉在没有控温、靠人守火的时候，夜里爆裂、伤到守火的人。办法要求的是一种设备，经一份名单认可。

第二问，它的代价落在谁身上？一套认可系统约合一座小窑坊一年的营收。第 29 年，镇上十一座小窑坊里四座停了窑，两位年轻窑工开窑的申请没有往下走。这几样与办法施行在同一年；停窑与申请停下各自是什么原因，事例里没有记，本篇不全归到办法上。那位窑坊主的话，是他自己的说法："这套系统我们买不起，名单上也只有那两家。"代价还有一处在名单上：认可的只有两家，买系统的人只能在两家里选，价钱也在这两家手里。这一处是不是成了一道门，第四篇读准入时问；评审组的组长是名单上一家供应商的母公司的副总经理，第六篇读评审的独立时问。这一篇只把它记作代价的一部分。

第三问，有没有别的办法？规则层可以有许多样子，下面几样只是例子，不排次序，也不说哪一样够：按一项性能标准认可，谁的系统达到都算，不只认一份名单；分期施行，给小窑坊一段过渡；购置的补助或低息的贷款；几座窑共用的夜间监测；雇人守火与自己守火分开设要求。这一篇不替州劳动厅选。办法里有没有考虑过这些，事例里没有记。

第四问，为什么选了这一种？这一问要由州劳动厅答，答在它作出办法的那一处：为什么是一份名单，为什么由这个协会评审，为什么第 29 年一律施行。陶瓷业协会答的是"评审照技术标准，谁都可以申请认可"；那一年另有一家申请，评审还没有结束。这一句答的是名单开不开放，没有答为什么用名单。

保障措施自身也守线。 停了窑的四家，此后的生计落在哪里，事例里没有记。若一项护人的规则让另一些人连基础层都守不住了，那一部分照第一篇核，不因为它出自一项保障就免于核线。

事故数。 全州窑炉事故数少了约六成。统计没有记窑炉总数与烧成量，这个数字不能改写成事故率，也不能说成每一座窑的风险少了六成；停了的窑、少烧的窑，也会让事故数变小。它不独自证明办法适当，也不证明事故的减少全由办法带来。照政治经济学那一句，结果是核查的线索，不是判决：它与办法要做的事同向，是一条线索，不是判决；它也没有告诉我们代价是不是非付不可。

雇人守火与自己守火，分开核。 停窑的四座里，三座原由窑主自己与家人守火，一座雇了一位守火的工人。爆裂那一夜烧伤的，是一位守夜的工人。

雇人守火的那一座，护的是工人，限的是窑主：两边落在不同的人身上。这里线在先：工人的身体，不拿去换窑主的那一座窑。窑主的涌现层受了限，这一限照上面四问核。

窑主自己与家人守火的三座，要再分一次。护家人的那一面，与雇工一样，落在不同的人身上。护窑主自己的那一面，落在同一个人身上：受护的是他，受限的也是他。照上一节，这一面两个方向都不许：不拿他开窑的路去换对他自己的保护，也不拿他没有保护去换他更多的选。两句合起来，问的是：护他自己的那一份，有没有不以拿走他的窑为代价的办法。善意不替他判断他要冒多大的险（政治经济学第六篇第六节）。这两面在一座窑里分不分得开，怎样分，在规则层与判断层；本篇不替它分，只说两面的核法不同，不能并成一句"为了安全"。

读到这里，办法是不是过度，本篇没有判，也判不出：代价、替代与理由，都要由作出它的位置去答。可这一读把要答的问题摆清楚了：护守火的人，不等；怎样护，要说得出。

判断层：办法的形态、施行的步子、名单怎样定，由州劳动厅定，它说明作用、代价、替代与理由；守火的人、窑主、年轻窑工都可以问它；评审的位置独不独立，第六篇写。

第五节　两层互相托起，是方向

政治经济学专论写过：「基础层守得好，人敢走、敢试，涌现层的空间就大；涌现层发展得好，经济活跃，又能回过头来托住基础层。」它接着说：「这一句写作方向，不写作担保」（政治经济学专论第二节）。前置篇照用这一句，这一篇用两件事看它为什么只能是方向。

一个样子。 老窑工的窑坊第 26 年装了一套控温系统，夜里不必有人守窑；原来轮流守夜看火的两个学徒，改去试新釉。公司论的专论读过这一件：事变了，人没有被拿走（公司论专论《AI 时代的公司》）。放在这一篇，它是互相托起的一个样子：一项让守火的人少冒险的安排，同时给人腾出了试的空间。

它不证明方向已经到了，也不担保它会再发生。第 29 年，窑炉安全办法施行，这座窑坊第 26 年装的那套系统不在认可名单上，换了装。那套系统安不安全，事例里没有记；它不在名单上，不证明它不安全，也不证明它安全。同一座窑坊里，一项护人的规则与一处已经长出来的空间，又碰了一次。互相托起，是一次一次的事，不是一劳永逸的状态。

增长不替制度完成保障的一例。 岭下市郊的通衢仓库，第 25 年扩建，雇员由约三千人增至约七千人。这是涌现层的一份发展：四千份新的工作。同一段时间里，一居室的平均房租涨了约七成；第 28 年，一位仓库分拣工的月房租约是他半个月的工资；通衢从第 26 年起给仓库工人每月一份住房津贴，约合一成房租；城里的规则里，住房一项保障也没有载入（第一篇第五节）。

仓库扩建与房租上涨是并存的事实，不是已经查明的原因，本篇不把房租的上涨归到仓库上。这一例能说的是：工作增加，并没有自动把住房保障写进当地的制度。新增的工作与那份津贴实际托住了哪些人的生活，本例没有核清；工资、私人的供给、家人与邻里的互助，都可能实际托住一些人，这一面不能略去。可它们的存在，也不替立规、执规的构完成住房保障这一份职责。有没有人被实际托住，与制度的保障落没落实，是两件事，要分别判。所以，不能从涌现层的发展，径认基础层的制度保障已经实现。互相托起不是市场自己一定会走到的地方，只能写作方向；制度那一份，做在规则层。

岭下还留下一问：积累有没有、怎样压到了居住的条件与保障的安排上。前一半，仓库的扩建对城里的租房有什么作用，要看谁在租、租在哪里、新来的雇员住在哪里，事例里都没有记；后一半，市议会第 28 年否决了租金申报一案，那一次表决里有没有积累换来的影响，事例里也没有记。这两问都要材料，本篇写到问为止。若有一天材料显出积累压到了立规上，那是政治经济学政经分离那一条要核的（政治经济学第四篇 E23），不在这里补。

方向不拿来要求每一处。 两层互相托起值得争取；它不拿来要求每一个市场、每一家公司都做到，做不到也不就是越线。可以回落，回落按发生了什么认。

判断层：一处的发展有没有回过头来托住基础层，由那一处的立规者看，由那里的人问；本篇不替它答。

SC5　两个"过度"不对称：核法与次序不同，两层同等重要。基础层一侧照线核，不留量：落到一个人身上，越线没有"不过度"的余地（承制度论 Z9、政治经济学 E21）。限制别人涌现层的保障措施，照作用核：说明所处理的作用、对生产服务与提供条件能力的代价、可替代的办法与选择的理由；保障措施自身也守线（与 E17 同形：原说对经济位置的制衡，这里推到保障规则的形态；推及自担）。落在同一个人身上的，两个方向都不许：不拿他的涌现层换他的保障，也不拿他基础层的没有保障换他更多的选（承 E35；政治经济学第六篇第四节）。"优先"是两相冲突时的次序，不读作"更重要"。两层互相托起，是方向，不是担保（承专论 E40）。

这一篇停在哪里

这一篇立了两条：线以上照常放开，放开不等于不问；条件要够得着（SC4）；两个"过度"不对称，不对称在核法与次序，两层同等重要，两层互相托起是方向（SC5）。

它没有证明的：增长率；哪一种产业政策更好；保障措施的强度怎样定量；窑炉安全办法过度还是正当；岭下的房租上涨由什么造成。

交给后面各篇的：认可名单只有两家，是不是成了一道门，在第四篇；评审组的组长是名单上一家供应商的母公司的副总经理，在第六篇；资金这一种条件，州的补助、私人的投资，在第五篇。

留下的余项：一处的条件与同一处的门槛怎样一起读，这一篇只给了方向，没有给方法；落在同一个人身上的那一面，在一项具体的规则里怎样与落在别人身上的那一面分开，在规则层与判断层；岭下那两问，要材料。

本篇命题（合列）

SC4　线以上照常放开，放开不等于不问；条件要够得着。闸以外，涌现层的交换照常放开，不以"可能被兑换"为由封住；两道闸不穷尽守线、保障与给条件的要求，放开的交换照样可被追问（承政治经济学 E24）。制度给通用的条件，不制造涌现；够不着的条件不算已经给了（承 E34）。

SC5　两个"过度"不对称：核法与次序不同，两层同等重要。基础层一侧照线核，不留量：落到一个人身上，越线没有"不过度"的余地（承制度论 Z9、政治经济学 E21）。限制别人涌现层的保障措施，照作用核：说明所处理的作用、对生产服务与提供条件能力的代价、可替代的办法与选择的理由；保障措施自身也守线（与 E17 同形：原说对经济位置的制衡，这里推到保障规则的形态；推及自担）。落在同一个人身上的，两个方向都不许：不拿他的涌现层换他的保障，也不拿他基础层的没有保障换他更多的选（承 E35；政治经济学第六篇第四节）。"优先"是两相冲突时的次序，不读作"更重要"。两层互相托起，是方向，不是担保（承专论 E40）。
