Abstract

This paper reads the second layer of Love Theory: the times when she is not before me. What this layer reads is remembering and anticipating, written 11DD+12DD in SAE's levels.

Longing is her still counting with me when she is not before me. The remembered her is not the original: each time I think of her, I take her up again; the anticipated her has a shape, and can fall through. When it falls through, what moves in return is the remembered her, in one of two ways: revising it, or not revising it and by it discerning that the her before me is "wrong." Rewriting is with me first, and carries no direction; only when, by way of words and deeds, it actually leaves space for her later understanding and action does it point in the direction of cultivation. Not revising, and saying aloud that she is "wrong," fixes for her who she is, and slides in the direction of colonization. Crystallization in itself carries no direction either; only when crystallization, or the first meeting, is used to measure the her who is later before me, fixing for her by way of words and deeds who she is, is the direction of colonization read. If what is loved is the person she resembles, anyone who resembles that person will do, and this love is not for her; changing her after the remembered one is how the direction of colonization shows itself clearly at this layer. Expectation is what I arrange on the strength of dealings and point at her later conduct; it does not turn into a duty of hers, and her response is material, not a settling of the account; attaching to an expectation the condition "If you loved me, you would ..." slides in the direction of colonization. Testing love is a demand arranged in advance; the response it obtains can become material, but cannot certify the love in her heart. When the one loved is gone, the remembered her is still there; death is not the same as the ending of love.

Love at this layer ends when the remembered her fades, or is rewritten until she no longer pulls at him; it can also end when what he anticipates falls through again and again and he stops waiting. This paper interfaces with Stendhal and Barthes. It does not prove that thinking of her much means loving her deeply, nor that love is true only if it remembers accurately.

Introduction

The man in Paper 1 wakes the next day in another city, and the voice is still there. He does not know who she is, and she is not before him, yet that jolt still counts. This paper takes up from there, with another pair of people.

Jiang Yuan and Ye Qing have known each other for three years. Last spring she went to a city in the south for a project that would keep her there more than a year. The project in the south is something she has taken on herself; Jiang Yuan did not want her to go, and he also understood why she had to; he too has things to do that keep him in this city, and she did not read his staying as not caring. The two of them agreed to talk on the phone every Sunday evening at nine.

Over the year, a few things happened. On Sunday evenings he began looking at his watch at half past eight; at five past nine, with no call yet, he was already wondering whether she had forgotten, whether something had happened. When he thought of her, what he thought of was often the first time he saw her, three years earlier, at a friend's house: she stood by the window peeling an orange, and the way she looked then was clearer than at any time since. For a while he thought that if she still cared, she would call an extra time of her own accord; one week he deliberately did not mention it, to see whether she would. At the end of the year she came back: her hair was cut short and she talked faster than before; when she laughed she no longer lowered her head. Watching her walk toward him at the station, his first thought was: she is no longer the one I remember.

For most of that year she was not before him. Then where, that year, was his love?

What this layer reads. Aesthetics Ray 9, The Beauty of Cognition, writes this layer (11DD+12DD) as the two halves of the beauty of cognition: remembering and anticipating. "A living thing still remembers what is no longer before it, and anticipates what has not yet come."1 Upstream also says that remembering is reconstructive retention, not faithful storage, and that anticipating is intuitive prediction, not reflective rational inference.2 Ray 9 asks about the point that those who look cannot not acknowledge together; this paper asks how, for one person, the remembered her and the anticipated her operate in a relation.

Where this layer begins and where it ends, Ray 9 gives tests for. Where it begins: "take away what has just sounded in the ear at this moment, take away the bars just played; is the right note still there? Whatever is still there and still at work belongs to this level."3 Applied to love: the sentence she has just finished saying, if one takes only the way her voice sounds while it is still in the ear, is the moment Paper 1 reads; if the pull already depends on the meaning of the words or on a memory, it is read separately, at the corresponding layer. She has gone, and that sentence is still at work in him; the face of it that is not before him and is still remembered and anticipated belongs to this paper; layers are not divided by whether a few seconds have passed or it is already the next day. Where it ends: "as soon as one asks who is remembering and who is anticipating, it is handed on to a later ray." Applied to love: once what is asked is "It is I who remember her, I who am waiting," it has reached Paper 3.4

The handover from Paper 1. The moment in Paper 1 concerns only that moment. This paper reads that moment's still counting when she is not before me, and every later recalling and every later waiting. It does not ask how this continuation becomes my own direction, where others would also make sense and still I cannot exchange her: that is Paper 4. Nor does it ask who it is that remembers and anticipates, that it is I who love her: that is Paper 3.

Method. As in Paper 1: the conclusions of upstream papers are said here in plain words; sources, the proposition numbers of other papers and the places where upstream words are carried beyond their original place all go in the notes, and where something is extended, this series takes it on as its own undertaking. What the relational layer has already said and already interfaced with is used directly and pointed back to in the notes. The examples use people and events this series makes up, and also literature; literature serves only as example. Jiang Yuan and Ye Qing's year runs through the whole paper.

§1 The Remembered Her

L10 Longing is her still counting with me when she is not before me; the remembered her is not the original, and each time I think of her, I take her up again

Ray 9 writes the point peculiar to this layer as: what is not before one, and not laid out before one either, still counts in a living thing.5 Longing falls at this point. Ye Qing is in the south and Jiang Yuan is here; she is not before him, nor is there a photograph of her laid out in front of him; she is at work in him all the same: he will stop for a moment outside a fruit shop because there are oranges on the stall.

The Book of Songs poem "Gathering Kudzu" writes of the same point: "There, gathering the kudzu: one day not seen is like three months";6 with him, a day without that one runs longer than a day. The Minor Preface of the Mao tradition reads the poem as "fear of slander"; here only the letter of it is borrowed, to read the side of not seeing and still being pulled.

The remembered her is not the original. Ray 9 says: "What it remembers is not the original: to recall is to take it up again".7 Each time Jiang Yuan thinks of Ye Qing peeling the orange, the way she looks is a little different: once it is afternoon outside the window, once it is evening; once the hand peeling the orange is her left. He cannot say which time was the original, and yet it is still her. Upstream also says that recalling is one more re-experiencing, possibly accompanied by developmental rewriting.8 Each time he thinks of her is one more taking up, and in being taken up it has already moved.

Li Shangyin writes: "This feeling might wait to become a recollection; only, at the time, one was already bewildered."9 Already bewildered at the time: what is recollected later is the time it was taken up again.

So longing is not a her stored and left untouched. It is a taking up, time after time, and every time it is with me.

L11 The anticipated her can fall through. When it does, what moves in return is the remembered her, in one of two ways: revising it, or not revising it and by it discerning that the her before me is "wrong." Rewriting is with me first; only when, by way of words and deeds, it actually leaves space for her later understanding and action does it point in the direction of cultivation. Not revising, and saying aloud that she is "wrong," fixes for her who she is and slides in the direction of colonization; whether that takes hold is seen in her later understanding and action

What is remembered reaches forward and becomes what is anticipated. At nine on Sunday he anticipates that her call will come; at the station at the end of the year, he anticipates that the one walking toward him is the one he remembers. What is anticipated has a shape, and when the time comes it can fall through.10

Ray 9 writes the two ways that follow falling through: "'moves in return' is of two kinds: revising what is remembered; or not revising it, and by it discerning that what is before one is wrong".11 Applied to love, these are the two.

One is revising it. Jiang Yuan looks at Ye Qing, who has cut her hair short and talks faster than before; after a few days the remembered her has changed: the one who peeled the orange with her head lowered is still there, only she has become "the earlier her"; the present her is this woman who talks fast. This revision is, to begin with, with him. It carries no direction toward her, because it has not yet entered her conditions of occurrence. Relation Theory reads direction once an effect has entered the other party's conditions of occurrence, and there are two directions: cultivation, which goes toward making space; colonization, which goes toward pouring in and occupying.12

Only when the rewriting reaches her by way of words and deeds is direction read. Suppose he says to her: "You've changed a lot this year; I'm still getting to know you." This tells her that he has seen the change, without fixing for her what the change amounts to; how she understands her own year is left with her. Only if this conversation actually gives her a place to explain herself and to revise what the two of them understand does it go toward making space, in the direction of cultivation; merely not fixing it for her is not yet enough to certify cultivation by itself.13 Whether it took hold is seen in how she afterward understands and how she acts.

The other is not revising it, and by it discerning that the her before him is wrong. He does not revise, and says to himself, "She shouldn't be like this"; this is still with him. Once it is said aloud, "You're not this kind of person," it fixes for her who she is: a remembered look of hers has become her ruler from then on. This heads toward pouring in and occupying, and slides in the direction of colonization. Whether it took hold is seen in how she afterward understands and how she acts; she may laugh it off, or she may, from then on, talk more slowly in front of him. From outside, only clues can be seen.

What is spoken of here is direction, not a label: nothing is said about what kind of person Jiang Yuan is; only where this one effect is headed is read.

What is anticipated is anticipated only if it can fall through. Ray 9 lists "anticipation that cannot fall through" as a counterfeit: a fortune slip says, "hold on, and the clouds will part and the moon appear"; whatever comes afterward fits it, "but at the time there was no shape in one's mind that would fall through if things did not fit".14 Longing has this kind too. She calls: she loves him. She does not call: she is afraid of disturbing him, and that is love too. She is cool toward him: she is testing him, and that is still love. Of anticipation like this, Ray 9's words hold: "if whatever comes fits, nothing arrived first":15 it is not waiting for her; it is waiting for a reading fixed long in advance. If this kind stays only in his mind, it is still with him; once it is said aloud and used to read each of her responses, it has reached the point Relation Theory speaks of: the qualifying condition has become a position, "such that no response of the other party can be registered as a refutation".16

L12 Rewriting the remembered her in longing is with me, and is not colonization; only when it lands on her by way of words and deeds is direction read

L11 has already said half of this proposition; it is set out on its own here because it is often read the wrong way round. In longing, a person will change the remembered her back and forth: adding a tenderness she did not have, deleting a coolness she did have, remembering a quarrel as a joke. This is rewriting.

Rewriting is with me; it is not colonization. When Relation Theory reads direction, it reads where an effect is headed once it has entered the other party's conditions of occurrence;17 how I remember her, here with myself, has not entered her conditions of occurrence, and so has no direction toward her. In reading unrequited love, Relation Theory has kept two things apart: "how the actual words and deeds of the one loved, or what that person has left, have entered this side; and a merely imagined image. In the second, one does not claim to have received that person's response."18 Whatever the remembered her has been rewritten into belongs to the second; one does not claim that it is her.

That the rewriting is with me does not mean that nothing has happened either. What it enters are my own later conditions of occurrence: how I wait for her from then on, how I see her, all start from the rewritten one. Matters on my own side Relation Theory has read where it reads a person's relation to himself; this paper does not develop them.19

Only when it lands on her by way of words and deeds is direction read. Once the rewritten one is said aloud and used to make demands of her, it has passed into the second of L11's two ways, or into L13 below.

§2 Crystallization and Resembling Someone

L13 Crystallization in itself carries no direction: the remembered reaches forward, and often reaches into ways she is not. Only when crystallization or the first meeting is used to measure the her who is later before me, fixing for her by way of words and deeds who she is, is the direction of colonization read

Stendhal gave this a name: crystallization (§6). A branch that has lost its leaves in winter is thrown into an abandoned salt mine; taken out two or three months later, it is covered in glittering crystals, and the original branch can no longer be recognized. In longing, the remembered her goes the same way: through the year, when Jiang Yuan thinks of her, he often thinks of her in ways she is not, more patient than she really is, quicker to laugh than she really is.

Crystallization in itself carries no direction. It is with me: the remembered reaching forward, into ways she is not; this reaching has not yet entered her conditions of occurrence. Proust writes of Swann: once, when Odette bent her head to look at an engraving, the way she looked put Swann in mind of Zipporah, Jethro's daughter, in a fresco in the Sistine Chapel; and after that "a fragment of the fresco appeared in her face and in her body, which from then on he always sought to find there again, whether he was with Odette or only thinking of her."20 When he thinks of her, a painting has been hung on her. This hanging is with Swann.

Whether crystallization misremembers is not judged at this layer. What is remembered is not the original to begin with (L10); remembering wrongly is also remembering.21 This layer does not judge whether one remembers accurately: remembering accurately is a matter of correctness.

Only when crystallization is used to measure the her who is later before me is direction read. When Ye Qing comes back at the end of the year, if Jiang Yuan says, "You used to be so patient," and that patience is something he hung on her during the year, this sentence measures her by crystallization, fixes for her by way of words and deeds who she is, and slides in the direction of colonization.

Measuring her by the first meeting has the same shape. Nalan Xingde writes: "If life were only as at the first meeting."22 The one of the first meeting is often clearer in remembering than at any later time, like the one peeling an orange whom Jiang Yuan remembers. Comparing the later her with the first meeting in his own mind is still with him; once it is said aloud, "If only you were still the way you were the first time I saw you," the first meeting has been made a ruler. Paper 1 said, in reading first sight, that measuring what follows by that moment falls to this paper.23

Zhang Ailing's Red Rose, White Rose has two faces of the same thing; only the gist is given here: the one he did not marry stays good in remembering; the one he married is before him every day and becomes ordinary. The one not before him crystallizes; the one before him falls through; both are with him, and neither is yet headed toward anyone.

L14 Resembling someone: if what is loved is the person she resembles, anyone who resembles that person will do; she is then replaceable, and this love is not for her. Changing her after the remembered one is how the direction of colonization shows itself clearly at this layer

Paper 1 divided the sense at first sight of having long known someone into three kinds: the quality of a familiar face stays in Paper 1; resembling someone, where there really is a remembered person or kind of person, depends on memory and belongs to this paper; a former life and destiny, readings given to that instant after the fact, belong to Paper 3.24

Resembling someone is another, remembered person reaching onto the her before me. The first time Jiang Yuan saw Ye Qing, the way she peeled the orange was like someone he had liked at university. A resemblance can be only a beginning; if what he later sees is Ye Qing, it passes. But if what he has loved all along is the one she resembles, then anyone who resembles that one will do: Ye Qing is replaceable, and this love is not for her. By the first proposition laid down in Paper 1, love is this one, irreplaceable;25 the share that is resemblance, if it stops at resemblance, is not yet a cannot-not toward this one.

Changing her after the remembered one is how the direction of colonization shows itself clearly at this layer. Hitchcock's Vertigo, given here only in gist and borrowed only for the structure of changing her: as Scottie understands it at the time, he has lost the woman he loved; later he meets someone who looks like her, and he changes her one thing at a time, her clothes, the color of her hair, the way she wears it and the way she walks, until she is like the other. What he changes is the outward look of the person before him; what he wants is the remembered one; her words and deeds, and what she herself wants, are all held by him to that look. This heads toward pouring in and occupying, and lands on her again and again; whether it took hold is seen in how she afterward understands herself and how she acts.26

The passage on Swann can also be read by this proposition: what he looks for in Odette is the fresco. The looking is with him; requiring her to match the fresco brings it under this proposition.

§3 Expectation and Testing

L15 Expectation is what I arrange on the strength of dealings and point at her later conduct, and it does not turn into a duty of hers; her response is material, not a settling of the account. "If you loved me, you would ..." attaches a qualifying condition to an expectation, fixing for her what something means to her, and slides in the direction of colonization

What is anticipated, when it bears on her conduct, is expectation. Relation Theory gives expectation this minimal definition: "Expectation is what one party, on the strength of dealings, arranges on his own side and points at the other party's later conduct; it is a construct left behind, does not turn into a duty, and does not certify that the other party has avowed anything".27 Jiang Yuan's anticipating the nine o'clock call on Sunday comes by two avenues. One is that the two of them agreed on it, each placing an expectation with the other: this is a promise, entered separately below;28 the other is what he himself has arranged over a year of dealings. This share that he anticipates is with him, and does not turn into a duty of Ye Qing's.

Her response is material, not a settling of the account: the other party's response enters my conditions of occurrence and becomes material for the next expectation; the mere fact that a response has occurred settles nothing, and does not certify that the other party has avowed anything.29 The call's coming at nine does not, by that alone, certify that she has avowed anything; when it has not come at five past nine, his expectation alone does not put her in his debt either. The share the two of them agreed on is a promise, and is entered separately: it binds her by where she herself stands and by the effects already left, not by her owing him something; whether this time is non-fulfillment, obstruction or a joint revision of the agreement, he, on his side, can only enter fallibly, together with traces; it cannot be erased by the entry of expectation, nor is it decided by that entry.30 The response still becomes material for his next waiting.

Expectation grows out of dealings. Relation Theory says that expectation grows out of repeated dealings: response and non-response, on time and not on time, all become material.31 The weekly call the two agreed on is one entry; the eagerness and the worry that sedimented later, and the hope that she would call an extra time of her own accord, are another. In the first month, when the call had not come by five past nine, he was still only waiting; by the tenth month he could no longer sit still. No one ever promised the second; it sedimented out of one Sunday after another.

Nor does it turn into a duty of hers because it has grown for a long time. Relation Theory has written the two exits by which expectation leaves that slot: the person of whom it is expected takes it on in his own law, or it becomes a role requirement explicitly arranged by both parties and maintained by relational power.32 If Ye Qing, on her own side, takes on "If he wants one more call, I'll call more," that is something she has taken on, and it is hers; his expectation cannot take it on in her place.

An expectation can be said out loud. To say, "This week I'd really like you to call one more time," is a form of questioning. Attaching a qualifying condition to it is different: "If you loved me, you'd call every day." This sentence hangs whether she calls on whether she loves him, and fixes for her what something means to her. Relation Theory says that "to attach a qualifying condition to an expectation, fixing for the other party what something means to him, for instance by hanging it as a criterion on who he is or what he cares about, is to slide in the direction of colonization, and whether that takes hold depends on the other party's later understanding and action".33 Hanging it on love is a common form at this layer.

Whether there is a duty to reciprocate is a matter for the relational layer; Relation Theory has already interfaced with Mauss, the Book of Rites and Mozi on it, and it is pointed back to here.34

L16 Testing love is a demand arranged in advance; a response obtained this way can become material, but cannot certify the love in her heart

That week, Jiang Yuan deliberately did not mention that he wanted one more call, to see whether Ye Qing would call an extra time of her own accord. She did. He was relieved: she cares.

This is a test. It is not waiting; it is a setup arranged in advance for her response to fall into, to be used to prove something. At the moment of arranging it, the expectation already has a condition attached: if she calls more of her own accord, that is love; if she does not, it is not (L15). So a test is a demand arranged in advance.

The response so obtained can become material: her extra call enters his conditions of occurrence, and his waiting from then on will be different. But it cannot certify the love in her heart. The mere fact that a response has occurred settles nothing, and does not certify that the other party has avowed anything;35 whether one loves is a matter for the one who loves, and from outside, only clues can be seen.36 She may make the extra call because she misses him, or because she is worried that something has happened to him, or only because she happens to be free that day; the response itself cannot tell these apart.

The words and deeds that arranged this setup are entered separately. If this week's not mentioning it has, in concrete dealings, already had an identifiable effect that lands on her, that entry is made as usual; knowing only that in his own mind he deliberately said nothing does not yet by itself certify this entry. That she does not know the criterion is not the same as these words and deeds having no effect. The criterion inside the setup, that an extra call means love and no extra call means no love, is only with him; so long as it has not been expressed in words and deeds, it has not yet entered her conditions of occurrence. If the criterion has already reached her by way of arrangement or pressure, the reading of direction need not wait, either, for him to spell it out. If he says the result out loud, "You didn't call an extra time, so obviously you don't care," the criterion has landed on her: it fixes for her what a phone call means to her, and slides in the direction of colonization; whether it took hold is seen in how she afterward understands and how she acts.37

What this proposition speaks of is testing love: the criterion lies with the one who arranges the test, and what is being tried is whether the other loves. Another question is verifying who the person before one is: a response obtained by way of a sign checks an identity and does not testify to a love; how this question is used is read separately, according to what it is used for. The two questions can fall within one and the same scene, and are to be read apart.38

§4 Gone

L17 When the one loved is gone, the remembered her is still there; death is not the same as the ending of love

Ye Qing is not before him because she is in the south. There is another way of not being before one: the person is gone.

Love is seen from the subject's side;39 so death is not the same as the ending of love. A man whose beloved died many years ago still remembers her and thinks of her, and each time he thinks of her he takes her up again (L10); that love is on his side, and does not end because her side is gone.

This has the same shape as Relation Theory's reading of the relation with the dead: "A relation with the dead, as in reading Plato, is a relation. The actual effect of the words, deeds and works the dead have left is read apart from today's imagination and reconstruction from memory; no response is required of the dead."40 In the remembered her there is the effect, still at work, of the words and deeds she left while she lived, and there is also the rewriting done each time he takes her up again afterward; the two are read apart. The rewriting does not claim to be her response (L12). Whether there is another side after death, and what it is like, Relation Theory does not judge, and this series does not judge either.

This proposition says only that the ending is not decided by death; it does not say that love necessarily continues. The remembered her may also slowly fade for him; that is the ending at this layer (§5), read from his side.

Boundary. Two cases are read at this layer. One is still remembering her after parting. The two have parted and the shared dealings have terminated; he still remembers her. Relation Theory says that what continues after parting can be the conditions and effects that still exist.41 Still remembering is an effect that still exists; it does not by itself prove that the shared dealings have not ended, and whether his love ends is still read from his side. The other is two people who have never met and are in touch only by letter. Her letters are constructs left by her words and deeds; they have entered his conditions of occurrence, and there is a relation.42 The anticipated her takes shape from the letters, and it too can fall through. The look of the handwriting belongs to Paper 1; the meaning in the letters, and the remembered her, are at this layer.

§5 How Love at This Layer Ends

This series divides ending into four kinds: an appearance passes; my love ends; the shared dealings terminate; commitments and effects remain unsettled. Ending at this layer takes two forms, and both fall under the second kind.

One is that the remembered her fades, or is rewritten until she no longer pulls at him: thinking of her is like thinking of a street he has not walked down in years. The other is that what he anticipates falls through again and again, and he stops waiting: at nine on Sunday he no longer looks at his watch. Both are read from his side and need not keep pace with the termination of the dealings: the dealings can still be going on when he has already stopped waiting; the dealings can be broken off while he is still waiting.

Still remembering after parting is an effect that still exists;43 it does not by itself prove that the dealings have not ended, nor does it by itself prove that his love has not ended.

At the ending, this layer too has its direction of colonization: holding, on the strength of the remembered her, that the present her is "wrong." "You changed a long time ago." If this sentence is used to require her to understand herself by the remembered look, and to fix for her what the outcome of this stretch amounts to, it slides in the direction of colonization; whether it took hold is seen in how she afterward understands this stretch of her own and how she acts. If it only puts forward a fallible observation and asks her to respond, no direction is judged on the strength of this sentence. How she understands her own change is hers; how he revises his own judgment is his. What she has actually changed can be discussed separately.

§6 Interfacing with Tradition

Several have asked about this layer, one after another. For each, this paper writes faithfully what he sees and why he looks, where he presses on this paper, where they meet and where they part.44

Crystallization. In Book One, Chapter 2 of On Love, Stendhal writes of how love comes into being in the mind. He takes as his figure a branch from the salt mines of Salzburg: a branch that has lost its leaves in winter is thrown deep into an abandoned shaft; taken out two or three months later, it is covered in glittering crystals, and "one can no longer recognize the original branch."45 He says: "What I call crystallization is the operation of the mind which, from everything that presents itself, draws the discovery that the beloved object has new perfections"; "one has only to think of a perfection to see it in the beloved."46 Once doubt has arisen there is a second crystallization: the lover goes back and forth through a night, "She loves me," "But does she love me?", and each time adds new crystals to it.47 He calls this "a book of the science of ideas," meant to describe minutely the feelings that together make up the passion of love:48 he looks in order to write down, step by step, how love proceeds in the mind. What he sees: in love, the one loved grows, with the one who thinks of her, perfections she does not have; this is done in the head of the one who thinks, and can be seen if one will "let a lover's head work for twenty-four hours."49 He presses on this paper: if love itself is crystallization, then what is loved was never her, but the branch covered in crystals; in saying that crystallization in itself carries no direction, does L13 play down the crux of love? They meet in: the remembered reaches forward, into ways she is not, and the reaching is done with the one who thinks (L13). They part at two points. First, he takes crystallization as a step in love's coming into being, even as the reason love can last; this paper does not use crystallization to account for where love comes from, and reads only whether, in a relation, it is headed toward her: with me, it carries no direction; only when it is used to measure her is direction read. Second, he means to set down the steps love generally goes through; this series reads by layers, not by stages, and a love need not pass through his steps in order.

Waiting. In A Lover's Discourse, Barthes sets down, one by one, certain "figures" that recur when the lover speaks; "Waiting" is one of them. Only the gist is given:50 he writes of the lover waiting for a phone call, waiting for a meeting, turning things over and over while he waits, every kind of conjecture rising at once; he places the lover's identity in waiting: when he asks himself whether he is in love, the answer is yes, because he is waiting; the lover is the one who waits, and the other never waits. He looks in order to write the lover's own speech, not to define love, and so he works in fragments, one after another. What he sees: in longing, waiting is not empty but full; what is anticipated reaches forward time after time, falls through time after time and comes back time after time. He presses on this paper: if the lover's identity is to be the one who waits, and the other never waits, then this layer is asymmetric by nature; the one who loves is occupied in waiting, and what occupies him is precisely his own love. They meet in: waiting is the anticipated her; it can fall through, and when it falls through, what moves in return is the remembered her (L11); Jiang Yuan looking at his watch from half past eight on Sunday is this very point. They part at: what he writes is the lover's language, and its site is a person speaking; what this paper reads is the direction of remembering and anticipating in a relation. That waiting occupies the one who waits is on his own side; this paper does not read it as an effect of hers on him, unless her words and deeds are actually headed there. Whether the other waits is not something this layer can judge either; that is the other's own matter.

§7 Drawing Together and Downstream

This paper has laid down eight propositions. Taken together, they say: longing is her still counting with me when she is not before me; the remembered her is not the original, and each time I think of her, I take her up again. The anticipated her can fall through; when it does, the remembered is revised, or by the remembered she is discerned to be "wrong"; rewriting is with me and carries no direction, and only when it reaches her by way of words and deeds is direction read. Crystallization in itself carries no direction; only when crystallization or the first meeting is used to measure her is the direction of colonization read. As for the share that is resemblance, if it stops at resemblance, this love is not for her; changing her after the remembered one is how the direction of colonization shows itself clearly at this layer. Expectation does not turn into a duty, and response is material, not a settling of the account; only when the condition attached to an expectation, or the criterion of a test, fixes for her by way of words and deeds what her response means is the direction of colonization toward her read; the response a test obtains cannot certify the love in her heart. When the one loved is gone, the remembered her is still there; death is not the same as the ending of love.

This paper does not prove that thinking of her much means loving her deeply, nor that love is true only if it remembers accurately.

The question left open. Jiang Yuan meets Ye Qing at the station, and his first thought is: she is no longer the one I remember. Who is saying this? The one who remembered her, waited for her and rewrote her through the year, and the one looking at her now at the station: are they the same? If he sees that it was he who misremembered, the error of that judgment can fold back onto himself; if he uses "She's the one who changed" to refuse to look at his own judgment again, and wants her as well to admit that it was she who made him see wrongly, he has pushed this judgment onto her. What she has actually changed is entered separately; from outside, a sentence does not certify whether he has folded back in his own mind. This layer cannot contain it. Ray 9 stops at the same point: who it is that remembers and anticipates.51 That is Paper 3, It Is I.

Downstream. What this paper has laid down, the papers that follow use in these ways. The remembered and the anticipated have an owner, and the seeing wrongly is mine: Paper 3. How the remembered her becomes a direction of mine that cannot be exchanged: Paper 4. Without waiting for her to do anything first: Paper 5; measuring what follows by the remembered occasion of mutuality falls back to this layer, and Paper 6 says it once more. The effects that still exist after parting, and how the four kinds of ending are spoken of together: Paper 0.

摘要

这一篇读爱情论的第二层：她不在眼前的时候。这一层读的是记着与料着，在 SAE 的层级里记作 11DD＋12DD。

相思，是她不在眼前，在我这里还算数。记着的她不是原样，每想一次，是重新拿起；料着的她有形状，可以落空。落空时回头动的是记着的她，有两样：改它，或者不改它，凭它认出眼前的她「错了」。改写先在我这里，不带方向；经言行实际给她此后的理解与行动留出空间，才朝涵育这个方向。不改而说出口，替她定了她是谁，滑向殖民的方向。结晶本身也不带方向；拿结晶、拿初见去量此后在眼前的她，经言行替她定她是谁，才读殖民的方向。爱的若是她像的那个人，谁像都可以，这份爱不在她身上；照着记着的那一个去改她，是这一层殖民方向显露得清楚的样子。期待是我据来往安排出来、指向她此后作为的，不落成她的义务，她的回应是材料，不是结算；给期待挂上「你若爱我，就会……」的条件，滑向殖民的方向。考验爱情，是事先布置的索取，得来的回应可以成为材料，不能认证她心里的爱。所爱的人不在了，记着的她还在；死亡不等于爱情结束。

这一层的爱，结束在记着的她淡了，或者被改写到不再牵动他；也可以是料着的一再落空，他不再等。这一篇与司汤达、巴特对接。这一篇不证明想得多就爱得深，也不证明记得准才是真爱。

江远和叶青认识三年。去年春天，她去南方一座城市做一个工程，要待一年多。南方的工程是她自己认下要做的事；江远不愿她走，也明白她为什么非去不可；他也有留在这座城市要做的事，她没有把他的留下读成不在乎。两个人约好，每个星期天晚上九点通电话。

一年里，有几件事。星期天晚上八点半，他就开始看表；九点过了五分钟，电话还没来，他已经在想她是不是忘了，是不是出了什么事。他想起她的时候，想起的常常是三年前在朋友家第一次见她，她站在窗边剥一个橘子，那个样子比后来任何一次都清楚。有一阵子他想，她要是还在乎，就会主动多打一个电话；那个星期他故意没提，看她会不会主动多打一个。年底她回来了，头发剪短了，说话比从前快，笑的时候不再低头。他在车站看着她走过来，第一个念头是：她不是我记得的那个人了。

这一年里，她大部分时间不在他眼前。那这一年，他的爱在哪里？

第一节 记着的她

L10 相思，是她不在眼前，在我这里还算数；记着的她不是原样，每想一次，是重新拿起

射线九把这一层独有的一处写成：不在眼前，也没有摆在眼前的，在一个活物里还算数。5 相思就落在这一处。叶青在南方，江远在这边；她不在他眼前，也没有一张照片摆在他面前，她照样在他这里起作用：他会在一家水果店门口站一下，因为摊上有橘子。

《诗经·王风·采葛》写的是同一处：「彼采葛兮，一日不见，如三月兮」；6 一日不见的人，在他这里比一天长。毛诗的小序把这一首读作「惧谗」；这里只借字面，读不见而仍被牵动的那一面。

记着的她不是原样。射线九说：「记着的不是原样，想起，是重新拿起」。7 江远每想一次叶青剥橘子，那个样子都有一点不同：有一回窗外是下午，有一回是傍晚；她剥橘子的手，有一回是左手。他说不清原来是哪一回，可那还是她。上游另说，想起是又一次再体验，可能伴着发展性的改写。8 每想一次，是又拿起一次，拿起的时候已经动了。

李商隐写：「此情可待成追忆，只是当时已惘然」。9 当时已经惘然，后来追忆起来的，是又拿起来的那一回。

所以相思不是一个存着不动的她。它是一回一回的拿起，每一回都在我这里。

L11 料着的她可以落空。落空时回头动的是记着的她，有两样：改它，或者不改它，凭它认出眼前的她「错了」。改写先在我这里；经言行实际给她此后的理解与行动留出空间时，才朝涵育这个方向。不改而说出口，替她定了她是谁，滑向殖民的方向，成没成看她此后的理解与行动

记着的往前伸，成了料着的。星期天九点，他料着她的电话会来；年底在车站，他料着走过来的是他记得的那个人。料着的有形状，到时候可以落空。10

射线九写了落空以后的两样：「『回头动』有两样：改它；或者不改它，凭它认出眼前错了」。11 照到爱里，就是这两样。

一样是改它。江远看着剪了短发、说话快了的叶青，过了几天，他记着的她换了：从前那个剥橘子低着头的人还在，只是成了「从前的她」；现在的她，是这个说话快的人。这一改先在他这里。它不带向她的方向，因为还没有进入她的发生条件。关系论读方向，在一次作用进入对方的发生条件以后读，有两个方向：涵育，朝腾出空间去；殖民，朝灌入并占住去。12

改写经由言行到了她那里，才读方向。他若对她说：「你这一年变了不少，我还在认识你。」这一句告诉她他看见了变化，没有替她定变化算什么，她怎样理解自己的这一年，留在她那里。若这一次交谈实际让她有地方说明自己、修订两人的理解，才朝腾出空间去，朝涵育这个方向；仅仅没有替她定，还不足以独自认证涵育。13 成没成，看她此后怎样理解，怎样做。

另一样是不改它，凭它认出眼前的她错了。他不改，心里说「她不该是这样」，这仍在他这里。说出口，「你不是这样的人」，就替她定了她是谁：一个记着的样子，成了她此后的尺子。这一向朝灌入并占住去，滑向殖民的方向。成没成，看她此后怎样理解，怎样做；她可以一笑了之，也可以从此在他面前放慢说话。从外面只见线索。

这里说的是方向，不是标签：不说江远是什么样的人，只读这一次作用朝哪里去。

料着的要能落空，才是料着的。射线九把「不会落空的料」列为冒充：一支签上写「守得云开见月明」，以后来什么都对得上，「可当初心里并没有一个对不上就会落空的样子」。14 相思里也有这一种。她打来了，是爱他；没打来，是怕打扰他，也是爱他；她冷淡，是在考验他，还是爱他。这样的料，照射线九的话，「什么来了都对得上，就没有什么先到过」：15 它不在等她，它在等一个早已定好的读法。这一种若只在他心里，仍在他这里；一旦说出来，用来读她的每一个回应，就到了关系论说的那一处：限定条件成了位置，「使对方的任何回应都不能作为反驳被登记」。16

L12 相思里改写记着的她，改写在我这里，不是殖民；它经由言行落到她身上，才读方向

L11 已经说到这一条的一半，这里单独立出来，是因为它常被读反。相思里，一个人会把记着的她改来改去：添上她没有的温柔，删掉她有过的冷淡，把一次争吵记成一次玩笑。这是改写。

改写在我这里，不是殖民。关系论读方向，读的是一次作用进入对方的发生条件以后朝哪里去；17 我在自己这里怎样记着她，没有进入她的发生条件，就没有朝她去的方向。关系论读单恋时分开过两样：「所恋者实际的言行或留存怎样进入了这一端，与只有想象出来的形象这两件事；后一件里，不冒称得到了那一个人的回应」。18 记着的她改成了什么样，是后一件；不冒称那就是她。

改写在我这里，也不是什么都没有发生。它进入的是我自己此后的发生条件：我此后怎样等她，怎样看她，都从改写过的那一个出发。我自己这一侧的事，关系论读一个人与他自己的关系时读过，这一篇不展开。19

它经由言行落到她身上，才读方向。改写过的那一个，一旦被说出来，被用来要求她，就走到 L11 的第二样或下面的 L13。

第二节 结晶与像谁

L13 结晶本身不带方向：记着的往前伸，常伸成她没有的样子。拿结晶、拿初见去量此后在眼前的她，经言行替她定她是谁，才读殖民方向

司汤达给这件事起过一个名字：结晶（第六节）。一根冬天落了叶子的树枝，扔进废弃的盐矿，两三个月后拿出来，挂满了亮晶晶的结晶，认不出原来的那根树枝了。相思里，记着的她也会这样：一年里，江远想起她，常常想到她没有的样子，她比实际耐心，比实际爱笑。

结晶本身不带方向。它在我这里，是记着的往前伸，伸成了她没有的样子；这一伸，还没有进入她的发生条件。普鲁斯特写斯万：奥黛特有一次低头看一幅版画，她的样子让斯万想起西斯廷礼拜堂壁画里叶忒罗的女儿西坡拉；从此，「壁画的一个片段出现在她的脸上、身上，他此后总想在那里重新找到它，不论是在奥黛特身边，还是只是想着她」。20 他想着她的时候，她身上挂上了一幅画。这一挂，在斯万这里。

结晶是否记错，不在这一层判。记着的本来不是原样（L10）；记错也是记着。21 这一层不判记得准不准：记得准，是对不对的事。

拿结晶去量此后在眼前的她，才读方向。年底叶青回来，江远若说「你以前多耐心」，而那份耐心是他一年里给她挂上去的，这一句就是拿结晶量她，经言行替她定她是谁，滑向殖民的方向。

拿初见去量她，是同一个形状。纳兰性德写：「人生若只如初见」。22 初见的那一个，在记着里往往比后来任何一次都清楚，像江远记着的那个剥橘子的人。他在心里拿初见比后来的她，仍在他这里；说出口，「你要是还像我第一次见你那样就好了」，就是拿初见当尺子。第一篇读一见时说过，拿那一刻去量此后，落到这一篇。23

张爱玲的《红玫瑰与白玫瑰》里有同一件事的两面，只述大意：没娶的那一个，在记着里一直好；娶了的那一个，天天在眼前，变得平常。不在眼前的结晶，在眼前的落空；两样都在他这里，都还没有朝谁去。

L14 像谁：爱的若是她像的那个人，谁像都可以，她就是可替换的，这份爱不在她身上。照着记着的那一个去改她，是这一层殖民方向显露得清楚的样子

第一篇把一见如故分成三样：面善的质地留在第一篇；像谁，真有一个记着的人或一类人，要靠记忆，归这一篇；前世、命定，是事后给那一下的读法，归第三篇。24

像谁，是记着的另一个人伸到了眼前的她身上。江远头一回见叶青，她剥橘子的样子，像他大学时喜欢过的一个人。像，可以只是一个起头；后来他看见的若是叶青，这一下就过去了。可若他爱的一直是她像的那一个，那么谁像都可以：叶青就是可替换的，这份爱不在她身上。照第一篇立下的第一条，爱情是这一个，不可替换；25 像谁的那一份，若停在像上，就还不是对这一个的不得不。

照着记着的那一个去改她，是这一层殖民方向显露得清楚的样子。希区柯克的《迷魂记》，只述大意，只借改她这一结构：从斯科蒂当时的认识看，他失去了他爱的女人，后来遇见一个长得像她的人，于是一样一样改她，衣服，发色，发型，走路的样子，直到她像那一个。他改的是眼前这个人的外表，要的是记着的那一个；她的言行、她自己要什么，都被他拿那个样子去要求。这一向朝灌入并占住去，而且一次一次落到她身上；成没成，看她此后怎样理解自己，怎样做。26

斯万那一处也可以照这一条读：他在奥黛特身上找的是壁画。找，在他这里；照着壁画去要她，就到了这一条。

第三节 期待与考验

L15 期待是我据来往安排出来、指向她此后作为的，不落成她的义务；她的回应是材料，不是结算。「你若爱我，就会……」给期待挂上限定条件，替她定一件事对她意味着什么，滑向殖民的方向

料着的落到她的作为上，就是期待。关系论给期待的起码定义是：「期待，是一方据来往在自己这里安排出来、指向对方此后作为的东西；它是一种留下的构，不落成义务，也不认证对方认领了什么」。27 江远料着星期天九点的电话，有两样来路：一样是两人约好的，各自把一份期待放到了对方那里，这是答应，下面另记；28 一样是一年的来往里他自己安排出来的。料着的这一份在他这里，不落成叶青的义务。

她的回应是材料，不是结算：对方的回应进入我的发生条件，成为下一次期待的材料；单凭回应发生这件事，不结清什么，也不认证对方认领了什么。29 单凭电话九点来了，不认证她认领了什么；九点零五分没来，单凭他的期待，也不使她欠下什么。两人已经约好的那一份，是答应，另记：它约束人，靠她自己的站与已经留下的作用，不是她欠他一笔；这一次是未做到、受阻还是两人改了约，他这一侧只能连同痕迹可错地登记，不能由期待这一笔抹掉，也不由期待这一笔判定。30 回应仍成为他下一次等的材料。

期待从来往里长出来。关系论说，期待从反复的来往里长出：回应与不回应、按时与不按时，都成为材料。31 两人约好的每周通话是一笔；后来沉积出来的急切、担心，以及希望她主动多打一通，是另一笔。第一个月，九点零五分没来，他还只是等；到第十个月，他已经坐不住了。后一份没有谁答应过，它是一个一个星期天沉积出来的。

它也不因为长得久，就成了她的义务。关系论写了期待离开那一格的两条出口：被期待的人在自己的法里认下，或成为双方明确安排、并由关系权力维持的一项角色要求。32 叶青若在自己那里认下「他想多通一次，我就多打」，那是她认下的，是她的；他的期待替不了她认。

期待可以说出来。说「这个星期我很想你再多打一次」，是一种追问。给它挂上限定条件，就不一样了：「你若爱我，就会每天打电话来。」这一句把打不打电话挂到她爱不爱上，替她定了一件事对她意味着什么。关系论说：「给期待挂上限定条件，替对方定下一件事对他意味着什么，如把它挂到对方是谁、在乎什么上作判准，就滑向殖民的方向，成没成看对方此后的理解与行动」。33 挂在爱上，是这一层常见的样子。

回还有没有义务，是关系一层的事，关系论已与莫斯、《礼记》、墨子对过；这里回指。34

L16 考验爱情，是事先布置的索取；这样得来的回应可以成为材料，不能认证她心里的爱

那个星期，江远故意没提想多通一次电话，看叶青会不会主动多打一次。她多打了。他放了心：她在乎。

这是考验。它不是等，是事先布置好了一个局，让她的回应落进去，用来证明一件事。布置的那一刻，期待已经挂上了条件：她主动多打，就是爱；不多打，就是不爱（L15）。所以考验是事先布置的索取。

得来的回应可以成为材料：她多打了这个电话，这件事进入他的发生条件，他此后的等会不一样。可它不能认证她心里的爱。单凭回应发生这件事，不结清什么，也不认证对方认领了什么；35 爱没爱，是爱的人自己的事，从外面只见线索。36 她多打这个电话，可以是因为想他，也可以是因为担心他出了事，也可以只是那天有空；回应本身分不出来。

布置这个局的言行另记。若这个星期的不提，在具体来往里已有可指认的作用落到她那里，这一笔照记；只知道他心里故意不提，还不能独自认证这一笔。她不知道判准，不等于这些言行没有作用。局里的判准，她多打就是爱，不多打就是不爱，只在他这里；没有以言行表出来，就还没有进入她的发生条件。判准若已经经由安排或施压落到她那里，也不必等他说破才读方向。他若把结果说出来，「你没多打，可见你不在乎」，判准就落到了她身上：替她定了一个电话对她意味着什么，滑向殖民的方向；成没成，看她此后怎样理解，怎样做。37

这一条说的是考验爱情：判准在布置的人那里，要试的是对方爱不爱。核验眼前这个人是谁，是另一问：凭一个记号得来的回应，核对的是身份，不是替一份爱作证；这一问怎样用，照它的用途另读。两问可以落在同一场里，要分开读。38

第四节 不在了

L17 所爱的人不在了，记着的她还在；死亡不等于爱情结束

叶青不在他眼前，是她在南方。还有一种不在眼前，是人不在了。

爱情是从主体这一侧看的；39 所以死亡不等于爱情结束。一个人的爱人去世多年，他还记着她，想起她，每想一次，是重新拿起（L10）；那份爱在他这一侧，不因为她那一端不在了就结束。

这与关系论读与死者的关系同形：「与死者，如读柏拉图，是一段关系。死者留下的言行与作品的实际作用，与今天的想象和记忆重构分开读；不要求死者回应」。40 记着的她里，有她生前留下的言行仍在起的作用，也有他此后一次一次重新拿起时的改写；两样分开读。改写的那一样，不冒称是她的回应（L12）。人死之后有没有那边，那边如何，关系论不判，这个系列也不判。

这一条只说结束不由死亡判定，不说爱必然继续。记着的她也可能在他那里慢慢淡了，那是这一层的结束（第五节），从他这一侧读。

边界。 两种情形照这一层读。一种是分开以后仍记着她。两个人分开了，共同的来往终止了，他还记着她。关系论说，分开以后继续的，可以是仍存的条件与作用。41 仍记着，是仍存的作用；它不独自证明共同的来往还没有结束，他的爱结不结束，仍从他这一侧读。一种是从没见过面、只靠通信的两个人。她的信是她的言行留下的构，进入了他的发生条件，关系就有了。42 他料着的她，从信里长出形状，也可以落空。字迹的样子归第一篇；信里的意思与记着的她，在这一层。

第五节 这一层的爱怎样结束

这个系列把结束分成四样：一次显现过去；我的爱结束；共同的来往终止；承诺与作用仍未结。这一层的结束有两种样子，落在第二样上。

一种是记着的她淡了，或者被改写到不再牵动他：想起来，像想起一条多年没走的街。一种是料着的一再落空，他不再等：星期天九点，他不再看表。两种都从他这一侧读，不必同步于来往的终止：来往可以还在，他已经不等了；来往断了，他还在等。

分开以后仍记着，是仍存的作用，43 不独自证明来往还没有结束，也不独自证明他的爱还没有结束。

结束的时候，这一层也有它的殖民方向：凭记着的她，认定现在的她「错了」。「你早就变了。」若这一句被用来要求她照记着的那个样子理解自己，并替她定这一段的结局算什么，就滑向殖民的方向；成没成，看她此后怎样理解自己这一段，怎样做。若只是提出一个可错的观察，请她回应，不凭这一句话判向。她怎样理解自己的变化，是她的；他怎样修订自己的判断，是他的。她实际改变了什么，可以另说。

第六节 与传统对接

这一层先后有几家问过。每一家照实写他认出了什么、为了什么看，在哪里向这一篇施压，在哪里相遇，在哪里分开。44

结晶。 司汤达在《论爱情》第一卷第二章写恋爱怎样在心里生出来。他用萨尔茨堡盐矿的树枝作比：一根冬天落了叶的树枝，扔进废弃的矿井深处，两三个月后拿出来，挂满了亮晶晶的结晶，「人们再也认不出原来的树枝了」。45 他说：「我所说的结晶，是心的一种作用，它从出现的一切里，发现所爱的对象有新的完美」；「只要想到一种完美，就在所爱的人身上看见它」。46 疑心生出以后，还有第二次结晶：恋人在一夜里来回，「她爱我」，「可她爱我吗」，每一次都给它添上新的结晶。47他说这是「一本观念学的书」，要细细描写组成爱这种激情的种种情感：48 他为把恋爱在心里的步骤一步一步写下来而看。他认出：恋爱里，所爱的人在想的人那里会长出她没有的完美；这件事是在想的人自己的头脑里做的，「让一个恋人的头脑工作二十四小时」，就看得见。49 他向这一篇施压：若恋爱本身就是结晶，那么爱的从来不是她，是挂满了结晶的那一根树枝；L13 说结晶本身不带方向，是不是把爱情的要害放轻了？相遇在：记着的往前伸，伸成她没有的样子，而且是在想的人自己这里伸的（L13）。分开在两处。一，他把结晶当作恋爱生成的一步，甚至是恋爱能持久的原因；这一篇不拿结晶说爱的来历，只读它在关系里朝不朝她去：在我这里，不带方向；拿它去量她，才读方向。二，他要写下恋爱一般经过的几步；这个系列按层读，不按阶段读，一段爱不必依次走过他的几步。

等待。 巴特在《恋人絮语》里，把恋人说话时反复出现的一些「形象」逐条写下，「等待」是其中一条。只述大意：50 他写恋人等一个电话、等一次约会，等的时候心里翻来覆去，种种猜测一起上来；他把恋人的身份落在等上：问自己是不是在恋爱，答案是，是，因为在等；恋人就是那个等的人，对方从不等。他为写恋人自己的话而看，不为给恋爱下定义，所以他用的是一个一个的片段。他认出：相思里，等不是空着，是满的；料着的一次一次往前伸，一次一次落空，又一次一次重来。他向这一篇施压：若恋人的身份就是等的那一个，对方从不等，那么这一层天生不对称，爱的那一方在等里被占住了，占住他的，正是他自己的爱。相遇在：等是料着的她，可以落空，落了空回头动的是记着的她（L11），江远星期天八点半就看表，就是这一处。分开在：他写的是恋人的语言，场地是一个说话的人；这一篇读的是记着、料着在关系里的方向。等占住了等的人，是在他自己这一侧；这一篇不把它读成她对他的作用，除非她的言行实际朝那里去。对方等不等，也不是这一层能判的，那是对方自己的事。

第七节 收束与下游

这一篇立了八条。合起来说的是：相思，是她不在眼前，在我这里还算数；记着的她不是原样，每想一次，是重新拿起。料着的她可以落空，落空时改记着的，或凭记着的认出她「错了」；改写在我这里，不带方向，经言行到了她那里才读方向。结晶本身不带方向，拿结晶、拿初见去量她，才读殖民的方向。像谁的那一份，若停在像上，这份爱不在她身上；照着记着的那一个改她，是这一层殖民方向显露得清楚的样子。期待不落成义务，回应是材料，不是结算；以期待的条件或考验的判准，经言行替她定她的回应意味着什么，才读朝她的殖民方向；考验得来的回应认证不了她心里的爱。所爱的人不在了，记着的她还在；死亡不等于爱情结束。

这一篇不证明想得多就爱得深，也不证明记得准才是真爱。

留下的问题。 江远在车站接到叶青，第一个念头是：她不是我记得的那个人了。说这一句的是谁？一年里记着她、等着她、改写过她的，和此刻在车站看着她的，是不是同一个？他若认出是自己记错了，那一判的错可以折回到自己这里；若拿「是她变了」来拒绝重看自己的判断，还要她认下是她使他看错，就把这一判推到了她那里。她实际改变了什么，另记；从外面，一句话不认证他心里有没有折回。这一层收不住。射线九在同一处停下：记着的、料着的，是谁。51 那是第三篇，是我。

下游。 这一篇立的，后面各篇这样用。记着的与料着的有了主人，看错是我的，在第三篇。记着的她怎样成为我换不掉的方向，在第四篇。不等她先怎样，在第五篇；拿记着的那一次双向去量此后，落回这一层，第六篇再说一次。分开以后仍存的作用，结束的四样怎样合说，在 Paper 0。
