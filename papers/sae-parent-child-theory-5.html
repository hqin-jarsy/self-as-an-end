Collisions in the Parent-Child Relation
亲子关系的碰撞

Han Qin (秦汉) · Independent Researcher · 2026
DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.24121552 · Full PDF on Zenodo · CC BY 4.0
Abstract

This paper reads the stretch in which the child's own cannot-not, once it has grown, meets the parents' law. At the 14DD turn, the child's own cannot-not crystallizes out of the remainder of 13DD and begins to meet the parents' law; rebellion is not the mechanism of 14DD's genesis, but frequently serves as its behavioral byproduct, and can function as one of its confirmation conditions in boundary encounters. In this stretch, the end of the father or the mother heading toward 15DD in one operation is read, in this matter, as: acknowledging that the child's cannot-not is his own, even if, as the parents see it, that direction will fail. Upstream writes this sentence as the condition of cultivation in this stretch; here it is read, by the series' scope, as the direction of one operation, and falling back is read as it occurs.

Colonization at 14DD takes three forms: direction substitution, superego internalization, and full-support resistance removal; the third appears in the guise of support, and a single increase or decrease in an arrangement of support is not enough to identify it. When the child's end appears as a requirement he bears in the first person and by which he goes on organizing his action, and it requires action incompatible with the parents' law in the same situation, whether that matter enters the Conflict Treatise is checked item by item against its two gates: the first asks after structure, with the object layer and the operation layer kept apart; the second asks whether the handling of this matter would put at risk the parent's own capacity to go on making first-person revisions and to go on being open to questioning, or might put the parent in the position of constituting on the child's behalf; this is not settled by the parent-child identity or by incompatibility itself. The parents do not certify whether his end is already a law of his own, nor cancel it on his behalf for want of certification; if the object later fails to obtain, this matter is exited, and he is not turned on and judged as not qualifying.

Mutual chiseling is judged by process, not by identity: where parents and child, in some matter, enter one and the same reciprocal process and actually chisel open each other's judgments, there is mutual chiseling in that matter; where all that is seen is that the two answered each other and changed an arrangement, what is entered first is that the arrangement was changed. Collision runs two ways: parent-child dealings may also lead the parents to re-examine their own law, and whether it is actually rewritten is read by the process on that occasion; where the child's refusal really altered an arrangement, it is entered that the arrangement was changed, not as either side's winning or losing. This paper interfaces with Kant and Hegel, and reads a letter of Kafka's. It does not prove that rebellion is a good thing, nor that the child's direction is bound to succeed, nor that parents ought to give way.

Introduction

After they moved to the coast, He Zhou often ran down to a wooden boat workshop by the wharf. The old master there was called Feng; he repaired fishing boats, and took on work for pleasure boats too. At first He Zhou watched from the doorway; later he helped hand over tools and sweep up shavings; later still Master Feng let him try a plane on a board. In that stretch he drew fewer boats, and often came home in the evening with splinters in his hands.

What He Chuan had planned for him was another road: get his math up, and later study engineering. He Zhou said he wanted to learn boatbuilding, the wooden kind. He Chuan said: "Wooden boats? Who uses wooden boats now? You're heading down a dead end." He Zhou did not argue; the next weekend he went to the workshop all the same, and did not go to the competition class He Chuan had signed him up for. When He Chuan found out, the two of them had a row at the dinner table. He Zhou slammed the door.

For more than a month after that, the house was quiet. He Zhou did his homework as usual, kept not going to the competition class, and on weekends mostly still went to the workshop. One day He Chuan got off work early and walked down to the wharf himself, and stood for a while at the workshop door. Master Feng recognized him, invited him in, and showed him the few boards He Zhou had planed; he said the boy had steady hands and was willing to work hard, only his math had to keep up, because building boats takes calculating. He Chuan went home and said to He Zhou: "Master Feng says you've got steady hands. He also says your math has to keep up." He Zhou said: "I know." A few days later, He Zhou said he would not go to the competition class, but he would make up his school math; he would go to the workshop on weekends, and not on weekdays. He Chuan said all right, and then: "That time I signed you up without asking you; I decided for you. I'll see to withdrawing from the class." The fee for the competition class was refunded as far as it could be.

Several things in this have to be read apart: what He Zhou's "I want to learn boatbuilding" is; whether his not going to the competition class and slamming the door are the root of this stretch; what He Chuan's saying "dead end," and signing him up for the competition class, are; whether that month and more of silence counts as conflict; and what He Chuan's walk down to the wharf, the sentence he said afterward, and the way that arrangement was changed, are.

What this paper reads. The parent-child relation this series reads has as its premise that the end of the father or the mother is headed in the 15DD direction, and it is read relation by relation.1 Paper 4 read the arrangements parents set and the child's "no." This paper reads another kind of meeting: what the child says is no longer only "no" but his own "I want"; what he wants is something of his own.

This paper reads six things. First, the 14DD turn: the child's own cannot-not grows and begins to meet the parents' law; what rebellion is here. Second, what the parents' end headed in the 15DD direction is read as in this stretch. Third, the three forms of colonization at 14DD. Fourth, when collisions in this stretch reach the threshold of the Conflict Treatise. Fifth, mutual chiseling. Sixth, that collision runs two ways.

Shared words. "Cannot-not" in this paper speaks of the child's own: an inner direction that does not depend on external permission and that, when suppressed, returns in other forms.2 The parents' end has its own cannot-not too; collision is two cannot-nots meeting in one matter.

"Collision" follows the usage at the layer of relation: it has two subclasses, conflict and mutual chiseling.3 "Conflict" is used only after the gates of the Conflict Treatise have been passed; what has not passed them is read as dealings and collision.

Method. As in the previous four papers. In this stretch, the main text gives no age for He Zhou; what is read is how He Chuan's and Lin Wan's ends act; how far He Zhou has come is read only as a condition, seen from words and deeds.

§1 The 14DD Turn

PC27 At the 14DD turn, the child's own cannot-not crystallizes out of the remainder of 13DD and begins to meet the parents' law. Rebellion is not the mechanism of 14DD's genesis, but frequently serves as its behavioral byproduct, and can function as one of its confirmation conditions in boundary encounters

The cannot-not grows

He Zhou's "I want to learn boatbuilding" at first looked like an interest, not much different from his earlier wanting to draw or to keep fish. Later it was different: when the competition class was signed up for, he did not go; after the row, he went to the workshop all the same; through more than a month of a quiet house, he mostly still went on weekends. Stopped, it did not vanish; pressed down, it came back somewhere else: the boats on the backs of his exercise books started again, and what he drew were cross-sections of boats.

Upstream calls a direction of this kind a cannot-not: an inner direction that does not depend on external permission and that, when suppressed, returns in other forms.2 It crystallizes out of the remainder of 13DD: a person has an "I," has scrutiny of many things and dissatisfaction with them, and these remainders pile up until one day they form into a direction he must take. This stretch is called the 14DD turn; by the kind of order Paper 1 spoke of, it is one example, not every child at the same place and the same time.

From the parents' end, whether He Zhou's "cannot-not" is his own is seen only in clues. Not vanishing when stopped, coming back elsewhere when pressed down, are clues; they are not certification.

Rebellion is not the root

He Zhou did not go to the competition class, and slammed the door. This is what rebellion looks like. Rebellion is not the mechanism of 14DD's genesis, but frequently serves as its behavioral byproduct, and can function as one of its confirmation conditions in boundary encounters.4 Judging whether 14DD has happened looks not at whether there is rebellion, but at whether there is a cannot-not: a direction that does not vanish in the face of obstacles.5

So neither side is read. The slammed door is not read as his direction having come to stand: a child slamming a door can be no more than being tired that day, with no direction at all. Nor is the absence of a slammed door read as having no direction: a child who never talks back can have a cannot-not, in a matter of his own, as strong as any. Slamming the door can "function as one of its confirmation conditions in boundary encounters" because it ran into a boundary: the competition class was the edge where the parents' law fell on his matter, he ran into it, and that collision let the parents see how hard that direction was.

Meeting

This stretch differs in one place from the "no" of Paper 4. That "no" was a child refusing what his parents wanted him to do; in this stretch, the child has a road of his own to take, and this road falls on the same matter as the parents' law. He Chuan has his law: by his own account, wooden boats are a dead end, and what he had planned for He Zhou was another road. Of He Zhou's end, what is seen from the parents' side is this: stopped, he went anyway; after the row, he went anyway. If that is a cannot-not of his own, two laws have met in this matter.

§2 The Parents' End

PC28 In this stretch, the end of the father or the mother heading toward 15DD in one operation is read, in this matter, as: acknowledging that the child's cannot-not is his own, even if, as the parents see it, that direction will fail. Upstream writes this as the condition of cultivation in this stretch; here it is read, by the series' scope, as the direction of one operation, and falling back is read as it occurs

Acknowledging it as his own

Upstream, writing the cultivation of this stretch, writes that it requires the cultivator to acknowledge the child's cannot-not as the child's own, even if one believes that direction will fail.6 This series reads that sentence by its scope: it does not say at which layer He Chuan as a whole stands; it reads which way his operations, one after another, go in this matter.

He Chuan saying "dead end," and signing He Zhou up for the competition class: these two put the road he had planned for He Zhou onto He Zhou's road, and in this matter were not headed toward acknowledgment. He Chuan walking down to the wharf, standing at the workshop door, going in to look at the boards He Zhou had planed, and bringing both of Master Feng's sentences home to He Zhou: this trip was his going to fetch material from He Zhou's road and bringing it home. The moment in this matter that headed toward acknowledgment falls on the sentence he said afterward: "That time I signed you up without asking you; I decided for you. I'll see to withdrawing from the class." He owned up to having decided He Zhou's road for him that time, and changed the arrangement. Acknowledging is not changing his view: he still told He Zhou his math had to keep up; what is acknowledged is that the direction is He Zhou's.

Even if it will fail

"Even if, as the parents see it, that direction will fail": this half-sentence bears the most weight. Where parents think a road will succeed, acknowledging that road as the child's does not run against their own judgment; where they think it will fail, and still acknowledge it as the child's, the two have to be held at once. After that trip, He Chuan still told He Zhou his math had to keep up: this sentence was not put away; it was set out, for He Zhou to weigh.

Falling back is read as it occurs

In this stretch, signing him up for the competition class without asking him, and saying he was "heading down a dead end": by what He Chuan himself later owned up to, that time was deciding He Zhou's road for him. By Paper 1, falling back is read as it occurs and does not overturn the direction; what is read is whether, after falling back, one can turn back. He Chuan said "That time I signed you up without asking you; I decided for you," and went and withdrew from the class: owning up to that effect, and making some change in the arrangements afterward, is a turning back. Looking at the boards, hearing Master Feng, and getting the money back each show only that material was obtained and an arrangement changed; what bears the "owning up" is the sentence he said. (The weekend He Chuan had Grandma keep He Zhou at home, Paper 6 reads.)

§3 Three Forms

PC29 Colonization at 14DD takes three forms: direction substitution, superego internalization, and full-support resistance removal; the third appears in the guise of support

Three

Upstream lists three forms of colonization at 14DD: direction substitution, superego internalization, and full-support resistance removal.7 Falling in this stretch, each has a look of its own.

Direction substitution. Putting the direction the parents decided for the child in the place of the child's own direction. He Chuan signing He Zhou up for the competition class without asking him has this shape: using the engineering road to replace the boatbuilding road. This time, He Zhou did not go; the direction was not replaced.

Superego internalization. Making the parents' requirement into an "ought" inside the child, so that he takes it to be his own. If a father said every day, "a good child doesn't make his parents worry," until the child himself, opening his mouth, said, "If I go and learn boatbuilding, I'll be making Mom and Dad worry, I can't do that": that "can't" looks like the child's, and its source is the parents. This series only registers this shape; from outside, one cannot tell where a child's "can't" comes from; that is a matter at his own end.

Full-support resistance removal. The third appears in the guise of support: an arrangement of support keeps removing resistance and negative feedback from different directions, so that the conditions under which a direction is examined are removed too. After He Zhou started going to the workshop, Lin Wan bought him a full set of tools, asked someone to put in a word with Master Feng, and said that if the boy ruined any material, she would pay for it. Master Feng later said to Lin Wan: "If he ruins some wood, let him pay for a piece himself; then he'll know what a cut weighs." Afterward Lin Wan did not pay for him again.

How far the third is read

Full-support resistance removal looks like supporting the child's direction, not replacing it. Its problem lies in resistance: whether a direction is his cannot-not shows only in the face of obstacles; if an arrangement of support keeps removing resistance and negative feedback from different directions, that direction has no chance to be examined. Those whose 14DD is not yet established are most vulnerable to being absorbed by another's strong 14DD.8 This kind of absorption can come in the guise of love.

The example of Lin Wan is read only as far as how an arrangement of support changed. Buying tools, having said that the cost of ruined material could be hers, and later no longer paying in his place: these words and deeds show that an arrangement of support changed; they are not yet enough to identify that full-support resistance removal took place earlier, still less do they show that a child must first run into a loss he cannot afford before he has a direction of his own. The resistance already written in this paper, He Chuan's opposition, the competition class, that row, does not vanish because Lin Wan paid for a piece of wood. Here neither colonization nor turning back is named on the strength of buying tools, paying for material, or ceasing to pay; ceasing to pay is a matter at her end, and does not certify that what came before was a falling back.

§4 The Threshold of the Conflict Treatise

PC30 When the child's end appears as a requirement he bears in the first person and by which he goes on organizing his action, and it requires action incompatible with the parents' law in the same situation: whether that matter enters the Conflict Treatise is checked item by item against its two gates: the first asks after structure, with the object layer and the operation layer kept apart; the second asks whether the handling of this matter would put at risk the parent's own capacity to go on making first-person revisions and to go on being open to questioning, or might put the parent in the position of constituting on the child's behalf; this is not settled by the parent-child identity or by incompatibility itself. The parents do not certify whether his end is already a law of his own, nor cancel it on his behalf for want of certification; if the object later fails to obtain, this matter is exited, and he is not turned on and judged as not qualifying. What has not entered is read as dealings and collision

The two gates of the Conflict Treatise

The Conflict Treatise handles one cell; a collision enters that cell through two gates, and both must be passed.9 The first asks after the structure of the collision: two already-formed laws of one's own require incompatible action in the same situation, and three things hold at once: they cannot certify each other's arrival, neither may legislate for the other, and no single party's withdrawal can complete the settlement.10 Within this gate, the object layer and the operation layer are kept apart: the object layer speaks of what is ontologically two independently formed laws; at the operation layer, I can confirm only my own end, and the other end enters as a requirement that the other bears in the first person and by which the other goes on organizing their action.11

The other gate is anchored on my side: would the way this matter is handled put at risk my capacity to go on making first-person revisions and to go on being open to questioning. The other's side enters this gate only in one form: might handling it this way put me in the position of constituting on the other's behalf.12 Falling between parents and child, the "I" here is the end of the father or the mother; the child's side comes in only by the latter question.

Between parents and child

He Zhou's end appears as a requirement he bears in the first person and by which he goes on organizing his action: he went to the workshop on weekends, went anyway when stopped, went anyway after the row. At the operation layer, the other end appears as a requirement of this kind; at this layer the Conflict Treatise is used as it stands.11 He Chuan's law requires incompatible action in the same matter: the competition class and the workshop, on the same weekend.

Whether this matter enters the Conflict Treatise still requires checking the requirement He Chuan's end avows, the incompatibility of action in this matter, which responsibilities a unilateral exit would still leave unsettled, and whether the handling touches his own position for going on revising and being questioned. The parent-child identity cannot stand in for these checks, and nor can the fact that "the requirements are incompatible."

Of the three things, the first: He Chuan cannot use his own judgment to certify whether He Zhou's end has arrived; see the next subsection. The second: He Chuan cannot legislate for He Zhou either: his law can say how he himself acts; it cannot be written as what He Zhou should do.13 The "cannot" here speaks of qualification, not of whether it can be stopped: signing He Zhou up for the competition class is putting his own plan onto He Zhou's road (§3: direction substitution); it is not that it fails to count as deciding his road for him only because He Zhou did not go. The third: if He Chuan walks away unilaterally, this matter is not settled; what remains unsettled after he walks away is checked item by item for this matter, and is not replaced by the single sentence "he is the father" (Paper 2: departure does not by itself settle that relation).

At the second gate, He Chuan's signing He Zhou up for the competition class is exactly what the latter question has to ask: might handling it this way put him in the position of constituting on He Zhou's behalf?

Neither certifying nor cancelling

Here, the parents' end leaves two things undone. One is certifying: He Chuan does not determine for He Zhou whether that "I want to learn boatbuilding" is already a law of his own. The other is cancelling on his behalf for want of certification: He Chuan cannot say "that's just a passing fancy, it doesn't count as yours," and treat it as nothing. Of the child's end, from the parents' end only clues are seen (Paper 1); not seeing proof is not the same as that end being empty.

Should He Zhou later say that what he really wanted was to draw boats, not to build them: on the strength of this sentence alone, one cannot yet tell whether the original judgment of entry was wrong, or whether he changed direction afterward; the earlier requirement and dealings are not to be written backward. If it is later confirmed that the object type of this matter never obtained, the series exits this matter, and does not turn on him and judge him as not qualifying.14 Dealings that have already happened are entered on the books as they are.

What has not entered

Not every collision reaches this cell. The two arrangements of Paper 4, the coat and the screens, have not, on the existing material, completed the checks for entry above, and are still read here as dealings and collision. Nor is a matter judged to be outside the gate because it is small, nor to have entered because it bears on the future: the size of a collision does not fix the scope.15 What reaches the threshold goes the Conflict Treatise's way; this series does not set that way up again.

§5 Mutual Chiseling

PC31 Mutual chiseling is judged by process, not by identity: where parents and child, in some matter, enter one and the same reciprocal process and actually chisel open each other's judgments, there is mutual chiseling in that matter; it is not established by the parent-child identity or by the name of a stage

Entering the same process

The precondition of mutual chiseling is that both enter the same process; having entered it, they must also, within that process, actually chisel open each other's judgments before there is mutual chiseling.16 Identity is not mutual chiseling; identity is a label that classifies relations. So mutual chiseling is judged by process, not by identity.

He Chuan brought Master Feng's words home; He Zhou proposed making up his school math and going to the workshop only on weekends; He Chuan agreed to drop the competition class. Here one can see that the two answered each other and changed an arrangement. He Zhou's "I know" cannot be read as his having earlier thought math did not matter; He Chuan's withdrawing from the competition class does not by itself prove that he changed his judgment about boatbuilding. On this occasion, what is entered first is that the arrangement was changed. Whether judgments were chiseled open is, from outside, seen only in clues; read conditionally: if the two people's judgments in this matter were each actually chiseled open at one point by the other, there is mutual chiseling in this matter.

Not by identity, not by stage

The layer of relation has written that parents and an adult child can, in some particular matter, enter the same reciprocal process, and in that matter there may be mutual chiseling.17 This paper uses the same process criterion for particular matters between parents and child: it is not said that mutual chiseling is impossible here because He Zhou is not yet an adult; nor is it said that there must be some here because the two are father and son.

Nor is it established by the name of a stage. To say "this is the 14DD turn" is not to say that this stretch has mutual chiseling; what is read is what actually happened in that matter: whether they entered the same process, and whether judgments were actually chiseled open. During that month and more when the house was quiet, nothing in their words and deeds shows the two entering the same process; mutual chiseling is not entered here.

§6 Collision Runs Two Ways

PC32 Collision runs two ways: parent-child dealings may also lead the parents to re-examine their own law; whether it is actually rewritten is read by the process on that occasion, and falling back is read as it occurs. Where the child's refusal really altered an arrangement, it is entered that the arrangement was changed, not as either side's winning or losing

Two ways

Read this far, this paper has mostly read how the parents treat the child's direction. Collision runs two ways: the child's cannot-not runs into the parents' law, and the parents' law may also take force in this collision. He Chuan's original plan has its origins; this paper does not say for him what they are. After He Zhou took up boatbuilding, He Chuan walked down to the wharf, looked at the boards, and brought Master Feng's words home: this trip was one operation of his own end.

This is a possibility, not a necessity. Parent-child dealings may lead the parents to re-examine their own law; whether it is actually rewritten is read by the process on that occasion. Afterward, He Chuan still told He Zhou his math had to keep up; whether his view of boatbuilding changed, the example does not write, and nothing is filled in for him here. What changed is what he said and did: owning up to having decided He Zhou's road for him that time, and the competition class being dropped.

No winning or losing

The competition class was dropped, and He Zhou went to the workshop on weekends. This is entered as the arrangement having been changed: He Zhou's refusal really altered that arrangement.18 It is not entered as He Zhou winning, nor as He Chuan losing; He Zhou's proposing to make up his school math is not entered as his giving way and He Chuan winning back a point either. What is entered here is how the arrangement of that matter changed.

§7 Interfacing with the Tradition

Kant: freedom under constraint. In On Pedagogy, Kant asks how freedom is to be cultivated under constraint.19 The common question: parents have their own law, the child is growing a law of his own, and when the two fall on one matter, how does it go? Where they shed light on each other: Kant neither avoids constraint nor sets freedom down, and what this paper reads is exactly two laws meeting in one matter. Where they part: Kant reads constraint as an educational means toward freedom; this series gives constraint no educational use, reads only which way effects go, one after another, and reads out the shape of colonization in three places: direction substitution, superego internalization and full-support resistance removal.

Hegel: discipline and self-will. In the main text of §174 of the Philosophy of Right, Hegel says that the parents' right over the arbitrary will (Willkür) of the children has as its end to discipline and to educate them; the Addition (Zusatz) to §174 says that the purpose of discipline is to break the child's self-will (Eigenwille); §175, on education, says that it is to raise the child out of natural immediacy into an independent and free personality.20 In setting them side by side, the antecedents on each side are checked first: the "self-will" Hegel speaks of and the "cannot-not of one's own" this series reads have different antecedents; the former is said against the universal, the latter against external permission. Where the two join and where they part has to be argued separately; here "self-will" is not translated directly into any DD layer, nor is "breaking self-will" read as direction substitution. This paper only registers this pairing.

A historical source: Kafka's Letter to His Father. Kafka wrote a very long letter to his father, looking back on his childhood and on the two men's dealings after he was grown; the letter was never sent, he asked in his lifetime that his papers be destroyed, and it was published only after his death.21 Here only how the adult Kafka writes the structure of those dealings is read: he writes how his father's judgment stood for him like a final verdict, and how hard it was for his own direction to stand in dealings of that kind. This series does not read back the inner life of the child of that time for him, nor certify on this basis which direction his father was headed in; what is read is a structure an adult wrote down: one party's law became, at the other's end, something that could not be refuted.

§8 Drawing Together, and Downstream

This paper has established six propositions. Taken together, they say: at the 14DD turn, the child's own cannot-not crystallizes out and meets the parents' law; rebellion is not the mechanism of genesis, frequently serves as its behavioral byproduct, and can function as one of its confirmation conditions in boundary encounters; judging 14DD looks at the cannot-not, not at rebellion. The end of the father or the mother headed in the 15DD direction in this stretch is read as acknowledging that the child's cannot-not is his own, even if, as the parents see it, that direction will fail; read by scope as one operation, with falling back read as it occurs. Colonization at 14DD takes three forms: direction substitution, superego internalization and full-support resistance removal, the third appearing in the guise of support; a change in an arrangement of support is not enough to identify the third earlier, nor enough to identify a turning back. When the child's requirement and the parents' law require incompatible action in the same situation, whether it enters the Conflict Treatise is checked against the two gates: the first asks after structure, with the object layer and the operation layer kept apart; the second is anchored on the parent's side, and the child's side comes in only by whether it puts the parent in the position of constituting on his behalf; this is not settled by the parent-child identity or by incompatibility itself, and "neither may legislate for the other" speaks of qualification; the parents neither certify, nor cancel for want of certification; if the object later fails to obtain, this matter is exited, without turning and judging. Mutual chiseling is judged by process, not by identity or by the name of a stage; where only answering each other and a changed arrangement are seen, it is first entered that the arrangement was changed. Collision runs two ways; the parents' re-examining their own law is a possibility, not a necessity; where an arrangement was changed, it is entered that the arrangement was changed, with no winning or losing.

This paper does not prove that rebellion is a good thing, nor that the child's direction is bound to succeed, nor that parents ought to give way.

What the next paper reads. Did Lin Wan know He Chuan had walked down to the wharf? Did He Chuan sign up for the competition class on his own, or had the two talked it over? When He Zhou's grandmother heard later that her grandson wanted to learn boatbuilding, she said, "A child of the He family going off to be a carpenter?": in which relation does that sentence fall? This paper has read only the relation between He Chuan and He Zhou.

Downstream. Among several caregivers: pairwise accounting between two relations, passing through a third person, mutual questioning, the case where one party treats the child merely as a means, and the child brought in by remarriage, together with grandparents, Paper 6 reads. Once He Zhou's cannot-not in boatbuilding has come to stand, handing over and leading, Paper 7 reads.

摘要

这一篇读孩子自己的不得不长出来以后，与父母的法相遇的那一段。14DD 翻转时，孩子自己的不得不从 13DD 的余项中结晶出来，开始与父母的法相遇；叛逆不是 14DD 的发生机制，常是它的行为副产品，并可在边界碰撞中充当确认条件之一。这一段，父或母那一端在一次运作里朝 15DD 去，在这件事上读作：承认孩子的不得不是他自己的，即使在父母看来那个方向会失败。上游把这一句写作这一段涵育的条件；这里照射程读作一次运作的方向，回落照读。

14DD 上的殖民有三种形态：方向替代、超我内化、全支持式阻力清除；第三种以支持的样子出现，单凭一项支持安排的增减，不足以认定。孩子那一端的要求，以他第一人称承担并持续组织其行动的一项要求出现，与父母的法在同一情境中要求不相容的行动时，那一件事是否进入冲突论，照它的两道门逐项核：第一道问结构，对象层与操作层分开；第二道问这件事的处理，会不会把父或母自己继续修订、继续受问的位置置于风险中，或可能使父或母占据替孩子构成的位置；不由亲子身份或不相容本身完成认定；父母不认证他那一端是否已经是他自己的法，也不以未能认证为由替他撤销；对象后来没有成立，退出这一件，不倒判他不够格。

互凿按过程判，不按身份判：父母与孩子在某一件事上进入同一个互向的过程，并实际互相凿开对方的判断，那一件事上就有互凿；只见两人回应了彼此、改了安排，先记安排被改了。碰撞是两向的：亲子来往也可能使父母重新审视自己的法，是否实际改写，照那一次过程读；孩子的拒绝真实地改变了安排，就记安排被改了，不记作哪一方的输赢。这一篇与康德、黑格尔对接，并读卡夫卡的一封信。它不证明叛逆是好事，不证明孩子的方向一定成，也不证明父母应当让步。

搬到海边以后，何舟常往码头边上的一家木船作坊跑。作坊的老师傅姓冯，修渔船，也接游船的活。何舟先是在门口看，后来帮着递工具、扫刨花，再后来冯师傅让他拿刨子试一块板。那一阵，他画的船少了，晚上回家手上常有木刺。

何川原先替他打算的是另一条路：数学补上去，以后学工程。何舟说他想学造船，木头的那种。何川说：「木船？现在谁还用木船？你这是往死胡同里走。」何舟没有吵，第二个周末照样去了作坊，没有去何川给他报的竞赛班。何川知道了，两人在饭桌上吵了一架。何舟摔了门。

那以后有一个多月，家里不太说话。何舟作业照写，竞赛班照不去，周末多半还去作坊。何川有一天下班早，自己走去码头，在作坊门口站了一会儿。冯师傅认出他，请他进去，给他看何舟刨的那几块板，说这孩子手稳，肯吃苦，就是数学得跟上，造船要算。何川回家，跟何舟说：「冯师傅说你手稳。也说你数学得跟上。」何舟说：「我知道。」过了几天，何舟说竞赛班他不去，学校的数学他会补；周末去作坊，平时不去。何川说好，又说：「那次报名我没有问你，替你定了。退班这件事我来办。」竞赛班的钱，能退的退了。

不得不长出来

何舟说「我想学造船」，起先像一个兴趣，跟他从前想画画、想养鱼差不多。后来不一样了：竞赛班报了，他不去；吵了一架，他照样去作坊；一个多月家里不太说话，他周末多半还是去。被拦住了，它不消失；压下去，它从别处回来：作业本背面的船，又画了起来，画的是船的剖面。

上游把这样一种方向叫作不得不：一个不依赖外部许可的、压制了也会以其他形式回来的内在方向。2 它从 13DD 的余项里结晶出来：一个人有了「我」，有了对许多事的审视与不满，这些余项攒到某一天，结成一个他非走不可的方向。这一段，叫 14DD 翻转；照第一篇说的那一种次序，它是一种示例，不是每个孩子都在同一处、同一时候。

从父母这一端，何舟那一个「不得不」是不是他自己的，只见线索。被拦住了不消失，压下去从别处回来，是线索；不是认证。

叛逆，不是根

何舟没有去竞赛班，摔了门。这是叛逆的样子。叛逆不是 14DD 的发生机制，常是它的行为副产品，并可在边界碰撞中充当确认条件之一。4 判断 14DD 有没有发生，看的不是有没有叛逆，是有没有不得不：一个在阻碍面前不消失的方向。5

所以，两头都不读。不从摔门读出何舟的方向已经站住：一个孩子摔门，可以只是那一天累了，可以什么方向都没有。也不从没有摔门读出他没有方向：一个从不顶嘴的孩子，可以在自己的事上不得不得很。摔门之所以「可在边界碰撞中充当确认条件之一」，是因为碰到了边界：竞赛班是父母的法落在他那件事上的边，他撞了上去，撞的那一下，让父母看见那个方向有多硬。

相遇

这一段与第四篇那一声「不」，不同在一处。那一声「不」，是孩子拒绝父母要他怎样；这一段，是孩子自己有了一条要走的路，这条路与父母的法落在同一件事上。何川有他的法：照他自己说的，木船是一条死胡同，他替何舟打算的是另一条路。何舟那一端，从父母这一边见到的是：拦了照去，吵了照去。若那是他自己的不得不，两条法就在这件事上相遇了。

上游写这一段的涵育，写它要涵育者站在 15DD：承认孩子的不得不是他自己的，即使你觉得那个方向会失败。6 这个系列把这一句照射程读：不说何川整个人站在哪一层，读他在这件事上的一次次运作朝哪里去。

何川说「死胡同」，又给何舟报了竞赛班：这两样，是把自己替何舟打算的那条路放到何舟的路上，在这件事上没有朝承认去。何川走到码头，站在作坊门口，进去看何舟刨的板，回家把冯师傅的两句话都带给何舟：这一趟，是他去取了何舟那条路上的材料，带回了家里。在这件事上朝承认去的那一下，落在他后来说的那一句：「那次报名我没有问你，替你定了。退班这件事我来办。」他认下那一次替何舟定了路，并改了安排。承认，不等于他改了看法：他仍跟何舟说数学得跟上；承认的是那个方向是何舟的。

「即使在父母看来那个方向会失败」，这半句最吃重。父母觉得一条路会成，认那条路是孩子的，与自己的判断不相抵；觉得它会失败，还认它是孩子的，两样就要同时放着。何川那一趟以后，仍跟何舟说数学得跟上：这一句没有收起来，摆在那里，由何舟掂量。

第三节 三种形态

PC29 14DD 上的殖民有三种形态：方向替代、超我内化、全支持式阻力清除；第三种以支持的样子出现

三种

上游列过 14DD 上殖民的三种形态：方向替代、超我内化、全支持式阻力清除。7 它们落在这一段，各有一个样子。

方向替代。 把父母替孩子定的方向，放到孩子自己的方向的位置上。何川给何舟报竞赛班，没有问他，是这一形状：拿工程那条路，去替造船那条路。这一次，何舟没有去；方向没有被替下来。

超我内化。 把父母的要求，做成孩子心里的「应当」，让他自己以为那是自己的。一个父亲若天天说「好孩子不让爸妈操心」，说到孩子自己开口就是「我要是去学造船，就是让爸妈操心，我不能那样」：那个「不能」看起来是孩子的，来源是父母。这个系列只登记这一形状；从外面，分不出一个孩子的「不能」从哪里来，那是他自己那一端的事。

全支持式阻力清除。 第三种以支持的样子出现：支持的安排把不同方向上的阻力与否定性反馈持续撤去，使方向接受审视的条件也被取消。何舟去作坊以后，林晚给他买了一整套工具，托人跟冯师傅打了招呼，说孩子要是做坏了料，钱她来出。冯师傅后来跟林晚说：「料坏了，让他自己赔一块，他才知道这一刀下去有多重。」林晚后来没有再替他赔。

第三种读到哪里为止

全支持式阻力清除，看起来是在支持孩子的方向，不是在替下它。它的问题在阻力：一个方向是不是他的不得不，要在阻碍面前才显出来；支持的安排若把不同方向上的阻力与否定性反馈持续撤去，那个方向就没有机会接受审视。14DD 尚未完备的人，最容易被他者强大的 14DD 卷入。8 这一种卷入，可以以爱的样子出现。

林晚这一例，读到支持安排怎样变化为止。买工具、说过做坏材料的钱可以由她出、后来不再代赔：这些言行说明一项支持安排改了，尚不足以认定先前发生了全支持式阻力清除，更不说明孩子须先碰上赔不起的损失，才有自己的方向。同篇写过的阻力，何川的反对、竞赛班、那一场争吵，也不因林晚赔了一块料而消失。这里不据买工具、赔材料或停止代赔，分别命名殖民与回头；停止代赔是她那一端的事，不认证此前是一次回落。

第四节 冲突论的门槛

PC30 孩子那一端的要求，以他第一人称承担并持续组织其行动的一项要求出现，与父母的法在同一情境中要求不相容的行动：那一件事是否进入冲突论，照它的两道门逐项核：第一道问结构，对象层与操作层分开；第二道问这件事的处理，会不会把父或母自己继续修订、继续受问的位置置于风险中，或可能使父或母占据替孩子构成的位置；不由亲子身份或不相容本身完成认定。父母不认证他那一端是否已经是他自己的法，也不以未能认证为由替他撤销；对象后来没有成立，退出这一件，不倒判他不够格。没有进入的，照来往与碰撞读

冲突论的两道门

冲突论处理的是一格，一场碰撞落在这一格，须同时过两道门。9 第一道问这场碰撞的结构：两条已经成形的自己的法，在同一情境里要求不相容的行动，而三件事同时成立：不能互相认证到达，不能替对方立法，也不能由任何一方单方退出而完成结算。10 这一道门里，对象层与操作层分开：对象层说的是本体上确实是两条各自形成的法；操作层上，我只能确认自己这一端，另一端以对方第一人称承担并持续组织其行动的一项要求出现。11

另一道门，锚在我这一侧：这件事的处理方式，会不会把我能否继续做第一人称的修订、能否继续受问的那个位置置于风险中。他者一侧只以一种形式进这道门：这样处理，会不会使我占据替他构成的位置。12 落到亲子之间，这里的「我」是父或母那一端；孩子那一侧，只以后一问进来。

亲子之间

何舟那一端的要求，以他第一人称承担、持续组织着他的行动的一项要求出现：他周末去作坊，拦了照去，吵了照去。在操作层，另一端是以这样一项要求出现的；这一层，冲突论照用。11 何川的法在同一件事上要求不相容的行动：竞赛班与作坊，在同一个周末。

这一件是否进入冲突论，还要核何川这一端认领的要求、这一件的行动不相容、单方退出仍留下哪些未结的责任，以及处理是否触及他自己继续修订与受问的位置。亲子身份不能代替这些核对，「要求不相容」这一点也不能。

三件里，第一件：何川不能拿自己的判断去认证何舟那一端到没到，见下一小节。第二件：何川也不能替何舟立法：他的法可以说他自己怎样做，不能写成何舟该怎样。13 这里的「不能」说的是资格，不是拦不拦得住：替何舟报竞赛班，是把自己的打算放到何舟的路上（第三节：方向替代）；不因为何舟没有去，才不算替他定路。第三件：何川单方走开，这一件结不了账；走开以后哪些仍未结，照这一件逐项核，不由「他是父亲」一句代替（第二篇：离开本身不使那一段结清）。

第二道门上，何川替何舟报竞赛班那一下，要问的正是后一问：这样处理，会不会使他占据替何舟构成的位置。

不认证，也不撤销

在这里，父母那一端有两样不做。一是不认证：何川不替何舟判定，那个「想学造船」是不是已经是他自己的法。二是不以未能认证为由替他撤销：何川不能说「你那不过是一时兴起，算不得你自己的」，就把它当作没有。孩子那一端，从父母这一端只见线索（第一篇）；见不到确证，不等于那一端没有。

何舟若后来改说，他其实是想画船，不是想造船：仅凭这一句，还分不清原先的入场判断有误，还是此后改变了方向；不能倒写先前的要求与来往。若后来确认这一件的对象类型原本没有成立，这个系列退出这一件，不倒判他不够格。14 已经发生的来往，照账记。

没有进门的

不是每一次碰撞都到这一格。第四篇的外套与屏幕那两项安排，现有材料没有完成上述入场核对，这里仍照来往与碰撞读。不由事情小就判它必在门外，也不由事情关乎将来就判它必已入场：碰撞的大小不定射程。15 到了门槛的，照冲突论的那一套走；这个系列不重立那一套。

第五节 互凿

PC31 互凿按过程判，不按身份判：父母与孩子在某一件事上进入同一个互向的过程，并实际互相凿开对方的判断，那一件事上就有互凿；不凭亲子身份或阶段名称认定

进入同一个过程

互凿的前提是双方进入同一个过程；进了同一个过程，还要在那个过程里实际互相凿开对方的判断，才是互凿。16 身份不是互凿；身份是关系的分类标签。所以互凿按过程判，不按身份判。

何川把冯师傅的话带回来，何舟提出补学校的数学、只在周末去作坊，何川同意撤掉竞赛班。这里可见两人回应了彼此，并改变了安排。何舟说「我知道」，不能读作他原先认为数学不重要；何川退掉竞赛班，也不独自证明他改变了对造船的判断。这一次先记安排被改了。判断有没有被凿开，从外面只见线索；照条件式读：两人的判断若在这件事上都实际被对方凿开了一处，这一件上就有互凿。

不凭身份，不凭阶段

关系一层写过，父母与成年子女，可以在某一件事上进入同一个互向的过程，那一件事上就可能有互凿。17 这一篇把同一条过程判据，用在亲子之间的具体事项上：不因为何舟还没有成年，就说这里不可能有互凿；也不因为两人是父子，就说这里一定有。

也不凭阶段名称认定。说「这是 14DD 翻转」，不等于这一段就有互凿；读的是那一件事上实际发生了什么：有没有进同一个过程，判断有没有实际被凿开。那一个多月不太说话的时候，从言行看不出两人进了同一个过程；这里不记互凿。

第六节 碰撞两向

PC32 碰撞是两向的：亲子来往也可能使父母重新审视自己的法；是否实际改写，照那一次过程读，回落照读。孩子的拒绝真实地改变了安排，就记安排被改了，不记作哪一方的输赢

两向

这一段读到这里，读的多半是父母怎样对待孩子的方向。碰撞是两向的：孩子的不得不撞上父母的法，父母的法也可能在这一撞里受力。何川原先的打算，有它的来处；这一篇不替他说那来处是什么。何舟学造船这件事以后，何川走到码头去，看了板，把冯师傅的话带回家：这一趟，是他自己那一端的一次运作。

这是可能，不是必然。亲子来往可能使父母重新审视自己的法；是否实际改写，照那一次过程读。何川后来仍跟何舟说数学得跟上；他对造船的看法改没改，例子没有写，这里不替他补。改了的，是他说出来、做出来的那一样：认下那一次替何舟定了路，竞赛班退了。

不记输赢

竞赛班退了，何舟周末去作坊。这一件，记作安排被改了：何舟的拒绝，真实地改变了那项安排。18 不记作何舟赢了，也不记作何川输了；何舟提出补学校的数学，也不记作他让了步、何川赢回一分。这里记的，是那件事怎样安排变了。

第七节 与传统对接

康德：强制之下的自由。 康德在《论教育》里问：怎样在强制之下培养自由。19 共同的问题：父母有自己的法，孩子在长出自己的法，两样落在一件事上，怎样走。相照的一处：康德不回避强制，也不放下自由，这一篇读的正是两条法在同一件事上的相遇。分开的一处：康德把强制读作通向自由的教育手段；这个系列不给强制一个教育上的用途，只读一次次作用朝哪里去，在方向替代、超我内化、全支持式阻力清除三处，读出殖民的形状。

黑格尔：管教与私意。 黑格尔在《法哲学原理》§174 正文说，父母对孩子任性（Willkür）的那份权，目的在于管教与教育；§174 的补充（Zusatz）说，管教的用意在于打破孩子的私意（Eigenwille）；§175 谈教育，说它要把孩子从自然的直接性里提升出来，成为独立自由的人格。20 对照时，先核两边各自的前件：黑格尔说的「私意」，与这个系列读的「自己的不得不」，前件不同；前者对着普遍性说，后者对着外部许可说。两者相接与分开的地方，要另行论证，这里不把「私意」直接对译成某一个 DD 层，也不把「打破私意」读作方向替代。这一篇只登记这一对照。

史料：卡夫卡《致父亲的信》。 卡夫卡写过一封很长的信给父亲，回看自己的童年与成年以后两人的来往；这封信没有寄出，他生前嘱托把遗稿销毁，信在他死后才发表。21 这里只读成年的卡夫卡怎样写那一段来往的结构：他写父亲的判断在他那里像是最后的裁定，他自己的方向在那样的来往里怎样难以站住。这个系列不替当年的孩子回读内心，也不据此认证他父亲在哪个方向上；读的是一个成年人写下的结构：一方的法，在另一方那里成了不可反驳的东西。

第八节 收束与下游

这一篇立了六条。合起来说的是：14DD 翻转时，孩子自己的不得不结晶出来，与父母的法相遇；叛逆不是发生机制，常是它的行为副产品，可在边界碰撞中充当确认条件之一；判 14DD，看不得不，不看叛逆。这一段父或母那一端在 15DD 方向上，读作承认孩子的不得不是他自己的，即使在父母看来那个方向会失败；照射程读作一次运作，回落照读。14DD 上殖民三种形态：方向替代、超我内化、全支持式阻力清除，第三种以支持的样子出现；一项支持安排改了，不足以认定先前是第三种，也不足以认定一次回头。孩子的要求与父母的法在同一情境要求不相容的行动时，进不进冲突论，照两道门核：第一道问结构，对象层与操作层分开，第二道锚在父或母这一侧，孩子那一侧只以是否占据替他构成的位置这一问进来；不由亲子身份或不相容本身认定，「不能替对方立法」说的是资格；父母不认证，也不以未能认证为由撤销；对象后来没有成立，退出这一件，不倒判。互凿按过程判，不凭身份、不凭阶段名称；只见回应与改了安排，先记安排被改了。碰撞是两向的，父母重新审视自己的法是可能，不是必然；安排被改了就记安排被改了，不记输赢。

这一篇不证明叛逆是好事，不证明孩子的方向一定成，也不证明父母应当让步。

下一篇读的。 何川走到码头去，林晚知道吗？竞赛班是何川一个人报的，还是两人商量过的？何舟的奶奶后来听说孙子要学造船，说了一句「何家的孩子去当木匠？」，这一句又落在哪一段？这一篇只读了何川与何舟这一段。

下游。 几个照料者之间：两段之间两两记账，经过第三人，互相追问，一方把孩子只当作手段时，祖辈与再婚带来的孩子，第六篇读。何舟在造船这件事上的不得不站住以后，交出与引领，第七篇读。
