Abstract

The first six papers wrote about the market within one state; this paper crosses borders. The line does not change at borders: that basic rights may not be violated holds against everyone. What changes across borders is who guards it, and how authority connects; line-crossing practices, or their costs, can also be moved abroad. Crossways Home buys from a porcelain factory in a district in the south of the NUT, at prices about forty percent below comparable goods made in the Kingdom State; a report of the UCSE's Office of Trade confirmed that the factory's dormitories are locked at night, that wages are paid yearly and those who leave in midyear are not paid that year's wages, and that the glazing stations have no dust protection. Crossways Home said: "We buy according to the law over there; the price is set by the market." From which costs the price gap comes has not been established; but without waiting for it to be established, each party has its own share to answer for. Responsibility goes with each party's own acts: the constructs that make and enforce rules on the importing side guard the line toward those whom their own acts reach and who have no way to avoid them by exiting, and letting goods in and keeping them out are both their acts; the purchasing company, under the Theory of the Company, cannot subcontract its accountability away; the account of the foreign supplier is kept in its own name, and keeping the account is not writing an ought for it; for constructs on the exporting side, this series writes no ought. Responsibility is not exempted by borders, nor is authority derived directly from the range of influence (SC14). At the level of state and state, there is no third party that legislates for both; treaties and common procedures bind the parties within their scope of application, and carrying out and questioning go through each party's own law. Farstream Water, under the Investment Protection Agreement between the UCSE and the NUT, brought an arbitration over the price rollback and the ban on winter cut-offs that followed the Kingdom State's lowering of the threshold, claiming compensation equivalent to about ten years of Springhollow Water Company's expected profits. An arbitral decision can be final in the case, and the positions that implement it are not final; a treaty binds this state, but carries nothing across the line. "The party questioned" is written out in three layers: whether a measure breaches the commitment of investment protection is asked of the state; supply, price and the stopping of supply are asked of the supplier and its regulator; the authority, entry, effect and implementation of arbitration are asked of the positions that make, accept and implement the procedure. The binding force of a common procedure and the procedure's own openness to questioning hold at the same time. The existing terms give the two villages of Upper Hollow and Lower Hollow no position from which to bring this arbitration themselves; the village committees may submit written material, and whether to take that material into account is for the tribunal to decide; what response this participation can require, and what actual efficacy it has, the example does not say, and it cannot be taken directly as their channel. A procedure that the people whose line it is cannot initiate is not their channel; this state may not use "there is already an arbitration, and the village committees can submit material too" as a reason to replace the two villages' questioning of supply, regulation and the implementation of the award, and this channel must be kept within this state (SC15).

Introduction

In Year 25, Crossways Group's own brand, Crossways Home, switched to buying its porcelain from elsewhere. The goods came from a porcelain factory in a district in the south of the Nation Union of Telos (NUT), at prices about forty percent below comparable goods from Bluekiln Porcelain Works. Some low-income households in Apple City could afford a full set of porcelain bowls for the first time, and the newspapers reported this side too.

In Year 27, an Apple City newspaper reported on this factory. In Year 28, an investigative report of the UCSE's Office of Trade confirmed three things: the factory's workers live in dormitories on the premises, and the dormitories are locked at night; wages are paid yearly, and those who leave in midyear are not paid that year's wages; the glazing stations have no dust-protection equipment. The report did not record who holds the keys, or whether residents can go out on their own at night. The Kingdom State's Wage Payment Act covers only employers who hire in the Kingdom State; of the law of that district, the Office of Trade's report said that it "places no restriction on the yearly payment of wages."

Crossways Home said: "We buy according to the law over there; the price is set by the market." In Year 28 it adopted a supplier code and engaged an audit firm to visit once a year, with the audit fee paid by the factory. By the end of Year 28, neither the UCSE nor the Kingdom State had taken measures concerning this factory's goods.

The Prequel said that this sentence, "the price is set by the market," holds two things: one is where to buy and whose goods to buy, and there is a position that decided it; the other is the price, which is indeed the result of many purchases and sales taken together. This paper returns to it.

There is one more matter. In Year 26, seventy percent of the shares of Springhollow Water Company, in the north of the Kingdom State, were bought by Farstream Water, a water group of the NUT. In Year 27 the Kingdom State lowered the threshold of the Public Utilities Act, and the Commission ruled for a price rollback, no cut-offs in winter, and restoring supply first; in the spring of Year 28, Farstream brought an arbitration against the UCSE under the Investment Protection Agreement between the UCSE and the NUT.

The NUT, the UCSE, the Kingdom State, and the factory, companies and arbitration here are all constructed examples of this series; this paper speaks of no real factory and no real arbitration.

Across borders, the question changes shape: whether the line is still there; who guards it; where there is no one above, to whom the channel of questioning belongs. This paper asks in turn: whether the line crosses borders (§1); once across borders, in which places responsibility lands (§2); at the level of state and state, whom a common procedure binds and who can use it to ask (§3); cross-border chokepoints and a road of questioning that has grown longer (§4).

§1 The Line Does Not Change at Borders

The Theory of Government has laid down that basic rights may not be violated, which "is derived from the subject's side and holds against everyone; it is not peculiar to government" (Theory of Government Paper 3, G17). The Theory of the State carried this sentence into the relations between states: toward the people of other states, follow the unified sentence; "It does not trade them for this state's safety" (Theory of the State Paper 5, N25).

So the line does not change at borders. Whether a person stands on this side of a border or that does not change whether his body is his own, or whether leaving a place of work is his own affair.

Across borders, two other things change. One is who guards: within one state, the line is guarded by that state's constructs that make and enforce rules; abroad, that place has its own constructs, and this state's constructs cannot reach there, nor should they legislate for it. The other is how authority connects: an import rule, a procurement contract, an arbitration under a treaty between two states, each made by whom, binding whom, governing what, and landing how.

One more thing is to be recorded: line-crossing practices, or the costs of crossing the line, can be moved abroad. What may not be done within one state can be handed to a place abroad to do; costs that would have to be paid within one state can be paid by people abroad. This is not to say that whatever is bought from abroad is suspect: cross-border buying and selling, like buying and selling within one state, is left open as usual (Paper 2). It is to say that the Base Layer of people elsewhere is not traded for the price in one market (extension: Theory of the State N25 originally says that the people of other states are not traded for this state's safety; here it is extended to the price in one market; own undertaking).

This paper takes up only the layer of the market. The Theory of Inter-State Cooperation stated at its close: "'Institutions between institutions' between companies, and between states and companies, this series does not take up" (Theory of Inter-State Cooperation Paper 8, §4); Political Economy, reading the departure of the strong, also registered the institutional relations between companies and states as a remainder (Political Economy Paper 3, E20). This paper takes up the remainders registered in these two places, and takes up only function: whose rules are made by whom, binding whom, landing how, and where the account falls (own undertaking). Tariffs and trade policy, and exchange rates, this paper does not take up; sanctions and blockades fall under the Theory of the State; the system of cooperation among states falls under the Theory of Inter-State Cooperation; how arbitration and cross-border mechanisms are to be designed, it does not take up.

Judgment layer: in whose authority a cross-border transaction, an import rule or a procedure under a treaty falls is recognized by each party's own judgment layer according to its law and the procedures it has acknowledged; those acted on can ask at the place where they themselves stand.

§2 Responsibility Goes with Each Party's Own Acts

Back to Crossways Home's sentence: "We buy according to the law over there; the price is set by the market."

The cause of the price gap is unclear; what each party must answer does not wait for it. Crossways Home's goods are about forty percent cheaper than comparable goods made in the Kingdom State. From which costs this forty percent comes, raw materials, wages, equipment, scale or exchange rates, the example has not established; one cannot supply from it that the whole forty percent comes from the arrangements the Office of Trade confirmed, nor that some part of it does. But what each party must answer does not wait until the cause of the gap is established: the three things the Office of Trade has confirmed are an entrance for asking; from here, each has its own share.

First read what each of the three is. Wages paid yearly, with those who leave in midyear not paid that year's wages: what is to be looked at is not the payment cycle of once a year, but that a person who leaves in midyear cannot get the money for the months he has already worked. Political Economy's first gate wrote: "nor may money, housing or the manner of paying wages be used to place people's ability to leave, refuse or revoke a standing bond under another party's control" (Political Economy Paper 4, E21). By structure, withholding a person's earned wages and tying them to whether he leaves is the very form that gate speaks of. The dormitories locked at night: who holds the keys and whether residents can go out on their own at night, the report does not record; one cannot supply from it that people are confined and cannot leave the factory. The glazing stations without dust protection: this falls on the bodies of those who work; where it presses must be examined by the judgment layer. What this factory's practices amount to under some particular law, their legal characterization, lies at the judgment layer, and this paper does not pronounce it.

Next, read who must answer. This paper divides four places.

The constructs that make and enforce rules on the importing side. The UCSE's Office of Trade and the Kingdom State's rule-makers each have their own acts: the rules and enforcement of access, procurement and import. The Theory of the State Prequel laid down: "those whom this state's acts reach and who have no way to avoid them by exiting are, as regards those acts, in this state's interior, whether or not they hold this state's membership and whether or not they are within its borders" (the Theory of the State Prequel, §6); how the five oughts apply to them follows the unified sentence of §8 of the same paper. The Theory of Government wrote within one state: "Jurisdiction is not the interior"; "reach of influence is not reach of authority" (Theory of Government Paper 4, G25). By these sentences, the constructs on the importing side guard the line by the unified sentence toward those whom their own acts reach and who have no way to avoid them by exiting (extension: the Theory of the State originally speaks of a state's acts, and the Theory of Government of a level of government; here it is extended to the constructs that make and enforce rules on the side of the importing market; own undertaking).

Letting goods in is their act; keeping them out is also their act. A constructed example makes this clear. Suppose that in Year 29 the UCSE's Office of Trade provided that goods may not be imported unless it can be shown that they do not come from arrangements such as "wages paid yearly, with those who leave in midyear not paid that year's wages"; Crossways Home switches to buying elsewhere, and that factory loses ten percent of its orders and lays off a group of workers. In this constructed example, the workers laid off are precisely the people this import rule reaches. Let the goods in, and those three things fall on them; keep them out, and the layoffs fall on them too. So the constructs on the importing side must answer not only "let in or not," but also what this act of theirs places on those people: they are not traded for the price in this state's market, nor for this state's peace of mind (the same form as N25, "It does not trade them for this state's safety"; extension; own undertaking). How this is achieved, a ban on entry, a deadline for correction, a requirement of checkable wage records, leaving a road for those laid off, lies at the rule layer; these are only examples, not ranked; this paper does not say which is better, nor that no longer buying from there is necessarily better.

By the end of Year 28, neither the UCSE nor the Kingdom State had taken measures concerning this factory's goods. Not taking new measures does not remove the existing arrangements of access and enforcement from questioning; not taking measures is also a form of acting (own undertaking). What is to be examined first is which of this side's arrangements continue to have effect, such as access and enforcement that routinely let this kind of goods through, on whom the effects fall, and which of the effects those people have no way to avoid by exiting; this side explains its own share, and does not book the foreign supplier's own share over to itself. What it must answer is the same as if it had taken measures: toward those it reaches, what it has done, and why.

The purchasing company. The Theory of the Company has laid down: "A person has no equivalent, all the way down the chain"; "The longer the chain, the more clearly it must be written who is accountable; accountability cannot be subcontracted away" (Theory of the Company Paper 4, GS10). Crossways Home's share in purchasing falls under this proposition, and this paper does not set it up anew. "Buy according to the law over there" answers whether the law over there permits it; it does not answer what this proposition asks: whether, along the chain of the goods Crossways Home buys in, costs have been pressed into the Base Layer of those who work. Adopting a code and engaging an auditor to visit once a year is one thing it has done; Paper 3 read that the party engaging the audit and the party paying the audit fee are both on the side being questioned; this does not prove that the audit's conclusions are controlled, nor does adopting a code count as having fulfilled this proposition (Paper 3, §4). Whether Crossways Home knew beforehand of this factory's practices, the example does not record, and this paper does not supply.

The foreign supplier. That factory's account is kept in its own name. Political Economy has laid down: "The construct is the address of attribution" (Political Economy Paper 1, E2). Dormitories, wages and dust protection are the factory's own acts; its share does not disappear because the party buying its goods has borne a share; nor is the buying party exempted from its own share because the factory has its own account. The two shares are each examined by their source, and not booked over to each other. Keeping the account is not writing an ought for it: here this series only records the address of this account, and does not tell a foreign construct what it ought to do (own undertaking).

Constructs on the exporting side. That district's government and the NUT have their own laws and their own channels. The Theory of the State says: "this series writes no ought for the other state, only how this state is to stand toward it" (the Theory of the State Prequel, §8). This series follows it, and writes no ought for constructs on the exporting side.

Responsibility is not exempted by borders, nor is authority derived from the range of influence. Taken together, these four places come to two sentences. One is that responsibility is not exempted by borders: the shares of the purchasing company and of the constructs on the importing side do not vanish because the goods come from abroad. The other is that authority is not derived directly from the range of influence: that the acts of the constructs on the importing side affect workers abroad does not give them the right to legislate for that district; Institution Theory says, "reach of influence is not reach of authority" (Institution Theory Z4). The two sentences hold at the same time. The particular scope and means go to the rule layer and the judgment layer respectively.

The face of low prices. Some low-income households in Apple City could afford a full set of porcelain bowls for the first time; this is true, and is recorded as it is; it does not offset the three things the Office of Trade confirmed, nor do the three erase it. Bluekiln Porcelain Works closed two kiln lines in Year 25; one cannot supply from this that the closure was caused wholly by this factory's low prices.

Judgment layer: where each of the three arrangements presses, and what the constructs on the importing side have done and ought to do toward those they reach, are answered by the judgment layer and the rule-makers on that side; the purchasing company answers under the Theory of the Company; whether those acted on can ask, §4.

§3 Where There Is No One Above

In Year 21, the UCSE and the NUT signed an Investment Protection Agreement: an investor of one party who considers a measure of the other party "tantamount to expropriation" or "unfair" may bring an arbitration. The Agreement applies to measures of the contracting parties' governments at every level, member states included; the respondent is the contracting state. The tribunal has three members: the investor chooses one, the respondent chooses one, and the presiding arbitrator is chosen jointly by the two; hearings are not public, and awards may be made public; an award may order compensation, but may not order a measure to be revoked.

In Year 26, Farstream Water bought seventy percent of the shares of Springhollow Water Company; in Year 27 it paid to replace the equipment of the water treatment station, and both of the State Health Agency's spot checks that year were passed. The same year, the Kingdom State lowered the threshold of the Public Utilities Act from five thousand households to five hundred, and the State Public Utilities Commission ruled: the water price returns to its level before the Year 23 increase, plus ten percent; households in arrears are not cut off in winter; those still cut off have their supply restored first. In the spring of Year 28, Farstream brought an arbitration against the UCSE under the Agreement, saying these measures were "tantamount to expropriation," and claiming compensation equivalent to about ten years of Springhollow Water Company's expected profits. By the end of Year 28, the tribunal had not yet decided on jurisdiction, and had made no award.

The village committees of Upper Hollow and Lower Hollow wrote to the tribunal asking to present their views. The tribunal replied: the village committees are not parties to the arbitration; they may submit written material, and whether to take that material into account is for the tribunal to decide.

There is no third party that legislates for both. The Theory of the State has laid down that between states there is no third party that legislates for both; this does not exclude treaties and common procedures acquiring binding force within their scope of application: "they bind both parties through each party's own law, or through a procedure both parties have acknowledged"; there is no one above to compel one party on the other's behalf, so while they have binding force, carrying out and questioning must land through each party's own law (Theory of the State Paper 5, N24).

Arbitration between investors and states is a common procedure of this kind: it is set up by a treaty between two states, yet one of its parties is not a state but an investor of the other state (extension: N24 originally speaks of state and state; here one party is replaced by an investor; own undertaking). Farstream is a company of the NUT, and the respondent is the UCSE; the Agreement binds the two contracting states, and the Kingdom State, as a member state of the UCSE, has its measures within the Agreement's scope of application.

A decision can be final; the positions that implement it are not final. The Theory of the State wrote: "An arbitral award can be final in the case; the positions that implement it are not final." It also wrote: "a treaty binds this state, but carries nothing across the line" (Theory of the State Paper 5). An award, once made, can close this case; but the positions that implement it, whether and how to pay, whether and how to amend the law, each still lie under a channel they cannot themselves close. That a treaty binds this state is true: having signed it, the UCSE is bound by it; but this binding carries nothing across the line: it does not make it permissible for this state, in order to comply, to cross the line against its own people.

Reading Springhollow by this sentence, both ends must be guarded. At one end, one cannot say, because the treaty carries nothing across the line, that the Kingdom State may ignore this Agreement: the Agreement was signed by the UCSE, and is binding within its scope of application. At the other end, one cannot say, because the Agreement is binding, that the Kingdom State must withdraw the price rollback and the ban on winter cut-offs to avoid paying compensation: the Agreement can order only compensation, not revocation of a measure; and even if compensation is ordered, whether the only water of Springhollow's two villages is cut off in winter is not decided by an award, but by the positions on this side that guard the line.

"The party questioned," written out in three layers. In an investment arbitration, several things are asked and several places are questioned, and they are not to be merged into one:

  • Whether a measure breaches an investment-protection commitment already acknowledged: the party questioned is the state and its relevant positions: the UCSE as the respondent contracting state, the Kingdom State as the member state that made those measures, and the State Public Utilities Commission as the position that made the rulings.
  • Supply, price and the stopping of supply: the party questioned is the supplier and its regulatory position: Springhollow Water Company and its owners, and the State Public Utilities Commission. Paper 1 has already read this layer: the line is there; what changed is whether the Commission's standing review covers Springhollow Water Company.
  • The authority, the entry, the effect and the implementation of arbitration: the party questioned is the positions that make, accept and implement this procedural arrangement: the two contracting states that signed the Agreement; the tribunal; the positions that must implement an award.

The three layers are written apart because each has its own channel. The first layer's ruling on breach and compensation cannot answer for the second layer's supply and guarding of the line: whether the two villages' water is cut off in winter is not decided on this side's behalf by this arbitration's ruling on compensation. Nor can the second layer's line be used to cancel the first layer's commitments: the Kingdom State's guarding its line does not mean that its measures already conform to the Agreement; the two are examined separately.

Binding force and the procedure's own openness to questioning hold at the same time. A common procedure is binding on the contracting parties; it can itself also be questioned. Both hold at the same time. A state cannot exempt itself from existing constraints merely on the ground that it is being questioned: suspending or exiting a treaty is itself an act of this state, examined separately under the Theory of the State, with its reasons stated publicly (Theory of the State Paper 5, N24). Nor can the regulated party use a procedure to close off on its own the institution's questioning of its own acts (extension: Political Economy Paper 4, E22 originally says that the channels of questioning an economic position reaches are not closed off on its own by the party questioned; here it is extended to procedures set up by treaty; own undertaking). Farstream's bringing the arbitration is a right it exercises under the Agreement; it does not thereby make the State Public Utilities Commission's questioning of its supply, price and stopping of supply come to a halt.

Bringing an arbitration, a large claim, hearings not in public, the village committees' not being parties: none of these alone proves that the investor has seized questioning on its own; nor is the compensation claimed treated as already awarded. By the end of Year 28, this case had recorded only a claim, with no decision on jurisdiction and no award. Which side the tribunal will favor, this paper does not prejudge; whether Farstream bought Springhollow Water Company for the sake of arbitration, and whether the Kingdom State lowered the threshold with Farstream in view, this paper does not read minds to say.

A procedure that the people whose line it is cannot initiate is not their channel. The households of the two villages are the people whose line is at stake in this case: the winter water falls on them. They are not parties to the arbitration; the village committees may submit written material, and whether to take that material into account is for the tribunal to decide. By that proposition of Institution Theory, the channel of questioning must be independent of the party questioned, "internal subjects can actually initiate it," and initiating it brings no retaliation (Institution Theory Z34). Three things are to be kept apart here: whether the two villages can themselves bring the kind of claim an investor brings against a state; whether, in an arbitration already started, the material they submit can activate a response on the relevant issues; whether they can question the authority, entry, effect and implementation of the arbitral procedure itself. What the example gives is that the existing terms give the two villages no position from which to bring this investment arbitration themselves, and that the village committees may submit written material, and that whether to take that material into account is for the tribunal to decide. What it does not give is how the material is handled, whether a reply is required, what procedural effect it has, and whether there are other roads of asking about the procedure itself. That there is no guarantee that the tribunal will adopt their views does not mean that their questions necessarily lack actual efficacy; but this participation cannot be taken directly as a channel that already meets these conditions either. So this state may not use "there is already an arbitration, and the village committees can submit material too" as a reason to replace the two villages' questioning of supply, regulation and the implementation of the award; this channel must be kept at the positions in this state that implement it: the State Public Utilities Commission, the law of the Kingdom State, and other positions in the Kingdom State (own undertaking). "Their road remains within this state" is read as a responsibility this state must still take up, not as an inference from present facts that there is no channel at all abroad. That the tribunal accepts only written material does not in itself prove that the arbitration crosses the line.

What arbitration can do, and where its limits lie. Under this Agreement, what arbitration can do: rule whether a measure breaches the commitment of investment protection, and order compensation. What it cannot do: order a measure to be revoked. Its limits: hearings are not public, the people whose line it is are not parties, and the presiding arbitrator is chosen jointly by the two whom the two sides chose. Where it may fail: the risk of compensation makes rule-makers weigh, when making the next rule, whether they will be taken to arbitration again; Paper 6 read the proposal in the State Legislature, "so we don't get taken to arbitration again," which by the end of Year 28 had not been put to a vote. These are each written out, and not merged into a single sentence, "arbitration is good" or "arbitration is bad."

Cooperation among states. Cooperation among states arising over such an arbitration or such an agreement, how to amend the treaty, how to set up a common review, falls within the functional boundaries the Theory of Inter-State Cooperation already has (Theory of Inter-State Cooperation Paper 8, §4); when Political Economy read the conditions offered for relocation, it said the same sentence (Political Economy Paper 3, E20). This paper designs neither arbitration nor cross-border mechanisms.

Judgment layer: whether a measure breaches the Agreement is judged by the tribunal; whether the Kingdom State guards its line is answered by the Kingdom State's judgment layer and rule-makers; how an award is implemented is answered by the positions that implement it, each under its own channel; the two villages can question the positions in this state.

§4 Cross-Border Chokepoints and a Longer Road of Questioning

Cross-border chokepoints. The kinds of chokepoints Paper 3 read, settlement, rating and platforms, can all cross borders: a settlement network handles the money of several states, a rating agency rates another state's bonds, a platform ranks in several markets. Whether something is a chokepoint, who draws the scope of the rules it makes, and how those rules can be questioned fall under Paper 3; across borders, what is added is only this paper's question: who guards, how authority connects, where the account falls. This paper does not set up anything new.

A longer road of questioning. Political Economy read Crossways' relocation declaration, and also left a constructed example: suppose that in Year 25 Crossways had really moved its headquarters, and its business in the Kingdom State were run by a subsidiary of that NUT company; a seller who sued would have to go through cross-border service (Political Economy Paper 3, §8). That paper said that the road of questioning has grown longer; the change of procedure does not by itself prove that the road of suing has been closed.

Read by this paper, a longer road must be asked about in two respects. One is whether it is still within reach: the channel of questioning must be one that internal subjects can actually initiate (Institution Theory Z34); for a small seller who sells only in the Kingdom State, if he must go through cross-border service to ask, whether this road is still within his reach depends on how long service takes, what it costs, and where and how a judgment is enforced, none of which the constructed example gives. The other is who keeps the road within his reach: by §2, this is a share to be answered by the constructs on the side of import and access toward those their own acts reach; how to keep it, such as requiring an address in the locality at which one can be sued, lies at the rule layer, and is only an example.

Judgment layer: whether a cross-border road of questioning is within reach is examined according to fact by the position that takes it up; how to keep it within reach lies at the rule layer.

Where This Paper Stops

This paper has laid down two propositions: the line does not change at borders, responsibility goes with each party's own acts, responsibility is not exempted by borders, and authority is not derived directly from the range of influence (SC14); where there is no one above, the channel of questioning does not belong to the party questioned either, an arbitral decision can be final while the positions that implement it are not final, a treaty binds this state but carries nothing across the line, and a procedure that the people whose line it is cannot initiate is not their channel (SC15).

What it has not proved: tariffs and trade policy; exchange rates; sanctions and blockades; the system of cooperation among states; how arbitration and cross-border mechanisms are to be designed; oughts for the constructs of other states; where it is better to buy; the legal characterization of a practice in a given jurisdiction; where that forty percent price gap comes from; how that arbitration will be decided.

Remainders left: how to examine what the constructs on the importing side "have done" toward the people abroad whom they reach, for which this paper has given only an address and a direction; how a common procedure can be brought within reach of the people whose line it is, which is handed to the design of arbitration and cross-border mechanisms and is not within this series. This paper is the last of the papers of the Theory of the Market on the market within one state and across borders; the interfacing with the tradition is in Paper 0.

The Two Propositions of This Paper

SC14 The line does not change at borders; responsibility goes with each party's own acts. That basic rights may not be violated holds against everyone (per Theory of Government G17, by way of Theory of the State N25).

  • The constructs that make and enforce rules on the side of the importing market: toward those whom their own acts (the rules and enforcement of access, procurement and import) reach and who have no way to avoid them by exiting, they guard the line by the unified sentence (per Theory of Government G25: jurisdiction is not the interior; at the national level, extension of the Theory of the State Prequel, §§6 and 8, and N29; own undertaking); the Base Layer of people elsewhere is not traded for the price in one market (the same form as N25, "It does not trade them for this state's safety"; extension; own undertaking).
  • The purchasing company's share along the supply chain falls under Theory of the Company GS10, and is not set up anew.
  • The account of the foreign supplier is kept in its own name, and does not disappear because the purchasing party has borne a share (per Political Economy E2: the construct is the address of attribution); keeping the account is not writing an ought for it.
  • For constructs on the exporting side, this series writes no ought (the Theory of the State Prequel, §8: "this series writes no ought for the other state"; N25).

Responsibility is not exempted by borders, nor is authority derived directly from the range of influence (per Institution Theory Z4: reach of influence is not reach of authority); the particular scope and means go to the rule layer and the judgment layer respectively.

SC15 Where there is no one above, the channel of questioning does not belong to the party questioned either. At the level of state and state, there is no third party that legislates for both; treaties and common procedures bind the parties within their scope of application, and carrying out and questioning go through each party's own law (per Theory of the State N24). Arbitration between investors and states is a common procedure set up by a treaty between two states; one party to the arbitration is an investor of the other state (extension of N24: the original speaks of state and state; here one party is replaced by an investor; own undertaking): its decision can be final in the case, and the positions that implement it are not final; a treaty binds this state, but carries nothing across the line (per Theory of the State Paper 5; that paper's abstract has "can be final in the case," and its close has "is final in the case"). "The party questioned" is written out separately:

  • whether a measure breaches an investment-protection commitment already acknowledged: the party questioned is the state and its relevant positions;
  • supply, price and the stopping of supply: the party questioned is the supplier and its regulatory position;
  • the authority, the entry, the effect and the implementation of arbitration: the party questioned is the positions that make, accept and implement the procedural arrangement.

The binding force of the common procedure and the procedure's own openness to questioning hold at the same time: a state cannot exempt itself from existing constraints merely on the ground that it is being questioned, and suspension or exit is examined separately under N24; nor can the regulated party use the procedure to close off on its own the institution's questioning of its own acts (extension of E22: the original speaks of the channels of questioning an economic position reaches; here it is extended to procedures set up by treaty; own undertaking). A procedure that the people whose line it is cannot initiate is not their channel; their road remains within this state (per Institution Theory Z34). What arbitration can do, and where its limits and points of failure lie, is stated separately (per N24). Cooperation among states arising from this follows the functional boundaries the Theory of Inter-State Cooperation already has (per Theory of Inter-State Cooperation Paper 8, §4; E20 has the same sentence, where the original speaks of the conditions one state gives for relocation into it).

引言

第 25 年，通衢集团的自营品牌通衢家用把它的瓷器改从外地进货。那一宗货，来自目的民族联盟（NUT）南面一个区的一家瓷厂，价比青窑瓷业的同类货低约四成。苹果城一些低收入家庭第一次买得起整套的瓷碗，报纸也登了这一面。

第 27 年，苹果城的报纸报道了这家瓷厂。第 28 年，UCSE 商贸署的调查报告确认了三项：瓷厂工人住厂里的宿舍，宿舍夜里上锁；工资按年结，年中离开的，当年的工资不发；上釉的工位没有防尘设备。报告没有记谁持钥匙、住户夜里能不能自行外出。王国州的《工资支付法》只管在王国州雇人的雇主；那个区的法，商贸署的报告说"对年结工资未作限制"。

通衢家用说："我们照那边的法买货，价格是市场定的。"第 28 年，它订了一份供应商守则，请一家审计公司每年去一次，审计费由瓷厂付。到第 28 年底，UCSE 与王国州都没有就这家瓷厂的货采取措施。

前置篇说过，这一句"价格是市场定的"里有两件事：一件是从哪里买、买谁的，有一个作出它的位置；一件是价格，确实是许多买卖合起来的。这一篇回到它。

还有一件。王国州北部的泉坳水业，第 26 年有七成股份被 NUT 的一家水务集团远川水务买下。第 27 年州降了《公用事业法》的门槛，委员会裁定回价、冬季不停水、先恢复供水；第 28 年春，远川依 UCSE 与 NUT 的《投资保护协定》对 UCSE 提起仲裁。

NUT、UCSE、王国州与这里的瓷厂、公司、仲裁，都是这个系列的设例；这一篇说的不是某一处真实的工厂，也不是某一宗真实的仲裁。

越过国界，问题换了样子：线还在不在；谁来守；没有上级的地方，问的通道归谁。这一篇依次问：线越不越得过国界（第一节）；跨境以后，责任落在哪几处（第二节）；国与国这一层，一项共同的程序约束谁、谁能用它问（第三节）；跨境的关口与一条变长的问路（第四节）。

第一节　线不随国界变

政府论定过：基础权利不受侵犯，「从主体那边推出，对谁都成立，不是政府特有的」（政府论第三篇 G17）。国家论把这一句带到了国与国之间：对别国的人，照统一句；「不拿他们换本国的平安」（国家论第五篇 N25）。

所以线不随国界变。一个人站在国界的这一边还是那一边，不改变他的身体是不是他的，他离不离得开一处做工的地方是不是他自己的事。

越过国界，变的是另外两样。一样是由谁来守：在一国之内，守线的是这一国立规、执规的构；到了境外，那一处有它自己的构，这一国的构管不到那里，也不该替那里立法。一样是权限怎样衔接：一项进口的规则、一份采购的合同、一宗两国条约下的仲裁，各自由谁立、约束谁、管什么、怎样落地。

还有一样要记下：越线的做法，或越线的代价，可以被移到境外。在一国之内不许做的事，可以交给境外的一处去做；在一国之内要付的代价，可以由境外的人去付。这不是说凡从境外买的都可疑：跨境的买卖，与一国之内的买卖一样，照常放开（第二篇）。这是说，一处市场里的价格，不拿别处的人的基础层去换（推及：国家论 N25 原说不拿别国的人换本国的平安；这里推到一处市场里的价格，自担）。

这一篇只接市场这一层。国家间协作论在收尾时写明：「公司之间、国家与公司之间的"制度间的制度"，本系列不接」（国家间协作论第八篇第四节）；政治经济学读强者的迁走时，也把公司与国家之间的制度关系登作余项（政治经济学第三篇 E20）。这一篇接这两处登下的余项，只接功能：哪一处的规则由谁立、约束谁、怎样落地，账落在哪里（自担）。关税与贸易政策、汇率，这一篇不做；制裁与封锁，照国家论；国家之间的协作体系，照国家间协作论；仲裁与跨境机制怎样设计，不做。

判断层：一项跨境的买卖、一项进口的规则、一宗条约下的程序，各落在哪一处的权限里，由各方自己的判断层按它们的法与已承认的程序认；受作用的人可以就自己所在的那一处问。

第二节　责任各归各的施为

回到通衢家用的那一句："我们照那边的法买货，价格是市场定的。"

价差的成因未明，各方要答的不等它。 通衢家用的货比州内同类的低约四成。这四成从哪些成本来，原料、工资、设备、规模、汇率，事例里没有查明；不能由它补成四成都来自商贸署确认的那几项安排，也不能补成某一部分来自它们。可是各方要答的，不等价差的成因查明：商贸署已经确认的三项，是一个问的入口；从这里起，各有各的一份。

先读那三项各是什么。工资按年结、年中离开的不发当年的工资：要看的不是一年结一次这一支付的周期，是年中离开的人，拿不到他已经做了的那几个月的钱。政治经济学的第一道闸写过：不「拿钱、住处或工资的发放方式去锁住人的离开、拒绝与撤回」（政治经济学第四篇 E21）。照结构，扣下一个人已挣的工资、系在他走不走上，是那一道闸所说的那一种形态。宿舍夜里上锁：谁持钥匙、住户夜里能不能自行外出，报告没有记；不能由它补成人被关住、不能离厂。上釉的工位没有防尘：这落在做工的人的身体上；它压到了哪里，要由判断层核。这一家瓷厂的做法在某一部法里算什么，法律的定性，在判断层，本篇不下。

再读谁要答。这一篇分四处。

进口一侧立规、执规的构。 UCSE 的商贸署、王国州的立规者，各有它们自己的施为：准入、采购、进口的规则与执行。国家论前置篇定过：「本国的施为所及、又无从以退出避开的人，不论有没有本国的成员资格、在不在本国境内，就这些施为而言，都在本国的内部」（国家论前置篇第六节）；五条应当怎样对他们适用，照同篇第八节的统一句。政府论在一国之内写过：「辖区不等于内部」；「影响所及不等于权限所及」（政府论第四篇 G25）。照这几句，进口一侧的构，就它自己的施为所及、又无从以退出避开的人，照统一句守线（推及：国家论原说一国的施为，政府论原说一级政府；这里推到进口市场一侧立规、执规的构，自担）。

放进来，是它的施为；挡在外面，也是它的施为。这一点有一个设例可以看得清楚。设若第 29 年，UCSE 商贸署规定：不能证明货物不出自"工资年结、年中离开者不发当年工资"一类安排的，不得进口；通衢家用改从别处进货，那家瓷厂少了一成订单，裁了一批工人。在这一设例里，被裁的那批工人，正是这一项进口规则所及的人。放进来，那三项在他们身上；挡在外面，裁员也在他们身上。所以进口一侧的构，不只要答"放不放"，还要答它的这一项施为落在那些人身上的是什么：不拿他们去换本国市场的价格，也不拿他们去换本国的一份安心（与 N25"不拿他们换本国的平安"同形，推及自担）。怎样做到，禁入、限期整改、要求可核的工资记录、给被裁的人留一条路，在规则层，这些只是例子，不排次序；本篇不说哪一样更好，也不说不再从那里进货就一定更好。

到第 28 年底，UCSE 与王国州都没有就这家瓷厂的货采取措施。未采取新增措施，不使现有的准入与执行安排退出追问；不采取措施，也是一种施为的样子（自担）。要先核哪一项本方的安排在继续起作用，比如照常放行这一类货的准入与执行，作用落到谁身上，哪些作用是他们无从以退出避开的；本方就这一份说明，不把境外供给者自己的那一份转记过来。它要答的，与采取了措施一样：就它所及的那些人，它作了什么、为什么。

买货的公司。 公司论定过：「人没有等价物沿着链往下」；「链越长，越要写清谁负责；责任不能被分包分没了」（公司论第四篇 GS10）。通衢家用买货的那一份，照这一条，本篇不另立。"照那边的法买"答的是那边的法许不许；它没有答这一条问的那件事：通衢家用买进来的货，链上有没有把成本压进做工的人的基础层。订了守则、请审计每年去一次，是它作的一样事；第三篇读过，请审计的与付审计费的都在被问的一侧，这不证明审计的结论受控，也不使订了守则就算尽了这一条（第三篇第四节）。通衢家用事先知不知道这家瓷厂的做法，事例里没有记，本篇不补。

境外的供给者。 那家瓷厂那一份账，记在它自己名下。政治经济学定过：「构是归责的地址」（政治经济学第一篇 E2）。宿舍、工资、防尘，是瓷厂自己的施为；它不因买它货的一方担了一份，就消失；买货的一方也不因瓷厂有它自己的账，就免了它自己的一份。两份各按来源核，不互相转记。记账不是对它写应当：本系列在这里只记下这一份账的地址，不对一家境外的构说它应当怎样（自担）。

出口一侧的构。 那个区的政府、NUT，有它们自己的法与它们自己的通道。国家论说：「这个系列不对别国写应当，只写本国怎样对它」（国家论前置篇第八节）。本系列照此，对出口一侧的构不写应当。

责任不因国界免除，权限也不由影响范围推出。 这四处合起来，是两句。一句是责任不因国界免除：买货的公司、进口一侧的构，各自的那一份，不因货是从境外来的就没有了。一句是权限也不由影响范围直接推出：进口一侧的构的施为影响到了境外的工人，不因此就有权替那个区立法；制度论说，「影响所及不等于权限所及」（制度论 Z4）。两句同时成立。具体的范围与办法，分别进入规则层与判断层。

低价的那一面。 苹果城一些低收入家庭第一次买得起整套的瓷碗，这是真的，照实记下；它不抵销商贸署确认的三项，三项也不抹掉它。青窑瓷业第 25 年关了两条窑线；不能由此补成全由这家瓷厂的低价导致。

判断层：那三项安排各压到了哪里，进口一侧的构就它所及的人作了什么、该作什么，由那一侧的判断层与立规者答；买货的公司照公司论答；受作用的人能不能问，第四节。

SC14　线不随国界变；责任各归各的施为。基础权利不受侵犯，对谁都成立（承政府论 G17，经国家论 N25）。

  • 进口市场一侧立规、执规的构：就它自己的施为（准入、采购、进口的规则与执行）所及、又无从以退出避开的人，照统一句守线（照政府论 G25：辖区不等于内部；国家一级推及国家论前置篇第六、八节与 N29；自担）；一处市场里的价格，不拿别处的人的基础层去换（与 N25"不拿他们换本国的平安"同形，推及自担）。
  • 买货的公司对供应链的那一份，照公司论 GS10，不另立。
  • 境外供给者那一份账，记在它自己名下，不因采购一方担了一份就消失（照政治经济学 E2：构是归责的地址）；记账不是对它写应当。
  • 对出口一侧的构，本系列不写应当（国家论前置篇第八节："这个系列不对别国写应当"；N25）。

责任不因国界免除，权限也不由影响范围直接推出（承制度论 Z4：影响所及不等于权限所及）；具体范围与办法，分别进入规则层与判断层。

第三节　没有上级的地方

第 21 年，UCSE 与 NUT 签了一份《投资保护协定》：一方的投资人认为另一方的措施"等同征收"或"不公平"的，可以提起仲裁。协定适用于缔约方各级政府的措施，成员邦在内；被诉的一方是缔约国。仲裁庭三人，投资人选一人，被诉方选一人，首席由两人共同选；审理不公开，裁决可以公开；裁决可以判赔偿，不能判撤销措施。

第 26 年，远川水务买下泉坳水业七成股份；第 27 年，它出资换了净水站的设备，州卫生厅当年两次抽检都合格。同年，王国州把《公用事业法》的门槛由五千户降到五百户，州公用事业委员会裁定：水价回到第 23 年调价前的水平再加一成，冬季欠费的不停水，仍停着水的先恢复供水。第 28 年春，远川依协定对 UCSE 提起仲裁，说这些措施"等同征收"，请求赔偿约合泉坳水业十年的预期利润。到第 28 年底，仲裁庭还没有就管辖作出决定，也没有裁决。

上坳、下坳的村委会写信给仲裁庭，请求陈述意见。仲裁庭回复：村委会不是仲裁的当事方，可以提交一份书面材料，是否采纳由仲裁庭决定。

没有替双方立法的第三者。 国家论定过：国与国之间没有替双方立法的第三者；这不排除条约与共同程序在其适用范围内取得约束力：「它们经双方各自的法，或经双方已承认的程序约束双方」；头上没有谁替一方强制另一方，所以它们有约束力，执行与追问却要经各方自己的法落地（国家论第五篇 N24）。

投资人与国家之间的仲裁，是这样一种共同程序：它经两国的条约设立，可它的一方不是国，是另一国的投资人（推及：N24 原说国与国之间，这里一方换作投资人，自担）。远川是 NUT 的公司，被诉的是 UCSE；协定约束的是两个缔约国，王国州作为 UCSE 的成员邦，它的措施在协定的适用范围里。

决定可以终局，落实它的位置不终局。 国家论写过：「仲裁的决定可以在本案终局，落实它的位置不终局。」又写：「条约约束本国，带不过线。」（国家论第五篇）一项裁决作出来，可以在这一案里了结；可落实它的那些位置，付不付、怎样付，改不改法、怎样改，仍各在一条它们自己关不上的通道之下。条约约束本国，这是真的：UCSE 签了它，就受它约束；可这一份约束，带不过线：它不使本国为了履约，就可以在自己的人身上越线。

照这一句读泉坳，要防两头。一头，不能因为条约带不过线，就说王国州可以不理这份协定：协定是 UCSE 签的，在它的适用范围里有约束力。另一头，不能因为协定有约束力，就说王国州要把回价、不停水撤回来，好免掉一份赔偿：协定只能判赔偿，不能判撤销措施；即使判了赔偿，泉坳两个村唯一的水在冬天断不断，也不由一份裁决来定，由这一侧守线的位置来定。

"被问的一方"，分三层写。 一宗投资仲裁里，问的是几件事，被问的是几处，不要并成一处：

  • 措施是否违反已承认的投资保护承诺，问的是国家及其相关的位置：UCSE 作为被诉的缔约国，王国州作为作出那几项措施的成员邦，州公用事业委员会作为作出裁定的位置。
  • 供给、价格与停供，问的是供给者与它的监管位置：泉坳水业与它的东家，州公用事业委员会。这一层第一篇已经读过：线在那里，变的是委员会这一常设审查覆不覆盖泉坳水业。
  • 仲裁的权限、入口、作用与落实，问的是作出、接受和落实这一程序安排的位置：签了协定的两个缔约国；仲裁庭；要落实一份裁决的那些位置。

三层分开写，是因为三层各有各的通道。不能拿第一层对违约与赔偿的裁断，替第二层的供给与守线作答：两个村的水在冬天断不断，不是由这一宗仲裁的赔偿裁断替这一侧决定的。也不能拿第二层的线，去取消第一层的承诺：州守它的线，不等于它的措施已经合于协定；两件各核。

约束力与程序自身可被问，同时成立。 一项共同的程序，对缔约各方有约束力；它自己也可以被问。两样同时成立。国家不能只以自己被问为由，免除既有的约束：中止或退出一项条约，本身是本国的施为，另照国家论核，公开说明理由（国家论第五篇 N24）。受规制的一方，也不能借一项程序，独自关闭制度对它自己施为的追问（推及：政治经济学第四篇 E22 原说经济位置所及的问的通道不由被问的一方独自关闭；这里推到条约设的程序，自担）。远川提起仲裁，是它照协定行使的一项权利；它不因此使州公用事业委员会对它的供给、价格与停供的追问停下来。

提起仲裁、请求的金额大、审理不公开、村委会不是当事方，这几样各自都不证明投资人已经独自握住了追问；请求的赔偿也不当作已经判出。这一案到第 28 年底，只记了一份请求，没有管辖的决定，没有裁决。仲裁庭会偏向哪一边，本篇不预判；远川买泉坳水业是不是为了仲裁，州降门槛是不是针对远川，本篇不读心。

线所在的人发动不了的程序，不是他们的通道。 两个村的人家，是这一案里线所在的人：冬天的水落在他们身上。他们不是仲裁的当事方；村委会可以递一份书面材料，是否采纳由仲裁庭决定。照制度论那一条，问的通道要独立于被追问的一方，「内部主体实际可以发动」，发动不被报复（制度论 Z34）。这里要分开三件：两个村能不能自行提起投资人对国家的那一种请求；在已经启动的仲裁里，他们递的材料能不能发动对相关问题的回应；他们能不能追问仲裁程序自身的权限、入口、作用与落实。事例给到的是：现有条款没有给两个村自行提起这一投资仲裁的位置；村委会可以递一份书面材料，是否采纳由仲裁庭决定。它没有给到的是：材料怎样处理，须不须答复，有什么程序上的效力，针对程序本身还有没有别的问路。意见没有必被采纳的保证，不等于发问必然没有实际的效力；可也不能把这一参与直接认作已经满足了这几项条件的通道。所以，本国不得以"已有仲裁，村委会也能递材料"为由，代替两个村对供给、监管与裁决落实的追问；这一份通道，须在本国落实它的那些位置上保留：州公用事业委员会，王国州的法，州里的其他位置（自担）。"路留在本国之内"读作本国仍须承接的责任，不由现有的事实推出境外绝无别的通道。仲裁庭只收书面材料，这本身不证明仲裁越了线。

仲裁能做什么，限度在哪里。 照这份协定，仲裁能做的：判一项措施是否违反了投资保护的承诺，判赔偿。它不能做的：判撤销措施。它的限度：审理不公开，线所在的人不是当事方，首席由两边选出的人共同选。它可能失灵的地方：一份赔偿的风险，让立规者在作下一项规则时先掂量会不会再被仲裁；第六篇读过州议会里"免得再被仲裁"的那一项提案，它到第 28 年底没有表决。这几样分别写明，不合成一句"仲裁好"或"仲裁坏"。

国家之间的协作。 各国就这样一宗仲裁、一份协定发生的协作，怎样修约、怎样设共同的复核，归国家间协作论已有的功能边界（国家间协作论第八篇第四节）；政治经济学读迁入的条件时，说的是同一句（政治经济学第三篇 E20）。这一篇不设计仲裁，也不设计跨境的机制。

判断层：措施违没违反协定，由仲裁庭判；州守不守它的线，由州的判断层与立规者答；裁决怎样落实，由落实它的位置答，各在自己的通道之下；两个村可以问本国的那些位置。

SC15　没有上级的地方，问的通道也不归被问的一方。国与国这一层，没有替双方立法的第三者；条约与共同程序在其适用范围内约束缔约各方，执行与追问经各方自己的法（承国家论 N24）。投资人与国家之间的仲裁，是经两国条约设的共同程序，一方是另一国的投资人（推及 N24：原说国与国，这里一方换作投资人；自担）：它的决定可以在本案终局，落实它的位置不终局；条约约束本国，带不过线（承国家论第五篇；该篇摘要作"可以在本案终局"，篇末作"在本案终局"）。"被问的一方"分开写：

  • 措施是否违反已承认的投资保护承诺，问的是国家及其相关位置；
  • 供给、价格与停供，问的是供给者与它的监管位置；
  • 仲裁的权限、入口、作用与落实，问的是作出、接受和落实程序安排的位置。

共同程序的约束力与程序自身可被问，同时成立：国家不能只以自己被问为由免除既有约束，中止或退出另照 N24 核；受规制的一方也不能借程序独自关闭制度对它自己施为的追问（推及 E22：原说经济位置所及的问的通道，这里推到条约设的程序；自担）。线所在的人发动不了的程序，不是他们的通道，他们的路留在本国之内（承制度论 Z34）。仲裁能做什么，限度与失灵处在哪里，分别写明（承 N24）。国家之间就此发生的协作，归国家间协作论已有的功能边界（照国家间协作论第八篇第四节；E20 有同句，原说一国给迁入的条件）。

第四节　跨境的关口与变长的问路

跨境的关口。 第三篇读过的那几类关口，结算、评级、平台，都可以跨境：一张结算网经手几国的钱，一家评级机构评别国的债，一个平台在几处市场里排序。是不是关口，所立的规由谁划范围、可以被问，照第三篇；越过国界，多出来的只是这一篇的那一问：谁来守，权限怎样衔接，账落在哪里。这一篇不另立。

变长的问路。 政治经济学读过通衢的迁址声明，也留下一个设例：设若第 25 年通衢真迁了总部，王国州的业务改由那家 NUT 公司的一家子公司经营；一个卖家起诉，要经跨国送达（政治经济学第三篇第八节）。那一篇说，问的路变长了；程序的改变本身，不独自证明起诉的路已经关了。

照这一篇读，变长的路要问两件。一件是它还够不够得着：问的通道要内部的人实际可以发动（制度论 Z34）；一个只在王国州卖货的小卖家，要跨国送达才问得到，这条路对他还够不够得着，要看送达要多久、花多少、判决在哪里怎样执行，设例里没有给。另一件是谁替他把路留在够得着的地方：依第二节，这是进口与准入一侧的构就它自己的施为所及的人要答的一份；怎样留，比如要求在本地设一个可以被起诉的地址，在规则层，只是例子。

判断层：一条跨境的问路够不够得着，由受理的位置按实际核；怎样把它留在够得着的地方，在规则层。

这一篇停在哪里

这一篇立了两条：线不随国界变，责任各归各的施为，责任不因国界免除，权限也不由影响范围直接推出（SC14）；没有上级的地方，问的通道也不归被问的一方，仲裁的决定可以终局，落实它的位置不终局，条约约束本国、带不过线，线所在的人发动不了的程序不是他们的通道（SC15）。

它没有证明的：关税与贸易政策；汇率；制裁与封锁；国家之间的协作体系；仲裁与跨境机制怎样设计；对别国的构写应当；从哪里进货更好；某一做法在某一法域的法律定性；那四成价差从哪里来；那一宗仲裁会怎样裁。

留下的余项：进口一侧的构就它所及的境外的人"作了什么"怎样核，这一篇只给了地址与方向；一项共同程序怎样让线所在的人也够得着，交仲裁与跨境机制的设计，不在本系列。这一篇是市场论一国之内与跨境两部分的最后一篇；与传统的对接，在 Paper 0。

共同程序的约束力与程序自身可被问，同时成立：国家不能只以自己被问为由免除既有约束，中止或退出另照 N24 核；受规制的一方也不能借程序独自关闭制度对它自己施为的追问（推及 E22：原说经济位置所及的问的通道，这里推到条约设的程序；自担）。线所在的人发动不了的程序，不是他们的通道，他们的路留在本国之内（承制度论 Z34）。仲裁能做什么，限度与失灵处在哪里，分别写明（承 N24）。国家之间就此发生的协作，归国家间协作论已有的功能边界（照国家间协作论第八篇第四节；E20 有同句，原说一国给迁入的条件）。
