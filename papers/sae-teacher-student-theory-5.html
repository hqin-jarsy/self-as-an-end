Abstract

This paper reads what follows once the thing the student himself cares about has come up. The student's own purpose and his cannot-not cannot be taught; he has to chisel them out himself. What the teacher's end can do is give space: not suppress, and not block that signal. This is the stretch, facing purpose, of the one thing that the tripartite division of the education paper specifically calls "nurturing."

Where one teacher has many students: what is done facing many is creating conditions for cultivation. Creating conditions can face many; questioning is put to one person who can hear and can answer, and how one responds after he has answered falls in his relation. One act can reach many at once, and is read separately as it lands in each relation. A shared arrangement cannot take the place of a response to a particular question, and one method cannot vouch for, or complete, the growing and the avowing of many. The teacher's time and strength are limited: where the three conditions do not all hold, or where he judges that this time he cannot give, not giving this time is not losing one's ground, and this judgment too can be questioned; nor is not giving the safer side in advance.

The three forms of colonization at the layer of purpose each have a shape between teacher and student. Direction substitution is treating the road the teacher has chosen for the student as the direction he himself cannot but take, or treating him as the one to carry on the teacher's own line; the teacher's deciding what to teach and what to teach first is not, in itself, this. Full-support resistance removal is fending off, on his behalf, both the difficulties and other people's objections; the increase or decrease of a single item of support is not enough to establish it. Cultivation in this stretch is negating a false cannot-not, not implanting a direction: the teacher can withdraw a requirement he himself added, and say "you need not," not "you should not." The teacher does not turn every time together into an occasion for growth. This paper interfaces with the Analects and with Rousseau's Emile. It does not prove that the teacher ought to let the student decide everything himself, nor that one-to-one is better than one-to-many, nor that the student's direction is bound to succeed, nor that the teacher's deciding what to teach is deciding his direction for him.

Introduction

One Wednesday, when Lu Miao had finished her problems, she said: "Uncle Fang, what I'd really rather do is draw."

Fang Xu paused, and said: "Last time I said 'don't talk about drawing any more.' I take that back. What do you want to draw?" Lu Miao said, the tree downstairs. Fang Xu said: "When you've drawn it, bring it and show me. Whether you go on with mathematics, and for how long, you talk over with your mother. For every Wednesday you come, I'll teach a Wednesday."

That is one response. Suppose another: "Drawing is play. Since you're learning with me, there's only one kind of person you can become."

Some time later Lu Miao went to a drawing class, and came back unhappy: the teacher there had said the perspective of her tree was wrong. Suppose Fang Xu responds like this: "Don't listen to him. Yours is the best."

Later still, one Wednesday, Lu Miao came over to bring the rice dumplings her grandmother had made. She sat for a while and talked about things at school. Fang Xu did not bring out any problems.

In Master Zheng's workshop, one day at the end of work, he said to the three boys: "I used to keep saying that the three of you would all take over this craft of mine. I withdraw that. Whether you go into this trade once you've learned it is for you to decide." A-Shu said: "I wanted to anyway." Xiaoman did not speak. Dongsheng asked: "And what if I want to go and learn carving?" After that, Master Zheng kept half an hour after work every day to teach Dongsheng carving on his own; that half hour had been the time when the three of them gathered round him with their questions.

Among these there are several things to be read apart. Lu Miao would rather draw: can Fang Xu teach this? The two responses: where do they differ? "Don't listen to him, yours is the best": does it give her something, or take something away? One sentence of Master Zheng's reached three people: how is it entered? In the half hour given to Dongsheng alone, where is A-Shu's share, and Xiaoman's? And the afternoon of the rice dumplings, with no lesson: what is that?

What this paper reads. Paper 3 read the stretch, facing self-awareness, of what the education paper calls nurturing; this paper reads the stretch facing purpose: what falls to the teacher's end once the thing the student himself cares about has come up.

This paper reads five things. First, that the student's own purpose cannot be taught, and that what the teacher's end can do is give space. Second, how this stands where one teacher has many students. Third, the three forms of colonization at the layer of purpose, as they look between teacher and student. Fourth, negating a false cannot-not. Fifth, not turning every time together into an occasion for growth.

Shared words. Two words are kept apart in this paper. "Nurturing" is the education paper's word, in that paper's special usage: providing space for the subject to chisel out self-awareness and purpose on its own, possible only one-on-one.1 "Cultivation," unless otherwise marked, follows the broad sense at the layer of relation, the direction of one effect. One phrase crosses the two: in "creating conditions for cultivation," the word is used in the special sense of "nurturing" (§2). "Cannot-not" is the layer of purpose spoken of in the Learning series: a direction that still cannot be put down after sustained scrutiny.2

Method. As in the earlier papers. Whether Lu Miao later draws, and whether her drawing comes to anything, are not in this paper.

§1 The One Thing That Cannot Be Taught

TS24　The student's own purpose and his cannot-not cannot be taught; he has to chisel them out himself. What the teacher's end can do is give space: not suppress, and not block that signal. This is the 14DD stretch of the one thing that the tripartite division of the education paper specifically calls "nurturing"; the 13DD stretch is in Paper 3

Cannot be taught

Lu Miao would rather draw. This Fang Xu cannot teach: not because he cannot draw, but because this kind of thing cannot be taught. The two layers of self-awareness and purpose cannot be taught, and have to be chiseled out by the subject itself.3 When the Learning series speaks of the position of the layer of purpose in education, it says the same: you cannot teach it.4

What Fang Xu can teach is mathematics; if Lu Miao is to learn drawing, there are others who can teach her to block in a form and mix a color. Why she would rather draw, and whether drawing is the one thing she cannot put down, no one can teach.

Giving space

What the teacher's end can do is give space: not suppress, and not block that signal.3 Fang Xu asked "what do you want to draw," and said "bring it and show me"; he did not turn the sentence back.

Giving space is not not teaching. Upstream writes it plainly: what is left blank is the final direction, not the intensity of the process; in the blank stretch, one does not rush to fill in a direction for the student.5 Fang Xu goes on teaching her mathematics as before, and goes on having her test her problems through; what he does not do is say for her where she ought to go.

Two stretches

The one thing the education paper specifically calls "nurturing" has two stretches: one faces self-awareness, where the teacher's end is not evading; one faces purpose, where the teacher's end is not suppressing. The former is in Paper 3, and this paper is the latter.

§2 One Teacher, Many Students

TS25　What is done facing many is creating conditions for cultivation. Creating conditions can face many; questioning is put to one person who can hear and can answer, and how one responds after he has answered falls in his relation. One act can reach many at once, and is read separately as it lands in each relation: withdrawing a requirement, giving out material, answering one student's question, can each act on several people at the same time. A shared arrangement cannot take the place of a response to a particular question, and one method cannot vouch for, or complete, the growing and the avowing of many. The teacher's time and strength are limited: whether the other is willing, whether it suits him, whether the moment is ripe; where these three do not all hold, or where he judges that this time he cannot give, not giving this time is not losing one's ground, and this judgment too can be questioned; nor is not giving the safer side in advance. Whether he would drain himself dry is for his own end to assess. Where the time in which everyone has class together is taken to give to this one, the other students' share is in it too

Creating conditions, and questioning

Teaching can be done for many at once; nurturing is possible only one-on-one, because each person's purpose takes a different form.6 Upstream has read this sentence more closely: a concrete relation of cultivation is one-to-one, while an act of cultivation can be sent out by one person toward many; thirty students cannot be cultivated at once by one method.7

This series reads these sentences as follows: what is done facing many is creating conditions for cultivation. It does not vouch for the growing of each of the many, nor complete the avowing of each on their behalf.

How to divide, a cut is borrowed here: creating conditions, and questioning. Upstream says that bringing a person into his own has mainly these two ways; questioning needs someone to be there, who can hear and can answer.8 Creating conditions can face many. Questioning needs one person who can hear and can answer; how one responds after he has answered falls in his relation.

Lu Miao brought the tree. Fang Xu looked at it and asked: "You've drawn this tree three times. Why this one?" Lu Miao said: "It doesn't lose its leaves in winter either." Fang Xu said: "When winter comes, I'll go down and look too." This question goes on from Lu Miao's answer, and falls in her relation. A shared arrangement facing many still cannot take the place of a response to the question each one actually raises; and answering one person's question in front of everyone can in turn enter the others' conditions, each relation being read separately.

One act, reaching many

One act can reach many at once, and is read separately as it lands in each relation.

Master Zheng's "I withdraw that" is one act, and it reached three people. A-Shu said "I wanted to anyway," Xiaoman did not speak, Dongsheng asked about carving: each of the three took it in his own way. How Master Zheng responds to A-Shu after this, how he responds to Dongsheng, and how he treats Xiaoman's not speaking, are three things in three relations. Withdrawing a requirement, giving out a piece of material, answering one student's question in front of everyone (Paper 3), are all of this kind.

A shared arrangement cannot take the place of a response to a particular question. Master Zheng's having said to the three "it's for you to decide" is not his having answered Dongsheng's question. Nor can one method vouch for, or complete, the growing and the avowing of many. A-Shu still says "I wanted to anyway": the choice stated on this occasion has not changed; the requirement that he should one day take over this craft has, for him too, been withdrawn. He did the same thing under that requirement before, and does it not under that requirement after; how he uses this space is seen separately. Xiaoman did not speak: that the requirement has been withdrawn can be seen; how he takes it cannot be read.

When one cannot give

The teacher's time and strength are limited. Having seen, one first assesses: on the other's side, whether he is willing, whether it suits him, whether the moment is ripe, and these three are not answered in his place; on one's own side, whether one can give, and whether it will drain one dry.9 Where the three do not all hold, or where one judges that this time one cannot give, not giving this time is not losing one's ground; this judgment too can be questioned. That one did not give this time does not by itself show whether the direction was cancelled, the judgment was wrong, or one judged and did not act.10 Nor is not giving the safer side in advance.11

Master Zheng has three apprentices. This month he can attend to Dongsheng's carving, and cannot attend to asking Xiaoman why he does not speak: this judgment is his, and whether he would drain himself dry is for his own end to assess; this judgment too can be questioned, Xiaoman can question it, and so can others.

The other students' share

Where the time everyone has together is taken to give to this one, the other students' share is in it too.12 The half hour given to Dongsheng alone used to be the time when the three asked their questions; the time in which A-Shu and Xiaoman can ask has changed with it. This does not say that Master Zheng ought not to teach Dongsheng alone; it says that this entry is not settled merely by Dongsheng's benefit, and that A-Shu's relation and Xiaoman's each have an entry to be made.

§3 Three Forms

TS26　The three forms of colonization at 14DD each have a shape between teacher and student. Direction substitution is treating the road of study or of life that the teacher has chosen for the student as the direction he himself cannot but take, or treating him as the one to carry on the teacher's own line; the teacher's deciding what to teach and what to teach first is not, in itself, this. Superego internalization: see TS21. Full-support resistance removal is fending off, on his behalf, both the difficulties and other people's objections; the increase or decrease of a single item of support is not enough to establish it. The student does not figure only as the teacher's continuation

The Learning series reads three forms of colonization at the layer of purpose: direction substitution, superego internalization, and full-support resistance removal.13 Between teacher and student each has a shape.

Direction substitution

Direction substitution is treating the road of study or of life that the teacher has chosen for the student as the direction he himself cannot but take; or treating him as the one to carry on the teacher's own line.

The teacher's deciding what to teach and what to teach first is not, in itself, direction substitution. Choosing what to teach and what to teach first is one thing; sealing some content in advance as a direction the other cannot change is another.14 A pair of contrasting sentences: "to learn this, you first have to practice that" puts forward an arrangement that can be questioned, and Lu Miao can ask why that comes first; "since you're learning with me, there's only one kind of person you can become" adds in a deciding of her direction for her.

Master Zheng's earlier "the three of you will all take over this craft of mine" headed toward the latter; he later withdrew it (§4). The student does not figure only as the teacher's continuation. The meaning on the teacher's side is not copied onto the student:15 what this craft is to Master Zheng is Master Zheng's; what it is to A-Shu is A-Shu's. A-Shu says "I wanted to anyway": the two can fall on the same thing, and are not on that account one.

Superego internalization

The teacher's approval becoming the measure of the student's own judgment was read in Paper 4, and is referred back to here. At the layer of purpose its shape is: the student says "I ought to study this," and when one asks further, the "ought" points back to the teacher.

Full-support resistance removal

The third form appears in the guise of support: fending off, on his behalf, both the difficulties and other people's objections. Upstream has written that if difficult feedback, other people's objections and the real consequences of his choices are all kept outside, and cannot become material for his judgment, there is nowhere for him to tell which thing he truly cannot put down and which he merely happens to be doing.16

"Don't listen to him. Yours is the best." The drawing teacher's saying that the perspective is wrong is an objection coming from outside. Fang Xu's sentence has two halves, and they are read apart.

The first half, "don't listen to him," fends off what came from outside. It has the same shape as "don't listen to what other people teach, listen to me" in Paper 3: heading toward making her listen only to this one teacher. The reading of this half does not change with whether the second half is well founded.

The second half, "yours is the best," is Fang Xu's own judgment. Which way this half heads is read at the time: if Fang Xu has looked at the drawing and can say why he sees it so, he is giving his own judgment; if he says it without having looked at the drawing, he is using a sentence of praise to push that "wrong" away.

Suppose what Fang Xu says is: "When he says the perspective is wrong, which part does he mean? Bring it and let me see. These few strokes in the branches, I think, are well drawn." What was said over there has not been fended off, and his own judgment has been given too.

The increase or decrease of a single item of support is not enough to establish this form.17 Fang Xu's praising one sentence more or one sentence less shows nothing; what is read is whether difficulties and objections have been kept, across the board, outside her judgment.

§4 Negating a False Cannot-Not

TS27　Cultivation in this stretch is negating a false cannot-not, not implanting a direction. The teacher can withdraw a requirement he himself added, and say "you need not," not "you should not"; which cannot-not is false is for the student to judge

Cultivation in this stretch is negating a false cannot-not, not implanting a direction.18

What the teacher can negate is the requirement he himself added. In removing a false cannot-not, the negation falls on the giving side's own demands: it says "you need not," not "you should not."19 Fang Xu says "I take that back," and Master Zheng says "I withdraw that": in each case what is withdrawn is what he himself added earlier. "Don't talk about drawing any more" was what Fang Xu had put on Lu Miao; "will all take over this craft of mine" was what Master Zheng had put on the three boys.

Withdrawing says "you need not": you need not stop mentioning drawing, you need not take over this craft. It does not say "you should not study mathematics," nor "you should not do carpentry." A-Shu wants to as before, and the withdrawal does not stand in his way.

Which cannot-not is false is for the student to judge.19 Fang Xu does not say for Lu Miao "wanting to draw is the real one," nor "wanting to draw is a passing fancy"; Master Zheng does not say for Xiaoman whether he wants to or not. The teacher can say what he himself has seen (Paper 2); the judging lies at the student's end.

§5 Not Turning Every Time Together into an Occasion for Growth

TS28　The teacher does not turn every time together into an occasion for growth. The student may refuse to be treated continually as someone yet to grow

On the afternoon Lu Miao came with the rice dumplings, Fang Xu did not bring out any problems, nor did he ask her "what did you learn this week?" The teacher does not turn every time together into an occasion for growth.20

The student may refuse to be treated continually as someone yet to grow.20 Suppose Lu Miao says one day: "Don't ask me every time what I've learned from it." This is her refusal, and whether it can land is read as in Paper 3. When the teacher looks at the student, he does not see only a person not yet grown: her standing as an end is full from the start (Paper 1).

§6 Interfacing with the Tradition

The Analects: urging on, holding back. Zilu and Ran You asked the same question, whether on hearing a thing one should act on it at once, and Confucius gave two opposite answers. Gongxi Hua asked why, and Confucius said: Qiu holds back, so I urged him on; You has the drive of two men, so I held him back.21 The shared question: one and the same teacher, facing more than one student. Where the two reflect each other: one question, two people, two answers; the response falls in each one's relation, and one method does not serve for two. Where they part: the ground of Confucius' two answers is his judgment of the two men; this series asks a further thing here, whether this judgment is open to questioning. In this chapter Gongxi Hua asked, and Confucius answered. The parties are adults, and only structure is read here.

Rousseau: letting him believe he is the master. In Book II of Emile, Rousseau writes of a way of proceeding: let the pupil always believe that he is the master, while it is the tutor who really is; the pupil does only what he wants, and what he wants is only what the tutor wants him to do.22 Only this piece of operation is read here, and no verdict is passed on the whole book; upstream has already interfaced with the side of Emile that is negative education,23 and that side heads the same way as "giving space." What this passage forces out is another boundary: "giving conditions," and "using conditions to decide his direction for him." The tutor gives no orders; yet everything the pupil can come up against has been arranged. On the reading of this series, the difference does not lie in whether there are orders, but in two places: whether the pupil can question those arrangements and change them; and whether where he is going is something he has grown into for himself out of these conditions, or something the conditions settled for him in advance. Giving no orders, while arranging everything he can come up against so that it leads to one place only, is one shape of direction substitution.

Historical material: Hamerton on Mr. Cape. The Englishman Philip Gilbert Hamerton writes in his autobiography of his schooldays at Doncaster. He suddenly wanted to learn the violin. His guardian, afraid it would interfere with his studies, wrote to ask the headmaster, Mr. Cape. Cape wrote back that the lad was getting on well enough with his studies, and that if he wanted to amuse himself a little by scraping catgut, he might as well be let scrape away. Hamerton adds at once that Cape did not assign to music any high rank in education.24 Only how the adult Hamerton writes is read here. One thing can be read: something the student himself wanted to do came up, and the teacher who was asked did not turn it back; his ground was that the studies were keeping up. Another thing cannot be read: whether he acknowledged the weight this had for the boy. In the reply, playing the violin is amusement; in the made-up example in the Introduction to this paper, "drawing is play" is a sentence that turns it back. The same word, in this letter, is followed by "let him." The letter was written to the guardian, not to the boy. This series does not on that basis certify which direction Cape was headed in.

§7 Drawing Together, and Downstream

This paper sets up five propositions. Taken together they say: the student's own purpose and his cannot-not cannot be taught; what the teacher's end can do is give space, not suppress, and not block that signal; giving space is not not teaching. What is done facing many is creating conditions for cultivation; questioning is put to one person who can hear and can answer, and falls in his relation; one act reaching many is read relation by relation; when one cannot give, not giving is not losing one's ground, this judgment can be questioned, and not giving is not the safer side in advance either; where everyone's time is taken to give to this one, the other students' share is in it too. The three forms of colonization at the layer of purpose are, between teacher and student: treating the road the teacher chose as the direction the student cannot but take, or treating him as the one to carry on the line; the teacher's approval becoming his measure; fending off for him both the difficulties and other people's objections. The teacher's deciding what to teach and what to teach first is not, in itself, deciding his direction for him. Cultivation in this stretch is negating a false cannot-not: the teacher withdraws the requirement he himself added, says "you need not," and does not say "you should not"; which one is false is for the student to judge. The teacher does not turn every time together into an occasion for growth.

This paper does not prove that the teacher ought to let the student decide everything himself, nor that one-to-one is better than one-to-many, nor that the student's direction is bound to succeed, nor that the teacher's deciding what to teach is deciding his direction for him.

What the next paper reads. A few years on, Lu Miao says she is going to study drawing and will not go to the training camp for the mathematics competition. Fang Xu does not think well of this road. How does he say so, and how far does he go? And once, Lu Miao brings a proof that is shorter than the one Fang Xu taught. What is each of these?

Downstream. The meeting of the student's cannot-not with the teacher's law, mutual chiseling, and the student's surpassing the teacher: Paper 6. Coming of age, the test, and the ending of the relation: Paper 7. The face of how the teacher himself lives, as seen by the student, is not read in this series.

摘要

这一篇读学生自己在意的东西冒出来以后的事。学生自己的目的与不得不，教不了，得由他自己凿出来。老师这一端能做的是给空间：不压制，不挡住那个信号。这是教育那一篇三分里专门叫作「涵育」的那一件对着目的的一段。

一位老师有许多学生的时候：面向许多人做的那一下，是为涵育创造条件。创造条件可以面向许多人；追问是对着一个听得见、答得了的人问，他答了以后怎样回，落在他那一段。一个动作可以同时到许多人那里，落到每一段各读。共同的安排不能代替对具体问题的回应，一个方法也不能替许多人担保或完成各自的生长与认领。老师的时间与心力有限：三样不齐，或者判断自己这一次给不了，这一次不给不是失守，这一判也可以被问；不给，也不是预先更安全的一边。

目的这一层上殖民的三种形态，在师生之间各有样子。方向替代，是把老师替学生选定的路当成他本人非如此不可的方向，或把他当作传自己衣钵的人；老师定教什么、先教什么，本身不是。全支持式阻力清除，是把难处与别人的异议都替他挡掉；单凭一项支持的增减，不足以认定。这一段的涵育，是否定假的不得不，不是植入方向：老师可以撤回自己加上去的要求，说「不必」，不说「你不该」。不把每一次相处都安排成成长。这一篇与《论语》、卢梭《爱弥儿》对接。它不证明老师应当让学生自己定一切，不证明一对一比一对多好，不证明学生的方向一定成，也不证明老师定教什么就是替他定方向。

有一个星期三，陆苗做完题，说：「方伯伯，我其实更想画画。」

这是一种回法。设另一种：「画画是玩。你既然跟我学，就只能成为这一种人。」

过了些日子，陆苗去上了画画的班，回来不高兴：那边的老师说她的树「透视不对」。设方叙这样回：「别听他的，你画得最好。」

再后来有一个星期三，陆苗过来，是把外婆包的粽子送来。她坐了一会儿，说了些学校里的事。方叙没有拿题出来。

郑师傅的铺子里，有一天收工，他对三个少年说：「我先前老说，你们三个将来都要接我这门手艺。那一条我撤回。学成了做不做这一行，你们自己定。」阿树说：「我本来就想做。」小满没有说话。冬生问：「那我要是想去学雕花呢？」后来郑师傅每天收工以后，留半个钟头单教冬生雕花；原先那半个钟头，是三个人围着他问问题的时候。

陆苗更想画画。这一件，方叙教不了：不是他不会画，是这一类东西教不了。自意识与目的这两层不能被教，得由主体自己凿出来。3 学习系列说到目的这一层在教育里的处境，说的也是这一句：你不能教它。4

方叙教得了的，是数学；陆苗若要学画，另有人教得了她打形与调色。她为什么更想画画，画画是不是她放不下的那一件，没有人教得了。

给空间

老师这一端能做的是给空间：不压制，不挡住那个信号。3 方叙问「你想画什么」，说「画了带来我看看」，没有把这一句挡回去。

给空间不是不教。上游写得明白：留白的是最终的方向，不是过程的强度；空白的那一段，不急于替学生填进一个方向。5 方叙照旧教她数学，照旧让她把题验完；他没有做的，是替她说她该往哪里去。

创造条件，与追问

教可以对许多人同时做；涵育只能一对一，因为每一个人的目的长得不一样。6 上游把这一句读细过：具体的涵育关系是一对一的，涵育的动作却可以由一个人发出，对象是许多人；三十个学生，不能同时用一个方法涵育。7

这个系列这样读这几句：面向许多人做的那一下，是为涵育创造条件。它不替许多人担保各自的生长，也不替他们完成各自的认领。

怎样分，这里借一刀：创造条件，与追问。上游说成就一个人主要有这两种做法；追问要有一个人在那里，他听得见，也答得了。8 创造条件可以面向许多人。追问要有一个听得见、答得了的人；他答了以后怎样回，落在他那一段。

陆苗把那张树带来了。方叙看了，问：「这棵树你画了三回。为什么是这一棵？」陆苗说：「它冬天也不掉叶子。」方叙说：「冬天的时候，我也下去看看。」这一问接着陆苗的回答往下走，落在她那一段。面向许多人的共同安排，仍不能代替对各人实际提出的问题的回应；当着大家回一个人的问，又可以进入其他人的条件，各段另读。

一个动作，到许多人那里

一个动作可以同时到许多人那里，落到每一段各读。

郑师傅说「那一条我撤回」，是一个动作，到了三个人那里。阿树说「我本来就想做」，小满没有说话，冬生问雕花：三个人各接各的。郑师傅此后怎样回阿树，怎样回冬生，小满不说话他怎样待，是三段里的三件事。撤回一个要求，给出一样材料，当着大家回一个学生的问（第三篇），都是这一种。

共同的安排，不能代替对具体问题的回应。郑师傅对三个人说了「你们自己定」，不等于回了冬生的那一问。一个方法，也不能替许多人担保或完成各自的生长与认领。阿树仍说「我本来就想做」：这一次说出的选择没有改变；要他将来接这门手艺的那一条，在他那里同样撤回了。同一件事，他此前在那一条底下做，此后不在那一条底下做；他怎样用这份空间，另看。小满没有说话：要求已经撤回，这是看得见的；他怎样接，读不出。

给不了的时候

老师的时间与心力有限。看见以后，先评估：对方那一边，愿不愿意、适不适合、时机成没成，这三样不替对方答；自己那一边，给不给得了，会不会把自己耗干。9 三样不齐，或者判断自己这一次给不了，这一次不给不是失守；这一判也可以被问。这一次没有给，本身说明不了是方向取消了、判断错了，还是判了没做。10 不给，也不是预先更安全的一边。11

郑师傅有三个徒弟。他这个月顾得上冬生的雕花，顾不上问小满为什么不说话：这一判是他的，会不会把自己耗干，由他这一端评估；这一判也可以被问，小满可以问，旁人也可以问。

学习系列在目的这一层读过殖民的三种形态：方向替代，超我内化，全支持式阻力清除。13 在师生之间，各有样子。

方向替代

方向替代，是把老师替学生选定的学习或生活的路，当成他本人非如此不可的方向；或把他当作传自己衣钵的人。

老师定教什么、先教什么，本身不是方向替代。选择教什么、先教什么，与把一项内容预先封成对方不可修改的方向，是两件事。14 一组对照的话：「要学这一样，先得练这个」，提出的是一项可以被追问的安排，陆苗可以问为什么先练这个；「你既然跟我学，就只能成为这一种人」，添进去的是对她方向的替定。

郑师傅先前说「你们三个将来都要接我这门手艺」，朝的是后一种；他后来撤回了（第四节）。学生不只作为老师的延续。老师这一侧的意义，不复制到学生身上：15 这门手艺对郑师傅是什么，是郑师傅的；对阿树是什么，是阿树的。阿树说「我本来就想做」，两份可以落在同一件事上，不因此是一份。

超我内化

老师的赞许成了学生自己判断的尺子，第四篇读过，这里回指。在目的这一层，它的样子是：学生说「我应该学这个」，追问下去，「应该」指回老师。

全支持式阻力清除

第三种以支持的样子出现：把难处与别人的异议都替他挡掉。上游写过，若困难的反馈、别人的异议、选择的真实后果都被挡在外面，不能成为他判断的材料，他在哪一件事上真放不下，哪一件只是碰巧在做，就没处分清。16

「别听他的，你画得最好。」画画班的老师说透视不对，这是一声外面来的异议。方叙这一句有两半，分开读。

前一半，「别听他的」，挡的是外面来的那一声。它与第三篇里「别人教的不要听，听我的」同形：朝着使她只听这一位老师去。这一半不因后一半说得有没有道理而改读。

后一半，「你画得最好」，是方叙自己的判断。这一半朝哪一向，照当时读：方叙若是看了那张画，说得出他为什么这样看，是给出自己的判断；若是没有看画就这样说，是拿一句夸去顶掉那一声「不对」。

设方叙说的是：「他说透视不对，指的是哪一处？拿来我看看。枝干这几笔，我看画得好。」那边的话没有被挡掉，他自己的判断也给了。

单凭一项支持的增减，不足以认定这一种。17 方叙多夸一句，少夸一句，不说明什么；读的是难处与异议是不是成片地被挡在了她的判断之外。

第四节 否定假的不得不

TS27　这一段的涵育，是否定假的不得不，不是植入方向。老师可以撤回自己加上去的要求，说「不必」，不说「你不该」；哪一条不得不是假的，由学生判

这一段的涵育，是否定假的不得不，不是植入方向。18

老师否定得了的，是他自己加上去的要求。拿掉假的不得不，否定落在给的一方自己的要求上，说「不必」，不说「你不该」。19 方叙说「那一句我收回」，郑师傅说「那一条我撤回」：撤回的都是自己先前加上去的。「别再说画画了」是方叙加给陆苗的；「都要接我这门手艺」是郑师傅加给三个少年的。

撤回，说的是「不必」：不必不提画画，不必接这门手艺。它不说「你不该学数学」，也不说「你不该做木工」。阿树照旧想做，撤回不拦他。

哪一条不得不是假的，由学生判。19 方叙不替陆苗说「你想画画才是真的」，也不说「你想画画是一时兴起」；郑师傅不替小满说他想不想。老师可以把自己看到的说出来（第二篇）；判，在学生那一端。

第五节 不把每一次相处都安排成成长

TS28　不把每一次相处都安排成成长。学生可以拒绝被持续当作有待成长的人

陆苗来送粽子的那个下午，方叙没有拿题出来，也没有问她「这一周学到了什么」。不把每一次相处都安排成成长。20

学生可以拒绝被持续当作有待成长的人。20 设陆苗有一回说：「你别每次都问我从这里面学到了什么。」这一句是她的拒绝，落不落得下，照第三篇读。老师看学生，不只看到一个还没有长成的人：她作为目的的地位，从一开始就是满的（第一篇）。

第六节 与传统对接

《论语》：进之，退之。 子路与冉有问了同一句话，「闻斯行诸」，孔子给了相反的两个回答。公西华问为什么，孔子说：「求也退，故进之；由也兼人，故退之。」21 共同的问题：同一位老师，面对不止一个学生。相照的一处：同一问，两个人，两个答；回应落在各人那一段，一个方法不通用于两个人。分开的一处：孔子的两个回答，根据是他对两个人的判断；这个系列在这里另问一句，这一判受不受问。这一章里，公西华问了，孔子答了。当事人是成年人，这里只读结构。

卢梭：让他觉得是自己在作主。 卢梭在《爱弥儿》第二卷里写过一种做法：让学生始终以为是自己在作主，实际作主的是导师；学生只做他想做的，而他想做的，只是导师要他做的。22 这里只读这一段操作，不给整部书下判断；上游已经对过《爱弥儿》里消极教育的一面，23 那一面与「给空间」同向。这一段逼出的是另一条界：「给条件」，与「借条件替他定方向」。导师不下命令；学生碰得到的东西，却都是安排好的。照这个系列的读法，分别不在有没有命令，在两处：那些安排学生能不能追问、能不能改；他要往哪里去，是他从这些条件里自己长出来的，还是条件预先替他定下的。不下命令而把他能碰到的都安排成只通向一处，是方向替代的一种样子。

史料：哈默顿写凯普先生。 英国人哈默顿在自传里写他在唐卡斯特上学的时候。他忽然想学小提琴。监护人担心误了功课，写信去问校长凯普先生。凯普回信说：这孩子功课跟得上；他想拉着玩一玩，锯锯琴弦，就让他锯去。哈默顿紧接着写，凯普并不把音乐在教育里看得那样高。24 这里只读成年的哈默顿怎样写。读得出的是一处：学生自己想做的一件事冒了出来，被问到的老师没有把它挡回去；他的根据是功课这一头跟得上。读不出的是另一处：他认没认这件事在这个孩子那里的分量。回信里，拉琴是「玩」；这一篇引言的设例里，「画画是玩」是挡回去的话。同一个字，在这封信里跟着的是「让他去」。信是写给监护人的，不是写给孩子的。这个系列不据此认证凯普在哪个方向上。

第七节 收束与下游

这一篇立了五条。合起来说的是：学生自己的目的与不得不，教不了；老师这一端能做的是给空间，不压制，不挡住那个信号；给空间不是不教。面向许多人做的那一下，是为涵育创造条件；追问对着一个听得见、答得了的人，落在他那一段；一个动作到许多人那里，各段各读；给不了的时候，不给不是失守，这一判可以被问，不给也不是预先更安全的一边；拿大家的时间去给这一个，别的学生那一份也在里面。目的这一层上殖民的三种形态，在师生之间是：把老师选的路当成学生非如此不可的方向，或把他当作传衣钵的人；老师的赞许成了他的尺子；把难处与别人的异议都替他挡掉。老师定教什么、先教什么，本身不是替他定方向。这一段的涵育是否定假的不得不：老师撤回自己加上去的要求，说「不必」，不说「你不该」；哪一条是假的，由学生判。不把每一次相处都安排成成长。

这一篇不证明老师应当让学生自己定一切，不证明一对一比一对多好，不证明学生的方向一定成，也不证明老师定教什么就是替他定方向。

下一篇读的。 几年以后，陆苗说她要去学画，数学竞赛的集训不去了。方叙看不好这条路。他怎样说，说到哪里为止？又有一回，陆苗拿来一个证法，比方叙教的那一个短。这两件，各是什么？

下游。 学生的不得不与老师的法相遇、互凿、学生超过老师，第六篇读。成年、检验与关系的结束，第七篇读。老师自己怎样过、被学生看在眼里的那一面，这个系列不读。
