School Care
学校的照看

DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.24382574 · Full PDF on Zenodo · CC BY 4.0
Abstract

Looking after a person who cannot yet say no cannot do without compulsion, and the school is no exception. This paper states five propositions. Compulsion in a school has three sources: what care requires, what teaching requires, and what is set up to make quiet and room; each is checked by its own limits, and none is called cultivation or giving conditions. Which measure counts as required is explained by the position that exercises the compulsion, and the explanation is reviewed through a road that position does not alone control; the line that separates acting on his behalf from his acting on his own is not drawn once and for all by grade. Alongside compulsion, the school's arrangements let a child's "no" land. A child's standing does not change with whether he can refuse. Discipline is handled by event and fixes no identity on the child; records may be kept, and how they are kept has to be stated. Where something in a school crosses a child's basic rights, the school answers for protection and disposition, and the child has a road the party involved does not control; a child whose rights have been violated at home or elsewhere and who comes for help is not pushed away. This paper does not say which way of discipline is good, does not write the particular rules of school safety, and does not write the legal forms of guardianship.

Introduction

Paper 4 of Cultivation Theory has a sentence: "Looking after a person who cannot yet say no cannot do without compulsion; this compulsion is not called cultivation, and guardianship belongs to the institutional side."

The school is one place on the institutional side. While a child is at school, the school decides many things for him: when he arrives, where he sits, where he may go at break, where his phone is kept.

China's Rules on Educational Discipline in Primary and Secondary Schools (for Trial Implementation), in force since 2021 and the Discipline Rules for short, say in Article 11: "where a student disrupts the classroom or the order of education and teaching, affecting others or possibly causing harm to himself or others, the teacher may take necessary measures to remove the student from the classroom or the teaching site, and subject him to educational management" (my translation; 学生扰乱课堂或者教育教学秩序，影响他人或者可能对自己及他人造成伤害的，教师可以采取必要措施，将学生带离教室或者教学现场，并予以教育管理). The same article says that where a student is found hiding unlawful or dangerous items, the desks and lockers where they may be hidden may be searched, and items that break the rules may be held for a time. The next article lists what teachers "shall not do" (my translation; 不得有下列行为): corporal punishment, disguised corporal punishment, insults to personal dignity, disciplining students because of their academic results, and more. The same rules that grant the authority also draw its limits.

This paper asks where compulsion in a school comes from and where it stops, and whether, alongside it, a child's "no" can land.

§1 While He Is at School

The scope of the school's care. The school looks after a child while he is at school. "While he is at school" is not drawn by the school gate. Institution Theory draws the interior of a construct by entanglement, not by status; whether a construct is organizing a person's life is judged by the actual relation; and reach of influence is not reach of authority (Institution Theory, Paper 1, §4). This paper extends that to care: the scope of the school's care follows the stretch in which the school actually organizes a child's life. Off-site activities the school organizes, the school bus, boarding, and online platforms the school requires children to use are all inside it; arrangements that lengthen time at school, such as staying behind to finish homework, lengthen the scope of care with them.

The reverse holds too. The school's effect can reach into the home, through homework for instance; the accountability of care does not thereby reach into the home, nor does the school thereby gain authority over the child's life outside school. This extension is this series' own undertaking.

What this section does not write. In a boarding school care runs all day, and "while he is at school" has to be read again; this paper only registers it. The legal forms of guardianship this paper does not write. This section states no proposition; it sets the scope for the sections that follow. The side that lies between classmates is read by Paper 4 within this scope.

§2 Three Sources

Where compulsion comes from. Compulsion in a school can come from three places. What care requires: not letting a child run into the road, taking a sick child to the school clinic. What teaching requires: attendance, order in class, homework. What is set up to make quiet and room: collecting phones and the like. Education as Subject-Condition says that when a child first meets something he cannot explain, he has no stretch in which to stop if he is surrounded by unceasing high-frequency, high-certainty stimulation; that stretch needs quiet. Rules such as collecting phones claim this source.

Each by its own limits. What care requires is limited to what care requires; what teaching requires is limited to what teaching requires; what is set up to leave room is checked by what it actually freed and what it sealed. Taking a child's phone can free a lesson's worth of quiet; it can also take away, at the same stroke, his line of contact with home, or an assistive device he needs.

A name is not an authorization. The three categories are for analysis. A measure called "safety" or "order" does not gain authority by that name. Where one measure claims several purposes, it does not settle its limits by picking the widest of them.

England's Education and Inspections Act 2006, section 93(1), allows school staff to use "such force as is reasonable in the circumstances" for three purposes: preventing a pupil from committing an offence; preventing injury to persons or damage to property; and preventing a pupil from "prejudicing the maintenance of good order and discipline". The statute gives the three one and the same standard. The first two concern care; the last concerns order in teaching; they can also overlap. This paper reads it this way: where the requirements differ, the limits differ too, and the particular limits are still checked against the accountability, the circumstances and the means used. Sweden's Education Act, Chapter 5, section 6, allows the school to take "an immediate and temporary measure" (my translation; omedelbar och tillfällig åtgärd); the authority carries a limit in time.1 All of these show only that where there is authority, there is still a scope. They do not judge any particular act of compulsion to be justified. In the category of what care requires, this paper has the first two purposes of section 93 and the two places in Article 11 of the Discipline Rules cited in the introduction. It has not yet found a rule text that deals specifically with how the boundaries of compulsion for safety and health are drawn, compulsion in health care for instance; that is left open.

The category of leaving room. In 2021 the General Office of China's Ministry of Education issued a notice on managing phones, which says: "schools shall inform students and parents that, in principle, personal mobile phones may not be brought onto campus" (my translation; 学校应当告知学生和家长，原则上不得将个人手机带入校园。). It goes on: "where a student truly needs to bring a mobile phone onto campus, the parent's consent is required and a written application is to be made; after entering school the phone is to be handed to the school for unified safekeeping, and bringing it into the classroom is prohibited" (my translation; 学生确有将手机带入校园需求的，须经学生家长同意、书面提出申请，进校后应将手机交由学校统一保管，禁止带入课堂。). The Provisions on the School Protection of Minors, in force the same year, allow schools to prohibit students from bringing phones into school or using them there, and to manage centrally those brought in with permission; another article says that, except for statutory reasons, students' letters, diaries, emails or other online communications may not be read.2 Here keeping and looking are two things. England's Children's Wellbeing and Schools Act 2026, section 36, requires schools to have regard to the Secretary of State's guidance on phones; that guidance "became statutory on 29 June 2026" and covers the time "throughout the school day, including during lessons, the time between lessons, breaktimes and lunchtime", with separate exceptions for disability and medical needs. Finland's Basic Education Act, section 29(4) as amended in 2025, says: "a pupil may not use a phone or other mobile device during a lesson" (my translation; Oppilas ei saa käyttää puhelinta tai muuta mobiililaitetta oppitunnin aikana). It has three exceptions: learning uses a teacher permits, health care a head teacher or teacher permits, and assistive devices. The exceptions are written into the rule.

None of them is called cultivation. None of these kinds of compulsion is renamed cultivation, nor renamed giving conditions, and none is used to prove that cultivation has happened. The quiet a lesson gains when phones are collected can be a condition of cultivation; collecting the phones is compulsion. Conditions given separately in the same arrangement, an afternoon with no classes for instance, are checked separately.

The source is acknowledged but not judged. Where law and higher rules lay a measure down and the school carries it out, the school answers for the scope and manner of its carrying out; whether that rule ought to exist this paper acknowledges but does not judge. Compulsion whose source cannot be stated has no ground.

XX12 Compulsion in a school is not called cultivation. This paper distinguishes three sources of compulsion in a school, each checked by the accountability it claims and by its actual limits: what care requires, limited to what care requires; what teaching requires (attendance, order, homework), limited to what teaching requires; what is set up to make quiet and room (such as collecting phones), checked by what it actually freed and what it sealed. The name of a category is not in itself an authorization; a measure that claims several purposes does not settle its limits by picking the widest of them. Where law and higher rules lay it down and the school carries it out, the school answers for the scope and manner of its carrying out, and the source is acknowledged but not judged; compulsion whose source cannot be stated has no ground. None of them is renamed cultivation or renamed giving conditions, nor used to prove that cultivation has happened; conditions given separately in the same arrangement are checked separately.

§3 Explanation, Review and the Line

Who explains. Which measure counts as required is explained by the position that exercises the compulsion: by what accountability, what is required, why it stops at this scope, when it stops, when it changes. On Rights and Entitlements, reading care for an infant, divides one and the same act of care into two faces. The face of giving does not converge the child's action. The face of the actual arrangement, where it compels or converges his action, is power, and the position of refusing and exiting in the face of that power is kept for him by the guardian-network. If it is power, it has to be able to say on what ground.

A road it does not alone control. On Rights and Entitlements also keeps three positions apart: the caregiver, the guardian-network, and the reviewing node. In fact they may coincide; in analysis they are kept apart. A party that has crossed the bound cannot, while crossing it, be the one that alone removes its own power over the child; removal has to land on another position that has not crossed it. This paper takes over that division. In the school: when the position that exercises compulsion has explained itself, there still has to be a road it does not alone control by which its explanation is reviewed.

The line. A school always has to separate which matters adults still decide on a child's behalf from which he can already do himself, and, in a given matter, where the position of raising and refusing lies. On Rights and Entitlements said something of the same shape: in a maturing child the negation-gate comes to run of itself bit by bit, and the line between entitlement and recognition-based right moves with it; imposing on him an externally fixed binary threshold, irrefutable and applied uniformly across situations, will at some point put some child, growing faster or slower, on the wrong side. The original speaks of the subject-to-be, and of the line between entitlement and recognition-based right. This paper extends it to the line by which the school separates acting on his behalf from his acting on his own, on the ground that both lines follow the same thing: whether a child can walk on his own in one matter after another goes matter by matter and by degrees, and a school's line that cannot keep up with it will likewise put children on the wrong side. So in separating acting on his behalf from his acting on his own, and in deciding where the position of raising and refusing belongs, the school does not set by grade a line that is externally fixed, cannot be overturned by the facts of an individual case, and is applied uniformly across matters. A line that can be overturned by the facts of an individual case, is judged matter by matter, and keeps appeal and review is not of this kind; On Rights and Entitlements itself keeps this kind apart from a dead line. The rules of curriculum, timetable, facilities and stages are not thereby all required to admit overturning in individual cases; whether they seal positions a child has already gained is checked by their actual effect.

Two things cannot be argued backward. Paper 4 of Cultivation Theory says of guardianship: "that he later grew up well does not prove the guardianship was all justified; that the guardianship had grounds does not show that cultivation took place." The same holds for compulsion in a school: that he later grew up well does not prove the school's earlier compulsion was all justified; that the earlier compulsion had grounds does not show that cultivation took place.

An objection. One line of thinking on discipline puts order first, with zero tolerance: uniform, strict, predictable discipline protects the learning of most children, and explaining and reviewing matter by matter slows handling down.3 Its force lies in two places: order itself is something teaching requires; and when handling is slow, the child who was harmed cannot wait. This paper does not deny compulsion that teaching requires; §2 has already counted it as a source. What it asks is that source and limits can be stated, and that there be a road of review. The side of a classmate who has been violated is taken up in §7 of this paper and in §5 of Paper 4. Stopping something in time and explaining and reviewing can go together: urgent protection can come first, and afterward the grounds and scope are still to be explained and remain open to later questioning; review is not written as a procedure to be completed before every act. The cost has to be stated too. Explaining and reviewing take teachers' and the school's time, and that time is counted in the workload Paper 5 speaks of.

XX12 (continued) Which measure counts as required is explained by the position that exercises the compulsion: by what accountability, what is required, why it stops at this scope, when it stops or changes; and the explanation is reviewed through a road that position does not alone control. Who still has others act on his behalf, who can already act on his own, and where the position of raising and refusing belongs, are not settled by a line drawn by grade that is externally fixed, cannot be overturned by the facts of an individual case, and is applied uniformly across matters (a line that can be overturned by the facts of an individual case, is judged matter by matter, and keeps appeal and review is not of this kind). Two things cannot be argued backward: that he later grew up well does not prove the earlier compulsion was all justified; that the earlier compulsion had grounds does not show that cultivation took place.

§4 The Share Beyond Compulsion

Three places in Cultivation Theory. Paper 4 of Cultivation Theory says that alongside guardianship and beyond it there is a share of cultivation: "his 'no' can land, that is, it is heard, not punished, not laughed at; his later looking back is not sealed; his future 'no' is not made costly." It also says: "In matters he cannot yet avow for himself, the form of sealing is pre-occupation, readable in the present by three signals: whether there is an arrangement for handing over the authority to interpret as he grows; whether the refusals, deviations and restructurings that gradually appear in him can really change the arrangement; whether the present arrangement is marked as changeable or as a conclusion that cannot be refuted in future."

In the school. The original speaks of the guardian's side. While a child is at school the school also holds a share of care and compulsion, so the three places and the three signals can be read just as well in the school's arrangements. A child says "no," and someone listens; he is not punished for saying "no," nor laughed at in front of others. The judgments written in his file: who interprets them, and whether there is an arrangement to hand that over to him as he grows. The objections he raises: whether they can enter a real rejudgment and really change an arrangement, or are all read as his not yet understanding. The present class assignment, seating and timetable: whether they are marked as this term's arrangement, or as a settled verdict on him as a person. This extension is this series' undertaking.

Being able to raise something is not the same as having it come to stand. Parent-Child Theory said, between parents and a child: "Being able to raise something is not the same as having it come to stand"; whether the arrangement in a matter changes, if it also involves others, is read by each party's own law, and is not decided by any one party alone. The same holds in school. A child's "no" to an arrangement that also involves classmates or the whole class does not mean the arrangement changes; that his "no" entered a rejudgment is the share that is there.

The cost of refusal. Compulsion follows the limits of §2. A stretch added because he said "no" puts a cost on refusal; Paper 5 of Cultivation Theory said that "the cost of refusal is not added by the giving side." A requirement that has independent grounds does not vanish automatically because he said "no"; but calling something "making up the work" does not prove that his refusal was not punished.

One way of writing it. Sweden's Education Act, Chapter 4, section 9, says: "The information and the forms of the children's and pupils' influence shall be adapted to their age and maturity." (my translation; Informationen och formerna för barnens och elevernas inflytande ska anpassas efter deras ålder och mognad.) The forms of participation are written into a rule and adjusted by age and maturity; this does not prove that any particular child's "no" actually lands.

XX13 The share beyond compulsion. Alongside compulsion, the school's arrangements let his "no" land: it is heard, not punished, not laughed at; his later looking back is not sealed; his future "no" is not made costly. There is an arrangement for handing over the authority to interpret as he grows; his refusals and deviations can enter a real rejudgment and really change the arrangement, and are not all read as his not yet understanding; being able to raise something is not the same as having it come to stand, and matters that involve others or shared arrangements are read by each party's own law, not decided by any one party alone; the present arrangement is marked as one that can be changed.

§5 Standing Does Not Change with the Ability to Refuse

Teacher-Student Theory says: "The student's standing as an end is full from the start, and is not conferred by the teacher; what he can do and how much he understands grow as he learns." It also says: "The two books are kept apart: 'he doesn't understand yet' at most describes what, in this matter, he cannot yet do; it does not lower his standing, nor is it used to read all his questions and his 'no' as signs of immaturity."

Paper 4 of Cultivation Theory says: "Not yet being able to say no does not mean having no preferences or refusals in any matter. One judges by domain, not the whole person." A child who cannot yet judge how fractions are worked out already has preferences, and refuses, in matters such as whether to eat the greens at lunch or whether he is willing to speak in front of the class. When the school weighs a matter that concerns him, it does not leave him out on the ground that "he's still little, he doesn't understand."

The Convention on the Rights of the Child, Article 12(1), requires States Parties to assure to a child capable of forming his own views the right to express those views freely in all matters affecting him, the views being given due weight according to his age and maturity.4 How judging by domain is written into a school's rules, this paper has not found a suitable rule text to show.

Age lines of the kind "at what age one may do what" are one form at the rule layer. Judging by domain is read by this section; an age line that can be overturned by the facts of an individual case, is judged matter by matter, and keeps review is not among the lines of §3.

XX14 Standing does not change with the ability to refuse. He is not excluded from the consideration of a matter concerning him on the ground that "he's still little, he doesn't understand." Not yet being able to say no does not mean having no preferences or refusals in any matter; one judges by domain, not the whole person.

§6 Discipline by Event

Handled by event. A child did something; the school handles that one thing. Once it is handled, no mark of identity is left, and one handling is not registered as a final verdict on this child. Institution Theory says the same: an institution's identification of a person goes no further than the event and does not become an identity; "this does not cancel the checking of particular facts." Facts that still matter for protection are not erased for the sake of "leaving no mark": that a child once hurt another child remains a fact that is still of use for protection. The Theory of the University said a proposition of the same shape at the level of the university.

A rule that writes a procedure. The Discipline Rules, Article 14: where educational discipline or a disciplinary sanction listed in Article 10 is to be imposed on a student, the school "shall hear the student's statement and defense" (my translation; 应当听取学生的陈述和申辩); "where the student or parent applies for a hearing, the school shall organize a hearing" (my translation; 学生或者家长申请听证的，学校应当组织听证); "where the student can sincerely admit the mistake and actively correct it, the educational discipline or disciplinary sanction may be lifted early" (my translation; 能够诚恳认错、积极改正的，可以提前解除教育惩戒或者纪律处分). The handling is written as one matter, with a procedure, and it can be lifted.

Sanctions have terms. The Provisions on the School Protection of Minors say: except for expulsion, every sanction is to have a term; students under sanction are followed and observed and given targeted education; those who have truly corrected themselves have the sanction lifted at the end of its term; once it is lifted, their eligibility for commendations, awards and other benefits is no longer affected by the original sanction.2 This comes close to "once handled, no mark of identity is left." The provision writes the term and the benefits; it does not write how the record of the sanction is handled after it is lifted. Lifting is not deleting.

Records may be kept. A record of the event may be kept, and how it is kept has to be stated: what it is for, who can see it, the child himself and his parents included, how long it is kept, and how it is corrected. When an old record is used in a new decision, its relevance to this matter is stated; it does not enter every later decision without explanation.

The Shaanxi Province Measures for the Management of Student Status Records in Compulsory Education (2019), Article 25, say: "decisions on a student's sanction are placed in the student's status file; after the sanction is revoked, the record of the sanction shall be promptly withdrawn from the student's status file" (my translation; 学生处分决定存入学生学籍档案，处分撤销后，应及时将处分记录从学生学籍档案中撤出。). The same article goes on: "decisions on sanctions, decisions revoking sanctions, and related materials are kept on file by the school" (my translation; 处分决定、撤销处分决定及有关材料由学校存档。). Withdrawn from the status file, the materials are still kept by the school. Both faces are in one article.5 In the full text of the Ministry of Education's 2025 Measures for the Management of Student Status Records of Primary and Secondary School Students, as checked in a reprinted copy, no article on records of sanctions appears. The United States' federal FERPA writes who can see and how to correct: when a parent or a student who has reached the age requests to inspect records, the school provides access within the set number of days; one who believes a record inaccurate or misleading may request amendment, and if the school declines, it has to inform him of the right to a hearing.6 Amendment here concerns whether the record is accurate, not a rehearing of the handling itself.

Records leaving the school. Where a record is given outside the school, for transfer, progression or employment, the giving side states what it gave and on what ground; the adopting side answers for its adoption. On the uses and limits of records once they leave the school, this paper has not yet found suitable rules; Paper 6 reads this again on the side of evaluation.

XX15 Discipline by event; no identity is fixed on the child. A matter is handled as an event; once handled, no mark of identity is left; one handling is not registered as a final verdict on this child. A record of the event may be kept: its keeping states the purpose, who can see it (the child himself and his parents included), until when, and how it is corrected; when an old record is used in a new decision, its relevance to this matter is stated, and it does not enter every later decision without explanation. Where a record is given outside the school (transfer, progression to the next stage, employment), the giving side states what it gave and on what ground, and the adopting side answers for its adoption. This does not cancel the checking of particular facts, nor, for the sake of "leaving no mark," erase facts that still matter for protection.

§7 After the Line Is Crossed

How far the relational side reads. Teacher-Student Theory reads what lies between a teacher and a student: "where the teacher's effect crosses the student's basic rights, this series reads as far as 'this is violation,' and protection and disposition after the violation lie on the institutional side." Parent-Child Theory hands over the same thing from between parents and a child: "The child's basic rights have their standing in himself and are not conferred by the parents; how they are protected and by what rules belongs to the institutional side, and applies whichever direction the parents are headed in." What the two hand to the institutional side is taken up at the level of the school.

The school answers for protection and disposition. Where something in a school crosses a child's basic rights, whether it comes from a teacher, a staff member or another child, the school answers for protection and disposition. China's Compulsory Education Law, Article 29(2), says: "teachers shall respect students' personal dignity, shall not discriminate against students, shall not inflict corporal punishment, disguised corporal punishment or other acts insulting personal dignity on students, and shall not infringe students' lawful rights and interests" (my translation; 教师应当尊重学生的人格，不得歧视学生，不得对学生实施体罚、变相体罚或者其他侮辱人格尊严的行为，不得侵犯学生合法权益。). The Preschool Education Law lists corporal or disguised corporal punishment of children among the unlawful acts of kindergarten teachers and other staff.2 The Convention on the Rights of the Child, Article 28(2), requires States Parties to take all appropriate measures to ensure that school discipline is administered in a manner consistent with the child's human dignity.4 The prohibitions are written into law.

A road the party involved does not control. The child has to have a road for telling, one the party involved does not control; his parents can tell too. Institution Theory set three conditions for such a channel: "it is independent of the party questioned, internal subjects can actually initiate it, and initiating it brings no retaliation". The Discipline Rules, Article 16, say: "where a parent objects to educational discipline imposed by a teacher, or considers that the teacher's conduct violates Article 12 of these Rules, the parent may complain or report to the school or to the competent education administrative department" (my translation; 家长对教师实施的教育惩戒有异议或者认为教师行为违反本规则第十二条规定的，可以向学校或者主管教育行政部门投诉、举报。). Replying to a public message in 2022, the Ministry of Education made clear that complaints and reports and appeals "are different matters" (my translation; 是不同的事项), and that "complaints and reports concern teachers' conduct and are not subject to time limits" (my translation; 投诉、举报针对的是教师行为，不受时间限制). The party this article sets in motion is the parent. It shows that "parents can tell too" has been written into a rule; it does not show that the child has a road of his own. The roads a child can start himself are written in Paper 4.

Two entrances. One is asking for help. A child who suffers an effect that crosses the line at home or elsewhere and comes to the school for help is not pushed away; the school answers for the protection and referral it can give. The Provisions on the School Protection of Minors say that where a student asks the school for protection because of abandonment or abuse, the school may not refuse or shift the matter off, and where rescue is needed, it gives rescue first.2

The other is discovery. Where the school, within its accountability, finds that a child has suffered a violation of his rights, may have suffered such a violation, or faces danger, it reports as the rules require; it does not require young children, or children who cannot fully express themselves, first to complete a formal request for help. China's Law on the Protection of Minors requires units and staff in close contact with children who discover such circumstances in their work to report at once to the public security, civil affairs, education and other relevant departments; the Provisions on the School Protection of Minors require schools and staff who discover them to report promptly as provided.2 Japan's Child Abuse Prevention Act, Article 5, says that schools and school staff "must endeavor to detect child abuse early" (my translation; 児童虐待の早期発見に努めなければならない); Article 6 says that one who discovers it "must notify" (my translation; 通告しなければならない). Abuse here is limited to what Article 2 of that Act defines: certain kinds of acts done by a guardian, that is, a person exercising parental authority, a guardian of a minor or another person actually taking care of the child, toward the child in his care. Article 5 is a duty to endeavor toward early detection; Article 6 is a duty to notify on discovering a child who appears to have been abused, and does not presuppose that the abuse has been proved. Sweden's Social Services Act, Chapter 19, section 1, requires schools and their staff to report "immediately" (my translation; genast).

Three things written apart. Protection, handling within the school, and reporting are three things. Having reported does not by itself settle the protection the school can still give; nor does the school, by having reported or by having been present, gain the whole power to investigate and decide the matter.

When parents keep the child from using it. There is a case in which an institution gives a child a share and his parents do not let him use it. What the school can do at its end is protect, respond and refer; what it did and did not do is questioned by the school's accountability. How the parents' end is read lies in Parent-Child Theory, not here.

XX16 After the line is crossed. Where something in a school crosses a child's basic rights, whether it comes from a teacher, a staff member or another child, the school answers for protection and disposition; the child has a road for telling that the party involved does not control, and so do his parents. Where a child suffers an effect that crosses the line at home or elsewhere and comes to the school for help, the school does not push him away, and answers for the protection and referral it can give; where the school, within its accountability, finds that a child has suffered a violation of his rights, may have suffered such a violation, or faces danger, it reports as the rules require, and does not require young children or children who cannot fully express themselves first to complete a formal request for help.

§8 Care in Three Stages

This section follows the axis of stages and writes only the arrangements the school mainly handles at each stage. That a matter is written at some stage does not mean it appears only there, nor is the stage used to judge where a particular child has got to.

Kindergarten. Care is the main item. The three signals are read in the arrangements of childcare: a child's "no" may not be a sentence; it may be crying, turning away, holding back. Whether such a "no" is taken up. The entrance of reporting on discovery matters at this stage: the child is not required first to complete a formal request for help.

Primary school. Rules for what teaching requires, such as attendance, order and homework; the authority and limits of discipline.

Secondary school. Rules on phones; online platforms the school requires students to use; channels students can start for themselves (Paper 4).

Boarding. Care runs all day; "while he is at school" has to be read again. This paper only registers it.

§9 Back to Kant's Question

Paper 4 of Cultivation Theory cites a question from Kant's lectures on pedagogy: "For compulsion is necessary! How do I cultivate freedom under compulsion?" That paper gave only the direction of an answer: "freedom grows in the share compulsion did not occupy, on the road along which guardianship is handed back." It also says: "This paper does not deny that compulsion is necessary; nor does it say that the formation of freedom will happen of itself." It does not take compulsion as a means of forming freedom.

The same holds at the level of the school. The share compulsion did not occupy is what §4 described: his "no" can land, the road of looking back is not blocked, his future "no" is not made costly. The road along which guardianship is handed back is the line of §3 not being drawn once and for all: in a matter where he can already walk on his own, the position of raising and refusing in that matter is his. This paper does not deny that compulsion is necessary; §2 divided it into three sources, each with its limits. Compulsion itself is not called cultivation.

Where This Paper Stops

What it has not done: it has not said which way of discipline is good; it has not written the particular rules of school safety; it has not written the legal forms of guardianship; it has not judged whether any particular sanction was right.

The Five Propositions of This Paper

XX12 Compulsion in a school is not called cultivation. This paper distinguishes three sources of compulsion in a school, each checked by the accountability it claims and by its actual limits: what care requires, limited to what care requires; what teaching requires (attendance, order, homework), limited to what teaching requires; what is set up to make quiet and room (such as collecting phones), checked by what it actually freed and what it sealed. The name of a category is not in itself an authorization; a measure that claims several purposes does not settle its limits by picking the widest of them. Where law and higher rules lay it down and the school carries it out, the school answers for the scope and manner of its carrying out, and the source is acknowledged but not judged; compulsion whose source cannot be stated has no ground. None of them is renamed cultivation or renamed giving conditions, nor used to prove that cultivation has happened; conditions given separately in the same arrangement are checked separately. Which measure counts as required is explained by the position that exercises the compulsion: by what accountability, what is required, why it stops at this scope, when it stops or changes; and the explanation is reviewed through a road that position does not alone control. Who still has others act on his behalf, who can already act on his own, and where the position of raising and refusing belongs, are not settled by a line drawn by grade that is externally fixed, cannot be overturned by the facts of an individual case, and is applied uniformly across matters (a line that can be overturned by the facts of an individual case, is judged matter by matter, and keeps appeal and review is not of this kind). Two things cannot be argued backward: that he later grew up well does not prove the earlier compulsion was all justified; that the earlier compulsion had grounds does not show that cultivation took place. (Conceptual distinction and normative; Cultivation Theory HY20 and HY23, which speak of guardianship, extended to compulsion in the school, this series' own undertaking; explanation and review take over On Rights and Entitlements F34 and F35; the line extends On Rights and Entitlements F34a, whose subject is the subject-to-be and whose line lies between entitlement and recognition-based right, this series' own undertaking; the category of leaving room points the same way as Education as Subject-Condition 3.3)

XX13 The share beyond compulsion. Alongside compulsion, the school's arrangements let his "no" land: it is heard, not punished, not laughed at; his later looking back is not sealed; his future "no" is not made costly. There is an arrangement for handing over the authority to interpret as he grows; his refusals and deviations can enter a real rejudgment and really change the arrangement, and are not all read as his not yet understanding; being able to raise something is not the same as having it come to stand, and matters that involve others or shared arrangements are read by each party's own law, not decided by any one party alone; the present arrangement is marked as one that can be changed. (Normative; Cultivation Theory HY21 and its three signals, which speak of the guardian's side, extended to the school's arrangements, this series' own undertaking; "being able to raise something is not the same as having it come to stand" takes over Parent-Child Theory PC39; the sentence on the cost of refusal takes over Cultivation Theory HY27)

XX14 Standing does not change with the ability to refuse. He is not excluded from the consideration of a matter concerning him on the ground that "he's still little, he doesn't understand." Not yet being able to say no does not mean having no preferences or refusals in any matter; one judges by domain, not the whole person. (Normative; taking over Cultivation Theory HY22 and HY24, and Teacher-Student Theory TS5)

XX15 Discipline by event; no identity is fixed on the child. A matter is handled as an event; once handled, no mark of identity is left; one handling is not registered as a final verdict on this child. A record of the event may be kept: its keeping states the purpose, who can see it (the child himself and his parents included), until when, and how it is corrected; when an old record is used in a new decision, its relevance to this matter is stated, and it does not enter every later decision without explanation. Where a record is given outside the school (transfer, progression to the next stage, employment), the giving side states what it gave and on what ground, and the adopting side answers for its adoption. This does not cancel the checking of particular facts, nor, for the sake of "leaving no mark," erase facts that still matter for protection. 摘要

照看一个还不能说不的人，免不了强制；学校也一样。这一篇立五条命题。学校里的强制有三个来处：照看所需的，教所需的，为留出安静与空而设的；各按各的限核，都不叫涵育，也不叫给条件。哪一件算所需，由行强制的位置说明，并受一条不由它独控的路复核；分开代行与自行的那条线，不以年级一刀画死。与强制同时，学校的安排让孩子的"不"落得下。孩子的地位不随他能不能拒绝而变。纪律按事件处理，不给孩子定身份；记录可以留，怎样留要说得出。学校里发生越过孩子基础权利的事，学校就保护与处置作答，孩子有一条不由涉事一方控制的路；孩子在家里或别处受侵来求助，学校不推开。这一篇不说哪一种纪律办法好，不写学校安全的具体规则，也不写监护的法律形式。

引言

涵育论第四篇有一句：「照看一个还不能说不的人，免不了强制；这份强制不叫涵育，监护归制度侧。」

学校是制度侧的一处。孩子在校的时候，学校替他定许多事：几点到，坐在哪里，下课能去哪里，手机放在哪里。

中国 2021 年施行的《中小学教育惩戒规则（试行）》第十一条写：「学生扰乱课堂或者教育教学秩序，影响他人或者可能对自己及他人造成伤害的，教师可以采取必要措施，将学生带离教室或者教学现场，并予以教育管理」；同一条还写，发现学生藏匿违法，危险物品的，可以检查可能藏匿物品的课桌与储物柜；违规的物品可以暂扣。下一条列出教师「不得有下列行为」：体罚，变相体罚，侮辱人格尊严，因为学业成绩惩戒学生，等等。同一份规则给了权限，也画了限度。

这一篇问：学校里的强制从哪里来，止于哪里；与它同时，孩子的"不"落不落得下。

第一节　在校的时候

学校照看的范围。 学校在孩子在校的时候照看他。"在校的时候"不按校门划。制度论划一个构的内部，按纠缠划，不按身份划；构是否在组织一个人的生活，按实际关系判；影响所及，又不等于权限所及（制度论第一篇第四节）。这一篇把它推到照看上：学校照看的范围，跟着学校实际组织孩子生活的那一段走。学校组织的校外活动，校车，寄宿，学校要求孩子使用的网上平台，都在内；留下来补做作业一类延长在校时间的安排，照看的范围也跟着延长。

反过来也一样。 学校的作用可以延到家里，比如作业；照看的职责不因此延到家里，学校也不因此取得管孩子校外生活的权限。这一段推及是这个系列自己承担的。

这一节不写的。 寄宿学校里，照看成了全天，"在校的时候"要重读，这一篇只登记。监护的法律形式，这一篇不写。这一节不立命题，它定的是后面几节的范围；同学之间的那一面，第四篇照这一范围读。

第二节　三个来处

强制从哪里来。 学校里的强制，可以从三处来。照看所需的：不让一个孩子跑上马路，生病的孩子送去校医。教所需的：到课，课堂的秩序，作业。为留出安静与空而设的：收手机一类。教育篇说过，孩子第一次碰到解释不了的东西，若周围不停地给着高频，确定的刺激，他就没有停下来的那一段；那一段需要安静。收手机一类的规则，主张的是这一个来处。

各按各的限。 照看所需的，以照看所需为限；教所需的，以教所需为限；为留空而设的，按它实际腾出了什么，封住了什么核。收起一个孩子的手机，可以腾出一节课的安静；也可以把他与家里联系的那条路，或一件他要用的辅助器具，一并收走。

名称不是授权。 分三类，是为了分析。一项措施叫作"安全"或者"秩序"，不因这个名字就有了权限。同一项措施主张几种目的的，不挑其中最宽的那一项，来结清它的限度。

英格兰《2006 年教育与督导法》第 93 条第 1 款，许学校工作人员使用「such force as is reasonable in the circumstances」，所为的有三项：防止学生犯罪；防止伤人或者损物；防止「prejudicing the maintenance of good order and discipline」。条文给三项的是同一个标准；前两项涉及照看，后一项涉及教学的秩序，几项也可以交叠。这一篇的读法是：所需不同，限度也就不同；具体的限度，仍就职责，情形与所用的手段核。瑞典《学校法》第 5 章第 6 条许学校采取「omedelbar och tillfällig åtgärd」，即立即而临时的措施；权限带着时间上的限。1 这几项都只说明：有权限，仍有范围。它们不替某一次强制作出正当的判断。照看所需一类里，这一篇有的是第 93 条的前两项，与引言里惩戒规则第十一条的两处；专写安全与健康上的强制，比如健康照护里的强制，边界怎样定的规则文本，还没有找到，留白。

留空一类。 2021 年，教育部办公厅的手机管理通知写：「学校应当告知学生和家长，原则上不得将个人手机带入校园。」又写：「学生确有将手机带入校园需求的，须经学生家长同意、书面提出申请，进校后应将手机交由学校统一保管，禁止带入课堂。」同年施行的《未成年人学校保护规定》许学校禁止学生带手机进校或在校内使用，对经允许带入的统一管理；另一条又写，除了法定事由，不得查阅学生的信件，日记，电子邮件或其他网络通讯内容。2 保管与查看，在这里是两件事。英格兰《2026 年儿童福祉与学校法》第 36 条要学校顾及大臣关于手机的指引；那份指引「became statutory on 29 June 2026」，管的是「throughout the school day, including during lessons, the time between lessons, breaktimes and lunchtime」，残障与医疗上的需要另有例外。芬兰《基础教育法》2025 年修正的第 29 条第 4 款写：「Oppilas ei saa käyttää puhelinta tai muuta mobiililaitetta oppitunnin aikana」，即学生在课上不得使用手机或别的移动设备；例外有三项：教师许可的学习用途，校长或教师许可的健康照护，辅助器具。例外写进了规则。

都不叫涵育。 几样强制，都不改名为涵育，也不改名为给条件；不拿来证明涵育已经发生。收手机腾出来的那一节安静，可以是涵育的条件；收手机本身是强制。同一次安排里另外给出的条件，比如一个不排课的下午，分开核。

来处认而不判。 法律与上级规则定下，学校执行的，学校就执行的范围与方式作答；那条规则本身该不该有，这一篇认而不判。说不出来处的强制，没有根据。

XX12　学校里的强制不叫涵育。 本篇区分学校里强制的三种来处，各按它所主张的职责与实际限度核：照看所需的，以照看所需为限；教所需的（到课，秩序，作业），以教所需为限；为留出安静与空而设的（如收手机），按它实际腾出了什么，封住了什么核。分类名称本身不是授权；同一项措施主张几种目的的，不挑其中最宽的一项来结清限度。法律与上级规则定下，学校执行的，学校就执行的范围与方式作答，来处认而不判；说不出来处的强制，没有根据。几样都不改名为涵育，不改名为给条件，也不拿来证明涵育已经发生；同一次安排里另外给出的条件，分别核。

第三节　说明，复核与那条线

由谁说明。 哪一件算所需，由行强制的那个位置说明：依什么职责，所需的是什么，为什么止于这个范围，什么时候停，什么时候改。权益专论在婴儿那里读照护，把同一件照护的事分成两面：供给的一面，不收敛孩子的行动；实际安排，强制或收敛了他的行动的那一面，是权力；对着这份权力回绝与退出的位置，由监护的网络替他守着。是权力，就要说得出凭什么。

不由它独控的路。 权益专论也分开了三个位置：照看的人，监护的网络，审查的那一处；现实里它们可以重合，分析上要分开；越过了界的那一方，不能一边越界，一边独自替孩子撤去自己的权力，撤换要落在另一处没有越界的位置上。这一篇取它的分法。落到学校：行强制的位置说明了，还要有一条不由它独控的路，去复核它的说明。

那条线。 学校总要分开：哪些事仍由大人替孩子做主，哪些事他已经能自己做；一件事上，发问与回绝的位置在谁那里。权益专论说过一件同形的事：一个正在长的孩子，否定的那一道门一点一点地自己运作起来，权益与承认性权利之间的线也随着移动；对他施加一道外部固定，不可反驳，跨情境统一使用的二元阈，必在某一点，对某一个长得或快或慢的孩子，落错边。原句说的是准主体，线在权益与承认性权利之间。这一篇把它推到学校分开代行与自行的线上，理由是两条线跟着的是同一样东西：孩子在一件件事上能不能自己走，是分事项，渐进的，学校的一条线追不上它，同样会落错边。所以学校分开代行与自行，以及发问与回绝的位置归谁，不以年级设一道外部固定，不可被个案事实推翻，跨事项统一使用的线。可以被个案事实推翻，按事项分别判，保留申诉与复核的线，不在此列；权益专论自己也把这一种与死线分开。课程，课表，设施与学段的规则，不因此一概要许个案推翻；它们封没封住孩子已经取得的位置，照实际作用核。

两个不能倒推。 涵育论第四篇说的是监护：「后来他长成了，不证明当初的监护都正当；当初的监护有根据，也不说明涵育已经发生。」学校里的强制同样：后来他长成了，不证明当初学校的强制都正当；当初的强制有根据，也不说明涵育已经发生。

一个反方。 有一路纪律主张，秩序优先，零容忍：统一，严格，可以预期的纪律，保护多数孩子的学习；对一件件事说明，复核，拖慢了处置。3 这一说的力量在两处：秩序本身是教所需；处置慢了，受害的孩子等不起。这一篇不否认教所需的强制，第二节已经把它算作一个来处；它要的是说得出来处与限度，有一条复核的路。同学受侵的那一面，本篇第七节与第四篇第五节接住。及时制止与说明复核可以并存：紧急的保护可以先行，事后仍要说明根据，范围，并受后续的问；复核不写成每一个动作之前都要办完的手续。代价也要说：说明与复核要占老师与学校的时间，这一份时间，计入第五篇说的工作量。

XX12（续） 哪一件算所需，由行强制的位置说明依什么职责，所需是什么，为什么止于这个范围，什么时候停或改，并受一条不由它独控的路复核。对谁仍由他人代行，谁已能自行，以及发问与回绝的位置归属，不以年级设一道外部固定，不可被个案事实推翻，跨事项统一使用的线（可被个案事实推翻，按事项分别判，保留申诉与复核的，不在此列）。两个不能倒推：后来他长成了，不证明当初的强制都正当；当初的强制有根据，也不说明涵育已经发生。

第四节　强制之外的那一份

涵育论那三处。 涵育论第四篇说，与监护同时，在监护之外，有一份涵育：「他的『不』落得下，即被听见，不受罚，不被笑；不封他日后的回看；不把他将来的『不』做贵。」它又说：「在他还不能自己认领的事上，封住的形态是预占，当下读得出三个信号：解释权有没有随他长大移交的安排；他慢慢出现的拒绝、偏离与重构，能不能真改动安排；眼下的安排，标作可以改的，还是标作将来不可反驳的。」

落到学校。 原句说的是监护的一方。学校在孩子在校的时候，也持着一份照看与强制，所以这三处与三个信号，在学校的安排上同样读得出。一个孩子说"不"，有人听；他不因为说了"不"就挨罚，被当众笑。他的档案里写着的那些判断，由谁解释，随他长大有没有交给他的安排。他提出的异议，能不能进入真的再判断，真改动一样安排，还是一律被读成还不懂事。眼下的分班，座位，课表，标作这一学期的安排，还是标作他这个人的定论。这一推及是这个系列承担的。

能提出，不等于提出得成。 亲子论在父母与孩子之间说过：「能提出，不等于提出得成」；那件事上的安排改不改，若也牵着别人，照各方读，不由哪一方单方说了算。学校里同样。他对一件牵着同学或全班的安排说"不"，不等于那件安排就改；他的"不"进入了再判断，这一份就在。

拒绝的代价。 强制照第二节的限度。因为他说了"不"而追加的那一截，是给拒绝加代价；涵育论第五篇说过：「拒绝的代价，不由给的一方加上去。」原本有独立根据的要求，不因他说了"不"就自动消失；可是叫作"补做"，也不证明没有惩罚他的拒绝。

一种写法。 瑞典《学校法》第 4 章第 9 条写：「Informationen och formerna för barnens och elevernas inflytande ska anpassas efter deras ålder och mognad.」即学生发挥影响的信息与形式，按他们的年龄与成熟调整。参与的形式写进了规则，按年龄与成熟调整；它不证明哪一个孩子的"不"实际落得下。

XX13　强制之外的那一份。 与强制同时，学校的安排让他的"不"落得下：被听见，不受罚，不被笑；不封他日后的回看；不把他将来的"不"做贵。解释权有随他长大移交的安排；他的拒绝与偏离能进入真实的再判断，能真改动安排，不一律被读作还不懂事；能提出，不等于提出得成，牵着别人或共同安排的事，照各方读，不由哪一方单方说了算；眼下的安排标作可以改的。

第五节　地位不随能不能拒绝而变

师生论说：「学生作为目的的地位，从一开始就是满的，不由老师授予；他会什么、懂多少，随学随长。」又说：「两本账分开记：『他还不懂』，至多描述他在这件事上还做不到什么，不降他的地位，也不拿来把他的问与『不』一律读作不懂事。」

涵育论第四篇说：「还不能说不，不等于在所有事上都没有偏好与拒绝。判分域，不判整个人。」一个孩子在怎样算分数这件事上还不能判断，在午饭吃不吃青菜，愿不愿意上台发言这些事上，他已经有偏好，也会拒绝。学校考量一件关于他的事，不以"他还小，还不懂"把他排除在外。

《儿童权利公约》第 12 条第 1 款，要缔约国确保有主见能力的儿童，对影响到他本人的一切事项，有权自由发表意见，并按他的年龄与成熟给以适当的看待。4 分域判断怎样写进学校的规则，这一篇没有找到合适的规则文本。

"几岁可以做什么"一类的年龄线，是规则层的一种形态。判分域照这一节读；年龄线可以被个案事实推翻，按事项分别判，保留复核的，不在第三节那条线之列。

XX14　地位不随能不能拒绝而变。 不以"他还小，还不懂"把他排除在一件关于他的事的考量之外。还不能说不，不等于在所有事上都没有偏好与拒绝；判分域，不判整个人。

第六节　纪律按事件

按事件处理。 一个孩子做了一件事，学校处理这一件事。处理完了，不留身份的标记，不把一次处理登记成对这个孩子的终局判定。制度论说过同样的一条：制度对人的识别只到事件为止，不变成身份；「这不取消对具体事实的核验」。不为"不留标记"抹去仍有保护意义的事实：一个孩子伤过别的孩子，这件事的事实，在保护上仍有用。大学论在大学这一层说过同形的一条。

一份规则写了程序。 惩戒规则第十四条：拟对学生实施第十条所列的教育惩戒或纪律处分的，「应当听取学生的陈述和申辩」；「学生或者家长申请听证的，学校应当组织听证」；「能够诚恳认错、积极改正的，可以提前解除教育惩戒或者纪律处分」。处理写成了一件事，有程序，可以解除。

处分有期限。 《未成年人学校保护规定》写：除了开除学籍，处分都要设期限；对受处分的学生跟踪观察，有针对性地教育；确有改正的，到期解除；解除以后，他获得表彰，奖励与别的权益，不再受原处分的影响。2 这近于"处理完了，不留身份的标记"。这一条写了期限与权益，没有写解除以后处分的记录怎样处理；解除不等于删除。

记录可以留。 事件的记录可以留，留法要说得出：用途是什么，谁能看，本人与父母在内，留到什么时候，怎样更正。旧的记录用到新的决定里，要说明它与这一件的关联，不无需说明地进入以后的一切决定。

《陕西省义务教育阶段学生学籍管理办法》（2019）第二十五条写：「学生处分决定存入学生学籍档案，处分撤销后，应及时将处分记录从学生学籍档案中撤出。」同一条又写：「处分决定、撤销处分决定及有关材料由学校存档。」学籍档案里撤出了，学校另存着材料。两面都在一条里。5 教育部 2025 年的《中小学生学籍管理办法》，所核的转载件全文，未见关于处分记录的条文。美国联邦的 FERPA 写了谁能看，怎样更正：家长或已到年龄的学生请求查阅，学校在规定的天数内给看；认为记录不准确或有误导的，可以请求修改，学校不改的，要告知他有听证的权利。6 这里的修改，针对的是记录准不准，不是对那一次处理的重审。

记录出校。 记录给到学校以外的，比如转学，升学，用人，给出的一方要说明给了什么，凭什么给；采用的一方，就它的采用作答。记录出校以后的用途与限用，这一篇还没有找到合适的规则，第六篇在评价一侧再读。

XX15　纪律按事件，不给孩子定身份。 处理一件事，按事件处理；处理完了，不留身份的标记；不把一次处理登记成对这个孩子的终局判定。事件的记录可以留：留存说明用途，谁能看（含本人与父母），到什么时候，怎样更正；旧记录用到新的决定里，说明它与这一件的关联，不无需说明地进入以后的一切决定。记录给出学校以外的（转学，升学，用人），给出的一方说明给了什么，凭什么给，采用的一方就采用作答。这不取消对具体事实的核验，也不为"不留标记"抹去仍有保护意义的事实。

第七节　越线以后

关系侧读到哪里为止。 师生论读老师与学生之间：「老师的作用越过学生基础权利的那一次，这个系列读到『这是侵』，侵以后的保护与处置在制度侧。」亲子论在父母与孩子之间也交来同样的一件：「孩子的基础权利，份位在他本人，不由父母授予；它怎样护、规则怎样定，归制度侧，不论父母朝哪个方向都适用。」两处交到制度侧的，在学校这一层接。

学校就保护与处置作答。 学校里发生越过孩子基础权利的事，不论出自老师，职员，还是别的孩子，学校就保护与处置作答。中国《义务教育法》第二十九条第二款写：「教师应当尊重学生的人格，不得歧视学生，不得对学生实施体罚、变相体罚或者其他侮辱人格尊严的行为，不得侵犯学生合法权益。」《学前教育法》把体罚或变相体罚儿童，列为幼儿园教师与其他工作人员的违法情形。2 《儿童权利公约》第 28 条第 2 款，要缔约国采取一切适当的措施，确保学校执行纪律的方式合乎儿童的人格尊严。4 禁止写进了法律。

一条不由涉事一方控制的路。 孩子要有一条路可以去说，这条路不由涉事的一方控制；父母也可以去说。制度论给这样的通道立过三项条件：「独立于被追问的一方，内部主体实际可以发动，发动不被报复」。惩戒规则第十六条写：「家长对教师实施的教育惩戒有异议或者认为教师行为违反本规则第十二条规定的，可以向学校或者主管教育行政部门投诉、举报。」教育部 2022 年答复留言时写明，投诉举报与申诉「是不同的事项」，「投诉、举报针对的是教师行为，不受时间限制」。这一条发动的是家长：它说明"父母也可以"写进了规则，不说明孩子自己有一条路。孩子自己能发动的路，第四篇写。

两个入口。 一个是求助。孩子在家里或别处受到越线的作用，来向学校求助，学校不推开，就它能做的保护与转介作答。《未成年人学校保护规定》写：学生因为遭受遗弃，虐待，向学校请求保护的，学校不得拒绝，推诿；需要救助的，先行救助。2

另一个是发现。学校在职责范围里发现孩子受侵，疑似受侵，或者面临危险，照规定报告；不要求幼儿，或者不能充分表达的孩子，先完成一次正式的求助。中国的《未成年人保护法》要密切接触孩子的单位与工作人员，在工作中发现这样的情形，立即向公安，民政，教育等有关部门报告；《未成年人学校保护规定》要学校与教职工发现时，依照规定及时报告。2 日本《儿童虐待防止法》第五条要学校与教职员「児童虐待の早期発見に努めなければならない」，第六条要发现者「通告しなければならない」。这里的虐待，限于该法第二条所界定的情形：保护者，即行使亲权的人，未成年监护人与其他实际监护孩子的人，对他所监护的孩子所为的几类行为；第五条是早期发现的努力义务，第六条是发现看来受了虐待的孩子以后的通告义务，不以虐待已经证实为前提。瑞典《社会服务法》第 19 章第 1 条要学校与工作人员「genast」，即立即报告。

三件分开写。 保护，校内的处置，报告，是三件。已经报告了，不独自结清学校仍能做的保护；学校也不因为报告了，或者因为在场，就取得这件事的全部调查与裁断权。

父母不让孩子用的时候。 有一种情形：制度给了孩子一份，父母不让他用。学校这一端能做的，是保护，回应与转介；做了什么，没做什么，照学校的职责受问。父母那一端怎样读，在亲子论，不在这里。

XX16　越线以后。 学校里发生越过孩子基础权利的事，无论出自老师，职员还是别的孩子，学校就保护与处置作答；孩子有一条不由涉事一方控制的路可以去说，父母也可以。孩子在家里或别处受到越线的作用，向学校求助的，学校不推开，就它能做的保护与转介作答；学校在职责范围里发现孩子受侵，疑似受侵或面临危险的，照规定报告，不要求幼儿或不能充分表达的孩子先完成正式的求助。

第八节　三段的照看

这一节照阶段轴写，只写各段学校着重处理的安排；写到某一段，不表示那件事只在那一段出现，也不以学段判某一个孩子到了哪一步。

幼儿园。 照看是主件。三个信号读在保育的安排上：孩子的"不"可以不是一句话，是哭，是躲开，是不肯；这样的"不"有没有人接。发现即报告这一个入口，在这一段要紧：不要求孩子先完成正式的求助。

小学。 到课，秩序与作业一类教所需的规则；惩戒的权限与限度。

中学。 手机的规则，学校要求使用的网上平台；学生自己能发动的通道（第四篇）。

寄宿。 照看成了全天；"在校的时候"要重读，这一篇只登记。

第九节　回到康德那一问

涵育论第四篇引过康德《论教育学》里的一问：「因为强制是必要的！我怎样在强制之下培养自由？」那一篇只给了一个回答的方向：「自由在强制没有占住的那一份里长，在监护归还的路上长。」它也说：「强制的必要，这一篇不否认；自由的养成，这一篇也不说会自己发生。」它不把强制当作养成自由的手段。

学校这一层同样。强制没有占住的那一份，是第四节说的：他的"不"落得下，回看的路不堵，将来的"不"不做贵。监护归还的路，是第三节说的那条线不一刀画死：在一件事上他已经能自己走，那件事上发问与回绝的位置就归他。强制的必要，这一篇不否认，第二节把它分成三个来处，各有其限；强制本身，不叫涵育。

这一篇停在哪里

它没有做的：没有说哪一种纪律办法好；没有写学校安全的具体规则；没有写监护的法律形式；没有判哪一次处分对不对。

XX12　学校里的强制不叫涵育。 本篇区分学校里强制的三种来处，各按它所主张的职责与实际限度核：照看所需的，以照看所需为限；教所需的（到课，秩序，作业），以教所需为限；为留出安静与空而设的（如收手机），按它实际腾出了什么，封住了什么核。分类名称本身不是授权；同一项措施主张几种目的的，不挑其中最宽的一项来结清限度。法律与上级规则定下，学校执行的，学校就执行的范围与方式作答，来处认而不判；说不出来处的强制，没有根据。几样都不改名为涵育，不改名为给条件，也不拿来证明涵育已经发生；同一次安排里另外给出的条件，分别核。哪一件算所需，由行强制的位置说明依什么职责，所需是什么，为什么止于这个范围，什么时候停或改，并受一条不由它独控的路复核。对谁仍由他人代行，谁已能自行，以及发问与回绝的位置归属，不以年级设一道外部固定，不可被个案事实推翻，跨事项统一使用的线（可被个案事实推翻，按事项分别判，保留申诉与复核的，不在此列）。两个不能倒推：后来他长成了，不证明当初的强制都正当；当初的强制有根据，也不说明涵育已经发生。（概念区分与规范；涵育论 HY20，HY23 原说监护，推到学校的强制，本系列承担；说明与复核承权益专论 F34，F35；那条线推及权益专论 F34a，原句的主词是准主体，线在权益与承认性权利之间，本系列承担；留空一类与教育篇 3.3 同向）

XX13　强制之外的那一份。 与强制同时，学校的安排让他的"不"落得下：被听见，不受罚，不被笑；不封他日后的回看；不把他将来的"不"做贵。解释权有随他长大移交的安排；他的拒绝与偏离能进入真实的再判断，能真改动安排，不一律被读作还不懂事；能提出，不等于提出得成，牵着别人或共同安排的事，照各方读，不由哪一方单方说了算；眼下的安排标作可以改的。（规范；涵育论 HY21 与它的三个信号原说监护的一方，推到学校的安排，本系列承担；"能提出，不等于提出得成"承亲子论 PC39；拒绝的代价一句承涵育论 HY27）

XX14　地位不随能不能拒绝而变。 不以"他还小，还不懂"把他排除在一件关于他的事的考量之外。还不能说不，不等于在所有事上都没有偏好与拒绝；判分域，不判整个人。（规范；承涵育论 HY22，HY24，师生论 TS5）

XX15　纪律按事件，不给孩子定身份。 处理一件事，按事件处理；处理完了，不留身份的标记；不把一次处理登记成对这个孩子的终局判定。事件的记录可以留：留存说明用途，谁能看（含本人与父母），到什么时候，怎样更正；旧记录用到新的决定里，说明它与这一件的关联，不无需说明地进入以后的一切决定。记录给出学校以外的（转学，升学，用人），给出的一方说明给了什么，凭什么给，采用的一方就采用作答。这不取消对具体事实的核验，也不为"不留标记"抹去仍有保护意义的事实。（规范；承制度论 Z3，末句照它的原句；与大学论 DX12 同形；记录出校一句，推及学科论第六篇 S34，原说标准，推到记录，与大学论 DX26 同形，本系列承担）

XX16　越线以后。 学校里发生越过孩子基础权利的事，无论出自老师，职员还是别的孩子，学校就保护与处置作答；孩子有一条不由涉事一方控制的路可以去说，父母也可以。孩子在家里或别处受到越线的作用，向学校求助的，学校不推开，就它能做的保护与转介作答；学校在职责范围里发现孩子受侵，疑似受侵或面临危险的，照规定报告，不要求幼儿或不能充分表达的孩子先完成正式的求助。（规范；接师生论 Paper 0 第一节与第二篇第四节，那里读到"这是侵"为止；接亲子论 PC10 交给制度侧的那一件；不由涉事一方控制的路，承制度论 Z34）
